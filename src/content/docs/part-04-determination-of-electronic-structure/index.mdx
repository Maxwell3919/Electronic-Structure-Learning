# 电子结构的基本求解方法

<BilingualSection titleZh="统一方法主线" titleEn="A unified route through the methods" eyebrow="Part IV">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Part IV 从同一个连续独立粒子方程出发，依次研究平面波与网格、局域原子轨道、APW/KKR/MTO 增强函数，以及利用局域性构造的线性标度方法。每章都把物理方程、有限表示、求解算法、数值误差和可支持的科学判断分开。

```text
连续方程
→ 表示或离散
→ Hc=εSc 或 Green's function
→ 密度或密度矩阵
→ 自洽循环
→ observable 收敛
```

Chapter 12 建立平面波与网格表示，Chapter 13 将其嵌入完整自洽计算；Chapter 14 建立局域轨道与 tight-binding 语言，Chapter 15 进入非正交局域基中的完整自洽计算；Chapter 16 转向 sphere/interstitial augmentation、nonlinear APW、KKR multiple scattering、CPA、MTO、canonical bands 与 screened locality。Chapters 12–16 已有实质内容，Chapters 17–18 仍保持目录级 outline，按顺序继续建设。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Part IV begins from the same continuous independent-particle equation and then studies plane waves and grids, localized atom-centered orbitals, APW/KKR/MTO augmented functions, and linear-scaling methods built from locality. Each chapter keeps the physical equation, finite representation, solution algorithm, numerical error, and supported scientific judgment separate.

```text
continuous equation
→ representation or discretization
→ Hc=εSc or Green's function
→ density or density matrix
→ self-consistency
→ observable convergence
```

Chapter 12 establishes plane-wave and grid representations, and Chapter 13 embeds them in complete self-consistent calculations. Chapter 14 develops localized-orbital and tight-binding language, Chapter 15 advances to full calculations in nonorthogonal local bases, and Chapter 16 introduces sphere/interstitial augmentation, nonlinear APW, KKR multiple scattering, CPA, MTOs, canonical bands, and screened locality. Chapters 12–16 now have substantive content; Chapters 17–18 remain directory-level outlines and will be developed in sequence.
