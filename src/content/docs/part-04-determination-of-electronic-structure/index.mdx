Each chapter keeps the physical equation, finite representation, solution algorithm, numerical error, and supported scientific judgment separate.

```text
continuous equation
→ representation or discretization
→ Hc=εSc or Green's function
→ density or density matrix
→ self-consistency
→ observable convergence
```
