# 概览与背景主题 | Overview and Background Topics

<BilingualSection titleZh="Part I 的阅读任务" titleEn="The task of Part I" eyebrow="Part I complete">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Part I 的五章已经完成。它不直接给出一个可运行的 DFT workflow，而是建立后续所有章节反复使用的语言：量子态与可观测量、多电子 Hamiltonian 与近似层级、周期平移与 Bloch 表示、均匀电子气的解析尺度，以及“理论对象—数值模型—材料主张”之间不可跨越的证据边界。

阅读顺序具有依赖关系。Chapter 1 解释电子结构问题为何形成；Chapter 2 给出全书对象和性质路线；Chapter 3 写出多体问题并区分 Born–Oppenheimer、独立电子、Hartree 与 Hartree–Fock；Chapter 4 把无限周期固体压缩到晶胞和 Brillouin 区；Chapter 5 用均匀电子气展示 Fermi statistics、exchange、correlation 与 screening 的基准结构。Part II 才从密度变分原理开始建立 DFT。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

All five chapters of Part I are complete. This Part does not yet provide a runnable DFT workflow. It establishes the language reused throughout the book: quantum states and observables, the many-electron Hamiltonian and approximation hierarchy, periodic translations and the Bloch representation, analytic scales of the uniform electron gas, and the evidence boundaries separating theoretical objects, numerical models, and material claims.

The order is dependency-driven. Chapter 1 explains how the electronic-structure problem emerged. Chapter 2 maps the objects and property routes of the book. Chapter 3 formulates the many-body problem and separates Born–Oppenheimer, independent-electron, Hartree, and Hartree–Fock levels. Chapter 4 reduces an infinite periodic solid to cells and the Brillouin zone. Chapter 5 uses the uniform electron gas to expose the reference structure of Fermi statistics, exchange, correlation, and screening. Part II then begins density-functional theory from density-space variational principles.

<BilingualSection titleZh="符号与表示的交接" titleEn="Handoff of notation and representations" eyebrow="Conventions">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

<ul>
  <li><code>R</code>、<code>r</code>、<code>k</code> 与 <code>G</code> 分别用于核/晶格位置、电子坐标、第一 Brillouin 区的 crystal momentum class 和 reciprocal-lattice vector。路径坐标不是全 BZ 数据。</li>
  <li><code>Ψ</code> 表示多体波函数，<code>ψ_i</code> 或 <code>ψ_nk</code> 表示给定独立粒子或辅助表示中的轨道。轨道表示的有效性不能从记号本身推出。</li>
  <li><code>n(r)</code> 是粒子密度；one-body density matrix、pair distribution、DOS、JDOS 和 response function 是不同对象，具有不同归一化和可观测关系。</li>
  <li><code>E</code>、单粒子 eigenvalue、quasiparticle energy 和 excitation energy 必须分别定义。总能差、band eigenvalue 差和光谱峰不能自动互换。</li>
  <li><code>r_s</code> 是均匀电子气的密度参数；它为 LDA 和许多 electron-gas constraints 提供输入，但不把非均匀晶体变成均匀体系。</li>
</ul>

所有章节中的 teaching visualizations 都是声明模型的有限实现。确定性测试验证公式和界面行为；它们不验证真实材料、生产代码、实验结果或未声明的连续极限。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

<ul>
  <li><code>R</code>, <code>r</code>, <code>k</code>, and <code>G</code> denote nuclear/lattice positions, electronic coordinates, crystal-momentum classes in the first Brillouin zone, and reciprocal-lattice vectors. A path coordinate is not full-BZ data.</li>
  <li><code>Ψ</code> denotes a many-body wavefunction; <code>ψ_i</code> or <code>ψ_nk</code> denotes an orbital in a declared independent-particle or auxiliary representation. Notation alone does not establish the validity of that representation.</li>
  <li><code>n(r)</code> is the particle density. A one-body density matrix, pair distribution, DOS, JDOS, and response function are different objects with different normalizations and observable relations.</li>
  <li>Total energies, one-particle eigenvalues, quasiparticle energies, and excitation energies require separate definitions. Total-energy differences, band-eigenvalue differences, and spectral peaks are not automatically interchangeable.</li>
  <li><code>r_s</code> is a density parameter of the uniform electron gas. It supplies input to LDA and many electron-gas constraints but does not make an inhomogeneous crystal homogeneous.</li>
</ul>

Every teaching visualization in Part I is a finite implementation of a declared model. Deterministic checks validate formulas and interface behaviour; they do not validate real materials, production scientific codes, experiments, or undeclared continuum limits.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="进入 Part II 的桥接" titleEn="Bridge into Part II" eyebrow="Next">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Chapter 5 的均匀电子气经常出现在 LDA、exchange–correlation hole 和 response-derived approximations 中，但 Part II 的逻辑起点仍是 Chapter 6 的 Hohenberg–Kohn 与 Levy–Lieb density-space formulation。不能从 electron-gas formulas 直接跳到“DFT 已被证明”，也不能把 Hartree–Fock、Kohn–Sham 和 quasiparticle bands 视为同一种单粒子理论。

进入 Part II 前，应能回答：Hamiltonian 中哪些相互作用被保留；采用了哪一层 Born–Oppenheimer/independent-particle/mean-field approximation；周期体系的数据来自路径还是全 BZ；一个 energy、gap、DOS 或 response claim 依赖哪些归一化、极限和外部证据。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The uniform electron gas of Chapter 5 enters LDA, exchange–correlation-hole constraints, and response-derived approximations, but the logical starting point of Part II remains the Hohenberg–Kohn and Levy–Lieb density-space formulation of Chapter 6. Electron-gas formulas do not by themselves prove DFT, and Hartree–Fock, Kohn–Sham, and quasiparticle bands are not one interchangeable single-particle theory.

Before entering Part II, the reader should be able to state which interactions remain in the Hamiltonian, which Born–Oppenheimer/independent-particle/mean-field level is used, whether periodic data come from a path or the full BZ, and which normalizations, limits, and external evidence support an energy, gap, DOS, or response claim.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>
