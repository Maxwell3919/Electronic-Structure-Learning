# Part VII · 附录 | Appendices

Part VII 用于补足正文压缩的数学步骤、数值算法和统一约定。各附录按照 A→R 顺序独立完成；附录中的公式参考不能替代正文的物理叙事、计算收敛检查或科学接受标准。

Part VII supplies mathematical steps, numerical algorithms, and common conventions that the numbered chapters legitimately compress. Appendices are completed independently in A→R order. A reference formula does not replace the physical narrative, calculation-convergence checks, or scientific acceptance criteria of the main chapters.
