# 原子与赝势基础 | Atoms and Pseudopotentials

<BilingualSection titleZh="Part III 的位置" titleEn="Position of Part III" eyebrow="Reading orientation">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Part II 给出密度泛函与 Kohn–Sham 辅助体系的一般形式。Part III 把这些方程放入原子中心势：Chapter 10 建立全电子径向参考问题、自洽原子、相对论与开壳层处理；Chapter 11 再从散射等价和冻结芯出发，构造适用于分子与固体计算的赝势、ultrasoft 和 PAW 表示。

两章的验证层级不同。一个原子参考态收敛，只说明生成问题的起点可用；赝势还需原子构型、散射、分子/固体、基组截断与目标 observable 的独立测试。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Part II develops the general density-functional and Kohn–Sham framework. Part III places those equations in an atom-centred setting. Chapter 10 establishes the all-electron radial reference problem, self-consistent atoms, and relativistic and open-shell treatments. Chapter 11 then uses scattering equivalence and the frozen-core approximation to construct pseudopotential, ultrasoft, and PAW representations for molecular and solid-state calculations.

The validation levels differ. Convergence of an atomic reference state establishes only the starting generation problem. A pseudopotential additionally requires atomic-configuration, scattering, molecular or solid-state, basis-cutoff, and target-observable tests.
