# 原子与赝势基础 | Atoms and Pseudopotentials

<BilingualSection titleZh="Part III 的位置" titleEn="Position of Part III" eyebrow="Reading orientation">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Part II 给出密度泛函与 Kohn–Sham 辅助体系的一般形式。Part III 把这些方程放入原子中心势：Chapter 10 建立全电子径向参考问题、自洽原子、相对论与开壳层处理；Chapter 11 从散射等效和冻结芯出发，构造 norm-conserving、ultrasoft 与 PAW 表示，并建立实际数据集和目标 observable 的验证边界。

两章的验证层级不同。一个原子参考态收敛，只说明生成问题的起点可用；赝势还需原子构型、散射、ghost-state、分子/固体、基组截断与目标 observable 的独立测试。PAW 或 ultrasoft 的方法名称也不能替代具体数据集身份。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Part II develops the general density-functional and Kohn–Sham framework. Part III places those equations in an atom-centred setting. Chapter 10 establishes the all-electron radial reference problem, self-consistent atoms, and relativistic and open-shell treatments. Chapter 11 begins from scattering equivalence and the frozen-core approximation, constructs norm-conserving, ultrasoft, and PAW representations, and establishes validation boundaries for real data sets and target observables.

The validation levels differ. Convergence of an atomic reference state establishes only the starting generation problem. A pseudopotential additionally requires atomic-configuration, scattering, ghost-state, molecular or solid-state, basis-cutoff, and target-observable tests. A method label such as PAW or ultrasoft also does not replace the identity of the specific data set.

  </div>
