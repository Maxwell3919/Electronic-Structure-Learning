<h2 id="section-3-7">3.7 交换与关联 | Exchange and Correlation</h2>

<BilingualSection titleZh="pair density 是两体相互作用的自然对象" titleEn="Pair density is the natural object for two-body interaction" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】 §3.7 · pp. 74–77">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

电子—电子相互作用只含粒子对，因此其期望值由 spin-resolved pair density 决定。定义

$$
P(\mathbf x,\mathbf x')
=\left\langle
\sum_{i\ne j}
\delta(\mathbf x-\mathbf x_i)
\delta(\mathbf x'-\mathbf x_j)
\right\rangle,
$$

其中 $\mathbf x=(\mathbf r,\sigma)$。归一化关系为

$$
\int d\mathbf x'\,P(\mathbf x,\mathbf x')
=(N-1)n(\mathbf x).
$$

电子—电子 Coulomb 能为

$$
\langle\hat V_{ee}\rangle
=\frac12\iint
\frac{P(\mathbf x,\mathbf x')}
{|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|}\,d\mathbf x\,d\mathbf x'.
$$

定义 pair distribution

$$
g(\mathbf x,\mathbf x')
=\frac{P(\mathbf x,\mathbf x')}
{n(\mathbf x)n(\mathbf x')},
$$

则不相关、可分解的 classical probability 对应 $g=1$。真实费米体系即使 Hamiltonian 无相互作用，也因反对称性具有 $g\ne1$；因此“correlation function”在广义统计意义下包含 exchange，而 electronic-structure 术语常把 exchange 与剩余 Coulomb correlation 分开。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Electron–electron interaction contains particle pairs, so its expectation value is determined by the spin-resolved pair density

$$
P(\mathbf x,\mathbf x')
=\left\langle
\sum_{i\ne j}
\delta(\mathbf x-\mathbf x_i)
\delta(\mathbf x'-\mathbf x_j)
\right\rangle,
$$

where $\mathbf x=(\mathbf r,\sigma)$. It satisfies

$$
\int d\mathbf x'\,P(\mathbf x,\mathbf x')
=(N-1)n(\mathbf x),
$$

and

$$
\langle\hat V_{ee}\rangle
=\frac12\iint
\frac{P(\mathbf x,\mathbf x')}
{|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|}\,d\mathbf x\,d\mathbf x'.
$$

The normalized pair distribution

$$
g(\mathbf x,\mathbf x')
=\frac{P(\mathbf x,\mathbf x')}
{n(\mathbf x)n(\mathbf x')}
$$

is one for factorized classical probabilities. A fermionic system can have $g\ne1$ even without dynamical interaction because antisymmetry creates exchange correlations. Electronic-structure terminology therefore separates exchange from the remaining Coulomb correlation.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="条件 exchange-correlation hole 与 sum rule | Conditional exchange-correlation hole and sum rule"
  assumptions={[
    'n(x)>0 at the reference coordinate;',
    'fixed particle number N;',
    'pair density excludes i=j self-pairs.']}
>

给定一个电子位于 $\mathbf x$，其他电子的条件密度为

$$
n_{\mathrm{cond}}(\mathbf x'|\mathbf x)
=\frac{P(\mathbf x,\mathbf x')}{n(\mathbf x)}.
$$

相对于无条件密度，定义 exchange-correlation hole

$$
n_{xc}(\mathbf x,\mathbf x')
=n_{\mathrm{cond}}(\mathbf x'|\mathbf x)-n(\mathbf x').
$$

积分得

$$
\int d\mathbf x'\,n_{xc}(\mathbf x,\mathbf x')
=(N-1)-N=-1.
$$

因此每个参考电子周围恰好少一个电子的条件概率。hole 可以在某些区域为正，表示从近处移走的概率在远处重新堆积；只有总积分固定为 $-1$。电子—电子能可写为

$$
\langle V_{ee}\rangle
=E_H[n]+\frac12\int d\mathbf x\,n(\mathbf x)
\int d\mathbf x'
\frac{n_{xc}(\mathbf x,\mathbf x')}
{|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|}.
$$

该式只分解 interaction energy。完整 correlation energy 还包含相关导致的 kinetic-energy change。

<p lang="en">The conditional density integrates to $N-1$, while the unconditional density integrates to $N$, so the conditional exchange-correlation hole integrates exactly to $-1$. The hole representation separates the Hartree interaction from the nonclassical pair correction, but the full correlation energy also contains a kinetic contribution.</p>

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="单 determinant 的 exchange hole" titleEn="The exchange hole of one determinant">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

对一个 Slater determinant，一体 spin-density matrix 为

$$
\gamma_\sigma(\mathbf r,\mathbf r')
=\sum_{i\in\mathrm{occ},\sigma}
\psi_{i\sigma}(\mathbf r)
\psi_{i\sigma}^*(\mathbf r').
$$

pair density 分解为

$$
P_{HF}(\mathbf r\sigma,\mathbf r'\sigma')
=n_\sigma(\mathbf r)n_{\sigma'}(\mathbf r')
-\delta_{\sigma\sigma'}
|\gamma_\sigma(\mathbf r,\mathbf r')|^2.
$$

于是条件 exchange hole 为

$$
n_x(\mathbf r\sigma,\mathbf r'\sigma')
=-\delta_{\sigma\sigma'}
\frac{|\gamma_\sigma(\mathbf r,\mathbf r')|^2}
{n_\sigma(\mathbf r)}.
$$

它只作用于同自旋，处处非正，并满足 $\sum_{\sigma'}\int n_x=-1$。在 $\mathbf r'=\mathbf r$ 且同自旋时，pair probability 为零，体现 Pauli exclusion。exchange energy 是参考电子与其 exchange hole 的 Coulomb 相互作用：

$$
E_x=\frac12\sum_{\sigma\sigma'}
\iint d^3r\,d^3r'\,
\frac{n_\sigma(\mathbf r)n_x(\mathbf r\sigma,\mathbf r'\sigma')}
{|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|}.
$$

由于 $n_x\le0$，Coulomb exchange energy 非正。一个电子体系中 hole 等于 $-n(\mathbf r')$，精确抵消 Hartree self-interaction，即使物理上没有第二个电子可“交换”。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For a Slater determinant, the spin-resolved one-body density matrix is

$$
\gamma_\sigma(\mathbf r,\mathbf r')
=\sum_{i\in\mathrm{occ},\sigma}
\psi_{i\sigma}(\mathbf r)
\psi_{i\sigma}^*(\mathbf r').
$$

The pair density becomes

$$
P_{HF}(\mathbf r\sigma,\mathbf r'\sigma')
=n_\sigma(\mathbf r)n_{\sigma'}(\mathbf r')
-\delta_{\sigma\sigma'}
|\gamma_\sigma(\mathbf r,\mathbf r')|^2.
$$

Thus

$$
n_x(\mathbf r\sigma,\mathbf r'\sigma')
=-\delta_{\sigma\sigma'}
\frac{|\gamma_\sigma(\mathbf r,\mathbf r')|^2}
{n_\sigma(\mathbf r)}.
$$

The exchange hole acts only between equal spins, is nonpositive, and integrates to minus one electron. At equal coordinates, same-spin pair probability vanishes. Its Coulomb interaction with the reference electron gives nonpositive exchange energy. In a one-electron system the hole is $-n(\mathbf r')$ and cancels Hartree self-interaction even though there is no second electron to exchange physically.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="correlation hole 重新分布 exchange hole" titleEn="The correlation hole redistributes the exchange hole">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

把完整 hole 分成

$$
n_{xc}=n_x+n_c.
$$

由于 $n_{xc}$ 和 $n_x$ 都积分为 $-1$，correlation hole 满足

$$
\int d\mathbf x'\,n_c(\mathbf x,\mathbf x')=0.
$$

它不是额外移走一个电子，而是在空间和自旋通道之间重新分布已有的条件概率。opposite-spin 电子没有 Pauli hole，短程 Coulomb avoidance 通常使其 correlation 更显著；same-spin 电子已经被 exchange hole 分开，但仍存在剩余 correlation。

对 ground state，以最佳单 determinant Hartree–Fock 能量为参考，传统 quantum-chemistry correlation energy 定义为

$$
E_c^{HF}=E_{\mathrm{exact}}-E_{HF}\le0.
$$

这里的符号来自 variational principle：扩大波函数空间不能提高 ground-state 最低能量。对激发态能量差，correlation correction 可以正也可以负，因为 ground 和 excited state 的 lowering 不同。DFT correlation 使用保持目标 exact density 的非相互作用参考，和 HF reference density 一般不同；两种 $E_c$ 定义不可静默混用。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Write the full hole as

$$
n_{xc}=n_x+n_c.
$$

Since both $n_{xc}$ and $n_x$ integrate to $-1$, the correlation hole satisfies

$$
\int d\mathbf x'\,n_c(\mathbf x,\mathbf x')=0.
$$

It does not remove an additional electron; it redistributes conditional probability in space and spin. Opposite-spin electrons lack a Pauli hole and often show strong short-range Coulomb avoidance. Same-spin electrons are already separated by exchange but still retain additional correlation.

With the best single-determinant Hartree–Fock energy as reference, the traditional ground-state correlation energy is

$$
E_c^{HF}=E_{\mathrm{exact}}-E_{HF}\le0.
$$

The sign follows from the variational principle. Correlation corrections to excitation energies may have either sign because the ground and excited states are lowered by different amounts. DFT correlation uses a noninteracting reference constrained to the exact target density, generally different from the Hartree–Fock reference density; the two definitions must not be silently mixed.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="density-fixed adiabatic connection 中的 E_xc | $E_{xc}$ in a density-fixed adiabatic connection"
  assumptions={[
    'for every λ there exists an external potential giving the same density n;',
    'the interaction is λVee and the branch remains the ground state;',
    'Hartree atomic units.']}
>

定义

$$
\hat H_\lambda=\hat T+\lambda\hat V_{ee}
+\sum_i v_\lambda(\mathbf r_i),
$$

并选择 $v_\lambda$ 使所有 $\lambda$ 的 ground-state density 都等于物理密度 $n$。普适泛函

$$
F_\lambda[n]
=\min_{\Psi\to n}
\langle\Psi|\hat T+\lambda\hat V_{ee}|\Psi\rangle
$$

满足

$$
\frac{dF_\lambda[n]}{d\lambda}
=\langle\Psi_\lambda[n]|\hat V_{ee}|\Psi_\lambda[n]\rangle.
$$

积分得到

$$
F_1[n]-F_0[n]
=\int_0^1 d\lambda\,
\langle\hat V_{ee}\rangle_\lambda.
$$

由于 $F_0[n]=T_s[n]$，并减去 Hartree 能，

$$
E_{xc}[n]
=\int_0^1d\lambda
\left(\langle\hat V_{ee}\rangle_\lambda-E_H[n]\right).
$$

用 coupling-constant averaged hole 表示：

$$
E_{xc}[n]
=\frac12\int d\mathbf r\,n(\mathbf r)
\int d\mathbf r'
\frac{\bar n_{xc}(\mathbf r,\mathbf r')}
{|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|},
$$

$$
\bar n_{xc}=\int_0^1n_{xc}^{\lambda}\,d\lambda.
$$

该式把 kinetic correlation 隐含进 $\lambda$ 平均的 pair response，而不是声称 $E_{xc}$ 只有物理 $\lambda=1$ 的势能 hole。

<p lang="en">Holding density fixed while scaling interaction defines the DFT adiabatic connection. The coupling-constant averaged hole incorporates both exchange and correlation, including the kinetic-correlation contribution through the path. Using only the physical $\lambda=1$ pair hole gives the interaction correction, not automatically the full exchange-correlation energy.</p>

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="dynamic 与 static correlation" titleEn="Dynamic and static correlation">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Dynamic correlation 指电子在一个主 determinant 周围因瞬时 Coulomb avoidance 产生的许多小振幅修正，常可由 perturbative、coupled-cluster 或局域 correlation 方法系统处理。static 或 nondynamic correlation 出现在多个 determinants 近简并时，例如拉伸化学键、过渡金属多重态或 Mott 极限；此时单 determinant reference 本身不稳定，低阶 perturbation 可能发散或产生错误对称性。

“强关联”不是由某个元素标签、DOS 峰或 DFT+$U$ 使用自动定义。需要结合 natural occupations、多个近简并解、谱权重转移、质量重整化、局域矩与实验温度依赖等证据，并明确目标 observable。不同方法对 ground-state energy、spectral function、finite-temperature order 和 real-time dynamics 的能力并不相同。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Dynamic correlation consists of many small-amplitude adjustments around one dominant determinant due to instantaneous Coulomb avoidance and is often accessible to perturbative, coupled-cluster, or local-correlation methods. Static or nondynamic correlation arises when several determinants are near degenerate, as in bond breaking, transition-metal multiplets, or a Mott limit. The one-determinant reference then becomes qualitatively inadequate and low-order perturbation may diverge or select the wrong symmetry.

“Strong correlation” is not defined automatically by an element label, a DOS peak, or use of DFT+$U$. Evidence may include fractional natural occupations, multiple near-degenerate solutions, spectral-weight transfer, mass renormalization, local moments, and temperature dependence, all tied to a stated observable. Methods have different capabilities for ground-state energy, spectral functions, finite-temperature order, and real-time dynamics.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>
