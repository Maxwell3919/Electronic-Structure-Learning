<h2 id="section-3-4">3.4 广义力定理与耦合常数积分 | Generalized Force Theorem and Coupling Constant Integration</h2>

<BilingualSection titleZh="任意 Hamiltonian 参数都有共轭广义力" titleEn="Every Hamiltonian parameter has a conjugate generalized force" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】 §3.4 · pp. 67–68">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

核位置只是 Hamiltonian 参数的一种。令 $\lambda$ 连续改变外势、相互作用强度、应变、磁场或某个模型耦合，对每个 $\lambda$ 都取同一目标本征支 $|\Psi_\lambda\rangle$，则

$$
\frac{dE_\lambda}{d\lambda}
=\left\langle\Psi_\lambda\left|
\frac{\partial\hat H_\lambda}{\partial\lambda}
\right|\Psi_\lambda\right\rangle.
$$

共轭广义力可定义为 $X_\lambda=-dE/d\lambda$，具体符号取决于物理约定。有限能量差由沿路径积分得到：

$$
E(\lambda_2)-E(\lambda_1)
=\int_{\lambda_1}^{\lambda_2}
\left\langle\frac{\partial\hat H_\lambda}{\partial\lambda}\right\rangle_\lambda d\lambda.
$$

这类路径常称 coupling-constant integration 或 adiabatic connection。这里“adiabatic”表示体系沿同一本征态支连续跟随，不是实际时间动力学必然缓慢，也不保证路径没有简并、相变或 hysteresis。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A nuclear coordinate is only one possible Hamiltonian parameter. Let $\lambda$ continuously change an external potential, interaction strength, strain, magnetic field, or model coupling. Following the same target eigenstate branch $|\Psi_\lambda\rangle$ gives

$$
\frac{dE_\lambda}{d\lambda}
=\left\langle\Psi_\lambda\left|
\frac{\partial\hat H_\lambda}{\partial\lambda}
\right|\Psi_\lambda\right\rangle.
$$

A conjugate generalized force may be defined as $X_\lambda=-dE/d\lambda$, with sign depending on physical convention. A finite energy difference is obtained by path integration:

$$
E(\lambda_2)-E(\lambda_1)
=\int_{\lambda_1}^{\lambda_2}
\left\langle\frac{\partial\hat H_\lambda}{\partial\lambda}\right\rangle_\lambda d\lambda.
$$

Such a path is often called coupling-constant integration or an adiabatic connection. “Adiabatic” here means continuous following of the same eigenstate branch, not necessarily a real slow-time process, and it does not guarantee absence of degeneracies, phase transitions, or hysteresis.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="把电子相互作用从 0 缩放到 1" titleEn="Scaling electron interaction from 0 to 1">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

考虑

$$
\hat H_\lambda=\hat T+\hat V_{\mathrm{ext}}+\lambda\hat V_{ee},
\qquad 0\le\lambda\le1.
$$

则

$$
\frac{dE_\lambda}{d\lambda}
=\langle\Psi_\lambda|\hat V_{ee}|\Psi_\lambda\rangle,
$$

从非相互作用到完全相互作用的能量变化为

$$
E_1-E_0=\int_0^1
\langle\hat V_{ee}\rangle_\lambda\,d\lambda.
$$

Martin 的这一简单路径保持外势不变，因此中间态的密度一般随 $\lambda$ 改变。DFT 中更常用 density-fixed adiabatic connection：随 $\lambda$ 同时调节外势 $V_\lambda(\mathbf r)$，使 $n_\lambda(\mathbf r)=n(\mathbf r)$。两条路径的 integrand、分解和物理解释不同，不能只因为积分端点相同就混用。

该方法的代价是需要非物理中间耦合的态或响应。数值上还需要选择 quadrature、处理端点曲率并确认积分路径没有跳到不同局域解。单个 $\lambda=0$ 或 $1$ 的值通常不足以恢复积分。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Consider

$$
\hat H_\lambda=\hat T+\hat V_{\mathrm{ext}}+\lambda\hat V_{ee},
\qquad 0\le\lambda\le1.
$$

Then

$$
\frac{dE_\lambda}{d\lambda}
=\langle\Psi_\lambda|\hat V_{ee}|\Psi_\lambda\rangle,
$$

and

$$
E_1-E_0=\int_0^1
\langle\hat V_{ee}\rangle_\lambda\,d\lambda.
$$

Martin's direct path keeps the external potential fixed, so the density generally changes with $\lambda$. The DFT adiabatic connection more commonly adjusts $V_\lambda(\mathbf r)$ so that $n_\lambda(\mathbf r)=n(\mathbf r)$. The two paths have different integrands, decompositions, and interpretations and must not be conflated merely because their endpoints are related.

The price of coupling-constant integration is the need for states or responses at unphysical intermediate coupling. Numerically, quadrature, endpoint curvature, and branch following must be checked. A value at only $\lambda=0$ or $1$ rarely determines the integral.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="补充推导 | Supplemental derivation: finite-temperature thermodynamic integration"
  assumptions={[
    'canonical ensemble at fixed N,V,T;',
    'Hamiltonian depends smoothly on λ;',
    'the equilibrium density matrix is used at every λ.']}
>

Helmholtz 自由能为

$$
F_\lambda=-\beta^{-1}\ln Z_\lambda,
\qquad
Z_\lambda=\operatorname{Tr}e^{-\beta\hat H_\lambda}.
$$

对 $\lambda$ 求导。即使 $\hat H$ 与 $\partial_\lambda\hat H$ 不对易，Duhamel identity 与 trace 的循环性给出

$$
\frac{dZ_\lambda}{d\lambda}
=-\beta\operatorname{Tr}
\left[e^{-\beta\hat H_\lambda}
\frac{\partial\hat H_\lambda}{\partial\lambda}
\right].
$$

因此

$$
\boxed{
\frac{dF_\lambda}{d\lambda}
=\operatorname{Tr}
\left(\hat\rho_\lambda
\frac{\partial\hat H_\lambda}{\partial\lambda}
\right)
=\left\langle\frac{\partial\hat H_\lambda}{\partial\lambda}\right\rangle_{T,\lambda}}
$$

以及

$$
F_1-F_0=\int_0^1
\left\langle\partial_\lambda\hat H_\lambda\right\rangle_{T,\lambda}\,d\lambda.
$$

这正是 classical/quantum thermodynamic integration 的共同结构。若采样在一阶相变附近滞留于亚稳 basin，积分得到的是路径和采样历史依赖的 branch free energy，而非自动得到平衡自由能。

<p lang="en">Differentiating the canonical partition function yields $dF/d\lambda=\langle\partial_\lambda H\rangle_T$. The formula does not require $H$ and its derivative to commute because trace cyclicity removes the operator-ordering complication. In practice, metastability and incomplete sampling can turn the integral into a branch-dependent free energy.</p>

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="力定理近似与冻结势思想" titleEn="Force-theorem approximations and frozen-potential ideas">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

严格广义力公式要求每个 $\lambda$ 上的态达到相应变分驻点。实际电子结构中常用冻结密度或冻结势近似：只改变 Hamiltonian 的显式一体部分，使用参考态估算能量差、磁各向异性或小结构扰动。这样可以降低成本，但被忽略的自洽响应通常从二阶开始只在参考态足够接近、能量泛函真正驻定且占据没有非解析变化时成立。

如果扰动导致能级交叉、金属占据改变、强电荷转移、磁态切换或软模，自洽重排可能是一阶重要量。冻结势结果应与若干完全自洽点比较，并明确它估计的是 band-energy difference、总能 difference 还是某个广义力。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The exact generalized-force formula requires a variational stationary state at every $\lambda$. Practical electronic-structure work often uses frozen-density or frozen-potential approximations, changing only the explicit one-body Hamiltonian and estimating structural, magnetic-anisotropy, or other small energy differences from a reference state. Neglected self-consistent response begins at higher order only when the reference is truly stationary, nearby, and occupations vary smoothly.

Level crossings, metallic occupation changes, strong charge transfer, magnetic-state switching, and soft modes can make self-consistent rearrangement first-order important. A frozen-potential result should be compared with selected fully self-consistent points and should state whether it estimates a band-energy difference, a total-energy difference, or a generalized force.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>
