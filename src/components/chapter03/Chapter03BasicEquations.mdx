<h2 id="section-3-1">3.1 相互作用电子与原子核的基本方程 | Basic Equations for Interacting Electrons and Nuclei</h2>

<BilingualSection titleZh="电子—核体系的非相对论 Coulomb Hamiltonian" titleEn="The nonrelativistic Coulomb Hamiltonian for electrons and nuclei" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】 §3.1 · pp. 60–64">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

对 $N_e$ 个电子和 $N_n$ 个原子核，固定总粒子数的非相对论 Hamiltonian 可分为电子动能、电子—核吸引、电子—电子排斥、核动能和核—核排斥：

$$
\begin{aligned}
\hat H={}&-\sum_{i=1}^{N_e}\frac{\hbar^2}{2m_e}\nabla_i^2
-\sum_{iI}\frac{Z_Ie^2}{4\pi\epsilon_0|\mathbf r_i-\mathbf R_I|}
+\frac12\sum_{i\ne j}\frac{e^2}{4\pi\epsilon_0|\mathbf r_i-\mathbf r_j|}\\
&-\sum_{I=1}^{N_n}\frac{\hbar^2}{2M_I}\nabla_I^2
+\frac12\sum_{I\ne J}\frac{Z_IZ_Je^2}{4\pi\epsilon_0|\mathbf R_I-\mathbf R_J|}.
\end{aligned}
$$

电子用小写指标 $i,j$，核用大写指标 $I,J$。每一个 Coulomb 双重求和都带 $1/2$，用于消除有序对 $(i,j)$ 与 $(j,i)$ 的重复计数。电子—核项没有 $1/2$，因为电子集合和核集合不同。

该 Hamiltonian 是后续近似的共同起点。自旋—轨道、外磁场的 vector potential、Zeeman 项、相对论效应和量子电动力学修正并未包含；它们需要在目标现象依赖时显式加入。对重元素拓扑、磁各向异性或芯层谱，忽略这些项可能改变定性结论。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For $N_e$ electrons and $N_n$ nuclei at fixed total particle number, the nonrelativistic Hamiltonian separates into electronic kinetic energy, electron–nuclear attraction, electron–electron repulsion, nuclear kinetic energy, and nuclear–nuclear repulsion:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\hat H={}&-\sum_{i=1}^{N_e}\frac{\hbar^2}{2m_e}\nabla_i^2
-\sum_{iI}\frac{Z_Ie^2}{4\pi\epsilon_0|\mathbf r_i-\mathbf R_I|}
+\frac12\sum_{i\ne j}\frac{e^2}{4\pi\epsilon_0|\mathbf r_i-\mathbf r_j|}\\
&-\sum_{I=1}^{N_n}\frac{\hbar^2}{2M_I}\nabla_I^2
+\frac12\sum_{I\ne J}\frac{Z_IZ_Je^2}{4\pi\epsilon_0|\mathbf R_I-\mathbf R_J|}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Lower-case indices denote electrons and upper-case indices denote nuclei. Each Coulomb double sum carries $1/2$ to remove duplicate ordered pairs. The electron–nuclear term has no such factor because the two index sets are distinct.

This Hamiltonian is the common starting point for later approximations. Spin–orbit coupling, vector potentials for external magnetic fields, Zeeman terms, relativistic corrections, and quantum-electrodynamic effects are not included. They must be added when the target phenomenon depends on them. For heavy-element topology, magnetic anisotropy, or core spectroscopy, omission can change the qualitative result.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="Born–Oppenheimer 分离" titleEn="Born–Oppenheimer separation">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

五项中唯一普遍含小参数的是核动能中的 $1/M_I$。令 $M_I\to\infty$，核动能首先被忽略，核位置成为电子 Hamiltonian 的参数：

$$
\hat H_e(\{\mathbf R_I\})
=\hat T_e+\hat V_{\mathrm{ext}}+\hat V_{ee}+E_{II}.
$$

在 Hartree 原子单位中，

$$
\hat T_e=-\frac12\sum_i\nabla_i^2,
\qquad
\hat V_{\mathrm{ext}}=\sum_i V_{\mathrm{ext}}(\mathbf r_i),
\qquad
\hat V_{ee}=\frac12\sum_{i\ne j}\frac1{|\mathbf r_i-\mathbf r_j|}.
$$

$E_{II}$ 对电子波函数只是加法常数，却是比较结构、求力和相稳定性时不可缺少的总能项。若使用 nonlocal pseudopotential，$V_{\mathrm{ext}}$ 不再是简单乘法势，但“固定核参数下的电子问题”这一分解仍成立。

Born–Oppenheimer 近似没有删除核自由度。它先求每组核坐标的电子本征值面 $E_a(\mathbf R)$，再让核在这些势能面上运动。声子、结构优化和从头算分子动力学通常从基态面开始；电子—声子耦合、非绝热动力学和锥形交叉则需要考虑不同电子面之间的导数耦合。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The only generally small parameter among the five terms is $1/M_I$ in the nuclear kinetic energy. Taking $M_I\to\infty$ first removes nuclear kinetic energy and turns nuclear positions into parameters of the electronic Hamiltonian:

$$
\hat H_e(\{\mathbf R_I\})
=\hat T_e+\hat V_{\mathrm{ext}}+\hat V_{ee}+E_{II}.
$$

In Hartree atomic units,

$$
\hat T_e=-\frac12\sum_i\nabla_i^2,
\qquad
\hat V_{\mathrm{ext}}=\sum_i V_{\mathrm{ext}}(\mathbf r_i),
\qquad
\hat V_{ee}=\frac12\sum_{i\ne j}\frac1{|\mathbf r_i-\mathbf r_j|}.
$$

$E_{II}$ is an additive constant for the electronic wavefunction, but it is indispensable for structural energies, forces, and phase stability. With nonlocal pseudopotentials, $V_{\mathrm{ext}}$ is no longer a simple multiplicative potential, while the fixed-nuclear-parameter decomposition remains valid.

Born–Oppenheimer separation does not remove nuclear degrees of freedom. It first defines electronic eigenvalue surfaces $E_a(\mathbf R)$ for each nuclear geometry and then lets the nuclei move on those surfaces. Phonons, relaxation, and ab initio molecular dynamics commonly start from the ground-state surface. Electron–phonon coupling, nonadiabatic dynamics, and conical intersections require derivative coupling among electronic surfaces.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="补充推导 | Supplemental derivation: adiabatic expansion and nonadiabatic couplings"
  assumptions={[
    '电子 Hamiltonian 在每组核坐标上具有完备正交本征态；',
    '暂不处理相对论和外磁场的规范结构；',
    '核坐标可作为慢变量展开。',
  ]}
>

把总波函数展开为瞬时电子态

$$
\Psi(\mathbf x,\mathbf R)
=\sum_a \chi_a(\mathbf R)\Phi_a(\mathbf x;\mathbf R),
\qquad
\hat H_e(\mathbf R)\Phi_a=E_a(\mathbf R)\Phi_a.
$$

核动能作用于乘积时同时微分 $\chi_a$ 和 $\Phi_a$：

$$
\nabla_I^2(\chi_a\Phi_a)
=(\nabla_I^2\chi_a)\Phi_a
+2(\nabla_I\chi_a)\cdot(\nabla_I\Phi_a)
+\chi_a\nabla_I^2\Phi_a.
$$

投影到 $\Phi_b$ 后得到耦合核方程。非对角项含

$$
\mathbf d_{ba}^{(I)}(\mathbf R)
=\langle\Phi_b|\nabla_I\Phi_a\rangle,
\qquad
D_{ba}^{(I)}(\mathbf R)
=\langle\Phi_b|\nabla_I^2\Phi_a\rangle.
$$

绝热近似忽略 $b\ne a$ 的导数耦合，使核主要在单个 $E_a(\mathbf R)$ 面上运动。该近似在电子能隙大、核慢且没有近简并时较好；在 avoided crossing、金属低能连续谱、强电子—声子耦合或超快激发中需要重新检查。

<p lang="en">Expanding the total state in instantaneous electronic eigenstates and applying nuclear kinetic energy produces first- and second-derivative couplings. The adiabatic approximation neglects off-diagonal couplings and propagates nuclei on one electronic energy surface. It is controlled when electronic gaps are large and nuclear motion is slow, and becomes delicate near avoided crossings, low-energy continua, strong electron–phonon coupling, or ultrafast driving.</p>

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="多体 Schrödinger 方程、期望值与密度" titleEn="Many-body Schrödinger equation, expectation values, and density">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

电子多体态满足

$$
i\hbar\frac{\partial}{\partial t}|\Psi(t)\rangle
=\hat H_e|\Psi(t)\rangle.
$$

定态写成 $|\Psi(t)\rangle=e^{-iEt/\hbar}|\Psi\rangle$，并满足 $\hat H_e|\Psi\rangle=E|\Psi\rangle$。对任意算符 $\hat O$，归一化态的期望值为

$$
\langle\hat O\rangle=\langle\Psi|\hat O|\Psi\rangle.
$$

一体密度算符

$$
\hat n(\mathbf r)=\sum_{i=1}^{N_e}\delta(\mathbf r-\mathbf r_i)
$$

给出

$$
n(\mathbf r)=\langle\Psi|\hat n(\mathbf r)|\Psi\rangle,
\qquad
\int n(\mathbf r)\,d^3r=N_e.
$$

总能可组织为

$$
E=\langle\hat T_e\rangle+\langle\hat V_{ee}\rangle
+\int V_{\mathrm{ext}}(\mathbf r)n(\mathbf r)\,d^3r+E_{II}.
$$

外势项只需要一体密度；电子—电子项一般需要 pair density 或更高层多体信息。这个差别正是“密度很有用”与“多体问题仍然困难”同时成立的原因。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The electronic many-body state obeys

$$
i\hbar\frac{\partial}{\partial t}|\Psi(t)\rangle
=\hat H_e|\Psi(t)\rangle.
$$

A stationary state has $|\Psi(t)\rangle=e^{-iEt/\hbar}|\Psi\rangle$ and satisfies $\hat H_e|\Psi\rangle=E|\Psi\rangle$. For a normalized state, the expectation value of an operator is

$$
\langle\hat O\rangle=\langle\Psi|\hat O|\Psi\rangle.
$$

The one-body density operator

$$
\hat n(\mathbf r)=\sum_{i=1}^{N_e}\delta(\mathbf r-\mathbf r_i)
$$

gives

$$
n(\mathbf r)=\langle\Psi|\hat n(\mathbf r)|\Psi\rangle,
\qquad
\int n(\mathbf r)\,d^3r=N_e.
$$

The total energy can be organized as

$$
E=\langle\hat T_e\rangle+\langle\hat V_{ee}\rangle
+\int V_{\mathrm{ext}}(\mathbf r)n(\mathbf r)\,d^3r+E_{II}.
$$

The external-potential term requires only the one-body density. The electron–electron term generally requires a pair density or higher many-body information. This is why density can be extremely useful while the many-body problem remains difficult.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="Rayleigh–Ritz 变分原理 | Rayleigh–Ritz variational principle"
  assumptions={[
    'Hamiltonian 为自伴算符并在目标子空间有下界；',
    '试探态满足归一化、反对称性和目标守恒量；',
    '简并情形按整个基态子空间处理。',
  ]}
>

对归一化试探态，能量泛函为

$$
\mathcal E[\Psi]
=\frac{\langle\Psi|\hat H|\Psi\rangle}
{\langle\Psi|\Psi\rangle}.
$$

引入归一化乘子 $E$，对 bra 作独立变分：

$$
\delta\left[
\langle\Psi|\hat H|\Psi\rangle
-E(\langle\Psi|\Psi\rangle-1)
\right]=0.
$$

得到

$$
\langle\delta\Psi|(\hat H-E)|\Psi\rangle=0.
$$

由于 $\langle\delta\Psi|$ 在允许空间任意，必须有

$$
\hat H|\Psi\rangle=E|\Psi\rangle.
$$

任一本征态是能量泛函的驻点；只有最低本征值是全局最小值。激发态在无额外正交约束时通常不是最小值，而是 saddle point。数值优化若限制在有限 ansatz 中，只证明该 ansatz 流形上的驻点或极小值。

<p lang="en">Stationarity of the normalized energy quotient gives the many-body eigenvalue equation. Every eigenstate is stationary, but only the lowest eigenvalue is the global minimum. Excited states are generally saddle points unless orthogonality or symmetry restrictions are imposed. A variational calculation in a finite ansatz establishes stationarity only within that ansatz.</p>

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="本节的证据上限" titleEn="Evidence ceiling of this section" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

写出基本 Hamiltonian 不等于能够求解真实材料。Born–Oppenheimer 电子能量面不自动包含核量子效应或非绝热跃迁；一个变分能量很低不证明试探空间完备；一个 SCF 解也不自动是全多体 Hamiltonian 的基态。后续所有近似都应明确说明它限制了波函数、密度、轨道、相关函数还是响应函数中的哪一部分。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Writing the fundamental Hamiltonian does not make a material solvable. A Born–Oppenheimer surface does not automatically include nuclear quantum effects or nonadiabatic transitions. A low variational energy does not establish completeness of the trial space, and an SCF solution is not automatically the ground state of the full many-body Hamiltonian. Every later approximation should state whether it restricts the wavefunction, density, orbitals, correlation functions, or response functions.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>
