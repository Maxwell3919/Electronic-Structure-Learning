<h2 id="section-3-6">3.6 独立电子近似 | Independent-Electron Approximations</h2>

<BilingualSection titleZh="非相互作用 many-body problem 分解为一体本征问题" titleEn="A noninteracting many-body problem separates into one-body eigenproblems" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】 §3.6 · pp. 69–74">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

若 Hamiltonian 可写成一体算符之和

$$
\hat H_0=\sum_{i=1}^{N}\hat h(i),
\qquad
\hat h\phi_p(\mathbf x)=\varepsilon_p\phi_p(\mathbf x),
$$

则多体能量是被占据轨道本征值之和。对费米子，多体态必须反对称，最简单构造是 Slater determinant：

$$
\Phi(\mathbf x_1,\ldots,\mathbf x_N)
=\frac1{\sqrt{N!}}
\det[\phi_i(\mathbf x_j)].
$$

如果 spin-orbitals 正交归一，determinant 自动归一并在交换两行或两电子坐标时变号。Pauli 原理表现为同一 spin-orbital 不能重复占据。

“独立电子”是 Hamiltonian 的结构，不是关于真实 Coulomb 相互作用不存在的实验陈述。Kohn–Sham、Hartree、Hartree–Fock、tight-binding 和 quasiparticle 模型都可产生形式上的一体方程，但它们的有效算符、能量泛函和本征值含义不同。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

If the Hamiltonian is a sum of one-body operators,

$$
\hat H_0=\sum_{i=1}^{N}\hat h(i),
\qquad
\hat h\phi_p(\mathbf x)=\varepsilon_p\phi_p(\mathbf x),
$$

many-body energies are sums of occupied one-particle eigenvalues. Fermionic states must be antisymmetric, and the simplest construction is a Slater determinant:

$$
\Phi(\mathbf x_1,\ldots,\mathbf x_N)
=\frac1{\sqrt{N!}}
\det[\phi_i(\mathbf x_j)].
$$

For orthonormal spin-orbitals, the determinant is normalized and changes sign when two electron coordinates are exchanged. Pauli exclusion appears as the impossibility of occupying the same spin-orbital twice.

“Independent electrons” describes the mathematical structure of a Hamiltonian, not an experimental statement that real Coulomb interaction is absent. Kohn–Sham, Hartree, Hartree–Fock, tight-binding, and quasiparticle models can all produce one-particle-looking equations, but their effective operators, energy functionals, and eigenvalue meanings differ.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="Fermi–Dirac 占据与一体密度" titleEn="Fermi–Dirac occupations and the one-body density">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

在 grand-canonical 非相互作用费米体系中，每个 spin-orbital 的平均占据为

$$
f_p=\frac1{e^{\beta(\varepsilon_p-\mu)}+1}.
$$

内部能、粒子数和一体密度为

$$
U=\sum_p f_p\varepsilon_p,
\qquad
N=\sum_p f_p,
$$

$$
n(\mathbf r)=\sum_p f_p\sum_\sigma
|\phi_p(\mathbf r,\sigma)|^2.
$$

零温时 $f_p$ 成为阶跃，除简并或部分占据边界外，低于化学势的态占据、高于化学势的态空置。金属数值 smearing 常使用相同形式帮助 Brillouin-zone 积分；此时输入 smearing 参数不必等于真实电子温度。

对 boson，同一推导把分母的 $+1$ 改为 $-1$。费米分布的符号来自每个 fermionic orbital 只有 0/1 两种占据，而不是来自具体空间轨道形状。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

In a grand-canonical noninteracting Fermi system, the mean occupation of spin-orbital $p$ is

$$
f_p=\frac1{e^{\beta(\varepsilon_p-\mu)}+1}.
$$

The internal energy, particle number, and one-body density are

$$
U=\sum_p f_p\varepsilon_p,
\qquad
N=\sum_p f_p,
$$

$$
n(\mathbf r)=\sum_p f_p\sum_\sigma
|\phi_p(\mathbf r,\sigma)|^2.
$$

At zero temperature, the distribution becomes a step except at degeneracies or partial-occupation boundaries. Metallic numerical smearing often uses the same form to regularize Brillouin-zone integration; the smearing parameter need not equal a physical electron temperature.

For bosons, the corresponding denominator contains $-1$. The fermionic sign follows from allowing only occupations 0 and 1 in each orbital, not from the spatial shape of the orbital.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="补充推导 | Supplemental derivation: Fermi–Dirac occupation from one orbital"
  assumptions={[
    'grand-canonical ensemble of noninteracting fermions;',
    'each spin-orbital can contain n=0 or 1 particle;',
    'orbital energies do not depend on occupations.']}
>

单个轨道 $p$ 的 grand partition factor 为

$$
\mathcal Z_p
=\sum_{n_p=0}^{1}
 e^{-\beta n_p(\varepsilon_p-\mu)}
=1+e^{-\beta(\varepsilon_p-\mu)}.
$$

平均占据

$$
\langle n_p\rangle
=\frac{0+e^{-\beta(\varepsilon_p-\mu)}}
{1+e^{-\beta(\varepsilon_p-\mu)}}
=\frac1{e^{\beta(\varepsilon_p-\mu)}+1}.
$$

独立性使总 partition function 因子化：$\mathcal Z=\prod_p\mathcal Z_p$。若有效轨道能随占据或密度自洽变化，这个因子化只在固定有效 Hamiltonian 的一步上成立；完整 SCF 自由能还包含 double-counting 或 functional terms。

<p lang="en">One fermionic orbital has only empty and occupied grand-canonical states, giving a two-term partition factor and the Fermi–Dirac expectation. Factorization over orbitals requires a fixed noninteracting Hamiltonian. In a self-consistent effective theory, the complete free energy generally contains additional functional and double-counting terms.</p>

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="Hartree 近似：轨道乘积与平均 Coulomb 场" titleEn="Hartree approximation: orbital product and mean Coulomb field">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Hartree 近似限制多体态为轨道乘积

$$
\Psi_H(\mathbf x_1,\ldots,\mathbf x_N)
=\prod_{i=1}^{N}\phi_i(\mathbf x_i),
$$

没有对全体电子坐标反对称化。变分得到每个轨道在外势和总密度 Hartree 势中的一体方程：

$$
\left[-\frac12\nabla^2+V_{\mathrm{ext}}(\mathbf r)
+V_H(\mathbf r)\right]\phi_i(\mathbf x)
=\varepsilon_i\phi_i(\mathbf x),
$$

$$
V_H(\mathbf r)=\int
\frac{n(\mathbf r')}{|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|}\,d^3r'.
$$

若 Hartree sum 包含 $i=j$，电子会感受到自己的经典电荷，产生 self-interaction。可以在 orbital-dependent Hartree 方程中排除自项，但仍缺少完整费米反对称性和 exchange hole。Hartree 的独立轨道图像可作为平均场起点，不是电子相关的最终定义。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Hartree theory restricts the many-body state to an orbital product,

$$
\Psi_H(\mathbf x_1,\ldots,\mathbf x_N)
=\prod_{i=1}^{N}\phi_i(\mathbf x_i),
$$

without antisymmetrizing all electron coordinates. Variation produces one-body equations in the external and classical Hartree fields:

$$
\left[-\frac12\nabla^2+V_{\mathrm{ext}}(\mathbf r)
+V_H(\mathbf r)\right]\phi_i(\mathbf x)
=\varepsilon_i\phi_i(\mathbf x),
$$

$$
V_H(\mathbf r)=\int
\frac{n(\mathbf r')}{|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|}\,d^3r'.
$$

If the Hartree sum includes $i=j$, an electron interacts with its own classical density. Orbital-dependent Hartree equations can exclude self-terms, but still lack global fermionic antisymmetry and the exchange hole. Hartree theory is a mean-field starting point, not the definition of electron correlation.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="Hartree–Fock：一个 determinant 上的变分最小化" titleEn="Hartree–Fock: variational minimization over one determinant">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

对正交 spin-orbitals 的 Slater determinant，能量为

$$
E_{HF}=\sum_i h_{ii}
+\frac12\sum_{ij}(J_{ij}-K_{ij})+E_{II},
$$

其中

$$
h_{ii}=\int\phi_i^*(\mathbf x)
\left[-\frac12\nabla^2+V_{\mathrm{ext}}\right]
\phi_i(\mathbf x)\,d\mathbf x,
$$

$$
J_{ij}=\iint
\frac{|\phi_i(\mathbf x)|^2|\phi_j(\mathbf x')|^2}
{|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|}\,d\mathbf x\,d\mathbf x',
$$

$$
K_{ij}=\iint
\frac{\phi_i^*(\mathbf x)\phi_j^*(\mathbf x')
\phi_i(\mathbf x')\phi_j(\mathbf x)}
{|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|}\,d\mathbf x\,d\mathbf x'.
$$

$K_{ij}$ 只有相同自旋部分非零。$J_{ii}=K_{ii}$，所以同一 occupied spin-orbital 的 Hartree self-interaction 精确抵消。对所有正交轨道变分得到 nonlocal Fock 算符

$$
\hat F\phi_i
=\left(\hat h+\hat J-\hat K\right)\phi_i
=\sum_j\Lambda_{ji}\phi_j.
$$

通过 occupied 空间中的 unitary rotation 可对角化乘子矩阵，得到 canonical HF equations $\hat F\phi_i=\varepsilon_i\phi_i$。exchange 算符依赖被作用的 orbital，不能一般表示为一个共同局域乘法势。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For a Slater determinant of orthonormal spin-orbitals,

$$
E_{HF}=\sum_i h_{ii}
+\frac12\sum_{ij}(J_{ij}-K_{ij})+E_{II},
$$

with the usual one-body, direct, and exchange integrals. $K_{ij}$ is nonzero only for compatible spin. The self-terms satisfy $J_{ii}=K_{ii}$, so the Hartree self-interaction of an occupied spin-orbital cancels exactly.

Variation under orbital orthonormality gives the nonlocal Fock equation

$$
\hat F\phi_i
=\left(\hat h+\hat J-\hat K\right)\phi_i
=\sum_j\Lambda_{ji}\phi_j.
$$

A unitary rotation within the occupied space diagonalizes the Lagrange-multiplier matrix and yields canonical equations. The exchange operator depends on the orbital on which it acts and is not generally a common local multiplicative potential.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="为什么总能不是 occupied HF eigenvalues 的简单和 | Why the total energy is not a simple sum of occupied HF eigenvalues"
  assumptions={[
    'canonical Hartree–Fock orbitals;',
    'orthonormal occupied spin-orbitals;',
    'fixed nuclei.']}
>

对 canonical orbital，

$$
\varepsilon_i=h_{ii}+\sum_j(J_{ij}-K_{ij}).
$$

求和得到

$$
\sum_i\varepsilon_i
=\sum_i h_{ii}+\sum_{ij}(J_{ij}-K_{ij}).
$$

而 HF 总能中的两体项只应计数一次：

$$
E_{HF}=\sum_i h_{ii}
+\frac12\sum_{ij}(J_{ij}-K_{ij})+E_{II}.
$$

因此

$$
\boxed{
E_{HF}=\sum_i\varepsilon_i
-\frac12\sum_{ij}(J_{ij}-K_{ij})+E_{II}}
$$

第二项去除 orbital eigenvalue sum 中的两次计数。类似 double-counting correction 也出现在 Kohn–Sham DFT、DFT+$U$、hybrid 和 mean-field model Hamiltonians 中，但具体表达式不同。

<p lang="en">Each Hartree–Fock eigenvalue contains interaction with every occupied orbital. Summing all eigenvalues counts each electron pair twice, whereas the total energy counts each pair once. The subtraction is a genuine double-counting correction, not an optional shift.</p>

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="Koopmans 与 ΔSCF 的含义" titleEn="Meaning of Koopmans and ΔSCF">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Koopmans theorem 在 frozen-orbital HF 中把 occupied orbital eigenvalue 与移去该轨道电子的总能变化联系起来；对 empty orbital 类似定义加电子能。它忽略其他轨道 relaxation 和所有 correlation，因此通常高估 fundamental gap。

有限体系可直接计算

$$
I=E(N-1)-E(N),
\qquad
A=E(N)-E(N+1)
$$

并分别让 $N\pm1$ 态自洽弛豫，这类 total-energy difference 称为 $\Delta$SCF。结果仍依赖方法、目标自旋/对称性、带电边界和状态跟踪；在周期固体中，带电超胞、delocalized added charge 和 thermodynamic limit 使简单 $\Delta$SCF 更复杂。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Koopmans' theorem relates a Hartree–Fock orbital eigenvalue to the total-energy change for adding or removing that orbital while all other orbitals remain frozen. Orbital relaxation and all correlation are omitted, so fundamental gaps are often overestimated.

For a finite system, one can calculate

$$
I=E(N-1)-E(N),
\qquad
A=E(N)-E(N+1)
$$

and relax each $N\pm1$ state self-consistently. Such total-energy differences are called $\Delta$SCF. They still depend on method, spin and symmetry targeting, charged boundary conditions, and state tracking. Periodic solids add complications from charged supercells, delocalized added charge, and the thermodynamic limit.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="独立电子图像的证据上限" titleEn="Evidence ceiling of an independent-electron picture" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

一组漂亮的一体能带不证明真实谱函数含无限寿命、单位谱权重或无卫星峰。HF self-interaction cancellation 只对 occupied 单 determinant exchange 成立，不等于所有 correlation 已处理。Fermi–Dirac occupation 是给定一体 Hamiltonian 的统计结果；在强关联体系中，低能自由度、准粒子权重和 occupation interpretation 可能完全不同。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A clean one-particle band plot does not establish infinite lifetime, unit spectral weight, or absence of satellites in the physical spectral function. Hartree–Fock self-interaction cancellation applies within occupied one-determinant exchange and does not mean correlation is solved. Fermi–Dirac occupation is a statistical result for a given one-body Hamiltonian; strongly correlated low-energy degrees of freedom and quasiparticle weights can require a different interpretation.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>
