<h2 id="reading-orientation">阅读定位 | Reading Orientation</h2>

<BilingualSection titleZh="本章在全书中的作用" titleEn="The role of this chapter" eyebrow="Martin Chapter 3 · printed pp. 60–80">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Chapter 2 按性质组织电子结构问题；Chapter 3 回到这些性质共同依赖的理论对象。起点不是 Kohn–Sham 轨道，而是电子与原子核的非相对论多体 Hamiltonian、反对称多体态、统计系综以及两体相关函数。独立电子、Hartree 和 Hartree–Fock 在本章后半部分作为受限近似出现，由此能够清楚区分严格多体定义和后续计算形式。

本章的主线可以压缩为

$$
\text{electron+nucleus Hamiltonian}
\to \text{Born--Oppenheimer electronic problem}
\to \text{energy/density/force/stress}
\to \text{ensembles and density matrix}
\to \text{independent-electron restrictions}
\to \text{exchange and correlation}.
$$

这条链条决定后续章节中的证据边界。力定理依赖电子态对允许变分达到驻点；应力是对空间尺度或应变的广义力；Fermi–Dirac 占据属于非相互作用或准粒子描述；交换来自费米反对称性，而关联涵盖单个 Slater determinant 不能表达的剩余多体结构。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Chapter 2 organized electronic-structure questions by material property. Chapter 3 returns to the theoretical objects shared by those properties. Its starting point is not a Kohn–Sham orbital, but the nonrelativistic many-electron–nuclear Hamiltonian, antisymmetric many-body states, statistical ensembles, and two-particle correlation functions. Independent electrons, Hartree, and Hartree–Fock enter only later as restricted approximations, keeping exact many-body definitions separate from subsequent computational forms.

The chapter follows the chain

$$
\text{electron+nucleus Hamiltonian}
\to \text{Born--Oppenheimer electronic problem}
\to \text{energy/density/force/stress}
\to \text{ensembles and density matrix}
\to \text{independent-electron restrictions}
\to \text{exchange and correlation}.
$$

This chain controls the evidence boundaries used later. The force theorem relies on stationarity of the electronic state under allowed variations. Stress is a generalized force associated with spatial scaling or strain. Fermi–Dirac occupations belong to a noninteracting or quasiparticle description. Exchange follows from fermionic antisymmetry, while correlation contains the remaining many-body structure that one Slater determinant cannot represent.

</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="前置知识与后续连接" titleEn="Prerequisites and later connections">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

需要熟悉波函数、算符、期望值、归一化、变分法、偏导数和基本热力学。Appendix A 的泛函变分、Appendix C 的 Born–Oppenheimer 近似、Appendix F–G 的周期 Coulomb 与 virial/stress 推导，以及 Appendix H–I 的交换关联洞和力表达式，是本章的数学支撑。

Chapter 4 将加入晶格、倒格矢与 Bloch 定理；Chapter 5 把独立电子与 Hartree–Fock 用于均匀电子气；Part II 以密度和辅助非相互作用体系重构基态问题。后续出现的 DFT 总能、Pulay 力、DFPT 响应、GW 自能、DMFT 局域关联和量子 Monte Carlo 都应能追溯到本章定义的对象。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The prerequisites are wavefunctions, operators, expectation values, normalization, variation, partial derivatives, and elementary thermodynamics. Appendix A on functional variation, Appendix C on Born–Oppenheimer separation, Appendices F–G on periodic Coulomb energy and virial/stress relations, and Appendices H–I on exchange-correlation holes and forces provide mathematical support.

Chapter 4 introduces lattices, reciprocal vectors, and Bloch theory. Chapter 5 applies independent-electron and Hartree–Fock ideas to the uniform electron gas. Part II reconstructs the ground-state problem using density and an auxiliary noninteracting system. Later DFT total energies, Pulay forces, DFPT response, GW self-energies, DMFT local correlations, and quantum Monte Carlo observables should all be traceable to objects defined here.

</BilingualSection>
