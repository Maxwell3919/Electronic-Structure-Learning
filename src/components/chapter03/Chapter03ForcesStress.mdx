<h2 id="section-3-3">3.3 力与应力定理 | Force and Stress Theorems</h2>

<BilingualSection titleZh="力是能量对参数的负导数" titleEn="Force is the negative derivative of energy" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】 §3.3 · pp. 65–67">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

对核位置 $\mathbf R_I$，共轭力定义为

$$
\mathbf F_I=-\frac{\partial E}{\partial\mathbf R_I}.
$$

若归一化电子态 $|\Psi(\mathbf R)\rangle$ 是 $\hat H(\mathbf R)$ 的精确本征态或能量泛函的完整变分驻点，能量导数只保留 Hamiltonian 的显式参数依赖：

$$
\frac{\partial E}{\partial\mathbf R_I}
=\left\langle\Psi\left|
\frac{\partial\hat H}{\partial\mathbf R_I}
\right|\Psi\right\rangle.
$$

对局域电子—核势，电子部分可写成密度积分：

$$
\mathbf F_I
=-\int n(\mathbf r)
\frac{\partial V_{\mathrm{ext}}(\mathbf r)}
{\partial\mathbf R_I}\,d^3r
-\frac{\partial E_{II}}{\partial\mathbf R_I}.
$$

因此即使电子动能、电子—电子能、交换和关联随核移动发生变化，它们的一阶隐式导数在驻点相互抵消。对纯 Coulomb 局域势，结果等价于核电荷乘以其他电荷产生的总电场。对 nonlocal pseudopotential，仍使用 $-\langle\partial_{R_I}\hat H\rangle$，但不能仅由 $n(\mathbf r)$ 表示。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For a nuclear coordinate $\mathbf R_I$, the conjugate force is

$$
\mathbf F_I=-\frac{\partial E}{\partial\mathbf R_I}.
$$

If the normalized electronic state $|\Psi(\mathbf R)\rangle$ is an exact eigenstate of $\hat H(\mathbf R)$ or a fully variational stationary point, the energy derivative contains only explicit parameter dependence of the Hamiltonian:

$$
\frac{\partial E}{\partial\mathbf R_I}
=\left\langle\Psi\left|
\frac{\partial\hat H}{\partial\mathbf R_I}
\right|\Psi\right\rangle.
$$

For a local electron–nuclear potential, the electronic contribution becomes a density integral:

$$
\mathbf F_I
=-\int n(\mathbf r)
\frac{\partial V_{\mathrm{ext}}(\mathbf r)}
{\partial\mathbf R_I}\,d^3r
-\frac{\partial E_{II}}{\partial\mathbf R_I}.
$$

Electronic kinetic, electron–electron, exchange, and correlation energies all change when a nucleus moves, but their first-order implicit derivatives cancel at the stationary state. For local Coulomb potentials, the result equals nuclear charge times the total field of the other charges. With a nonlocal pseudopotential, $-\langle\partial_{R_I}\hat H\rangle$ remains valid, but it cannot generally be written from $n(\mathbf r)$ alone.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="Hellmann–Feynman 推导 | Hellmann–Feynman derivation"
  assumptions={[
    '态归一化并且对参数可微；',
    'Hamiltonian 为自伴，态满足精确本征方程；',
    '简并情形先在简并子空间对 ∂λH 对角化。',
  ]}
>

从

$$
E(\lambda)=\langle\Psi_\lambda|\hat H_\lambda|\Psi_\lambda\rangle
$$

求导：

$$
\frac{dE}{d\lambda}
=\langle\partial_\lambda\Psi|\hat H|\Psi\rangle
+\left\langle\Psi\left|\partial_\lambda\hat H\right|\Psi\right\rangle
+\langle\Psi|\hat H|\partial_\lambda\Psi\rangle.
$$

使用 $\hat H|\Psi\rangle=E|\Psi\rangle$ 以及 Hermiticity：

$$
\frac{dE}{d\lambda}
=E\left[
\langle\partial_\lambda\Psi|\Psi\rangle
+\langle\Psi|\partial_\lambda\Psi\rangle
\right]
+\langle\Psi|\partial_\lambda\hat H|\Psi\rangle.
$$

归一化条件 $\langle\Psi|\Psi\rangle=1$ 的导数使方括号为零，因此

$$
\boxed{
\frac{dE}{d\lambda}
=\langle\Psi|\partial_\lambda\hat H|\Psi\rangle}.
$$

更一般地，如果 $|\Psi\rangle$ 不是精确本征态但使近似能量对所有内部变分参数达到驻点，同样的隐式参数导数在该近似空间内消失。若优化不充分，残余一阶项会污染力。

<p lang="en">Differentiating the eigenstate expectation value produces two state-derivative terms and one explicit Hamiltonian derivative. The eigenvalue equation converts the state terms into the derivative of normalization, which vanishes. A variational approximate state obeys the same cancellation only within its allowed parameter space and only when that space has been optimized to stationarity.</p>

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="Pulay 项来自移动的不完备表示" titleEn="Pulay terms arise from a moving incomplete representation">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

设近似轨道写在依赖核位置的基组中：

$$
|\psi_n\rangle=\sum_\mu c_{\mu n}|\chi_\mu(\mathbf R)\rangle.
$$

即使系数 $c_{\mu n}$ 已在当前有限基组中变分优化，移动核时基函数本身也改变：$\partial_{R_I}|\chi_\mu\rangle\ne0$。能量导数出现 basis-response 或 Pulay 项。非正交基组的广义本征问题

$$
\mathbf H\mathbf c_n=\varepsilon_n\mathbf S\mathbf c_n
$$

求导时同时含 $\partial_{R_I}\mathbf H$ 和 $\partial_{R_I}\mathbf S$。轨道级力贡献具有

$$
\frac{d\varepsilon_n}{dR_I}
=\mathbf c_n^\dagger
\left(\frac{\partial\mathbf H}{\partial R_I}
-\varepsilon_n\frac{\partial\mathbf S}{\partial R_I}
\right)\mathbf c_n
$$

的结构，第二项是典型 overlap/Pulay 修正。

平面波基在固定 cutoff 与固定胞下不随原子位置移动，原子位移 Pulay 力通常较小或为零；但变胞时基组中平面波数目会变化，产生 Pulay stress。局域原子轨道、APW/LAPW、PAW augmentation 和自适应实空间基都有各自的响应项，不能复用别的方法公式。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Suppose an approximate orbital is expanded in a nuclear-position-dependent basis:

$$
|\psi_n\rangle=\sum_\mu c_{\mu n}|\chi_\mu(\mathbf R)\rangle.
$$

Even when the coefficients are variationally optimized in the current finite basis, the basis itself moves with the nuclei. The energy derivative then contains basis-response or Pulay terms. For the generalized eigenproblem

$$
\mathbf H\mathbf c_n=\varepsilon_n\mathbf S\mathbf c_n,
$$

differentiation gives the characteristic form

$$
\frac{d\varepsilon_n}{dR_I}
=\mathbf c_n^\dagger
\left(\frac{\partial\mathbf H}{\partial R_I}
-\varepsilon_n\frac{\partial\mathbf S}{\partial R_I}
\right)\mathbf c_n.
$$

The overlap derivative is a typical Pulay correction. Plane waves at fixed cell and cutoff do not move with atomic positions, so atomic Pulay forces are often absent, but variable-cell calculations change the represented plane-wave set and create Pulay stress. Local orbitals, APW/LAPW, PAW augmentation, and adaptive real-space bases require their own response terms.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="应力是对均匀应变的广义力" titleEn="Stress is the generalized force for homogeneous strain">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

令空间按小应变 $\epsilon_{\alpha\beta}$ 缩放，$r_\alpha\to(\delta_{\alpha\beta}+\epsilon_{\alpha\beta})r_\beta$。Martin 采用

$$
\sigma_{\alpha\beta}
=-\frac1\Omega\frac{\partial E}{\partial\epsilon_{\alpha\beta}}
$$

定义应力。电子坐标、核位置和晶格矢量必须一致缩放，波函数还要乘 Jacobian 因子以保持归一化。由于波函数和内部核坐标在平衡时为驻点，一阶能量变化只保留显式尺度依赖。

对所有粒子和中心势相互作用，广义 virial 结构为

$$
\sigma_{\alpha\beta}
=-\frac1\Omega\left[
\sum_k\left\langle\frac{p_{k\alpha}p_{k\beta}}{m_k}\right\rangle
-\frac12\sum_{k\ne l}
\left\langle
\frac{x_{kl,\alpha}x_{kl,\beta}}{x_{kl}}
\frac{dV(x_{kl})}{dx_{kl}}
\right\rangle
\right].
$$

取 trace 得到静水压力。纯 Coulomb 相互作用满足 $r\,dV/dr=-V$，于是

$$
3P\Omega=2E_{\mathrm{kin}}+E_{\mathrm{pot}}.
$$

符号约定在不同领域和程序中可能相反；比较前必须确认“正值表示拉伸还是压缩”。有限温度、pseudopotential、Ewald、exchange-correlation、熵和基组 cutoff 都会产生实现特有项。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Under a small homogeneous strain, $r_\alpha\to(\delta_{\alpha\beta}+\epsilon_{\alpha\beta})r_\beta$. Martin defines

$$
\sigma_{\alpha\beta}
=-\frac1\Omega\frac{\partial E}{\partial\epsilon_{\alpha\beta}}.
$$

Electronic coordinates, nuclei, and lattice vectors must be scaled consistently, and the wavefunction acquires a Jacobian factor to preserve normalization. At equilibrium, wavefunction and internal-coordinate first variations vanish, leaving explicit scale dependence.

For particles interacting through central potentials, the generalized virial structure is

$$
\sigma_{\alpha\beta}
=-\frac1\Omega\left[
\sum_k\left\langle\frac{p_{k\alpha}p_{k\beta}}{m_k}\right\rangle
-\frac12\sum_{k\ne l}
\left\langle
\frac{x_{kl,\alpha}x_{kl,\beta}}{x_{kl}}
\frac{dV(x_{kl})}{dx_{kl}}
\right\rangle
\right].
$$

The trace yields hydrostatic pressure. For Coulomb interactions, $r\,dV/dr=-V$, giving

$$
3P\Omega=2E_{\mathrm{kin}}+E_{\mathrm{pot}}.
$$

Sign conventions differ among communities and codes. Finite temperature, pseudopotentials, Ewald terms, exchange-correlation, entropy, and basis cutoffs all add implementation-specific stress contributions.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="力与应力的最低验证" titleEn="Minimum validation of force and stress" kind="practice">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <ul>
      <li>用正负有限位移或有限应变验证解析力/应力的斜率和符号；</li>
      <li>分别收敛 SCF、基组、FFT/grid、$k$ 点和 augmentation 参数；</li>
      <li>比较解析力与总能差时使用相同电子占据、对称性和弛豫约束；</li>
      <li>检查总平移力、总转矩、声学和规则及应力对胞取向的张量变换；</li>
      <li>残余力小只证明局域驻点，不证明全局、声子或有限温度稳定。</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <ul>
      <li>verify analytic force and stress signs and slopes with positive and negative finite displacements or strains;</li>
      <li>converge SCF, basis, FFT/grid, $k$ sampling, and augmentation parameters separately;</li>
      <li>use identical occupations, symmetry, and relaxation constraints when comparing analytic derivatives with total-energy differences;</li>
      <li>check net translation force, net torque, acoustic sum rules, and tensor transformation under cell rotation;</li>
      <li>small residual forces establish a local stationary geometry, not global, phonon, or finite-temperature stability.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</BilingualCallout>
