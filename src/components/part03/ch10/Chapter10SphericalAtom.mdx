## 10.2 球对称独立粒子方程 | Independent-Particle Equations in Spherical Potentials

<BilingualSection titleZh="从单电子中心势到自洽原子" titleEn="From a one-electron central potential to a self-consistent atom" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

多电子原子中的单粒子方程仍可采用径向形式，但 $V(r)$ 不再是预先给定的纯 Coulomb 势。Kohn–Sham 情形中

$$
V_{\mathrm{eff}}^\sigma(r)
=V_{\mathrm{ext}}(r)+V_{\mathrm H}[n](r)+V_{\mathrm{xc}}^\sigma[n_\uparrow,n_\downarrow](r),
$$

并通过轨道重构的密度自洽确定。闭壳层、无自旋极化且无显式 SOC 时，势与 $m$ 无关，每个 $(n,l)$ 通道满足

$$
\left[-\frac12\frac{d^2}{dr^2}
+\frac{l(l+1)}{2r^2}+V_{\mathrm{eff}}(r)\right]u_{nl}(r)
=\varepsilon_{nl}u_{nl}(r).
$$

Hartree–Fock 也产生径向方程，但交换算符依赖被作用的轨道并且是非局域的。把 Kohn–Sham 的乘法势 V_xc(r) 与 Hartree–Fock 的 Fock 交换算符写成同一个“局域势”会丢失方法差别。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The one-particle equation of a many-electron atom retains a radial form, but $V(r)$ is no longer a prescribed pure Coulomb potential. In the Kohn–Sham case,

$$
V_{\mathrm{eff}}^\sigma(r)
=V_{\mathrm{ext}}(r)+V_{\mathrm H}[n](r)+V_{\mathrm{xc}}^\sigma[n_\uparrow,n_\downarrow](r),
$$

and is determined self-consistently from the orbital density. For a closed shell without spin polarization or explicit spin–orbit coupling, the potential is independent of $m$, and every $(n,l)$ channel obeys

$$
\left[-\frac12\frac{d^2}{dr^2}
+\frac{l(l+1)}{2r^2}+V_{\mathrm{eff}}(r)\right]u_{nl}(r)
=\varepsilon_{nl}u_{nl}(r).
$$

Hartree–Fock also leads to radial equations, but its exchange operator is orbital dependent and nonlocal. Replacing both the Kohn–Sham multiplicative potential V_xc(r) and the Fock exchange operator by a single generic “local potential” erases a substantive methodological distinction.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="严格关系 | Exact relation: 闭壳层密度与电子数"
  assumptions={[
    '每个 (n,l) 子壳层在 m 和自旋上均匀占据 | Each (n,l) subshell is uniformly occupied over m and spin.',
    '球谐函数满足加法定理 Σ_m |Y_lm|²=(2l+1)/(4π) | The spherical-harmonic addition theorem is used.',
    '径向函数按 ∫|u_nl|²dr=1 归一化 | Each reduced radial function is normalized by ∫|u_nl|²dr=1.',
  ]}
>

设 g_nl 是整个 $(n,l)$ 子壳层的电子数，范围为 0 ≤ g_nl ≤ 2(2l+1)。球平均占据意味着每个 $m$ 和自旋分量的平均占据相同。利用

$$
\sum_{m=-l}^{l}|Y_{lm}(\Omega)|^2=\frac{2l+1}{4\pi},
$$

可写出三维球对称密度

$$
\boxed{
n(r)=\sum_{nl}\frac{g_{nl}}{4\pi}|R_{nl}(r)|^2
=\sum_{nl}\frac{g_{nl}}{4\pi r^2}|u_{nl}(r)|^2
}.
$$

对完整闭壳层，g_nl = 2(2l+1)。电子数检查为

$$
\begin{aligned}
4\pi\int_0^\infty r^2n(r)dr
&=\sum_{nl}g_{nl}\int_0^\infty |u_{nl}(r)|^2dr\\
&=\sum_{nl}g_{nl}=N.
\end{aligned}
$$

这个等式同时检查占据、角向因子和径向归一化。若程序输出的是径向密度 $4\pi r^2n(r)$，则不能再额外乘一次 $4\pi r^2$。

<p lang="en">Let g_nl be the total population of the $(n,l)$ subshell, with 0 ≤ g_nl ≤ 2(2l+1). Uniform spherical occupation and the addition theorem give</p>

$$
\boxed{
n(r)=\sum_{nl}\frac{g_{nl}}{4\pi}|R_{nl}(r)|^2
=\sum_{nl}\frac{g_{nl}}{4\pi r^2}|u_{nl}(r)|^2
}.
$$

<p lang="en">A complete closed shell has g_nl = 2(2l+1). The electron-number check is</p>

$$
4\pi\int_0^\infty r^2n(r)dr
=\sum_{nl}g_{nl}\int_0^\infty |u_{nl}(r)|^2dr
=\sum_{nl}g_{nl}=N.
$$

<p lang="en">This identity simultaneously checks occupations, angular factors, and radial normalization. If a code reports the radial density $4\pi r^2n(r)$, the shell factor must not be applied a second time.</p>

</DerivationBlock>

<DerivationBlock
  title="严格关系 | Exact relation: 球对称 Hartree 势"
  assumptions={[
    'n(r) 是三维电子数密度，电子电荷符号已吸收到 Hartree 势的常用定义中 | n(r) is the three-dimensional electron-number density in the standard Hartree-potential convention.',
    '孤立原子的边界条件为 V_H(r→∞)→N/r | For an isolated atom, V_H(r→∞) approaches N/r.',
  ]}
>

球对称密度的 Hartree 势满足 Poisson 方程

$$
\frac{1}{r^2}\frac{d}{dr}\left(r^2\frac{dV_{\mathrm H}}{dr}\right)=-4\pi n(r).
$$

也可从 Coulomb 积分直接分解内外球壳：半径小于 $r$ 的电荷对观察点表现为中心电荷，半径大于 $r$ 的球壳在壳内产生常数势。因此

$$
\boxed{
V_{\mathrm H}(r)=4\pi\left[
\frac{1}{r}\int_0^r r'^2n(r')dr'
+\int_r^\infty r'n(r')dr'
\right]
}.
$$

第一项在 $r\to\infty$ 时趋于 $N/r$，第二项趋于零。若定义 w(r) = rV_H(r)，则

$$
w''(r)=-4\pi r n(r),
$$

这才是没有一阶导数的二阶形式。数值实现中必须说明求解的是 V_H 还是 w = rV_H；两者的源项和边界条件不同。

<p lang="en">For a spherical density, the Hartree potential satisfies</p>

$$
\frac{1}{r^2}\frac{d}{dr}\left(r^2\frac{dV_{\mathrm H}}{dr}\right)=-4\pi n(r),
$$

<p lang="en">or, equivalently,</p>

$$
\boxed{
V_{\mathrm H}(r)=4\pi\left[
\frac{1}{r}\int_0^r r'^2n(r')dr'
+\int_r^\infty r'n(r')dr'
\right].
}
$$

<p lang="en">The first term approaches $N/r$ and the second vanishes at large radius. Defining w = rV_H gives $w''=-4\pi r n(r)$. A numerical implementation must state whether it solves for V_H or for $w$ because the source and boundary conditions differ.</p>

</DerivationBlock>

## 原子自洽计算 | Atomic Self-Consistency

<BilingualSection titleZh="固定点问题与占据稳定性" titleEn="The fixed-point problem and occupation stability">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

给定输入密度 n^(k)，构造有效势并求解所有所需径向通道，得到输出密度 n_out^(k)。最简单的线性 mixing 为

$$
n^{(k+1)}=(1-\alpha)n^{(k)}+\alpha n_{\mathrm{out}}^{(k)},
\qquad 0<\alpha\le1.
$$

密度残差可定义为 Δn^(k) = n_out^(k) − n^(k)，但具体范数、径向权重和阈值必须声明。核附近的绝对密度很大，尾部密度很小；只报告未加权最大误差可能掩盖某一空间区域的问题。

过渡元素的近简并 $3d$、$4s$ 状态可能在迭代中交换次序。每一步都按瞬时最低本征值重新填充，会使占据和势发生不连续跳变。解决方法可能包括固定参考占据、分数占据、有限温度展宽或更稳健的 mixing；它们改变的是数值路径或参考状态，必须与最终物理态的选择分开说明。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Given an input density n^(k), one constructs the effective potential, solves the required radial channels, and obtains an output density n_out^(k). The simplest linear mixing is

$$
n^{(k+1)}=(1-\alpha)n^{(k)}+\alpha n_{\mathrm{out}}^{(k)},
\qquad 0<\alpha\le1.
$$

A density residual may be defined as Δn^(k) = n_out^(k) − n^(k), but its norm, radial weight, and threshold must be stated. The absolute density is large near the nucleus and small in the tail, so an unweighted maximum error can hide a failure in one spatial region.

Near-degenerate $3d$ and $4s$ states in transition elements may exchange order during iteration. Refilling strictly by the instantaneous eigenvalue order can produce discontinuous changes in occupation and potential. Fixed reference occupations, fractional occupations, finite-temperature smearing, or more robust mixing may stabilize the path. These choices alter the numerical route or reference state and must be distinguished from the selection of the final physical state.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>
