## 10.6 Delta-SCF：加电子、去电子与相互作用能 | Delta-SCF: Addition, Removal, and Interaction Energies

<BilingualSection titleZh="总能差的定义" titleEn="Definitions from total-energy differences" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

设 $E(N)$ 表示在相同核构型、相同 Hamiltonian 近似与充分收敛数值设置下，$N$ 电子目标态的自洽总能。正的垂直电离能和电子亲和能定义为

$$
I=E(N-1)-E(N),
$$

$$
A=E(N)-E(N+1).
$$

两者都允许其余轨道在新粒子数下重新弛豫，因此通常比从中性体系单个本征值直接估计更接近对应的总能过程。这里的“更接近”仍依赖所用泛函、目标态约束、相对论处理和数值收敛；Delta-SCF 并不使近似泛函自动变成精确理论。

若在两个指定构型 $1$、$2$ 之间比较激发或 promotion，总能差为 $\Delta E=E_2-E_1$。两个计算必须使用相同的能量零点与方法设置。几何是否固定决定这是垂直过程还是绝热过程，不能在公式之外省略。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Let $E(N)$ denote the self-consistent total energy of a specified $N$-electron state at the same nuclear geometry, Hamiltonian approximation, and converged numerical settings. The positive vertical ionization energy and electron affinity are defined by

$$
I=E(N-1)-E(N),
$$

$$
A=E(N)-E(N+1).
$$

Both permit the remaining orbitals to relax at the new particle number and therefore often represent the corresponding total-energy process better than a direct estimate from one neutral-system eigenvalue. This advantage still depends on the functional, state constraint, relativistic treatment, and numerical convergence; Delta-SCF does not make an approximate functional exact.

For an excitation or promotion between specified configurations 1 and 2, the total-energy difference is $\Delta E=E_2-E_1$. The two calculations must share the same method and energy reference. Whether the geometry is fixed determines whether the process is vertical or adiabatic and cannot be left implicit.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="补充推导 | Supplemental derivation: transition-state 中点规则"
  assumptions={[
    '总能可作为目标轨道占据 f 的可微函数 | The total energy is differentiable with respect to the target occupation f.',
    '采用 Janak 型关系 ∂E/∂f=ε_i(f) | A Janak-type relation ∂E/∂f=ε_i(f) is applicable.',
    '中点规则要求 ε_i(f) 在区间内足够平滑；它是数值近似而非恒等式 | Midpoint quadrature requires a sufficiently smooth ε_i(f) and is an approximation, not an identity.',
  ]}
>

考虑从 $f=1$ 去除一个电子到 $f=0$。总能差的严格积分形式为

$$
E(f=0)-E(f=1)
=-\int_0^1\frac{\partial E}{\partial f}\,df
=-\int_0^1\varepsilon_i(f)\,df.
$$

对积分采用中点求积，得到

$$
I_i\approx-\varepsilon_i\left(f=\frac12\right).
$$

若采用“电子能量为负、去电子代价为正”的约定，前面的负号不可省略。文献中也可能把去电子能量写成带符号的本征值；比较公式前应先核对能量定义。

中点规则对 $\varepsilon_i(f)$ 的常数项与线性项积分精确，误差从更高阶曲率开始。实际计算可直接取多个分数占据点检查线性程度，而不能把 $f=1/2$ 的单点结果自动视为已验证。

<p lang="en">Removing one electron changes the target occupation from $f=1$ to $f=0$. The exact integral form is</p>

$$
E(0)-E(1)=-\int_0^1\frac{\partial E}{\partial f}\,df
=-\int_0^1\varepsilon_i(f)\,df.
$$

<p lang="en">Midpoint quadrature gives $I_i\approx-\varepsilon_i(f=1/2)$ when the positive removal-energy convention is used. The sign must be checked because some sources quote a signed removal eigenvalue instead. The midpoint rule integrates the constant and linear terms of $\varepsilon_i(f)$ exactly; curvature produces the leading error. Several fractional-occupation points provide a direct test of the assumed linearity.</p>

</DerivationBlock>

<DerivationBlock
  title="定义与推断 | Definition and inference: 离散曲率与有效相互作用"
  assumptions={[
    '三个总能对应同一局域子空间定义和同一外部条件 | The three total energies use the same localized-subspace definition and external conditions.',
    '不同粒子数状态都已分别自洽 | Each particle-number state is independently self-consistent.',
  ]}
>

粒子数方向的二阶有限差分

$$
U_{\mathrm{disc}}=E(N+1)+E(N-1)-2E(N)
$$

衡量相邻加、去电子能量之差。对只考虑连续去电子的记号，也可写成第一与第二电离能之差。它包括所用自洽近似内的轨道弛豫，因此是“有效”相互作用，而非裸 Coulomb 积分。

把原子 $U_{\mathrm{disc}}$ 解释为固体 Hubbard $U$ 还需要定义固体局域轨道和屏蔽通道。Martin 讨论用原子内的补偿占据模拟部分屏蔽；这是物理类比与估计方法，不是从孤立原子到固体的严格等式。

<p lang="en">The second finite difference</p>

$$
U_{\mathrm{disc}}=E(N+1)+E(N-1)-2E(N)
$$

<p lang="en">measures the difference between adjacent addition and removal energies. In a removal-only notation, it can also be expressed as the difference between first and second ionization energies. It includes orbital relaxation within the chosen self-consistent approximation and is therefore an effective interaction rather than a bare Coulomb integral.</p>

<p lang="en">Interpreting an atomic $U_{\mathrm{disc}}$ as a solid-state Hubbard $U$ additionally requires a definition of the localized orbitals and screening channels in the solid. Martin discusses compensating occupations inside an atom as a model of screening; this is a physical analogy and estimate, not an exact mapping.</p>

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="阴离子和有限盒的额外门槛" titleEn="Additional gates for anions and finite boxes" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

$E(N+1)$ 只有在额外电子被物理束缚或计算边界能够正确表示散射/连续态时才有直接意义。局域基组可能人为束缚阴离子；有限实空间盒会把连续谱离散化；周期超胞还会引入带电体系的镜像相互作用和补偿背景。电子亲和能必须对基组弥散函数、盒尺寸或超胞修正单独收敛。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

$E(N+1)$ has a direct physical interpretation only when the extra electron is genuinely bound or the boundary conditions correctly represent a scattering or continuum state. A localized basis can artificially bind an anion; a finite real-space box discretizes the continuum; and a periodic supercell introduces image interactions and a compensating background for a charged system. Electron affinities require separate convergence with diffuse basis functions, box size, or charged-supercell corrections.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 10.7 固体中的原子球近似 | Atomic Sphere Approximation in Solids

<BilingualSection titleZh="用边界条件模拟带底和带顶" titleEn="Representing band edges by radial boundary conditions" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

固体中靠近原子核的波函数常保持原子式形状。原子球近似把真实 Wigner–Seitz 多面体替换为半径 $r_0$ 的球，并对每个 $l$ 通道改变自由原子的远场边界条件。径向函数在球面满足零导数时，对应相邻原子同相组合的成键型极限；在球面取零时，对应反相组合的反键型极限。两种本征值之差给出该模型中的带宽。

该图像保留了一个重要机制：球面处波函数的幅度和斜率越大，原子间重叠越强，形成的能带通常越宽。它没有显式构造 Bloch 波，也不解析晶体的全部非球对称势。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Near a nucleus in a solid, wavefunctions often retain an atom-like form. The atomic-sphere approximation replaces the true Wigner–Seitz polyhedron by a sphere of radius $r_0$ and changes the free-atom outer boundary condition for each $l$ channel. A zero radial derivative at the sphere corresponds to a bonding-like, in-phase limit, while a zero value corresponds to an antibonding-like, out-of-phase limit. The eigenvalue difference defines the band width within this model.

The picture retains an important mechanism: a larger amplitude and slope at the sphere imply stronger intersite overlap and generally a broader band. It does not explicitly construct Bloch waves or resolve the complete nonspherical crystal potential.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="严格恒等式 | Exact identity: 两个径向解的 Wronskian 关系"
  assumptions={[
    '两个解属于同一 l 通道和同一径向势，只是外边界条件及本征值不同 | The two solutions share the same l channel and radial potential but have different boundary conditions and eigenvalues.',
    '采用 R(r) 形式并在 0≤r≤r₀ 积分 | The R(r) form is integrated over 0≤r≤r₀.',
    '原点正则性使 r²(R₂R₁′−R₁R₂′) 在 r=0 消失 | Origin regularity removes the lower-boundary term.',
  ]}
>

令 $R_1$、$R_2$ 满足

$$
-\frac{1}{2r^2}\frac{d}{dr}\left(r^2\frac{dR_i}{dr}\right)
+V_l(r)R_i=\varepsilon_iR_i,
$$

其中 $V_l(r)=V(r)+l(l+1)/(2r^2)$。第一式乘 $r^2R_2$，第二式乘 $r^2R_1$，相减后势项抵消：

$$
-\frac12\left[
R_2\frac{d}{dr}(r^2R_1')
-R_1\frac{d}{dr}(r^2R_2')
\right]
=(\varepsilon_1-\varepsilon_2)r^2R_1R_2.
$$

左侧是全导数，积分得到

$$
-\frac12 r_0^2
\left[R_2(r_0)R_1'(r_0)-R_1(r_0)R_2'(r_0)\right]
=(\varepsilon_1-\varepsilon_2)
\int_0^{r_0}r^2R_1R_2dr.
$$

取 $R_1'(r_0)=0$ 为带底、$R_2(r_0)=0$ 为带顶，定义 $W=\varepsilon_2-\varepsilon_1$，则

$$
\boxed{
W=-\frac{1}{2}
\frac{r_0^2R_1(r_0)R_2'(r_0)}
{\int_0^{r_0}r^2R_1(r)R_2(r)dr}
}.
$$

这一步是给定球形势与两种边界条件下的严格关系。进一步用单个自由原子函数近似 $R_1$、$R_2$ 的幅度、斜率和重叠，才得到 Martin 讨论的简化带宽估计；那一步是模型近似。

<p lang="en">For two solutions of the same radial potential and $l$ channel, multiplication by the opposite solution, subtraction, and integration give</p>

$$
-\frac12 r_0^2
\left[R_2(r_0)R_1'(r_0)-R_1(r_0)R_2'(r_0)\right]
=(\varepsilon_1-\varepsilon_2)
\int_0^{r_0}r^2R_1R_2dr.
$$

<p lang="en">With $R_1'(r_0)=0$ for the bonding-like lower edge and $R_2(r_0)=0$ for the antibonding-like upper edge,</p>

$$
\boxed{
W=\varepsilon_2-\varepsilon_1
=-\frac{1}{2}
\frac{r_0^2R_1(r_0)R_2'(r_0)}
{\int_0^{r_0}r^2R_1R_2dr}
}.
$$

<p lang="en">This is exact for the stated spherical potential and boundary conditions. Replacing both solutions and their overlap by expressions from one free-atom radial function is an additional model approximation.</p>

</DerivationBlock>
