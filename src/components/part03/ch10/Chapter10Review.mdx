## 常见误判、练习与来源边界 | Misconceptions, Exercises, and Source Boundaries

### 会影响实际计算的误判 | Misconceptions that affect calculations

<BilingualSection titleZh="1. 把径向函数平方当作径向概率" titleEn="1. Treating the square of the radial function as the radial probability">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

$|R(r)|^2$ 缺少球壳面积因子。半径概率是 $r^2|R(r)|^2dr=|u(r)|^2dr$。混用两者会错误判断峰值位置、节点附近权重和芯—价重叠。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

$|R(r)|^2$ lacks the spherical-shell factor. The radial probability is $r^2|R(r)|^2dr=|u(r)|^2dr$. Confusing the two shifts apparent maxima and misrepresents node weights and core–valence overlap.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="2. 把离心势理解成额外相互作用" titleEn="2. Interpreting the centrifugal term as an additional interaction">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

$l(l+1)/(2r^2)$ 是动能算符角向部分在变量分离后的表示。它依赖角动量通道，但并非新加入的外势或电子—电子作用。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

$l(l+1)/(2r^2)$ is the angular part of the kinetic-energy operator after separation of variables. It depends on the angular-momentum channel but is not a new external or electron–electron interaction.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="3. SCF 收敛等同于原子态正确" titleEn="3. Equating SCF convergence with a correct atomic state">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

迭代可收敛到错误占据、亚稳多重态或由球平均约束产生的参考态。需要同时记录电子构型、自旋、角动量处理、相对论设置和与候选态的总能比较。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

An iteration can converge to an incorrect occupation, a metastable multiplet, or a reference state imposed by spherical averaging. The electronic configuration, spin, angular treatment, relativistic setting, and total-energy comparison with competing states must be recorded separately.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="4. 非相对论计算自动包含 SOC" titleEn="4. Assuming a nonrelativistic calculation automatically contains spin–orbit coupling">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

质量速度与 Darwin 类修正可进入标量相对论方程，但 $j=l\pm1/2$ 分裂需要显式自旋—轨道结构或等价的完全相对论数据。标量相对论与 fully relativistic 不能互换。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Mass–velocity and Darwin-like corrections may enter a scalar-relativistic equation, but the $j=l\pm1/2$ splitting requires explicit spin–orbit structure or equivalent fully relativistic data. Scalar-relativistic and fully relativistic treatments are not interchangeable.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="5. 轨道本征值差等于 promotion 或电离能" titleEn="5. Equating orbital-eigenvalue differences with promotion or ionization energies">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

构型改变会引起全部轨道和有效势弛豫。Delta-SCF 总能差包含所用自洽近似内的这部分响应；固定构型的本征值差通常不是同一量。是否存在严格本征值解释必须按具体定理和能量定义判断。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Changing a configuration relaxes all orbitals and the effective potential. A Delta-SCF total-energy difference includes this response within the chosen self-consistent approximation, whereas a fixed-configuration eigenvalue difference is generally a different quantity. Any exact eigenvalue interpretation must be justified by a specific theorem and energy definition.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="6. 原子中的局域化趋势直接决定固体结果" titleEn="6. Assuming atomic localization trends determine the solid">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

原子径向尺度可提示带宽、semicore 重叠和泛函敏感性。晶体中的杂化、屏蔽、配位与晶场仍可改变能级次序和局域化。原子结果是机制依据，不是完整固体证据。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Atomic radial scales can indicate likely band widths, semicore overlap, and functional sensitivity. Hybridization, screening, coordination, and crystal fields can still change the level order and localization in a solid. Atomic results are mechanistic evidence, not a complete solid-state validation.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

### 原创练习 | Original Exercises

1. **变量分离。** 从球坐标 Laplacian 出发，不跳步地推导 $R_{nl}$ 方程和 $u_{nl}=rR_{nl}$ 方程。标出在哪一步使用了球对称，以及 $l(l+1)$ 的来源。

   **English:** Starting from the spherical-coordinate Laplacian, derive both the $R_{nl}$ and $u_{nl}=rR_{nl}$ equations without skipping the product-rule steps. Identify where spherical symmetry and the eigenvalue $l(l+1)$ enter.

2. **原点指数。** 假设 $V(r)\sim -Z/r$，令 $u\sim r^p(a_0+a_1r+\cdots)$。求允许的最低阶指数，并说明 Coulomb 奇异性为什么不改变 $p=l+1$ 的正则分支。

   **English:** For $V(r)\sim-Z/r$, use $u\sim r^p(a_0+a_1r+\cdots)$ to find the leading exponents and explain why the Coulomb singularity does not alter the regular branch $p=l+1$.

3. **归一化与单位。** 给定一个数值数组 `radial_density = 4πr²n(r)`，写出电子数的离散求积式。再说明若数组实际存储的是 $n(r)$，公式应如何改变。

   **English:** Given a numerical array `radial_density = 4πr²n(r)`, write the discrete quadrature for the electron number. Then state how the formula changes if the array stores $n(r)$ itself.

4. **Hartree 势检查。** 对归一化球对称密度，证明 $V_H(r)\to N/r$。构造一个数值验收：在多大的 $r$ 区间比较，使用绝对误差还是相对误差，如何避免尾部接近零时的误判？

   **English:** Prove that a normalized spherical density gives $V_H(r)\to N/r$. Design a numerical acceptance test, specifying the radial interval and an error measure that remains meaningful in the tail.

5. **SCF 与占据。** 一个原子计算的密度残差单调下降，但第 18 次迭代后 $3d$ 与 $4s$ 次序交换并改变占据。列出判断该结果是否可接受所需的最少输出和两个候选修复方案。

   **English:** An atomic density residual decreases monotonically, but the $3d$ and $4s$ levels exchange order after iteration 18 and the occupation changes. List the minimum evidence required to accept the result and two possible stabilization strategies.

6. **SOC 代数。** 对 $l=3$ 计算两支 $j$、简并度、$\mathbf L\cdot\mathbf S$ 本征值，并验证简并度加权迹为零。该检查能否验证真实原子的 SOC 分裂？说明缺少的径向信息。

   **English:** For $l=3$, compute both $j$ values, degeneracies, and $\mathbf L\cdot\mathbf S$ eigenvalues and verify the degeneracy-weighted trace. Explain why this check does not validate the spin–orbit splitting of a real atom.

7. **球平均。** 对单个 $p_z$ 轨道密度写出其角向依赖，并与三个 $p$ 轨道等占据后的总密度比较。指出球平均丢失的最低非零多极矩。

   **English:** Write the angular dependence of a single $p_z$ orbital density and compare it with equal occupation of all three $p$ orbitals. Identify the lowest nonspherical multipole removed by the average.

8. **Delta-SCF 平移不变性。** 证明给所有 $E(N-1)$、$E(N)$、$E(N+1)$ 加同一常数不会改变 $I$、$A$ 与 $U_{disc}$。若三次计算采用不同赝势或不同核能零点，该证明为什么失去物理意义？

   **English:** Prove that adding a common constant to $E(N-1)$, $E(N)$, and $E(N+1)$ leaves $I$, $A$, and $U_{disc}$ unchanged. Explain why the argument loses physical meaning if the three calculations use different ionic energy references.

9. **阴离子收敛。** 设计一个判断 $E(N+1)$ 是否为真实束缚态的最低成本测试，包括尾部密度、外边界或弥散基组以及能量随盒尺寸的行为。

   **English:** Design a low-cost test for whether $E(N+1)$ represents a genuinely bound state, including the density tail, outer boundary or diffuse basis, and the energy dependence on box size.

10. **原子球带宽。** 从 Wronskian 恒等式推导零导数与零值边界条件下的带宽表达式。随后列出用自由原子函数替代两条球内解时新增的近似。

    **English:** Derive the atomic-sphere band-width expression from the Wronskian identity for zero-derivative and zero-value boundary conditions. Then list the additional approximations introduced by replacing both sphere solutions with one free-atom function.

### 数值实现连接 | Connection to Numerical Implementation

```text
原子构型与相对论选择
  -> 径向网格、原点级数与外边界
  -> 每个角动量通道的节点/本征值求解
  -> 占据与球平均规则
  -> Hartree、交换关联或 Fock 项
  -> 密度/势 mixing 与 SCF 残差
  -> 总能、Delta-SCF 或径向矩阵元收敛
  -> 原子参考态与候选构型比较
  -> Chapter 11 的赝势或增广数据生成
```

程序正常结束只证明执行路径到达终点。径向本征解正确、SCF 固定点达到阈值、目标能量差对网格收敛、方法适用于目标原子态，以及后续赝势可迁移性，分别需要不同证据。

<p lang="en">Normal program termination establishes only that execution reached an endpoint. Correct radial eigenstates, a converged SCF fixed point, grid convergence of the target energy difference, suitability of the atomic-state approximation, and transferability of a later pseudopotential require separate evidence.</p>
