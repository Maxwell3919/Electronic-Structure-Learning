## 阅读定位 | Reading Orientation

<BilingualSection titleZh="本章处理的对象" titleEn="The object of this chapter" eyebrow="Orientation">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

本章把一般三维独立粒子方程限制到原子中心势。球对称性把角变量交给球谐函数，把原本的三维偏微分方程化为每个角动量通道的一维径向本征值问题。这个约化同时暴露了核附近边界条件、离心势、径向节点、芯态与价态的空间尺度，以及原子自洽计算的数值结构。

Martin 在此不追求完整的原子光谱学。原子被用作凝聚态电子结构的准备问题：Chapter 11 的赝势从全电子原子参考态出发；Chapter 16 的增广方法需要原子式部分波；原子球近似则把径向方程连接到能带宽度与体积依赖的定性判断。

前置知识包括定态 Schrödinger 方程、角动量本征值、Kohn–Sham 方程、Hartree 势、交换关联势和自洽迭代。正文采用 Hartree 原子单位，除非另有说明：$\hbar=m_e=e=4\pi\varepsilon_0=1$，能量单位为 Hartree，长度单位为 Bohr。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

This chapter restricts a general three-dimensional independent-particle equation to a central atomic potential. Spherical symmetry assigns the angular variables to spherical harmonics and converts the original partial differential equation into a one-dimensional radial eigenproblem for each angular-momentum channel. The reduction exposes the nuclear boundary condition, centrifugal potential, radial nodes, the different length scales of core and valence states, and the numerical structure of a self-consistent atomic calculation.

Martin does not attempt a complete treatment of atomic spectroscopy. The atom is used as preparation for condensed-matter electronic structure: Chapter 11 constructs pseudopotentials from all-electron atomic reference states; the augmentation methods of Chapter 16 require atom-like partial waves; and the atomic-sphere approximation connects radial equations to qualitative estimates of band width and volume dependence.

The prerequisites are the stationary Schrödinger equation, angular-momentum eigenvalues, the Kohn–Sham equation, Hartree and exchange-correlation potentials, and self-consistent iteration. Unless stated otherwise, the exposition uses Hartree atomic units, $\hbar=m_e=e=4\pi\varepsilon_0=1$, with energy in Hartree and length in Bohr.

## 物理对象与符号 | Physical Objects and Notation

<BilingualSection titleZh="同一轨道的三种径向表达" titleEn="Three radial representations of one orbital">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

对中心势中的空间轨道，本文采用

$$
\psi_{nlm}(\mathbf r)=R_{nl}(r)Y_{lm}(\Omega)
=\frac{u_{nl}(r)}{r}Y_{lm}(\Omega),\qquad \Omega=(\theta,\phi).
$$

$\psi_{nlm}$ 是三维轨道；$R_{nl}$ 是径向函数；$u_{nl}=rR_{nl}$ 是约化径向函数。Martin 的记号把 $R_{nl}$ 写成 $\psi_{nl}(r)$，把 $u_{nl}$ 写成 $\phi_{nl}(r)$。网站使用 $R$ 与 $u$，并在引用原书公式时注明映射。

$|R_{nl}(r)|^2$ 本身不是半径落在 $[r,r+dr]$ 的概率。完成角积分后，径向概率为

$$
P_{nl}(r)\,dr=r^2|R_{nl}(r)|^2dr=|u_{nl}(r)|^2dr.
$$

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For a spatial orbital in a central potential, this chapter uses

$$
\psi_{nlm}(\mathbf r)=R_{nl}(r)Y_{lm}(\Omega)
=\frac{u_{nl}(r)}{r}Y_{lm}(\Omega),\qquad \Omega=(\theta,\phi).
$$

Here $\psi_{nlm}$ is the three-dimensional orbital, $R_{nl}$ is the radial function, and $u_{nl}=rR_{nl}$ is the reduced radial function. Martin denotes $R_{nl}$ by $\psi_{nl}(r)$ and $u_{nl}$ by $\phi_{nl}(r)$. The website uses $R$ and $u$ and states the mapping when it refers to Martin’s equations.

The quantity $|R_{nl}(r)|^2$ alone is not the probability for the radius to lie in $[r,r+dr]$. After integrating over the angles, the radial probability is

$$
P_{nl}(r)\,dr=r^2|R_{nl}(r)|^2dr=|u_{nl}(r)|^2dr.
$$

  </div>
</BilingualSection>
