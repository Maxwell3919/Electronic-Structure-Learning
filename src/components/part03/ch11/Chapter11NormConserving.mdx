## 11.4 范数守恒赝势 | Norm-Conserving Pseudopotentials

<BilingualSection titleZh="从单点散射匹配到一阶能量响应" titleEn="From one-energy matching to first-order energy response" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

范数守恒方法选择一个原子参考组态，并为每个价电子角动量通道指定参考能量和芯半径。全电子与赝参考本征值相同；在芯半径外，两条径向函数相同；边界函数值和斜率连续，因此对数导数相同；芯半径内的径向范数也相同。归一化后的赝态由此在成键区直接等于全电子价态，而不再只是 OPW 中未归一化的平滑分量。

关键联系是：芯内范数相同不仅保存一项积分，也保存边界对数导数对能量的一阶导数。因此参考能量附近的小能量移动或小势变化可以得到正确的一阶散射响应。该结论是局部展开，不是整个能谱上的精确等价。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A norm-conserving construction selects an atomic reference configuration and assigns a reference energy and core radius to each valence angular-momentum channel. The all-electron and pseudo reference eigenvalues agree; the radial functions are equal outside the core; value and slope are continuous at the boundary, giving equal logarithmic derivatives; and the radial norms inside the core are equal. The normalized pseudo state therefore equals the all-electron valence state directly in the bonding region, rather than being only the unnormalized smooth component of an OPW transformation.

The central connection is that equal core norms preserve not only an integral but also the first energy derivative of the boundary logarithmic derivative. Small changes of energy or potential around the reference state can therefore have the correct first-order scattering response. This is a local expansion, not exact equivalence over the complete spectrum.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<div class="table-wrap">

| Condition | Mathematical statement at the reference state | What it controls |
|---|---|---|
| Reference eigenvalue | $\varepsilon_l^{PS}=\varepsilon_l^{AE}$ | Energy at the generating configuration. |
| Exterior wavefunction | $u_l^{PS}(r)=u_l^{AE}(r)$ for $r\ge r_c$ | Bonding-region amplitude and phase at the reference energy. |
| Boundary logarithmic derivative | $D_l^{PS}(\varepsilon_l,r_c)=D_l^{AE}(\varepsilon_l,r_c)$ | Exterior scattering solution at that energy. |
| Interior norm | $\int_0^{r_c}\lvert u_l^{PS}\rvert^2dr=\int_0^{r_c}\lvert u_l^{AE}\rvert^2dr$ | Normalized exterior amplitude and first energy response. |
| First energy derivative | $\partial_E D_l^{PS}=\partial_E D_l^{AE}$ at $\varepsilon_l$ | Linearized scattering response near the reference energy. |

</div>

<DerivationBlock
  title="核心推导 | Core derivation: 范数守恒与对数导数的能量导数"
  assumptions={[
    '实球对称局域参考势，固定角动量 l | A real spherical local reference potential at fixed l.',
    '约化径向函数 u(E,r) 在原点满足规则边界条件 | The reduced radial function u(E,r) is regular at the origin.',
    '比较时边界函数 u(E,R) 非零 | The boundary value u(E,R) is nonzero.',
  ]}
>

约化径向方程写成

$$
-\frac12\frac{d^2u}{dr^2}
+\left[\frac{l(l+1)}{2r^2}+V(r)\right]u
=Eu.
$$

对能量求导，记 $u_E=\partial u/\partial E$：

$$
-\frac12u_E''+
\left[\frac{l(l+1)}{2r^2}+V-E\right]u_E=u.
$$

原方程乘 $u_E$，能量导数方程乘 $u$，两式相减。势和离心项完全消去：

$$
-\frac12\left(u_E''u-u''u_E\right)=u^2.
$$

括号是 Wronskian 的导数，因为

$$
\frac{d}{dr}\left(u_E'u-u'u_E\right)
=u_E''u-u''u_E.
$$

从 $0$ 积分到任意匹配半径 $R$。规则解满足 $u(0)=0$，相应原点边界项为零：

$$
-\frac12\left[u_E'(R)u(R)-u'(R)u_E(R)\right]
=\int_0^R u(E,r)^2dr.
$$

定义约化径向函数的对数斜率 $L=u'/u$。对能量求导：

$$
\frac{\partial L(E,R)}{\partial E}
=\frac{u_E'(R)u(R)-u'(R)u_E(R)}{u(R)^2}
=-\frac{2}{u(R)^2}\int_0^R u(E,r)^2dr.
$$

若使用 Martin 的三维径向对数导数

$$
D_l(E,R)=R\frac{R_l'}{R_l}
=R\frac{u_l'}{u_l}-1,
$$

则

$$
\boxed{
\frac{\partial D_l(E,R)}{\partial E}
=-\frac{2R}{u_l(E,R)^2}
\int_0^R u_l(E,r)^2dr
}.
$$

不同单位制或径向方程写法可能把公共因子 2 吸收到能量定义中；两边使用同一单位制时，比较结论不变。若 AE 与 PS 在 $R=r_c$ 具有相同边界幅度并满足相同芯内范数，右侧相同，因此 $\partial_E D_l$ 相同。结合外区相移与 $D_l$ 的一一关系，赝势在参考能量附近重现相移的一阶展开。

<p lang="en">Differentiating the radial equation with respect to energy and subtracting the original equation multiplied by the energy derivative removes the potential and centrifugal terms. The remaining expression is a Wronskian derivative. Integration from the regular origin to $R$ relates the energy derivative of the boundary logarithmic slope to the interior radial norm. Equal boundary amplitude and equal interior norm therefore imply equal first energy derivatives of the logarithmic derivative. Unit conventions may redistribute a common factor, but the equality statement is unchanged when both calculations use the same convention.</p>

该推导不能证明二阶及更高能量响应相同，也不覆盖非局域 Hartree–Fock/EXX 算符的全部外区作用；这些边界分别由多投影子扩展和非局域交换问题处理。

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="范数守恒的证据上限" titleEn="Evidence ceiling of norm conservation" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>范数守恒是在生成参考态附近的数学约束。它不验证冻结芯近似、不选择正确的价电子/semicore 划分，也不检测可分离形式中的 ghost state。不同化学环境仍需显式测试。</p>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>Norm conservation is a mathematical constraint local to the generating reference state. It does not validate the frozen-core approximation, select the correct valence/semicore partition, or detect ghost states in a separable representation. Different chemical environments still require explicit tests.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 11.5 角动量依赖 NCPP 的生成 | Generation of l-Dependent NCPPs

<BilingualSection titleZh="参考原子、赝化与径向反演" titleEn="Reference atom, pseudization, and radial inversion" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

生成流程从 Chapter 10 的全电子自洽原子开始。必须固定元素、交换—关联泛函、电子组态、价电子与芯电子划分、相对论级别和每个通道的参考能量。随后为每个 $l$ 构造无芯节点的光滑赝径向函数，使其在 $r_c$ 外与全电子函数相同，并满足所选连续性和范数条件。不同生成方法的区别主要体现在芯内参数化、匹配导数数目和优化目标。

给定赝函数和参考能量，可以反演径向方程得到 screened channel potential。反演要求分母不在构造区内产生未处理的零点；这也是赝函数通常选择无节点的原因。反演得到的是参考原子中已经被价电子屏蔽的势，还不能直接作为可迁移离子势。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Generation begins with the self-consistent all-electron atom of Chapter 10. Element, exchange–correlation functional, electronic configuration, valence/core partition, relativistic level, and channel reference energies must be fixed. A smooth core-nodeless pseudo radial function is then constructed for each $l$, equal to the all-electron function outside $r_c$ and satisfying the selected continuity and norm constraints. Generation schemes differ mainly in the core parameterization, number of matched derivatives, and optimization objective.

Given the pseudo function and reference energy, the radial equation can be inverted to obtain a screened channel potential. The denominator must not contain untreated zeros in the construction region, which is why the pseudo function is normally chosen nodeless. The inverted potential is still screened by the valence electrons of the reference atom and is not yet the transferable ionic potential.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="补充推导 | Supplemental derivation: 从赝径向函数反演通道势"
  assumptions={[
    '给定非零的参考赝径向函数 u_l^PS 与能量 epsilon_l | A nonzero reference pseudo radial function and energy are prescribed.',
    '采用局域径向通道方程；非局域通道耦合在算符组装时处理 | A local radial channel equation is used; nonlocal channel selection is handled in the operator assembly.',
  ]}
>

参考通道满足

$$
-\frac12\frac{d^2u_l^{PS}}{dr^2}
+\left[\frac{l(l+1)}{2r^2}+V_l^{scr}(r)\right]u_l^{PS}
=\varepsilon_lu_l^{PS}.
$$

把势项单独移到一边并除以 $u_l^{PS}$：

$$
\boxed{
V_l^{scr}(r)=
\varepsilon_l+
\frac12\frac{u_l^{PS\prime\prime}(r)}{u_l^{PS}(r)}
-\frac{l(l+1)}{2r^2}
}.
$$

量纲检查：$u''/u$ 和 $r^{-2}$ 都是长度的负二次方，在原子单位中等价于能量。$r\to0$ 时，赝函数的幂次必须与离心项相消，使所得势有限或满足声明的光滑条件。在 $r\ge r_c$，AE 与 PS 函数和能量相同，反演势恢复全电子参考势。

数值实现通常在径向网格上计算导数。二阶导数会放大网格噪声，因此“函数看起来平滑”不足以验收；应检查网格收敛、边界导数连续性、反演势稳定性和重新求解后的本征值/波函数复现。

<p lang="en">Rearranging the reference radial equation and dividing by the prescribed nodeless pseudo function gives the screened channel potential. Both $u''/u$ and the centrifugal term have energy dimensions in atomic units. Core power-law behavior must cancel the centrifugal singularity according to the declared construction. Numerical differentiation amplifies grid noise, so visual smoothness is not an acceptance test; the inverted potential must reproduce the reference solution under converged radial discretization.</p>

</DerivationBlock>

选取一个公共局域分量后，通道势分解为

$$
V_l^{ion}(r)=V_{loc}(r)+\Delta V_l(r),
$$

其中所有长程离子 Coulomb 尾放入 $V_{loc}$，$\Delta V_l$ 在芯区外消失。半局域离子算符为

$$
\hat V_{SL}^{ion}=V_{loc}(r)+
\sum_{lm}|Y_{lm}\rangle\Delta V_l(r)\langle Y_{lm}|.
$$

相对论生成必须从相应的全电子参考开始。scalar-relativistic 势包含质量—速度和 Darwin 型标量效应；fully relativistic 势还保留 $j=l\pm1/2$ 通道差。使用非相对论文件时，后续打开 SOC 并不会凭空恢复缺失的核区相对论散射信息。

## 11.6 去屏蔽与芯修正 | Unscreening and Core Corrections

<BilingualSection titleZh="screened potential 不是 ionic potential" titleEn="A screened potential is not an ionic potential" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

径向反演得到的 $V_l^{scr}$ 已经包含参考价电子密度产生的 Hartree 和交换—关联势。若把它直接带入分子或固体，并再次由新价电子密度计算这些项，就会重复计入屏蔽。去屏蔽的目标是提取只代表原子核与冻结芯作用的 ionic pseudopotential，再让新环境的价电子自洽地产生自己的屏蔽。

Hartree 势对密度是线性的，芯与价电子贡献可以直接相加减。交换—关联势通常是密度的非线性泛函，芯—价重叠区不能用独立的“芯 XC 势 + 价 XC 势”精确分解。nonlinear core correction 因而在计算 XC 时保留一个声明的芯密度或平滑 partial-core density。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The inverted $V_l^{scr}$ already contains the Hartree and exchange–correlation potentials generated by the reference valence density. Using it directly in a molecule or solid and recomputing those contributions from the new valence density would count screening twice. Unscreening extracts an ionic pseudopotential representing the nucleus plus frozen core, after which valence electrons in the new environment generate their own self-consistent screening.

The Hartree potential is linear in the density, so core and valence contributions can be added or subtracted directly. Exchange–correlation potentials are generally nonlinear density functionals, and the core–valence overlap region cannot be decomposed exactly into independent core and valence XC potentials. A nonlinear core correction therefore retains a declared core density, or a smooth partial-core density, when evaluating XC.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

对一个局域/半局域密度泛函，生成关系可写成

$$
V_l^{scr}(r)=V_l^{ion}(r)
+V_H[n_v^{PS}](r)
+V_{xc}[n_v^{PS}+n_c^{part}](r).
$$

因此

$$
\boxed{
V_l^{ion}(r)=V_l^{scr}(r)
-V_H[n_v^{PS}](r)
-V_{xc}[n_v^{PS}+n_c^{part}](r)
}.
$$

在实际固体计算中，XC 部分使用新的自洽价密度加同一 partial-core 数据：

$$
V_{xc}^{solid}(\mathbf r)
=V_{xc}[n_v^{solid}(\mathbf r)+n_c^{part}(\mathbf r)].
$$

若 $n_c^{part}=0$，得到无 NLCC 的冻结芯形式。partial core 必须足够平滑以适合所用基组和 FFT 网格，同时在芯—价重叠区保留相关密度尺度。它是生成数据的一部分，不能从文件名或元素符号猜测。

<BilingualCallout titleZh="非局域交换的额外边界" titleEn="Additional boundary for nonlocal exchange" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>Hartree–Fock 或 exact exchange 的交换算符在空间上非局域，芯态影响不严格局限于 $r_c$。局域 DFT 中“核外波函数完全相同”的构造关系不能原样推广。使用混合泛函或其他非局域算符时，应依赖与该方法兼容的正式数据和验证，而不是只检查 LDA/GGA 原子匹配。</p>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>Hartree–Fock or exact-exchange operators are spatially nonlocal, so core effects do not end sharply at $r_c$. The local-DFT construction statement of exactly equal exterior wavefunctions does not transfer unchanged. Hybrid or other nonlocal calculations require formally compatible data and validation rather than only an LDA/GGA atomic match.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 11.7 可迁移性与硬度 | Transferability and Hardness

<BilingualSection titleZh="生成条件与测试条件必须不同" titleEn="Generating and testing conditions must differ" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

transferability 指赝势离开生成原子组态后仍能重现所声明的全电子价态性质。只在生成组态上复现本征值、波函数和范数属于构造自洽检查，不是独立可迁移性测试。最低成本测试应改变电子占据、离化状态或参考能量，并比较激发能、对数导数和散射行为。进一步测试可覆盖分子键、不同晶体结构、体积范围、磁态、力、应力和声子。

hardness 常被用于两类概念。数值 hardness 指赝函数高频 Fourier 尾和达到目标精度所需的基组截断；物理响应意义上的“硬/刚性芯”指冻结芯在环境变化下是否保持有效。较小 $r_c$ 往往改善近核描述但增加平面波成本；较大 $r_c$ 往往更软，却增加芯区重叠和环境依赖风险。没有单调的“越软越好”或“越硬越准”。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Transferability means that a pseudopotential continues to reproduce declared all-electron valence properties outside its generating atomic configuration. Reproducing the generating eigenvalues, wavefunctions, and norms is an internal construction check, not an independent transferability test. A low-cost test changes occupations, ionization, or reference energy and compares excitation energies, logarithmic derivatives, and scattering behavior. Further tests may cover molecular bonds, multiple crystal structures, volume ranges, magnetic states, forces, stresses, and phonons.

Hardness is used for two distinct ideas. Numerical hardness refers to the high-frequency Fourier tail of pseudo functions and the basis cutoff required for a target accuracy. Physical core rigidity asks whether the frozen core remains adequate as the environment changes. A smaller $r_c$ often improves near-core fidelity but raises plane-wave cost; a larger $r_c$ is often softer but increases core-overlap and environmental risks. Neither “softer is always better” nor “harder is always more accurate” is valid.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

Martin 的 reciprocal-space 指标以赝径向函数的变换为起点：

$$
\psi_l(q)=\int_0^\infty dr\,r^2j_l(qr)\psi_l(r).
$$

给定倒空间截断 $q_c$，一种 residual kinetic energy 指标为

$$
E_l^{res}(q_c)=\int_{q_c}^{\infty}dq\,q^4|\psi_l(q)|^2.
$$

它衡量被截断高频分量携带的动能权重，可作为优化目标。该指标只针对特定赝函数和表示；真实总能、力或声子误差还取决于所有通道、局域势、密度/增广截断、k 点和自洽精度。
