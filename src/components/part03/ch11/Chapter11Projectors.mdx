## 11.8 可分离赝势算符与投影子 | Separable Pseudopotential Operators and Projectors

<BilingualSection titleZh="为什么改写半局域算符" titleEn="Why rewrite a semilocal operator" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

半局域算符需要把每个待作用波函数分解为球谐通道，并为每个波函数对执行径向积分。可分离形式把核区信息压缩为有限个局域投影子：先计算每个投影子与波函数的重叠，再对这些有限维系数做矩阵运算，最后重构非局域修正。对大规模平面波计算，这通常比逐通道双函数积分便宜。

改写必须在生成参考态上保持原通道作用。它对其他能量和其他径向形状的作用可能改变，因此 separability 是额外近似层。局域分量的选择、投影子分母的符号和算符谱都可能影响 ghost states；计算更快不能作为科学验收。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A semilocal operator requires each target wavefunction to be resolved into spherical-harmonic channels and radial integrals to be evaluated for every pair of functions. A separable form compresses the core information into a finite set of localized projectors: calculate overlaps with the state, apply a finite-dimensional matrix to those coefficients, and reconstruct the nonlocal correction. In large plane-wave calculations this is usually cheaper than repeated channel-resolved pair integrals.

The rewrite must reproduce the original channel action on the generating reference state. Its action on other energies and radial shapes can change, so separability is an additional approximation layer. The local component, projector denominator signs, and operator spectrum can all affect ghost states; computational speed is not a scientific acceptance criterion.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="核心推导 | Core derivation: 单投影子可分离形式"
  assumptions={[
    '已生成半局域通道 V_l=V_loc+Delta V_l | Semilocal channels have been generated and split into local and channel corrections.',
    '每个 l,m 选一个参考赝态 | One reference pseudo state is selected for each l,m.',
    '分母不为零 | The projector denominator is nonzero.',
  ]}
>

半局域非局域部分对参考态的作用是

$$
\Delta\hat V_{SL}|\phi_{lm}\rangle
=\Delta V_l(r)|\phi_{lm}\rangle.
$$

定义局域投影函数

$$
|\beta_{lm}\rangle
=\Delta V_l|\phi_{lm}\rangle,
$$

以及标量分母

$$
D_l=\langle\phi_{lm}|\Delta V_l|\phi_{lm}\rangle
=\langle\phi_{lm}|\beta_{lm}\rangle.
$$

构造有限秩算符

$$
\Delta\hat V_{NL}
=\sum_{lm}\frac{|\beta_{lm}\rangle\langle\beta_{lm}|}{D_l}.
$$

把它作用在同一参考态：

$$
\Delta\hat V_{NL}|\phi_{lm}\rangle
=\frac{|\beta_{lm}\rangle
\langle\beta_{lm}|\phi_{lm}\rangle}{D_l}.
$$

由于势和参考径向函数可取为实，

$$
\langle\beta_{lm}|\phi_{lm}\rangle
=\langle\phi_{lm}|\Delta V_l|\phi_{lm}\rangle
=D_l,
$$

所以

$$
\Delta\hat V_{NL}|\phi_{lm}\rangle
=|\beta_{lm}\rangle
=\Delta V_l|\phi_{lm}\rangle.
$$

总算符因此为

$$
\boxed{
\hat V_{KB}=V_{loc}(r)+
\sum_{lm}\frac{|\beta_{lm}\rangle\langle\beta_{lm}|}{D_l}
}.
$$

对一般状态 $|\psi\rangle$，作用过程是先求 $c_{lm}=\langle\beta_{lm}|\psi\rangle$，再形成 $\sum_{lm}|\beta_{lm}\rangle c_{lm}/D_l$。投影子只在 $\Delta V_l$ 非零的芯区有支撑，因而每个重叠是局域积分。

<p lang="en">Defining a projector as the channel correction acting on its reference pseudo state produces a rank-one operator. Its denominator is selected so the separable operator acts exactly like the semilocal channel on that reference state. For a general state, the action reduces to localized projector overlaps followed by reconstruction. Exactness on the reference state does not imply exactness on other states.</p>

</DerivationBlock>

  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>ghost state 是可分离赝 Hamiltonian 出现的非物理解，可能落在占据态附近或目标能量窗口中。它不能由原子参考本征值复现、文件可读取或 SCF 收敛排除。至少应扫描各通道的对数导数/本征谱，并测试局域通道选择。若局域势过于吸引而非局域修正主要排斥，低能 ghost 风险尤其需要检查。</p>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>A ghost state is an unphysical eigenstate of the separable pseudo-Hamiltonian and can lie near occupied states or within the target energy window. Reproducing the atomic reference eigenvalue, parsing the file, or converging the SCF does not exclude it. Channel logarithmic derivatives or spectra must be scanned and local-channel choices tested. A strongly attractive local potential combined with mainly repulsive nonlocal corrections requires particular care.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

相对论可分离算符使用由 Dirac 原子参考生成的 $j=l\pm1/2$ 投影子。把 scalar-relativistic 通道平均与 spin–orbit 通道差组合后，后续代码仍需使用与数据一致的自旋量子结构。fully relativistic 文件与非相对论文件不能只凭元素和 XC 名称互换。

## 11.9 扩展范数守恒：超出线性区 | Extended Norm Conservation: Beyond the Linear Regime

<BilingualSection titleZh="单参考能量的限制" titleEn="The limitation of one reference energy" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

单投影子 NCPP 在参考能量处匹配函数和一阶响应；离开该能量后，高阶相移误差可能迅速增长，尤其当浅芯态、semicore 与价态同时需要描述时。一条路线是直接约束对数导数关于能量的更高阶展开；另一条更易实现的路线是在同一 $l$ 通道选取多个参考能量和多个投影子，使若干参考态都成为同一赝 Hamiltonian 的解。

多投影子扩大了声明的能量范围，但并不自动覆盖任意高能散射。投影子之间形成有限矩阵，必须检查线性独立性、条件数和目标窗口内的谱。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A one-projector NCPP matches the function and first response at one reference energy. Higher-order phase-shift errors may grow away from that point, especially when shallow core, semicore, and valence states must all be represented. One route constrains higher derivatives of the energy expansion directly. A more practical route uses several reference energies and projectors in the same $l$ channel so multiple reference states are solutions of one pseudo-Hamiltonian.

Multiple projectors enlarge the declared energy range but do not provide arbitrary high-energy scattering accuracy. The projectors form a finite matrix whose linear independence, conditioning, and spectrum in the target window must be checked.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="补充推导 | Supplemental derivation: 多投影子有限矩阵"
  assumptions={[
    '同一 l 通道有参考态 |phi_s> 与能量 epsilon_s | Several reference states and energies are available in one l channel.',
    '选择一个公共 local Hamiltonian H0 | A common local Hamiltonian H0 is selected.',
    '重叠矩阵 B 可逆 | The finite overlap matrix B is invertible.',
  ]}
>

定义 residual functions

$$
|\chi_s\rangle=(\varepsilon_s-\hat H_0)|\phi_s\rangle,
$$

以及有限矩阵

$$
B_{ss'}=\langle\phi_s|\chi_{s'}\rangle.
$$

构造对偶投影子

$$
|\beta_s\rangle=\sum_{s'}|\chi_{s'}\rangle(B^{-1})_{s's}.
$$

于是

$$
\langle\phi_t|\beta_s\rangle
=\sum_{s'}B_{ts'}(B^{-1})_{s's}=\delta_{ts}.
$$

一种等价的非局域修正写为

$$
\Delta\hat V_{NL}
=\sum_{ss'}|\beta_s\rangle B_{ss'}\langle\beta_{s'}|.
$$

作用在任意参考态 $|\phi_t\rangle$：

$$
\Delta\hat V_{NL}|\phi_t\rangle
=\sum_{ss'}|\beta_s\rangle B_{ss'}\delta_{s't}
=\sum_s|\beta_s\rangle B_{st}
=|\chi_t\rangle.
$$

因此

$$
(\hat H_0+\Delta\hat V_{NL})|\phi_t\rangle
=\hat H_0|\phi_t\rangle+|\chi_t\rangle
=\varepsilon_t|\phi_t\rangle.
$$

该推导说明多个参考态可以被同一有限秩算符精确复现。它依赖 $B$ 可逆；近线性相关投影子会放大数值误差。实际形式可能采用不同但等价的矩阵规范，必须以具体生成器和文件格式定义为准。

<p lang="en">Residual functions are defined relative to a common local Hamiltonian. Inverting their finite overlap matrix produces dual projectors. The resulting finite-rank correction returns the appropriate residual for every reference state, so all selected states solve the same pseudo-Hamiltonian. The construction requires an invertible, well-conditioned finite matrix; equivalent implementations may use different projector normalizations.</p>

</DerivationBlock>

## 11.10 优化范数守恒势 | Optimized Norm-Conserving Potentials

<BilingualSection titleZh="ONCV 的三个约束层" titleEn="Three constraint layers in ONCV" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

optimized norm-conserving Vanderbilt（ONCV）把三类目标放在同一构造中：保留范数守恒的普通正交结构；在同一 $l$ 通道使用多个投影子扩大能量范围；在满足边界连续性和参考条件的剩余自由度中，最小化高频 Fourier/残余动能指标。它因此能够在保持 NCPP 使用形式的同时，缓解单投影子误差和过高截断成本。

“optimized” 只说明相对于指定目标函数、芯半径、参考态和连续性约束做了优化。不同属性的误差函数并不相同；原子 residual kinetic energy 最小不能替代固体方程状态、磁性、ghost、声子或响应测试。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Optimized norm-conserving Vanderbilt (ONCV) construction combines three layers: ordinary norm-conserving orthogonality is retained; multiple projectors in one $l$ channel enlarge the energy range; and remaining freedom under boundary and reference constraints is used to minimize a high-frequency Fourier or residual-kinetic-energy objective. It can therefore reduce one-projector errors and excessive cutoff cost while preserving the usual NCPP usage form.

“Optimized” means optimized for a specified objective, core radius, reference set, and continuity constraints. Different properties have different error functionals. Minimizing an atomic residual kinetic energy does not replace solid equation-of-state, magnetism, ghost-state, phonon, or response tests.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

一个典型约束优化问题可抽象为

$$
\min_{u_l^{PS}}\quad
E_l^{res}(q_c)
=\int_{q_c}^{\infty}dq\,q^4|\psi_l^{PS}(q)|^2,
$$

满足

$$
\begin{aligned}
u_l^{PS}(r_c)&=u_l^{AE}(r_c),\\
u_l^{PS\prime}(r_c)&=u_l^{AE\prime}(r_c),\\
\int_0^{r_c}|u_l^{PS}|^2dr&=
\int_0^{r_c}|u_l^{AE}|^2dr,
\end{aligned}
$$

并可加入更多边界导数与多个参考态条件。数值上，辅助基函数展开把条件写成有限维线性约束，再在其零空间内优化。验收时应把“生成目标函数下降”“原子测试通过”“固体目标量收敛”分别记录。

<BilingualCallout titleZh="多投影子不是任意 semicore 的通行证" titleEn="Multiple projectors are not a license for arbitrary semicore freezing" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>多投影子可改善同一通道的能量依赖，但不能消除错误的 core–valence partition。若 semicore 在成键、压力、氧化态、磁性或响应中参与明显，应把它纳入价空间并重新评估 cutoff，而不是仅增加一个高能投影子。</p>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>Multiple projectors improve energy dependence within a channel but cannot repair an inappropriate core–valence partition. If semicore states participate materially in bonding, pressure response, oxidation, magnetism, or other observables, they should be placed in the valence space and the cutoff reassessed rather than hidden behind an additional high-energy projector.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualCallout>
