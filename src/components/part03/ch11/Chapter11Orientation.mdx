## 阅读定位 | Reading Orientation

<BilingualSection titleZh="从全电子原子到有效离子算符" titleEn="From the all-electron atom to an effective ionic operator" eyebrow="Orientation">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Chapter 10 给出了核附近强 Coulomb 势中的全电子径向参考问题。该参考问题同时带来两个数值困难：芯态结合很深；价态必须与芯态正交，因此在核附近出现快速振荡和节点。平面波表示要解析这些短尺度结构，所需动能截断会很高。

本章研究一个受限替换问题：选定价电子空间、交换—关联近似、相对论处理和参考能量范围后，用较平滑的有效离子算符替代原子核与冻结芯电子的作用，同时保留指定的价电子散射、能量响应和核外波函数。替换的可靠性取决于构造条件和后续测试，不能由“赝势”这一名称自动保证。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Chapter 10 supplied the all-electron radial reference problem in the strong Coulomb field near a nucleus. That reference creates two numerical difficulties: core states are deeply bound, and valence states must remain orthogonal to them, producing rapid oscillations and nodes near the nucleus. A plane-wave representation must resolve these short length scales and can therefore require a very high kinetic-energy cutoff.

This chapter studies a restricted replacement problem. After choosing a valence space, exchange–correlation approximation, relativistic treatment, reference configuration, and energy range, the nucleus plus frozen core is replaced by a smoother effective ionic operator while selected valence scattering properties, energy response, and exterior wavefunctions are retained. Reliability follows from construction conditions and subsequent tests; it is not guaranteed by the label “pseudopotential.”

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## 物理对象与符号 | Physical Objects and Notation

<BilingualSection titleZh="参考问题、替代对象与适用范围" titleEn="Reference problem, replacement object, and validity range">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

本章默认 Hartree 原子单位，除非另行说明。球对称参考原子的约化径向函数记为 $u_l(E,r)=rR_l(E,r)$；Martin 常用 $\phi_l=r\psi_l$，两者在本页承担同一径向角色。$r_c$ 是给定角动量通道的芯半径。全电子量使用上标 AE，赝量使用 PS；波浪号表示 ultrasoft 或 PAW 中的辅助平滑量。

全电子有效势包含核吸引以及参考原子自洽产生的电子项。screened pseudopotential 是从某一原子参考组态反演得到、仍含价电子 Hartree 与交换—关联屏蔽的通道势。ionic pseudopotential 是将这些价电子屏蔽项去除后用于新环境的离子算符。local、semilocal 和 separable nonlocal 指算符结构，不是“经验”或“第一性原理”的质量等级。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Hartree atomic units are used unless stated otherwise. The reduced radial function of the spherical reference atom is written $u_l(E,r)=rR_l(E,r)$; Martin commonly writes $\phi_l=r\psi_l$, which plays the same radial role here. The channel core radius is $r_c$. All-electron quantities carry AE and pseudo quantities carry PS; a tilde denotes an auxiliary smooth quantity in an ultrasoft or PAW construction.

The all-electron effective potential contains nuclear attraction and the electronic terms generated by the self-consistent reference atom. A screened pseudopotential is the channel potential inverted from that reference calculation and still contains valence Hartree and exchange–correlation screening. The ionic pseudopotential is the operator used in a new environment after those valence screening contributions are removed. Local, semilocal, and separable nonlocal describe operator structure rather than a quality ranking between empirical and first-principles methods.

  </div>
