Martin 第 2 版印刷式 (11.24) 把固定势下的 Riccati 方程写为

$$
\frac{\partial x_l}{\partial r}+x_l^2
=\frac{l(l+1)}{r^2}+2\,[V(r)-\varepsilon].
$$

但紧接的印刷式 (11.25) 在对同一能量变量求导时把右端写为 $-1$，后续式 (11.27)–(11.28) 也相应不含因子 $2$。若 $V$ 在求导时固定，直接对上式求导应得到

$$
\frac{\partial}{\partial r}
\left(\frac{\partial x_l}{\partial\varepsilon}\right)
+2x_l\frac{\partial x_l}{\partial\varepsilon}
=-2.
$$

因此，本站前文从 Hartree 原子单位径向方程

$$
\left[-\frac12\frac{d^2}{dr^2}+U_l(r)\right]u=Eu
$$

重新进行 Wronskian 推导，并保留

$$
\frac{\partial D_l(E,R)}{\partial E}
=-\frac{2R}{u(E,R)^2}\int_0^R u(E,r)^2dr.
$$

若另行定义 Rydberg 标度能量 $E_{\mathrm{Ry}}=2E_{\mathrm{Ha}}$，则 $\partial/\partial E_{\mathrm{Ry}}=(1/2)\partial/\partial E_{\mathrm{Ha}}$，同一关系对 $E_{\mathrm{Ry}}$ 的导数会出现系数 $-1$。这种改写必须同时声明能量变量和径向方程的标度，不能在同一个 $E$ 定义下混用两个系数。

这里把原书相邻公式之间的差异记录为<strong>印刷因子不一致或未声明的能量标度歧义</strong>，而不擅自断定作者意图。范数守恒所需的稳健结论不受影响：只要全电子与赝问题使用同一能量变量、同一单位制，并在匹配半径具有相同边界幅度与芯内范数，它们的对数导数能量响应就相同。

In the printed second edition, Eq. (11.24) writes the fixed-potential Riccati equation as

$$
\frac{\partial x_l}{\partial r}+x_l^2
=\frac{l(l+1)}{r^2}+2\,[V(r)-\varepsilon].
$$

The immediately following printed Eq. (11.25), however, gives $-1$ on the right after differentiation with respect to the same energy variable, and Eqs. (11.27)–(11.28) consequently contain no factor of two. Holding $V$ fixed and differentiating Eq. (11.24) directly gives

$$
\frac{\partial}{\partial r}
\left(\frac{\partial x_l}{\partial\varepsilon}\right)
+2x_l\frac{\partial x_l}{\partial\varepsilon}
=-2.
$$

The derivation above therefore starts again from the Hartree-atomic-unit radial equation

$$
\left[-\frac12\frac{d^2}{dr^2}+U_l(r)\right]u=Eu
$$

and retains

$$
\frac{\partial D_l(E,R)}{\partial E}
=-\frac{2R}{u(E,R)^2}\int_0^R u(E,r)^2dr.
$$

If one separately defines a Rydberg-scaled energy $E_{\mathrm{Ry}}=2E_{\mathrm{Ha}}$, then $\partial/\partial E_{\mathrm{Ry}}=(1/2)\partial/\partial E_{\mathrm{Ha}}$, and the derivative with respect to $E_{\mathrm{Ry}}$ carries the coefficient $-1$. Such a rewrite must rescale both the energy variable and the radial equation explicitly; the two coefficients cannot be mixed under one definition of $E$.

The site records the adjacent printed formulas as a <strong>factor inconsistency or an unstated energy-scaling ambiguity</strong>, without claiming knowledge of the author’s intended correction. The convention-independent norm-conservation conclusion remains intact: when the all-electron and pseudo problems use the same energy variable and units, equal boundary amplitude and equal interior norm imply equal energy response of the logarithmic derivative.
