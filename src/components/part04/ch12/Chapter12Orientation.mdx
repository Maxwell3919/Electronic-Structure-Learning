## 阅读定位 | Reading Orientation

<BilingualSection titleZh="本章解决的判断问题" titleEn="The judgment problem addressed here" eyebrow="Orientation">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

连续的独立粒子方程定义在无限维函数空间中，计算机却只能保存有限个数。Chapter 12 研究这一步如何发生：选择周期边界条件后，波函数可在平面波中展开；选择实空间网格后，微分算符可由有限 stencil、单元基函数或多分辨率函数表示。两条路线处理的是同一个连续方程，但截断参数、矩阵结构和误差传播不同。

本章的目标不是给出一套完整的自洽 DFT 输入流程。读完后，应能从一个平面波或网格计算中辨认连续 Hamiltonian、有限矩阵、基组截断、网格分辨率和求解误差，并说明这些量为什么不能与赝势误差、交换关联泛函误差或科学结论混为一谈。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A continuous independent-particle equation is defined in an infinite-dimensional function space, whereas a computer can retain only finitely many numbers. Chapter 12 examines how that reduction is made. With periodic boundary conditions, a wavefunction can be expanded in plane waves. On a real-space grid, differential operators can be represented by finite stencils, element-local basis functions, or multiresolution functions. Both routes address the same continuous equation, but their truncation parameters, matrix structures, and error propagation differ.

The aim is not to prescribe a complete self-consistent DFT input workflow. By the end, the reader should be able to identify the continuous Hamiltonian, finite matrix, basis cutoff, grid resolution, and solver error in a plane-wave or grid calculation, and explain why these must remain distinct from pseudopotential error, exchange-correlation functional error, and scientific interpretation.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="前置知识和章节边界" titleEn="Prerequisites and chapter boundary" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

前置知识是定态 Schrödinger 方程、晶格平移、复指数 Fourier 级数和线性代数中的 Hermitian 本征值问题。Chapter 4 已从平移对称性建立 Bloch 定理；本章给出 Fourier 表示下的另一条推导。Chapter 11 讨论赝势的构造和可转移性；本章只使用局域或非局域势作为 Hamiltonian 的给定部分。Chapter 13 才把这些表示嵌入第一性原理自洽循环。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The prerequisites are the stationary Schrödinger equation, lattice translations, complex Fourier series, and Hermitian eigenvalue problems. Chapter 4 derived Bloch's theorem from translation symmetry; this chapter supplies an alternative Fourier-space derivation. Chapter 11 treats pseudopotential construction and transferability; here a local or nonlocal potential is taken as a specified part of the Hamiltonian. Chapter 13 then places these representations inside an ab initio self-consistent cycle.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Unless stated otherwise, derivations use Hartree atomic units, $\hbar=m_e=e=4\pi\epsilon_0=1$, so the free-electron kinetic energy is $|\mathbf k+\mathbf G|^2/2$. The visualizations use dimensionless variables to prevent the teaching curves from being mistaken for results of a particular material.
