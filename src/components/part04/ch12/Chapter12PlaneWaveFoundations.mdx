## 12.1 平面波基中的独立粒子方程 | The Independent-Particle Equation in a Plane-Wave Basis

<BilingualCallout titleZh="来源层级" titleEn="Source layer" kind="source">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">【Martin 原书 | Martin source】印刷页 262–264 给出周期体积中的平面波展开、正交归一和 Hamiltonian 矩阵。下面的离散波矢、矩阵推导、截断与维度检查属于原创补充推导。</div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">【Martin source】Printed pp. 262–264 introduce the plane-wave expansion, orthonormality, and Hamiltonian matrix in a periodic volume. The explicit wave-vector discretization, matrix derivation, cutoff analysis, and dimensional checks below are supplemental derivations.</div>
</BilingualCallout>

<BilingualSection titleZh="连续方程与周期盒" titleEn="The continuous equation and a periodic box">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

在 Hartree 原子单位中，局域有效势下的独立粒子方程写为

$$
\hat H\psi_n(\mathbf r)
=\left[-\frac12\nabla^2+V_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathbf r)\right]\psi_n(\mathbf r)
=\varepsilon_n\psi_n(\mathbf r).
$$

算符 $\hat H$ 作用于连续函数空间。为建立可数基，先在体积 $\Omega=L_1L_2L_3$ 的大盒中施加 Born–von Kármán 周期边界条件：

$$
\psi(\mathbf r+L_i\hat{\mathbf e}_i)=\psi(\mathbf r).
$$

这一步是有限体积正则化。它与晶体原胞周期性有关，但两者的角色不同：大盒决定允许的离散采样点，晶格周期性决定哪些波矢由势相互耦合。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

In Hartree atomic units, an independent-particle equation in a local effective potential is

$$
\hat H\psi_n(\mathbf r)
=\left[-\frac12\nabla^2+V_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathbf r)\right]\psi_n(\mathbf r)
=\varepsilon_n\psi_n(\mathbf r).
$$

The operator $\hat H$ acts in a continuous function space. To construct a countable basis, impose Born–von Kármán periodic boundary conditions in a large box of volume $\Omega=L_1L_2L_3$:

$$
\psi(\mathbf r+L_i\hat{\mathbf e}_i)=\psi(\mathbf r).
$$

This is a finite-volume regularization. It is related to primitive-cell periodicity but serves a different role: the large box sets the allowed sampling points, while the crystal lattice determines which wave vectors are coupled by the potential.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="周期边界如何离散化波矢 | How periodic boundaries quantize wave vectors"
  assumptions={[
    '矩形周期盒，边长为 L_i；',
    '复指数函数在 Ω 上平方可积；',
    '此处只使用大盒周期性，随后再引入晶格周期性。',
  ]}
>

对平面波 $e^{i\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf r}$，第 $i$ 个方向的周期条件给出

$$
e^{iq_i(x_i+L_i)}=e^{iq_ix_i}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
q_iL_i=2\pi n_i,
\qquad n_i\in\mathbb Z.
$$

所以

$$
\mathbf q=2\pi\left(\frac{n_1}{L_1},\frac{n_2}{L_2},\frac{n_3}{L_3}\right).
$$

相邻 $q_i$ 的间隔为 $2\pi/L_i$。归一化基函数定义为

$$
\langle\mathbf r|\mathbf q\rangle=\frac{1}{\sqrt\Omega}e^{i\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf r}.
$$

利用三个方向上的指数积分，

$$
\langle\mathbf q'|\mathbf q\rangle
=\frac1\Omega\int_\Omega d^3r\,e^{i(\mathbf q-\mathbf q')\cdot\mathbf r}
=\delta_{\mathbf q\mathbf q'}.
$$

$\Omega^{-1/2}$ 的量纲为 $L^{-3/2}$，与三维归一化波函数一致。此处出现的是 Kronecker delta；只有在无限体积极限中，离散归一化才转化为 Dirac delta 归一化。

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="Fourier 展开与有限坐标" titleEn="Fourier expansion and finite coordinates">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

完备展开为

$$
\psi_n(\mathbf r)=\frac1{\sqrt\Omega}\sum_{\mathbf q}c_n(\mathbf q)e^{i\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf r},
\qquad
c_n(\mathbf q)=\langle\mathbf q|\psi_n\rangle.
$$

波函数是连续物理对象，$c_n(\mathbf q)$ 是该对象在选定基中的坐标。完整基下二者等价；截断后，波函数被投影到有限子空间。改变 cutoff 会改变有限表示，不会改变原始连续方程。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The complete expansion is

$$
\psi_n(\mathbf r)=\frac1{\sqrt\Omega}\sum_{\mathbf q}c_n(\mathbf q)e^{i\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf r},
\qquad
c_n(\mathbf q)=\langle\mathbf q|\psi_n\rangle.
$$

The wavefunction is the continuous physical object, whereas $c_n(\mathbf q)$ is its coordinate in the selected basis. The descriptions are equivalent in a complete basis. After truncation, the wavefunction is projected into a finite subspace. Changing the cutoff changes the finite representation, not the original continuous equation.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="Hamiltonian 矩阵：动能对角，局域势形成卷积 | Hamiltonian matrix: diagonal kinetic term and potential convolution"
  assumptions={[
    '采用 Hartree 原子单位；',
    'V_eff(r) 具有晶格周期性；',
    'V_eff 为局域乘法算符；非局域势另行处理。',
  ]}
>

将展开代入 $\hat H\psi=\varepsilon\psi$，左乘 $\langle\mathbf q'|$：

$$
\sum_{\mathbf q}\langle\mathbf q'|\hat H|\mathbf q\rangle c_n(\mathbf q)
=\varepsilon_n c_n(\mathbf q').
$$

动能项满足

$$
-\frac12\nabla^2e^{i\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf r}
=\frac{|\mathbf q|^2}{2}e^{i\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf r},
$$

因此

$$
\langle\mathbf q'|\hat T|\mathbf q\rangle
=\frac{|\mathbf q|^2}{2}\delta_{\mathbf q'\mathbf q}.
$$

定义周期势的原胞 Fourier 系数

$$
V(\mathbf G)=\frac1{\Omega_{\mathrm{cell}}}
\int_{\Omega_{\mathrm{cell}}}d^3r\,
V(\mathbf r)e^{-i\mathbf G\cdot\mathbf r}.
$$

局域势矩阵元为

$$
\begin{aligned}
\langle\mathbf q'|V|\mathbf q\rangle
&=\frac1\Omega\int_\Omega d^3r\,
 e^{-i\mathbf q'\cdot\mathbf r}V(\mathbf r)e^{i\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf r}\\
&=V(\mathbf q'-\mathbf q).
\end{aligned}
$$

晶格周期性使该矩阵元只在 $\mathbf q'-\mathbf q$ 为倒格矢时非零。令 $\mathbf q=\mathbf k+\mathbf G$，固定同一个 $\mathbf k$，得到

$$
\sum_{\mathbf G'}H_{\mathbf G\mathbf G'}(\mathbf k)c_{n\mathbf k}(\mathbf G')
=\varepsilon_{n\mathbf k}c_{n\mathbf k}(\mathbf G),
$$

$$
\boxed{
H_{\mathbf G\mathbf G'}(\mathbf k)
=\frac{|\mathbf k+\mathbf G|^2}{2}\delta_{\mathbf G\mathbf G'}
+V(\mathbf G-\mathbf G')
}.
$$

若 $V(\mathbf r)$ 为实函数，则 $V(-\mathbf Q)=V(\mathbf Q)^*$，所以有限矩阵仍为 Hermitian。对非局域赝势，势能项一般依赖两个独立波矢 $V_{\mathrm{NL}}(\mathbf k+\mathbf G,\mathbf k+\mathbf G')$，不能缩写为单个差矢量的 Fourier 系数。

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="表示互补性与 FFT" titleEn="Complementary representations and FFTs" kind="derivation">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

动能在平面波基中对角，局域势在实空间中对角。算法通常把系数 FFT 到实空间，乘以 $V(\mathbf r)$，再 FFT 回倒空间，避免显式保存稠密势矩阵。该数据流改变的是计算成本，不是有限基问题的数学定义。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The kinetic operator is diagonal in the plane-wave basis, whereas a local potential is diagonal in real space. Algorithms normally transform coefficients to real space, multiply by $V(\mathbf r)$, and transform back, avoiding storage of a dense potential matrix. This data flow changes computational cost, not the mathematical definition of the finite-basis problem.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

### 动能截断与变分边界 | Kinetic-energy cutoff and the variational boundary

<BilingualSection titleZh="从无限基到有限基" titleEn="From an infinite basis to a finite basis">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

实际计算保留满足

$$
\frac{|\mathbf k+\mathbf G|^2}{2}\le E_{\mathrm{cut}}
$$

的平面波。近似地，$G_{\max}\simeq\sqrt{2E_{\mathrm{cut}}}$。在三维和较大 cutoff 下，基组数量随倒空间球体积增长：

$$
N_{\mathrm{PW}}\propto\Omega_{\mathrm{cell}}E_{\mathrm{cut}}^{3/2}.
$$

精确整数取决于晶格形状和 $\mathbf k$。同一个 cutoff 对不同原胞尺寸并不产生相同基函数数目。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A calculation retains plane waves satisfying

$$
\frac{|\mathbf k+\mathbf G|^2}{2}\le E_{\mathrm{cut}}.
$$

Approximately, $G_{\max}\simeq\sqrt{2E_{\mathrm{cut}}}$. In three dimensions and at sufficiently large cutoff, the basis count grows with the reciprocal-space sphere volume:

$$
N_{\mathrm{PW}}\propto\Omega_{\mathrm{cell}}E_{\mathrm{cut}}^{3/2}.
$$

The exact integer depends on lattice shape and $\mathbf k$. The same cutoff does not produce the same basis size for cells of different volume.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="固定 Hamiltonian 下的 Rayleigh–Ritz 单调性 | Rayleigh–Ritz monotonicity for a fixed Hamiltonian"
  assumptions={[
    '连续 Hamiltonian、势、边界条件和 k 点保持不变；',
    '有限子空间随 cutoff 嵌套增大；',
    '讨论线性 Hermitian 本征问题，不把重新自洽后的势变化包含在内。',
  ]}
>

有限子空间 $\mathcal V_M$ 中的最低本征值为

$$
\varepsilon_0^{(M)}=
\min_{\psi\in\mathcal V_M,\langle\psi|\psi\rangle=1}
\langle\psi|\hat H|\psi\rangle.
$$

若 $\mathcal V_M\subset\mathcal V_{M+1}$，较大空间包含较小空间的全部试探函数，因此

$$
\varepsilon_0^{(M+1)}\le\varepsilon_0^{(M)}.
$$

按 min–max 原理，固定线性 Hermitian 问题的前若干本征值从上方逼近。该结论不能直接升级为“自洽总能对 cutoff 必然严格单调”：自洽势、占据、FFT 网格、k 点和迭代阈值都可能同时改变。目标 observable 仍需独立收敛序列。

</DerivationBlock>

<PlaneWaveCutoffExplorer />

<BilingualCallout titleZh="程序结束与基组收敛是两个判定" titleEn="Program completion and basis convergence are separate judgments" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

成功对角化只说明给定 cutoff 下的有限问题得到近似解。总能差、带隙、力或密度是否可用，需要保持其他条件不变并增加 cutoff，直到预先声明的目标量在容差内稳定。单个绝对总能和两个结构之间的能量差可能以不同速度收敛。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Successful diagonalization shows only that the finite problem at the chosen cutoff has been approximately solved. Total-energy differences, gaps, forces, or densities require a separate cutoff sequence with other conditions fixed until the declared target stabilizes within its tolerance. An absolute total energy and an energy difference between two structures may converge at different rates.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 12.2 Bloch 定理与电子能带 | Bloch Theorem and Electron Bands

<BilingualSection titleZh="固定 k 的不变子空间" titleEn="Invariant subspaces at fixed k">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

周期势只耦合相差倒格矢的平面波。所有 $\mathbf k+\mathbf G$ 共享同一个约化波矢 $\mathbf k$，Hamiltonian 不把该集合耦合到不同的 $\mathbf k'$ 集合。因此无限矩阵按 $\mathbf k$ 分块，每个块可独立求解。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A periodic potential couples only plane waves whose wave vectors differ by a reciprocal lattice vector. All $\mathbf k+\mathbf G$ vectors share the same reduced wave vector $\mathbf k$, and the Hamiltonian does not couple this set to a distinct $\mathbf k'$ set. The infinite matrix is block diagonal in $\mathbf k$, so each block can be solved independently.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="Fourier 表示中的 Bloch 形式 | Bloch form in the Fourier representation"
  assumptions={[
    '晶体势具有原胞周期性；',
    '固定 k 子空间由所有 k+G 平面波张成；',
    '波函数在 N_cell 个原胞组成的周期体积 Ω 上归一。',
  ]}
>

固定 $\mathbf k$ 的本征态为

$$
\begin{aligned}
\psi_{n\mathbf k}(\mathbf r)
&=\frac1{\sqrt\Omega}\sum_{\mathbf G}c_{n\mathbf k}(\mathbf G)
 e^{i(\mathbf k+\mathbf G)\cdot\mathbf r}\\
&=\frac{e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf r}}{\sqrt{N_{\mathrm{cell}}}}
 u_{n\mathbf k}(\mathbf r),
\end{aligned}
$$

其中

$$
u_{n\mathbf k}(\mathbf r)=
\frac1{\sqrt{\Omega_{\mathrm{cell}}}}
\sum_{\mathbf G}c_{n\mathbf k}(\mathbf G)e^{i\mathbf G\cdot\mathbf r}.
$$

对晶格矢量 $\mathbf R$，$e^{i\mathbf G\cdot\mathbf R}=1$，所以

$$
u_{n\mathbf k}(\mathbf r+\mathbf R)=u_{n\mathbf k}(\mathbf r),
$$

并得到

$$
\boxed{
\psi_{n\mathbf k}(\mathbf r+\mathbf R)
=e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf R}\psi_{n\mathbf k}(\mathbf r)
}.
$$

若 $\psi$ 在总周期体积归一，则可令 $u$ 在一个原胞归一。不同资料也可能把 $u$ 归一到原胞体积；比较矩阵元和密度公式时必须先检查约定。

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="k 与 k+G 的等价" titleEn="Equivalence of k and k+G">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

把 $\mathbf k$ 改为 $\mathbf k+\mathbf G_0$，同时将展开指标重标记为 $\mathbf G-\mathbf G_0$，得到同一组物理波函数和本征值。因此晶体动量只在模倒格矢意义下定义，第一 Brillouin 区可作为不重复代表域。这里的 $\mathbf k$ 是平移算符的量子数，不应在没有额外条件时解释为自由粒子的机械动量。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Replacing $\mathbf k$ by $\mathbf k+\mathbf G_0$ and relabeling the expansion index as $\mathbf G-\mathbf G_0$ leaves the physical wavefunctions and eigenvalues unchanged. Crystal momentum is therefore defined modulo a reciprocal lattice vector, and the first Brillouin zone is a nonredundant representative domain. This $\mathbf k$ is a quantum number of lattice translations; it is not automatically the mechanical momentum of a free particle.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="k 点求和与 Brillouin 区积分 | k-point sums and Brillouin-zone integrals"
  assumptions={[
    'Born–von Kármán 超胞包含 N_cell 个原胞；',
    '允许 k 点均匀离散第一 Brillouin 区；',
    '被积函数在热力学极限下可积。',
  ]}
>

每个允许 $\mathbf k$ 占据倒空间体积 $\Omega_{\mathrm{BZ}}/N_{\mathrm{cell}}$。因此每原胞平均在极限下变为

$$
\frac1{N_{\mathrm{cell}}}\sum_{\mathbf k}F(\mathbf k)
\longrightarrow
\frac1{\Omega_{\mathrm{BZ}}}\int_{\mathrm{BZ}}d^3k\,F(\mathbf k).
$$

实际计算采用有限加权点集：

$$
\frac1{\Omega_{\mathrm{BZ}}}\int_{\mathrm{BZ}}d^3k\,F(\mathbf k)
\approx\sum_{\mathbf k}w_{\mathbf k}F(\mathbf k),
\qquad \sum_{\mathbf k}w_{\mathbf k}=1.
$$

这一步产生 k 点积分误差，与平面波 cutoff 误差独立。增加 cutoff 不能补偿过稀 k 网格，增加 k 点也不能补偿缺少高 $|\mathbf G|$ 分量。

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="高对称线能带的证据上限" titleEn="Evidence ceiling of a symmetry-line band plot" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

沿高对称线绘制 $\varepsilon_{n\mathbf k}$ 只采样 Brillouin 区中的一维路径。它能显示路径上的简并、交叉和能隙，却不能排除路径外的费米口袋或更小间接隙。金属性、完整费米面和全局带隙需要覆盖完整 Brillouin 区的采样或具有证明力的搜索。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Plotting $\varepsilon_{n\mathbf k}$ along symmetry lines samples only a one-dimensional path through the Brillouin zone. It can reveal degeneracies, crossings, and gaps on that path, but cannot exclude off-path Fermi pockets or a smaller indirect gap. Metallic character, a complete Fermi surface, and the global gap require full-zone sampling or an adequately justified search.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>
