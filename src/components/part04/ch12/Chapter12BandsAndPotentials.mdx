## 12.3 近自由电子近似 | Nearly-Free-Electron Approximation

<BilingualCallout titleZh="来源层级" titleEn="Source layer" kind="source">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">【Martin 原书 | Martin source】印刷页 265–267 从自由电子折叠能带进入弱周期势，并以 Bragg 条件附近的简并耦合解释能隙。下述二态矩阵、相位处理和证据边界为原创补充。</div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">【Martin source】Printed pp. 265–267 move from folded free-electron bands to a weak periodic potential and explain gap formation through degenerate coupling near a Bragg condition. The explicit two-state matrix, phase treatment, and evidence boundary below are supplemental.</div>
</BilingualCallout>

<BilingualSection titleZh="折叠并不等于开隙" titleEn="Folding alone does not open a gap">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

自由电子色散 $\varepsilon^0(\mathbf q)=|\mathbf q|^2/2$ 可按 $\mathbf q=\mathbf k+\mathbf G$ 折叠到第一 Brillouin 区。折叠会让不同 $\mathbf G$ 标记的抛物线在区边界相交，但在 $V(\mathbf r)=0$ 时这些交点仍是真简并。能隙来自周期势 Fourier 分量对简并态的非零矩阵元，不是绘图时把抛物线放入约化区本身。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The free-electron dispersion $\varepsilon^0(\mathbf q)=|\mathbf q|^2/2$ can be folded into the first Brillouin zone by writing $\mathbf q=\mathbf k+\mathbf G$. Folding makes parabolas carrying different $\mathbf G$ labels intersect at zone boundaries, but when $V(\mathbf r)=0$ those intersections remain exact degeneracies. A gap comes from a nonzero matrix element of a periodic-potential Fourier component between degenerate states, not from the act of plotting the parabolas in the reduced zone.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="Bragg 面附近的二态 Hamiltonian | Two-state Hamiltonian near a Bragg plane"
  assumptions={[
    '只保留两个近简并平面波 |k⟩ 与 |k−G⟩；',
    '其他远离简并的态只产生较高阶修正；',
    '周期势较弱，且 V_G 在局部 k 区间内视为常数。',
  ]}
>

Bragg 条件为

$$
|\mathbf k|^2=|\mathbf k-\mathbf G|^2
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
2\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf G=|\mathbf G|^2.
$$

在基 $\{|\mathbf k\rangle,|\mathbf k-\mathbf G\rangle\}$ 中，去掉共同平均能量后，二态矩阵可写为

$$
H_{2\times2}=
\begin{pmatrix}
\Delta/2 & V_{\mathbf G}\\
V_{\mathbf G}^* & -\Delta/2
\end{pmatrix},
$$

其中 $\Delta=\varepsilon^0_{\mathbf k}-\varepsilon^0_{\mathbf k-\mathbf G}$。特征方程为

$$
E^2-\left(\frac{\Delta^2}{4}+|V_{\mathbf G}|^2\right)=0,
$$

所以

$$
E_\pm=\pm\sqrt{\frac{\Delta^2}{4}+|V_{\mathbf G}|^2}.
$$

在精确 Bragg 面上 $\Delta=0$，两支能量间距为

$$
\boxed{E_+-E_-=2|V_{\mathbf G}|}.
$$

$V_{\mathbf G}$ 的复相位改变本征矢中两平面波的相对相位，但二态能隙只依赖其模。若对称性迫使该矩阵元为零，则该简并不会被这一 Fourier 分量打开。

</DerivationBlock>

<NearlyFreeElectronExplorer />

<BilingualCallout titleZh="二态模型的证据上限" titleEn="Evidence ceiling of the two-state model" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

该推导证明的是给定二态 Hamiltonian 的 avoided crossing。它不能单独给出真实材料的带隙，因为真实 Hamiltonian 还包含多组 $\mathbf G$、非局域赝势、多个原子、占据、自洽屏蔽和可能的自旋轨道耦合。数值能带中的 gap 仍需完整 Hamiltonian、完整 Brillouin 区和参数收敛支持。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The derivation establishes an avoided crossing for the stated two-state Hamiltonian. It does not determine the band gap of a real material, whose Hamiltonian includes many $\mathbf G$ vectors, nonlocal pseudopotentials, multiple atoms, occupations, self-consistent screening, and possibly spin–orbit coupling. A numerical gap still requires the full Hamiltonian, full-zone sampling, and parameter convergence.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 12.4 形状因子与结构因子 | Form Factors and Structure Factors

<BilingualSection titleZh="把原子势与几何排列分开" titleEn="Separating the atomic potential from geometry">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

若晶体局域势近似为原胞内各原子型势的平移和，

$$
V(\mathbf r)=\sum_{\mathbf R}\sum_\kappa
v_\kappa(\mathbf r-\mathbf R-\boldsymbol\tau_\kappa),
$$

则 Fourier 变换把“单个物种的空间形状”和“原胞内位置相位”分离。前者是 atomic form factor，后者是 structure factor。该分解说明同一原子势在不同基元几何中可产生不同的允许耦合与消光。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

If the local crystal potential is approximated as a sum of translated atomic-like potentials,

$$
V(\mathbf r)=\sum_{\mathbf R}\sum_\kappa
v_\kappa(\mathbf r-\mathbf R-\boldsymbol\tau_\kappa),
$$

then a Fourier transform separates the spatial form of an individual species from the phase factors associated with positions inside the cell. The first object is an atomic form factor and the second a structure factor. The decomposition explains why the same atomic potential can produce different allowed couplings and extinctions in different basis geometries.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="V(G) 的物种 form factor 与结构相位 | Species form factors and structural phases in V(G)"
  assumptions={[
    '势可写为局域原子型势的线性叠加；',
    '原胞包含位置 τ_κ 和物种依赖势 v_κ；',
    'Fourier 系数按原胞体积归一。',
  ]}
>

定义单原子势的 Fourier 分量

$$
v_\kappa(\mathbf G)=\int d^3r\,
v_\kappa(\mathbf r)e^{-i\mathbf G\cdot\mathbf r}.
$$

对平移后的函数令 $\mathbf u=\mathbf r-\boldsymbol\tau_\kappa$，得到

$$
\int d^3r\,v_\kappa(\mathbf r-\boldsymbol\tau_\kappa)e^{-i\mathbf G\cdot\mathbf r}
=e^{-i\mathbf G\cdot\boldsymbol\tau_\kappa}v_\kappa(\mathbf G).
$$

因此原胞 Fourier 系数可写成

$$
\boxed{
V(\mathbf G)=\frac1{\Omega_{\mathrm{cell}}}
\sum_\kappa v_\kappa(\mathbf G)
 e^{-i\mathbf G\cdot\boldsymbol\tau_\kappa}
}.
$$

若所有原子具有相同 form factor $v(\mathbf G)$，可进一步写为

$$
V(\mathbf G)=\frac{v(\mathbf G)}{\Omega_{\mathrm{cell}}}S(\mathbf G),
\qquad
S(\mathbf G)=\sum_\kappa e^{-i\mathbf G\cdot\boldsymbol\tau_\kappa}.
$$

$S(\mathbf G)=0$ 表示几何相消。它消除的是相应 Fourier 分量或散射通道，不能不加限定地解释为“该晶体没有势”或“所有能隙均关闭”。

</DerivationBlock>

<StructureFactorExplorer />

<BilingualCallout titleZh="非局域势的边界" titleEn="Boundary for nonlocal potentials" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

局域势的矩阵元只依赖 $\mathbf G-\mathbf G'$，因此可用单变量 form factor 表示。非局域势的矩阵元依赖入射与出射波矢两个变量，通常还带角动量和 projector 指标。把非局域赝势强行压缩为一个 $V(\mathbf G)$ 会丢失其定义信息。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A local-potential matrix element depends only on $\mathbf G-\mathbf G'$ and can therefore be represented by a single-variable form factor. A nonlocal-potential matrix element depends on both incoming and outgoing wave vectors and normally carries angular-momentum and projector labels. Compressing a nonlocal pseudopotential into one $V(\mathbf G)$ discards defining information.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 12.5 近似原子型势 | Approximate Atomic-Like Potentials

<BilingualSection titleZh="从裸核势到有效弱势" titleEn="From a bare nuclear potential to an effective weak potential">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

近自由电子图像要求价电子看到的有效周期势相对平滑且较弱。裸 Coulomb 核势在核附近强烈奇异，也没有包含核心电子的屏蔽与正交排斥。Martin 在本节讨论几类把这些效应压缩为原子型有效势的思路：屏蔽 Coulomb 形式、空芯或排斥核心模型，以及由散射性质构造的势。

这些近似势的共同目标是重现指定能量范围内的价电子散射，而不是逐点复制全电子势。势函数在实空间看起来相似，不保证相移、能级和晶体能带相同；反之，两个形状不同的有效势也可能在受限能量窗口内给出相近散射。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The nearly-free-electron picture requires a relatively smooth and weak effective periodic potential for valence electrons. A bare Coulomb nuclear potential is strongly singular near the nucleus and does not include core-electron screening or orthogonality repulsion. This section discusses ways of compressing those effects into atomic-like effective potentials, including screened Coulomb forms, empty-core or repulsive-core models, and potentials constructed from scattering properties.

The shared objective is to reproduce valence-electron scattering over a specified energy range, not to copy the all-electron potential point by point. Similar-looking real-space potentials need not produce the same phase shifts, levels, or crystal bands. Conversely, differently shaped effective potentials may yield similar scattering within a limited energy window.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="与 Chapter 11 的边界" titleEn="Boundary with Chapter 11" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

本章把原子型势视为平面波 Hamiltonian 的输入，关注 Fourier 分量如何形成能带。赝势生成、范数守恒、可转移性、ghost states 和相对论处理属于 Chapter 11。采用更平滑的势通常降低所需 cutoff，但“更软”不等于在所有化学环境中更准确。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Here an atomic-like potential is treated as input to the plane-wave Hamiltonian, with attention to how its Fourier components form bands. Pseudopotential generation, norm conservation, transferability, ghost states, and relativistic treatment belong to Chapter 11. A smoother potential usually reduces the required cutoff, but softness does not imply greater accuracy in every chemical environment.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 12.6 经验赝势方法 | Empirical Pseudopotential Method

<BilingualSection titleZh="把 Fourier 分量拟合到能带信息" titleEn="Fitting Fourier components to band information">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

经验赝势方法以少量对称允许的 form factors 参数化晶体势，并用实验能带、光学临界点或参考计算约束参数。对给定晶格和基元，structure factor 固定几何相位；可调部分主要是不同 $|\mathbf G|$ 壳层上的 atomic form factors。随后对每个 $\mathbf k$ 对角化平面波 Hamiltonian，得到参数化能带。

该方法把“数据拟合”和“本征问题求解”分开。对角化可以非常精确，但参数化势的可转移性仍由拟合窗口、训练材料、对称性和观测量决定。拟合能带成功不能自动证明电荷密度、总能、力或新结构预测可靠。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The empirical pseudopotential method parameterizes the crystal potential by a small number of symmetry-allowed form factors and constrains them using experimental bands, optical critical points, or reference calculations. For a fixed lattice and basis, the structure factor determines the geometric phases, while the adjustable quantities are mainly atomic form factors on different $|\mathbf G|$ shells. The plane-wave Hamiltonian is then diagonalized at each $\mathbf k$ to obtain parameterized bands.

The method separates data fitting from solution of the eigenvalue problem. Diagonalization may be numerically precise while transferability of the parameterized potential remains controlled by the fitting window, training materials, symmetry, and target observables. Successful band fitting does not automatically validate densities, total energies, forces, or predictions for new structures.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="对称等价 G 壳层如何减少参数数目 | How symmetry-equivalent G shells reduce parameter count"
  assumptions={[
    '局域中心势的 form factor 只依赖 |G|；',
    '晶体点群把若干倒格矢映射到同一星；',
    '参数化不包含显式非局域 projector。',
  ]}
>

对中心势，$v(\mathbf G)=v(|\mathbf G|)$。若点群操作 $R$ 保持晶格不变，则 $|R\mathbf G|=|\mathbf G|$，同一 star 中的倒格矢共享一个参数。Hamiltonian 仍含所有被截断的 $\mathbf G$，但独立拟合参数数目可远小于矩阵维数。

这种降维来自对称性和模型假设，不是数据自动证明的性质。若材料含多物种、非中心项、自旋轨道耦合或非局域性，参数集合必须相应扩展。

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="拟合精度、数值精度与物理适用性" titleEn="Fit accuracy, numerical accuracy, and physical applicability" kind="practice">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

EPM 计算至少有三类独立检查：矩阵对角化残差是否足够小；平面波 cutoff 和 k 采样是否使拟合模型的输出收敛；参数化势是否覆盖当前材料、结构、能量区间和目标 observable。前两项通过，仍不能补足第三项的证据。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

An EPM calculation has at least three independent checks: whether eigensolver residuals are sufficiently small; whether plane-wave cutoff and k sampling converge the output of the fitted model; and whether the parameterized potential covers the present material, structure, energy window, and target observable. Passing the first two does not supply evidence for the third.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>
