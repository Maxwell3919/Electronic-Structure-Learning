## 方法比较与实际计算连接 | Method Comparison and Connection to Computation

<BilingualSection titleZh="本章方法的固定比较维度" titleEn="Fixed comparison dimensions for the methods in this chapter">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

| 方法 | 表示与边界 | 矩阵结构 | 系统控制参数 | 主要误差 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 平面波 | 周期体积中的全局 Fourier 基 | 正交基；动能对角；局域势借助 FFT 应用 | $E_{\mathrm{cut}}$、FFT 网格、k 点 | 基组截断、aliasing、k 积分、周期镜像 |
| 均匀有限差分 | 网格点值与指定边界条件 | 局部稀疏 stencil | spacing $h$、stencil 阶数、盒长 | 截断误差、数值色散、边界误差 |
| 有限元 / DG | 单元局部多项式与弱形式 | 稀疏块矩阵；DG 含界面通量 | 单元尺寸、阶数、penalty/flux | quadrature、界面、网格质量、局部加密误差 |
| Wavelet / 多分辨率 | 按尺度与位置组织的局域函数 | 层级稀疏或压缩结构 | 尺度层级、阈值、细化准则 | 截断、层级传递、长程项与全局量误差 |

“系统可收敛”必须指明控制序列和范数。平面波通过增加 cutoff 建立嵌套空间；有限差分通过 $h\to0$ 或提高阶数；有限元通过 $h/p$ refinement；wavelet 通过增加尺度和降低阈值。不同路线在严格极限中可逼近同一连续问题，但有限成本下的误差分布不同。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

| Method | Representation and boundary | Matrix structure | Systematic control | Main errors |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Plane waves | Global Fourier basis in a periodic volume | Orthogonal; diagonal kinetic term; local potential applied by FFT | $E_{\mathrm{cut}}$, FFT grid, k points | Basis truncation, aliasing, k integration, periodic images |
| Uniform finite difference | Grid values with stated boundary conditions | Local sparse stencil | Spacing $h$, stencil order, box size | Truncation, numerical dispersion, boundary error |
| Finite element / DG | Element-local polynomials and weak form | Sparse block matrix; DG includes interface fluxes | Element size, order, penalty or flux | Quadrature, interface, mesh quality, refinement error |
| Wavelet / multiresolution | Localized functions organized by scale and position | Hierarchical sparse or compressed structure | Scale depth, threshold, refinement criterion | Truncation, interlevel transfer, long-range and global-observable error |

“Systematically convergent” requires a declared control sequence and norm. Plane waves enlarge an embedded space by raising the cutoff; finite differences take $h\to0$ or raise order; finite elements use $h/p$ refinement; wavelets add scales and lower thresholds. The routes can approach the same continuous problem in their strict limits, while distributing finite-cost error differently.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="理论方法到科学判断的证据链" titleEn="Evidence chain from method to scientific judgment" kind="practice">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

```text
连续 Hamiltonian 与边界条件
→ 有限表示：Ecut / h / element order / wavelet threshold
→ 有限矩阵或算子应用
→ 本征残差与数值停止条件
→ 基组、网格、k 点、盒长的独立收敛
→ 总能、力、带隙、密度等目标 observable
→ 方法适用性和物理解释
```

任一箭头都需要输入和判定标准。程序正常退出只覆盖执行状态；小本征残差只覆盖有限问题；SCF 收敛属于 Chapter 13 的另一层；这些条件都不能单独证明目标 observable 或论文主张。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

```text
continuous Hamiltonian and boundary conditions
→ finite representation: Ecut / h / element order / wavelet threshold
→ finite matrix or operator application
→ eigen-residual and numerical stopping criteria
→ independent convergence in basis, grid, k points, and box size
→ target observable: energy, force, gap, density, ...
→ method applicability and physical interpretation
```

Every arrow needs inputs and an acceptance criterion. Normal program termination covers execution state only; a small eigen-residual covers the finite problem; SCF convergence is another layer introduced in Chapter 13. None of these conditions alone validates the target observable or a scientific claim.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

### 实践连接 | Practical connection

<BilingualSection titleZh="如何设计最低充分收敛检查" titleEn="Designing a minimally sufficient convergence check">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Sholl–Steckel 对简单固体和表面计算的实践讨论可转化为四个独立序列：

1. 固定几何、k 网格和其他设置，增加平面波 cutoff 或减小实空间 spacing；
2. 固定表示精度，增加 k 点密度；
3. 对分子、表面或缺陷，增加 supercell 尺寸或真空厚度；
4. 对每条序列记录目标量，而不只记录总能或程序 residual。

判定标准应在运行前给出，例如相对能量差、最大力、带隙或密度积分在相邻两个更严格设置间变化小于给定容差。一个参数的稳定不能替代另一个参数的检查；若序列间强耦合，应采用二维或分阶段验证并说明固定条件。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The practical discussions of simple solids and surfaces in Sholl–Steckel can be converted into four independent sequences:

1. hold geometry, k mesh, and other settings fixed while raising the plane-wave cutoff or reducing the real-space spacing;
2. hold representation accuracy fixed while increasing k-point density;
3. for molecules, surfaces, or defects, enlarge the supercell or vacuum region;
4. record the target observable in every sequence rather than only the total energy or program residual.

The acceptance criterion should be declared before the runs, for example a change below a specified tolerance in a relative energy, maximum force, band gap, or density integral between the two strictest settings. Stability in one parameter does not replace checks in another. Strongly coupled sequences require a two-dimensional or staged validation with fixed conditions stated explicitly.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="为什么不能给出通用 cutoff" titleEn="Why no universal cutoff can be prescribed" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

所需 cutoff 或 spacing 取决于赝势/全电子处理、元素、局域化程度、目标 observable、力或应力精度以及程序对参数的定义。固定数值只能在给定输入族和验收标准下成立。本章因此提供收敛逻辑，不给所有材料通用的参数。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The required cutoff or spacing depends on pseudopotential or all-electron treatment, chemical elements, localization, target observable, force or stress accuracy, and the program's parameter definition. A fixed value is valid only for a stated input family and acceptance criterion. This chapter therefore supplies convergence logic rather than a universal parameter for all materials.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

### 常见误判 | Common misjudgments

<BilingualSection titleZh="需要被排除的推断跳跃" titleEn="Inference jumps that must be rejected">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

- **“平面波基是完备的，所以有限 cutoff 已经准确。”** 完备性属于无限极限；有限计算仍有截断误差。
- **“cutoff 增加后总能下降，因此所有性质都收敛。”** Rayleigh–Ritz 单调性只在固定线性问题和嵌套子空间中成立；不同 observable 需要分别检查。
- **“FFT 网格很细，所以 k 点也足够。”** 实空间分辨率与 Brillouin 区积分是独立离散。
- **“高对称线没有穿越费米能，所以体系绝缘。”** 路径外状态仍可能形成口袋。
- **“实空间矩阵稀疏，所以算法一定更快。”** 自由度数、谱条件、预条件、通信和精度目标共同决定成本。
- **“两个程序结果接近，所以结果正确。”** 一致性只有在 Hamiltonian、边界、核处理、占据和收敛标准对齐后才有明确含义；共同偏差仍可能存在。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

- **“The plane-wave basis is complete, so a finite cutoff is already accurate.”** Completeness belongs to the infinite limit; a finite calculation retains truncation error.
- **“The total energy decreases with cutoff, so every property is converged.”** Rayleigh–Ritz monotonicity applies to a fixed linear problem in nested subspaces; different observables require separate checks.
- **“The FFT grid is fine, so the k mesh is also sufficient.”** Real-space resolution and Brillouin-zone integration are independent discretizations.
- **“No symmetry-line band crosses the Fermi level, so the system is insulating.”** Off-path states can still form pockets.
- **“A real-space matrix is sparse, so the algorithm must be faster.”** Degrees of freedom, spectral conditioning, preconditioning, communication, and accuracy jointly set cost.
- **“Two codes agree, so the result is correct.”** Agreement has a clear meaning only after aligning Hamiltonian, boundaries, core treatment, occupations, and convergence criteria; a shared bias may remain.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## 原创练习 | Original Exercises

<BilingualSection titleZh="推导与证据审查" titleEn="Derivation and evidence audit">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

1. **周期归一化。** 从一维积分开始，推导三维平面波的 Kronecker 正交关系，并说明无限体积极限中求和测度如何变成积分测度。
2. **势矩阵。** 对 $V(x)=2V_1\cos(G_0x)$，列出非零 $\langle k+G|V|k+G'\rangle$，画出矩阵带状结构，并说明为何只耦合相差 $\pm G_0$ 的基函数。
3. **Bragg 能隙。** 从一般复数 $V_G=|V_G|e^{i\phi}$ 的二态矩阵求本征值和归一化本征矢，判断 $\phi$ 影响哪些量。
4. **结构因子。** 对一维三点基元 $0,a/3,2a/3$ 计算 $S_m$，找出消光级次，并区分振幅消光和强度消光。
5. **密度带宽。** 设轨道仅含 $G=0,\pm G_0$，显式展开 $|\psi|^2$，列出密度中出现的所有 Fourier 分量。
6. **有限差分误差。** 用 Taylor 展开推导五点四阶二阶导数 stencil，并对 $e^{ikx}$ 求数值色散至 $O((kh)^4)$。
7. **收敛设计。** 为“两个晶体结构的相对能量达到 1 meV/atom 精度”设计 cutoff、k 点和体积三类检查，列出固定输入、输出和接受标准。
8. **证据上限。** 给定一次成功运行、一个小 SCF residual、一张高对称线能带和两个程序接近的总能，逐项写出它们能支持和不能支持的结论。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

1. **Periodic normalization.** Begin from a one-dimensional integral, derive the three-dimensional Kronecker orthogonality of plane waves, and explain how the sum measure becomes an integral measure in the infinite-volume limit.
2. **Potential matrix.** For $V(x)=2V_1\cos(G_0x)$, list the nonzero $\langle k+G|V|k+G'\rangle$ elements, sketch the matrix bandwidth, and explain why only basis functions separated by $\pm G_0$ are coupled.
3. **Bragg gap.** Starting from a general complex $V_G=|V_G|e^{i\phi}$, obtain eigenvalues and normalized eigenvectors of the two-state matrix and identify which quantities depend on $\phi$.
4. **Structure factor.** For a one-dimensional three-site basis at $0,a/3,2a/3$, calculate $S_m$, find extinct orders, and distinguish amplitude extinction from intensity extinction.
5. **Density bandwidth.** Let an orbital contain only $G=0,\pm G_0$. Expand $|\psi|^2$ explicitly and list all Fourier components appearing in the density.
6. **Finite-difference error.** Derive a five-point fourth-order stencil for the second derivative by Taylor expansion and obtain its numerical dispersion for $e^{ikx}$ through $O((kh)^4)$.
7. **Convergence design.** Design cutoff, k-point, and volume checks for a relative energy target of 1 meV/atom between two crystal structures, specifying fixed inputs, outputs, and acceptance criteria.
8. **Evidence ceiling.** Given a successful run, a small SCF residual, one symmetry-line band plot, and similar total energies from two codes, state separately what each observation does and does not support.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>
