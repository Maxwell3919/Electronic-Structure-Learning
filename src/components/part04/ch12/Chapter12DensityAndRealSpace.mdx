## 12.7 电子密度的计算：网格的引入 | Calculating Electron Density: Introducing Grids

<BilingualCallout titleZh="来源层级" titleEn="Source layer" kind="source">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">【Martin 原书 | Martin source】印刷页 272–274 讨论由平面波轨道构造密度、FFT、密度 Fourier 分量和网格分辨率。Appendix M 提供 FFT 与迭代求解的数值背景。下述 support、Nyquist 和 aliasing 推导为原创补充。</div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">【Martin source】Printed pp. 272–274 discuss construction of density from plane-wave orbitals, FFTs, density Fourier components, and grid resolution. Appendix M supplies numerical background on FFTs and iterative solution. The support, Nyquist, and aliasing derivations below are supplemental.</div>
</BilingualCallout>

<BilingualSection titleZh="轨道表示与密度表示的带宽不同" titleEn="Orbitals and densities have different bandwidths">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

给定占据 $f_{n\mathbf k}$，电子密度为

$$
n(\mathbf r)=\sum_{n\mathbf k}w_{\mathbf k}f_{n\mathbf k}
|\psi_{n\mathbf k}(\mathbf r)|^2.
$$

平面波系数天然存于倒空间，而 $|\psi|^2$ 和局域势乘法在实空间最直接。计算流程通常是：把每个轨道 FFT 到均匀网格，构造密度；由密度求 Hartree/XC 势；将局域势乘到轨道；再变换回倒空间。网格是表示的一部分，并非只用于绘图。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For occupations $f_{n\mathbf k}$, the electron density is

$$
n(\mathbf r)=\sum_{n\mathbf k}w_{\mathbf k}f_{n\mathbf k}
|\psi_{n\mathbf k}(\mathbf r)|^2.
$$

Plane-wave coefficients are naturally stored in reciprocal space, while $|\psi|^2$ and multiplication by a local potential are most direct in real space. A typical data path transforms each orbital to a uniform grid, constructs the density, obtains Hartree and exchange-correlation potentials from that density, applies the local potential, and transforms back. The grid is part of the representation, not merely a plotting device.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="波函数 cutoff 为什么不等于密度 cutoff | Why the wavefunction cutoff is not the density cutoff"
  assumptions={[
    '轨道只含 |k+G|≤Gmax 的平面波；',
    '密度由同一轨道的乘积构造；',
    '先忽略数值舍入和非线性滤波。',
  ]}
>

写出一个 Bloch 轨道

$$
\psi_{n\mathbf k}(\mathbf r)=\frac1{\sqrt\Omega}
\sum_{\mathbf G}c_{n\mathbf k}(\mathbf G)
 e^{i(\mathbf k+\mathbf G)\cdot\mathbf r}.
$$

其模平方为

$$
|\psi_{n\mathbf k}(\mathbf r)|^2
=\frac1\Omega\sum_{\mathbf G\mathbf G'}
 c_{n\mathbf k}(\mathbf G)c^*_{n\mathbf k}(\mathbf G')
 e^{i(\mathbf G-\mathbf G')\cdot\mathbf r}.
$$

若两个系数均满足 $|\mathbf k+\mathbf G|,|\mathbf k+\mathbf G'|\le G_{\max}$，差矢量的最大模可达约 $2G_{\max}$。因此密度可含有比单个轨道更高的 Fourier 分量。以动能标度表示，若轨道 cutoff 为 $E_{\mathrm{cut}}=G_{\max}^2/2$，容纳全部密度乘积所需的理想 Fourier 能量上界可达到约 $4E_{\mathrm{cut}}$。

该几何上界不等于所有程序都必须采用固定的 4 倍参数：赝势形式、augmentation charge、滤波、dual-grid 策略和实现定义可能不同。输入参数必须按程序官方定义解释，并通过目标 observable 收敛测试验收。

</DerivationBlock>

<DerivationBlock
  title="均匀网格的 Nyquist 条件与 aliasing | Nyquist condition and aliasing on a uniform grid"
  assumptions={[
    '一维均匀网格 x_j=jh；',
    '周期长度 L=Nh；',
    '采样函数含 Fourier 模式 exp(ikx)。',
  ]}
>

网格点上，两个波矢 $k$ 与 $k+2\pi m/h$ 给出相同样本：

$$
e^{i(k+2\pi m/h)jh}=e^{ikjh}e^{i2\pi mj}=e^{ikjh}.
$$

因此独立可表示区间可选为

$$
-\frac\pi h\le k<\frac\pi h,
$$

Nyquist 波矢为 $k_{\mathrm N}=\pi/h$。若真实函数含 $|k|>k_{\mathrm N}$ 的分量，这些分量会折叠到低频，产生 aliasing。减小 $h$ 扩大可表示带宽；增加盒长而保持 $h$ 不变只使波矢采样更密，并不提高最高可表示频率。

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="FFT 网格与物理边界条件" titleEn="FFT grids and physical boundary conditions" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

离散 Fourier 变换天然对应周期网格。对表面、分子和缺陷，使用 FFT 并没有取消周期性，而是把对象放入周期重复的 supercell。真空厚度、带电镜像、偶极耦合和有限缺陷浓度属于边界误差，不能由更细网格自动消除；Chapter 13 将系统处理这些问题。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A discrete Fourier transform naturally represents a periodic grid. For surfaces, molecules, and defects, use of an FFT does not remove periodicity; it places the object in a periodically repeated supercell. Vacuum thickness, charged-image interactions, dipole coupling, and finite defect concentration are boundary errors that a finer grid cannot automatically remove. Chapter 13 treats these issues systematically.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 12.8 实空间方法 I：有限差分与不连续 Galerkin | Real-Space Methods I: Finite Difference and Discontinuous Galerkin

<BilingualSection titleZh="在网格上直接表示微分算符" titleEn="Representing differential operators directly on a grid">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

实空间方法直接存储 $\psi(\mathbf r_i)$ 或局部单元系数。局域势成为对角或块对角对象，动能则由邻点 stencil、有限元弱形式或单元边界通量表示。矩阵可具有局域稀疏结构，但精度仍由网格间距、阶数、单元形状和边界条件控制。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Real-space methods store $\psi(\mathbf r_i)$ or coefficients on local elements directly. A local potential becomes diagonal or block diagonal, while the kinetic operator is represented by neighboring-point stencils, a finite-element weak form, or fluxes across element boundaries. The matrix can be local and sparse, but accuracy remains controlled by grid spacing, polynomial order, element geometry, and boundary conditions.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="二阶中心差分及其截断误差 | Second-order central difference and its truncation error"
  assumptions={[
    '函数在 x 附近至少四次可微；',
    '均匀网格间距为 h；',
    '内部点使用对称三点 stencil。',
  ]}
>

Taylor 展开为

$$
\psi(x\pm h)=\psi(x)\pm h\psi'(x)+\frac{h^2}{2}\psi''(x)
\pm\frac{h^3}{6}\psi'''(x)+\frac{h^4}{24}\psi^{(4)}(x)+O(h^5).
$$

相加并消去奇数阶导数：

$$
\psi(x+h)-2\psi(x)+\psi(x-h)
=h^2\psi''(x)+\frac{h^4}{12}\psi^{(4)}(x)+O(h^6).
$$

所以

$$
\boxed{
\psi''(x)=\frac{\psi(x+h)-2\psi(x)+\psi(x-h)}{h^2}
-\frac{h^2}{12}\psi^{(4)}(x)+O(h^4)
}.
$$

三点公式对二阶导数具有 $O(h^2)$ 截断误差。缩小 $h$ 一般降低局部离散误差，但增加未知量、内存和通信，并可能改变迭代矩阵的条件数。高阶 stencil 以更多邻点换取更高阶精度；在非光滑势、边界或自适应网格附近，形式阶数不自动等于全局收敛阶数。

</DerivationBlock>

<DerivationBlock
  title="网格平面波的离散动能色散 | Discrete kinetic dispersion for a grid plane wave"
  assumptions={[
    '周期均匀一维网格；',
    '使用三点中心差分；',
    '试探向量 ψ_j=exp(ikjh)。',
  ]}
>

把 $\psi_j=e^{ikjh}$ 代入负二阶差分算符：

$$
-\frac{\psi_{j+1}-2\psi_j+\psi_{j-1}}{h^2}
=\frac{2-2\cos(kh)}{h^2}\psi_j.
$$

连续算符 $-d^2/dx^2$ 的本征值为 $k^2$。二者比值为

$$
R(kh)=\frac{2[1-\cos(kh)]}{(kh)^2}.
$$

利用 $\cos x=1-x^2/2+x^4/24+\cdots$，得到

$$
R(kh)=1-\frac{(kh)^2}{12}+O((kh)^4).
$$

长波极限中离散色散趋于连续结果；接近 Nyquist 边缘时误差显著。网格能够表示某个波矢，不等于该波矢的动能已经被准确表示。

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="有限元、Mehrstellen 与不连续 Galerkin" titleEn="Finite elements, Mehrstellen, and discontinuous Galerkin">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

有限差分直接近似强形式导数。Mehrstellen 方法联合离散 Hamiltonian 和 overlap-like averaging operator，以紧凑 stencil 改善谱误差。有限元从弱形式出发，把空间划分为单元并在单元内使用低阶或高阶多项式；基函数通常局域，因而矩阵稀疏。连续 Galerkin 在单元界面要求函数连续，不连续 Galerkin 允许单元内展开相互独立，并通过数值通量或 penalty 项耦合界面。

DG 的局部性适合非均匀分区和并行，但结果依赖单元大小、局部阶数、界面通量及 penalty 稳定性。矩阵稀疏不自动表示总成本低：每单元自由度、谱宽、预条件器、通信和所需精度共同决定 wall time。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Finite differences approximate derivatives in strong form. Mehrstellen schemes combine a discrete Hamiltonian with an overlap-like averaging operator to improve spectral error using compact stencils. Finite elements begin from a weak form, partition space into elements, and use low- or high-order polynomials within each element; their local support normally gives sparse matrices. Continuous Galerkin enforces continuity across interfaces, whereas discontinuous Galerkin permits independent element expansions and couples interfaces through numerical fluxes or penalty terms.

DG locality is useful for nonuniform partitions and parallelism, but results depend on element size, local order, interface fluxes, and penalty stability. Sparse matrices do not automatically imply low total cost: degrees of freedom per element, spectral width, preconditioning, communication, and target accuracy jointly determine wall time.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="边界条件必须写入离散算子" titleEn="Boundary conditions must be part of the discrete operator" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

周期、Dirichlet、Neumann 和开放边界近似会产生不同的矩阵行与谱。只比较内部 stencil 而忽略边界实现，不能证明两个离散程序求解同一个问题。对 Coulomb 长程项，波函数局部离散和 Poisson 边界还可能分别采用不同策略。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Periodic, Dirichlet, Neumann, and approximate open boundaries produce different matrix rows and spectra. Comparing interior stencils while ignoring boundary implementation does not establish that two discretizations solve the same problem. For the long-range Coulomb term, local wavefunction discretization and Poisson boundary treatment may also use distinct strategies.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 12.9 实空间方法 II：多分辨率方法 | Real-Space Methods II: Multiresolution Methods

<BilingualSection titleZh="把分辨率放到需要的位置" titleEn="Placing resolution where it is needed">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

均匀平面波或均匀网格在全空间使用同一最高分辨率。电子结构函数的局部复杂度并不均匀：核附近、键区和缺陷区可能变化快速，真空或缓慢变化区域只需较粗表示。多重网格、有限元局部加密、wavelet 和自适应坐标都试图把自由度集中到误差较大的区域。

多分辨率的优势来自更合适的自由度分布，而不是放弃收敛。局部细化必须配合误差估计、层级间传递、边界匹配和全局 observable 验收。若细化准则只观察局部残差，仍可能漏掉长程 Coulomb、全局能量差或应力误差。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Uniform plane waves and uniform grids use the same maximum resolution everywhere. Electronic-structure functions do not have uniform local complexity: rapid variation may occur near nuclei, bonds, or defects, while vacuum and slowly varying regions need less resolution. Multigrid, local finite-element refinement, wavelets, and adaptive coordinates all try to concentrate degrees of freedom where error is larger.

The benefit comes from distributing degrees of freedom more appropriately, not from abandoning convergence. Local refinement must be accompanied by error estimation, interlevel transfer, boundary matching, and validation of global observables. A criterion based only on local residuals may still miss long-range Coulomb, global energy-difference, or stress errors.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="四类多分辨率思想" titleEn="Four multiresolution ideas">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

**Multigrid** 在粗细网格间传递残差与修正：高频误差在细网格上容易平滑，低频误差在粗网格上变得相对高频，从而可被高效消除。**有限元自适应** 根据单元误差指示器做 $h$-refinement、提高局部多项式阶数的 $p$-refinement，或同时使用两者。**Wavelet** 通过尺度和位置双局域基函数表达函数，并可按系数阈值压缩。**自适应坐标** 用坐标映射在物理空间形成非均匀有效网格，同时在计算坐标中保留规则结构。

这些方法的矩阵、积分和边界处理不同，不能仅凭“都使用局部加密”视为数值等价。比较时至少需要对齐函数空间、变分或非变分性质、误差范数、核附近处理和目标 observable。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

**Multigrid** transfers residuals and corrections between fine and coarse grids: high-frequency error is readily smoothed on a fine grid, while low-frequency error appears at higher relative frequency on a coarse grid and can be removed efficiently. **Adaptive finite elements** perform $h$-refinement using smaller elements, $p$-refinement using higher local polynomial order, or both. **Wavelets** use functions localized in scale and position and can compress a representation by coefficient thresholding. **Adaptive coordinates** map physical space to produce a nonuniform effective grid while retaining regular structure in computational coordinates.

The methods have different matrices, quadrature rules, and boundary treatments; the fact that all use local refinement does not make them numerically equivalent. A comparison must at least align the function space, variational or nonvariational character, error norm, near-nucleus treatment, and target observable.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="坐标映射为何改变动能算符 | Why a coordinate map changes the kinetic operator"
  assumptions={[
    '存在可逆光滑映射 r=r(ξ)；',
    'Jacobian J=∂r/∂ξ 非奇异；',
    '积分和导数均按同一映射变换。',
  ]}
>

体积元满足

$$
d^3r=|\det J|\,d^3\xi.
$$

梯度按链式法则变换为

$$
\nabla_{\mathbf r}=J^{-T}\nabla_{\boldsymbol\xi}.
$$

因此 Laplacian 不再是计算坐标中的简单常系数二阶导数，而是含度量张量 $g^{ij}$ 与 Jacobian 的 Laplace–Beltrami 形式：

$$
\nabla_{\mathbf r}^2\psi
=\frac1{\sqrt g}\partial_i\left(\sqrt g\,g^{ij}\partial_j\psi\right).
$$

局部压缩坐标提高物理空间分辨率，同时引入变系数算符和额外离散复杂度。若只移动网格点而未一致变换积分权重与动能算符，所得问题并非原方程的合法坐标表示。

</DerivationBlock>
