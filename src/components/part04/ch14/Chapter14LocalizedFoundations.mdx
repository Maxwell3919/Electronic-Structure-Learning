## 14.1 局域原子中心轨道 | Localized Atom-Centered Orbitals

<BilingualCallout titleZh="来源层级" titleEn="Source layer" kind="source">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">【Martin 原书 | Martin source】印刷页 296–297 建立原子中心局域函数、实空间矩阵元、Bloch sums、Hamiltonian/overlap Fourier 矩阵和广义本征方程，并指出局域性使矩阵可截断但不保证截断精度。下面补足归一化、正定 metric 与 k-block 推导。</div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">【Martin source】Printed pp. 296–297 introduce atom-centred localized functions, real-space matrix elements, Bloch sums, Fourier Hamiltonian/overlap matrices, and the generalized eigenproblem, while noting that locality permits truncation but does not guarantee its accuracy. The derivations below make normalization, positive-definite metrics, and k-block structure explicit.</div>
</BilingualCallout>

<BilingualSection titleZh="实空间基函数与矩阵元" titleEn="Real-space basis functions and matrix elements">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

用复合指标 $m=(\kappa,n,l,\mu,\ldots)$ 标记原胞内第 $\kappa$ 个原子上的轨道、径向层和角动量分量。位于晶格平移 $\mathbf T$ 的基函数写为

$$
\chi_{m\mathbf T}(\mathbf r)
=\chi_m(\mathbf r-\boldsymbol\tau_m-\mathbf T).
$$

平移不变的单粒子 Hamiltonian 满足

$$
H_{mm'}(\mathbf T)
=\langle\chi_{m\mathbf 0}|\hat H|\chi_{m'\mathbf T}\rangle,
\qquad
S_{mm'}(\mathbf T)
=\langle\chi_{m\mathbf 0}|\chi_{m'\mathbf T}\rangle.
$$

只需要一个相对平移，是因为同时平移 bra、ket 和 Hamiltonian 不改变矩阵元。$H(\mathbf T)$ 与 $S(\mathbf T)$ 是表示数据，不是连续算符本身。若基函数指数衰减或具有有限支撑，远距离矩阵元通常衰减；把它们在某个半径后置零仍是额外近似。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Use a composite index $m=(\kappa,n,l,\mu,\ldots)$ for an orbital, radial shell, and angular component on atom $\kappa$ inside the reference cell. The basis function in lattice translate $\mathbf T$ is

$$
\chi_{m\mathbf T}(\mathbf r)
=\chi_m(\mathbf r-\boldsymbol\tau_m-\mathbf T).
$$

For a translation-invariant one-particle Hamiltonian,

$$
H_{mm'}(\mathbf T)
=\langle\chi_{m\mathbf 0}|\hat H|\chi_{m'\mathbf T}\rangle,
\qquad
S_{mm'}(\mathbf T)
=\langle\chi_{m\mathbf 0}|\chi_{m'\mathbf T}\rangle.
$$

Only one relative translation is needed because translating bra, ket, and Hamiltonian together leaves the matrix element unchanged. $H(\mathbf T)$ and $S(\mathbf T)$ are representation data, not the continuous operator itself. Exponentially decaying or finite-support basis functions usually produce decaying distant matrix elements; setting them identically to zero beyond a cutoff remains an additional approximation.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="Bloch sums 与 k-block 矩阵 | Bloch sums and k-block matrices"
  assumptions={[
    'Born–von Kármán 边界含 N 个原胞；',
    '所有平移相关矩阵元只依赖相对晶格矢；',
    '采用 Bloch phase exp(ik·T)，其他相位规范必须一致改变矩阵。',
  ]}
>

定义

$$
|\chi_{m\mathbf k}\rangle
=\frac{1}{\sqrt N}\sum_{\mathbf T}
e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf T}|\chi_{m\mathbf T}\rangle.
$$

计算两个 Bloch sums 的 Hamiltonian 元：

$$
\begin{aligned}
\langle\chi_{m\mathbf k}|\hat H|\chi_{m'\mathbf k'}\rangle
&=\frac1N\sum_{\mathbf T\mathbf T'}
e^{-i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf T}
e^{i\mathbf k'\cdot\mathbf T'}
H_{mm'}(\mathbf T'-\mathbf T)\\
&=\delta_{\mathbf k\mathbf k'}
\sum_{\mathbf R}e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf R}H_{mm'}(\mathbf R).
\end{aligned}
$$

晶格求和产生 $\delta_{\mathbf k\mathbf k'}$，所以不同 k 不耦合。定义

$$
H_{mm'}(\mathbf k)=\sum_{\mathbf T}e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf T}H_{mm'}(\mathbf T),
\qquad
S_{mm'}(\mathbf k)=\sum_{\mathbf T}e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf T}S_{mm'}(\mathbf T).
$$

Hermiticity 来自

$$
H_{mm'}(\mathbf T)=H_{m'm}^*(-\mathbf T),
\quad
S_{mm'}(\mathbf T)=S_{m'm}^*(-\mathbf T),
$$

因此 $H(\mathbf k)=H^\dagger(\mathbf k)$、$S(\mathbf k)=S^\dagger(\mathbf k)$。

</DerivationBlock>

<DerivationBlock
  title="非正交基中的广义本征问题 | Generalized eigenproblem in a nonorthogonal basis"
  assumptions={[
    '有限 Bloch 基线性无关，因此 S(k) 正定；',
    'Hamiltonian 在该有限空间中 Hermitian；',
    '轨道系数按同一 Bloch phase convention 排列。',
  ]}
>

展开

$$
|\psi_{n\mathbf k}\rangle
=\sum_m c_{mn}(\mathbf k)|\chi_{m\mathbf k}\rangle.
$$

把 Schrödinger 方程投影到 $\langle\chi_{m\mathbf k}|$：

$$
\sum_{m'}H_{mm'}(\mathbf k)c_{m'n}(\mathbf k)
=\varepsilon_{n\mathbf k}
\sum_{m'}S_{mm'}(\mathbf k)c_{m'n}(\mathbf k).
$$

即

$$
\boxed{H(\mathbf k)c_n(\mathbf k)=
\varepsilon_{n\mathbf k}S(\mathbf k)c_n(\mathbf k)}.
$$

物理归一化不是 $c_n^\dagger c_n=1$，而是

$$
c_n^\dagger(\mathbf k)S(\mathbf k)c_m(\mathbf k)=\delta_{nm}.
$$

若 $S$ 正定，可用 $S^{-1/2}$ 变换为普通 Hermitian 问题

$$
S^{-1/2}HS^{-1/2}u_n=\varepsilon_nu_n,
\qquad c_n=S^{-1/2}u_n.
$$

该正交化通常使原本短程的 Hamiltonian 变得更长程，因为矩阵函数 $S^{-1/2}$ 一般不是严格局域。正交与局域不能同时视为无成本条件。

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="S(k) 的正定性是模型有效条件" titleEn="Positive-definite S(k) is a model-validity condition" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

若 $S(\mathbf k)$ 出现零或负本征值，有限基在该 k 点线性相关或经验 overlap 参数不再定义合法 Hilbert-space metric。此时得到的能带不能通过“继续对角化”修复。需要改变基、参数或截断。小正本征值也会放大条件数和数值误差。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A zero or negative eigenvalue of $S(\mathbf k)$ indicates linear dependence at that k point or an empirical overlap model that no longer defines a valid Hilbert-space metric. Continuing diagonalization cannot repair the resulting bands; the basis, parameters, or truncation must change. A very small positive eigenvalue also amplifies conditioning and numerical error.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 14.2 原子型轨道的矩阵元 | Matrix Elements with Atomic-Like Orbitals

<BilingualSection titleZh="径向函数乘角向函数" titleEn="Radial functions times angular functions">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

原子型基函数通常分离为

$$
\chi_{nl\mu}(\mathbf r)=R_{nl}(r)Y_{l\mu}(\hat{\mathbf r}),
$$

或等价的实 Cartesian/spherical harmonics。对于一条连接两个中心的 bond，可把局部 z 轴取在 bond 方向。围绕 bond 轴的旋转对称使矩阵元按磁量子数绝对值分类：$|m|=0$ 为 $\sigma$，$|m|=1$ 为 $\pi$，$|m|=2$ 为 $\delta$。例如 p–p 只需 $V_{pp\sigma}$ 与 $V_{pp\pi}$ 两个 bond-aligned integrals。

这些 irreducible integrals 包含径向函数、Hamiltonian 和原子间距。方向旋转只重组它们，不计算径向数值。若 Hamiltonian 还显著依赖第三个原子或环境密度，two-centre 表不足以表示完整矩阵元。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

An atomic-like basis is commonly separated as

$$
\chi_{nl\mu}(\mathbf r)=R_{nl}(r)Y_{l\mu}(\hat{\mathbf r}),
$$

or in equivalent real Cartesian/spherical harmonics. For a bond joining two centres, choose the local z axis along the bond. Rotational symmetry around that axis classifies matrix elements by absolute magnetic quantum number: $|m|=0$ is $\sigma$, $|m|=1$ is $\pi$, and $|m|=2$ is $\delta$. A p–p pair therefore needs only $V_{pp\sigma}$ and $V_{pp\pi}$ in bond-aligned axes.

These irreducible integrals contain radial functions, the Hamiltonian, and interatomic distance. Directional rotation only recombines them; it does not determine radial values. If a third atom or environmental density contributes substantially to the Hamiltonian, a two-centre table is not a complete representation.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="代表性的 s–p 与 p–p Slater–Koster 旋转 | Representative s–p and p–p Slater–Koster rotation"
  assumptions={[
    '使用实 Cartesian p_x,p_y,p_z 轨道；',
    '键单位向量的方向余弦为 (l,m,n)；',
    '采用声明的 orbital phase convention；奇宇称轨道换相位会改变相应符号。',
  ]}
>

s 轨道是标量，沿 bond 的 p 轨道为

$$
|p_\sigma\rangle=l|p_x\rangle+m|p_y\rangle+n|p_z\rangle.
$$

因此

$$
\langle s|\hat H|p_x\rangle=lV_{sp\sigma},
$$

以及循环置换。p–p operator 在 bond-aligned 分解中为

$$
\hat H_{pp}=V_{pp\pi}I+
(V_{pp\sigma}-V_{pp\pi})|\hat{\mathbf d}\rangle\langle\hat{\mathbf d}|.
$$

投影到 Cartesian 轨道得到

$$
\boxed{
\langle p_x|H|p_x\rangle
=l^2V_{pp\sigma}+(1-l^2)V_{pp\pi}
}
$$

和

$$
\boxed{
\langle p_x|H|p_y\rangle
=lm(V_{pp\sigma}-V_{pp\pi}).
}
$$

当 bond 沿 x，$l=1$，于是 $p_xp_x=V_{pp\sigma}$、$p_yp_y=V_{pp\pi}$、off-diagonal 元为零。该极限是方向余弦实现的最低回归测试。

</DerivationBlock>

<SlaterKosterOrientationExplorer />

<BilingualSection titleZh="严格对称性与 two-centre 近似" titleEn="Exact symmetry versus the two-centre approximation">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

点群和空间群可以严格要求某些矩阵元相等、互为共轭或为零；这些约束不依赖 Slater–Koster 拟合。two-centre approximation 进一步假设一个 pair matrix element 只由两中心物种、轨道、距离和 bond 方向决定。晶体势来自所有原子，因此该假设通常是经验简化。

多中心修正可以表现为 coordination-dependent onsite、bond-order/environment-dependent hopping 或显式三中心项。加入它们可能提升 transferability，但同时增加参数量与可识别性问题。更多参数不自动意味着更可靠。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Point and space-group symmetry can rigorously require matrix elements to be equal, conjugate, or zero; these constraints do not depend on Slater–Koster fitting. The two-centre approximation further assumes that a pair matrix element depends only on the two species, orbitals, distance, and bond direction. Since the crystal potential is generated by all atoms, this is generally an empirical simplification.

Multicentre corrections can enter as coordination-dependent onsite terms, bond-order or environment-dependent hoppings, or explicit three-centre contributions. They may improve transferability but also increase parameter count and identifiability problems. More parameters do not automatically produce a more reliable model.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## 14.3 自旋–轨道相互作用 | Spin–Orbit Interaction

<BilingualCallout titleZh="来源层级" titleEn="Source layer" kind="source">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">【Martin 原书 | Martin source】印刷页 301–302 将 SOC 在局域基中近似为原子中心的 on-site $\mathbf L\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma$ 项，并展示 p-shell spinor matrix 的结构。Appendix O 说明其相对论来源与近似边界。本页不复制教材矩阵表。</div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">【Martin source】Printed pp. 301–302 approximate SOC in a localized basis by an atom-centred onsite $\mathbf L\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma$ term and show the structure of the p-shell spinor matrix. Appendix O supplies the relativistic origin and approximation boundary. The textbook matrix table is not reproduced.</div>
</BilingualCallout>

<BilingualSection titleZh="局域基中的 spinor Hamiltonian" titleEn="Spinor Hamiltonian in a localized basis">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

加入自旋后，每个空间轨道对应两个 spinor 分量，矩阵维度加倍。常用 atomic-centre 近似写为

$$
\hat H_{\mathrm{SO}}
=\sum_R\sum_l\frac{\zeta_l^R}{2}
\mathbf L_R\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma,
$$

其中 $\zeta_l^R$ 是原子 R 的 l shell 参数，$\boldsymbol\sigma$ 为 Pauli matrices。$L_z\sigma_z$ 给出 spin-conserving diagonal/off-diagonal orbital mixing，$L_\pm\sigma_\mp$ 产生 spin-flip matrix elements。

该形式在 p、d shell 的局域轨道基中易于构造，并保持 Hermiticity 与时间反演约束。它忽略不同原子之间的 SOC matrix elements 及晶体势的完整非球形梯度；对重元素、强杂化或低对称环境，on-site 参数的 transferability 需要验证。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

With spin, every spatial orbital has two spinor components and the matrix dimension doubles. A common atom-centred approximation is

$$
\hat H_{\mathrm{SO}}
=\sum_R\sum_l\frac{\zeta_l^R}{2}
\mathbf L_R\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma,
$$

where $\zeta_l^R$ is an l-shell parameter on atom R and $\boldsymbol\sigma$ are Pauli matrices. $L_z\sigma_z$ contributes spin-conserving orbital mixing, while $L_\pm\sigma_\mp$ generates spin-flip matrix elements.

This form is straightforward in localized p- and d-shell bases and can preserve Hermiticity and time-reversal constraints. It omits intersite SOC matrix elements and the full nonspherical gradient of the crystal potential. For heavy elements, strong hybridization, or low-symmetry environments, transferability of an onsite parameter requires validation.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="原子 L·S 的 j splitting 与加权重心 | Atomic L·S splitting and the weighted centre of gravity"
  assumptions={[
    '单电子 l shell，s=1/2；',
    '使用 H_SO=ζ L·S；',
    '忽略晶场和不同原子之间的 SOC。',
  ]}
>

利用

$$
\mathbf L\cdot\mathbf S
=\frac12[J(J+1)-L(L+1)-S(S+1)],
$$

对 $j=l+1/2$：

$$
\langle\mathbf L\cdot\mathbf S\rangle_+=\frac l2,
$$

对 $j=l-1/2$：

$$
\langle\mathbf L\cdot\mathbf S\rangle_-=-\frac{l+1}{2}.
$$

能量差为

$$
\Delta E=\zeta\frac{2l+1}{2}.
$$

两个 multiplet 的简并度分别为 $2l+2$ 和 $2l$，加权 trace 满足

$$
(2l+2)\frac{\zeta l}{2}
+(2l)\left[-\frac{\zeta(l+1)}{2}\right]=0.
$$

SOC 在完整 l shell 内重排能级但不移动其简并加权重心。晶场、截断基或环境依赖参数会改变实际 band splitting，不能只用原子公式外推。

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="SOC 参数不是材料不变量" titleEn="An SOC parameter is not a material invariant" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

$\zeta_l$ 可以来自原子计算、bulk band fitting 或下折叠。不同基、价电子集合、晶场和正交化会改变有效参数。一个参数在某条高对称线拟合良好，不证明全 BZ spin texture、拓扑不变量或磁各向异性正确；这些量需使用完整 spinor Hamiltonian 与相应收敛/对称性检查。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

$\zeta_l$ may come from an atomic calculation, bulk-band fit, or downfolding. Its effective value changes with basis, valence space, crystal field, and orthogonalization. A good fit along one symmetry line does not validate full-BZ spin texture, a topological invariant, or magnetic anisotropy; those require the complete spinor Hamiltonian and their own convergence and symmetry checks.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 14.4 Slater–Koster 二中心近似 | Slater–Koster Two-Center Approximation

<BilingualSection titleZh="从矩阵元到参数表" titleEn="From matrix elements to a parameter table">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Slater–Koster 方法把所有 bond orientations 的矩阵元写成少数 bond-aligned integrals 与方向余弦的多项式。对给定物种对和距离，参数集可能包括

$$
ss\sigma,\ sp\sigma,\ pp\sigma,\ pp\pi,
\ sd\sigma,\ pd\sigma,\ pd\pi,
\ dd\sigma,\ dd\pi,\ dd\delta,
$$

以及相应 overlap integrals。空间群相关的所有键必须由同一参数和旋转规则生成，否则模型会破坏晶体对称。

参数可以来自显式原子轨道积分、第一性原理 bands 的下折叠/拟合、实验能带或混合策略。参数来源决定可支持的主张；从 valence bands 拟合出的 Hamiltonian 不自动描述 conduction bands、总能或结构变化。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The Slater–Koster construction writes matrix elements for every bond orientation as direction-cosine polynomials multiplying a small set of bond-aligned integrals. For a species pair at a given distance, the set can include

$$
ss\sigma,\ sp\sigma,\ pp\sigma,\ pp\pi,
\ sd\sigma,\ pd\sigma,\ pd\pi,
\ dd\sigma,\ dd\pi,\ dd\delta,
$$

and corresponding overlap integrals. Every space-group-related bond must be generated from the same parameters and rotation rules, or the model breaks crystal symmetry.

Parameters can come from explicit atomic-orbital integrals, downfolding or fitting of first-principles bands, experimental bands, or mixed strategies. Their origin limits the supported claim. A Hamiltonian fitted to valence bands does not automatically describe conduction bands, total energies, or structural changes.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="band fitting 与 total-energy fitting" titleEn="Band fitting versus total-energy fitting">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

若目标只是固定结构附近的 band interpolation，可以最小化一组参考本征值的误差，并允许不影响目标窗口的参数组合保持不唯一。若目标是 total energy、force 或 stress，还需指定 band-energy double counting、短程 repulsion、charge transfer、environment dependence 和参数对距离的导数。两类拟合的损失函数与训练数据不同。

因此，“能带拟合 RMS 很小”不能支持结构优化或相稳定性。反过来，一个为总能拟合的模型也可能牺牲未占据带的精度。验收标准必须对应目标 observable，而不是用单一拟合分数概括模型。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

If the target is band interpolation near one structure, a fit can minimize errors in selected eigenvalues while leaving parameter combinations outside the target window underdetermined. A total-energy, force, or stress model additionally needs band-energy double counting, short-range repulsion, charge transfer, environment dependence, and distance derivatives of parameters. The loss functions and training data are different.

A small band-fit RMS therefore does not support structural optimization or phase stability. Conversely, a total-energy fit may sacrifice unoccupied-band accuracy. Acceptance criteria must follow the target observable rather than a single generic fit score.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="模型适用域必须显式记录" titleEn="The model domain must be explicit" kind="practice">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

最低记录包括：物种与价轨道集合、结构/应变/距离范围、charge 与 spin 状态、参考方法、拟合能量窗口、是否包含 overlap/SOC/environment terms、训练/验证拆分及目标误差。对新 coordination 或键长的预测属于外推；需要独立 reference 数据，而不是仅依赖模型内部平滑性。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A minimum record states species and valence-orbital set, structure/strain/distance range, charge and spin state, reference method, fitted energy window, whether overlap/SOC/environment terms are included, train/validation split, and target error. Prediction for new coordination or bond length is extrapolation and needs independent reference data rather than model smoothness alone.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>
