## 13.5 超胞：表面、界面与分子动力学 | Supercells: Surfaces, Interfaces, and Molecular Dynamics

<BilingualCallout titleZh="来源层级" titleEn="Source layer" kind="source">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">【Martin 原书 | Martin source】印刷页 289–292 讨论超胞作为人工周期表示、finite-size scaling、长程 Coulomb、frozen phonons、interfaces、surfaces 和 quantum MD。Sholl–Steckel Chapter 4 用于补充 slab、vacuum 和 k 点的实践检查，不提供通用数值参数。</div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">【Martin source】Printed pp. 289–292 discuss supercells as artificial periodic representations, finite-size scaling, long-range Coulomb terms, frozen phonons, interfaces, surfaces, and quantum MD. Sholl–Steckel Chapter 4 supplements practical slab, vacuum, and k-point checks without supplying universal parameter values.</div>
</BilingualCallout>

<BilingualSection titleZh="人工周期性是一种边界条件" titleEn="Artificial periodicity is a boundary condition">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

平面波天然服从周期边界。要表示缺陷、界面、slab 或分子，可以构造更大的 cell 并周期重复：

$$
V(\mathbf r+\mathbf A_i)=V(\mathbf r),
$$

其中 $\mathbf A_i$ 是超胞晶格矢。计算实际求解的是**周期超晶格**；孤立缺陷、单个界面或单个表面只在适当的大胞极限中出现。有限超胞结果应写为

$$
O(L_1,L_2,L_3,\text{shape},\mathbf k\text{-mesh},\ldots),
$$

并通过尺寸/形状序列判断目标量是否趋于所需物理极限。

超胞的优势是 Hamiltonian、FFT、本征求解和力公式不需为新几何重写。代价是大量“空”区域仍占据均匀平面波/网格自由度，而且周期镜像可能通过电子、Coulomb、弹性或磁性通道耦合。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Plane waves naturally obey periodic boundaries. A defect, interface, slab, or molecule can be represented by constructing a larger cell and repeating it periodically:

$$
V(\mathbf r+\mathbf A_i)=V(\mathbf r),
$$

where $\mathbf A_i$ are supercell lattice vectors. The computed problem is a periodic superlattice. An isolated defect, a single interface, or a single surface appears only in an appropriate large-cell limit. A finite-supercell result should be written as

$$
O(L_1,L_2,L_3,\text{shape},\mathbf k\text{-mesh},\ldots),
$$

and a size/shape sequence is needed to establish the desired physical limit.

The advantage is that Hamiltonian, FFT, eigensolver, and force machinery need not be rewritten for each geometry. The cost is that empty space still consumes uniform plane-wave/grid degrees of freedom and periodic images can couple through electronic, Coulomb, elastic, or magnetic channels.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

若一维超胞长度为 $A=Na$，对应 reciprocal spacing 从

$$
G_0=\frac{2\pi}{a}
$$

变为

$$
G_0^{\mathrm{SC}}=\frac{2\pi}{Na}=\frac{G_0}{N}.
$$

超胞 Brillouin zone 缩小为 primitive zone 的 $1/N$。primitive-cell 中相差超胞 reciprocal vector 的状态在 supercell 表示中折叠到同一 $\mathbf k_{\mathrm{SC}}$。因此，超胞 Γ 点可以同时包含多个 primitive-cell k 点的信息，但只有在结构仍保持 primitive periodicity 时可按简单 folding 解释；缺陷或重构会真正耦合这些 folded states。

扩大超胞并固定相同的 Monkhorst–Pack 整数网格会改变物理 k 点密度。公平比较应明确保持的是 reciprocal spacing、k 点数、不可约采样还是特定积分误差。Γ-only 对大胞可能足够，但不能由“胞很大”自动推出。

</DerivationBlock>

### Frozen phonons and elastic distortions | 冻结声子与弹性畸变

<BilingualSection titleZh="可交换波矢由超胞决定" titleEn="Commensurate wave vectors are fixed by the supercell">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

在 frozen-phonon 方法中，原子位移图样必须与超胞周期相容。一个 supercell 允许的调制波矢是其 reciprocal vectors；对每个图样计算力或能量曲率，可以得到对应离散 q 点的振动信息。直接力常数法进一步从超胞中不同原子受力恢复实空间 force constants，再 Fourier 变换到一般 q。

Martin 指出，若要避免相互作用跨越超胞边界后与自身混叠，cell 需覆盖 force-constant 范围；“至少两倍作用范围”是该截断模型中的几何条件。真实材料的范围未知，必须通过增加 supercell 或比较 dispersion/force constants 验证。该内容只说明超胞表示，完整 phonon/DFPT 理论属于 Chapter 20。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

In a frozen-phonon method, an atomic displacement pattern must be commensurate with the supercell. The allowed modulation wave vectors are reciprocal vectors of that cell. Energies or forces for each pattern provide vibrational information at the corresponding discrete q points. A direct force-constant method instead reconstructs real-space force constants from forces on atoms in a supercell and Fourier transforms them to general q.

Martin notes that the cell must cover the force-constant range to prevent interactions crossing a boundary from aliasing with themselves; twice the interaction range is the geometric requirement of that truncation picture. The range is unknown for a real material and must be tested by enlarging the cell or comparing dispersions/force constants. This section describes the representation only; full phonon and DFPT theory belongs to Chapter 20.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

### Interfaces | 界面

<BilingualSection titleZh="一个周期 superlattice 通常含不止一个界面" titleEn="A periodic superlattice usually contains more than one interface">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

将材料 A 和 B 沿一个方向堆叠并周期重复，会形成 A/B superlattice。周期边界通常产生两个界面；若两者化学终止或极性不同，它们可能互相影响。层厚、面内应变、registry、原子弛豫和界面化学必须作为结构定义的一部分记录。

band offset 不能只由两个独立 bulk 能带边直接相减。需要在同一 electrostatic reference 下对齐 bulk-like 区域，检查层厚是否恢复各自内部势，并区分界面偶极、应变和 functional/band-gap 误差。若 supercell 内没有足够 bulk-like 区域，“bulk band offset”本身可能没有清楚极限。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Stacking materials A and B along one direction and repeating them periodically creates an A/B superlattice. Periodic boundaries normally produce two interfaces, which can interact if their chemical terminations or polarities differ. Layer thickness, in-plane strain, registry, atomic relaxation, and interface chemistry are part of the structural definition.

A band offset is not obtained reliably by subtracting band edges from two unrelated bulk calculations. Bulk-like regions must be aligned to a common electrostatic reference, layer thickness must be sufficient to recover internal potentials, and interface dipoles, strain, and functional/gap errors must be separated. Without sufficiently bulk-like regions in the supercell, a bulk band-offset limit may not be defined clearly.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

### Surfaces and slabs | 表面与 slab

<BilingualSection titleZh="vacuum 是边界控制参数" titleEn="Vacuum is a boundary-control parameter">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

slab supercell 在表面法向加入 vacuum，并在面内保持二维周期性。Sholl–Steckel 的实践讨论给出三项可复查原则：vacuum 应使密度尾部和势在镜像间足够解耦；面内 k mesh 仍需独立收敛；法向长 cell 通常只需一个 k 点，但这来自该方向的 reciprocal spacing，而不是通用固定规则。

对不对称 slab，两个面产生净偶极，周期重复会形成非物理电场；需使用对称 slab、dipole correction、Coulomb truncation 或尺寸外推，并说明所求 observable。对带电 slab/adsorbate，背景电荷和 electrostatic reference 更复杂。仅观察 vacuum 中密度接近零不能证明静电镜像误差已消失。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A slab supercell adds vacuum along the surface normal while retaining two-dimensional periodicity in-plane. The practical discussion in Sholl–Steckel yields three checkable principles: vacuum should decouple density tails and potentials between images; the in-plane k mesh requires independent convergence; and a long normal direction often needs only one k point because of its reciprocal spacing, not because of a universal rule.

An asymmetric slab can carry a net dipole and create an artificial electric field under periodic repetition. Symmetric slabs, dipole corrections, Coulomb truncation, or size extrapolation are then required, with the target observable stated. Charged slabs or adsorbates make background charge and electrostatic references still more delicate. Observing a nearly zero density in the vacuum does not prove that electrostatic image error has vanished.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

### Molecular dynamics | 分子动力学

<BilingualSection titleZh="本节只处理 cell 与 electronic solve 的边界" titleEn="This section addresses only cell and electronic-solve boundaries">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

quantum MD 在每个离子步调用电子结构力，因此电子 SCF、轨道 residual 和力误差会进入时间积分。周期边界常用于凝聚态以避免移动粒子遇到开放边界，但有限 cell 会限制长波涨落、扩散路径和关联长度。time step、thermostat、ensemble、能量漂移和采样充分性属于 Chapter 19；此处只强调 plane-wave/supercell 计算为每一步提供的有限体系力。

不能因单步力已收敛就声称轨迹统计已收敛，也不能因总能短时稳定就证明 cell size 足够。电子误差、积分误差、有限尺寸误差和统计误差需要独立控制。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Quantum MD calls an electronic-structure force evaluation at every ionic step, so electronic SCF, orbital residual, and force errors enter the time integration. Periodic boundaries are common for condensed matter because particles do not encounter open walls, but a finite cell restricts long-wavelength fluctuations, diffusion paths, and correlation lengths. Time step, thermostat, ensemble, energy drift, and sampling adequacy belong to Chapter 19; the present section only identifies the finite-system forces supplied by a plane-wave/supercell solve.

A converged force at one step does not establish converged trajectory statistics, and short-time energy stability does not prove adequate cell size. Electronic, integration, finite-size, and statistical errors require separate control.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="超胞收敛的最低充分记录" titleEn="Minimum sufficient record for supercell convergence" kind="practice">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

固定 Hamiltonian、赝势、functional、geometry protocol、cutoff 和 reciprocal k density，改变 cell length/shape；记录目标量及其 reference/alignment；至少比较两个更严格尺寸并说明预期渐近形式。若带电、偶极、弹性或金属长程响应存在，必须记录 correction 或 extrapolation，而不是只报告 vacuum Å 数或原子数。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Hold Hamiltonian, pseudopotential, functional, geometry protocol, cutoff, and reciprocal k density fixed while changing cell length or shape. Record the target observable and its reference/alignment, compare at least two stricter sizes, and state the expected asymptotic form. For charged, dipolar, elastic, or metallic long-range response, record the correction or extrapolation rather than reporting only a vacuum thickness or atom count.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 13.6 团簇与分子 | Clusters and Molecules

<BilingualSection titleZh="open boundary 与 plane-wave supercell" titleEn="Open boundaries versus a plane-wave supercell">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

有限体系自然满足波函数和密度在远处衰减的 open boundary。real-space finite-difference、finite-element 或 multiresolution 方法可以直接在有限区域施加边界条件。平面波方法则把分子放入三维 supercell，使密度在边界前足够小，并通过增大 cell 控制镜像耦合。

均匀 plane-wave basis 的数量近似与 cell volume 成正比，即使新增区域几乎为空；这是有限体系的主要效率代价。另一方面，平面波基不依赖原子中心，不产生传统 Gaussian basis-set superposition error，并可复用周期固体的同一 FFT/force machinery。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A finite system naturally has open boundaries, with wavefunction and density decaying at large distance. Real-space finite-difference, finite-element, or multiresolution methods can impose boundary conditions directly on a finite domain. A plane-wave method instead places the molecule in a three-dimensional supercell, requiring the density to be sufficiently small before the boundary and controlling image coupling by enlarging the cell.

The number of uniform plane waves grows approximately with cell volume even when the added region is nearly empty; this is the principal efficiency cost for finite systems. On the other hand, plane waves are not tied to atomic centres, avoid conventional Gaussian basis-set superposition error, and reuse the same FFT and force machinery as periodic solids.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="cell length、cutoff 与基组数是不同控制轴 | Cell length, cutoff, and basis count are distinct controls"
  assumptions={[
    '固定动能 cutoff E_cut；',
    '三维盒体积 Ω 足够大，可使用倒空间球体积估算；',
    '只讨论 plane-wave count 的渐近尺度。',
  ]}
>

$G_{\max}=\sqrt{2E_{\mathrm{cut}}}$，每个 reciprocal state 占据体积 $(2\pi)^3/\Omega$，所以

$$
N_{\mathrm{PW}}
\approx\frac{\Omega}{(2\pi)^3}\frac{4\pi}{3}G_{\max}^3
\propto\Omega E_{\mathrm{cut}}^{3/2}.
$$

增大 cell 在固定 cutoff 下线性增加 plane-wave count，主要控制 periodic-image/boundary error；增大 cutoff 在固定 cell 下按 $E_{\mathrm{cut}}^{3/2}$ 增加 count，主要控制表示误差。二者不能用一个参数序列替代。对于带电或强偶极有限体系，Coulomb error 的 cell dependence 还可能比密度尾部更长程。

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="有限体系的验证顺序" titleEn="Validation order for a finite system">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

最低成本顺序通常是：先在代表性 cell 中确定 cutoff/FFT 对能量和力的要求；再固定表示精度增加 cell，检查总能差、轨道能级差、dipole 或目标 response；最后检查 symmetry、spin、charge 和 geometry 状态。若 geometry 随 cell 改变，尺寸误差与结构误差会混合，应保存固定几何和重新弛豫两类比较。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A low-cost sequence is usually to determine cutoff/FFT requirements for energies and forces in a representative cell, then hold representation accuracy fixed while enlarging the cell and checking energy differences, level differences, dipoles, or the target response, and finally verify symmetry, spin, charge, and geometry state. If geometry changes with cell size, boundary and structural errors become mixed; both fixed-geometry and re-relaxed comparisons should be retained.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## 13.7 平面波与网格方法的应用 | Applications of Plane-Wave and Grid Methods

<BilingualSection titleZh="应用清单是能力地图，不是验证证明" titleEn="An application list is a capability map, not validation evidence">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Martin 将结构预测、状态方程、相变、声子、有效电荷、极化、能带、激发、表面态和量子 MD 等实例指向后续章节。共同点是这些任务都可从同一 total-energy/force/response 框架出发；差异在于目标 observable、边界条件和验收标准。

方法曾成功用于某类问题，只支持“该表示与算法具备处理此类对象的先例”。它不证明新的赝势、functional、cell、磁态或 sampling 已满足要求。实际项目需要从目标量反推独立 convergence axes：结构/压力关注 stress 与相对焓，声子关注 force constants/q sampling，表面关注 slab/vacuum/alignment，激发关注超越基态 DFT 的方法边界。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Martin points to later chapters for structure prediction, equations of state, phase transitions, phonons, effective charges, polarization, bands, excitations, surface states, and quantum MD. These tasks share a total-energy/force/response framework but differ in target observable, boundary condition, and acceptance criterion.

Prior success in an application class supports only the existence of precedent for treating that object with the representation and algorithm. It does not validate a new pseudopotential, functional, cell, magnetic state, or sampling. A project must derive independent convergence axes from the target quantity: stress and relative enthalpy for structure/pressure, force constants and q sampling for phonons, slab/vacuum/alignment for surfaces, and method boundaries beyond ground-state DFT for excitations.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="程序通用性不能替代问题定义" titleEn="Code generality cannot replace problem definition" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

“只改几行输入即可处理 bulk、interface、surface 或 molecule”描述实现复用性，不说明这些计算共享相同误差或解释。每次改变边界、维度、charge、spin、occupations 或 observable，都要重新声明物理问题和验证序列。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The ability to treat a bulk solid, interface, surface, or molecule by changing only a few input lines describes implementation reuse, not shared errors or interpretation. Every change of boundary, dimensionality, charge, spin, occupations, or observable requires a renewed physical definition and validation sequence.

  </div>
