## 13.2 走向自洽与介电屏蔽 | Approach to Self-Consistency and Dielectric Screening

<BilingualCallout titleZh="来源层级" titleEn="Source layer" kind="source">

<BilingualSection titleZh="自洽是固定点，不是单次本征问题" titleEn="Self-consistency is a fixed point, not one eigenproblem">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

给定输入密度 $n_i^{\mathrm{in}}$，一次 Kohn–Sham 求解定义映射

$$
n_i^{\mathrm{out}}=\mathcal F[n_i^{\mathrm{in}}].
$$

自洽解满足

$$
n^\star=\mathcal F[n^\star].
$$

同样可以选择势、密度矩阵或预条件 residual 作为迭代变量；不同程序的变量和规范不必相同。最简单的线性 mixing 为

$$
n_{i+1}^{\mathrm{in}}
=(1-\alpha)n_i^{\mathrm{in}}+\alpha n_i^{\mathrm{out}}
=n_i^{\mathrm{in}}+\alpha R_i,
$$

其中 $R_i=n_i^{\mathrm{out}}-n_i^{\mathrm{in}}$。$\alpha$ 是算法参数，不是物理介电常数。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Given an input density $n_i^{\mathrm{in}}$, one Kohn–Sham solution defines a map

$$
n_i^{\mathrm{out}}=\mathcal F[n_i^{\mathrm{in}}].
$$

A self-consistent solution satisfies

$$
n^\star=\mathcal F[n^\star].
$$

The iteration variable may instead be a potential, density matrix, or preconditioned residual; implementations need not use identical variables or gauges. The simplest linear mixing is

$$
n_{i+1}^{\mathrm{in}}
=(1-\alpha)n_i^{\mathrm{in}}+\alpha n_i^{\mathrm{out}}
=n_i^{\mathrm{in}}+\alpha R_i,
$$

with $R_i=n_i^{\mathrm{out}}-n_i^{\mathrm{in}}$. The coefficient $\alpha$ is an algorithmic parameter, not a physical dielectric constant.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="线性 mixing 的局部谱条件 | Local spectral condition for linear mixing"
  assumptions={[
    '存在一个待求固定点 x*；',
    '映射 F 在固定点附近可线性化；',
    '当前讨论有限离散空间中的 Jacobian，不证明连续问题存在唯一解。',
  ]}
>

写误差 $e_i=x_i-x^\star$。固定点附近

$$
F(x_i)-F(x^\star)=Je_i+O(\|e_i\|^2),
$$

其中 $J=\partial F/\partial x|_{x^\star}$。线性 mixing

$$
x_{i+1}=x_i+\alpha[F(x_i)-x_i]
$$

给出

$$
e_{i+1}=\left[I+\alpha(J-I)\right]e_i+O(\|e_i\|^2).
$$

若 $J$ 可在当前度量下分解为本征模 $Jv_j=s_jv_j$，则该模的误差因子为

$$
\boxed{q_j=1+\alpha(s_j-1)}.
$$

局部线性收敛需要所有被表示模式满足 $|q_j|<1$。$q_j>0$ 给出同号衰减，$-1<q_j<0$ 给出振荡衰减，$|q_j|>1$ 发散。一个全局 $\alpha$ 必须同时稳定最困难的模式，因此常会让其他模式收敛得不必要地慢。

这个推导使用抽象映射斜率 $s_j$。若迭代变量改为势，或者 residual 定义取相反号，介电矩阵与 $J$ 的符号、尺度和度量关系会改变；不能脱离定义把一个介电常数直接代入上式。

</DerivationBlock>

### 介电响应与 charge sloshing | Dielectric response and charge sloshing

<BilingualSection titleZh="为什么大超胞更容易出现长波不稳定" titleEn="Why large supercells expose long-wavelength instabilities">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Martin 用势误差的线性响应写出

$$
\delta V^{\mathrm{out}}(\mathbf G)
=\sum_{\mathbf G'}\epsilon(\mathbf G,\mathbf G')
\delta V^{\mathrm{in}}(\mathbf G').
$$

在其内部场约定下，介电响应在大 $G$ 处接近单位，而小波矢处可以很大；金属长波响应更强。小原胞没有非常小的非零倒格矢，因此最危险的长波模式可能根本不在离散集合中。增大超胞后，$G_{\min}\sim2\pi/L$ 下降，新的低成本电荷重排模式进入计算。金属 slab 中电荷可在两个表面或 slab 与 vacuum 之间来回移动，形成 charge sloshing。

这说明困难来自“物理响应 × 边界条件 × 离散波矢”的组合，不是因为更大的超胞使 Kohn–Sham 方程本身改变。减小 mixing 可能稳定迭代，但会减慢所有模式；更合理的策略是对不同波矢采用不同更新尺度。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Martin writes the linear response of a potential error as

$$
\delta V^{\mathrm{out}}(\mathbf G)
=\sum_{\mathbf G'}\epsilon(\mathbf G,\mathbf G')
\delta V^{\mathrm{in}}(\mathbf G').
$$

In his internal-field convention the dielectric response approaches unity at large G but can be large at small wave vector; metallic long-wavelength response is stronger. A small primitive cell contains no extremely small nonzero reciprocal vector, so the most difficult long-wavelength mode may be absent from the discrete set. Enlarging a supercell lowers $G_{\min}\sim2\pi/L$ and admits low-cost charge-transfer modes. In a metallic slab, charge can move between surfaces or between slab and vacuum, producing charge sloshing.

The difficulty therefore comes from the combination of physical response, boundary conditions, and discrete wave vectors, not from a change in the Kohn–Sham equation itself. Reducing the mixing fraction can stabilize an iteration but slows every mode; a more selective strategy assigns different update scales to different wave vectors.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="对角长波 preconditioner 的作用 | Action of a diagonal long-wavelength preconditioner"
  assumptions={[
    '只构造教学用对角 reciprocal-space 模型；',
    '不声称代表某一程序的 Kerker/Thomas–Fermi 实现；',
    'G=0 电荷模式由电中性或单独约束处理。',
  ]}
>

一个常见尺度模型为

$$
P(G)=\frac{G^2}{G^2+G_0^2},
$$

并用

$$
n_{i+1}^{\mathrm{in}}(\mathbf G)
=n_i^{\mathrm{in}}(\mathbf G)
+\alpha P(G)R_i(\mathbf G).
$$

当 $G\ll G_0$ 时，$P(G)\approx G^2/G_0^2$，长波 residual 被抑制；当 $G\gg G_0$ 时，$P(G)\to1$，短波模式接近普通 mixing。$G_0$ 控制的是算法的波矢选择性，不是自动可转移到所有材料的物理参数。

若 preconditioner 只是对 residual 的可逆/一致线性变换，固定点条件仍为 $R=0$；它改变迭代谱而不改变目标解。若算法还截断模式、改变电荷约束或使用不一致的 response，则目标问题可能被改变，必须另行说明。

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="预条件不等于改变 dielectric physics" titleEn="Preconditioning is not a change of dielectric physics" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

模型介电函数、Kerker 型因子或经验 mixing 只用于构造更好的数值步长。它们不替换 Kohn–Sham Hamiltonian 中的 Hartree/XC 响应，也不能作为材料介电常数的计算结果。SCF 更快只说明求解器路径改善，不说明泛函、赝势或最终 observable 更准确。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A model dielectric function, Kerker-type factor, or empirical mixing rule constructs a better numerical step. It does not replace Hartree/XC response in the Kohn–Sham Hamiltonian and is not a calculated material dielectric constant. Faster SCF convergence indicates an improved solver path, not a more accurate functional, pseudopotential, or final observable.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

### History-based updates | 基于历史的更新

<BilingualSection titleZh="Broyden、DIIS 与可用信息" titleEn="Broyden, DIIS, and the information being reused">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

完整 Jacobian 或 dielectric matrix 的构造通常比原问题更昂贵。Broyden 类方法从连续迭代中的 $\Delta x$ 与 $\Delta R$ 建立低秩近似；DIIS/RMM-DIIS 在历史 residual 张成的子空间中选择组合，使预测 residual 变小。二者都在复用迭代历史，但优化对象、度量和稳定化细节可以不同。

历史方法仍需要：有限历史长度、线性依赖处理、初始 preconditioner、最大步长和失败回退。对一个体系有效的默认参数不构成对另一个体系的保证。尤其在结构变化、磁态变化、占据重排或金属费米面变化时，旧历史可能不再描述当前 Jacobian。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Constructing the full Jacobian or dielectric matrix is normally more expensive than the original problem. Broyden families build low-rank approximations from successive $\Delta x$ and $\Delta R$ pairs. DIIS/RMM-DIIS chooses combinations in a subspace spanned by historical residuals so that a predicted residual is reduced. Both reuse iteration history, but their optimized objects, metrics, and stabilization details can differ.

History-based methods still need a finite history length, treatment of linear dependence, an initial preconditioner, step limits, and a failure fallback. Defaults that work for one system do not guarantee another. During structural, magnetic, occupation, or metallic Fermi-surface changes, old history may cease to represent the current Jacobian.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

### 停止条件和证据上限 | Stopping criteria and evidence ceilings

<BilingualSection titleZh="至少四个独立门槛" titleEn="At least four independent gates">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

一个生产计算通常同时观察：

1. 固定势本征问题的轨道 residual；
2. 输入/输出密度或势 residual 的范数；
3. 总能或自由能在相邻迭代间的变化；
4. 当前任务所需的力、应力、磁矩、带隙或其他 observable。

对金属还要核对占据、smearing 与 k 点是否使残差和能量定义一致。小能量变化可能来自 mixing 步长过小；小密度 residual 也不证明 cutoff 或 k mesh 足够。合理验收应记录 residual 定义、归一化、阈值、连续满足次数和目标 observable 的独立标准。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A production calculation commonly monitors, at minimum:

1. orbital residuals for the fixed-potential eigenproblem;
2. a norm of the input/output density or potential residual;
3. the change in total or free energy between iterations;
4. forces, stress, moments, gaps, or another observable required by the task.

For metals, occupations, smearing, and k points must be consistent with residual and energy definitions. A small energy change can result from an excessively small mixing step, and a small density residual does not establish cutoff or k-mesh convergence. A useful acceptance record states the residual definition, normalization, threshold, number of consecutive passes, and an independent criterion for the target observable.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="SCF 收敛能支持什么" titleEn="What SCF convergence supports" kind="practice">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

SCF 收敛支持“在当前有限表示、边界、占据、赝势和泛函下找到了满足所声明固定点阈值的数值解”。它不单独支持：连续基极限、正确基态磁构型、全布里渊区金属性、力/应力收敛、相对能量精度或实验一致性。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

SCF convergence supports the statement that, under the current finite representation, boundary conditions, occupations, pseudopotential, and functional, a numerical solution met the declared fixed-point threshold. It does not by itself establish the complete-basis limit, the correct magnetic ground state, full-Brillouin-zone metallicity, converged forces or stress, relative-energy accuracy, or agreement with experiment.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>
