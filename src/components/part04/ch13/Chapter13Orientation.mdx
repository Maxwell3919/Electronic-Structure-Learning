## 阅读定位 | Reading Orientation

<BilingualSection titleZh="本章解决的问题" titleEn="The problem addressed in this chapter" eyebrow="Chapter 13">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Chapter 12 回答了“如何表示一个周期独立粒子问题”；Chapter 13 回答“如何把该有限表示变成完整的 Kohn–Sham 计算”。新的困难不再只是基组截断，而是多个数值层相互耦合：轨道决定密度，密度决定有效势，有效势又决定下一组轨道。只有这个固定点问题达到声明的停止标准，才可以讨论总能、力、应力或派生物性。

本章以平面波为主，因为动能、局域势和 Fourier 变换之间的分工清楚；实空间网格始终参与局域乘法和密度构造。由此可看见一般原则：表示空间、算符应用、本征求解、自洽更新和科学判断是五个不同对象。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Chapter 12 answered how a periodic independent-particle problem is represented. Chapter 13 asks how that finite representation becomes a complete Kohn–Sham calculation. The new difficulty is no longer basis truncation alone: orbitals determine a density, the density determines an effective potential, and the potential determines the next orbitals. Only after this fixed-point problem satisfies a declared stopping criterion can total energies, forces, stresses, or derived properties be assessed.

Plane waves are the main language because the division of labour among kinetic energy, local potentials, and Fourier transforms is transparent. Real-space grids remain intrinsic to local multiplication and density construction. The chapter therefore exposes a general principle: representation, operator application, eigensolution, self-consistent updating, and scientific interpretation are five distinct objects.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

Appendices F, G, and M supply periodic Coulomb, stress, and iterative-solver details. These results are used rather than reproduced as separate chapters.

<BilingualSection titleZh="本章之后应能判断什么" titleEn="Judgments enabled by the chapter">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

完成本章后，应能够：

- 从总能泛函识别电子动能、局域/非局域外势、Hartree、xc、Ewald 和 double-counting 项；
- 解释为什么 $G=0$ Coulomb 分量必须以中性组合处理；
- 区分本征 residual、密度 residual、总能变化和目标 observable 收敛；
- 用线性化 Jacobian/介电响应解释 charge sloshing、mixing 与 preconditioning；
- 说明 PAW 的 smooth、one-center all-electron 和 one-center auxiliary 三部分；
- 解释平面波 exact exchange 的非局域性、$q=0$ 奇异处理和成本来源；
- 为 slab、interface、defect、molecule 或 cluster 设计超胞尺寸与周期镜像检查；
- 拒绝从“程序退出”“SCF 收敛”或“两个程序接近”直接推出科学正确性。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

After this chapter, a reader should be able to:

- identify electronic kinetic, local/nonlocal external, Hartree, exchange–correlation, Ewald, and double-counting terms in a total-energy expression;
- explain why the $G=0$ Coulomb component must be handled in neutral groupings;
- distinguish eigen-residual, density residual, total-energy change, and target-observable convergence;
- use a linearized Jacobian/dielectric response to explain charge sloshing, mixing, and preconditioning;
- describe the smooth, one-centre all-electron, and one-centre auxiliary parts of PAW;
- explain nonlocal plane-wave exact exchange, treatment of the $q=0$ singularity, and its cost drivers;
- design supercell-size and periodic-image checks for slabs, interfaces, defects, molecules, or clusters;
- reject an inference from normal termination, SCF convergence, or inter-code agreement directly to scientific correctness.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="五类误差不能合并为一个阈值" titleEn="Five error classes cannot be collapsed into one tolerance" kind="boundary">

本章持续分开：平面波/网格表示误差、k 点积分误差、有限本征求解误差、SCF 固定点误差和超胞边界误差。它们可以相互耦合，但一个阈值通过不能替代另一类检查。泛函和赝势的物理近似还位于这些数值误差之外。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The chapter keeps separate plane-wave/grid representation error, k-point integration error, finite eigensolver error, SCF fixed-point error, and supercell boundary error. They can interact, but passing one tolerance does not replace another check. Functional and pseudopotential approximations lie outside these numerical errors.

  </div>
