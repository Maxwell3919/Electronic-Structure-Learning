  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">【Martin source】Printed pp. 324–327 discuss all-electron and pseudopotential numerical orbitals, confinement, two-centre tables, separable nonlocal terms, local-potential grids, and partitioned integration. <BilingualSection titleZh="radial numerical functions" titleEn="Numerical radial functions">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>atom-centred numerical orbital 通常写为</p>

$$
\chi_{I\zeta lm}(\mathbf r)
=\frac{u_{I\zeta l}(r_I)}{r_I}Y_{lm}(\widehat{\mathbf r_I}),
\qquad
\mathbf r_I=\mathbf r-\mathbf R_I.
$$

    <p>radial function $u_{I\zeta l}$ 可来自 isolated/pseudo atom equation，再通过 modified potential、energy shift、hard/soft wall 或 variational optimization 调整 range。multiple-ζ 可由不同 confinement、split-valence、energy derivatives 或 response functions 生成；polarization orbitals 应针对 nonspherical response，而不只是复制一个极 diffuse unoccupied atomic state。</p>
    <p>all-electron orbitals 必须解析 near-nuclear rapid variation；pseudoorbitals 更 smooth，可在较均匀 grid 上使用。二者都可能 finite support，也都需要对 radial grid、angular expansion 和 confinement independently converge。</p>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>An atom-centred numerical orbital is commonly written as</p>

$$
\chi_{I\zeta lm}(\mathbf r)
=\frac{u_{I\zeta l}(r_I)}{r_I}Y_{lm}(\widehat{\mathbf r_I}),
\qquad
\mathbf r_I=\mathbf r-\mathbf R_I.
$$

    <p>The radial function $u_{I\zeta l}$ can come from an isolated or pseudo-atom equation and then be range-adjusted by a modified potential, energy shift, hard or soft wall, or variational optimization. Multiple-zeta functions may be generated by different confinement conditions, split valence, energy derivatives, or response functions. Polarization orbitals should target nonspherical response rather than simply copying a very diffuse unoccupied atomic state.</p>
    <p>All-electron orbitals must resolve rapid near-nuclear variation; pseudoorbitals are smoother and can use a more uniform grid. Either can have finite support, and both require separate convergence of radial grid, angular expansion, and confinement.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="finite support 如何生成 sparsity | How finite support produces sparsity"
  assumptions={[
    'basis function μ has support radius R_μ；',
    'local/semilocal operator or finite-range tabulated integral；',
    'atoms are separated by R_IJ。',
  ]}
>

若

$$
\chi_\mu(\mathbf r-\mathbf R_I)=0
\quad\text{for}\quad
|\mathbf r-\mathbf R_I|\ge R_\mu,
$$

且 $R_{IJ}>R_\mu+R_\nu$，则 supports 不相交，因此

$$
S_{I\mu,J\nu}=0,
\qquad
T_{I\mu,J\nu}=0
$$

对局域 kinetic/overlap-like pair integrals 成立。固定 density 与原子数密度下，每个 orbital 只与有限邻域连接，matrix nonzero count 可近似随 system size 线性增长。

这只证明 matrix construction 的 sparsity。若随后执行 dense diagonalization 或 global orthogonalization，整体仍可具有 $O(N^3)$ time 与 $O(N^2)$ memory。Chapter 18 才讨论如何同时避免这些 global stages。

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="confinement、smoothness 与 completeness" titleEn="Confinement, smoothness, and completeness">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>缩小 support radius 会减少 neighbours 与 matrix elements，但也截断 vacuum tail、weak bonding 与 polarization response。hard wall 可产生 derivative discontinuity 或高 kinetic-energy components；smooth confinement 降低 derivative artifact，却仍改变 variational space。basis error 必须用 energy differences、forces、stress、dipoles、bands 或目标 response 分别检查，不能只看 total energy 单调变化。</p>
    <p>增加 cutoff 不必单调改善 numerical conditioning：新的 diffuse functions 可能与已有 functions near-linearly dependent。需要同时监控 $S$ 的 smallest eigenvalue/condition number 与目标 observable。</p>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>Reducing the support radius lowers the neighbour count and number of matrix elements but truncates vacuum tails, weak bonding, and polarization response. A hard wall can introduce derivative discontinuities or high kinetic-energy components. Smooth confinement reduces derivative artifacts while still changing the variational space. Basis error must be checked separately for energy differences, forces, stress, dipoles, bands, or the target response; monotonic total-energy change alone is insufficient.</p>
    <p>Increasing cutoff need not improve numerical conditioning monotonically. New diffuse functions can become nearly linearly dependent on existing functions. The smallest eigenvalue or condition number of $S$ and the target observable should be monitored together.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>

### Integral paths | 积分路径

<BilingualSection titleZh="position-only two-centre tables" titleEn="Position-only two-centre tables">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>overlap 与 kinetic terms 在固定 species/orbitals 下可写为 inter-centre distance 和 bond orientation 的函数：</p>

$$
S_{\mu\nu}(\mathbf R)=\int d^3r\,
\chi_\mu^*(\mathbf r)\chi_\nu(\mathbf r-\mathbf R),
$$

$$
T_{\mu\nu}(\mathbf R)=\int d^3r\,
\chi_\mu^*(\mathbf r)\left(-\frac12\nabla^2\right)\chi_\nu(\mathbf r-\mathbf R).
$$

    <p>radial tables 可预先计算并插值，angular dependence 由 Chapter 14/Appendix N 的 rotation relations 处理。interpolation spacing、spline smoothness、table cutoff 和 derivatives 都必须作为 numerical parameters 验证，尤其是在 forces/stress 中。</p>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>For fixed species and orbitals, overlap and kinetic terms can be tabulated as functions of inter-centre distance and bond orientation:</p>

$$
S_{\mu\nu}(\mathbf R)=\int d^3r\,
\chi_\mu^*(\mathbf r)\chi_\nu(\mathbf r-\mathbf R),
$$

$$
T_{\mu\nu}(\mathbf R)=\int d^3r\,
\chi_\mu^*(\mathbf r)\left(-\frac12\nabla^2\right)\chi_\nu(\mathbf r-\mathbf R).
$$

    <p>Radial tables can be precomputed and interpolated, with angular dependence handled by the rotation relations of Chapter 14 and Appendix N. Interpolation spacing, spline smoothness, table cutoff, and derivatives are numerical parameters that must be validated, especially for forces and stress.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="nonlocal projectors 与 local-potential grids" titleEn="Nonlocal projectors and local-potential grids">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>separable nonlocal operator</p>

$$
\hat V_{\mathrm{NL}}=\sum_a\sum_{ij}|\beta_i^a\rangle D_{ij}^a\langle\beta_j^a|
$$

    <p>给出 factorized matrix element</p>

$$
(V_{\mathrm{NL}})_{\mu\nu}
=\sum_{aij}\langle\chi_\mu|\beta_i^a\rangle D_{ij}^a
\langle\beta_j^a|\chi_\nu\rangle,
$$

    <p>把 apparent three-centre integral 化为 projector-overlap products。local effective potential 则通常在 grid 上处理：</p>

$$
(V_{\mathrm{loc}})_{\mu\nu}
=\int d^3r\,\chi_\mu^*(\mathbf r)V_{\mathrm{loc}}[n](\mathbf r)\chi_\nu(\mathbf r).
$$

    <p>basis cutoff 与 real-space grid spacing 是不同参数。matrix sparsity 不允许把 grid aliasing、Poisson boundary 或 XC quadrature error 归入 basis error。</p>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>A separable nonlocal operator</p>

$$
\hat V_{\mathrm{NL}}=\sum_a\sum_{ij}|\beta_i^a\rangle D_{ij}^a\langle\beta_j^a|
$$

    <p>gives the factorized matrix element</p>

$$
(V_{\mathrm{NL}})_{\mu\nu}
=\sum_{aij}\langle\chi_\mu|\beta_i^a\rangle D_{ij}^a
\langle\beta_j^a|\chi_\nu\rangle,
$$

    <p>turning an apparent three-centre integral into products of projector overlaps. The local effective potential is commonly treated on a grid:</p>

$$
(V_{\mathrm{loc}})_{\mu\nu}
=\int d^3r\,\chi_\mu^*(\mathbf r)V_{\mathrm{loc}}[n](\mathbf r)\chi_\nu(\mathbf r).
$$

    <p>Basis cutoff and real-space grid spacing are distinct parameters. Matrix sparsity does not permit grid aliasing, Poisson-boundary error, or XC quadrature error to be relabelled as basis error.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="all-electron partitioned grids" titleEn="Partitioned grids for all-electron calculations">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>near-nuclear variation 可用 atom-centred radial/angular grids，interstitial 用较平滑 grid。partition-of-unity weights $w_A(\mathbf r)$ 满足</p>

$$
\sum_A w_A(\mathbf r)=1,
\qquad
\int d^3r\,f(\mathbf r)=\sum_A\int d^3r\,w_A(\mathbf r)f(\mathbf r).
$$

    <p>weights、radial mapping、angular quadrature、overlap domains 和 pruning 都影响 integral accuracy。partition identity 只保证形式上的全空间覆盖，不保证有限 quadrature 已收敛。</p>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>Rapid near-nuclear variation can be handled on atom-centred radial and angular grids, with a smoother interstitial grid. Partition-of-unity weights $w_A(\mathbf r)$ satisfy</p>

$$
\sum_A w_A(\mathbf r)=1,
\qquad
\int d^3r\,f(\mathbf r)=\sum_A\int d^3r\,w_A(\mathbf r)f(\mathbf r).
$$

    <p>Weights, radial mapping, angular quadrature, overlap domains, and pruning all affect integral accuracy. The partition identity guarantees formal coverage of space, not convergence of a finite quadrature.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="basis 与 grid 的二维收敛" titleEn="Two-dimensional basis/grid convergence" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">固定粗 grid 增大 basis，或固定小 basis 加密 grid，都可能得到虚假的 plateau。至少需要交叉测试：在两个以上 basis levels 上收敛 grid，并在两个以上 sufficiently fine grids 上收敛 basis。forces/stress 还需检查 table/grid derivatives。</div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">Increasing the basis on a fixed coarse grid, or refining the grid with a fixed small basis, can produce a false plateau. At minimum, converge the grid at more than one basis level and the basis on more than one sufficiently fine grid. Forces and stress also require tests of table and grid derivatives.</div>
</BilingualCallout>
