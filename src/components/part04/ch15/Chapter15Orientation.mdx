## 阅读定位 | Reading Orientation

<BilingualSection titleZh="本章解决的问题" titleEn="What this chapter solves" eyebrow="Chapter 15">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>Chapter 14 把局域轨道写成有限 Hamiltonian/overlap 模型；Chapter 15 要求这些矩阵元来自明确的 basis functions、Kohn–Sham potential 和 density，并在自洽循环中更新。计算对象不再只是参数化 bands，而是</p>
    <p><strong>basis → H/S → generalized eigenvectors → density matrix → real-space density/potential → H/S</strong>。</p>
    <p>局域性使矩阵构造、真空体系和原子附近描述更经济；代价是 basis 不再具有平面波 cutoff 那样单一且系统的完备序列。basis 选择、confinement、diffuse/polarization functions、linear dependence 和 derivative consistency 都成为独立误差源。</p>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>Chapter 14 represented localized orbitals by finite Hamiltonian and overlap models. Chapter 15 requires those matrix elements to come from declared basis functions, a Kohn–Sham potential, and a density, and to be updated self-consistently. The computational object is no longer only a parameterized band model, but</p>
    <p><strong>basis → H/S → generalized eigenvectors → density matrix → real-space density/potential → H/S</strong>.</p>
    <p>Locality can make matrix construction, vacuum-containing systems, and near-atom resolution efficient. The price is that basis convergence is no longer governed by one plane-wave-like cutoff. Basis selection, confinement, diffuse and polarization functions, linear dependence, and derivative consistency become separate error sources.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>
