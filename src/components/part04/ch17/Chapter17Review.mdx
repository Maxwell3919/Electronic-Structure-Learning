## 方法比较、收敛协议与计算连接 | Method comparison, convergence, and computational connections

<BilingualSection titleZh="linear augmented methods comparison" titleEn="Comparison of linear augmented methods">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

| 方法 | fixed basis construction | 主要优势 | 独立误差 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LAPW | interstitial plane waves + sphere `u_l, u-dot_l` value/slope matching | systematic interstitial convergence、full potential、all-electron resolution | G cutoff、angular cutoffs、linearization/local orbitals、core/relativity |
| LMTO | minimal augmented heads/tails + linearized potential functions | compact physical representation、interpretation、screened short range | ASA/interstitial、minimal-basis completeness、screening/downfolding、linearization |
| NMTO | multi-energy interpolation and downfolded target channels | wide target window、isolated target bands、compact effective Hamiltonians | mesh placement/order、active-subspace choice、gauge/nonuniqueness、force implementation |
| full-potential augmented | sphere angular + interstitial smooth representations | removes potential-shape approximation | radial/angular/interstitial truncation and all method-specific errors remain |

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

| Method | Fixed-basis construction | Main strengths | Independent errors |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LAPW | interstitial plane waves plus sphere `u_l, u-dot_l` value and slope matching | systematic interstitial convergence, full potential, all-electron resolution | G cutoff, angular cutoffs, linearization or local orbitals, core and relativity |
| LMTO | minimal augmented heads and tails plus linearized potential functions | compact physical representation, interpretation, screened short range | ASA or interstitial treatment, minimal-basis completeness, screening, downfolding, linearization |
| NMTO | multi-energy interpolation and downfolded target channels | wide target windows, isolated target bands, compact effective Hamiltonians | mesh placement and order, active-subspace choice, gauge nonuniqueness, force implementation |
| Full-potential augmented | sphere angular plus interstitial smooth representations | removal of the potential-shape approximation | radial, angular, and interstitial truncations and all method-specific errors remain |

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="最低收敛协议" titleEn="Minimum convergence protocol">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh"><ol>
    <li>固定 structure、functional、spin、relativity、core/semicore 与 occupation conventions。</li>
    <li>对每个 l channel 扫描 linearization energies、local orbitals 或 NMTO mesh。</li>
    <li>分别收敛 radial mesh、sphere radii、wavefunction/density/potential angular cutoffs。</li>
    <li>LAPW 收敛 interstitial cutoff；LMTO 收敛 envelopes、screening、basis channels 与 ASA/full-potential choice；NMTO 收敛 energy mesh/order 与 downfolding。</li>
    <li>收敛 k mesh、smearing、SCF residual、energy differences 与 target observable。</li>
    <li>forces/stress/phonons 用 finite differences、symmetry、sum rules 与 alternative sphere partitions 验证。</li>
    <li>在第二个合理 representation 或 independent implementation 上复核高影响结论。</li>
  </ol></div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en"><ol>
    <li>Fix structure, functional, spin, relativity, core or semicore, and occupation conventions.</li>
    <li>Scan linearization energies, local orbitals, or NMTO meshes for every l channel.</li>
    <li>Converge radial mesh, sphere radii, and wavefunction, density, and potential angular cutoffs separately.</li>
    <li>Converge the LAPW interstitial cutoff; LMTO envelopes, screening, basis channels, and ASA or full-potential choice; and NMTO energy mesh, order, and downfolding.</li>
    <li>Converge k sampling, smearing, SCF residual, energy differences, and the target observable.</li>
    <li>Validate forces, stress, and phonons with finite differences, symmetry, sum rules, and alternative sphere partitions.</li>
    <li>Cross-check high-impact conclusions with a second reasonable representation or independent implementation.</li>
  </ol></div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="常见误判" titleEn="Recurrent misjudgments" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh"><ol>
    <li>把 numerical linearization 当成 Schrödinger equation 从 nonlinear 变 linear。</li>
    <li>把 dot 误读成 time derivative。</li>
    <li>把 reference energy 当作固定 physical orbital energy。</li>
    <li>认为 $u_l$ 与 $\dot u_l$ 会自动使 basis dimension 翻倍。</li>
    <li>只增加 plane waves，不检查 radial matching conditioning 或 linearization window。</li>
    <li>用一个 $E_l$ 同时覆盖 distant semicore 与 conduction states，却不加 local orbitals。</li>
    <li>把 all-electron/full-potential 标签当作无 basis、functional 或 relativistic error。</li>
    <li>把 minimal LMTO basis 的可解释性当作 completeness。</li>
    <li>把 screened/tight-binding LMTO parameter 当作 unique transferable hopping。</li>
    <li>把 NMTO/downfolded orbital 当作唯一真实 orbital。</li>
    <li>因 mesh energies 上 exact 就忽略 mesh 之间与 window 外 error。</li>
    <li>只比较 bands，不检查 density、energy、force 与 sphere/representation invariance。</li>
  </ol></div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en"><ol>
    <li>Confusing numerical linearization with changing a nonlinear Schrödinger equation into a linear one.</li>
    <li>Reading the dot as a time derivative.</li>
    <li>Treating a reference energy as a fixed physical orbital energy.</li>
    <li>Assuming $u_l$ and $\dot u_l$ automatically double the basis dimension.</li>
    <li>Adding plane waves without checking radial-matching conditioning or the linearization window.</li>
    <li>Using one $E_l$ for distant semicore and conduction states without local orbitals.</li>
    <li>Treating all-electron or full-potential labels as absence of basis, functional, or relativistic error.</li>
    <li>Equating interpretability of a minimal LMTO basis with completeness.</li>
    <li>Treating a screened tight-binding LMTO parameter as a unique transferable hopping.</li>
    <li>Treating an NMTO or downfolded orbital as the unique real orbital.</li>
    <li>Ignoring between-node and outside-window error because the mesh energies are exact.</li>
    <li>Comparing bands without density, energy, force, and sphere or representation invariance.</li>
  </ol></div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 原创练习与来源边界 | Original exercises and source boundary

<BilingualSection titleZh="十道原创练习" titleEn="Ten original exercises">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh"><ol>
    <li><strong>derivative equation：</strong>从 $(H_l-E)u_l(E)=0$ 推导 u-dot equation，并说明 normalization gauge。</li>
    <li><strong>orthogonality：</strong>对 fixed sphere norm 推导 inner product(u,u-dot)=0，并比较 fixed boundary-value convention。</li>
    <li><strong>error scaling：</strong>对 analytic radial family 验证 first-order state error 随 ΔE² 缩放。</li>
    <li><strong>LAPW matching：</strong>解 value/slope 2×2 system，扫描 determinant 与 reference energy。</li>
    <li><strong>semicore：</strong>构造 valence/semicore 两个 target energies，比较 one-reference basis 与 local-orbital enrichment。</li>
    <li><strong>angular cutoffs：</strong>用 spherical-harmonic product rules 估计 wavefunction、density 与 potential cutoffs。</li>
    <li><strong>LMTO range：</strong>扫描 screening 与 neighbour cutoff，比较 band、energy 与 force surrogate errors。</li>
    <li><strong>ASA sensitivity：</strong>改变 sphere radii/empty-sphere filling，区分 representation changes 与 physical trends。</li>
    <li><strong>NMTO interpolation：</strong>用三个 energy nodes 重建 cubic target，验证 product error 与 outside-window growth。</li>
    <li><strong>full potential：</strong>列出 sphere/interstitial density、potential、Poisson 与 matrix-element cutoffs，并设计独立 convergence matrix。</li>
  </ol></div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en"><ol>
    <li><strong>Derivative equation:</strong> derive the u-dot equation and identify the normalization gauge.</li>
    <li><strong>Orthogonality:</strong> derive the zero inner product for fixed sphere norm and compare a fixed-boundary-value convention.</li>
    <li><strong>Error scaling:</strong> verify ΔE² state-error scaling for an analytic radial family.</li>
    <li><strong>LAPW matching:</strong> solve the value and slope system and scan determinant conditioning versus reference energy.</li>
    <li><strong>Semicore:</strong> compare one reference with local-orbital enrichment for separated valence and semicore targets.</li>
    <li><strong>Angular cutoffs:</strong> estimate wavefunction, density, and potential cutoffs from spherical-harmonic products.</li>
    <li><strong>LMTO range:</strong> scan screening and neighbour cutoff and compare band, energy, and force-surrogate errors.</li>
    <li><strong>ASA sensitivity:</strong> vary sphere radii and empty-sphere filling and distinguish representation changes from physical trends.</li>
    <li><strong>NMTO interpolation:</strong> reconstruct a cubic target from three energy nodes and verify product error and outside-window growth.</li>
    <li><strong>Full potential:</strong> list sphere and interstitial density, potential, Poisson, and matrix-element cutoffs and design a convergence matrix.</li>
  </ol></div>
</BilingualSection>
