## 17.6 Linear Muffin-Tin Orbital (LMTO) Method | 线性 muffin-tin orbital 方法

<BilingualCallout titleZh="来源定位" titleEn="Source locator" kind="source">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">【Martin 原书 | Martin source】印刷页 375–379 从 MTO tail cancellation 进入 energy-independent LMTO，强调 minimal basis、ASA 与 physical interpretation。以下按 head、tail、potential function、structure matrix 与 approximation gates 重组。</div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">【Martin source】Printed pp. 375–379 move from MTO tail cancellation to energy-independent LMTOs and emphasize minimal bases, ASA, and physical interpretation. The material below is reorganized around heads, tails, potential functions, structure matrices, and approximation gates.</div>
</BilingualCallout>

<BilingualSection titleZh="LMTO orbital 的 head 与 tails" titleEn="Heads and tails of an LMTO">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>LMTO 以一个 site/channel 为中心。central sphere 内的 head 由 reference partial wave 与 energy derivative 组成；interstitial envelope 延伸到 neighbouring spheres，并在那里被 augmented 成 tails。抽象写为</p>

$$
|\chi_{RL}\rangle
=|\phi_{RL}\rangle
+\sum_{R'L'} h_{R'L',RL}|\dot\phi_{R'L'}\rangle.
$$

    <p>coefficients $h$ 由 envelope、structure constants、screening 与 boundary conditions 决定。它们不是 empirical hopping parameters；但在 screened representation 中会呈现 on-site/coupling-like matrix structure。</p>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>An LMTO is centred on one site and angular channel. Its head inside the central sphere combines a reference partial wave and an energy derivative; the interstitial envelope reaches neighbouring spheres and is augmented there as tails. Schematically,</p>

$$
|\chi_{RL}\rangle
=|\phi_{RL}\rangle
+\sum_{R'L'} h_{R'L',RL}|\dot\phi_{R'L'}\rangle.
$$

    <p>The coefficients $h$ are determined by envelopes, structure constants, screening, and boundary conditions. They are not empirical hopping parameters, although a screened representation can display on-site and coupling-like matrix structure.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock title="energy-independent LMTO from a linearized MTO | 从线性化 MTO 到 energy-independent orbital" assumptions={['one selected reference energy per retained channel；','the envelope and augmentation conventions are fixed；','terms beyond first order in energy are assigned to the linearization error。']}>

Start from an energy-dependent MTO,

$$
|\chi(E)\rangle=|\phi(E)\rangle+|J(E)\rangle,
$$

and expand near $E_\nu$:

$$
|\chi(E)\rangle
\approx |\phi_\nu\rangle+|J_\nu\rangle
+(E-E_\nu)(|\dot\phi_\nu\rangle+|\dot J_\nu\rangle).
$$

A linear transformation of head and tail pieces can choose a fixed orbital whose first-order energy variation is represented by the Hamiltonian coefficients rather than by changing the basis itself. The resulting matrices are energy independent, while omitted quadratic terms define a finite energy window.

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="potential functions 与 structure matrices" titleEn="Potential functions and structure matrices">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>LMTO secular equation 常分离为 site/radial object 与 geometry object：</p>

$$
\det[P(E)-S(\mathbf k)]=0.
$$

    <p>$P(E)$ 由 radial potential 与 boundary log derivative 决定；$S(\mathbf k)$ 由 lattice geometry 与 envelope convention 决定。linearization 把 $P(E)$ 在 $E_\nu$ 附近展开，并将 problem 映射为 fixed Hamiltonian/overlap matrices。</p>
    <p>“potential parameter”是 representation-dependent radial summary，不是 isolated atomic observable。sphere radii、screening、normalization 与 downfolding 改变其数值。</p>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>An LMTO secular equation is often separated into a site or radial object and a geometry object:</p>

$$
\det[P(E)-S(\mathbf k)]=0.
$$

    <p>$P(E)$ is determined by the radial potential and boundary logarithmic derivative, while $S(\mathbf k)$ is determined by lattice geometry and the envelope convention. Linearization expands $P(E)$ near $E_\nu$ and maps the problem to fixed Hamiltonian and overlap matrices.</p>
    <p>A “potential parameter” is a representation-dependent radial summary, not an isolated atomic observable. Sphere radii, screening, normalization, and downfolding change its value.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="ASA 与 minimal basis" titleEn="ASA and a minimal basis">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh"><p>atomic-sphere approximation 用 overlapping space-filling spheres 简化 interstitial 与 electrostatics，使 minimal spd-like basis 可以高效描述 close-packed systems。它同时引入独立近似：</p><ul><li>spherical potential/density inside spheres；</li><li>space filling 与 controlled overlap；</li><li>combined corrections/empty spheres；</li><li>limited interstitial description。</li></ul><p>minimal basis 的 interpretability 不等于 completeness。open structures、surfaces、molecules、strongly directional bonds 与 high-lying states 常需要 empty spheres、multiple envelopes 或 full-potential extensions。</p></div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en"><p>The atomic-sphere approximation uses overlapping space-filling spheres to simplify the interstitial and electrostatics, allowing a minimal spd-like basis to describe many close-packed systems efficiently. It also introduces independent approximations:</p><ul><li>spherical potentials and densities inside spheres;</li><li>space filling and controlled overlap;</li><li>combined corrections or empty spheres;</li><li>a limited interstitial description.</li></ul><p>Interpretability of a minimal basis is not completeness. Open structures, surfaces, molecules, strongly directional bonds, and high-lying states often require empty spheres, multiple envelopes, or full-potential extensions.</p></div>
</BilingualSection>

## 17.7 Tight-Binding Formulation | Tight-binding 表述

<BilingualSection titleZh="screening 先于 truncation" titleEn="Screening comes before truncation">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>bare structure constants 具有长程 tails。screening transformation 重新选择 envelope/boundary representation，使 matrix elements 更快衰减：</p>

$$
S^{\alpha}=S^0(I-\alpha S^0)^{-1}.
$$

    <p>potential functions 必须按同一 transformation 改写，未截断 secular roots 才保持不变。之后才可以做 neighbour-shell truncation；该步骤产生 controllable approximation，必须用 bands、charge、energy 与 forces 验证。</p>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>Bare structure constants have long-ranged tails. A screening transformation chooses a different envelope or boundary representation so matrix elements decay faster:</p>

$$
S^{\alpha}=S^0(I-\alpha S^0)^{-1}.
$$

    <p>Potential functions must be transformed consistently so the untruncated secular roots remain unchanged. Neighbour-shell truncation is a later, controllable approximation that must be tested on bands, charge, energy, and forces.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="first-principles tight-binding 的含义" titleEn="Meaning of first-principles tight binding">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh"><p>screened LMTO Hamiltonian 可写成 compact on-site/coupling blocks，适合 orbital-resolved interpretation、downfolding 与 large-system algebra。与 empirical tight binding 的主要区别是：</p><ul><li>matrix elements 由 self-consistent radial potential 与 structure matrices 导出；</li><li>overlap、screening、energy window 与 environment dependence 显式存在；</li><li>改变 representation 会改变 individual parameters，但 converged spectrum/observables 应保持；</li><li>短程 truncation 与 minimal basis 都必须验证。</li></ul></div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en"><p>A screened LMTO Hamiltonian can be written in compact on-site and coupling blocks, which is useful for orbital interpretation, downfolding, and large-system algebra. Its distinction from empirical tight binding includes:</p><ul><li>matrix elements derived from a self-consistent radial potential and structure matrices;</li><li>explicit overlap, screening, energy-window, and environment dependence;</li><li>representation-dependent individual parameters but invariant converged spectra and observables;</li><li>required validation of short-range truncation and the minimal basis.</li></ul></div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="parameter interpretation ceiling" titleEn="Parameter-interpretation ceiling" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">一个 diagonal LMTO matrix element 不能不加 convention 地称为唯一 atomic orbital energy；一个 off-diagonal element 也不能脱离 screening/downfolding 被称为 transferable hopping。可比较对象是完整 declared representation 中的 spectrum、density matrix、energy difference 或 observable。</div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">A diagonal LMTO matrix element is not a unique atomic-orbital energy without its convention, and an off-diagonal element is not a transferable hopping outside its screening and downfolding definition. Comparable objects are spectra, density matrices, energy differences, or observables in the complete declared representation.</div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 17.8 Applications of the LMTO Method | LMTO 方法的应用边界

<BilingualSection titleZh="应用按 evidence object 分类" titleEn="Applications classified by evidence object">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh"><ul><li><strong>ground-state trends：</strong>equation of state、pressure trends、magnetic competition 与 structural energy differences；</li><li><strong>orbital interpretation：</strong>canonical-band baseline、hybridization、relativity、crystal-field 与 downfolded bands；</li><li><strong>disorder：</strong>与 KKR/CPA 结合的 average spectral quantities；</li><li><strong>large systems：</strong>screened short-range representations 与 sparse algebra；</li><li><strong>forces/dynamics：</strong>只有在 full potential、basis derivatives、empty-sphere handling 与 finite-difference evidence 完整时。</li></ul></div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en"><ul><li><strong>Ground-state trends:</strong> equations of state, pressure trends, magnetic competition, and structural energy differences;</li><li><strong>Orbital interpretation:</strong> canonical-band baselines, hybridization, relativity, crystal fields, and downfolded bands;</li><li><strong>Disorder:</strong> average spectral quantities through KKR or CPA combinations;</li><li><strong>Large systems:</strong> screened short-range representations and sparse algebra;</li><li><strong>Forces and dynamics:</strong> only with complete full-potential, basis-derivative, empty-sphere, and finite-difference evidence.</li></ul></div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="empty spheres 的特殊风险" titleEn="Special risks of empty spheres">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh"><p>在 ASA/open structures 中，empty spheres 用于填充 interstitial volume 并承载 basis/potential components。它们不是 atoms，却会影响 structure matrices、charge partition 与 energy。若 atoms 移动，empty-sphere positions/radii 的处理会引入非唯一 choices，尤其影响 forces 与 molecular dynamics。</p><p>因此 empty-sphere calculation 应报告 placement algorithm、radii、charge、basis channels、structure/force 对 alternative fillings 的 sensitivity，并避免把 empty-sphere charge 解释为 physical ion charge。</p></div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en"><p>In ASA or open structures, empty spheres fill interstitial volume and carry basis or potential components. They are not atoms, yet they affect structure matrices, charge partitioning, and energy. When atoms move, choices for empty-sphere positions and radii become nonunique, especially for forces and molecular dynamics.</p><p>An empty-sphere calculation should report placement, radii, charge, basis channels, and sensitivity of structures and forces to alternative fillings. Empty-sphere charge is not a physical ionic charge.</p></div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="efficiency claim" titleEn="Efficiency claim" kind="practice">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">比较 LMTO 与 LAPW/plane-wave/local-orbital methods 时，应在相同 structure、functional、relativity 与 target tolerance 下报告 setup、SCF iterations、matrix construction、diagonalization、memory、force quality 和 basis-convergence cost。matrix dimension alone 不能支持“更快”。</div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">Comparing LMTO with LAPW, plane-wave, or local-orbital methods requires the same structure, functional, relativity, and target tolerance, plus setup, SCF iterations, matrix construction, diagonalization, memory, force quality, and basis-convergence cost. Matrix dimension alone does not establish speed.</div>
</BilingualCallout>
