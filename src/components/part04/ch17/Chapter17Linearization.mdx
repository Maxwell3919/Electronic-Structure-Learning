## 17.1 Linearization of Equations and Linear Methods | 方程线性化与线性方法

<BilingualCallout titleZh="来源定位" titleEn="Source locator" kind="source">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">【Martin 原书 | Martin source】印刷页 365–366 区分 Schrödinger equation 的基本线性与 augmented basis 的 numerical nonlinearity，并以 $u_l$、$\dot u_l$ 说明 reference-energy linearization。下面展开误差、basis 与 solver 层级。</div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">【Martin source】Printed pp. 365–366 distinguish the fundamental linearity of the Schrödinger equation from numerical nonlinearity caused by an energy-dependent augmented basis, and introduce reference-energy linearization through $u_l$ and $\dot u_l$. The error, basis, and solver layers are developed below.</div>
</BilingualCallout>

<BilingualSection titleZh="三种不同的 linearity" titleEn="Three distinct meanings of linearity">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <ol>
      <li><strong>differential equation：</strong>给定 effective potential 时，$\hat H\psi=E\psi$ 对 wavefunction 是线性的。</li>
      <li><strong>basis expansion：</strong>对固定 basis，$\psi_n=\sum_i c_{in}\chi_i$ 导致 ordinary/generalized matrix eigenproblem。</li>
      <li><strong>secular dependence：</strong>若 $\chi_i(E)$ 随 trial energy 改变，则 matrix $K(E)$ 的 determinant root problem 对 E 是 nonlinear。</li>
    </ol>
    <p>linearized augmented method 保留第一层线性，改变第三层：把 energy-dependent radial functions 在固定 $E_l$ 附近展开，使 basis 对未知 eigenenergy 固定。它不是把 exchange-correlation functional、SCF map 或 many-body problem “线性化”。</p>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Differential equation:</strong> for a fixed effective potential, $\hat H\psi=E\psi$ is linear in the wavefunction.</li>
      <li><strong>Basis expansion:</strong> a fixed basis, $\psi_n=\sum_i c_{in}\chi_i$, produces an ordinary or generalized matrix eigenproblem.</li>
      <li><strong>Secular dependence:</strong> if $\chi_i(E)$ changes with trial energy, the determinant equation for $K(E)$ is nonlinear in E.</li>
    </ol>
    <p>A linearized augmented method preserves the first meaning and changes the third: energy-dependent radial functions are expanded near fixed $E_l$, making the basis independent of the unknown eigenenergy. It does not linearize the exchange-correlation functional, the SCF map, or the many-body problem.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock title="first-order radial linearization | 一阶 radial linearization" assumptions={['u_l(E,r) is differentiable in energy in a node-free local window；','the spherical reference potential is fixed during the derivative；','one normalization convention is used at all nearby energies。']}>

For $E=E_l+\Delta E$,

$$
u_l(E,r)=u_l(E_l,r)+\Delta E\,\dot u_l(E_l,r)
+\frac{(\Delta E)^2}{2}\ddot u_l(\xi,r),
$$

for some $\xi$ between $E_l$ and E. The first-order basis therefore has state error

$$
\delta u_l=\mathcal O((\Delta E)^2).
$$

This statement is local in energy. It does not guarantee accuracy for a distant semicore state, a high conduction band, or a channel crossing a radial node. The appropriate evidence is an energy-window scan or basis enrichment, not the smallness of one coefficient.

</DerivationBlock>

This does not mean every observable becomes third-order accurate. Forces, stress, response, and unoccupied-state quantities require their own derivative and basis tests.</p>

## 17.2 Energy Derivative of the Wavefunction: ψ and ψ-dot | 波函数的能量导数

<BilingualSection titleZh="radial equation 与 energy derivative" titleEn="Radial equation and its energy derivative">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>对固定 spherical potential 和 angular channel l，把 radial operator 记作 $\hat H_l$。reference function 满足</p>

$$
(\hat H_l-E_l)u_l(E_l,r)=0.
$$

    <p>定义</p>

$$
\dot u_l(E_l,r)=\left.\frac{\partial u_l(E,r)}{\partial E}\right|_{E_l}.
$$

    <p>dot 是 energy derivative，单位为 wavefunction per energy。它不是 time derivative，也不是额外 eigenstate。</p>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>For a fixed spherical potential and angular channel l, write the radial operator as $\hat H_l$. The reference function satisfies</p>

$$
(\hat H_l-E_l)u_l(E_l,r)=0.
$$

    <p>Define</p>

$$
\dot u_l(E_l,r)=\left.\frac{\partial u_l(E,r)}{\partial E}\right|_{E_l}.
$$

    <p>The dot denotes an energy derivative, with units of wavefunction per energy. It is neither a time derivative nor an additional eigenstate.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock title="inhomogeneous equation for u-dot | u-dot 的非齐次方程" assumptions={['H_l is held fixed in the partial energy derivative；','u_l(E,r) is differentiable in the selected window。']}>

Differentiate

$$
(\hat H_l-E)u_l(E)=0
$$

with respect to E:

$$
-(u_l)+(\hat H_l-E)\dot u_l=0.
$$

At $E=E_l$,

$$
(\hat H_l-E_l)\dot u_l=u_l.
$$

Thus $\dot u_l$ is obtained from an inhomogeneous radial equation with source $u_l$. A solver must also impose a normalization or boundary convention because adding a homogeneous multiple of $u_l$ changes $\dot u_l$ without changing the inhomogeneous equation.

</DerivationBlock>

<DerivationBlock title="orthogonality from fixed normalization | 固定 normalization 导出的正交性" assumptions={['the sphere norm is fixed to one for nearby energies；','real radial functions for notational simplicity。']}>

If

$$
\langle u_l(E)|u_l(E)\rangle_{S}=1,
$$

then differentiation gives

$$
\langle\dot u_l|u_l\rangle_S+
\langle u_l|\dot u_l\rangle_S=0.
$$

For real functions,

$$
\langle u_l|\dot u_l\rangle_S=0.
$$

Changing to a fixed-boundary-value convention generally changes this overlap. Therefore an implementation or paper must state its convention before comparing $\dot u_l$, matrix elements, or logarithmic-derivative formulas.

</DerivationBlock>

  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>在 sphere radius S 处定义</p>

$$
D_l(E)=S\frac{u_l'(E,S)}{u_l(E,S)}.

    <p>$D_l$ 把 radial solution 压缩为 boundary value/slope ratio，是 APW matching、KKR phase shift 与 MTO potential-function construction 的共同接口。接近 $u_l(E,S)=0$ 时，$D_l$ 可出现 pole；这通常是 representation singularity，需要换 reference energy、local orbital 或等价 formulation，而不能直接解释为 divergent physical state。</p>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>At sphere radius S define</p>

$$
D_l(E)=S\frac{u_l'(E,S)}{u_l(E,S)}.
$$

    <p>$D_l$ compresses a radial solution into a boundary value-to-slope ratio and provides a common interface for APW matching, KKR phase shifts, and MTO potential functions. A pole near $u_l(E,S)=0$ is normally a representation singularity that calls for a different reference energy, a local orbital, or an equivalent formulation—not a divergent physical state.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## 17.3 General Form of Linearized Equations | 线性化方程的一般形式

<BilingualSection titleZh="从 energy-dependent basis 到 fixed basis" titleEn="From an energy-dependent basis to a fixed basis">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>设 nonlinear augmented function 为 $\chi_i(E)$。在 reference energies 附近，</p>

$$
\chi_i(E)\approx\phi_i+(E-E_i)\dot\phi_i.
$$

    <p>通过 boundary conditions 把 $\phi_i$ 与 $\dot\phi_i$ 的 coefficients 绑定后，每个 envelope 仍对应一个 basis function；basis dimension 不因写出两个 radial functions 自动加倍。所有 states 使用同一 fixed set $\{\chi_i\}$，于是</p>

$$
\sum_j H_{ij}c_{jn}=E_n\sum_j S_{ij}c_{jn}.
$$

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>Let the nonlinear augmented function be $\chi_i(E)$. Near selected reference energies,</p>

$$
\chi_i(E)\approx\phi_i+(E-E_i)\dot\phi_i.
$$

    <p>Boundary conditions tie the coefficients of $\phi_i$ and $\dot\phi_i$, so each envelope still defines one basis function; writing two radial functions does not automatically double the basis dimension. All states use the same fixed set $\{\chi_i\}$, producing</p>

$$
\sum_j H_{ij}c_{jn}=E_n\sum_j S_{ij}c_{jn}.
$$

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock title="matrix organization with u and u-dot | 用 u 与 u-dot 组织矩阵" assumptions={['fixed reference functions and derivatives；','Hermitian H and a positive-definite overlap after removing linear dependencies。']}>

Write a fixed basis schematically as

$$
|\chi_i\rangle=|\phi_i\rangle+
\sum_L h_{Li}|\dot\phi_L\rangle.
$$

Then

$$
S_{ij}=\langle\chi_i|\chi_j\rangle,
\qquad
H_{ij}=\langle\chi_i|\hat H|\chi_j\rangle.
$$

Using $(\hat H-E_L)|\dot\phi_L\rangle=|\phi_L\rangle$ reorganizes matrix elements into fixed radial integrals and boundary/envelope coefficients. The apparent energy dependence is absorbed into an overlap-metric problem rather than retained as a nonlinear determinant in the unknown E.

This algebra does not ensure $S>0$. Redundant local orbitals, nearby reference energies, or an overcomplete envelope set can create small overlap eigenvalues and must be diagnosed separately.

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="linear solve 不等于 one-shot calculation" titleEn="A linear solve is not a one-shot calculation">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh"><p>在一次 fixed-potential step 中，多个 eigenpairs 可由 linear algebra 一起获得。但 Kohn–Sham potential 仍依赖 density，radial functions、reference energies、core states 与 full-potential components 也可能在 SCF iteration 之间更新。因此完整 calculation 仍是 nonlinear fixed-point problem：</p>

$$
n^{(m)}\to V_{\mathrm{eff}}^{(m)}\to
\{u_l,\dot u_l,H,S\}^{(m)}\to n^{(m+1)}.
$$

<p>“linear method”只描述 inner eigenproblem/basis construction，不表示 total calculation 无 SCF nonlinearity。</p></div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en"><p>For one fixed-potential step, many eigenpairs can be obtained together by linear algebra. The Kohn–Sham potential still depends on the density, and radial functions, reference energies, core states, and full-potential components may be updated between SCF iterations. The complete calculation remains a nonlinear fixed-point problem:</p>

$$
n^{(m)}\to V_{\mathrm{eff}}^{(m)}\to
\{u_l,\dot u_l,H,S\}^{(m)}\to n^{(m+1)}.
$$

<p>“Linear method” describes the inner eigenproblem and basis construction, not the absence of SCF nonlinearity.</p></div>
</BilingualSection>
