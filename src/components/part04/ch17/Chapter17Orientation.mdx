## 阅读定位 | Reading orientation

<BilingualSection titleZh="本章线性化的对象" titleEn="What is being linearized">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>本章的“linear”不涉及改变量子力学的线性 Schrödinger equation。Chapter 16 的数值非线性来自 basis functions 自身依赖未知 eigenenergy：每个 trial energy 都改变 radial partial waves、phase shifts 与 boundary matching，因而需要逐根搜索 secular determinant。</p>
    <p>Chapter 17 选择 reference energies $E_l$，用 radial solution 与其能量导数建立固定基：</p>

$$
 u_l(E,r)
 \approx u_l(E_l,r)+(E-E_l)\dot u_l(E_l,r).
$$

    <p>这样，一个 energy window 内的多个 states 可以进入普通 generalized eigenproblem。代价是新增 linearization error；reference energies、semicore/local orbitals 与 basis enrichment 成为独立收敛维度。</p>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>“Linear” here does not alter the fundamental linearity of the Schrödinger equation. The numerical nonlinearity of Chapter 16 arose because the basis itself depended on the unknown eigenenergy: each trial energy changed radial partial waves, phase shifts, and boundary matching, requiring separate secular-root searches.</p>
    <p>Chapter 17 chooses reference energies $E_l$ and builds a fixed basis from a radial solution and its energy derivative:</p>

$$
 u_l(E,r)
 \approx u_l(E_l,r)+(E-E_l)\dot u_l(E_l,r).
$$

    <p>Many states inside an energy window can then be obtained from a conventional generalized eigenproblem. The price is a separate linearization error, making reference energies, semicore or local orbitals, and basis enrichment independent convergence dimensions.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="对象链" titleEn="Object chain">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh"><ol>
    <li><strong>continuous radial problem：</strong>在 spherical reference potential 中求 $u_l(E_l,r)$。</li>
    <li><strong>energy derivative：</strong>求 $\dot u_l=\partial_Eu_l|_{E_l}$，并声明 normalization convention。</li>
    <li><strong>augmentation：</strong>用 $u_l$ 与 $\dot u_l$ 匹配 interstitial envelopes 或构造 head/tail orbitals。</li>
    <li><strong>fixed matrices：</strong>形成 energy-independent $H$ 与 $S$。</li>
    <li><strong>linear solve：</strong>一次求多个 eigenpairs，而不是逐 eigenenergy root search。</li>
    <li><strong>self-consistency：</strong>由 occupied states 重建 density/potential，更新 radial functions 与 matrices。</li>
    <li><strong>evidence：</strong>分别收敛 linearization、radial/angular/interstitial basis、semicore、k mesh、SCF、forces 与 target observable。</li>
  </ol></div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en"><ol>
    <li><strong>Continuous radial problem:</strong> solve $u_l(E_l,r)$ in a spherical reference potential.</li>
    <li><strong>Energy derivative:</strong> obtain $\dot u_l=\partial_Eu_l|_{E_l}$ under a declared normalization convention.</li>
    <li><strong>Augmentation:</strong> use $u_l$ and $\dot u_l$ to match interstitial envelopes or construct head-and-tail orbitals.</li>
    <li><strong>Fixed matrices:</strong> form energy-independent $H$ and $S$.</li>
    <li><strong>Linear solve:</strong> obtain many eigenpairs together instead of searching each eigenenergy separately.</li>
    <li><strong>Self-consistency:</strong> rebuild density and potential from occupied states and update radial functions and matrices.</li>
    <li><strong>Evidence:</strong> converge linearization, radial/angular/interstitial bases, semicore treatment, k mesh, SCF, forces, and the target observable separately.</li>
  </ol></div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="符号与 convention" titleEn="Notation and conventions" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh"><ul><li>dot 表示 energy derivative，不是 time derivative；</li><li>$E_l$ 是 chosen linearization energy，不自动等于 eigenvalue 或 Fermi level；</li><li>$u_l$、$\dot u_l$ 的 normalization 会改变 matrix elements，但完整 converged observables 不应依赖任意 convention；</li><li>LAPW/LMTO/NMTO 是不同 basis constructions，不是 accuracy 等级标签；</li><li>full potential 删除 potential-shape approximation，不删除 linearization 与 finite-basis error。</li></ul></div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en"><ul><li>A dot denotes an energy derivative, not a time derivative.</li><li>$E_l$ is a chosen linearization energy, not automatically an eigenvalue or the Fermi level.</li><li>Normalization of $u_l$ and $\dot u_l$ changes matrix elements, while converged observables should not depend on an arbitrary convention.</li><li>LAPW, LMTO, and NMTO are different basis constructions, not labels of an accuracy hierarchy.</li><li>Full potential removes a potential-shape approximation, not linearization or finite-basis error.</li></ul></div>
</BilingualCallout>
