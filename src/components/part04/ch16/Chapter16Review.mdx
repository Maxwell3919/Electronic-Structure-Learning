<BilingualSection titleZh="最低收敛协议" titleEn="Minimum convergence protocol">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh"><ol><li>固定 structure、exchange-correlation、spin/relativistic、core 与 boundary conventions。</li><li>分别收敛 radial mesh、sphere radii、$l_{\max}$ 与 nonspherical angular expansion。</li><li>APW 收敛 interstitial cutoff/root mesh；KKR 收敛 structure-constant sums/energy contour；MTO 收敛 tail range、screening 与 downfolding。</li><li>收敛 k mesh、occupations/smearing、charge residual、total energy 与 target observable。</li><li>forces/stress 用 finite differences、symmetry 与 sum rules 验证。</li><li>改变 partition/representation，确认 physical observables 在 tolerance 内不变。</li></ol></div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en"><ol><li>Fix structure, exchange-correlation, spin or relativistic treatment, core treatment, and boundary conventions.</li><li>Converge radial mesh, sphere radii, $l_{\max}$, and nonspherical angular expansion separately.</li><li>Converge APW cutoffs and roots, KKR structure sums and contours, and MTO tail range, screening, and downfolding.</li><li>Converge k sampling, occupations or smearing, charge residual, total energy, and the target observable.</li><li>Validate forces and stress with finite differences, symmetry, and sum rules.</li><li>Change partition or representation and confirm physical-observable invariance within tolerance.</li></ol></div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="常见误判" titleEn="Recurrent misjudgments" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh"><ol><li>把 sphere/interstitial partition 与 muffin-tin potential approximation 当成同一件事。</li><li>认为单个 APW basis function 必须 value/slope 同时连续。</li><li>把 radial boundary-node pole 当作 physical divergent state。</li><li>因 KKR matrix 小就断言完整计算更便宜。</li><li>把 single-site phase-shift resonance 当作 periodic material band。</li><li>把 CPA effective medium 当作真实 average structure。</li><li>把 ASA volume filling 当作 full-potential accuracy。</li><li>把 canonical band shape 当作 material-specific absolute energy。</li><li>把 screened locality 当作已证明 O(N)。</li><li>只比较 eigenvalues 而不检查 charge、energy、force 与 partition invariance。</li></ol></div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en"><ol><li>Identifying a spatial partition with the muffin-tin potential approximation.</li><li>Requiring every APW basis function to be continuous in both value and slope.</li><li>Interpreting a radial-node pole as a divergent physical state.</li><li>Assuming that a small KKR matrix makes the complete calculation cheap.</li><li>Identifying a single-site resonance with a periodic material band.</li><li>Treating a CPA effective medium as a real average structure.</li><li>Equating ASA volume filling with full-potential accuracy.</li><li>Interpreting canonical band shape as an absolute material energy.</li><li>Equating screened locality with demonstrated O(N) scaling.</li><li>Comparing eigenvalues without charge, energy, force, and partition checks.</li></ol></div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 原创练习与来源边界 | Original exercises and source boundary

<BilingualSection titleZh="十道原创练习" titleEn="Ten original exercises">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh"><ol><li><strong>free partition：</strong>证明 sphere potential 等于 interstitial reference 时 APW log-derivative mismatch 消失。</li><li><strong>boundary node：</strong>区分 $u_l(S,E)=0$ 附近 matching-coefficient pole 与 physical wavefunction。</li><li><strong>root derivative：</strong>对 $2\times2$ energy-dependent secular matrix 比较 determinant 与 null-vector derivative。</li><li><strong>phase shift：</strong>扫描 square-well depth，定位 $\sin^2\delta_0$ peak，并说明其不足以定义 crystal band。</li><li><strong>KKR dimension：</strong>计算多 site/$l_{\max}$ matrix dimension，并列出未计入的 structure/energy costs。</li><li><strong>contour：</strong>比较 real-axis integration 与 upper-half-plane contour quadrature。</li><li><strong>CPA endpoints：</strong>证明 $c=0,1$ 回到 pure components。</li><li><strong>canonical bands：</strong>固定 structure eigenvalues，改变 potential-function scale，区分 shape 与 energy scale。</li><li><strong>screening：</strong>对两种 screening 做 neighbour truncation，以 eigenvalue/force error 判定 locality。</li><li><strong>moving boundary：</strong>用一维 moving-domain surrogate 验证 boundary term。</li></ol></div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en"><ol><li><strong>Free partition:</strong> prove the APW mismatch vanishes when sphere and interstitial references agree.</li><li><strong>Boundary node:</strong> distinguish a matching-coefficient pole near $u_l(S,E)=0$ from the physical wavefunction.</li><li><strong>Root derivative:</strong> compare determinant and null-vector derivatives for a $2\times2$ nonlinear secular matrix.</li><li><strong>Phase shift:</strong> scan square-well depth, locate a $\sin^2\delta_0$ peak, and explain why it is not a crystal band.</li><li><strong>KKR dimension:</strong> calculate dimensions and list omitted structure and energy costs.</li><li><strong>Contour:</strong> compare real-axis and upper-half-plane quadrature.</li><li><strong>CPA endpoints:</strong> prove that $c=0,1$ recover pure components.</li><li><strong>Canonical bands:</strong> separate structure shape from potential-function energy scale.</li><li><strong>Screening:</strong> judge neighbour truncation from eigenvalue and force errors.</li><li><strong>Moving boundary:</strong> verify a boundary term in a one-dimensional moving domain.</li></ol></div>
</BilingualSection>
