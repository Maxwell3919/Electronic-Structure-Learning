## 16.4 Alloys and the coherent potential approximation | 合金与相干势近似

<BilingualCallout titleZh="来源定位" titleEn="Source locator" kind="source">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">【Martin 原书 | Martin source】印刷页 349–350 将 KKR single-site scattering language 用于 substitutional disorder，并概述 coherent potential approximation。以下 scalar derivation 与交互模型只展示平均 excess scattering 为零的条件。</div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">【Martin source】Printed pp. 349–350 apply the KKR single-site scattering language to substitutional disorder and outline the coherent potential approximation. The scalar derivation and interactive model below only illustrate the zero-average-excess-scattering condition.</div>
</BilingualCallout>

<BilingualSection titleZh="有效介质与真实 configuration 是不同对象" titleEn="An effective medium is not a real configuration">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>对 binary substitutional alloy $A_cB_{1-c}$，CPA 用 translationally invariant coherent medium 取代显式 disorder configurations。介质由 complex、energy-dependent coherent potential $\Sigma(z)$ 描述；把真实 A 或 B site 放回 cavity 后产生 excess scattering：</p>

$$
t_\alpha^{\mathrm{ex}}(z)=
\frac{V_\alpha-\Sigma(z)}{1-[V_\alpha-\Sigma(z)]G_c(z)},
\qquad \alpha\in\{A,B\}.
$$

    <p>single-site CPA condition 为</p>

$$
c\,t_A^{\mathrm{ex}}+(1-c)t_B^{\mathrm{ex}}=0.
$$

    <p>它要求 coherent cavity 中平均额外散射消失，不要求每个 site 的 scattering 消失。$\operatorname{Im}\Sigma$ 可表示 disorder broadening，但其数值依赖 energy、lattice Green function、orbital channel 与 self-consistent potential。</p>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>For a binary substitutional alloy $A_cB_{1-c}$, CPA replaces explicit disorder configurations by a translationally invariant coherent medium described by a complex, energy-dependent coherent potential $\Sigma(z)$. Restoring a real A or B site in the cavity produces excess scattering:</p>

$$
t_\alpha^{\mathrm{ex}}(z)=
\frac{V_\alpha-\Sigma(z)}{1-[V_\alpha-\Sigma(z)]G_c(z)},
\qquad \alpha\in\{A,B\}.
$$

    <p>The single-site CPA condition is</p>

$$
c\,t_A^{\mathrm{ex}}+(1-c)t_B^{\mathrm{ex}}=0.
$$

    <p>It requires average additional scattering in the coherent cavity to vanish, not every site to stop scattering. $\operatorname{Im}\Sigma$ can represent disorder broadening, but depends on energy, the lattice Green function, orbital channel, and the self-consistent potential.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock title="scalar CPA quadratic | scalar CPA 二次方程" assumptions={['one scalar orbital and fixed retarded cavity Green function g；','real site potentials V_A and V_B；','no local relaxation, charge transfer, or short-range order。']}>

For scalar $g$,

$$
c\frac{V_A-\Sigma}{1-g(V_A-\Sigma)}
+(1-c)\frac{V_B-\Sigma}{1-g(V_B-\Sigma)}=0
$$

becomes

$$
\bar V-\Sigma-g(V_A-\Sigma)(V_B-\Sigma)=0,
\qquad \bar V=cV_A+(1-c)V_B,
$$

or

$$
g\Sigma^2+[1-g(V_A+V_B)]\Sigma+gV_AV_B-\bar V=0.
$$

The physical retarded branch is selected by analytic continuity from the pure endpoints and by lying in the retarded half-plane. Choosing a root merely because its magnitude is smaller is not a physical criterion.

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="平均介质不等于平均结构" titleEn="An average medium is not an average structure" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">CPA translational invariance 属于 ensemble-averaged Green-function construction。它不意味着原子在平均位置形成真实 periodic crystal，也不能从 broadened CPA spectrum 直接读取局域键长、site-resolved force 或 phonon stability。</div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">CPA translational invariance belongs to an ensemble-averaged Green-function construction. It does not imply a real periodic crystal at average atomic positions, and a broadened CPA spectrum does not directly provide local bond lengths, site-resolved forces, or phonon stability.</div>
</BilingualCallout>
