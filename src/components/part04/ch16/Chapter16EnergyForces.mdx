## 16.8 Total energy, force, and pressure in augmented methods | 增强方法中的总能、力与压力

  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">【Martin source】Printed pp. 360–361 summarize the total-energy, force, and pressure difficulties of augmented methods: moving boundaries, energy-dependent bases, sphere terms, surface integrals, and full-potential corrections. <BilingualSection titleZh="variational stationarity 不消除 representation derivatives" titleEn="Variational stationarity does not remove representation derivatives">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>对 nuclear coordinate $R_I$，self-consistent total-energy derivative 可按来源拆分：</p>

$$
\frac{dE}{dR_I}
=\left.\frac{\partial E}{\partial R_I}\right|_{n,\chi,\Omega_s}
+\frac{\partial E}{\partial n}\frac{dn}{dR_I}
+\frac{\partial E}{\partial\chi}\frac{d\chi}{dR_I}
+\sum_s\frac{\partial E}{\partial\Omega_s}\frac{d\Omega_s}{dR_I}.
$$

    <p>在 exact variational/self-consistent limit，density-response term 可由 stationarity 重组；但 augmentation functions 与 sphere boundaries 随 atoms 移动，仍产生 Pulay-like 与 surface terms。APW basis 还显式依赖 trial energy，root derivative 必须从 nonlinear secular equation 一致求得。</p>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>For nuclear coordinate $R_I$, a self-consistent total-energy derivative can be separated by source:</p>

$$
\frac{dE}{dR_I}
=\left.\frac{\partial E}{\partial R_I}\right|_{n,\chi,\Omega_s}
+\frac{\partial E}{\partial n}\frac{dn}{dR_I}
+\frac{\partial E}{\partial\chi}\frac{d\chi}{dR_I}
+\sum_s\frac{\partial E}{\partial\Omega_s}\frac{d\Omega_s}{dR_I}.
$$

    <p>In the exact variational and self-consistent limit, density-response terms can be reorganized through stationarity. Augmentation functions and sphere boundaries still move with the atoms, producing Pulay-like and surface terms. The APW basis also depends on trial energy, so root derivatives must be evaluated consistently from the nonlinear secular equation.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock title="nonlinear secular root derivative | 非线性 secular root 导数" assumptions={['a simple differentiable root E_n(R)；','∂F/∂E is nonzero at the root；','all radial and boundary dependencies are included in F。']}>

The root satisfies

$$
F(E_n(R),R)=0,
$$

so

$$
\frac{dE_n}{dR}
=-\frac{\partial_R F}{\partial_E F}\bigg|_{E_n}.
$$

For a secular matrix $K(E,R)$ with right and left null vectors,

$$
K(E_n,R)c_n=0,
\qquad
\widetilde c_n^\dagger K(E_n,R)=0,
$$

one obtains

$$
\frac{dE_n}{dR}
=-\frac{\widetilde c_n^\dagger(\partial_RK)c_n}
{\widetilde c_n^\dagger(\partial_EK)c_n}.
$$

The denominator is absent from an energy-independent generalized eigenproblem and is essential for nonlinear APW/KKR/MTO roots.

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="moving-surface contribution" titleEn="Moving-surface contribution">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>当积分区域 $\Omega_s(R)$ 随 sphere center 或 radius 变化时，Reynolds transport theorem 给出</p>

$$
\frac{d}{dR}\int_{\Omega_s(R)} f(\mathbf r,R)d^3r
=\int_{\Omega_s}\partial_R f\,d^3r
+\oint_{\partial\Omega_s}f(\mathbf v_b\cdot\mathbf n)dS.
$$

    <p>sphere 与 interstitial 的 surface contributions 必须按同一 boundary convention 配对。若 density、potential 或 basis derivatives 在 boundary 不一致，表面项不会自动抵消。</p>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>When an integration region $\Omega_s(R)$ moves with a sphere centre or radius, the Reynolds transport theorem gives</p>

$$
\frac{d}{dR}\int_{\Omega_s(R)} f(\mathbf r,R)d^3r
=\int_{\Omega_s}\partial_R f\,d^3r
+\oint_{\partial\Omega_s}f(\mathbf v_b\cdot\mathbf n)dS.
$$

    <p>Surface contributions from sphere and interstitial terms must be paired in one boundary convention. If density, potential, or basis derivatives are inconsistent at the boundary, the terms do not cancel automatically.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="pressure 与 stress 的额外依赖" titleEn="Additional dependencies of pressure and stress">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>uniform strain 同时改变 lattice vectors、structure constants、sphere geometry、radial matching、Brillouin-zone weights 与 electrostatic reference。pressure 不能只由 band-energy derivative 得到：</p>

$$
P=-\frac{dE_{\mathrm{tot}}}{d\Omega},
\qquad
\sigma_{\alpha\beta}=\frac1\Omega\frac{dE_{\mathrm{tot}}}{d\varepsilon_{\alpha\beta}}.
$$

    <p>muffin-tin/ASA calculations 还需区分 physical strain 与改变 space-filling approximation 的 derivative。</p>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>A uniform strain changes lattice vectors, structure constants, sphere geometry, radial matching, Brillouin-zone weights, and the electrostatic reference. Pressure is not the band-energy derivative alone:</p>

$$
P=-\frac{dE_{\mathrm{tot}}}{d\Omega},
\qquad
\sigma_{\alpha\beta}=\frac1\Omega\frac{dE_{\mathrm{tot}}}{d\varepsilon_{\alpha\beta}}.
$$

    <p>Muffin-tin or ASA calculations must also distinguish physical strain from a derivative that changes the space-filling approximation.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>
