## 阅读定位 | Reading orientation

<BilingualSection titleZh="本章改变的求解语言" titleEn="The change of computational language">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>Chapters 12–13 用单一平面波或网格表示整个 cell，Chapters 14–15 用固定局域函数跨越整个空间。Chapter 16 把空间分成核附近的 atomic spheres 与其余 interstitial：interstitial 保留 smooth representation，sphere 内改写为 radial partial waves 与 spherical harmonics，并通过 boundary data 连接。</p>

$$
\text{smooth interstitial field}
\xrightarrow{\text{boundary data}}
\text{atomic partial waves}.
$$

    <p>radial solution、phase shift、logarithmic derivative、single-site scattering 与 boundary matching 都依赖 trial energy。因此 original APW、KKR 与 MTO 的求解对象通常是 energy-dependent secular equation，而不只是一次 energy-independent diagonalization。</p>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>Chapters 12–13 represented the whole cell with one plane-wave or grid language, whereas Chapters 14–15 used fixed localized functions across space. Chapter 16 divides space into atomic spheres near the nuclei and the remaining interstitial: a smooth representation is retained in the interstitial, while radial partial waves and spherical harmonics are used inside each sphere and connected through boundary data.</p>

$$
\text{smooth interstitial field}
\xrightarrow{\text{boundary data}}
\text{atomic partial waves}.
$$

    <p>Radial solutions, phase shifts, logarithmic derivatives, single-site scattering, and boundary matching depend on trial energy. Original APW, KKR, and MTO therefore lead to energy-dependent secular equations rather than merely one energy-independent diagonalization.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="对象层级" titleEn="Object hierarchy">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <ol>
      <li><strong>物理方程：</strong>给定 self-consistent effective potential 的 independent-particle Schrödinger/Kohn–Sham equation。</li>
      <li><strong>空间分区：</strong>nonoverlapping spheres 与 interstitial；muffin-tin/ASA 还会对 potential 或 volume partition 增加近似。</li>
      <li><strong>site physics：</strong>radial solution、log derivative、phase shift 或 single-site <em>t</em> matrix。</li>
      <li><strong>geometry：</strong>structure constants、reference Green function 或 MTO structure matrix。</li>
      <li><strong>secular condition：</strong>APW boundary determinant、KKR determinant 或 MTO tail-cancellation determinant。</li>
      <li><strong>self-consistency：</strong>由 states/Green function 重建 density，更新 potential，再重复 site 与 geometry problem。</li>
      <li><strong>科学判断：</strong>在 angular cutoff、sphere choice、root search、charge、energy、force 与 target observable 分别收敛后才进入。</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Physical equation:</strong> the independent-particle Schrödinger or Kohn–Sham equation for a given self-consistent effective potential.</li>
      <li><strong>Spatial partition:</strong> nonoverlapping spheres and the interstitial; muffin-tin or ASA models add approximations to the potential or volume partition.</li>
      <li><strong>Site physics:</strong> radial solutions, logarithmic derivatives, phase shifts, or single-site <em>t</em> matrices.</li>
      <li><strong>Geometry:</strong> structure constants, a reference Green function, or an MTO structure matrix.</li>
      <li><strong>Secular condition:</strong> an APW boundary determinant, KKR determinant, or MTO tail-cancellation determinant.</li>
      <li><strong>Self-consistency:</strong> reconstruct the density from states or Green functions, update the potential, and repeat.</li>
      <li><strong>Scientific judgment:</strong> only after separate convergence of angular cutoff, sphere choice, root search, charge, energy, force, and target observables.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>
