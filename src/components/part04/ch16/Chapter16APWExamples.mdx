<BilingualSection titleZh="nonlinear secular workflow" titleEn="Nonlinear secular workflow">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>对固定 $\mathbf k$ 与 trial energy $E$，先解每个 site/channel 的 radial equation，构造 $D_{ls}(E)$ 与 APW matrix $M(E,\mathbf k)$，再寻找</p>

$$
F(E,\mathbf k)=\det M(E,\mathbf k)=0.
$$

    <p>与 fixed-basis diagonalization 不同，一次 matrix factorization 不会同时给出所有 bands，因为 matrix elements 随正在搜索的 $E$ 改变。实际 root tracing 必须：</p>
    <ol>
      <li>在 energy mesh 上定位 determinant 或 eigenvalue-sign changes；</li>
      <li>避开/识别 radial boundary nodes 导致的 poles；</li>
      <li>逐根 refine，并追踪相邻 $\mathbf k$ points 的 band identity；</li>
      <li>分别增加 plane-wave cutoff 与 $l_{\max}$；</li>
      <li>在 self-consistency 中更新 potential 后重新求 radial functions 与 roots。</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>For fixed $\mathbf k$ and trial energy $E$, solve the radial equation in each site and channel, construct $D_{ls}(E)$ and the APW matrix $M(E,\mathbf k)$, and search for</p>

$$
F(E,\mathbf k)=\det M(E,\mathbf k)=0.
$$

    <p>Unlike a fixed-basis diagonalization, one matrix factorization does not yield all bands because the matrix elements change with the energy being searched. Root tracing must:</p>
    <ol>
      <li>locate determinant or eigenvalue sign changes on an energy mesh;</li>
      <li>identify poles caused by radial boundary nodes;</li>
      <li>refine each root and track band identity between neighbouring $\mathbf k$ points;</li>
      <li>increase the plane-wave cutoff and $l_{\max}$ separately;</li>
      <li>recompute radial functions and roots after the potential changes in self-consistency.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="weak scattering、resonance 与 band width" titleEn="Weak scattering, resonances, and band width">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>若 sphere potential 与 interstitial reference 相同，则 $u_l\propto j_l$，所以 $\Delta D_l(E)=0$。artificial partition 不应改变 free-electron spectrum；这是方法的重要 limiting test。</p>
    <p>若某 channel 的 phase shift 相对 free reference 变化缓慢，band 可以保持 nearly-free dispersion，即使真实 wavefunction 在 sphere 内并不接近单一 plane wave。相反，当 $D_l(E)$ 或 phase shift 随 energy 快速变化，满足不同 Bloch boundary conditions 所需的 energy shift 较小，因而产生较窄、较 atomic-like 的 band。这个解释是 scattering statement，不等价于“potential 很弱/很强”的简单幅值判断。</p>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>If the sphere potential equals the interstitial reference, then $u_l\propto j_l$ and $\Delta D_l(E)=0$. The artificial partition must not change the free-electron spectrum; this is an essential limiting test.</p>
    <p>If a channel's phase shift changes slowly relative to the free reference, a band can retain nearly-free dispersion even when the true wavefunction inside the sphere is not close to one plane wave. Conversely, rapid energy variation of $D_l(E)$ or the phase shift means that different Bloch boundary conditions are satisfied with small energy changes, producing a narrower and more atomic-like band. This is a scattering statement, not a simple judgment based on the magnitude of the potential.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>
