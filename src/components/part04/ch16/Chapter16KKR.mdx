## 16.3 KKR / multiple-scattering theory | KKR / 多重散射理论

    <p>选择 reference Hamiltonian $H_0$ 与 resolvent</p>

$$
G^0(z)=(z-H_0)^{-1}.
$$

    <p>将 potential 写成 site contributions $V=\sum_sV_s$。single-site $t_s$ 汇总在 site $s$ 内重复发生的散射：</p>

$$

$$
T=t+tG^0t+tG^0tG^0t+\cdots
=(t^{-1}-G^0)^{-1},
$$

    <p>其中 $t$ 在 site/angular-momentum space 中通常是 block diagonal，$G^0$ 的 off-site blocks 只编码 propagation geometry。完整 Green function 为</p>

$$
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>Choose a reference Hamiltonian $H_0$ and resolvent</p>

$$
G^0(z)=(z-H_0)^{-1}.
$$

    <p>Write the potential as site contributions $V=\sum_sV_s$. A single-site $t_s$ sums repeated scattering at site $s$:</p>

$$
t_s=V_s+V_sG^0t_s.
$$

    <p>All paths visiting different sites form</p>

$$
T=t+tG^0t+tG^0tG^0t+\cdots
=(t^{-1}-G^0)^{-1},
$$

    <p>where $t$ is usually block diagonal in site and angular-momentum space, while the off-site blocks of $G^0$ encode propagation geometry. The full Green function is</p>

$$
G=G^0+G^0TG^0.
$$
  </div>

<DerivationBlock
  title="KKR secular determinant | KKR 久期行列式"
  assumptions={[
    'a reference propagator and site partition are fixed；',
    'the angular-momentum basis is truncated at l_max；',
    'the same phase and unit conventions are used for t and structure constants。',
  ]}
>

A stationary state has a pole in the scattering-path operator. Therefore

$$
\det\left[t^{-1}(E)-G^0(E)\right]=0.
$$

For a periodic solid, transform site separations to Bloch momentum and expand each block in spherical harmonics:

$$
\det\left[t^{-1}_{Ls}(E)\delta_{LL'}\delta_{ss'}
-B_{Ls,L's'}(E,\mathbf k)\right]=0.
$$

The single-site quantity $t_{ls}(E)$ carries the radial potential and phase shift; $B(E,\mathbf k)$ carries lattice geometry and reference propagation. In a spherical potential, $t$ is diagonal in $L$; a nonspherical potential couples angular channels and enlarges the site blocks.

For $N_s$ sites and angular cutoff $l_{\max}$, the nominal matrix dimension is

$$
N_{\mathrm{KKR}}=N_s\sum_{l=0}^{l_{\max}}(2l+1)
=N_s(l_{\max}+1)^2.
$$

Small matrix dimension does not remove energy nonlinearity, dense site coupling, or the cost of constructing converged structure constants and charge density.

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="phase shift 与 single-site t matrix" titleEn="Phase shift and the single-site t matrix">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>对 spherical single-site potential，每个 channel 的 exterior solution 可写成 regular 与 irregular free solutions 的组合。以一种常用 convention 表示：</p>

$$
R_l(r,E)\propto
j_l(kr)\cos\delta_l(E)-n_l(kr)\sin\delta_l(E).
$$

    <p>radial log derivative at the sphere boundary 决定 $\delta_l$；反过来，$t_l$ 可写成 phase shift 的复函数。不同文献对 $t_l$ 的 sign、$k$ prefactor 与 Green-function normalization 不同，只有 secular equation 与 spectral formula 整体保持 convention-consistent 才可比较。</p>
    <p>resonance 对应 $\sin^2\delta_l$ 接近 1 或 $k\cot\delta_l$ 快速穿越 structure constant。一个 phase-shift peak 是 single-site scattering 证据，尚不是 periodic material band 或 many-body excitation。</p>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>For a spherical single-site potential, each exterior channel is a combination of regular and irregular free solutions. In one common convention,</p>

$$
R_l(r,E)\propto
j_l(kr)\cos\delta_l(E)-n_l(kr)\sin\delta_l(E).
$$

    <p>The radial logarithmic derivative at the sphere boundary determines $\delta_l$, and $t_l$ is then a complex function of the phase shift. Literature conventions differ in the sign of $t_l$, factors of $k$, and Green-function normalization. Comparisons are meaningful only when the secular equation and spectral formulas use one consistent convention.</p>
    <p>A resonance corresponds to $\sin^2\delta_l$ approaching unity or to $k\cot\delta_l$ crossing a structure constant rapidly. A phase-shift peak is single-site scattering evidence, not yet a periodic material band or a many-body excitation.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="local Green function、DOS 与 contour integration" titleEn="Local Green functions, DOS, and contour integration">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>在明确 projector/normalization 后，site/channel-resolved spectral density 可由 retarded Green function 构造：</p>

$$
n_{Ls}(E)=-\frac1\pi\operatorname{Im}
\operatorname{Tr}_{Ls}\left[\mathcal P_{Ls}G(E+i\eta)\right].
$$

    <p>occupied charge 与 one-electron energy moments 为</p>

$$
N_{Ls}=\int dE\,f(E)n_{Ls}(E),
\qquad
M_{Ls}^{(1)}=\int dE\,E f(E)n_{Ls}(E).
$$

    <p>real axis 上 poles 密集且尖锐；沿 upper-half complex contour，Green function 更 smooth，可用较少 energy points 做 occupied-state integration，最后仍需控制 contour、temperature/smearing、pole enclosure 与 charge sum rule。</p>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>With an explicit projector and normalization convention, a site- and channel-resolved spectral density can be constructed from the retarded Green function:</p>

$$
n_{Ls}(E)=-\frac1\pi\operatorname{Im}
\operatorname{Tr}_{Ls}\left[\mathcal P_{Ls}G(E+i\eta)\right].
$$

    <p>Occupied charge and one-electron energy moments are</p>

$$
N_{Ls}=\int dE\,f(E)n_{Ls}(E),
\qquad
M_{Ls}^{(1)}=\int dE\,E f(E)n_{Ls}(E).
$$

    <p>Poles are dense and sharp on the real axis. Along a contour in the upper half-plane the Green function is smoother, so occupied-state integration can use fewer energy points. The contour, temperature or smearing, enclosed poles, and charge sum rule still require convergence.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>
