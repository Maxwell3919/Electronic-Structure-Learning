## 16.7 Localized tight-binding MTO and KKR formulations | 局域 tight-binding MTO 与 KKR

<BilingualCallout titleZh="来源定位" titleEn="Source locator" kind="source">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">【Martin 原书 | Martin source】印刷页 358–360 讨论 screening transformations 如何把长程 structure constants 转换为更短程的 tight-binding-like MTO/KKR representation，同时保持未截断 secular content。以下 tail model 为原创 surrogate。</div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">【Martin source】Printed pp. 358–360 discuss screening transformations that convert long-ranged structure constants into shorter-ranged tight-binding-like MTO or KKR representations while retaining the untruncated secular content. The tail model below is an original surrogate.</div>
</BilingualCallout>

<BilingualSection titleZh="screening 是 representation change" titleEn="Screening is a change of representation">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>bare KKR/MTO structure constants 来自 reference propagation，常有长程 algebraic tails。local screening channels 可重新组合 regular/irregular solutions，使 envelope 在 neighbouring spheres 被部分抵消。抽象写为</p>

$$
S^{(\alpha)}=S^{(0)}[I-\alpha S^{(0)}]^{-1}.
$$

    <p>potential function 必须按同一 convention 改写，才能保持 secular zeros：</p>

$$
\det[P^{(0)}(E)-S^{(0)}(\mathbf k)]
\propto
\det[P^{(\alpha)}(E)-S^{(\alpha)}(\mathbf k)].
$$

    <p>screening 改变 matrix range、conditioning 与 orbital shape，却不应改变完整、未截断 spectrum。只有在随后截断 neighbours 时，locality 才转化为近似与效率问题。</p>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>Bare KKR or MTO structure constants arise from reference propagation and often have long-ranged algebraic tails. Local screening channels recombine regular and irregular solutions so that envelopes are partially cancelled on neighbouring spheres. Schematically,</p>

$$
S^{(\alpha)}=S^{(0)}[I-\alpha S^{(0)}]^{-1}.
$$

    <p>The potential function must be transformed in the same convention to preserve secular zeros:</p>

$$
\det[P^{(0)}(E)-S^{(0)}(\mathbf k)]
\propto
\det[P^{(\alpha)}(E)-S^{(\alpha)}(\mathbf k)].
$$

    <p>Screening changes matrix range, conditioning, and orbital shape without changing the complete untruncated spectrum. Locality becomes an approximation and efficiency question only when neighbour terms are then truncated.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock title="screening 与 neighbour truncation 的分离 | Separating screening from neighbour truncation" assumptions={['the representation change is invertible；','potential and structure functions use one screening convention；','no neighbour truncation has yet been applied。']}>

Let

$$
K^{(0)}(E)=P^{(0)}(E)-S^{(0)}.
$$

An invertible change gives

$$
K^{(\alpha)}(E)=A(E)K^{(0)}(E)B(E),
$$

and therefore

$$
\det K^{(\alpha)}(E)=\det A(E)\det K^{(0)}(E)\det B(E).
$$

The physical roots are unchanged wherever $A$ and $B$ are nonsingular. Truncation is a separate approximation,

$$
K^{(\alpha)}\rightarrow K_R^{(\alpha)},
$$

whose error must be measured on eigenvalues, charge, total energy, forces, or the declared target observable.

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="tight-binding-like 不等于 empirical tight binding" titleEn="Tight-binding-like is not empirical tight binding">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>screened MTO/KKR matrices 可以呈现 on-site 与 short-range hopping-like blocks，但 matrix elements 来自 radial potential functions、structure constants 与 scattering，并依赖 reference energies、sphere partition、screening 与 self-consistent potential。</p>
    <p>因此，“tight-binding representation”描述矩阵形式；它不表示参数必然来自 fit，也不能把 representation-dependent on-site term 直接解释为唯一 atomic level。screening/downfolding 会把 eliminated channels 的影响重分配到 retained blocks。</p>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>Screened MTO or KKR matrices can look like on-site and short-ranged hopping blocks, but the elements arise from radial potential functions, structure constants, and scattering, and depend on reference energies, sphere partition, screening, and the self-consistent potential.</p>
    <p>“Tight-binding representation” therefore describes matrix form; it does not imply fitted parameters or make a representation-dependent on-site term a unique atomic level. Screening and downfolding redistribute eliminated-channel effects among retained blocks.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="locality 的证据链" titleEn="Evidence chain for locality" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">要声称 screened formulation 更局域，应报告 matrix elements 随 distance 的衰减、不同 screening choices、neighbour-shell truncation、eigenvalue/charge/force error、conditioning 与 total runtime。只有 sparsity pattern 而无 target-observable convergence，不能支持效率结论。</div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">A locality claim requires distance-dependent matrix elements, alternative screening choices, neighbour-shell truncation, eigenvalue, charge, and force errors, conditioning, and total runtime. A sparsity pattern without target-observable convergence does not establish efficiency.</div>
</BilingualCallout>
