## 16.5 Muffin-tin orbitals | Muffin-tin 轨道

<BilingualCallout titleZh="来源定位" titleEn="Source locator" kind="source">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">【Martin 原书 | Martin source】印刷页 350–352 将 KKR reformulate 为 augmented spherical-wave basis：一个 site 的 radial head 与 interstitial envelope 连接，envelope 穿过其他 spheres，再由各 spheres 内的 regular partial waves 取消 unwanted tails。网站用统一 head/envelope/tail language 展开。</div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">【Martin source】Printed pp. 350–352 reformulate KKR as an augmented spherical-wave basis: a radial head at one site is connected to an interstitial envelope, that envelope passes through other spheres, and regular partial waves inside those spheres cancel the unwanted tails. The site develops this using a unified head, envelope, and tail language.</div>
</BilingualCallout>

<BilingualSection titleZh="MTO 的 head、envelope 与 augmentation" titleEn="Head, envelope, and augmentation in an MTO">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>选择 site $R$ 与 channel $L$。在中心 sphere 内放置 radial partial wave $u_{Rl}(r,E)Y_L$ 作为 <em>head</em>；在 interstitial 中使用 spherical Hankel/Neumann-like solution $K_L(\kappa,\mathbf r-\mathbf R)$ 作为 <em>envelope</em>。当 envelope 进入其他 sphere $R'$ 时，用 addition theorem 展开为 regular waves：</p>

$$
K_L(\kappa,\mathbf r-\mathbf R)
=\sum_{L'}J_{L'}(\kappa,\mathbf r-\mathbf R')
B_{L'L}(\kappa,\mathbf R'-\mathbf R).
$$

    <p>augmentation replaces the interior continuation of the envelope by a radial solution at each sphere. 因而一个 MTO 不只是“atomic orbital plus arbitrary tail”，而是由 Helmholtz/reference solution、site radial scattering 和 boundary matching 共同定义。</p>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>Choose site $R$ and channel $L$. Inside the central sphere place a radial partial wave $u_{Rl}(r,E)Y_L$ as the <em>head</em>; in the interstitial use a spherical Hankel- or Neumann-like solution $K_L(\kappa,\mathbf r-\mathbf R)$ as the <em>envelope</em>. When the envelope enters another sphere $R'$, an addition theorem expands it in regular waves:</p>

$$
K_L(\kappa,\mathbf r-\mathbf R)
=\sum_{L'}J_{L'}(\kappa,\mathbf r-\mathbf R')
B_{L'L}(\kappa,\mathbf R'-\mathbf R).
$$

    <p>Augmentation replaces the interior continuation of the envelope by a radial solution in each sphere. An MTO is therefore not merely an atomic orbital plus an arbitrary tail; it is jointly defined by a Helmholtz or reference solution, site radial scattering, and boundary matching.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="tail cancellation as a secular condition | tail cancellation 作为久期条件"
  assumptions={[
    'one energy and envelope parameter are fixed；',
    'the sphere potential is represented by channel potential functions；',
    'the same angular basis is used for all head and tail expansions。',
  ]}
>

A Bloch sum of MTOs has coefficients $a_L(\mathbf k)$. Inside a sphere, the sum consists of the local head plus regular tails re-expanded from all other sites. For a physical solution, the coefficient of the unwanted regular continuation must cancel against the radial boundary mismatch. This gives

$$
\sum_{L'}\left[S_{LL'}(\mathbf k)-P_l(E)\delta_{LL'}\right]a_{L'}(\mathbf k)=0,
$$

and hence

$$
\det\left[S(\mathbf k)-P(E)\right]=0.
$$

$S(\mathbf k)$ is a structure matrix determined by lattice geometry and envelope convention; $P_l(E)$ is a potential function determined by the radial solution and its boundary logarithmic derivative. This separation is the MTO analogue of the KKR split between structure constants and single-site scattering.

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="MTO 与 ordinary tight binding" titleEn="MTO versus ordinary tight binding" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">An MTO basis can be compact and physically interpretable, but its matrix elements are derived from augmented scattering solutions and are generally energy dependent before linearization. Calling it “tight binding” describes locality or a small orbital basis, not an empirical parameterization. Chapter 17 is required before replacing the nonlinear potential function by a linearized Hamiltonian.</div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">An MTO basis can be compact and physically interpretable, but its matrix elements are derived from augmented scattering solutions and are generally energy dependent before linearization. Calling it “tight binding” describes locality or a small orbital basis, not an empirical parameterization. Chapter 17 is required before replacing the nonlinear potential function by a linearized Hamiltonian.</div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 16.6 Canonical bands | 规范带

<BilingualCallout titleZh="来源定位" titleEn="Source locator" kind="source">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">【Martin 原书 | Martin source】印刷页 352–358 先引入 atomic-sphere approximation，再利用 potential function 与 structure constants 的分离讨论 canonical unhybridized bands、band widths 与 canonical DOS。网站保留 approximation chain，不复制 source band/DOS plots。</div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">【Martin source】Printed pp. 352–358 introduce the atomic-sphere approximation and then use the separation of potential functions and structure constants to discuss canonical unhybridized bands, band widths, and canonical densities of states. The site retains the approximation chain without reproducing source band or DOS plots.</div>
</BilingualCallout>

<BilingualSection titleZh="atomic-sphere approximation 的额外假设" titleEn="Additional assumptions in the atomic-sphere approximation">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>nonoverlapping muffin-tin spheres 不填满一般 crystal volume。ASA 用 space-filling atomic spheres 近似整个 volume，并把每个 sphere 内 potential/density 的主要部分按 spherical components 处理。常见 consequence 包括：</p>
    <ul>
      <li>sphere volumes 被调整到总 volume；</li>
      <li>open structures 可能需要 empty spheres；</li>
      <li>overlap corrections 或 combined corrections 需要单独说明；</li>
      <li>nonspherical charge、electrostatics 与 forces 的 accuracy ceiling 低于 full-potential treatment。</li>
    </ul>
    <p>ASA 使 zero-energy envelope 与 inverse-power multipoles particularly simple，但这种 algebraic simplicity 来自 declared geometry/potential approximation。</p>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>Nonoverlapping muffin-tin spheres do not fill a general crystal volume. The ASA approximates the entire volume by space-filling atomic spheres and treats the dominant potential and density components inside each sphere as spherical. Common consequences are:</p>
    <ul>
      <li>sphere volumes are adjusted to the total volume;</li>
      <li>open structures may require empty spheres;</li>
      <li>overlap or combined corrections must be stated separately;</li>
      <li>nonspherical charge, electrostatics, and forces have a lower accuracy ceiling than in a full-potential treatment.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>The ASA makes zero-energy envelopes and inverse-power multipoles especially simple, but that algebraic simplicity follows from a declared geometry and potential approximation.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="potential function 与 structure eigenvalue" titleEn="Potential function and structure eigenvalue">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>忽略 channel hybridization 时，MTO secular equation reduces to</p>

$$
P_l(E)=s_{l\nu}(\mathbf k),
$$

    <p>其中 $s_{l\nu}(\mathbf k)$ 是 geometry-only structure matrix 在该 $l$ subspace 的 eigenvalue。若 $P_l(E)$ 在 band window 内 monotonic，可反演</p>

$$
E_{l\nu}(\mathbf k)=P_l^{-1}\!\left(s_{l\nu}(\mathbf k)\right).
$$

    <p>canonical band shape 来自 $s_{l\nu}(\mathbf k)$；energy scale、centre 与 width 由 $P_l^{-1}$ 以及 sphere radial amplitudes 设置。不同 materials 共享相似 lattice/channel geometry 时可以显示相关 canonical shapes，但真实 bands 还受 hybridization、crystal-field splitting、self-consistent potential、spin 和 relativistic effects 影响。</p>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>When channel hybridization is neglected, the MTO secular equation reduces to</p>

$$
P_l(E)=s_{l\nu}(\mathbf k),
$$

    <p>where $s_{l\nu}(\mathbf k)$ is an eigenvalue of the geometry-only structure matrix in that $l$ subspace. If $P_l(E)$ is monotonic over the band window, it may be inverted:</p>

$$
E_{l\nu}(\mathbf k)=P_l^{-1}\!\left(s_{l\nu}(\mathbf k)\right).
$$

    <p>The canonical band shape comes from $s_{l\nu}(\mathbf k)$, while the energy scale, centre, and width are set by $P_l^{-1}$ and sphere radial amplitudes. Materials with similar lattice and channel geometry can show related canonical shapes, but real bands also depend on hybridization, crystal-field splitting, the self-consistent potential, spin, and relativistic effects.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="canonical DOS transformation | canonical DOS 变换"
  assumptions={[
    'one unhybridized angular-momentum channel；',
    'P_l(E) is monotonic in the selected window；',
    'the structure eigenvalue density ρ_l^S(s) is normalized per channel。',
  ]}
>

If $s=P_l(E)$, conservation of states gives

$$
\rho_l(E)dE=\rho_l^S(s)ds.
$$

Therefore

$$
\rho_l(E)=\rho_l^S\!\left(P_l(E)\right)
\left|\frac{dP_l}{dE}\right|.
$$

The lattice controls the canonical structure density $\rho_l^S$; the radial potential stretches and shifts it through $P_l(E)$. This identity is useful for interpretation, but its assumptions fail when channels hybridize strongly or $P_l(E)$ is not one-to-one over the chosen window.

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="canonical 不等于 parameter-free prediction" titleEn="Canonical does not mean parameter-free prediction" kind="boundary">

They support comparisons such as lattice-induced degeneracies or relative band-shape trends. They do not independently predict a material Fermi level, exchange splitting, correlation shift, topology or experimental excitation spectrum.</div>
