## 20.8 电子—声子相互作用与超导 | Electron–Phonon Interactions and Superconductivity

<BilingualSection titleZh="声子位移如何调制屏蔽电子势" titleEn="How a phonon modulates the screened electronic potential" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

DFPT 提供每个原子位移微扰的自洽屏蔽势 $\partial_{\mathbf q s\alpha}V_{\mathrm{KS}}$。将这些 Cartesian 响应按声子本征向量组合，并量子化正规坐标，得到电子从 $|n\mathbf k\rangle$ 散射到 $|m,\mathbf k+\mathbf q\rangle$ 的矩阵元。一个常用规范为

$$
\boxed{
 g_{mn\nu}(\mathbf k,\mathbf q)
=
\sqrt{\frac{\hbar}{2\omega_{\mathbf q\nu}}}
\sum_{s\alpha}
\frac{e_{s\alpha}^{\nu}(\mathbf q)}{\sqrt{M_s}}
\left\langle
\psi_{m,\mathbf k+\mathbf q}
\left|
\partial_{\mathbf q s\alpha}V_{\mathrm{KS}}
\right|
\psi_{n\mathbf k}
\right\rangle.
}
$$

若本征向量已包含质量因子，或正规坐标定义不同，前因子会改变。比较不同代码或数据库前必须统一 $k/q$ 权重、晶胞或超胞归一化、spin degeneracy、声子本征向量、频率单位和是否含零点位移因子。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

DFPT provides the self-consistent screened potential derivative $\partial_{\mathbf q s\alpha}V_{\mathrm{KS}}$ for each atomic perturbation. Combining Cartesian responses with a phonon eigenvector and quantizing the normal coordinate gives the matrix element for scattering an electron from $|n\mathbf k\rangle$ to $|m,\mathbf k+\mathbf q\rangle$. A common convention is

$$
\boxed{
 g_{mn\nu}(\mathbf k,\mathbf q)
=
\sqrt{\frac{\hbar}{2\omega_{\mathbf q\nu}}}
\sum_{s\alpha}
\frac{e_{s\alpha}^{\nu}(\mathbf q)}{\sqrt{M_s}}
\left\langle
\psi_{m,\mathbf k+\mathbf q}
\left|
\partial_{\mathbf q s\alpha}V_{\mathrm{KS}}
\right|
\psi_{n\mathbf k}
\right\rangle.
}
$$

The prefactor changes if eigenvectors already contain masses or the normal coordinate is defined differently. Before comparing codes or databases one must align $k/q$ weights, cell or supercell normalization, spin degeneracy, phonon eigenvectors, frequency units, and inclusion of the zero-point displacement factor.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="从矩阵元到模式耦合和 Eliashberg 谱 | From matrix elements to mode coupling and the Eliashberg spectrum"
  assumptions={[
    '金属 Fermi 面附近的绝热散射；',
    '采用声明的 spin、BZ 权重和 delta-function convention；',
    '声子频率为正且模式归一化一致；',
    '先讨论各向同性 Fermi-surface 平均。',
  ]}
>

电子—声子耦合对低能金属态的影响包含 Fermi 面双重约束。用 $N(E_F)$ 表示每个声明 spin 或 cell convention 下的态密度，一个常见模式分辨形式为

$$
\lambda_{\mathbf q\nu}
=
\frac{2}{N(E_F)\,\omega_{\mathbf q\nu}}
\sum_{mn}
\int_{\mathrm{BZ}}\frac{d\mathbf k}{\Omega_{\mathrm{BZ}}}
|g_{mn\nu}(\mathbf k,\mathbf q)|^2
\delta(\varepsilon_{n\mathbf k}-E_F)
\delta(\varepsilon_{m,\mathbf k+\mathbf q}-E_F),
$$

其中 prefactor 会随 $g$ 与 DOS 规范改变。Eliashberg 谱将模式贡献按频率分箱：

$$
\alpha^2F(\omega)
=
\frac{1}{2N_q}
\sum_{\mathbf q\nu}
\lambda_{\mathbf q\nu}
\omega_{\mathbf q\nu}
\delta(\omega-\omega_{\mathbf q\nu}).
$$

于是总耦合

$$
\boxed{
\lambda
=
2\int_0^\infty
\frac{\alpha^2F(\omega)}{\omega}\,d\omega
=
\frac{1}{N_q}
\sum_{\mathbf q\nu}\lambda_{\mathbf q\nu}
}
$$

并定义对数频率矩

$$
\boxed{
\ln\omega_{\log}
=
\frac{2}{\lambda}
\int_0^\infty
\frac{\alpha^2F(\omega)}{\omega}
\ln\omega\,d\omega.
}
$$

低频模式因 $1/\omega$ 权重可显著增加 $\lambda$，但同时降低特征频率并放大非谐、结构不稳定、数值展宽和 Migdal 或绝热假设风险。由此不能把“更软”单调等同于“更高 $T_c$”。

Mode-resolved coupling is a Fermi-surface double average of $|g|^2$ with a frequency normalization. The Eliashberg spectral function bins these contributions by phonon frequency. Its inverse-frequency moment gives $\lambda$, while a logarithmic moment gives $\omega_{\log}$. Soft modes can increase $\lambda$ yet lower the frequency scale and invalidate harmonic or adiabatic assumptions; softness alone does not imply a higher critical temperature.

</DerivationBlock>

<EliashbergSpectrumExplorer />

<BilingualSection titleZh="从 α²F 到 Tc 还需要哪些假设" titleEn="Assumptions required to move from α²F to Tc" eyebrow="Scientific claim boundary">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

各向同性 Allen–Dynes 或 McMillan 型估计通常写成

$$
T_c
=
\frac{f_1f_2\omega_{\log}}{1.2}
\exp\left[
-\frac{1.04(1+\lambda)}
{\lambda-\mu^*(1+0.62\lambda)}
\right],
$$

其中 $\mu^*$ 是 Coulomb pseudopotential，$f_1,f_2$ 修正强耦合和谱形。该式不是从 Bands 或 DOS 直接得到的经验标签，而是 Migdal–Eliashberg 框架在各向同性和参数化 Coulomb 排斥下的近似表达。不同 $\mu^*$、谱各向异性、多带结构、低载流子密度、非绝热效应和强非谐性可显著改变结果。

支持一个可靠 $T_c$ 估计至少需要：稳定且与目标相一致的声子；$k$ 网格、$q$ 网格、空带或能窗、smearing 或 tetrahedron、插值和 delta 积分收敛；$g$ 与本征向量规范一致；$\alpha^2F$ 和累计 $\lambda$ 稳定；$\mu^*$ 或更显式 Coulomb kernel 的敏感性；各向同性近似的适用性；必要时与各向异性 Eliashberg、anharmonic phonons 或非绝热处理比较。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

An isotropic Allen–Dynes or McMillan-type estimate is commonly written

$$
T_c
=
\frac{f_1f_2\omega_{\log}}{1.2}
\exp\left[
-\frac{1.04(1+\lambda)}
{\lambda-\mu^*(1+0.62\lambda)}
\right],
$$

where $\mu^*$ is a Coulomb pseudopotential and $f_1,f_2$ correct strong coupling and spectral shape. This is not a label inferred directly from bands or DOS; it is an approximation within Migdal–Eliashberg theory under isotropic and parameterized-Coulomb assumptions. Different $\mu^*$ values, spectral anisotropy, multiband structure, low carrier density, nonadiabatic effects, and strong anharmonicity can materially change the result.

A defensible $T_c$ estimate requires phonons stable and consistent with the target phase; convergence of $k$ and $q$ meshes, bands or windows, smearing or tetrahedra, interpolation, and delta-function integration; consistent $g$ and eigenvector conventions; stable $\alpha^2F$ and cumulative $\lambda$; sensitivity to $\mu^*$ or a more explicit Coulomb kernel; applicability of isotropy; and where needed comparison with anisotropic Eliashberg, anharmonic phonons, or nonadiabatic treatments.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

At low density or small Fermi energy, the Migdal parameter can also become unfavorable, motivating nonadiabatic or vertex-correction assessment.</p>

## 20.9 磁振子与自旋响应函数 | Magnons and Spin Response Functions

<BilingualSection titleZh="冻结磁振子是绝热能量面上的自旋螺旋" titleEn="A frozen magnon is a spin spiral on an adiabatic energy surface" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

在共线磁基态附近，可约束局域磁矩方向形成波矢 $\mathbf q$ 的横向自旋螺旋，并计算总能 $E(\mathbf q,\theta)$。小倾角 $\theta$ 下的二阶能量可映射到有效交换 kernel；在局域 Heisenberg 极限，冻结螺旋色散与磁振子频率相关。无自旋轨道耦合时，广义 Bloch 定理可把自旋旋转与晶格平移结合，在原胞中处理不可公度螺旋。

该映射依赖绝热分离：电子与磁矩幅值足够快地跟随缓慢方向变化。若磁矩不刚性、存在强 Stoner particle–hole continuum、自旋轨道耦合或非共线多子晶格耦合，单一 Heisenberg 参数化和静态能量差可能不足。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Near a collinear magnetic ground state, local moments can be constrained into a transverse spin spiral of wavevector $\mathbf q$, and the total energy $E(\mathbf q,\theta)$ evaluated. At small cone angle $\theta$, the quadratic energy maps to an effective exchange kernel; in a localized Heisenberg limit, the frozen-spiral dispersion is related to magnon frequencies. Without spin–orbit coupling, a generalized Bloch theorem combines spin rotation with lattice translation, allowing an incommensurate spiral to be treated in the primitive cell.

This mapping relies on adiabatic separation: electronic degrees of freedom and moment magnitudes must follow slow directional changes. If moments are not rigid, a strong Stoner particle–hole continuum is present, spin–orbit coupling matters, or several noncollinear sublattices are coupled, a single Heisenberg parametrization and static energy difference may be insufficient.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="动态横向自旋响应与磁振子极点 | Dynamic transverse spin response and magnon poles"
  assumptions={[
    '围绕已定义的磁基态做小横向微扰；',
    '采用 retarded response；',
    '频率和 Fourier 符号在整节保持一致。',
  ]}
>

施加横向磁场 $\delta B^+(\mathbf q,\omega)$，诱导横向磁化

$$
\delta m^+(\mathbf q,\omega)
=
\chi^{+-}(\mathbf q,\omega)
\delta B^+(\mathbf q,\omega).
$$

retarded susceptibility 的极点给出集体自旋激发。若一个孤立弱阻尼模式可近似为

$$
\chi^{+-}(\mathbf q,\omega)
\approx
\frac{A_{\mathbf q}}
{\omega-\omega_{\mathbf q}+i\Gamma_{\mathbf q}},
$$

则 $\omega_{\mathbf q}$ 是峰位，$\Gamma_{\mathbf q}$ 与寿命或线宽相关，$A_{\mathbf q}$ 是谱权重。可观测谱与

$$
-\operatorname{Im}\chi^{+-}(\mathbf q,\omega)
$$

相关，并通过 fluctuation–dissipation relation 与动态结构因子连接。一个清晰磁振子需要相对窄且可追踪的谱峰；只有静态 $\chi(\mathbf q,0)$ 大或冻结螺旋能量低，并不能确定其线宽。

在 itinerant magnet 中，单粒子 spin-flip transitions 形成 Stoner continuum。集体极点进入 continuum 后可发生 Landau damping，峰变宽甚至不再是良定义准粒子。此时报告单条“magnon band”会丢失谱权重和阻尼信息。

The retarded transverse susceptibility maps a small transverse field to an induced transverse magnetization. An isolated weakly damped pole has a peak frequency, linewidth, and spectral weight. In an itinerant magnet, particle–hole spin-flip excitations form a Stoner continuum; a collective pole entering this continuum can broaden or cease to be a well-defined quasiparticle. A static frozen-spiral energy does not determine that linewidth.

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="自旋响应的 Kohn–Sham 与 kernel 结构" titleEn="Kohn–Sham response and the spin kernel" eyebrow="Response analogy">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

形式上，自旋 response 与密度 response 类似：先由 Kohn–Sham 自旋翻转跃迁构造 $\chi_0^{+-}$，再通过交换—关联 spin kernel 得到相互作用响应，

$$
\chi^{+-}
=
\chi_0^{+-}
+
\chi_0^{+-}K_{\mathrm{xc}}^{+-}\chi^{+-}.
$$

静态 adiabatic kernel 可以产生 Goldstone mode 和低能交换刚度，但频率依赖、多体顶角、自旋轨道耦合、无序和有限温涨落会改变峰位与线宽。Chapter 21 的 TDDFT 结构提供更一般的频率依赖框架；本节只建立声子和磁振子作为不同响应通道的对照。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Formally, spin response parallels density response: a Kohn–Sham spin-flip susceptibility $\chi_0^{+-}$ is dressed by an exchange–correlation spin kernel,

$$
\chi^{+-}
=
\chi_0^{+-}
+
\chi_0^{+-}K_{\mathrm{xc}}^{+-}\chi^{+-}.
$$

A static adiabatic kernel can produce a Goldstone mode and low-energy exchange stiffness, but frequency dependence, many-body vertices, spin–orbit coupling, disorder, and finite-temperature fluctuations affect peak positions and linewidths. The TDDFT structure of Chapter 21 provides the more general frequency-dependent framework; the present section establishes phonons and magnons as distinct response channels.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="声子与磁振子的对应和差别" titleEn="Analogy and difference between phonons and magnons" kind="boundary">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>两者都可由平衡态附近的小扰动、波矢分类和响应函数极点描述。声子以核位移和质量为慢变量，恢复力来自总能 Hessian；磁振子以磁化方向或自旋密度为变量，惯性与阻尼来自电子自旋动力学。动力学矩阵的实本征值问题不能直接替代一般频率依赖、自旋非守恒的响应谱。</p>
  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>Both can be classified by wavevector and described through small perturbations and response-function poles. Phonons use nuclear displacement and mass as slow variables, with restoring forces from the total-energy Hessian. Magnons use magnetization direction or spin density, with inertia and damping emerging from electronic spin dynamics. A real dynamical-matrix eigenproblem does not generally replace a frequency-dependent, spin-nonconserving response spectrum.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualCallout>
