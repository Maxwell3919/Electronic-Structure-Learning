## 20.1 从电子结构理论得到晶格动力学 | Lattice Dynamics from Electronic Structure Theory

<BilingualSection titleZh="与 Chapter 19 共用同一能量面" titleEn="The same energy surface as Chapter 19" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

在 Born–Oppenheimer 近似下，固定核坐标的电子基态定义总能面 $E_{\mathrm{BO}}(\{\mathbf R\})$。Chapter 19 沿该能量面的梯度传播离子；本章在平衡构型 $\mathbf R_0$ 附近展开同一个函数。令

$$
\mathbf R_{ls}=\mathbf R_{ls}^{0}+\mathbf u_{ls},
$$

其中 $l$ 标记晶胞、$s$ 标记原胞内原子。平衡条件为

$$
\left.\frac{\partial E_{\mathrm{BO}}}{\partial u_{ls\alpha}}\right|_{\mathbf u=0}=0.
$$

这只表示在允许的结构自由度中达到驻点。要区分极小值、鞍点和受约束驻点，需要二阶导数及约束空间的信息。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Within the Born–Oppenheimer approximation, the electronic ground state at fixed nuclei defines a total-energy surface $E_{\mathrm{BO}}(\{\mathbf R\})$. Chapter 19 propagated ions along its gradient; the present chapter expands the same function around an equilibrium geometry $\mathbf R_0$. Write

$$
\mathbf R_{ls}=\mathbf R_{ls}^{0}+\mathbf u_{ls},
$$

where $l$ labels a cell and $s$ an atom in the primitive cell. Equilibrium requires

$$
\left.\frac{\partial E_{\mathrm{BO}}}{\partial u_{ls\alpha}}\right|_{\mathbf u=0}=0.
$$

This condition identifies a stationary point within the allowed structural degrees of freedom. Distinguishing a minimum, saddle, or constrained stationary point requires second derivatives and knowledge of the constraint space.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

其中

$$
\boxed{
\Phi_{ls\alpha,l's'\beta}
=
\left.
\frac{\partial^2E}
{\partial u_{ls\alpha}\partial u_{l's'\beta}}
\right|_0

-\left.
\frac{\partial F_{ls\alpha}}

\right|_0

$$

平衡条件使一次项消失。若能量是光滑实标量，混合偏导可交换，因此

$$
\Phi_{ls\alpha,l's'\beta}
=
\Phi_{l's'\beta,ls\alpha}.
$$

将谐振能量对 $u_{ls\alpha}$ 求导，得到线性恢复力

$$
F_{ls\alpha}^{(1)}
=
-\sum_{l's'\beta}
\Phi_{ls\alpha,l's'\beta}u_{l's'\beta}.
$$

量纲为 $[\Phi]=\text{energy}/\text{length}^2=\text{force}/\text{length}$。若用有限位移得到 $\partial F/\partial u$，必须保证力和位移使用同一坐标、单位和原子映射。

The equilibrium gradient vanishes, leaving a quadratic form defined by the Hessian. Smoothness gives the permutation symmetry of the force-constant matrix. Differentiation of the quadratic energy produces a linear restoring force. This is a strict relation within the harmonic expansion; truncating after second order is the physical approximation.

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="平移对称性与声学和规则" titleEn="Translational invariance and the acoustic sum rule" eyebrow="Strict symmetry relation">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

将所有原子同时平移同一向量 $\mathbf a$ 不改变周期体系的能量。对任意参考原子 $ls\alpha$，在均匀位移 $u_{l's'\beta}=a_\beta$ 下线性力必须为零，因此

$$
\boxed{
\sum_{l's'}\Phi_{ls\alpha,l's'\beta}=0
\quad\text{for every }ls\alpha,\beta.
}
$$

这就是 acoustic sum rule（ASR）。它先是 Hamiltonian 与边界条件的平移不变性，随后才成为数值诊断或修正。若离散结果轻微违反 ASR，可以投影掉平移误差；但修正不能替代对基组、SCF、超胞、积分网格、非局域力和长程项的收敛检查。较大的修正可能掩盖真实实现问题。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A common translation of every atom by a vector $\mathbf a$ leaves the energy of a periodic system unchanged. For any reference component $ls\alpha$, the linear force under $u_{l's'\beta}=a_\beta$ must therefore vanish:

$$
\boxed{
\sum_{l's'}\Phi_{ls\alpha,l's'\beta}=0
\quad\text{for every }ls\alpha,\beta.
}
$$

This acoustic sum rule (ASR) is first a consequence of translational invariance of the Hamiltonian and boundary conditions, and only then a numerical diagnostic or correction. Projecting out a small violation can remove translational noise, but cannot replace convergence of the basis, SCF response, supercell, integration grid, nonlocal forces, or long-range terms. A large correction can conceal an implementation error.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

离子运动方程为

$$
M_s\ddot u_{ls\alpha}
=
-\sum_{l's'\beta}

$$

晶体平移对称允许写成 $\Phi_{0s\alpha,Ls'\beta}$，其中 $L=l'-l$。取 Bloch 形式

$$
 u_{ls\alpha}(t)
=
\frac{1}{\sqrt{M_s}}
 e_{s\alpha}^{\nu}(\mathbf q)
 e^{i(\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf R_l-\omega_{\mathbf q\nu}t)},
$$

代入运动方程，并消去共同的相位因子：

$$
\omega_{\mathbf q\nu}^2e_{s\alpha}^{\nu}(\mathbf q)
=
\sum_{s'\beta}
D_{s\alpha,s'\beta}(\mathbf q)
 e_{s'\beta}^{\nu}(\mathbf q),
$$

其中

$$
\boxed{
D_{s\alpha,s'\beta}(\mathbf q)
=
\frac{1}{\sqrt{M_sM_{s'}}}
\sum_L
\Phi_{0s\alpha,Ls'\beta}
 e^{i\mathbf q\cdot(\mathbf R_L+\boldsymbol\tau_{s'}-\boldsymbol\tau_s)}
}
$$

是质量加权动力学矩阵。若把原胞内位置 $\boldsymbol\tau_s$ 的相位吸收到本征向量中，公式会出现不同但等价的相位。比较代码、文献和插值文件时必须先统一 convention。

对于保守实能量面，$D(\mathbf q)$ 是 Hermitian，故 $\omega^2$ 为实数。采用

$$
\sum_{s\alpha}e_{s\alpha}^{\nu *}(\mathbf q)e_{s\alpha}^{\nu'}(\mathbf q)=\delta_{\nu\nu'}
$$

时，真实原子位移还包含 $1/\sqrt{M_s}$。不同软件也可把质量因子放进本征向量；电子—声子矩阵元和模式有效电荷必须使用同一规范。

The Bloch ansatz converts the infinite real-space equations into a finite Hermitian eigenproblem at each $\mathbf q$. Its eigenvalues are squared angular frequencies, not frequencies themselves. Phase and mass conventions can move between the matrix and eigenvectors without changing physical displacements, but matrix elements cannot be compared until those conventions are translated.

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="Gamma 极限、声学支与光学支" titleEn="The Gamma limit, acoustic branches, and optical branches" eyebrow="Normal modes">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

在 $\mathbf q=0$，ASR 保证三维自由晶体存在三个刚性平移零模，对应三条声学支在 Gamma 点的 $\omega=0$。靠近 Gamma 时，普通三维晶体的纵向和横向声学频率通常与 $|\mathbf q|$ 线性，斜率给出声速并与弹性常数相关。含 $S$ 个原子的原胞共有 $3S$ 条支，其中三条声学支，其余 $3S-3$ 条在 Gamma 点通常为光学支。

二维自由膜的弯曲声学模式在理想连续极限可呈 $\omega\propto q^2$，数值上对旋转不变性、真空处理和残余应力敏感。极性晶体的纵向光学模式还会受到宏观电场导致的非解析 $\mathbf q\to0$ 项；这部分在 20.7 处理。把所有 Gamma 非零或近零频率直接称作“数值误差”会遗漏边界条件与长程物理。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

At $\mathbf q=0$, the ASR guarantees three rigid-translation zero modes for a free three-dimensional crystal, giving three acoustic branches with $\omega=0$ at Gamma. Close to Gamma, ordinary longitudinal and transverse acoustic frequencies are typically linear in $|\mathbf q|$; their slopes give sound velocities and connect to elastic constants. A primitive cell with $S$ atoms has $3S$ branches: three acoustic and usually $3S-3$ optical branches at Gamma.

The flexural acoustic mode of an ideal free two-dimensional membrane can behave as $\omega\propto q^2$ and is numerically sensitive to rotational invariance, vacuum treatment, and residual stress. In a polar crystal, longitudinal optical modes also acquire a nonanalytic $\mathbf q\to0$ term from the macroscopic electric field, treated in §20.7. Labelling every small nonzero or near-zero Gamma frequency as numerical error would ignore boundary conditions and long-range physics.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="负本征值与 imaginary frequency" titleEn="Negative eigenvalues and imaginary frequencies" eyebrow="Evidence boundary">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

动力学矩阵的物理本征值是 $\omega^2$。若 $\omega^2<0$，常将 $\omega=i|\omega|$ 并在声子图上画成负频率。严格含义是在所用结构、电子态、边界条件和谐振能量面上存在负二阶曲率；小扰动沿该本征向量指数增长，而不是发生周期振动。

一个负值是否具有科学意义还要检查：结构是否真正达到同一方法下的驻点；该模式是否对应平移/旋转规则破坏；电子占据、磁态和 smearing 是否一致；$k/q$ 网格、截止、超胞、位移幅度或 DFPT 阈值是否收敛；长程非解析项和二维 Coulomb 处理是否正确；插值是否在未采样区域产生伪振荡。收敛的负曲率支持“该谐振结构沿此模式不稳定”，但不单独证明最终相、有限温相变温度或真实动力学路径。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The physical eigenvalue of the dynamical matrix is $\omega^2$. When $\omega^2<0$, one often writes $\omega=i|\omega|$ and plots it as a negative frequency. Strictly, the chosen structure, electronic state, boundary conditions, and harmonic energy surface have negative curvature along that eigenvector; a small perturbation grows exponentially rather than oscillating periodically.

Scientific interpretation additionally requires checking that the structure is stationary under the same method; the mode is not a broken translation or rotation; occupations, magnetic state, and smearing are consistent; cutoffs, $k/q$ meshes, supercell, displacement or DFPT thresholds are converged; long-range and two-dimensional Coulomb terms are correct; and interpolation has not created an unsampled artefact. A converged negative curvature supports harmonic instability along that mode, but does not by itself identify the final phase, transition temperature, or dynamical pathway.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## 20.2 直接方法：“冻结声子”和磁振子 | The Direct Approach: “Frozen Phonons,” Magnons

<BilingualSection titleZh="有限位移把导数转化为普通力计算" titleEn="Finite displacements turn derivatives into ordinary force calculations" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

直接法在一个与目标波矢相容的超胞中移动原子，重新进行自洽电子结构计算，并由能量或力差近似 Hessian。它的优势是只需要基态总能和力接口，可自然观察有限位移下的非线性与结构重排。代价是超胞尺寸随目标长波长和力常数范围增长；每个独立位移还需一组电子计算。

“冻结声子”表示原子按某一空间位移模式静态固定，电子完全响应后计算能量和力。它不是时间冻结的真实声子波包，也不包含声子寿命。小位移极限提取谐振曲率；较大位移可以拟合高阶非谐项，但此时结果不再等同于线性响应力常数。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The direct method displaces atoms in a supercell commensurate with the target wavevector, repeats the self-consistent electronic calculation, and approximates the Hessian from energy or force differences. It needs only an ordinary ground-state energy/force interface and naturally exposes nonlinear response and structural rearrangement at finite amplitude. Its cost grows with the supercell required by the wavelength and force-constant range, and each independent displacement requires additional electronic calculations.

A “frozen phonon” is a static atomic displacement pattern for which the electrons are fully relaxed before energy and forces are evaluated. It is not a physically frozen phonon wave packet and does not contain a phonon lifetime. The small-displacement limit extracts harmonic curvature; larger amplitudes can fit higher-order anharmonic terms, but then the result is no longer identical to a linear-response force constant.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="能量与力的中心差分 | Central differences of energies and forces"
  assumptions={[
    '选择一个归一化位移坐标 Q；',
    '在 Q=0 附近能量足够光滑；',
    '每个位移点使用相同电子方法、基组、占据和收敛定义。',
  ]}
>

沿模式坐标 $Q$ 写

$$
E(Q)=E_0+\frac12\kappa Q^2+\frac16a_3Q^3+\frac1{24}a_4Q^4+\cdots.
$$

对称三点能量差分给出

$$
\boxed{
\kappa_E(h)
=
\frac{E(h)-2E(0)+E(-h)}{h^2}
=
\kappa+\frac{a_4}{12}h^2+O(h^4)
}.
$$

奇次项因对称性消失。若使用力 $F(Q)=-dE/dQ$，则

$$
\boxed{
\kappa_F(h)
=
-\frac{F(h)-F(-h)}{2h}
=
\kappa+\frac{a_4}{6}h^2+O(h^4).
}
$$

两种公式的截断误差在固定光滑能量面上均为 $O(h^2)$，但数值噪声缩放不同。若每个能量含独立量级 $\epsilon_E$ 的误差，二阶能量差分的噪声可按 $\epsilon_E/h^2$ 放大；力差分则按 $\epsilon_F/h$ 放大。因此减小 $h$ 只在电子误差同步收紧时接近解析极限。

实际多原子 Hessian 可以通过逐个 Cartesian 位移得到

$$
\Phi_{I\alpha,J\beta}
\approx
-\frac{F_{I\alpha}(u_{J\beta}=h)-F_{I\alpha}(u_{J\beta}=-h)}{2h}.
$$

晶体对称性可减少独立位移数，但原子映射、约束和磁态必须在正负位移中一致。若结构在位移后切换电子解、对称分支或占据，中心差分不再逼近同一光滑函数的导数。

Symmetric energy and force differences cancel odd-order anharmonic terms and have second-order truncation error. Their noise amplification differs: energy noise is divided by $h^2$, force noise by $h$. The small-displacement limit is therefore a joint limit of displacement and electronic accuracy, not a command to choose the smallest representable displacement.

</DerivationBlock>

<FrozenPhononCurvatureExplorer />

<BilingualSection titleZh="超胞、可公度波矢与实空间截断" titleEn="Supercells, commensurate wavevectors, and real-space truncation" eyebrow="Numerical representation">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

若位移模式满足

$$
\mathbf u_{ls}\propto e^{i\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf R_l},
$$

则超胞平移 $\mathbf L_{\mathrm{SC}}$ 必须满足 $e^{i\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf L_{\mathrm{SC}}}=1$。因此一个给定超胞只直接表示与它可公度的有限 $\mathbf q$ 集合。常用实空间方法先在超胞中提取 $\Phi_{0s,ls'}$，再 Fourier 变换到任意 $\mathbf q$；这隐含超胞之外力常数可忽略或由周期映像代表。

超胞收敛不只是原子数收敛。还要检查电子 $k$ 点折叠、长程偶极力、金属中的 Friedel 型长程响应、二维真空方向映像、磁序和缺陷周期性。极性晶体的偶极—偶极项衰减慢，通常应分离解析长程部分与短程力常数后再插值。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For a displacement pattern

$$
\mathbf u_{ls}\propto e^{i\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf R_l},
$$

a supercell translation $\mathbf L_{\mathrm{SC}}$ must obey $e^{i\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf L_{\mathrm{SC}}}=1$. A chosen supercell therefore represents only a finite set of commensurate wavevectors directly. Real-space approaches usually extract $\Phi_{0s,ls'}$ in the supercell and Fourier transform to arbitrary $\mathbf q$, implicitly assuming that force constants beyond the cell are negligible or represented by periodic images.

Supercell convergence is not merely convergence in atom count. Electronic $k$-point folding, long-range dipolar forces, Friedel-like metallic response, vacuum-direction images in two dimensions, magnetic order, and defect periodicity must also be checked. Dipole–dipole force constants in polar crystals decay slowly and are normally separated into analytic long-range and short-range interpolated parts.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="直接法可以接触非谐性，但不会自动给出温度依赖" titleEn="Direct calculations can access anharmonicity but not temperature dependence automatically" eyebrow="Anharmonic boundary">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

在多个正负位移幅度上拟合 $E(Q)$ 或力，可以估计三阶、四阶及模式间耦合系数。这样得到的是所选基态能量面的局域高阶导数。要预测有限温频率、热膨胀、寿命、热导或相变，仍需选择相应统计力学与非谐理论，例如准谐近似、self-consistent phonons、温度依赖有效势或分子动力学相关函数，并分别验证采样和截断。

若沿软模的大位移弛豫到更低能结构，结果支持原结构在所用能量面上存在下降方向。它不单独给出真实相变温度，也不证明该路径是动力学上唯一或最快的路径。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Fitting energies or forces at several positive and negative amplitudes can estimate third- and fourth-order coefficients and mode coupling. These are local higher derivatives of the chosen ground-state energy surface. Predicting finite-temperature frequencies, thermal expansion, lifetimes, thermal conductivity, or phase transitions still requires an appropriate statistical and anharmonic framework—such as quasiharmonic theory, self-consistent phonons, temperature-dependent effective potentials, or molecular-dynamics correlation functions—with its own sampling and truncation checks.

Relaxation to a lower-energy structure along a soft-mode displacement supports the existence of a downhill direction on the chosen energy surface. It does not by itself give the transition temperature or establish that this is the unique or fastest dynamical path.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="冻结电场与冻结磁振子" titleEn="Frozen fields and frozen magnons" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

同一直接法思想可用于周期外势或自旋构型。施加有限波矢的静电势并计算诱导密度/总势，可以在离子固定或允许弛豫时得到电子或晶格贡献。对磁体系，可施加约束自旋螺旋并比较总能随波矢的变化，得到绝热冻结磁振子色散的近似。

静态自旋螺旋能量只描述受约束慢变量的能量面。动态自旋 response $\chi(\mathbf q,\omega)$ 还包含粒子—空穴连续谱、阻尼和谱权重。若模式在连续谱中强烈衰减，静态能量曲率不能替代其频率与寿命。20.9 将返回这一差别。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The same direct idea applies to periodic external fields or spin configurations. Applying an electrostatic potential at finite wavevector and computing the induced density or total potential yields electronic or lattice contributions depending on whether ions are fixed or relaxed. In a magnet, constrained spin spirals can be used to map total energy versus wavevector and approximate an adiabatic frozen-magnon dispersion.

A static spin-spiral energy describes the energy surface of constrained slow variables. The dynamic spin response $\chi(\mathbf q,\omega)$ also contains particle–hole continua, damping, and spectral weight. When a mode is strongly damped inside a continuum, a static curvature cannot replace its frequency and lifetime. Section 20.9 returns to this distinction.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="直接法的最低充分验证" titleEn="Minimum sufficient validation for a direct calculation" kind="practice">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">
