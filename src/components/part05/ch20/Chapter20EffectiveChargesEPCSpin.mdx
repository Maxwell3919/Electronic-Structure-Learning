## 20.7 介电响应函数与有效电荷 | Dielectric Response Functions and Effective Charges

<BilingualSection titleZh="均匀电场与周期边界的冲突" titleEn="The conflict between a homogeneous electric field and periodic boundaries" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

有限体系中的均匀静电场可通过势 $-e\mathbf E\cdot\mathbf r$ 表示，但位置算符不保持周期性。无限晶体中，直接把该势放入周期 Hamiltonian 会破坏普通 Bloch 边界。因此介电响应可以从有限波矢 $\mathbf q\to0$ 的极限、位置算符的带间矩阵元、Berry-phase 极化或有限电场能量泛函得到。Chapter 24 将系统建立 Berry-phase 极化；本节只保留得到介电张量、Born 有效电荷和长程声子项所需的接口。

需要区分电子固定离子响应 $\epsilon_\infty$ 与允许红外活性声子弛豫后的静态响应 $\epsilon_0$。前者来自电子密度在固定核坐标下的线性响应；后者还包含离子位移通过有效电荷与电场耦合的贡献。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For a finite system a uniform electric field can be represented by the potential $-e\mathbf E\cdot\mathbf r$, but the position operator does not preserve periodicity. In an infinite crystal, inserting this potential directly into a periodic Hamiltonian breaks ordinary Bloch boundary conditions. Dielectric response can instead be obtained from a finite-wavevector limit, interband position matrix elements, Berry-phase polarization, or a finite-field energy functional. Chapter 24 develops Berry-phase polarization systematically; the present section keeps only the interface needed for dielectric tensors, Born effective charges, and long-range phonon terms.

The clamped-ion electronic response $\epsilon_\infty$ must be distinguished from the static response $\epsilon_0$ after infrared-active phonons are allowed to relax. The former is the electronic density response at fixed nuclei; the latter also includes ionic displacement coupled to the field through effective charges.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="带间位置矩阵元与速度/对易子 | Interband position matrix elements from a commutator"
  assumptions={[
    '考虑不同本征值的两个本征态；',
    'Hamiltonian 与位置对易子的矩阵元存在；',
    '非局域赝势贡献不被遗漏。',
  ]}
>

由

$$
\hat H|\psi_n\rangle=\varepsilon_n|\psi_n\rangle
$$

可得

$$
\langle\psi_m|[\hat H,\hat r_\alpha]|\psi_n\rangle
=
(\varepsilon_m-\varepsilon_n)
\langle\psi_m|\hat r_\alpha|\psi_n\rangle.
$$

因此对 $m\ne n$，

$$
\boxed{
\langle\psi_m|\hat r_\alpha|\psi_n\rangle
=
\frac{\langle\psi_m|[\hat H,\hat r_\alpha]|\psi_n\rangle}
{\varepsilon_m-\varepsilon_n}.
}
$$

对局域 Hamiltonian，$[H,r_\alpha]$ 与速度/动量算符相关；在原子单位、无矢势时，$[p^2/2,r_\alpha]=-ip_\alpha$。非局域赝势还贡献 $[V_{\mathrm{NL}},r_\alpha]$。只用动量矩阵元而忽略该项会破坏光学和有效电荷的一致性。

在简并子空间内分母为零，单个带的矩阵元不是合适对象；应在子空间内做协变导数、投影或退化微扰处理。这个等式处理的是带间位置矩阵元，不定义周期晶体的绝对位置期望值。

The commutator converts an off-diagonal position matrix element into a Hamiltonian/velocity matrix element divided by an energy difference. A nonlocal potential contributes its own commutator. Degenerate subspaces require covariant or projected treatment, and this relation does not define an absolute position expectation value in a periodic crystal.

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="Born 有效电荷是混合响应" titleEn="A Born effective charge is a mixed response" eyebrow="Definition">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

Born effective charge tensor 定义为固定宏观电场条件下，极化对一个原子子晶格位移的导数：

$$
\boxed{
Z^*_{s,\alpha\beta}
=
\Omega
\left.
\frac{\partial P_\alpha}
{\partial u_{s\beta}}
\right|_{\mathbf E=0}
}
$$

在能量中采用 $-\Omega\mathbf E\cdot\mathbf P$ 耦合时，Maxwell 关系给出等价的力响应

$$
Z^*_{s,\alpha\beta}
=
\left.
\frac{\partial F_{s\beta}}
{\partial E_\alpha}
\right|_{\mathbf u=0}
=
-\frac{\partial^2E}
{\partial E_\alpha\partial u_{s\beta}},
$$

具体符号取决于电荷和场耦合 convention。$Z^*$ 描述移动原子时跨晶胞输运的动态电荷，可显著偏离形式价态。它与 Bader、Mulliken 或静态差分电荷不是同一 observable；后者不能替代极化—位移或力—电场导数。

总平移不改变体极化（模 polarization quantum），因此绝缘体满足 charge-neutrality sum rule

$$
\sum_s Z^*_{s,\alpha\beta}=0.
$$

与 ASR 相同，这先是严格对称/电荷守恒关系，再是数值诊断。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The Born effective-charge tensor is defined as the derivative of polarization with respect to a sublattice displacement at fixed macroscopic electric field:

$$
\boxed{
Z^*_{s,\alpha\beta}
=
\Omega
\left.
\frac{\partial P_\alpha}
{\partial u_{s\beta}}
\right|_{\mathbf E=0}.
}
$$

With an energy coupling $-\Omega\mathbf E\cdot\mathbf P$, a Maxwell relation gives the equivalent force response

$$
Z^*_{s,\alpha\beta}
=
\left.
\frac{\partial F_{s\beta}}
{\partial E_\alpha}
\right|_{\mathbf u=0}
=
-\frac{\partial^2E}
{\partial E_\alpha\partial u_{s\beta}},
$$

up to the chosen charge/field sign convention. $Z^*$ measures the dynamic charge transported across the crystal by a sublattice displacement and can differ greatly from a formal valence. It is not a Bader, Mulliken, or static charge-difference observable, none of which replaces a polarization–displacement or force–field derivative.

A uniform translation does not change the bulk polarization modulo its quantum, giving the insulating charge-neutrality sum rule $\sum_s Z^*_{s,\alpha\beta}=0$. As with the ASR, this is first a symmetry/conservation identity and then a numerical diagnostic.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

长波纵向位移产生宏观极化电荷和电场。其对动力学矩阵的 leading nonanalytic contribution 可写为

$$
\boxed{
D^{\mathrm{NA}}_{s\alpha,s'\beta}(\mathbf q\to0)
=
\frac{4\pi}{\Omega\sqrt{M_sM_{s'}}}
\frac{
\left[\sum_\gamma q_\gamma Z^*_{s,\gamma\alpha}\right]
\left[\sum_\delta q_\delta Z^*_{s',\delta\beta}\right]

{\sum_{\gamma\delta}q_\gamma\epsilon_{\infty,\gamma\delta}q_\delta}

$$

（Gaussian/atomic-unit 型 convention；SI 单位需相应常数）。分子为沿 $\mathbf q$ 的模式有效电荷，分母为电子介电屏蔽。横向模式若 $\mathbf q\cdot Z^*\cdot\mathbf e=0$，不产生该宏观场；纵向极性模式则提高 $\omega^2$，形成方向依赖的 LO–TO splitting。

该项在 $\mathbf q=0$ 的值依赖宏观电边界与取极限方向，因此不能只从一个 Gamma 动力学矩阵无条件恢复。实际 Fourier 插值常把短程解析力常数与长程非解析项分开。二维材料的长波 Coulomb kernel 与三维体不同，不能直接使用含真空体积的三维公式而期待真空收敛。

The leading long-range contribution is a directional matrix built from Born effective charges and the clamped-ion dielectric tensor. It shifts longitudinal polar modes but not transverse modes with zero longitudinal mode charge. The Gamma limit depends on electrical boundary conditions and direction; short-range interpolation and the long-range nonanalytic term must be treated separately. Two-dimensional systems require a different long-wavelength Coulomb form.

</DerivationBlock>

同一二阶能量框架还产生：固定离子介电 susceptibility $\partial P/\partial E$；内部应变张量 $\partial F_s/\partial\eta$；clamped-ion 压电张量 $\partial P/\partial\eta$；以及原子弛豫后通过 $Z^*$、内部应变和力常数逆矩阵形成的离子贡献。柔软模式会放大 relaxed-ion response，但接近零频率时谐振逆矩阵也更敏感于数值误差、非谐性和边界条件。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The same second-order energy framework generates the clamped-ion dielectric susceptibility $\partial P/\partial E$, internal-strain tensor $\partial F_s/\partial\eta$, clamped-ion piezoelectric tensor $\partial P/\partial\eta$, and relaxed-ion contributions formed from $Z^*$, internal strain, and the inverse force-constant matrix. Soft modes can amplify a relaxed-ion response, but a nearly singular harmonic inverse is also more sensitive to numerical error, anharmonicity, and boundary conditions.

A dielectric or piezoelectric result must state whether ions are clamped or relaxed, strain or stress is fixed, the electrical boundary is $\mathbf E$ or $\mathbf D$, electronic and ionic contributions are included, and which unit/cell-volume convention is used. For a two-dimensional material, a three-dimensional dielectric constant based on arbitrary vacuum is not a unique material property; an areal polarizability or declared effective thickness is normally needed.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## 20.8 电子—声子相互作用与超导 | Electron–Phonon Interactions and Superconductivity

<BilingualSection titleZh="声子位移如何调制屏蔽电子势" titleEn="How a phonon modulates the screened electronic potential" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

DFPT 提供每个原子位移微扰的自洽屏蔽势 $\partial_{\mathbf q s\alpha}V_{\mathrm{KS}}$。将这些 Cartesian 响应按声子本征向量组合，并量子化正规坐标，得到电子从 $|n\mathbf k\rangle$ 散射到 $|m,\mathbf k+\mathbf q\rangle$ 的矩阵元。一个常用规范为

$$
\boxed{
 g_{mn\nu}(\mathbf k,\mathbf q)
=
\sqrt{\frac{\hbar}{2\omega_{\mathbf q\nu}}}
\sum_{s\alpha}
\frac{e_{s\alpha}^{\nu}(\mathbf q)}{\sqrt{M_s}}
\left\langle
\psi_{m,\mathbf k+\mathbf q}
\left|
\partial_{\mathbf q s\alpha}V_{\mathrm{KS}}
\right|
\psi_{n\mathbf k}
\right\rangle.
}
$$

若本征向量已包含质量因子、或正规坐标定义不同，前因子会改变。比较不同代码或数据库前必须统一：$k/q$ 权重、晶胞/超胞归一化、spin degeneracy、声子本征向量、频率单位和是否含零点位移因子。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

DFPT provides the self-consistent screened potential derivative $\partial_{\mathbf q s\alpha}V_{\mathrm{KS}}$ for each atomic perturbation. Combining Cartesian responses with a phonon eigenvector and quantizing the normal coordinate gives the matrix element for scattering an electron from $|n\mathbf k\rangle$ to $|m,\mathbf k+\mathbf q\rangle$. A common convention is

$$
\boxed{
 g_{mn\nu}(\mathbf k,\mathbf q)
=
\sqrt{\frac{\hbar}{2\omega_{\mathbf q\nu}}}
\sum_{s\alpha}
\frac{e_{s\alpha}^{\nu}(\mathbf q)}{\sqrt{M_s}}
\left\langle
\psi_{m,\mathbf k+\mathbf q}
\left|
\partial_{\mathbf q s\alpha}V_{\mathrm{KS}}
\right|
\psi_{n\mathbf k}
\right\rangle.
}
$$

The prefactor changes if eigenvectors already contain masses or the normal coordinate is defined differently. Before comparing codes or databases one must align $k/q$ weights, cell/supercell normalization, spin degeneracy, phonon eigenvectors, frequency units, and inclusion of the zero-point displacement factor.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="从矩阵元到模式耦合和 Eliashberg 谱 | From matrix elements to mode coupling and the Eliashberg spectrum"
  assumptions={[
    '金属 Fermi 面附近的绝热散射；',
    '采用声明的 spin、BZ 权重和 delta-function convention；',
    '声子频率为正且模式归一化一致；',
    '先讨论各向同性 Fermi-surface 平均。',
  ]}
>

电子—声子耦合对低能金属态的影响包含 Fermi 面双重约束。用 $N(E_F)$ 表示每个声明 spin/cell convention 下的态密度，一个常见模式分辨形式为

$$
\lambda_{\mathbf q\nu}
=
\frac{2}{N(E_F)\,\omega_{\mathbf q\nu}}
\sum_{mn}
\int_{\mathrm{BZ}}\frac{d\mathbf k}{\Omega_{\mathrm{BZ}}}
|g_{mn\nu}(\mathbf k,\mathbf q)|^2
\delta(\varepsilon_{n\mathbf k}-E_F)
\delta(\varepsilon_{m,\mathbf k+\mathbf q}-E_F),
$$

其中 prefactor 会随 $g$ 与 DOS 规范改变。Eliashberg 谱将模式贡献按频率分箱：

$$
\alpha^2F(\omega)
=
\frac{1}{2N_q}
\sum_{\mathbf q\nu}
\lambda_{\mathbf q\nu}
\omega_{\mathbf q\nu}
\delta(\omega-\omega_{\mathbf q\nu}).
$$

于是总耦合

$$
\boxed{
\lambda
=
2\int_0^\infty
\frac{\alpha^2F(\omega)}{\omega}\,d\omega
=
\frac{1}{N_q}
\sum_{\mathbf q\nu}\lambda_{\mathbf q\nu}
}
$$

并定义对数频率矩

$$
\boxed{
\ln\omega_{\log}
=
\frac{2}{\lambda}
\int_0^\infty
\frac{\alpha^2F(\omega)}{\omega}
\ln\omega\,d\omega.
}
$$

低频模式因 $1/\omega$ 权重可强烈增加 $\lambda$，但同时降低特征频率并放大非谐、结构不稳定、数值展宽和 Migdal/绝热假设风险。由此不能把“更软”单调等同于“更高 $T_c$”。

Mode-resolved coupling is a Fermi-surface double average of $|g|^2$ with a frequency normalization. The Eliashberg spectral function bins these contributions by phonon frequency. Its inverse-frequency moment gives $\lambda$, while a logarithmic moment gives $\omega_{\log}$. Soft modes can increase $lambda$ yet lower the frequency scale and invalidate harmonic or adiabatic assumptions; softness alone does not imply a higher critical temperature.

</DerivationBlock>

An isotropic Allen–Dynes/McMillan-type estimate is commonly written

$$
T_c
=
\frac{f_1f_2\omega_{\log}}{1.2}
\exp\left[
-\frac{1.04(1+\lambda)}
{\lambda-\mu^*(1+0.62\lambda)}
\right],
$$

where $\mu^*$ is a Coulomb pseudopotential and $f_1,f_2$ correct strong coupling and spectral shape. This is not a label inferred directly from bands or DOS; it is an approximation within Migdal–Eliashberg theory under isotropic and parameterized-Coulomb assumptions. Different $\mu^*$ values, spectral anisotropy, multiband structure, low carrier density, nonadiabatic effects, and strong anharmonicity can materially change the result.

A defensible $T_c$ estimate requires phonons stable and consistent with the target phase; convergence of $k$ and $q$ meshes, bands/windows, smearing or tetrahedra, interpolation, and delta-function integration; consistent $g$ and eigenvector conventions; stable $\alpha^2F$ and cumulative $\lambda$; sensitivity to $\mu^*$ or a more explicit Coulomb kernel; applicability of isotropy; and where needed comparison with anisotropic Eliashberg, anharmonic phonons, or nonadiabatic treatments.

## 20.9 磁振子与自旋响应函数 | Magnons and Spin Response Functions

<BilingualSection titleZh="冻结磁振子是绝热能量面上的自旋螺旋" titleEn="A frozen magnon is a spin spiral on an adiabatic energy surface" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

在共线磁基态附近，可约束局域磁矩方向形成波矢 $\mathbf q$ 的横向自旋螺旋，并计算总能 $E(\mathbf q,\theta)$。小倾角 $\theta$ 下的二阶能量可映射到有效交换 kernel；在局域 Heisenberg 极限，冻结螺旋色散与磁振子频率相关。无自旋轨道耦合时，广义 Bloch 定理可把自旋旋转与晶格平移结合，在原胞中处理不可公度螺旋。

该映射依赖绝热分离：电子与磁矩幅值足够快地跟随缓慢方向变化。若磁矩不刚性、存在强 Stoner particle–hole continuum、自旋轨道耦合或非共线多子晶格耦合，单一 Heisenberg 参数化和静态能量差可能不足。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Near a collinear magnetic ground state, local moments can be constrained into a transverse spin spiral of wavevector $\mathbf q$, and the total energy $E(\mathbf q,\theta)$ evaluated. At small cone angle $\theta$, the quadratic energy maps to an effective exchange kernel; in a localized Heisenberg limit, the frozen-spiral dispersion is related to magnon frequencies. Without spin–orbit coupling, a generalized Bloch theorem combines spin rotation with lattice translation, allowing an incommensurate spiral to be treated in the primitive cell.

This mapping relies on adiabatic separation: electronic degrees of freedom and moment magnitudes must follow slow directional changes. If moments are not rigid, a strong Stoner particle–hole continuum is present, spin–orbit coupling matters, or several noncollinear sublattices are coupled, a single Heisenberg parametrization and static energy difference may be insufficient.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="动态横向自旋响应与磁振子极点 | Dynamic transverse spin response and magnon poles"
  assumptions={[
    '围绕已定义的磁基态做小横向微扰；',
    '采用 retarded response；',
    '频率和 Fourier 符号在整节保持一致。',
  ]}
>

施加横向磁场 $\delta B^+(\mathbf q,\omega)$，诱导横向磁化

$$
\delta m^+(\mathbf q,\omega)
=
\chi^{+-}(\mathbf q,\omega)
\delta B^+(\mathbf q,\omega).
$$

retarded susceptibility 的极点给出集体自旋激发。若一个孤立弱阻尼模式可近似为

$$
\chi^{+-}(\mathbf q,\omega)
\approx
\frac{A_{\mathbf q}}
{\omega-\omega_{\mathbf q}+i\Gamma_{\mathbf q}},
$$

则 $\omega_{\mathbf q}$ 是峰位，$Gamma_{\mathbf q}$ 与寿命/线宽相关，$A_{\mathbf q}$ 是谱权重。可观测谱与

$$
-\operatorname{Im}\chi^{+-}(\mathbf q,\omega)
$$

相关，并通过 fluctuation–dissipation relation 与动态结构因子连接。一个清晰磁振子需要相对窄且可追踪的谱峰；只有静态 $chi(\mathbf q,0)$ 大或冻结螺旋能量低，并不能确定其线宽。

在 itinerant magnet 中，单粒子 spin-flip transitions 形成 Stoner continuum。集体极点进入 continuum 后可发生 Landau damping，峰变宽甚至不再是良定义准粒子。此时报告单条“magnon band”会丢失谱权重和阻尼信息。

The retarded transverse susceptibility maps a small transverse field to an induced transverse magnetization. An isolated weakly damped pole has a peak frequency, linewidth, and spectral weight. In an itinerant magnet, particle–hole spin-flip excitations form a Stoner continuum; a collective pole entering this continuum can broaden or cease to be a well-defined quasiparticle. A static frozen-spiral energy does not determine that linewidth.

</DerivationBlock>

=
\chi_0^{+-}
+

静态 adiabatic kernel 可以产生 Goldstone mode 和低能交换刚度，但频率依赖、多体顶角、自旋轨道耦合、无序和有限温涨落会改变峰位与线宽。Chapter 21 的 TDDFT 结构提供更一般的频率依赖框架；本节只建立声子/磁振子作为不同响应通道的对照。

Formally, spin response parallels density response: a Kohn–Sham spin-flip susceptibility $\chi_0^{+-}$ is dressed by an exchange–correlation spin kernel,

$$
\chi^{+-}
=
\chi_0^{+-}
+
\chi_0^{+-}K_{\mathrm{xc}}^{+-}\chi^{+-}.
$$

A static adiabatic kernel can produce a Goldstone mode and low-energy exchange stiffness, but frequency dependence, many-body vertices, spin–orbit coupling, disorder, and finite-temperature fluctuations affect peak positions and linewidths. The TDDFT structure of Chapter 21 provides the more general frequency-dependent framework; the present section establishes phonons and magnons as distinct response channels.
