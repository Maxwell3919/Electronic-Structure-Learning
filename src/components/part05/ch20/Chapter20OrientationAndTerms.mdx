## 阅读定位 | Reading Orientation

<BilingualSection titleZh="从有限轨迹转向平衡点附近的导数" titleEn="From finite trajectories to derivatives around equilibrium" eyebrow="Orientation">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

Chapter 19 通过电子结构力生成一条有限时间轨迹。Chapter 20 改变问题：先确定平衡构型，再研究总能、密度和自旋密度对微小位移或外部微扰的导数。力常数是总能对核位移的二阶导数；声子是谐振近似下质量加权 Hessian 的正规模；介电张量、Born 有效电荷和压电系数是电场、极化、位移或应变之间的混合响应；电子—声子矩阵元是 Kohn–Sham 有效势沿声子正规坐标的一阶屏蔽导数。

这些量共享同一基态电子结构，却不是能带图的不同读法。要从基态得到响应，需要定义微扰、边界条件、响应变量、规范、离散网格和目标 observable。Chapter 21 将把静态或绝热响应推广到显式频率依赖和光学激发；本章只在自旋谱与电子—声子散射需要时保留动力学频率。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Chapter 19 generated a finite-time trajectory from electronic-structure forces. Chapter 20 changes the question: after locating an equilibrium structure, it studies derivatives of the total energy, density, and spin density with respect to small displacements or external perturbations. Force constants are second derivatives of the total energy with respect to nuclear positions; phonons are the normal modes of the mass-weighted Hessian in the harmonic approximation; dielectric tensors, Born effective charges, and piezoelectric coefficients are mixed responses among field, polarization, displacement, and strain; and an electron–phonon matrix element is the screened first derivative of the Kohn–Sham effective potential along a phonon normal coordinate.

These quantities share a ground-state electronic structure but are not alternative readings of a band plot. Moving from a ground state to a response requires a defined perturbation, boundary condition, response variable, gauge, discretization, and target observable. Chapter 21 extends static or adiabatic response to explicitly frequency-dependent optical excitations; the present chapter retains a dynamic frequency only where spin spectra or electron–phonon scattering require it.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## 物理对象、证据层级与符号 | Physical Objects, Evidence Levels, and Notation

<BilingualSection titleZh="基态、响应与激发必须分开" titleEn="Ground state, response, and excitation must remain distinct" eyebrow="Objects">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

**基态对象**包括平衡核坐标 $\mathbf R_0$、基态密度 $n_0(\mathbf r)$、Kohn–Sham 轨道和总能 $E_0$。**静态响应对象**包括一阶密度 $\delta n$、屏蔽势 $\delta V_{\mathrm{KS}}$、力常数、介电与有效电荷。**谐振模式对象**包括动力学矩阵本征值 $\omega_{\mathbf q\nu}^2$ 和归一化本征向量。**散射与集体激发对象**包括电子—声子矩阵元、声子谱函数、动态自旋 susceptibility 的极点、峰位和线宽。

一个稳定 SCF 基态不保证 Hessian 正定；一个正定的已采样 Hessian 不保证全布里渊区稳定；一个稳定声子谱不保证电子—声子耦合显著；一个显著耦合也不自动保证常规超导。每一步都需要自己的计算对象和收敛判据。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

**Ground-state objects** include the equilibrium coordinates $\mathbf R_0$, ground-state density $n_0(\mathbf r)$, Kohn–Sham orbitals, and total energy $E_0$. **Static-response objects** include the first-order density $\delta n$, screened potential $\delta V_{\mathrm{KS}}$, force constants, dielectric response, and effective charges. **Harmonic-mode objects** include dynamical-matrix eigenvalues $\omega_{\mathbf q\nu}^2$ and normalized eigenvectors. **Scattering and collective-excitation objects** include electron–phonon matrix elements, phonon spectral functions, and poles, peak positions, and linewidths of the dynamic spin susceptibility.

A converged SCF ground state does not ensure a positive Hessian; a positive Hessian on sampled points does not ensure full-Brillouin-zone stability; a stable phonon spectrum does not ensure significant electron–phonon coupling; and significant coupling does not automatically establish conventional superconductivity. Every step has its own computational object and convergence criterion.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>
