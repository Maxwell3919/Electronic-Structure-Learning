## 20.5 变分表达 | Variational Expressions

<BilingualSection titleZh="线性方程与二次泛函是同一驻点问题" titleEn="A linear equation and a quadratic functional define the same stationary problem" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

投影 Sternheimer 方程可以直接迭代求解，也可以转化为对一阶轨道的二次变分问题。这个等价关系有两个用途：其一，成熟的电子结构最小化算法可以用于 response；其二，驻点处的二阶能量对一阶轨道误差具有二阶敏感性，为数值误差分析提供结构。

设投影空间中的 Hermitian 正定或经适当移位后正定算符为 $A$，右端为 $b$。线性方程

$$
A x=-b
$$

等价于使

$$
\mathcal F[x]
=
\frac12\langle x|A|x\rangle
+\operatorname{Re}\langle x|b\rangle
$$

达到驻点。DFPT 的一阶轨道、密度 kernel 和正交约束使实际泛函更复杂，但数学结构相同。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The projected Sternheimer equation may be solved directly or recast as a quadratic variational problem for the first-order orbitals. The equivalence has two uses: established electronic minimization algorithms can be applied to response, and the stationary second-order energy has second-order sensitivity to errors in the first-order orbitals, providing a useful numerical error structure.

For a Hermitian positive operator $A$ in the projected space—or one made positive by an allowed shift—and right-hand side $b$, the equation

$$
A x=-b
$$

is equivalent to stationarity of

$$
\mathcal F[x]
=
\frac12\langle x|A|x\rangle
+\operatorname{Re}\langle x|b\rangle.
$$

The actual DFPT functional includes first-order orbitals, density kernels, and orthogonality constraints, but retains this mathematical structure.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="二次泛函的驻点、残差和误差 | Stationarity, residual, and error of a quadratic functional"
  assumptions={[
    'A 在求解子空间内 Hermitian；',
    '零模已通过投影、约束或正移位处理；',
    '内积和复变量 convention 保持一致。',
  ]}
>

对任意变分 $|\delta x\rangle$，

$$
\delta\mathcal F
=
\operatorname{Re}
\langle\delta x|Ax+b\rangle.
$$

要求所有允许 $\delta x$ 下 $\delta\mathcal F=0$，得到

$$
Ax+b=0.
$$

令精确驻点为 $x_*$，近似解为 $x=x_*+e$。由于 $Ax_*=-b$，

$$
\mathcal F[x]-\mathcal F[x_*]
=
\frac12\langle e|A|e\rangle.
$$

一阶误差项消失，因此泛函值误差为 $O(e^2)$。但线性残差

$$
r=Ax+b=Ae
$$

仍为 $O(e)$。这说明二阶能量看似稳定时，一阶轨道、诱导密度或某些非驻定 observable 仍可能未收敛。实际停止条件应同时监控残差、响应密度和目标力常数/介电量，而不是只看泛函变化。

Variation of the quadratic functional yields the linear equation. Expanding around the stationary solution cancels the first-order functional error, whereas the algebraic residual remains first order in the solution error. A stable second-order energy alone is therefore not a complete response-convergence test.

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="DFPT 二阶能量泛函的组成" titleEn="Structure of the DFPT second-order energy functional" eyebrow="Supplemental derivation">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

对绝缘体的静态微扰，二阶电子能量可组织为：一阶轨道在 $(H^{(0)}-\varepsilon_n)$ 上的二次项；一阶轨道与外部微扰的线性耦合；诱导密度通过 Hartree—XC kernel 的二次项；外势和离子能的显式二阶项；以及维持正交归一与 occupied-space gauge 的约束项。符号化地，

$$
E^{(2)}[\{\psi_n^{(1)}\}]
=
\sum_n\langle\psi_n^{(1)}|
(H^{(0)}-\varepsilon_n)|\psi_n^{(1)}\rangle
+
2\operatorname{Re}\sum_n
\langle\psi_n^{(1)}|Q_cV_{\mathrm{ext}}^{(1)}|\psi_n^{(0)}\rangle
+
\frac12\iint n^{(1)}K_{\mathrm{Hxc}}n^{(1)}
+
E_{\mathrm{explicit}}^{(2)}.
$$

精确 prefactor 会随自旋、占据、复 $\mathbf q$ 微扰和正负 $\mathbf q$ 配对 convention 改变；页面不把该符号式当作代码接口。对一阶轨道变分得到自洽投影 Sternheimer 方程，对外部参数再取混合导数得到力常数、介电或有效电荷。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For a static perturbation of an insulator, the second-order electronic energy can be organized into a quadratic term in first-order orbitals with $(H^{(0)}-\varepsilon_n)$, a linear coupling to the external perturbation, a quadratic induced-density term through the Hartree–XC kernel, explicit second derivatives of the external and ionic terms, and constraints that maintain orthonormality and the occupied-space gauge. Schematically,

$$
E^{(2)}[\{\psi_n^{(1)}\}]
=
\sum_n\langle\psi_n^{(1)}|
(H^{(0)}-\varepsilon_n)|\psi_n^{(1)}\rangle
+
2\operatorname{Re}\sum_n
\langle\psi_n^{(1)}|Q_cV_{\mathrm{ext}}^{(1)}|\psi_n^{(0)}\rangle
+
\frac12\iint n^{(1)}K_{\mathrm{Hxc}}n^{(1)}
+
E_{\mathrm{explicit}}^{(2)}.
$$

Exact prefactors depend on spin, occupations, complex-$\mathbf q$ conventions, and pairing of $\mathbf q$ and $-\mathbf q$; this schematic form is not a program interface. Variation with respect to the first-order orbitals yields the self-consistent projected Sternheimer equation, while mixed derivatives with external parameters give force constants, dielectric response, or effective charges.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="2n+1 定理的实际含义" titleEn="Practical meaning of the 2n+1 theorem" eyebrow="【Martin Appendix D | Supplemental boundary】">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

对满足变分驻点的能量泛函，知道波函数到 $n$ 阶通常足以得到能量导数到 $2n+1$ 阶。$n=0$ 时，基态波函数给出一阶 Hellmann–Feynman 导数；$n=1$ 时，一阶响应可以构造二阶乃至三阶能量导数，而无需显式求二阶波函数。这个结果解释了为何一阶 DFPT 可用于二阶力常数，并可进一步进入某些三阶系数。

该定理不消除物理近似和实现项。有限基组、非局域赝势、占据变化、金属 Fermi 面、应变坐标、Berry-phase 电场和不完全驻定都会改变需要保留的显式项与约束。它也不表示三阶量与二阶量具有相同的网格或数值收敛速度。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For an energy functional evaluated at a variational stationary point, knowledge of the wavefunction through order $n$ generally suffices for energy derivatives through order $2n+1$. At $n=0$, the ground-state wavefunction gives first-order Hellmann–Feynman derivatives; at $n=1$, first-order response can construct second- and in suitable cases third-order energy derivatives without an explicit second-order wavefunction. This explains why first-order DFPT yields second-order force constants and can enter selected third-order coefficients.

The theorem does not remove physical approximations or implementation terms. Finite bases, nonlocal pseudopotentials, occupation changes, metallic Fermi surfaces, strain coordinates, Berry-phase fields, and incomplete stationarity alter the explicit terms and constraints. Nor does it imply identical numerical convergence for second- and third-order quantities.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="变分稳定不等于 observable 已收敛" titleEn="Variational stability is not observable convergence" kind="boundary">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>二阶能量对一阶轨道误差可呈二阶稳定，但声子本征值会放大接近简并模式间的小矩阵误差；EPC 矩阵元又对规范、Fermi 面和插值更敏感。应直接测试最终动力学矩阵、本征向量、有效电荷或 EPC observable 的收敛，而不能从响应泛函变化很小推出全部派生量可靠。</p>
  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>The second-order energy may be quadratically stable with respect to first-order orbital error, yet phonon eigenvectors can amplify small matrix errors near degeneracies, and EPC matrix elements are more sensitive to gauge, Fermi-surface sampling, and interpolation. Convergence must be tested directly for the dynamical matrix, eigenvectors, effective charges, or EPC observable; a small change in the response functional does not validate every derived quantity.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 20.6 周期微扰与声子色散 | Periodic Perturbations and Phonon Dispersion Curves

<BilingualSection titleZh="波矢 q 把无限晶体响应分解为独立块" titleEn="Wavevector q block-diagonalizes the response of an infinite crystal" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

基态晶体 Hamiltonian 对晶格平移不变。若外部微扰具有波矢 $\mathbf q$，可写成

$$
V_{\mathrm{ext}}^{(1,\mathbf q)}(\mathbf r)
=
e^{i\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf r}
\bar V_{\mathrm{ext}}^{(1,\mathbf q)}(\mathbf r),
$$

其中 $\bar V$ 对原胞周期。线性响应不会混合无关波矢，因此诱导密度和屏蔽势具有同一 $\mathbf q$：

$$
n^{(1,\mathbf q)}(\mathbf r)
=
e^{i\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf r}
\bar n^{(1,\mathbf q)}(\mathbf r).
$$

这允许在一个原胞内处理任意指定 $\mathbf q$ 的响应，而无需构造波长相同的真实空间超胞。不同 $\mathbf q$ 的 DFPT 问题可以独立求解，随后组成均匀 $q$ 网格、色散路径或 Fourier 插值所需数据。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The ground-state crystal Hamiltonian is invariant under lattice translations. An external perturbation with wavevector $\mathbf q$ can be written

$$
V_{\mathrm{ext}}^{(1,\mathbf q)}(\mathbf r)
=
e^{i\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf r}
\bar V_{\mathrm{ext}}^{(1,\mathbf q)}(\mathbf r),
$$

where $\bar V$ is primitive-cell periodic. Linear response does not mix unrelated wavevectors, so the induced density and screened potential carry the same $\mathbf q$:

$$
n^{(1,\mathbf q)}(\mathbf r)
=
e^{i\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf r}
\bar n^{(1,\mathbf q)}(\mathbf r).
$$

A response at an arbitrary specified $\mathbf q$ can therefore be treated in one primitive cell without a real-space supercell of the same wavelength. Independent DFPT problems at different $\mathbf q$ values can be assembled into a uniform mesh, a dispersion path, or data for Fourier interpolation.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="k 到 k+q 的一阶轨道响应 | First-order orbital response from k to k+q"
  assumptions={[
    '基态轨道采用 Bloch 形式；',
    '微扰只含一个 q 分量；',
    '线性响应，忽略不同 q 分量之间的非线性混频。',
  ]}
>

基态轨道为

$$
\psi_{n\mathbf k}^{(0)}(\mathbf r)
=
e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf r}u_{n\mathbf k}^{(0)}(\mathbf r).
$$

$\mathbf q$ 微扰作用后，一阶轨道属于 $\mathbf k+\mathbf q$ Bloch 扇区：

$$
\psi_{n\mathbf k}^{(1,\mathbf q)}(\mathbf r)
=
e^{i(\mathbf k+\mathbf q)\cdot\mathbf r}
\bar u_{n\mathbf k}^{(1,\mathbf q)}(\mathbf r).
$$

把平面波因子移入 Hamiltonian，定义

$$
H_{\mathbf k+\mathbf q}^{(0)}
=
e^{-i(\mathbf k+\mathbf q)\cdot\mathbf r}
H^{(0)}
 e^{i(\mathbf k+\mathbf q)\cdot\mathbf r},
$$

则 cell-periodic Sternheimer 方程为

$$
\boxed{
Q_{c,\mathbf k+\mathbf q}
\left(H_{\mathbf k+\mathbf q}^{(0)}-\varepsilon_{n\mathbf k}^{(0)}\right)
Q_{c,\mathbf k+\mathbf q}
|\bar u_{n\mathbf k}^{(1,\mathbf q)}\rangle
=
-Q_{c,\mathbf k+\mathbf q}
\bar V_{\mathrm{KS}}^{(1,\mathbf q)}
|u_{n\mathbf k}^{(0)}\rangle.
}
$$

密度响应由所有 $\mathbf k$ 的 $u_{n\mathbf k}^{(0)*}\bar u_{n\mathbf k}^{(1,\mathbf q)}$ 组成。数值实现中的 $\mathbf k+\mathbf q$ 需按 reciprocal lattice 折回第一布里渊区，并保持 band/subspace gauge 一致。若 $\mathbf q$ 不与所选 $k$ 网格闭合，代码可能需要移位网格、插值或独立波函数集合；这是一项离散兼容性问题，而不是物理禁止。

A perturbation at $\mathbf q$ maps a Bloch state at $\mathbf k$ into the $\mathbf k+\mathbf q$ sector. Removing the Bloch phases yields a primitive-cell-periodic projected Sternheimer equation. Numerical implementations must fold $\mathbf k+\mathbf q$ consistently and maintain the subspace gauge; incompatibility with a chosen discrete mesh is a representation issue, not a physical selection rule.

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="从 q 网格到实空间插值" titleEn="From a q mesh to real-space interpolation" eyebrow="Numerical Fourier pair">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

在一组均匀 $\mathbf q$ 点得到 $D(\mathbf q)$ 后，可做离散逆 Fourier 变换获得与该网格对应的实空间力常数，再变换到更密 $\mathbf q$ 网格或路径。这个步骤假定网格对应超胞之外的有效力常数可忽略或已单独处理。插值误差常表现为远程力常数截断、低频支振荡、ASR 违反或一般 $\mathbf q$ 处伪软模。

验证应包括：加密原始 $q$ 网格；比较直接计算的额外一般 $\mathbf q$ 点；检查实空间力常数随距离衰减；分离极性长程项；对二维体系采用适当 Coulomb 截断/非解析 convention；对金属测试电子 $k$ 网格与 smearing 对每个 $q$ 的敏感性。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

After evaluating $D(\mathbf q)$ on a uniform mesh, a discrete inverse Fourier transform gives real-space force constants associated with that mesh, which can then be transformed to a denser mesh or path. The procedure assumes that effective force constants beyond the corresponding supercell are negligible or treated separately. Interpolation errors can appear as truncated long-range tails, oscillating low-frequency branches, ASR violations, or spurious soft modes at general $\mathbf q$.

Validation should refine the original $q$ mesh, compare extra directly calculated general-$\mathbf q$ points, inspect real-space decay, separate polar long-range terms, use an appropriate Coulomb truncation/nonanalytic convention in two dimensions, and test electronic $k$ sampling and smearing for every relevant metallic $\mathbf q$.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="直接超胞法与 DFPT 的可比对象" titleEn="Comparable objects in supercell and DFPT methods" eyebrow="Cross-validation">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

在相同基态方法、结构、边界和谐振极限下，有限位移与 DFPT 应收敛到同一二阶能量 Hessian。可比对象是同一 convention 下的力常数或动力学矩阵，而不是未对齐相位、质量、非解析项和 ASR 处理的声子图。独立实现的对照可以发现力实现、响应 kernel、超胞截断或 Fourier convention 错误。

两种方法给出相近高对称路径并不自动构成全区独立验证：它们可能共用同一赝势、泛函、基态结构和电子网格偏差。更强的验证需要预先定义误差标准，并覆盖一般 $q$、本征向量、长程项和目标 observable。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

With the same ground-state method, structure, boundary conditions, and harmonic limit, finite displacement and DFPT should converge to the same second-order energy Hessian. The comparable object is a force-constant or dynamical matrix under a common convention—not two plots with unaligned phases, masses, nonanalytic terms, and ASR treatments. Cross-implementation comparison can expose errors in forces, response kernels, supercell truncation, or Fourier conventions.

Agreement on a high-symmetry path is not automatically an independent full-zone validation: the methods may share the same pseudopotential, functional, structure, and electronic sampling bias. Stronger validation requires a predefined error criterion and coverage of general $q$, eigenvectors, long-range terms, and the target observable.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="q 点状态必须逐层描述" titleEn="q-point status must be described in layers" kind="practice">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>应分别记录：某个 q 的线性方程是否完成；该 q 的动力学矩阵是否数值收敛；一组 q 是否足以插值；高对称路径是否无虚频；全区是否有足够证据排除一般 q 不稳定；有限温非谐稳定是否另有计算支持。前一层不能自动推出后一层。</p>
  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>Record separately whether the linear solve completed at a q point; the dynamical matrix at that q is numerically converged; a q mesh is sufficient for interpolation; a high-symmetry path is free of imaginary modes; the full zone is adequately sampled to exclude a general-q instability; and finite-temperature anharmonic stability has separate support. Each earlier layer is weaker than the next.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualCallout>
