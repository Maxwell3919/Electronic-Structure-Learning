## 21.7 分子与团簇的光学性质 | Optical Properties of Molecules and Clusters

<BilingualSection titleZh="有限体系的谱从极化率得到" titleEn="Finite-system spectra follow from the polarizability" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

对分子或尺寸远小于光波长的团簇，偶极极化率是自然响应量。给定偏振单位向量 $\mathbf e$，定义

$$
\alpha_{\mathbf e\mathbf e}(\omega)
=\mathbf e\cdot\boldsymbol\alpha(\omega)\cdot\mathbf e.
$$

在本章的 $e^{-i\omega t}$ retarded convention 下，正频率的 $\mathrm{Im}\,\alpha_{\mathbf e\mathbf e}(\omega)$ 给出吸收谱权。截面、molar extinction、strength function 和 oscillator-strength distribution 之间含有单位制、方向平均和体积/粒子数约定，因此代码输出的纵轴不能只写 “intensity”。

分子的离散激发在理想孤立模型中产生 delta peaks；实际计算用有限 $\eta$ 或有限传播时间把它们表示为有限宽峰。团簇随着尺寸增长会出现密集单粒子跃迁、形状和表面效应，也可能形成集体电子振荡。一个宽强峰可以具有 plasmon-like 集体性质，但峰形仍受离子结构、对称性、电子数和离散跃迁影响；把团簇替换为均匀背景只能是受限模型。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For a molecule or cluster much smaller than the optical wavelength, the dipole polarizability is the natural response. For a polarization unit vector $\mathbf e$, define

$$
\alpha_{\mathbf e\mathbf e}(\omega)
=\mathbf e\cdot\boldsymbol\alpha(\omega)\cdot\mathbf e.
$$

Under the chapter's retarded $e^{-i\omega t}$ convention, $\mathrm{Im}\,\alpha_{\mathbf e\mathbf e}(\omega)$ at positive frequency carries absorption weight. Cross section, molar extinction, strength function, and oscillator-strength distribution differ by unit-system, orientational-average, and volume/particle-number factors, so a code-output axis should not be labelled merely “intensity”.

Discrete excitations of an ideal isolated molecule produce delta peaks; a calculation represents them by finite $\eta$ or a finite propagation time. As cluster size grows, single-particle transitions become dense and shape and surface effects enter; collective electronic oscillations may also form. A broad intense peak can be plasmon-like, but its shape still depends on ionic structure, symmetry, electron count, and discrete transitions. Replacing the cluster by a uniform background is therefore a controlled model, not an identity.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="振子强度与 f-sum rule | Oscillator strength and the f-sum rule"
  assumptions={[
    '非相对论电子、局域标量势和固定核；',
    '完整的中性激发集合；',
    '讨论一个 Cartesian 方向，方向平均另行处理；',
    '非局域赝势、有限基组和 periodic current 会改变实现层面的 commutator 处理。',
  ]}
>

对真实多体基态 $|0\rangle$ 和中性激发 $|S\rangle$，方向 $x$ 的 dimensionless oscillator strength 可定义为

$$
 f_S^{(x)}
 =
 \frac{2m_e(E_S-E_0)}{\hbar^2}
 \left|\left\langle 0\left|\sum_{a=1}^{N_e}x_a\right|S\right\rangle\right|^2.
$$

利用完备性与双重对易子，

$$
\sum_S(E_S-E_0)|\langle0|\hat X|S\rangle|^2
=
\frac12\langle0|[\hat X,[\hat H,\hat X]]|0\rangle.
$$

当 $\hat H=\sum_a\hat p_a^2/(2m_e)+V(\{\mathbf r\})$ 且 $V$ 与位置对易时，

$$
[\hat X,[\hat H,\hat X]]
=
\frac{\hbar^2}{m_e}N_e,
$$

于是 Thomas–Reiche–Kuhn 关系为

$$
\boxed{\sum_S f_S^{(x)}=N_e.}
$$

连续谱时求和改为离散与积分之和。数值上 sum rule 同时诊断基组/网格、高能响应空间、矩阵元、传播时间和非局域 operator correction。接近 $N_e$ 是必要一致性检查，但不能单独验证低能峰位或激子物理；不同误差可在总积分中抵消。

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="尺寸、结构与谱的证据边界" titleEn="Evidence boundary for size, structure, and spectra" eyebrow="Finite systems">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

小分子和小团簇的最低激发常表现出量子限域：几何尺寸改变轨道间隔、screening 与表面态。但把某个最强峰直接定义为 “band gap” 会混合至少四个量：静态 KS/GKS gap、addition/removal gap、最低中性激发和实验上可见的强吸收 onset。低能 dark excitation 可能位于第一个 bright peak 以下。

频率域 Casida/TDDFPT 与实时间 kick 可以在相同泛函、基组和线性区内给出同一极化率。它们的数值误差分布不同：前者受 transition/response subspace 和线性求解影响，后者受时间步、总时间、窗口与边界影响。方法间一致是高价值交叉检查，但只有在 observable、broadening 和归一化一致时才有意义。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The lowest excitations of small molecules and clusters often show quantum confinement: size changes orbital spacing, screening, and surface states. Calling the strongest peak a “band gap” conflates at least four quantities: the static KS/GKS gap, addition/removal gap, lowest neutral excitation, and experimentally visible strong-absorption onset. A low-energy dark excitation may lie below the first bright peak.

Frequency-domain Casida/TDDFPT and a real-time kick can produce the same polarizability for the same functional, basis, and linear regime. Their numerical errors are distributed differently: the former depends on the transition/response subspace and linear solves, while the latter depends on time step, total time, window, and boundaries. Agreement is a high-value cross-check only when the observable, broadening, and normalization are matched.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## 21.8 晶体的光学性质 | Optical Properties of Crystals

<BilingualSection titleZh="宏观光学量来自微观响应的消元" titleEn="A macroscopic optical quantity follows from eliminating microscopic fields" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 + Appendix E | Martin source + Appendix E】">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

晶体中的外场具有 macroscopic 分量，但电子密度在原胞内不均匀，诱导势会产生 reciprocal vectors $\mathbf G\ne0$ 的 microscopic 分量。对给定 $\mathbf q,\omega$，介电对象是矩阵

$$
\epsilon_{\mathbf G\mathbf G'}(\mathbf q,\omega),
$$

而不是单个数。外部宏观场可通过 microscopic fields 激发局域密度，再反馈到 macroscopic current；这就是 local-field effect。

在纵向 $q\to0$ 表述的一种常用 convention 中，宏观介电函数为

$$
\boxed{
\epsilon_M(\omega)
=
\frac{1}{\epsilon^{-1}_{00}(\mathbf q\to0,\omega)}.
}
$$

关键运算顺序是先对完整 $G,G'$ 矩阵求逆，再取 head。一般

$$
\frac{1}{\epsilon_{00}}
e(\epsilon^{-1})_{00}.
$$

删除 off-diagonal wings/bodies 得到 “no local fields” 近似。比较两者时必须保持同一 $k$ 网格、band window、kernel 和 broadening。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The external field in a crystal has a macroscopic component, but the electron density is inhomogeneous within the cell, so the induced potential develops microscopic components at reciprocal vectors $\mathbf G\ne0$. For a specified $\mathbf q,\omega$, the dielectric object is a matrix

$$
\epsilon_{\mathbf G\mathbf G'}(\mathbf q,\omega),
$$

not a single number. An external macroscopic field can excite local density through microscopic fields that feed back into the macroscopic current; this is the local-field effect.

In one common longitudinal $q\to0$ convention, the macroscopic dielectric function is

$$
\boxed{
\epsilon_M(\omega)
=
\frac{1}{\epsilon^{-1}_{00}(\mathbf q\to0,\omega)}.
}
$$

The order of operations is essential: invert the full $G,G'$ matrix before extracting its head. In general,

$$
\frac{1}{\epsilon_{00}}
e(\epsilon^{-1})_{00}.
$$

Dropping off-diagonal wings and body terms gives a “no local fields” approximation. A comparison must retain the same $k$ mesh, band window, kernel, and broadening.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="起始 gap、continuum edge 与光学 onset" titleEn="Starting gap, continuum edge, and optical onset" eyebrow="Gap boundary">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

独立粒子 interband response 的第一个允许跃迁由 KS/GKS transition energies 和矩阵元决定。绝热 semilocal TDDFT 在同一轨道起点上构造 response matrix；它可以移动和混合离散跃迁，但不会普遍把错误的起始 band gap 转换成正确 quasiparticle gap。对于晶体，连续电子—空穴对的 threshold 与起始 single-particle spectrum 紧密相关。

因此至少要区分

$$
E_g^{\mathrm{KS/GKS}},
\qquad
E_g^{\mathrm{QP}},
\qquad
E_{\mathrm{opt}},
\qquad
E_b=E_g^{\mathrm{QP}}-E_{\mathrm{opt}}.
$$

只有当 continuum edge 明确对应 quasiparticle gap 时，$E_b$ 才是物理激子结合能。若用 KS/GKS gap 作为 edge，得到的是相对于该起点的模型 binding，不能直接与实验差值比较。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The first allowed transition of an independent-particle interband response is set by KS/GKS transition energies and matrix elements. Adiabatic semilocal TDDFT constructs a response matrix on the same orbital starting point; it can move and mix discrete transitions, but does not generally turn an incorrect starting band gap into the correct quasiparticle gap. In a crystal, the continuum threshold of electron–hole pairs remains closely tied to the starting single-particle spectrum.

At least four quantities must therefore remain distinct:

$$
E_g^{\mathrm{KS/GKS}},
\qquad
E_g^{\mathrm{QP}},
\qquad
E_{\mathrm{opt}},
\qquad
E_b=E_g^{\mathrm{QP}}-E_{\mathrm{opt}}.
$$

Only when the continuum edge is explicitly the quasiparticle gap does $E_b$ have the physical meaning of an exciton binding energy. If a KS/GKS gap is used as the edge, the result is binding relative to that model starting point and cannot be compared directly with an experimental difference.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="bound exciton 是 response pole 而不是单条能带 | A bound exciton is a response pole, not one band"
  assumptions={[
    '清晰定义一个 electron-hole continuum edge E_g；',
    '线性中性响应；',
    '忽略 phonon-assisted、disorder 和 finite-temperature broadening；',
    '用有效 transition-space Hamiltonian 表示电子—空穴相互作用。',
  ]}
>

在跃迁基 $|v c\mathbf k\rangle$ 中，中性激发方程可抽象写为

$$
\sum_{v'c'\mathbf k'}
\left[
(\epsilon_{c\mathbf k}-\epsilon_{v\mathbf k})\delta_{vv'}\delta_{cc'}\delta_{\mathbf k\mathbf k'}
+K^{eh}_{vc\mathbf k,v'c'\mathbf k'}
\right]A^S_{v'c'\mathbf k'}
=
\Omega_S A^S_{vc\mathbf k}.
$$

若吸引 channel 足够强，使

$$
\Omega_S&lt;E_g,
$$

该 pole 位于自由 electron-hole continuum 以下，定义 binding

$$
E_b=E_g-\Omega_S&gt;0.
$$

激子态是许多 $v c\mathbf k$ 跃迁的相干叠加 $A^S$。它既不是 conduction band 中一条新 KS band，也不能从 electron density alone 的静态图直接识别。光学可见性还要求

$$
\sum_{vc\mathbf k}A^S_{vc\mathbf k}d_{vc\mathbf k}
e0.
$$

在 extended solid 中，产生长程 bound exciton 通常要求 kernel 在小 $q$ 处具有足够强的长期程结构。局域/短程 adiabatic LDA、GGA 以及只保留短程 exact exchange 的 screened hybrid 通常缺失这一结构。带长期程 exact exchange 的 range-separated route 可以构造吸引 pole，但参数、screening 和起始 gap 仍需独立验证。

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="周期实时间电流路线" titleEn="Periodic real-time current route" eyebrow="Real time in solids">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

周期晶体可用均匀矢势的阶跃或短脉冲改变 crystal momentum，并在周期 Hamiltonian 下传播 occupied Bloch orbitals。记录宏观电流

$$
\mathbf J(t)=\frac{1}{\Omega}\int_\Omega d\mathbf r\,\mathbf j(\mathbf r,t)
$$

并 Fourier 变换得到 $\sigma(\omega)$ 与 $\epsilon(\omega)$。这一实现必须一致处理 diamagnetic current、nonlocal pseudopotential/hybrid velocity、$k$ 点权重和 time-dependent gauge。

实时间与频率域晶体 TDDFT 在相同 kernel 和线性极限中应给出同一 macroscopic response，但局域场提取和 longitudinal/transverse convention 必须一致。只比较图形峰位而不比较复介电函数、sum rule 和低频极限，不足以确认两条路线等价。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A periodic crystal can be driven by a step or short pulse in a spatially uniform vector potential, shifting crystal momentum while occupied Bloch orbitals are propagated under a periodic Hamiltonian. Record the macroscopic current

$$
\mathbf J(t)=\frac{1}{\Omega}\int_\Omega d\mathbf r\,\mathbf j(\mathbf r,t)
$$

and Fourier transform it to obtain $\sigma(\omega)$ and $\epsilon(\omega)$. The implementation must treat diamagnetic current, nonlocal pseudopotential/hybrid velocity, $k$-point weights, and the time-dependent gauge consistently.

Real-time and frequency-domain crystal TDDFT should produce the same macroscopic response for the same kernel in the linear limit, but local-field extraction and longitudinal/transverse conventions must match. Comparing only visual peak positions without the complex dielectric function, sum rule, and low-frequency limit is insufficient to establish equivalence.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>
