## 21.4 时间依赖 DFT 与线性响应 | Time-Dependent DFT and Linear Response

<BilingualSection titleZh="独立粒子响应必须经过诱导势自洽" titleEn="The independent-particle response must be closed through the induced potential" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

§21.3 的 $\chi_0$ 描述固定 Kohn–Sham Hamiltonian 对有效势变化的响应。真实 TDDFT 线性响应中，外场改变密度，密度又改变 Hartree 和 exchange–correlation 势。写成算符卷积，

$$
\delta n=\chi_0\,\delta v_{\mathrm{eff}},
\qquad
\delta v_{\mathrm{eff}}
=
\delta v_{\mathrm{ext}}+f_{\mathrm{Hxc}}\,\delta n,
$$

其中

$$
f_{\mathrm{Hxc}}(1,2)
=
\frac{e^2}{|\mathbf r_1-\mathbf r_2|}\delta(t_1-t_2)
+f_{\mathrm{xc}}(1,2).
$$

这里的 $1,2$ 同时代表空间、自旋和时间变量。把两式联立得到相互作用响应；Hartree 项提供经典诱导场，$f_{\mathrm{xc}}$ 必须补偿独立粒子系统与真实相互作用系统之间剩余的动态差别。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The $\chi_0$ of §21.3 describes the response of a fixed Kohn–Sham Hamiltonian to a change in its effective potential. In the true TDDFT linear response, an external field changes the density, and that density changes the Hartree and exchange–correlation potentials. In operator-convolution notation,

$$
\delta n=\chi_0\,\delta v_{\mathrm{eff}},
\qquad
\delta v_{\mathrm{eff}}
=
\delta v_{\mathrm{ext}}+f_{\mathrm{Hxc}}\,\delta n,
$$

where

$$
f_{\mathrm{Hxc}}(1,2)
=
\frac{e^2}{|\mathbf r_1-\mathbf r_2|}\delta(t_1-t_2)
+f_{\mathrm{xc}}(1,2).
$$

The labels $1,2$ include space, spin, and time variables. Combining the equations gives the interacting response. The Hartree term supplies the classical induced field, while $f_{\mathrm{xc}}$ must provide the remaining dynamic difference between the auxiliary independent-particle system and the interacting system.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="Dyson 方程与极点条件 | Dyson equation and pole condition"
  assumptions={[
    '围绕稳定基态做一阶线性化；',
    'retarded Fourier 变换，所有算符乘积隐含空间/自旋积分；',
    '逆算符只在所选离散响应子空间中定义。',
  ]}
>

代入 $\delta v_{\mathrm{eff}}$：

$$
\delta n
=
\chi_0\delta v_{\mathrm{ext}}
+
\chi_0 f_{\mathrm{Hxc}}\delta n.
$$

移项后

$$
\left(1-\chi_0 f_{\mathrm{Hxc}}\right)\delta n
=
\chi_0\delta v_{\mathrm{ext}},
$$

因此

$$
\boxed{
\chi(\omega)
=
\left[1-\chi_0(\omega)f_{\mathrm{Hxc}}(\omega)\right]^{-1}\chi_0(\omega)
}
$$

或等价地

$$
\chi=\chi_0+\chi_0f_{\mathrm{Hxc}}\chi.
$$

非平凡无外场解满足

$$
\det\left[1-\chi_0(\omega)f_{\mathrm{Hxc}}(\omega)\right]=0,
$$

这些频率是相互作用响应的 collective poles。由于矩阵不交换，$[1-\chi_0f]^{-1}\chi_0$ 与 $\chi_0[1-f\chi_0]^{-1}$ 的顺序必须与定义一致，不能把标量代数直接搬到离散矩阵实现。

The poles of $\chi_0$ are independent Kohn–Sham transitions. The poles of $\chi$ follow after self-consistent coupling through $f_{\mathrm{Hxc}}$: they can shift, split, mix, become bright or dark, and acquire a different macroscopic weight. A kernel is therefore not a post-processing rigid shift of every line.

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="从空间响应到跃迁空间" titleEn="From spatial response to transition space" eyebrow="Transition basis">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

用基态轨道展开一阶密度矩阵，

$$
\delta n_\sigma(\mathbf r,\omega)
=
\sum_{ij}
\varphi^*_{i\sigma}(\mathbf r)
\,\delta\rho_{ij\sigma}(\omega)\,
\varphi_{j\sigma}(\mathbf r).
$$

固定有效势下，密度矩阵元素含有 occupation difference 和能量分母：

$$
\delta\rho_{ij\sigma}^{0}(\omega)
=
\frac{f_{i\sigma}-f_{j\sigma}}
{\omega-(\epsilon_{j\sigma}-\epsilon_{i\sigma})+i\eta}
\,\delta v^{\mathrm{eff}}_{ij\sigma}(\omega).
$$

因此只有 occupation 不同的轨道对直接形成跃迁基。kernel 的矩阵元为

$$
K_{ia,jb}
=
\iint d\mathbf r\,d\mathbf r'\,
\varphi_i^*(\mathbf r)\varphi_a(\mathbf r)
 f_{\mathrm{Hxc}}(\mathbf r,\mathbf r',\omega)
\varphi_j(\mathbf r')\varphi_b^*(\mathbf r').
$$

它同时包含 Coulomb screening、spin channel、空间非局域性和可能的频率依赖。若起点含 hybrid 或其他非局域算符，响应方程还必须一致地线性化该非局域项；仅替换基态本征值而不替换 kernel 不是同一个理论近似。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Expand the first-order density matrix in ground-state orbitals:

$$
\delta n_\sigma(\mathbf r,\omega)
=
\sum_{ij}
\varphi^*_{i\sigma}(\mathbf r)
\,\delta\rho_{ij\sigma}(\omega)\,
\varphi_{j\sigma}(\mathbf r).
$$

At fixed effective potential, a density-matrix element contains an occupation difference and energy denominator:

$$
\delta\rho_{ij\sigma}^{0}(\omega)
=
\frac{f_{i\sigma}-f_{j\sigma}}
{\omega-(\epsilon_{j\sigma}-\epsilon_{i\sigma})+i\eta}
\,\delta v^{\mathrm{eff}}_{ij\sigma}(\omega).
$$

Thus orbital pairs with different occupations form the direct transition basis. The kernel matrix element is

$$
K_{ia,jb}
=
\iint d\mathbf r\,d\mathbf r'\,
\varphi_i^*(\mathbf r)\varphi_a(\mathbf r)
 f_{\mathrm{Hxc}}(\mathbf r,\mathbf r',\omega)
\varphi_j(\mathbf r')\varphi_b^*(\mathbf r').
$$

It contains Coulomb screening, spin channel, spatial nonlocality, and possible frequency dependence. If the starting point contains a hybrid or another nonlocal operator, the response equation must linearize that nonlocal term consistently; replacing only the ground-state eigenvalues without replacing the kernel is not the same approximation.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="Casida 型本征问题 | Casida-type eigenproblem"
  assumptions={[
    '闭壳层或已明确 spin channel；',
    '先采用 frequency-independent adiabatic kernel；',
    '跃迁 i to a 的能量 Delta_ia 为正；',
    '矩阵元采用与轨道规范一致的实/共轭 convention。',
  ]}
>

将正、负频率跃迁成对组织并做对称化，可得到平方频率本征问题的一般结构

$$
\boxed{
\sum_{jb}
\left[
\Delta_{ia}^2\delta_{ij}\delta_{ab}
+
2\sqrt{\Delta_{ia}\Delta_{jb}}\,K_{ia,jb}
\right]F_{jb}^{S}
=
\Omega_S^2 F_{ia}^{S}
}.
$$

$\Omega_S$ 是中性激发能量，$F^S$ 描述各 KS 跃迁在 collective excitation 中的权重。若 $K=0$，本征值退回独立跃迁；若 $K\ne0$，不同 $ia$ 对相互混合。偶极谱权来自

$$
D_S
\propto
\sum_{ia}
\sqrt{\Delta_{ia}}\,d_{ia}F_{ia}^{S},
$$

所以 kernel 不只移动峰，还改变 bright/dark 性质。

完整 Casida 形式可写成含 resonant block $A$ 和 antiresonant block $B$ 的非平凡本征问题。忽略 $B$ 得到 Tamm–Dancoff approximation (TDA)，其 Hermitian transition-space Hamiltonian 更易求解，但在强耦合、低能集体模式或 sum-rule 精度要求高时需要单独验证。下面的交互图只实现二跃迁 TDA 型模型，并明确保持该边界。

</DerivationBlock>

## 21.5 时间依赖密度泛函微扰理论 | Time-Dependent Density-Functional Perturbation Theory

<BilingualSection titleZh="只求目标微扰的响应" titleEn="Solve only for the response to the target perturbation" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

显式跃迁空间需要 $N_{\mathrm{occ}}N_{\mathrm{empty}}$ 个轨道对，并可能形成同阶大小的矩阵。平面波或实空间基组中，空态数和矩阵存储可成为主要瓶颈。TDDFPT 改写问题：对给定频率、偏振或外部场，直接求占据轨道的一阶响应或一阶密度矩阵，而不先枚举完整空态集合。

这与 Chapter 20 的静态 Sternheimer 思路相同，但 resolvent 现在含 $\pm\omega+i\eta$，并且需要同时处理正频和负频分量。自洽性仍然存在：一阶密度产生一阶 Hartree–XC 势，再反作用于一阶轨道。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

An explicit transition basis contains $N_{\mathrm{occ}}N_{\mathrm{empty}}$ orbital pairs and may generate matrices of comparable dimension. In plane-wave or real-space bases, the number of empty states and matrix storage can become the dominant bottleneck. TDDFPT reformulates the task: for a specified frequency, polarization, or external field, solve directly for the first-order occupied-orbital response or first-order density matrix without first enumerating a complete empty-state set.

This is the dynamic counterpart of the static Sternheimer logic in Chapter 20, but the resolvent now contains $\pm\omega+i\eta$ and both positive- and negative-frequency components are needed. Self-consistency remains: the first-order density generates a first-order Hartree–XC potential that acts back on the first-order orbitals.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="频率域投影 Sternheimer 方程 | Projected frequency-domain Sternheimer equations"
  assumptions={[
    '基态 Kohn–Sham 方程已收敛；',
    '线性响应且占据数固定；',
    'P_c=1-P_v 投影到 occupied manifold 的正交补；',
    'eta is positive，避免在实轴 pole 上直接求逆。',
  ]}
>

令外场含 $e^{-i\omega t}$ 分量，并写

$$
\delta\varphi_i(t)
=
\delta\varphi_i^{+}(\omega)e^{-i\omega t}
+
\delta\varphi_i^{-}(\omega)e^{+i\omega t}.
$$

对 TDKS 方程线性化并投影到空子空间，得到典型形式

$$
\boxed{
\left(\hat H_0-\epsilon_i-\hbar\omega-i\eta\right)
|\delta\varphi_i^{+}\rangle
=
-\hat P_c\,\delta\hat v_{\mathrm{eff}}^{+}|\varphi_i\rangle
}
$$

和

$$
\boxed{
\left(\hat H_0-\epsilon_i+\hbar\omega+i\eta\right)
|\delta\varphi_i^{-}\rangle
=
-\hat P_c\,\delta\hat v_{\mathrm{eff}}^{-}|\varphi_i\rangle.
}
$$

一阶密度由两部分组合：

$$
\delta n(\mathbf r,\omega)
=
\sum_i f_i
\left[
\varphi_i^*(\mathbf r)\delta\varphi_i^{+}(\mathbf r)
+
\delta\varphi_i^{- *}(\mathbf r)\varphi_i(\mathbf r)
\right].
$$

再用

$$
\delta v_{\mathrm{eff}}
=
\delta v_{\mathrm{ext}}+f_{\mathrm{Hxc}}\delta n
$$

闭合自洽循环。把左侧算符在空态上谱展开会恢复显式 empty-state denominators，因此 Sternheimer 与 sum-over-states 在同一离散空间和同一 kernel 下是重写关系，不是两种不同物理理论。

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="Liouvillian 与 Lanczos 路线" titleEn="Liouvillian and Lanczos route" eyebrow="Algorithmic formulation">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

也可直接对一阶密度矩阵写运动方程。基态满足 $[\hat H_0,\hat\rho_0]=0$；线性化 von Neumann 方程得到

$$
\left(\omega-\mathcal L\right)\delta\rho(\omega)
=
[\delta v_{\mathrm{ext}}(\omega),\rho_0],
$$

其中 Liouvillian $\mathcal L$ 同时包含 $[H_0,\delta\rho]$ 和诱导 Hartree–XC 势的作用。目标响应是 resolvent 的矩阵元，

$$
\langle A|(\omega-\mathcal L)^{-1}|B\rangle.
$$

Lanczos 或其他 Krylov 方法可从一个外场向量构造低维递推，在不对角化全部激发的情况下获得较宽频率区间的谱。效率优势取决于预条件、频率范围、阻尼、非 Hermitian 结构和收敛标准；“未显式计算空态”不等于计算成本与空态数完全无关，因为基组和线性求解难度仍编码相同高能空间。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The equation of motion can also be written directly for the first-order density matrix. The ground state obeys $[\hat H_0,\hat\rho_0]=0$; linearizing the von Neumann equation gives

$$
\left(\omega-\mathcal L\right)\delta\rho(\omega)
=
[\delta v_{\mathrm{ext}}(\omega),\rho_0],
$$

where the Liouvillian $\mathcal L$ contains both $[H_0,\delta\rho]$ and the induced Hartree–XC potential. A target response is a resolvent matrix element,

$$
\langle A|(\omega-\mathcal L)^{-1}|B\rangle.
$$

Lanczos or another Krylov method can build a low-dimensional recursion from one perturbation vector and recover a broad frequency range without diagonalizing every excitation. The efficiency depends on preconditioning, frequency range, damping, non-Hermitian structure, and convergence criteria. “No explicit empty states” does not mean that cost is independent of the high-energy space: the basis and linear-solve difficulty still encode it.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>
