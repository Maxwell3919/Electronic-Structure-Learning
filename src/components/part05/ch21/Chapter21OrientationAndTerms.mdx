## 阅读定位 | Reading Orientation

<BilingualSection titleZh="从静态响应转向电子激发谱" titleEn="From static response to electronic excitation spectra" eyebrow="Orientation">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

Chapter 20 主要研究离子位移、静态电场和自旋微扰引起的基态附近响应。Chapter 21 保留同一响应函数语言，但把外部微扰写成随时间或频率变化的场，并把关注点转向电子系统的中性激发：吸收光子后电子数不变，实验信号来自极化、电流或密度对外场的动态响应。

这一步不能简化成“读取空带与占据带的能量差”。独立粒子跃迁是构造响应的起点；诱导 Hartree 场、交换—相关 kernel、局域场、电子—空穴吸引、起始带隙和时间/频率离散共同决定最终谱。有限体系通常用偶极矩与极化率描述；周期晶体需要以电流、矢势或等价周期表述避免直接使用无界位置算符。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Chapter 20 focused mainly on near-ground-state response to ionic displacements, static fields, and spin perturbations. Chapter 21 retains the response-function language but makes the perturbation explicitly time or frequency dependent and turns to neutral electronic excitations: photon absorption preserves particle number, and the measured signal follows from the dynamic response of polarization, current, or density to an external field.

This step cannot be reduced to reading energy differences between empty and occupied bands. Independent-particle transitions are the starting basis; the induced Hartree field, exchange–correlation kernel, local fields, electron–hole attraction, starting gap, and time/frequency discretization together shape the final spectrum. Finite systems are naturally described by dipoles and polarizabilities, whereas periodic crystals require current, vector potential, or an equivalent periodic formulation rather than an unbounded position operator.

</BilingualSection>

## 物理对象、证据层级与符号 | Physical Objects, Evidence Levels, and Notation

<BilingualSection titleZh="三个不同的能量问题" titleEn="Three distinct energy questions" eyebrow="Objects">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

**基态 Kohn–Sham 本征值差** $\epsilon_a-\epsilon_i$ 是辅助独立粒子系统中的跃迁能量。**加/减电子能量**改变粒子数，属于光电子、逆光电子或 quasiparticle 谱。**中性激发能量**保持粒子数不变，是相互作用响应函数的极点；光学谱还包含跃迁矩阵元、选择定则和环境屏蔽。

三者可能在弱相互作用极限下数值接近，但定义并不相同。只展示能带与 DOS 无法确定峰位、峰强、激子结合、局域场修正或寿命。TDDFT 的任务是在给定基态起点上构造粒子数不变的密度或电流响应；它不是自动生成正确 quasiparticle gap 的修补器。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A **ground-state Kohn–Sham eigenvalue difference** $\epsilon_a-\epsilon_i$ is a transition energy in the auxiliary independent-particle system. An **addition/removal energy** changes particle number and belongs to photoemission, inverse-photoemission, or quasiparticle spectra. A **neutral excitation energy** preserves particle number and appears as a pole of the interacting response; an optical spectrum additionally contains transition matrix elements, selection rules, and environmental screening.

The three can be numerically close in a weakly interacting limit, but they are not the same definition. Bands and DOS alone cannot determine peak positions, intensities, exciton binding, local-field corrections, or lifetimes. TDDFT constructs fixed-particle-number density or current response on a chosen ground-state starting point; it is not an automatic repair of an incorrect quasiparticle gap.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>
