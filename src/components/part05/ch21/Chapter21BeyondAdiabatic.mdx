## 21.9 超越绝热近似 | Beyond the Adiabatic Approximation

<BilingualSection titleZh="精确 exchange–correlation 势具有记忆" titleEn="The exact exchange–correlation potential has memory" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

绝热近似把一个静态 exchange–correlation 势在瞬时密度上求值，因此

$$
v_{\mathrm{xc}}^{\mathrm A}(\mathbf r,t)
=v_{\mathrm{xc}}^{\mathrm{gs}}[n(t)](\mathbf r).
$$

精确时间依赖势一般依赖从初始时刻到当前时刻的密度历史，并依赖初始相互作用态和初始 Kohn–Sham 态。在线性响应中，这种记忆表现为 kernel 对两个时刻的非局域性：

$$
f_{\mathrm{xc}}(\mathbf r t,\mathbf r't')
=
\frac{\delta v_{\mathrm{xc}}(\mathbf r,t)}
{\delta n(\mathbf r',t')}.
$$

因果性要求 retarded kernel 在 $t'<t_0$ 或 $t'>t$ 的非允许区域不响应。Fourier 变换后，时间差结构转化为真正的频率依赖 $f_{\mathrm{xc}}(\omega)$。绝热 kernel 对频率没有内禀结构，因此只通过 $\chi_0(\omega)$ 的分母产生频率变化。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

An adiabatic approximation evaluates a static exchange–correlation potential at the instantaneous density:

$$
v_{\mathrm{xc}}^{\mathrm A}(\mathbf r,t)
=v_{\mathrm{xc}}^{\mathrm{gs}}[n(t)](\mathbf r).
$$

The exact time-dependent potential generally depends on the density history from the initial time to the present, as well as on the initial interacting and Kohn–Sham states. In linear response this memory appears as nonlocality in two times:

$$
f_{\mathrm{xc}}(\mathbf r t,\mathbf r't')
=
\frac{\delta v_{\mathrm{xc}}(\mathbf r,t)}
{\delta n(\mathbf r',t')}.
$$

Causality requires a retarded kernel that does not respond to inadmissible future times. Fourier transformation converts the time-difference structure into genuine frequency dependence $f_{\mathrm{xc}}(\omega)$. An adiabatic kernel has no intrinsic frequency structure; its frequency variation enters only through the denominators of $\chi_0(\omega)$.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="频率依赖 kernel 如何增加 response poles | How a frequency-dependent kernel can add response poles"
  assumptions={[
    '采用有限 transition-space 标量模型，仅用于说明解析结构；',
    '独立粒子响应含一个单跃迁 pole；',
    'kernel 的动态部分含一个因果辅助 pole；',
    '模型参数不代表任何具体材料。',
  ]}
>

考虑一个标量独立粒子响应

$$
\chi_0(\omega)=\frac{A}{\omega-\Delta+i\eta},
$$

以及带动态 pole 的 kernel

$$
f_{\mathrm{xc}}(\omega)
=f_0+\frac{g^2}{\omega-\Omega_d+i\gamma}.
$$

相互作用 response pole 满足

$$
1-\chi_0(\omega)f_{\mathrm{xc}}(\omega)=0.
$$

乘去分母后得到二次方程：

$$
(\omega-\Delta+i\eta)(\omega-\Omega_d+i\gamma)
-Af_0(\omega-\Omega_d+i\gamma)-Ag^2=0.
$$

即使 $\chi_0$ 只有一个显式跃迁，动态 kernel 的辅助 pole 也可使 $\chi$ 出现两个混合解。静态 $f_0$ 只能移动原有单跃迁 pole；它不能凭空生成具有独立频率结构的第二个解。这说明为什么 double/multiple excitation 需要 memory 或等价的扩大响应空间。

The auxiliary pole is a teaching representation of memory, not a universal physical model. A practical frequency-dependent kernel must satisfy causality, symmetry, high-frequency limits, conservation laws, and consistency with the chosen ground-state approximation.

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="双激发为何暴露绝热 response 的结构限制" titleEn="Why double excitations expose the structural limit of adiabatic response" eyebrow="Multiple excitations">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

Casida/TDA 的基向量通常是一个占据轨道到一个空轨道的单跃迁 $i\to a$。静态 kernel 可以把这些向量任意线性组合，产生 collective state 和振子强度重分配；但矩阵维度仍由单跃迁空间决定。一个主要由两个电子同时跃迁构成的多体态没有对应的独立单跃迁 basis vector。

若双激发通过 configuration mixing 与单激发强烈耦合，精确 response 的频率依赖 kernel 必须把缺失的多体结构折回单跃迁空间。绝热 kernel 没有该频率依赖，通常不能给出正确数量和位置的 poles。扩大到显式 doubles、dressed TDDFT、many-body Green-function/BSE 变体或其他多参考方法是不同的理论路线；不能只把 broadening 调小来“显现”缺失 pole。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The basis vectors of a Casida/TDA calculation are usually single occupied-to-empty transitions $i\to a$. A static kernel can form arbitrary linear combinations of these vectors, producing collective states and redistributing oscillator strength, but the matrix dimension remains that of the single-transition space. A many-body state dominated by simultaneous excitation of two electrons has no corresponding independent single-transition basis vector.

When a double excitation mixes strongly with singles, the exact response kernel must fold the missing many-body structure back into the single-transition space through frequency dependence. An adiabatic kernel lacks that structure and generally cannot produce the correct number and positions of poles. Explicit doubles, dressed TDDFT, variants of many-body Green-function/BSE methods, and other multireference approaches are distinct theoretical routes; reducing numerical broadening cannot reveal a pole that is absent from the approximation.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="轨道依赖与 exact-exchange kernel" titleEn="Orbital-dependent and exact-exchange kernels" eyebrow="Orbital dependence">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

当基态泛函显式依赖轨道，例如 exact exchange、hybrid 或 range-separated hybrid，响应必须线性化轨道依赖势或 generalized-Kohn–Sham 非局域算符。exact-exchange kernel 具有空间非局域性，可改善自相互作用、长程电荷转移和某些晶体电子—空穴吸引，但代价更高，并且结果依赖起始轨道、exchange fraction、range-separation 参数和 screening。

在有限体系中，正确的长程电荷转移激发要求供体—受体距离增大时保留合适的 $1/R$ 结构，同时起始 frontier orbital energies 也必须合理。局域或 semilocal 绝热 kernel 通常同时缺少正确的势渐近和长期程 kernel。range-separated 方法可修复部分结构，但 tuned 参数、环境介电 screening 和 size consistency 需要独立声明。

在晶体中，bound exciton 需要小 $q$ 处足够强的长程 kernel。一个 screened hybrid 可能改善 band gap，却因截断长期程 exchange 而仍不能产生正确的 exciton series；一个 long-range-corrected kernel 可以产生 bound pole，但也可能对 screening 参数高度敏感。改善 gap 与改善 electron–hole kernel 是两个不同目标。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

When a ground-state functional depends explicitly on orbitals, as in exact exchange, a hybrid, or a range-separated hybrid, the response must linearize the orbital-dependent potential or generalized-Kohn–Sham nonlocal operator. An exact-exchange kernel is spatially nonlocal and can improve self-interaction, long-range charge transfer, and some crystal electron–hole attraction, but is more expensive and depends on the starting orbitals, exchange fraction, range-separation parameters, and screening.

For a finite system, a correct long-range charge-transfer excitation must retain the appropriate $1/R$ structure as donor–acceptor separation grows, while the starting frontier orbital energies must also be reasonable. A local or semilocal adiabatic kernel usually lacks both the correct potential asymptote and long-range kernel. A range-separated method can repair part of this structure, but tuning, environmental dielectric screening, and size consistency require separate declaration.

In a crystal, a bound exciton requires a sufficiently strong long-range kernel at small $q$. A screened hybrid may improve the band gap yet truncate long-range exchange and still miss the exciton series; a long-range-corrected kernel can generate a bound pole but may be highly sensitive to screening parameters. Improving the gap and improving the electron–hole kernel are distinct targets.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="记忆 kernel 的实现门槛" titleEn="Implementation gates for a memory kernel" eyebrow="Numerical structure">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

一个 frequency-dependent kernel 使 eigenvalue problem 变成 nonlinear eigenvalue problem，或要求在每个频率重新构造 kernel。若 kernel 具有复数结构，response poles 和 lifetime 可能同时进入，矩阵可变为 non-Hermitian。计算必须检查：

- retarded causality 和复共轭对称；
- $\omega\to0$ 与高频极限；
- particle-number、continuity equation 与 sum rule；
- spin channel、gauge 与 nonlocal velocity 的一致性；
- kernel pole 与数值 broadening 的区分；
- ground-state functional、GKS operator 与 response kernel 的一致组合。

把经验 frequency-dependent correction 加到一个不匹配的起始 response 上，可能改善少数峰，却破坏总谱权、静态极限或其他偏振方向。benchmark 必须覆盖峰位、振子强度、dark/bright ordering、continuum、charge-transfer distance 和 size trend，而不是只看一个平均误差。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A frequency-dependent kernel turns the excitation problem into a nonlinear eigenvalue problem or requires rebuilding the kernel at every frequency. If the kernel is complex, response poles and lifetimes may enter together and the matrix can become non-Hermitian. A calculation must check:

- retarded causality and complex-conjugation symmetry;
- static and high-frequency limits;
- particle number, the continuity equation, and sum rules;
- consistency of spin channel, gauge, and nonlocal velocity;
- separation of kernel poles from numerical broadening;
- a consistent combination of the ground-state functional, GKS operator, and response kernel.

Adding an empirical frequency-dependent correction to a mismatched starting response may improve a few peaks while damaging total spectral weight, the static limit, or other polarizations. Benchmarks must cover peak positions, oscillator strengths, dark/bright ordering, continua, charge-transfer distance, and size trends rather than one mean error.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>
