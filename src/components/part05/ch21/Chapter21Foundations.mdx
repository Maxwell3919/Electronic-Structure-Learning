## 21.1 概览 | Overview

<BilingualSection titleZh="先问激发是否改变粒子数" titleEn="First ask whether the excitation changes particle number" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

电子激发首先分成两类。第一类向体系加入电子或从体系移走电子，比较的是 $N$、$N+1$ 与 $N-1$ 粒子体系；光电子谱、逆光电子谱和单粒子 Green 函数讨论这一类对象。第二类保持电子数 $N$ 不变，外部场把体系从基态耦合到另一个多体态；光吸收、电子能量损失和非弹性散射属于这一类动态响应。

静态 Kohn–Sham 方程的本征值构成辅助独立粒子谱。占据态到空态的差值

$$
\Delta_{ia}^{\mathrm{KS}}=\epsilon_a-\epsilon_i
$$

提供构造中性响应的跃迁基，但它既不是一般的 quasiparticle addition/removal gap，也不是自动正确的光学激发能。真实中性激发包含电子与留下的空穴之间的相互作用，以及其余电子的屏蔽。实验谱还由矩阵元、偏振、选择定则、简并、寿命和仪器分辨率加权。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Electronic excitations first divide into two classes. The first adds an electron to or removes an electron from the system and compares the $N$, $N+1$, and $N-1$ particle problems; photoemission, inverse photoemission, and one-particle Green functions address this class. The second preserves electron number $N$ while an external field couples the ground state to another many-body state; optical absorption, electron-energy loss, and inelastic scattering are dynamic responses of this class.

The eigenvalues of the static Kohn–Sham equations form an auxiliary independent-particle spectrum. An occupied-to-empty difference

$$
\Delta_{ia}^{\mathrm{KS}}=\epsilon_a-\epsilon_i
$$

provides a transition basis for constructing a neutral response, but it is neither a general quasiparticle addition/removal gap nor an automatically correct optical excitation energy. A real neutral excitation contains interaction between the promoted electron and the hole it leaves behind, screened by the other electrons. Experimental spectra are also weighted by matrix elements, polarization, selection rules, degeneracy, lifetime, and instrumental resolution.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="光谱是响应函数的极点和谱权重" titleEn="A spectrum consists of response poles and spectral weight" eyebrow="Object hierarchy">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

设外部微扰与算符 $\hat B$ 耦合，测量算符为 $\hat A$。线性响应写为

$$
\delta A(\omega)=\chi_{AB}(\omega)F(\omega).
$$

retarded 响应的极点给出允许的中性激发能量；残数或谱权重给出该外场和测量通道对激发的耦合强度。一个存在于多体 Hamiltonian 中的本征态可以在某个偏振下是 dark state，因此“有能级”不等于“在该实验中有峰”。反之，一个宽峰可能由多个相近跃迁、连续谱、有限寿命或数值 broadening 共同产生。

本章的核心 observable 包括有限体系的极化率 $\alpha(\omega)$、周期体系的电导率 $\sigma(\omega)$ 和介电响应 $\epsilon(\omega)$、以及能量损失中常用的 $-\mathrm{Im}\,\epsilon^{-1}$。这些量相关但不能相互替代；必须报告计算的是哪一个张量、哪个方向、哪种边界条件和哪种宏观平均。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Let an external perturbation couple to an operator $\hat B$ and let $\hat A$ be measured. Linear response has the form

$$
\delta A(\omega)=\chi_{AB}(\omega)F(\omega).
$$

Poles of the retarded response give allowed neutral excitation energies, while residues or spectral weights give the coupling of each excitation to the specified perturbation and measurement channel. A many-body eigenstate may be dark for a chosen polarization, so the existence of a level does not guarantee a peak in that experiment. Conversely, a broad feature may combine nearby transitions, a continuum, finite lifetime, and numerical broadening.

The central observables in this chapter include the finite-system polarizability $\alpha(\omega)$, periodic conductivity $\sigma(\omega)$ and dielectric response $\epsilon(\omega)$, and the energy-loss quantity $-\mathrm{Im}\,\epsilon^{-1}$. They are related but not interchangeable; a calculation must specify the tensor, direction, boundary condition, and macroscopic averaging procedure actually reported.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## 21.2 时间依赖密度泛函理论 | Time-Dependent Density Functional Theory

<BilingualSection titleZh="时间演化不是基态能量极小化" titleEn="Time evolution is not ground-state energy minimization" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 + 补充说明 | Martin source + clarification】">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

静态 DFT 通过基态变分原理组织。时间依赖问题则从给定初态和外势 $v_{\mathrm{ext}}(\mathbf r,t)$ 出发，要求真实多体波函数满足含时 Schrödinger 方程。可用作用量的驻定表达这一动力学约束：

$$
\mathcal A[\Psi]
=
\int_{t_0}^{t_1}dt\,
\left\langle\Psi(t)\left|i\hbar\partial_t-\hat H(t)\right|\Psi(t)\right\rangle,
$$

并在固定初态与合适端点条件下令一阶变分为零。这里是 stationary action，不是把每个时刻的能量独立最小化。真实系统的密度和电流必须连续地继承过去的演化。

Runge–Gross 型映射在给定初态和允许的外势类别内，把时间依赖密度与外势联系起来。Kohn–Sham 构造进一步引入产生同一密度的独立粒子轨道：

$$
 n(\mathbf r,t)=\sum_i f_i|\varphi_i(\mathbf r,t)|^2.
$$

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Static DFT is organized by a ground-state variational principle. A time-dependent problem instead begins from a specified initial state and an external potential $v_{\mathrm{ext}}(\mathbf r,t)$, and requires the true many-body wavefunction to obey the time-dependent Schrödinger equation. A stationary action can express this dynamical constraint:

$$
\mathcal A[\Psi]
=
\int_{t_0}^{t_1}dt\,
\left\langle\Psi(t)\left|i\hbar\partial_t-\hat H(t)\right|\Psi(t)\right\rangle,
$$

with the first variation set to zero for a fixed initial state and suitable endpoint conditions. This is stationary action, not an independent minimization of the energy at every time. The density and current inherit the history of the evolving state.

A Runge–Gross-type mapping relates the time-dependent density to the external potential for a specified initial state and admissible class of potentials. The Kohn–Sham construction then introduces independent-particle orbitals reproducing the same density:

$$
 n(\mathbf r,t)=\sum_i f_i|\varphi_i(\mathbf r,t)|^2.
$$

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="时间依赖 Kohn–Sham 方程 | Time-dependent Kohn–Sham equation"
  assumptions={[
    '初始相互作用态与初始 Kohn–Sham 态已指定，并产生同一初始密度；',
    '外势属于时间依赖密度—势映射适用的类别；',
    '以下写法采用标量势和固定核；周期均匀场的矢势形式在 §21.3 单独处理。',
  ]}
>

将相互作用体系的密度写成独立轨道密度，并把未知相互作用效应放入 $v_{\mathrm{xc}}$，得到

$$
\boxed{
 i\hbar\frac{\partial}{\partial t}\varphi_i(\mathbf r,t)
 =
 \left[
 -\frac{\hbar^2\nabla^2}{2m_e}
 +v_{\mathrm{ext}}(\mathbf r,t)
 +v_{\mathrm H}[n](\mathbf r,t)
 +v_{\mathrm{xc}}[n;\Psi_0,\Phi_0](\mathbf r,t)
 \right]\varphi_i(\mathbf r,t)
 }.
$$

Hartree 势是瞬时密度的 Coulomb 泛函，

$$
 v_{\mathrm H}(\mathbf r,t)
 =e^2\int d\mathbf r'\,
 \frac{n(\mathbf r',t)}{|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|}
$$

（单位制常数按所用 convention 调整）。精确 $v_{\mathrm{xc}}$ 一般依赖从 $t_0$ 到当前时刻的密度历史，也依赖初始相互作用态 $\Psi_0$ 与初始 KS 态 $\Phi_0$。因此把它简写为 $v_{\mathrm{xc}}[n(t)]$ 是近似而不是精确结构。

The time-dependent Kohn–Sham system reproduces the exact density in principle, not the exact interacting wavefunction. Individual time-dependent Kohn–Sham orbitals are auxiliary objects; observable excitation energies are extracted from the response of the density/current or from equivalent response eigenproblems.

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="绝热近似删除了记忆" titleEn="The adiabatic approximation removes memory" eyebrow="Approximation boundary">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

最常用的实现把某个静态近似泛函在瞬时密度上求导：

$$
 v_{\mathrm{xc}}^{\mathrm A}(\mathbf r,t)
 =
 \left.
 \frac{\delta E_{\mathrm{xc}}^{\mathrm{approx}}[n]}
 {\delta n(\mathbf r)}
 \right|_{n=n(t)}.
$$

ALDA 是其中的局域版本。该近似保持自洽时间演化，却删除显式记忆：线性化后的 kernel 在时间上与 $\delta(t-t')$ 成正比，因此在频率域中没有真实的 $\omega$ 结构。它往往能描述由单个 KS 跃迁主导的有限体系激发，但对双激发、强电荷转移和晶体长程激子可能缺少必要的非局域或频率依赖。

“绝热”在此指 exchange–correlation 势只看当前密度，不等于离子 Born–Oppenheimer 绝热近似。两种近似作用于不同自由度，必须分开报告。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The most common implementations differentiate a static approximate functional at the instantaneous density:

$$
 v_{\mathrm{xc}}^{\mathrm A}(\mathbf r,t)
 =
 \left.
 \frac{\delta E_{\mathrm{xc}}^{\mathrm{approx}}[n]}
 {\delta n(\mathbf r)}
 \right|_{n=n(t)}.
$$

ALDA is the local version. This approximation retains self-consistent time evolution but removes explicit memory: the linearized kernel is proportional to $\delta(t-t')$ in time and has no genuine $\omega$ structure in frequency space. It often describes finite-system excitations dominated by one Kohn–Sham transition, but can lack the nonlocality or frequency dependence needed for double excitations, strong charge transfer, and long-range crystal excitons.

“Adiabatic” here means that the exchange–correlation potential sees only the instantaneous density; it is not the Born–Oppenheimer adiabatic approximation for ions. The two approximations act on different degrees of freedom and must be reported separately.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## 21.3 非相互作用粒子的介电响应 | Dielectric Response for Noninteracting Particles

<BilingualSection titleZh="有限体系：从偶极矩到极化率" titleEn="Finite systems: from dipole moment to polarizability" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

当体系尺寸远小于光波长且边界是有限的，可以在 dipole approximation 中写电子的外场耦合

$$
\delta\hat v_{\mathrm{ext}}(t)=-e\,\mathbf E(t)\cdot\mathbf r,
$$

其中电子电荷为 $-e$、$e&gt;0$。诱导偶极矩为

$$
\delta\mathbf d(t)=-e\int d\mathbf r\,\mathbf r\,\delta n(\mathbf r,t),
$$

线性频率域关系定义极化率张量

$$
\delta d_\alpha(\omega)
=\sum_\beta\alpha_{\alpha\beta}(\omega)E_\beta(\omega).
$$

位置原点改变会给总偶极加入与总电荷有关的常数；对中性有限体系的诱导偶极响应，该常数不影响线性变化。带电有限体系、周期体系和跨越周期边界的电荷输运需要额外处理。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

When the system is finite and much smaller than the optical wavelength, the dipole approximation gives the electronic coupling

$$
\delta\hat v_{\mathrm{ext}}(t)=-e\,\mathbf E(t)\cdot\mathbf r,
$$

where the electron charge is $-e$ with $e&gt;0$. The induced dipole is

$$
\delta\mathbf d(t)=-e\int d\mathbf r\,\mathbf r\,\delta n(\mathbf r,t),
$$

and the frequency-domain linear relation defines the polarizability tensor:

$$
\delta d_\alpha(\omega)
=\sum_\beta\alpha_{\alpha\beta}(\omega)E_\beta(\omega).
$$

Changing the origin adds a constant proportional to total charge to the total dipole; for the induced response of a neutral finite system, that constant does not affect the linear change. Charged finite systems, periodic systems, and charge transport across a periodic boundary require additional care.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="独立粒子极化率的跃迁表示 | Transition representation of independent-particle polarizability"
  assumptions={[
    '固定独立粒子 Hamiltonian；暂不包含诱导 Hartree–XC 势；',
    '零温绝缘有限体系，i 为占据态、a 为空态；',
    '采用 exp(-i omega t) Fourier convention 和 retarded omega+i eta；',
    '为简洁起见使用原子单位，恢复单位时补回 e 和 hbar。',
  ]}
>

一阶微扰中，占据态 $i$ 与空态 $a$ 的正频率和负频率贡献必须同时保留。对实轨道或适当共轭配对，可写成

$$
\boxed{
\alpha_{\mu\nu}^{0}(\omega)
=
\sum_{ia}
\frac{2\Delta_{ia}\,
 d_{ia}^{\mu}d_{ai}^{\nu}}
{\Delta_{ia}^{2}-(\omega+i\eta)^2}
},
$$

其中

$$
\Delta_{ia}=\epsilon_a-\epsilon_i&gt;0,
\qquad
 d_{ia}^{\mu}=\langle i|r_\mu|a\rangle.
$$

这与未合并分母的形式等价：

$$
\frac{1}{\Delta-\omega-i\eta}
+
\frac{1}{\Delta+\omega+i\eta}
=
\frac{2\Delta}{\Delta^2-(\omega+i\eta)^2}.
$$

对 $\omega&gt;0$、$\eta&gt;0$，本章 convention 下 $\mathrm{Im}\,\alpha^0$ 为非负吸收谱。峰的位置由 $\Delta_{ia}$ 开始，权重由偶极矩阵元决定；一个能量允许的跃迁可以因对称性使 $d_{ia}=0$ 而不出现在指定偏振的谱中。

在 $\omega=0$、$\eta\to0^+$ 极限，

$$
\alpha_{\mu\nu}^{0}(0)
=2\sum_{ia}\frac{d_{ia}^{\mu}d_{ai}^{\nu}}{\Delta_{ia}},
$$

显示低能跃迁对静态极化率的增强。这个式子仍是独立粒子结果；TDDFT 会通过诱导势把所有跃迁耦合起来。

</DerivationBlock>

<TwoLevelPolarizabilityExplorer />

<BilingualSection titleZh="周期晶体：位置算符必须让位于周期表述" titleEn="Periodic crystals: replace the position operator by a periodic formulation" eyebrow="Periodic boundary">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

Bloch 态延伸到整个晶体，$\mathbf r$ 不是 Born–von Kármán 周期 Hilbert 空间中的普通有界算符。均匀电场不能在周期标量势中简单写成 $-e\mathbf E\cdot\mathbf r$。一种直接路线是使用空间均匀的矢势 $\mathbf A(t)$，由

$$
\mathbf E(t)=-\frac{1}{c}\frac{d\mathbf A}{dt}
$$

定义电场，并通过 minimal coupling 修改 Hamiltonian。宏观 observable 取电流密度而不是有限体系偶极。线性化后电流包含 paramagnetic 响应与 diamagnetic 项；省略后者会破坏规范关系、低频极限和 sum rule。

在 Gaussian 单位中，电导率与介电函数满足

$$
\epsilon_{\alpha\beta}(\omega)
=
\delta_{\alpha\beta}
+\frac{4\pi i}{\omega}\sigma_{\alpha\beta}(\omega),
$$

SI 单位则为 $\epsilon=1+i\sigma/(\epsilon_0\omega)$。代码输出和论文图必须声明单位制、是否包含 intraband/Drude 项以及使用的宏观平均。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Bloch states extend through the crystal, and $\mathbf r$ is not an ordinary bounded operator in a Born–von Kármán periodic Hilbert space. A uniform electric field cannot simply be written as $-e\mathbf E\cdot\mathbf r$ in a periodic scalar potential. One direct route uses a spatially uniform vector potential $\mathbf A(t)$ with

$$
\mathbf E(t)=-\frac{1}{c}\frac{d\mathbf A}{dt},
$$

and introduces the field by minimal coupling. The macroscopic observable is then current density rather than a finite-system dipole. Linearization produces both a paramagnetic response and a diamagnetic term; omitting the latter breaks gauge relations, low-frequency limits, and sum rules.

In Gaussian units, conductivity and dielectric response obey

$$
\epsilon_{\alpha\beta}(\omega)
=
\delta_{\alpha\beta}
+\frac{4\pi i}{\omega}\sigma_{\alpha\beta}(\omega),
$$

whereas SI uses $\epsilon=1+i\sigma/(\epsilon_0\omega)$. Code output and published plots must state the unit system, whether an intraband/Drude contribution is included, and which macroscopic average is used.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="晶体吸收不是裸联合态密度 | Crystal absorption is not a bare joint density of states"
  assumptions={[
    '独立粒子、垂直光学跃迁 q approximately 0；',
    '绝缘体的 interband 部分；',
    'Bloch 态和速度/动量矩阵元采用一致的非局域 Hamiltonian 处理；',
    '省略与单位制和归一化相关的整体常数，只强调结构。',

>

对偏振方向 $\mathbf e$，独立粒子吸收的结构为

$$
\epsilon_2^{0}(\omega)
\propto
\sum_{v,c,\mathbf k}
 w_{\mathbf k}
 \left|\mathbf e\cdot\mathbf v_{cv}(\mathbf k)\right|^2
 \delta\!\left(
 \epsilon_{c\mathbf k}-\epsilon_{v\mathbf k}-\hbar\omega
 \right).
$$

若删除矩阵元，只保留 delta 函数计数，得到 joint density of states。真实独立粒子光谱是**矩阵元加权**的联合态密度。矩阵元控制偏振选择、禁戒跃迁和不同能带对峰强的贡献；速度算符还必须包含非局域赝势或 generalized-Kohn–Sham 非局域算符带来的相应修正。

将 delta 函数替换为宽度 $\eta$ 的 Lorentzian 或 Gaussian 是数值表示。只有在额外的 many-body/lifetime 模型与实验条件明确时，才能把该宽度解释为物理线宽。即使独立粒子谱在 $\epsilon_c-\epsilon_v$ 处开始，TDDFT kernel 与局域场也可重新分配谱权，长程电子—空穴吸引还可在 continuum edge 以下产生 bound exciton。

</DerivationBlock>
