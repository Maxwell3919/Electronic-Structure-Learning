<h2 id="reading-orientation">阅读定位 | Reading Orientation</h2>

<BilingualSection titleZh="本章处理的对象" titleEn="The object treated in this chapter" eyebrow="Reading orientation">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

Bloch 本征态把平移对称性显式写在波矢 $\mathbf k$ 上，适合求能带；Wannier 函数把同一个有限维带子空间改写成晶格平移相关的局域函数，适合讨论成键、实空间跃迁、插值和降阶模型。两种表示在选定子空间内可以严格等价，但局域函数并不唯一：每个 $\mathbf k$ 点的相位、复合带之间的幺正旋转，以及 entangled bands 中对子空间本身的选择都会改变最终函数。

本章要回答四类问题：哪些变换只改变 gauge 而不改变 band projector；局域中心和展宽怎样由 $k$ 空间重叠得到；当目标能带与其他能带纠缠时怎样定义有限维近似子空间；以及一个 Wannier 插值或低能模型需要哪些独立验证。Chapter 24 再把 Wannier 中心与极化、局域化和 Berry 相位联系起来；Part VI 负责拓扑阻碍和分类。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Bloch eigenstates expose translational symmetry through crystal momentum and are natural for band calculations. Wannier functions rewrite the same finite-dimensional band subspace as lattice-related localized functions, making bonding, real-space hopping, interpolation, and reduced models transparent. The two representations can be exactly equivalent inside a selected subspace, but the localized functions are not unique: phases at each $\mathbf k$, unitary rotations among composite bands, and—when bands are entangled—the choice of subspace itself all change the result.

The chapter asks which transformations change only the gauge while preserving the band projector; how centres and spreads follow from $k$-space overlaps; how a finite-dimensional approximate subspace is selected when target bands are entangled with others; and which independent checks make a Wannier interpolation or low-energy model reliable. Chapter 24 connects centres to polarization, localization, and Berry phase, while Part VI owns topological obstruction and classification.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<h2 id="objects-and-conventions">物理对象、数学对象与约定 | Objects and Conventions</h2>

<div className="chapter23-terms">
  <dl>
    <div><dt>Bloch eigenstate $|\psi_{n\mathbf k}\rangle$</dt><dd>在固定 $\mathbf k$ 处对 Hamiltonian 对角化得到的延展本征态。</dd></div>
    <div><dt>cell-periodic state $|u_{n\mathbf k}\rangle$</dt><dd>由 $|\psi_{n\mathbf k}\rangle=e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf r}|u_{n\mathbf k}\rangle$ 定义，规范选择作用于这一 frame。</dd></div>
    <div><dt>band subspace $P(\mathbf k)$</dt><dd>由目标 bands 或经 disentanglement 选择的有限维子空间；projector 是 gauge invariant。</dd></div>
    <div><dt>gauge/frame $U^{(\mathbf k)}$</dt><dd>固定子空间内的 $\mathbf k$ 依赖幺正旋转；改变单个 Wannier 函数而不改变 projector。</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Wannier function $|w_{n\mathbf R}\rangle$</dt><dd>对平滑周期 Bloch frame 作 Brillouin-zone Fourier transform 得到的晶格平移族。</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Wannier centre $\bar{\mathbf r}_n$</dt><dd>局域函数的一阶矩；在周期体系中只在 lattice-vector branch 意义下定义。</dd></div>
    <div><dt>spread $\Omega$</dt><dd>所有目标 Wannier 函数的二阶中心矩之和；分为 gauge-invariant 与 gauge-dependent 部分。</dd></div>
    <div><dt>overlap matrix $M_{mn}^{(\mathbf k,\mathbf b)}$</dt><dd>相邻 $k$ 点 cell-periodic states 的重叠，是离散中心、展宽和 localization 的基本输入。</dd></div>
    <div><dt>trial projection</dt><dd>原子、键或其他局域试探函数对 Bloch 子空间的投影；决定初始 frame，不自动保证最终物理唯一性。</dd></div>
    <div><dt>outer window</dt><dd>允许用于构造目标子空间的候选本征态范围；窗外 bands 不属于 reduced space。</dd></div>
    <div><dt>inner/frozen window</dt><dd>要求 reduced subspace 精确包含的目标本征态范围；属于补充的现代实现术语。</dd></div>
    <div><dt>disentanglement</dt><dd>从维度更大的候选空间中选择随 $\mathbf k$ 平滑变化的固定维目标子空间。</dd></div>
    <div><dt>hybrid Wannier function</dt><dd>只沿一个方向 Fourier transform、在垂直方向保留 Bloch momentum 的混合表示。</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Wannier interpolation</dt><dd>由有限实空间 Hamiltonian/matrix elements 反变换到细 $k$ 网格；精度取决于子空间和截断。</dd></div>
  </dl>
</div>

<BilingualSection titleZh="归一化、Fourier 与相位约定" titleEn="Normalization, Fourier, and phase conventions" eyebrow="Convention audit">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

连续推导采用每原胞归一化的 Bloch 态与 Brillouin-zone 体积 $V_{\rm BZ}$：

$$
|w_{n\mathbf R}\rangle
=
\frac{1}{V_{\rm BZ}}
\int_{\rm BZ}d\mathbf k\,
e^{-i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf R}
|\widetilde\psi_{n\mathbf k}\rangle.
$$

离散均匀网格采用 $1/N_k$。不同软件可能把相位号、Bloch 归一化和 Fourier 前因子放在不同位置；只要正变换与逆变换一致，物理 projector 和插值能带不受影响。中心的 branch、overlap product 的相位和 hopping 的格矢标签必须在同一 convention 下审计。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Continuous derivations use cell-normalized Bloch states and Brillouin-zone volume $V_{\rm BZ}$,

$$
|w_{n\mathbf R}\rangle
=
\frac{1}{V_{\rm BZ}}
\int_{\rm BZ}d\mathbf k\,
e^{-i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf R}
|\widetilde\psi_{n\mathbf k}\rangle.
$$

A uniform discrete mesh uses $1/N_k$. Codes may distribute signs and normalization factors differently; a consistent forward/inverse pair preserves the physical projector and interpolated bands. Centre branches, overlap-product phases, and hopping lattice vectors must be audited in one convention.
