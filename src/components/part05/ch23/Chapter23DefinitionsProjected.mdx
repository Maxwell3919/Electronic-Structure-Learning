## 23.1 定义与性质 | Definition and Properties

<BilingualSection titleZh="从 Bloch 本征态到平滑 frame" titleEn="From Bloch eigenstates to a smooth frame" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

周期 Hamiltonian 的本征态可写为

$$
|\psi_{n\mathbf k}\rangle
=e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf r}|u_{n\mathbf k}\rangle.
$$

对孤立非简并 band，数值对角化给出的每个 $|\psi_{n\mathbf k}\rangle$ 都可乘任意相位 $e^{i\alpha_n(\mathbf k)}$，能量、密度和所有 gauge-invariant observable 不变。若若干 bands 作为一个复合子空间处理，更一般的自由度是

$$
|\widetilde u_{n\mathbf k}\rangle
=
\sum_{m=1}^{J}|u_{m\mathbf k}\rangle U_{mn}^{(\mathbf k)},
\qquad
U^{(\mathbf k)\dagger}U^{(\mathbf k)}=I_J.
$$

这里 $P(\mathbf k)=\sum_m|u_{m\mathbf k}\rangle\langle u_{m\mathbf k}|$ 不随 $U^{(\mathbf k)}$ 改变，但每一列 frame 的相位、轨道 character 和实空间 Fourier transform 会改变。Wannier 构造要求选择随 $\mathbf k$ 足够平滑、并在 reciprocal-lattice identification 下周期一致的 frame。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A periodic Hamiltonian has eigenstates

$$
|\psi_{n\mathbf k}\rangle
=e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf r}|u_{n\mathbf k}\rangle.
$$

For an isolated nondegenerate band, each numerically returned state may be multiplied by an arbitrary phase $e^{i\alpha_n(\mathbf k)}$ without changing the energy, density, or any gauge-invariant observable. For a composite set of $J$ bands, the freedom generalizes to

$$
|\widetilde u_{n\mathbf k}\rangle
=
\sum_{m=1}^{J}|u_{m\mathbf k}\rangle U_{mn}^{(\mathbf k)},
\qquad
U^{(\mathbf k)\dagger}U^{(\mathbf k)}=I_J.
$$

The projector $P(\mathbf k)=\sum_m|u_{m\mathbf k}\rangle\langle u_{m\mathbf k}|$ is unchanged, while each frame vector, orbital character, and real-space Fourier transform changes. A Wannier construction selects a frame that is sufficiently smooth in $\mathbf k$ and consistent under reciprocal-lattice identification.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="Bloch–Wannier 变换与逆变换 | Bloch–Wannier transform and inverse"
  assumptions={[
    '目标 band 或 composite subspace 在整个 Brillouin zone 维数固定；',
    '选定 frame 在 k 上平滑，并采用一致的 periodic gauge；',
    '离散推导采用 N_k 个均匀 k 点和 Born–von Karman 周期。',
  ]}
>

在均匀离散网格上定义

$$
|w_{n\mathbf R}\rangle
=
\frac{1}{N_k}\sum_{\mathbf k}
e^{-i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf R}
|\widetilde\psi_{n\mathbf k}\rangle.
$$

将两边乘 $e^{i\mathbf k'\cdot\mathbf R}$ 并对所有 $\mathbf R$ 求和，利用离散正交关系

$$
\sum_{\mathbf R}
e^{i(\mathbf k'-\mathbf k)\cdot\mathbf R}
=N_k\,\delta_{\mathbf k\mathbf k'},
$$

得到逆变换

$$
|\widetilde\psi_{n\mathbf k}\rangle
=
\sum_{\mathbf R}
e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf R}|w_{n\mathbf R}\rangle.
$$

因此在选定子空间内信息没有丢失。平移算符给出

$$
|w_{n\mathbf R}\rangle
=\hat T_{\mathbf R}|w_{n\mathbf 0}\rangle,
$$

所以只需存储一个原胞的函数及其 lattice translations。连续形式把 $N_k^{-1}\sum_{\mathbf k}$ 替换为 $V_{\rm BZ}^{-1}\int_{\rm BZ}d\mathbf k$。

The discrete Fourier pair is invertible because the crystal-momentum and lattice-vector grids are dual. No information is lost inside the selected subspace; locality is a property of the chosen smooth frame, not an extra approximation in the exact transform.

</DerivationBlock>

<DerivationBlock
  title="正交性与 projector 等价 | Orthonormality and projector equivalence"
  assumptions={[
    '每个 k 点的 frame 在 band index 上正交；',
    'U(k) 为幺正矩阵；',
    '积分或内积覆盖完整 Born–von Karman 晶体。',
  ]}
>

由 Bloch 正交性

$$
\langle\widetilde\psi_{m\mathbf k'}|\widetilde\psi_{n\mathbf k}\rangle
=\delta_{mn}\delta_{\mathbf k\mathbf k'}
$$

可得

$$
\begin{aligned}
\langle w_{m\mathbf R'}|w_{n\mathbf R}\rangle
&=\frac{1}{N_k^2}
\sum_{\mathbf k\mathbf k'}
e^{i\mathbf k'\cdot\mathbf R'}
e^{-i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf R}
\langle\widetilde\psi_{m\mathbf k'}|\widetilde\psi_{n\mathbf k}\rangle\\
&=\delta_{mn}\frac{1}{N_k}\sum_{\mathbf k}
e^{i\mathbf k\cdot(\mathbf R'-\mathbf R)}\\
&=\delta_{mn}\delta_{\mathbf R\mathbf R'}.
\end{aligned}
$$

同样，完整 Wannier 族的 projector 为

$$
\sum_{n\mathbf R}|w_{n\mathbf R}\rangle\langle w_{n\mathbf R}|
=
\sum_{n\mathbf k}|\widetilde\psi_{n\mathbf k}\rangle\langle\widetilde\psi_{n\mathbf k}|.
$$

幺正 gauge 只改变 projector 的分解方式。若 entangled-band 步骤改变了 $P(\mathbf k)$，则不再只是 gauge 变化。

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="中心、二阶矩与 branch" titleEn="Centres, second moments, and branches" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

对原胞 $\mathbf R=0$ 的局域函数，中心定义为 $\bar{\mathbf r}_n=\langle w_{n0}|\mathbf r|w_{n0}\rangle$。把位置算符插入 Fourier transform，并利用 $\mathbf r e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf r}=-i\nabla_{\mathbf k}e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf r}$，可把中心写成 cell-periodic frame 的 $k$ 导数积分；二阶矩同理由二阶导数得到。该形式预告了 Berry connection，但在周期晶体中中心只在加上晶格矢量的意义下定义。

相位 $\alpha_n(\mathbf k)=\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf T$ 把 Wannier 函数整体平移一个 lattice vector $\mathbf T$，不改变任何 bulk projector。复合带中，单个中心可随 gauge 连续移动，而同一占据子空间全部中心之和具有更强的 gauge-invariant 意义；Chapter 24 将把它与极化 branch 联系起来。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For the home-cell function, the centre is $\bar{\mathbf r}_n=\langle w_{n0}|\mathbf r|w_{n0}\rangle$. Inserting the position operator into the Fourier transform and using $\mathbf r e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf r}=-i\nabla_{\mathbf k}e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf r}$ expresses the centre through a $k$ derivative of the cell-periodic frame; second derivatives yield the second moment. This anticipates the Berry connection, but a crystalline centre is defined only modulo lattice vectors.

A phase $\alpha_n(\mathbf k)=\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf T$ translates a Wannier function by a lattice vector without changing the bulk projector. In a composite set, individual centres move with the gauge, whereas the sum over the complete occupied subspace has a stronger invariant meaning developed in Chapter 24.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## 23.2 最大投影 Wannier 函数 | Maximally Projected Wannier Functions

<BilingualSection titleZh="用试探局域函数固定初始 frame" titleEn="Using localized trials to fix an initial frame" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

单 band 的直观选择是让每个 $\mathbf k$ 处 Bloch 函数在指定原子或键中心具有实且正的振幅。更一般地，选择局域试探函数 $|g_n\rangle$，计算

$$
A_{mn}(\mathbf k)=\langle\psi_{m\mathbf k}|g_n\rangle.
$$

$A$ 把 trial orbital 投影到目标 Bloch 子空间。若投影在整个 BZ 保持满秩，可由它构造连续初始 frame；若某些 $\mathbf k$ 处奇异值趋零，trial 与目标子空间失配，后续 localization 即使数值结束也缺少稳定起点。

原子中心 $s/p/d$、键中心组合和分子片段都可作为试探函数。选择反映了要描述的化学或低能自由度，因此是模型定义的一部分，而不是隐藏的数值细节。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For one band, an intuitive choice makes the Bloch amplitude real and positive at a selected atom or bond centre. More generally, localized trials $|g_n\rangle$ define

$$
A_{mn}(\mathbf k)=\langle\psi_{m\mathbf k}|g_n\rangle.
$$

The matrix projects trial orbitals into the target Bloch subspace. If the projection remains full rank throughout the BZ, it can define a continuous initial frame. If a singular value approaches zero, the trials fail to span the target locally; successful termination of a later localization step does not repair that missing starting information.

Atom-centred $s/p/d$ functions, bond-centred combinations, and molecular fragments are all possible trials. The choice encodes the intended chemical or low-energy degrees of freedom and is part of the model definition.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="投影后的对称正交化 | Symmetric orthonormalization after projection"
  assumptions={[
    '候选 Bloch states 在每个 k 点正交；',
    'trial 数目等于目标子空间维数 J；',
    'S(k)=A†A 在整个 BZ 正定。',
  ]}
>

先定义未归一化投影态

$$
|\phi_{n\mathbf k}\rangle
=\sum_m|\psi_{m\mathbf k}\rangle A_{mn}(\mathbf k).
$$

它们的 overlap 为

$$
S_{nn'}(\mathbf k)
=\langle\phi_{n\mathbf k}|\phi_{n'\mathbf k}\rangle
=[A^\dagger A]_{nn'}.
$$

采用 Löwdin 对称正交化，得到

$$
|\widetilde\psi_{n\mathbf k}\rangle
=\sum_{n'}|\phi_{n'\mathbf k}\rangle
[S^{-1/2}(\mathbf k)]_{n'n}.
$$

于是 $\langle\widetilde\psi_m|\widetilde\psi_n\rangle=\delta_{mn}$。最小奇异值控制数值条件；$S$ 奇异表示 trial projections 没有定义完整 frame。该矩阵形式是对 Martin“最大投影”思想的补充实现推导。

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="硅键中心例子与解释边界" titleEn="Bond-centred silicon example and interpretation boundary" eyebrow="Source example, original exposition">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

在金刚石结构 Si 中，四条占据价带共同构成一个 composite subspace。选择指向四条 Si–Si 键的 trial functions，可得到键中心、近似 $sp^3$ character 的局域函数。Martin 比较的投影构造与最大局域化结果形状相近，并报告长距离指数衰减；这说明化学直觉和 spread minimization 在该体系中选择了相近 frame。

这不构成一般唯一性定理。不同投影可得到原子中心或键中心表示；只要它们张成同一子空间，精确 band 信息等价。要把某一种表示称为“更自然”，需要说明目标是成键解释、局域相互作用、插值范围、对称性还是 transferability。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The four occupied valence bands of diamond-structure Si form one composite subspace. Trials directed along the four Si–Si bonds produce bond-centred localized functions with approximate $sp^3$ character. Martin notes that a projection construction and a maximal-localization construction yield similar shapes and long-range exponential decay in this case, indicating that chemical intuition and spread minimization select nearby frames.

This is not a general uniqueness theorem. Atom-centred and bond-centred gauges may span the same exact subspace. Calling one representation more natural requires a declared objective: bonding interpretation, local interactions, interpolation range, symmetry, or transferability.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>
