## 23.6 混合 Wannier 函数 | Hybrid Wannier Functions

<BilingualSection titleZh="只在一个方向局域化" titleEn="Localize in only one direction" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

在三维晶体中，可以只对一个 reciprocal coordinate，例如 $k_z$，作 Fourier transform，而保留面内 $\mathbf k_\parallel=(k_x,k_y)$。混合 Wannier 函数定义为

$$
|W_{n l}(\mathbf k_\parallel)\rangle
=
\frac{1}{N_{k_z}}
\sum_{k_z}
e^{-ik_z l c}
|\widetilde\psi_{n,\mathbf k_\parallel,k_z}\rangle,
$$

其中 $l$ 是沿 $z$ 的晶胞标签，$c$ 是相应 lattice period。该函数沿 $z$ 局域、在平面内仍为 Bloch 延展态。它适合表面、层分解、极化和界面问题，因为边界法向采用实空间、平行方向仍保留晶体动量。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

In a three-dimensional crystal one may Fourier transform only one reciprocal coordinate, such as $k_z$, while retaining the in-plane momentum $\mathbf k_\parallel=(k_x,k_y)$. A hybrid Wannier function is

$$
|W_{n l}(\mathbf k_\parallel)\rangle
=
\frac{1}{N_{k_z}}
\sum_{k_z}
e^{-ik_z l c}
|\widetilde\psi_{n,\mathbf k_\parallel,k_z}\rangle,
$$

where $l$ labels cells along $z$ and $c$ is the corresponding lattice period. It is localized along $z$ and remains Bloch extended in the plane. This mixed representation is natural for surfaces, layers, polarization, and interfaces because the boundary normal is treated in real space while parallel momentum remains explicit.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="hybrid centre 与闭合 overlap product | Hybrid centre and a closed overlap product"
  assumptions={[
    '固定 transverse momentum 时，目标 subspace 沿 k_z 连续且闭合；',
    '采用与 single-direction Fourier transform 一致的相位 convention；',
    '离散 k_z loop 具有周期连接。',
  ]}
>

对固定 $\mathbf k_\parallel$，home-cell hybrid centre 为

$$
\bar z_n(\mathbf k_\parallel)
=
\langle W_{n0}(\mathbf k_\parallel)|z|W_{n0}(\mathbf k_\parallel)\rangle.
$$

连续形式写成

$$
\bar z_n(\mathbf k_\parallel)
=
\frac{c}{2\pi}
\int_{m loop}dk_z\,
 i\langle u_{n\mathbf k}|\partial_{k_z}u_{n\mathbf k}\rangle,
$$

只在加整数 $c$ 的 branch 意义下定义。离散单 band 形式为

$$
\boxed{
\bar z_n
=-\frac{c}{2\pi}
\operatorname{Im}\ln
\prod_j
\langle u_{n,k_{z,j}}|u_{n,k_{z,j+1}}\rangle.
}
$$

复合占据子空间使用 overlap matrix determinant 或 Wilson-loop eigenphases。中心随 $\mathbf k_\parallel$ 的连续运动是 Chapter 24 与 Part VI 的重要输入，但单独画出一组 centre curves 不等于已经给出拓扑分类；还需完整 occupied subspace、体隙、对称性和对应 invariant contract。

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="表面与层状体系中的用途" titleEn="Use for surfaces and layered systems" eyebrow="Connection to Chapter 22">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

hybrid functions 可把 bulk subspace 分解为沿法向定位的层，从而构造 layer polarization、surface charge bookkeeping 或半无限模型的初始 basis。与原子投影相比，hybrid centre 来自整个 band subspace，较少依赖原子球半径；但它仍依赖 gauge、branch、band selection 和是否存在拓扑 obstruction。

对 slab，有限厚度会把两个表面混合，hybrid centres 也可受中心层不足、外场和非等价终止影响。对 metal，occupied subspace 随 k 变化维数或缺少全局 gap，不能直接套用绝缘体的 occupied-manifold centre 解释。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Hybrid functions decompose a bulk subspace into layers localized along a normal direction, providing a basis for layer polarization, surface-charge bookkeeping, or semi-infinite models. Unlike an atomic-sphere projection, a hybrid centre is built from the whole band subspace and is less tied to a sphere radius, yet it still depends on gauge, branch, band selection, and possible topological obstruction.

In a slab, finite thickness mixes opposite surfaces, and hybrid centres can be altered by an insufficient bulk-like interior, external fields, or inequivalent terminations. In a metal, a globally gapped occupied manifold may not exist, so the insulating-manifold interpretation cannot be transferred without modification.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## 23.7 应用 | Applications

<BilingualSection titleZh="实空间 Hamiltonian 与 Wannier 插值" titleEn="Real-space Hamiltonian and Wannier interpolation" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

在选定 Wannier basis 中定义

$$
H_{mn}(\mathbf R)
=
\langle w_{m\mathbf 0}|\hat H|w_{n\mathbf R}\rangle.
$$

平移对称性给出细 k 点 Hamiltonian

$$
H_{mn}(\mathbf k)
=
\sum_{\mathbf R}
e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf R}H_{mn}(\mathbf R).
$$

对该小矩阵对角化即可得到插值 bands。若 Wannier functions 局域，$H(\mathbf R)$ 随 $|\mathbf R|$ 衰减，有限 coarse k mesh 与有限 real-space range 可以高效覆盖细 k 网格。插值精度由被省略的 long-range matrix elements、coarse mesh、subspace selection 和距离 convention 共同决定。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

In a Wannier basis define

$$
H_{mn}(\mathbf R)
=
\langle w_{m\mathbf 0}|\hat H|w_{n\mathbf R}\rangle.
$$

Translational symmetry gives the fine-k Hamiltonian

$$
H_{mn}(\mathbf k)
=
\sum_{\mathbf R}
e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf R}H_{mn}(\mathbf R).
$$

Diagonalizing this small matrix yields interpolated bands. Localized Wannier functions make $H(\mathbf R)$ decay with distance, allowing a finite coarse mesh and real-space range to cover a dense k mesh efficiently. Accuracy depends jointly on omitted long-range matrix elements, the coarse mesh, subspace selection, and the distance convention used in the Fourier reconstruction.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="coarse mesh 与 real-space periodicity | Coarse mesh and real-space periodicity"
  assumptions={[
    '输入 k mesh 为 N1×N2×N3 均匀网格；',
    '目标 matrix elements 在该网格上无离散不连续；',
    'Fourier transform 使用与 lattice vectors 一致的离散对偶。',
  ]}
>

离散 transform 只能区分由 coarse mesh 对偶超胞内的 $\mathbf R$。因此求得的 $H(\mathbf R)$ 在该 Born–von Karman 超胞上周期重复。插值时希望把它解释为局域 matrix elements 并在超胞边界外截断或按 Wigner–Seitz 等价距离分配。若真实 hopping 在边界处仍显著，不同等价距离选择会产生可见差异，说明 coarse mesh 尚不足。

官方 Wannier90 文档将 `use_ws_distance` 描述为 real-space interpolation 距离处理的一部分，并说明 coarse k mesh 对应一个周期实空间超胞。该实现细节不改变理论定义，但提醒用户必须对 mesh 和 distance convention 做数值验证。

</DerivationBlock>

<InterpolationLocalityExplorer />

<BilingualSection titleZh="插值验收不能只看一条能带路径" titleEn="Interpolation acceptance cannot rely on one band path" eyebrow="Validation contract">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

最低验证应在独立、通常更密的 k mesh 上比较：每条目标 band 的 max/RMS error；交叉与简并附近的 subspace/projector distance；Fermi surface topology 和 $E_F$；速度 $\nabla_{\mathbf k}E$。若后续计算 Berry curvature、optics、EPC、spin 或 orbital matrix elements，还必须直接比较对应 matrix element，而不是从能量吻合推断。

high-symmetry path 只覆盖 measure-zero 的 k 集合，可能漏掉 avoided crossing、Fermi pocket、band reorder 和局域 interpolation spikes。对 entangled bands，验证区域必须与声明的 inner/outer window 和目标 observable 的能量范围一致。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Minimum validation uses an independent, usually denser k mesh to compare maximum and RMS errors for every target band; subspace/projector distance near crossings and degeneracies; Fermi-surface topology and $E_F$; and velocities $\nabla_{\mathbf k}E$. Berry curvature, optical, EPC, spin, or orbital calculations require direct comparison of the corresponding matrix elements rather than inference from band-energy agreement.

A high-symmetry path samples a measure-zero set and may miss avoided crossings, Fermi pockets, band reorderings, and localized interpolation spikes. For entangled bands, the validation region must match the declared inner/outer windows and the energy range relevant to the target observable.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="局域大尺度方法、交换与输运" titleEn="Localized large-scale methods, exchange, and transport" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

Wannier 或其他局域 functions 支持多类计算：利用密度矩阵近视性构造线性标度方法；在 hybrid functional 中以局域轨道减少 exact-exchange pair；将有限器件连接到周期电极以构造 transport Hamiltonian；把第一性原理 bands 映射为 tight-binding 或 Hubbard/DMFT 模型。

每类应用需要不同对象。线性标度关注 density-matrix decay 和截断误差；exchange 关注 occupied-pair overlap 与积分精度；transport 关注电极 self-energy、contact、能量范围和 current conservation；many-body model 关注 interaction basis、screening、double counting 和 window dependence。一个共同的 Wannier Hamiltonian 文件不能自动通过所有这些验收。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Wannier and related localized functions support several applications: density-matrix nearsightedness in linear-scaling methods; reduced exact-exchange pairs in hybrid functionals; device-to-periodic-electrode Hamiltonians for transport; and first-principles tight-binding or Hubbard/DMFT models.

Each application validates a different object. Linear scaling needs density-matrix decay and truncation error; exchange needs occupied-pair overlap and integral accuracy; transport needs electrode self-energies, contacts, energy range, and current conservation; many-body models need an interaction basis, screening, double counting, and window dependence. One Wannier Hamiltonian file does not automatically satisfy all of these contracts.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="与当前 Wannier90 工作流的连接" titleEn="Connection to a current Wannier90 workflow" eyebrow="【外部权威来源 | Official software documentation】">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

当前 Wannier90 官方文档把 `num_wann < num_bands` 作为启动 disentanglement 的条件；`dis_win_min/max` 定义 outer window，`dis_froz_min/max` 定义 inner/frozen window。官方源码文档进一步区分：outer window 提供候选 states，frozen states 直接纳入目标 manifold，其余维度通过最小化 $\Omega_I$ 选取。

这些关键词只是程序接口，不是科学判据。可审计工作流应保存生成 Bloch states 的 DFT 输入与版本、coarse k mesh、projection/overlap matrices、所有窗口和 trial projections、最终 centres/spreads、插值独立 mesh 与 error manifest。对实际材料，没有通用的 projection、window 或 spread threshold。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The current official Wannier90 documentation activates disentanglement when `num_wann < num_bands`; `dis_win_min/max` define the outer window and `dis_froz_min/max` define the inner or frozen window. The official source documentation further separates candidate states in the outer window, frozen states included directly in the target manifold, and the remaining dimensions selected by minimizing $\Omega_I$.

These keywords are program interfaces, not scientific acceptance criteria. An auditable workflow stores the DFT input and version used to generate Bloch states, the coarse k mesh, projection and overlap matrices, all windows and trial projections, final centres and spreads, and an independent interpolation mesh with an error manifest. No universal projection, window, or spread threshold applies to all materials.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>
