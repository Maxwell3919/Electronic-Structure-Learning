## 19.3 Car–Parrinello 电子—离子统一算法 | Car–Parrinello Unified Algorithm for Electrons and Ions

<BilingualSection titleZh="把轨道放入扩展动力学" titleEn="Embedding orbitals in an extended dynamics" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

Car–Parrinello molecular dynamics（CPMD）不在每个离子步完全重做一次独立的电子基态最小化，而是把占据轨道 $\{\psi_n\}$ 与离子坐标一起作为扩展 Lagrangian 的动态变量。轨道获得一个人为设置的虚拟惯性 $\mu$；只要虚拟电子模式与离子模式保持充分频率分离，轨道可在离子移动时留在瞬时基态流形附近。

这种计算轨迹不是电子真实时间演化。$\mu$、轨道速度和相应虚拟动能属于数值构造；它们的作用是把反复电子最小化转化为连续的受约束传播。判断 CPMD 是否可靠，需要比较它与 Born–Oppenheimer 面的偏离，而不是解释虚拟轨道振荡的实验频率。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Car–Parrinello molecular dynamics (CPMD) does not fully restart an independent ground-state minimization at every ionic step. Instead, the occupied orbitals $\{\psi_n\}$ and ionic coordinates are both dynamical variables of an extended Lagrangian. The orbitals receive an artificial inertia $μ$; if the fictitious electronic modes remain well separated in frequency from the ionic modes, the orbitals can stay close to the instantaneous ground-state manifold as the ions move.

This computational trajectory is not the real-time motion of physical electrons. The fictitious mass, orbital velocities, and their kinetic energy are numerical constructions that replace repeated minimization by constrained propagation. CPMD reliability is judged by proximity to the Born–Oppenheimer surface, not by assigning experimental meaning to fictitious orbital oscillations.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="Car–Parrinello 扩展 Lagrangian 与约束 | Extended Lagrangian and constraints"
  assumptions={[
    '离子按经典坐标 R_I 处理；',
    '占据轨道是独立动态变量，但保持正交归一；',
    '电子势能由同一个 Kohn–Sham 总能泛函 E[{psi},R] 给出；',
    '虚拟质量 mu 是数值参数，不等于真实电子质量。',
  ]}
>

【Martin 原书 + 补充推导 | Martin source + supplemental derivation】扩展 Lagrangian 可写为

$$
L_{\mathrm{CP}}
=
\frac12\sum_I M_I|\dot{\mathbf R}_I|^2
+
\frac\mu2\sum_n\langle\dot\psi_n|\dot\psi_n\rangle
-
E[\{\psi_n\},\{\mathbf R_I\}]
+
\sum_{mn}\Lambda_{mn}
\left(\langle\psi_m|\psi_n\rangle-\delta_{mn}\right).
$$

四项依次是离子动能、虚拟轨道动能、电子—离子总能和正交归一约束。$\Lambda$ 必须是 Hermitian 矩阵，才能使约束与实 Lagrangian 一致。对复轨道，应把 $\psi_n$ 与 $\psi_n^*$ 视为独立变分变量。

对 $\psi_n^*$ 使用 Euler–Lagrange 方程：

$$
\frac{d}{dt}
\frac{\delta L}{\delta\langle\dot\psi_n|}
-
\frac{\delta L}{\delta\langle\psi_n|}=0.
$$

第一项给出 $\mu|\ddot\psi_n\rangle$；能量泛函的轨道导数定义 Kohn–Sham Hamiltonian 的作用；约束项混合占据轨道，因此

$$
\boxed{
\mu|\ddot\psi_n\rangle
=
-\hat H_{\mathrm{KS}}|\psi_n\rangle
+
\sum_m|\psi_m\rangle\Lambda_{mn}
}.
$$

对离子坐标变分得到

$$
\boxed{
M_I\ddot{\mathbf R}_I
=
-\frac{\partial E}{\partial\mathbf R_I}
}
$$

其中偏导在当前轨道变量处计算；若轨道严格位于瞬时定态点，它退化为 Born–Oppenheimer 力。约束矩阵通过要求

$$
\frac{d}{dt}\langle\psi_m|\psi_n\rangle=0,
\qquad
\frac{d^2}{dt^2}\langle\psi_m|\psi_n\rangle=0
$$

在数值传播中维持正交归一。实际算法可用约束求解、子空间旋转或重新正交化实现，但它们的离散守恒性质并不完全相同。

The four terms are ionic kinetic energy, fictitious orbital kinetic energy, the electronic–ionic energy functional, and orthonormality constraints. Variation gives a second-order fictitious orbital equation and the ionic force equation. The multiplier matrix enforces preservation of the occupied subspace; its numerical implementation is part of the algorithm rather than an additional physical interaction.

</DerivationBlock>

<DerivationBlock
  title="静态极限为何返回 Kohn–Sham 方程 | Stationary limit"
  assumptions={[
    '固定离子坐标；',
    '轨道速度与加速度趋于零；',
    '在占据子空间内允许 unitary 旋转。',
  ]}
>

令 $\ddot\psi_n=0$，轨道方程变为

$$
\hat H_{\mathrm{KS}}|\psi_n\rangle
=
\sum_m|\psi_m\rangle\Lambda_{mn}.
$$

在占据子空间内对 Hermitian 矩阵 $\Lambda$ 做 unitary 对角化，可选取“规范轨道”使

$$
\hat H_{\mathrm{KS}}|\tilde\psi_n\rangle
=
\varepsilon_n|\tilde\psi_n\rangle.
$$

因此 CP 扩展动力学的静态定态点与受约束 Kohn–Sham 极值一致。这个结论只涉及定态点；有限 $\mu$、有限时间步和非零轨道动能下的瞬时轨迹仍可能偏离 Born–Oppenheimer 轨迹。

Setting the fictitious acceleration to zero yields the Kohn–Sham stationarity condition within the occupied subspace. Diagonalizing the Hermitian multiplier matrix produces canonical orbitals. Equality of stationary points does not imply equality of finite-time CPMD and BOMD trajectories.

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="绝热频率分离" titleEn="Adiabatic frequency separation" eyebrow="Approximation">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

在基态附近把轨道偏离展开为局域二次形式，虚拟电子模式的频率尺度满足

$$
\omega_{\mathrm e}^2\sim\frac{\kappa_{\mathrm e}}{\mu},
$$

其中 $\kappa_{\mathrm e}$ 是电子能量曲率，与占据—非占据能量间隔和预条件表示有关。粗略地，$\omega_{\mathrm e}\propto\Delta/\sqrt\mu$。为了避免离子模式把能量持续传入虚拟电子自由度，希望

$$
\omega_{\mathrm e}^{\min}\gg\omega_{\mathrm I}^{\max}.
$$

增大 $\mu$ 会降低虚拟电子频率，允许更大积分步的直觉并不总成立，因为它同时削弱频率分离。减小 $\mu$ 有利于绝热跟随，却把最高虚拟频率推高，从而要求更小时间步。可用区间是两个要求之间的折中。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Near the ground state, an orbital displacement can be expanded in a local quadratic form, giving a fictitious electronic frequency scale

$$
\omega_{\mathrm e}^2\sim\frac{\kappa_{\mathrm e}}{\mu},
$$

where $\kappa_{\mathrm e}$ is an electronic curvature related to occupied–unoccupied separations and preconditioning. A rough trend is $\omega_{\mathrm e}\propto\Delta/\sqrt\mu$. To suppress sustained transfer of ionic energy into the fictitious electronic subsystem one seeks

$$
\omega_{\mathrm e}^{\min}\gg\omega_{\mathrm I}^{\max}.
$$

Increasing $\mu$ lowers fictitious frequencies and weakens adiabatic separation; decreasing $\mu$ improves separation but raises the highest frequency and reduces the stable timestep. The useful interval is therefore a compromise between adiabatic following and integration cost.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

在无热浴、无显式时间依赖且约束精确施加的连续 CP 动力学中，对应的扩展能量为

$$
\mathcal H_{\mathrm{CP}}
=
\sum_I\frac12M_I|\dot{\mathbf R}_I|^2
+
\frac\mu2\sum_n\langle\dot\psi_n|\dot\psi_n\rangle

$$

它包含虚拟轨道动能，因此与 BOMD 的离子动能加基态能不同。扩展能量近似守恒只能检查离散 CP 方程是否自洽传播；若虚拟电子动能持续上升、离子温度下降，可能发生电子—离子虚拟能量交换，即便总扩展能量仍很好守恒。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For continuous CP dynamics without a thermostat or explicit time dependence and with exact constraints, the extended energy is

$$
\mathcal H_{\mathrm{CP}}
=
\sum_I\frac12M_I|\dot{\mathbf R}_I|^2
+
\frac\mu2\sum_n\langle\dot\psi_n|\dot\psi_n\rangle
+
E[\{\psi_n\},\mathbf R].
$$

It contains fictitious orbital kinetic energy and is therefore not the BOMD ionic kinetic plus ground-state energy. Conservation of the extended energy tests consistency of the discretized CP equations, but sustained growth of fictitious kinetic energy accompanied by ionic cooling signals energy transfer even when the total extended energy remains nearly constant.

  </div>

## 19.4 平面波表达 | Expressions for Plane Waves

<BilingualSection titleZh="轨道系数成为动态变量" titleEn="Plane-wave coefficients as dynamical variables" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

在周期晶胞中，可将轨道展开为

$$
\psi_{n\mathbf k}(\mathbf r,t)
=
\frac{1}{\sqrt\Omega}
\sum_{\mathbf G}^{|\mathbf k+\mathbf G|^2/2\le E_{\mathrm{cut}}}
 c_{n\mathbf k}(\mathbf G,t)
 e^{i(\mathbf k+\mathbf G)\cdot\mathbf r}.
$$

固定晶胞和固定截止集合下，基函数不随原子位置移动，CP 虚拟动能化为系数速度的平方和：

$$
T_{\mathrm{fict}}
=
\frac\mu2
\sum_{n\mathbf k}w_{\mathbf k}
\sum_{\mathbf G}|\dot c_{n\mathbf k}(\mathbf G)|^2.
$$

轨道正交归一变为系数向量的内积约束。Hamiltonian 作用通常在 reciprocal space 施加动能项，在 real space 施加局域势与密度依赖势，并通过 FFT 往返；非局域赝势则以投影形式计算。这里的 FFT 只是表示转换，不改变能量泛函的物理定义。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

In a periodic cell, an orbital can be expanded as

$$
\psi_{n\mathbf k}(\mathbf r,t)
=
\frac{1}{\sqrt\Omega}
\sum_{\mathbf G}^{|\mathbf k+\mathbf G|^2/2\le E_{\mathrm{cut}}}
 c_{n\mathbf k}(\mathbf G,t)
 e^{i(\mathbf k+\mathbf G)\cdot\mathbf r}.
$$

For a fixed cell and cutoff set, the basis functions do not move with the atoms, and the fictitious kinetic energy becomes a weighted sum of squared coefficient velocities:

$$
T_{\mathrm{fict}}
=
\frac\mu2
\sum_{n\mathbf k}w_{\mathbf k}
\sum_{\mathbf G}|\dot c_{n\mathbf k}(\mathbf G)|^2.
$$

Orthonormality becomes an inner-product constraint on coefficient vectors. Hamiltonian application normally treats kinetic energy in reciprocal space, local and density-dependent potentials in real space, and transforms between them with FFTs; nonlocal pseudopotentials are applied through projectors. The FFT is a representation change, not a modification of the physical energy functional.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="最高平面波动能为何限制 CP 时间步 | High-G timestep restriction"
  assumptions={[
    '用简化自由平面波曲率估计最高虚拟频率；',
    '忽略预条件、势耦合和约束矩阵对精确谱的改变；',
    '结论只用于尺度分析。',
  ]}
>

对高 $G$ 分量，Kohn–Sham Hamiltonian 主要由动能

$$
\varepsilon_G\approx\frac12|\mathbf k+\mathbf G|^2
$$

控制。线性化 CP 方程给出

$$
\mu\ddot c_G\approx-\varepsilon_G c_G,
\qquad
\omega_G\approx\sqrt{\frac{\varepsilon_G}{\mu}}.
$$

截止能越高，$\omega_{\max}\sim\sqrt{E_{\mathrm{cut}}/\mu}$ 越大。显式 Verlet 类传播的稳定性要求 $\omega_{\max}\Delta t$ 小于算法边界，因此尺度上

$$
\Delta t_{\max}\propto\sqrt{\frac{\mu}{E_{\mathrm{cut}}}}.
$$

这说明“提高截止能”同时改进空间表示并收紧虚拟轨道时间离散。实际 CPMD 常使用质量预条件或其他变量变换压缩虚拟频率范围，但预条件改变的是数值动力学，不是电子真实质量。

For a high-$G$ component, kinetic curvature dominates and gives $\omega_G\sim\sqrt{\varepsilon_G/\mu}$. Raising the plane-wave cutoff improves spatial completeness while increasing the largest fictitious frequency, so an explicit stable timestep scales roughly as $\sqrt{\mu/E_{\mathrm{cut}}}$. Preconditioning can compress this numerical spectrum but does not make the fictitious dynamics physical.

</DerivationBlock>
