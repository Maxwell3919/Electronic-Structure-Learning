## 19.5 非自洽 QMD 方法 | Non-self-consistent QMD Methods

<BilingualSection titleZh="速度来自改变模型，而非免费获得同一精度" titleEn="Speed comes from changing the model, not from obtaining the same surface for free" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

非自洽 QMD 用预先参数化或近似更新的电子 Hamiltonian 生成力，例如紧束缚、密度泛函紧束缚、经验势、机器学习势或只做有限电子迭代的近似方案。它们可以把可访问的原子数和时间尺度大幅推高，但计算的已经是另一个近似能量面 $\widetilde E(\mathbf R)$，其误差不能只用运行速度或对少量平衡结构的能量拟合评价。

对动力学而言，局部梯度、势垒、曲率、长程相互作用和异常构型覆盖都重要。一个模型即使在训练点上满足 $|\widetilde E-E|$ 很小，也可能具有不可接受的 $|\nabla\widetilde E-\nabla E|$，或在轨迹离开训练分布后产生错误的反应通道。模型验证应覆盖目标温压、相、组分、缺陷与过渡区，而不是只覆盖基态附近。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Non-self-consistent QMD obtains forces from a parameterized or approximately updated electronic Hamiltonian, including tight-binding, density-functional tight binding, empirical potentials, machine-learned potentials, or schemes with only limited electronic iteration. These methods can greatly extend atom counts and timescales, but they propagate a different approximate surface $\widetilde E(\mathbf R)$. Their error cannot be judged from speed or energy agreement at a few equilibrium structures alone.

Dynamics depends on local gradients, barriers, curvature, long-range interactions, and coverage of unusual configurations. A model with small $|\widetilde E-E|$ at training points may still have an unacceptable $|\nabla\widetilde E-\nabla E|$, or produce a wrong reaction channel outside its training distribution. Validation must cover the target temperature, pressure, phase, composition, defects, and transition regions rather than only the vicinity of the ground state.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<div className="chapter19-evidence-table">

| 方法层级 | 每步电子处理 | 可支持的典型结论 | 仍需独立验证 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 充分收敛 BOMD | 在当前构型求近似电子基态 | 所选电子方法的绝热经典核轨迹 | 核量子效应、泛函误差、有限尺寸、采样与真实速率 |
| 合格 CPMD | 受约束虚拟轨道随离子传播 | 与 BOMD 对照后，在绝热分离范围内的近基态轨迹 | 虚拟质量/时间步、电子加热、金属/近简并、BOMD 偏差 |
| 有限 SCF/外推 BOMD | 电子态只部分更新 | 在力残差与漂移受控时的近似 BOMD | 残差偏置、历史依赖、时间可逆性 |
| 紧束缚/DFTB | 参数化电子 Hamiltonian | 参数适用域内的电子敏感动力学 | 参数转移性、电荷/自旋状态、势垒和参考 DFT 误差 |
| 经验或 ML 势 | 无显式电子自由度或只隐式编码 | 训练/验证域内的大规模长时间原子动力学 | 域外检测、参考数据质量、长程物理、电子 observable |

</div>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="方法名称不能替代误差说明" titleEn="A method name does not replace an error statement" kind="boundary">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>“第一性原理 MD”“DFT-MD”或“机器学习 MD”都不是单一精度等级。必须同时声明电子结构近似、基组/赝势、温压与系综、时间步、电子阈值、原子数、轨迹长度、平衡化、统计不确定度和目标 observable。</p>
  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>“First-principles MD,” “DFT-MD,” and “machine-learning MD” are not unique accuracy classes. A useful report must state the electronic approximation, basis/pseudopotential, thermodynamic conditions and ensemble, timestep, electronic tolerance, atom count, trajectory length, equilibration, statistical uncertainty, and target observable.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 19.6 模拟实例与时间尺度 | Examples of Simulations and Accessible Scales

<BilingualSection titleZh="Martin 的实例在方法链中的位置" titleEn="How Martin’s examples fit the methodological chain" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

Martin 以高压物质与液态铁、水与氢键网络、催化反应、碳的相行为和输运，以及缺陷、表面与团簇等问题说明 QMD 的用途。这些案例共享同一逻辑：电子结构力允许原子在复杂局域环境中重排，从而获得静态结构优化难以给出的有限温构型、相关函数或反应路径信息。

实例不能被压缩成“轨迹显示了某现象，因此该现象已证明”。高压熔体需要有限尺寸、温压控制和方程状态检查；液态水对交换—关联近似、色散和核量子效应敏感；催化事件受势垒误差和稀有事件采样限制；导电性还需要在轨迹快照上计算合适的电子响应，而不是从原子运动直接读取。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Martin uses high-pressure matter and liquid iron, water and hydrogen-bond networks, catalytic reactions, carbon phase behaviour and transport, and defects, surfaces, and clusters to illustrate QMD. The common logic is that electronic-structure forces allow atoms to reorganize in complex local environments, yielding finite-temperature configurations, correlation functions, or reaction pathways that a static relaxation cannot provide.

These examples cannot be reduced to “the trajectory displayed the phenomenon, therefore the phenomenon is proven.” High-pressure liquids require finite-size, pressure/temperature, and equation-of-state checks; liquid water is sensitive to the exchange–correlation approximation, dispersion, and nuclear quantum effects; catalytic events are limited by barrier errors and rare-event sampling; and conductivity requires a suitable electronic-response calculation on trajectory configurations rather than direct reading from atomic motion.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="有限轨迹中的概率与未观察事件" titleEn="Probability and unobserved events in a finite trajectory" eyebrow="Sampling">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

若某事件以平均速率 $k$ 近似 Poisson 发生，在总有效观察时间 $T$ 内零次事件的概率为

$$
P(N=0)=e^{-kT}.
$$

因此“没有观察到事件”最多给出与 $T$ 和置信水平相关的速率上界。例如要求 $P(N=0)=0.05$，得到 $k\approx-\ln(0.05)/T\approx3/T$。这仍依赖独立 Poisson 事件、稳定热力学状态和足够平衡化等假设。普通皮秒级轨迹通常无法排除远慢于该尺度的转变。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

If an event occurs approximately as a Poisson process with mean rate $k$, the probability of observing no event during an effective time $T$ is

$$
P(N=0)=e^{-kT}.
$$

Thus absence of an observed event provides only a confidence-dependent upper bound tied to $T$. Setting $P(N=0)=0.05$ gives $k\approx-\ln(0.05)/T\approx3/T$. Even this statement assumes independent Poisson events, a stationary thermodynamic state, and adequate equilibration. An ordinary picosecond trajectory generally cannot exclude transformations that are much slower than that scale.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="轨迹长度与独立样本数" titleEn="Trajectory length and effective sample size" kind="derivation">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>相邻时间点通常高度相关。若 observable 的积分自相关时间为 $\tau_{\mathrm{int}}$，长度为 $T$ 的单条轨迹只有约 $N_{\mathrm{eff}}\sim T/(2\tau_{\mathrm{int}})$ 个独立样本，而不是 $T/\Delta t$ 个。减小时间步增加存储点数，却不按同样比例增加统计信息。</p>
  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>Adjacent frames are strongly correlated. For an observable with integrated autocorrelation time $\tau_{\mathrm{int}}$, a trajectory of length $T$ contains only about $N_{\mathrm{eff}}\sim T/(2\tau_{\mathrm{int}})$ independent samples, not $T/\Delta t$. Reducing the timestep increases the number of stored frames without proportionally increasing statistical information.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 与实际计算的连接 | Connection to Practical Calculations

<BilingualSection titleZh="从理论定义到科学解释" titleEn="From theoretical definition to scientific interpretation" eyebrow="Workflow">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

一个可审查的 QMD 工作流应按以下顺序记录：

1. **理论对象**：BOMD、CPMD 或改变后的近似势能面；经典核或核量子方法；目标系综。
2. **电子离散**：交换—关联近似、赝势/全电子处理、截止能、k 点、占据与自旋。
3. **力实现**：Hellmann–Feynman、Pulay、非局域、Ewald、约束与应力项是否完整。
4. **时间离散**：积分器、$\Delta t$、约束、恒温/恒压方法和 conserved/extended quantity。
5. **数值收敛**：电子阈值、时间步、超胞、采样、轨迹长度以及目标 observable 的稳定性。
6. **适用性**：经典核、绝热近似、泛函、稀有事件和有限尺寸是否允许回答当前问题。
7. **科学解释**：结论只覆盖实际收敛并满足适用性条件的 observable。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

An auditable QMD workflow should record the following sequence:

1. **Theoretical object:** BOMD, CPMD, or a modified approximate surface; classical or quantum nuclei; target ensemble.
2. **Electronic discretization:** exchange–correlation approximation, pseudopotential/all-electron treatment, cutoff, k sampling, occupations, and spin.
3. **Force implementation:** completeness of Hellmann–Feynman, Pulay, nonlocal, Ewald, constraint, and stress terms.
4. **Time discretization:** integrator, $\Delta t$, constraints, thermostat/barostat, and the relevant conserved or extended quantity.
5. **Numerical convergence:** electronic tolerance, timestep, cell size, sampling, trajectory length, and stability of the target observable.
6. **Applicability:** whether classical nuclei, the adiabatic approximation, functional, rare-event scale, and finite size permit the question to be answered.
7. **Scientific interpretation:** claims remain limited to observables that are both converged and methodologically applicable.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<div className="chapter19-evidence-table">

| 主张 | 最低证据 | 仍不能由此推出 |
|---|---|---|
| 单步电子计算正常完成 | 程序终止状态、可解析输出、当前构型与输入身份 | SCF 达标、力准确、轨迹可用 |

## 常见误判 | Common Misjudgments

<BilingualSection titleZh="会改变结论的误判" titleEn="Misjudgments that change the conclusion" eyebrow="Review">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">
    <ul>
      <li><strong>把 QMD 当作电子实时动力学：</strong>BOMD 假定电子绝热跟随；CP 轨道运动是虚拟数值动力学。</li>
      <li><strong>用总能收敛替代力收敛：</strong>能量误差可为残差二阶，而力误差保持一阶。</li>
      <li><strong>把稳定积分当作物理稳定：</strong>轨迹不发散只说明数值传播未明显失稳，不证明结构是热力学或动力学稳定相。</li>
      <li><strong>把帧数当作独立样本数：</strong>相关时间决定有效样本量。</li>
      <li><strong>从单次事件得到速率：</strong>一个事件只能给出很弱的统计约束，通常还受初始条件和非平衡历史影响。</li>
      <li><strong>从短轨迹排除慢过程：</strong>未观察事件的结论必须绑定观察时间和概率模型。</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Treating QMD as real-time electron dynamics:</strong> BOMD assumes adiabatic following; CP orbital motion is fictitious numerical dynamics.</li>
      <li><strong>Replacing force convergence with energy convergence:</strong> energy errors can be second order in a residual while force errors remain first order.</li>
      <li><strong>Equating stable integration with physical stability:</strong> a non-divergent trajectory shows only that propagation is not obviously unstable.</li>
      <li><strong>Counting frames as independent samples:</strong> the correlation time controls the effective sample size.</li>
      <li><strong>Inferring a rate from one event:</strong> a single event supplies weak statistics and may depend strongly on initial conditions.</li>
      <li><strong>Excluding slow processes from a short run:</strong> a null observation must be tied to observation time and a probability model.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## 原创练习 | Original Exercises

1. **能量面与力。** 对 $E(x)=a(x^2-b^2)^2$ 写出力，求定态点并用二阶导数分类。说明一条从 $x=-b$ 出发、总能低于势垒的 NVE 轨迹能否访问 $x=+b$。
2. **Hellmann–Feynman 边界。** 从未归一化态 $|\Phi(R)\rangle$ 的 Rayleigh quotient 重新推导能量导数，指出归一化导数如何进入。
3. **Verlet 回归。** 对 $\omega=1$ 的谐振子，在固定总时间下比较 $\Delta t=0.4,0.2,0.1$ 的最大位置误差，验证二阶全局趋势，并解释为何接近稳定边界时该趋势变差。
4. **有偏与无偏力误差。** 构造相同 RMS 幅度的交替力误差和恒定正偏差，比较它们对速度和总能的长期影响。
5. **CP 虚拟质量。** 在模型 $\omega_{\mathrm e}=\Delta/\sqrt\mu$ 中，使 $\Delta$ 减半时求保持相同频率比所需的 $\mu$ 变化，并说明金属极限为何仍不能由该比例模型解决。
6. **证据等级。** 给定“1000 步轨迹结束、温度均值 300 K、未见结构转变”三条记录，列出要支持“该相在 300 K 稳定”仍缺少的最低检查。
7. **采样。** 轨迹长 20 ps，某 observable 的 $\tau_{\mathrm{int}}=0.5$ ps。估算有效样本数；把输出频率提高十倍是否改变该数值？
8. **方法选择。** 对液态水的结构、质子转移和热导率三个目标，分别列出经典核 BOMD 的关键适用性风险，并说明哪些目标需要额外响应或核量子处理。
