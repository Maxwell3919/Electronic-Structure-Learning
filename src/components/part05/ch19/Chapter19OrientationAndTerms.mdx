## 阅读定位 | Reading Orientation

<BilingualSection titleZh="从静态电子结构进入原子轨迹" titleEn="From static electronic structure to atomic trajectories" eyebrow="Orientation">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

Chapter 19 处理的对象是随时间变化的离子坐标 $\{\mathbf R_I(t)\}$。电子自由度仍通过给定核坐标下的电子结构问题提供总能和力；离子则在经典近似下按这些力运动。这个组合通常称为第一性原理分子动力学或量子分子动力学。这里的“量子”主要指力来自量子电子结构，而不是说原子核轨迹本身已经包含核量子效应。

本章从 Part IV 的基态求解方法出发，建立三层关系：电子基态定义 Born–Oppenheimer 势能面；势能面对核坐标的导数给出力；离散时间积分把力转化为轨迹。Chapter 20 将转而研究平衡位置附近的二阶响应、声子和磁振子，因此本章只在解释轨迹与小振动的联系时触及曲率，不提前展开完整晶格动力学。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Chapter 19 studies time-dependent ionic coordinates $\{\mathbf R_I(t)\}$. The electronic degrees of freedom still provide an energy and forces through an electronic-structure problem at fixed nuclei, while the ions move classically under those forces. This combination is commonly called first-principles or quantum molecular dynamics. Here “quantum” refers primarily to the electronic origin of the forces; it does not mean that the nuclear trajectories automatically contain nuclear quantum effects.

Starting from the ground-state methods of Part IV, the chapter establishes three links: the electronic ground state defines a Born–Oppenheimer potential-energy surface; derivatives of that surface give ionic forces; and a discrete time integrator converts those forces into a trajectory. Chapter 20 treats second-order response, phonons, and magnons near equilibrium, so the present chapter mentions curvature only where it clarifies trajectories and small oscillations.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## 物理对象、数学对象与符号 | Physical Objects, Mathematical Objects, and Notation

<BilingualSection titleZh="必须分开的四类量" titleEn="Four classes of quantities that must remain separate" eyebrow="Objects">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

**基态量**包括固定核坐标下的电子密度 $n_{\mathbf R}(\mathbf r)$、Kohn–Sham 轨道、总能 $E_{\mathrm{BO}}(\mathbf R)$ 和由其导出的静态力。**动力学量**包括 $\mathbf R_I(t)$、速度 $\dot{\mathbf R}_I(t)$、加速度和离散时间步。**统计量**包括温度估计、径向分布函数、扩散系数、时间相关函数和系综平均。**数值诊断量**包括 SCF 残差、力误差估计、守恒量漂移和积分器回归误差。

这些类别之间存在计算关系，但不具有自动等价性。SCF 残差小不保证力已达到轨迹所需精度；总能在单步内变化小不保证长期无系统漂移；轨迹中未观察到结构转变不证明该转变不存在；短轨迹得到的平均值也不自动代表热力学极限。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

**Ground-state quantities** include the electronic density $n_{\mathbf R}(\mathbf r)$, Kohn–Sham orbitals, the total energy $E_{\mathrm{BO}}(\mathbf R)$, and static forces at fixed nuclei. **Dynamical quantities** include $\mathbf R_I(t)$, velocities, accelerations, and discrete timesteps. **Statistical quantities** include temperature estimators, radial distribution functions, diffusion coefficients, time-correlation functions, and ensemble averages. **Numerical diagnostics** include SCF residuals, force-error estimates, conserved-quantity drift, and integrator regression errors.

These classes are connected computationally but are not interchangeable. A small SCF residual does not guarantee force accuracy sufficient for a trajectory; a small one-step energy change does not guarantee absence of long-time systematic drift; failure to observe a transformation does not prove that it cannot occur; and a short-trajectory average does not automatically represent the thermodynamic limit.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>
