<h2 id="calculation-connection">与实际计算的连接 | Connection to Practical Calculations</h2>

<BilingualSection titleZh="从研究问题到可审计输入" titleEn="From research question to an auditable input" eyebrow="Workflow">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

表面和界面计算应先定义 observable 与物理边界，再选择超胞。需要记录：晶面、终止、重构、registry、覆盖度、缺陷和化学计量；对称或非对称 slab、双界面周期、固定层、真空、dipole correction 与 Coulomb truncation；功能、赝势或全电子方法、SOC、磁性、cutoff、smearing 和 k 网格；结构弛豫自由度；真空平台、宏观平均势、core/Wannier reference 与带边 convention；以及目标量对厚度、真空、面内晶胞、k 网格、展宽和投影的收敛。

程序结束、SCF 收敛和力较小只覆盖求解链的局部环节。功函数、表面能、界面 lineup、表面局域长度和低维响应必须分别验收；它们的最慢收敛参数可以不同。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A surface or interface calculation should define the observable and physical boundary before selecting a supercell. The record must include facet, termination, reconstruction, registry, coverage, defects, and stoichiometry; symmetric or asymmetric slab, double-interface periodicity, fixed layers, vacuum, dipole correction, and Coulomb truncation; functional, pseudopotential or all-electron treatment, SOC, magnetism, cutoff, smearing, and k mesh; structural freedoms; vacuum plateau, macroscopic average, core or Wannier reference, and band-edge convention; and convergence of the target quantity with thickness, vacuum, in-plane cell, k mesh, broadening, and projection.

Program termination, SCF convergence, and small forces cover only local gates. Work function, surface energy, interface lineup, surface localization length, and lower-dimensional response require separate acceptance; their slowest convergence parameters need not be the same.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="参数扫描围绕目标量" titleEn="Parameter scans follow the target quantity" eyebrow="Convergence design">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

对功函数，检查指定侧真空平台、两侧差、slab 厚度、真空、k 网格和偶极处理。对表面能，保持 slab 与体参考的基组、应变、k 点和化学势一致，并检查层数振荡。对表面态，构造同一 $\mathbf k_\parallel$ 的 projected bulk continuum，检查层权重、两表面劈裂、衰减和展宽。对带阶，使用匹配应变态带边与自洽 interface lineup，并检查两侧是否形成体样平台。对二维或一维响应，把每体积输出转换为每面积或每长度量，再检查真空与截断 Coulomb 边界。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For a work function, check the selected vacuum plateau, side asymmetry, slab thickness, vacuum, k mesh, and dipole treatment. For a surface energy, keep slab and bulk basis, strain, k sampling, and chemical potentials consistent and inspect layer oscillations. For a surface state, construct the projected bulk continuum at the same $\mathbf k_\parallel$ and check layer weight, opposite-surface splitting, decay, and broadening. For an offset, combine matched-strain bulk edges with the self-consistent interface lineup and require bulk-like plateaus. For a two- or one-dimensional response, convert volume-normalized output to per-area or per-length form and test vacuum and truncated-Coulomb boundaries.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<h2 id="common-misjudgments">常见误判 | Consequential Misjudgments</h2>

<div className="chapter22-misjudgments">
  <ol>
    <li><strong>“真空足够大，所以 slab 已收敛。”</strong> 真空只控制镜像间隔；slab 中央体样化和两表面耦合需独立检查。</li>
    <li><strong>“一条带在 bulk gap 里，所以是表面态。”</strong> 需要同一 $k_\parallel$ 的 projected bulk continuum、局域化和厚度证据。</li>
    <li><strong>“表面态有 Rashba 分裂，所以是拓扑态。”</strong> 普通反演破缺与 SOC 也能产生 Rashba；拓扑需要体不变量。</li>
    <li><strong>“Bader 转移很小，所以没有界面偶极或杂化。”</strong> 净分区电荷可小，但连续电荷重排仍可产生势阶。</li>
    <li><strong>“孤立层真空带边直接给出异质结带阶。”</strong> 它只适合作为弱耦合预筛选。</li>
    <li><strong>“界面金属化证明 polar discontinuity 的电子重构。”</strong> 缺陷、互扩散、表面和电极也可供载流子。</li>
    <li><strong>“二维介电常数随真空变化只是数值误差。”</strong> 每体积定义本来依赖超胞高度，应转换为面极化率。</li>
    <li><strong>“SCF 和结构收敛证明 offset 或表面能收敛。”</strong> 目标差值有独立的厚度、k 网格、reference 和方法门槛。</li>
  </ol>
</div>

<h2 id="exercises">原创练习 | Original Exercises</h2>

<div className="chapter22-exercises">
  <ol>
    <li><strong>偶极与功函数。</strong> 从一维 Poisson 方程推导中性平面电荷重排造成的两侧势阶，并检查原点平移。</li>
    <li><strong>对称与非对称 slab。</strong> 证明不同终止的一个 slab 总能只能给出两个表面能之和，并设计额外参考。</li>
    <li><strong>Tamm 阈值。</strong> 推导 $E=\Delta+t^2/\Delta$ 与 $|\Delta|>|t|$，检查带边处局域长度发散。</li>
    <li><strong>共振判别。</strong> 设计 smearing、slab 厚度与表面投影扫描以区分物理共振和数值宽化。</li>
    <li><strong>Rashba convention。</strong> 求 $H_R=\alpha_R(\hat z\times\mathbf k)\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma$ 的能量和自旋，并说明它未计算拓扑不变量。</li>
    <li><strong>带阶分解。</strong> 用应变态 $E_{v/c}-\bar V$ 与界面 lineup 计算有向 VBO/CBO，并交换材料标签检查符号。</li>
    <li><strong>极性层序。</strong> 递推交替 $\pm\sigma$ 平面的场和势，验证半补偿极限，并列出三种真实补偿机制。</li>
    <li><strong>二维归一化。</strong> 比较不同真空高度的超胞介电输出，转换为面极化率并识别残余镜像耦合。</li>
    <li><strong>维度 DOS。</strong> 从 $d$ 维相空间推导 $g_d(E)$，检查单位和 broadening 对 1D 奇点的影响。</li>
    <li><strong>证据分级。</strong> 对层 PDOS 峰、slab 线性色散、Bader 转移和 Γ 点声子记录，写出各自能支持的最强结论。</li>
  </ol>
</div>
