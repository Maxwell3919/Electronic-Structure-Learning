## 22.6 界面：半导体 | Interfaces: Semiconductors

<BilingualSection titleZh="相干界面的三个独立层级" titleEn="Three independent layers of a coherent interface problem" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

同价半导体可以在保持体配位的情况下形成近似非极性界面，但这并不把问题简化为两张能带图的拼接。界面电子结构至少同时包含三个层级：外延约束引起的体带边移动与简并劈裂；局域键合和电荷重排产生的界面偶极；近似泛函、SOC 和准粒子修正对价带与导带的不同影响。

面内晶格通常由基底或匹配超胞固定。即使界面原子完全弛豫，两侧材料仍处于与自由体不同的应变状态。静水应变可整体移动带边，剪切和单轴分量可分裂原本简并的价带或导带。因此体参考必须使用与界面相同的面内晶格、法向应力边界和理论层级。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Isovalent semiconductors may form nearly nonpolar interfaces while preserving bulk-like coordination, but the problem is not reduced to pasting two band plots together. The interface contains at least three independent layers: strain-induced shifts and splittings of the bulk edges, a local dipole created by bonding and charge rearrangement, and method-dependent corrections to valence and conduction states through the functional, SOC, or quasiparticle treatment.

The in-plane lattice is normally fixed by a substrate or commensurate supercell. Even after all interface atoms are relaxed, each constituent remains in a strain state different from its free bulk crystal. Hydrostatic strain may shift an edge, while shear or uniaxial components split degenerate manifolds. The bulk references must therefore use the same in-plane lattice, normal-stress boundary, and theoretical level as the interface.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="体带边与界面 lineup 的分解 | Decomposition into bulk edges and interface lineup"
  assumptions={[
    'A、B 体参考使用界面中的应变和同一理论层级；',
    '界面超胞两侧存在可识别的体样平均势平台；',
    '没有未处理的宏观电场、自由载流子空间电荷区或带电背景。',
  ]}
>

令 $E_v^A$ 与 $E_v^B$ 是两种应变态的价带顶，$\bar V^A$ 与 $\bar V^B$ 是各自体计算中的平均参考势。界面计算远离界面后得到两侧平台差

$$
\Delta V_{B-A}=\bar V^{B,\mathrm{int}}-\bar V^{A,\mathrm{int}}.
$$

采用“材料 B 减材料 A”的有向 convention，价带阶为

$$
\boxed{
\Delta E_v^{B-A}
=
\left(E_v^B-\bar V^B\right)
-
\left(E_v^A-\bar V^A\right)
+
\Delta V_{B-A}.
}
$$

导带阶同理：

$$
\boxed{
\Delta E_c^{B-A}
=
\left(E_c^B-\bar V^B\right)
-
\left(E_c^A-\bar V^A\right)
+
\Delta V_{B-A}.
}
$$

若从未应变态出发，可以另加 deformation-potential 项，但它必须与直接应变态体计算在声明近似内一致。常见工作流用较便宜的自洽界面计算得到 lineup，再用 hybrid 或 GW 修正体带边；“lineup 对理论层级不敏感”只是一项待验证近似。若两侧平均势没有平台而是持续倾斜，上述两平台公式已经失效，必须转向位置依赖的局域带边和 Poisson 问题。

The offset is the difference of each strained bulk edge relative to its own bulk reference plus the self-consistent interface lineup. A higher-level correction may be applied to the bulk terms only after verifying that the lineup is sufficiently transferable. A macroscopic field or space-charge region invalidates a simple two-plateau decomposition.

</DerivationBlock>

<InterfaceLineupExplorer />

<BilingualSection titleZh="价带阶、导带阶与带隙误差" titleEn="Valence offset, conduction offset, and gap error" eyebrow="Method boundary">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

价带阶和导带阶是两个不同 observable。半局域泛函可能给出较合理的价带 lineup，却因带隙偏小而显著错置导带阶。一个标量 VBM 还可能隐藏 SOC、晶场和应变下的多重简并劈裂；实际报告应说明所用的极值、轨道/对称性和动量位置。

Type-I、Type-II 或 broken-gap 标签需要全部相关带边的有向关系。显式界面若已金属化，原先孤立材料的绝缘带边可能不再形成同一自洽体系中的可观测边界。此时继续使用类型标签，应明确它描述的是接触前真空对齐、局域层投影，还是某个仍可定义的准粒子参考，而不能把它当作显式金属界面的完整分类。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Valence and conduction offsets are distinct observables. A semilocal functional may yield a useful valence lineup while strongly misplacing the conduction offset through its gap error. A scalar VBM may also hide SOC-, crystal-field-, and strain-induced splitting; the reported extremum, orbital or symmetry character, and momentum must be identified.

Type-I, Type-II, or broken-gap labels require every relevant directed edge. If the explicit interface becomes metallic, the insulating edges of the isolated constituents may no longer exist as boundaries of one self-consistent system. Any retained label must be declared as a pre-contact vacuum alignment, a local layer projection, or another explicitly defined quasiparticle reference.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="PDOS、深能级与局域参考的作用" titleEn="Roles of PDOS, core levels, and localized references" eyebrow="Reference audit">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

层分或轨道分辨 DOS 可以判断费米附近态主要来自哪一侧，但峰边受 smearing、投影球、基组和有限层数影响。它适合作为来源诊断，不应单独替代带阶公式。深层 core level、宏观平均势或局域 Wannier/轨道能可提供交叉参考，但每一种都带有自己的化学位移、投影和 final-state 边界。

最低复现记录应包含界面取向、终止、化学计量、registry、超胞匹配、应变、法向弛豫、两侧体样层数、参考势定义、SOC 与磁性、功能/准粒子修正，以及 slab 厚度、k 网格、cutoff、smearing 和电边界的收敛。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Layer- and orbital-resolved DOS can identify which side contributes states near the Fermi level, but apparent edges depend on smearing, projection radii, basis, and finite thickness. PDOS is a source diagnostic, not a replacement for the offset decomposition. Deep core levels, macroscopic potentials, or localized Wannier/orbital energies provide cross-references, each with its own chemical-shift, projection, and final-state boundary.

A minimum reproducibility record includes orientation, termination, stoichiometry, registry, supercell match, strain, normal relaxation, bulk-like thickness on both sides, reference definition, SOC and magnetism, functional or quasiparticle correction, and convergence of thickness, k mesh, cutoff, smearing, and electrical boundary.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout title="界面带阶的结论上限 | Claim ceiling of an interface offset" tone="warning">
  <div className="bilingual-callout__grid">
    <div>
      <p>匹配体参考与自洽 lineup 可以支持声明结构、应变、终止和方法下的有向带阶。它不能自动支持掺杂器件中的长程带弯曲、偏压下输运、缺陷主导 pinning，或未计算的 quasiparticle/optical gap。</p>

They do not automatically establish long-range bending in a doped device, biased transport, defect-controlled pinning, or an uncalculated quasiparticle or optical gap.</p>

## 22.7 界面：氧化物 | Interfaces: Oxides

<BilingualSection titleZh="绝缘体—绝缘体界面的极性不连续" titleEn="Polar discontinuity at an insulator–insulator interface" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

STO/LAO 等氧化物界面同时耦合形式价态、晶格极化、缺陷、互扩散、强关联和量子限域。沿理想 (001) 层序，SrO 与 TiO₂ 可按形式计数视为中性层，而 LaO 与 AlO₂ 形成交替正负平面。若无限堆叠这些理想平面且不允许任何补偿，层间电场具有非零宏观平均，电子势能随厚度线性增长，即极性灾难模型。

真实体系可以通过电子从表面或第二界面转移、氧空位、阳离子互扩散、非化学计量、晶格位移、表面吸附、电极或有限厚度残余场降低能量。形式层计数约束理想厚膜极限所需的补偿量，但不能唯一决定实验载流子的来源、密度、迁移率或空间分布。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Oxide interfaces such as STO/LAO couple formal valence, lattice polarization, defects, intermixing, correlation, and quantum confinement. Along ideal (001), SrO and TiO₂ may be formally neutral, whereas LaO and AlO₂ alternate positive and negative. If those ideal planes are stacked indefinitely without compensation, the layer fields have a nonzero macroscopic average and the electron potential energy grows linearly with thickness: the polar-catastrophe model.

A real system can reduce this energy through electronic transfer from a surface or second interface, oxygen vacancies, cation intermixing, nonstoichiometry, lattice displacement, adsorption, electrodes, or a finite-thickness residual field. Formal counting constrains an ideal thick-film compensation limit, but it does not uniquely determine the experimental carrier source, density, mobility, or spatial distribution.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="交替带电平面的场跳变与半补偿 | Field jumps of alternating charged planes and half compensation"
  assumptions={[
    '每层视为无限均匀带电平面；',
    '层间距和介电常数固定；',
    '只讨论经典宏观静电，不包含能带、缺陷形成能或非线性晶格极化。',
  ]}
>

跨过面电荷 $\sigma_j$，场的右侧值与左侧值满足

$$
E^{\mathrm{right}}_j-E^{\mathrm{left}}_j
=\frac{\sigma_j}{\epsilon}.
$$

相邻层之间的势差为

$$
V^{\mathrm{right}}_j-V^{\mathrm{left}}_j
=-E_jd.
$$

对交替 $+\sigma$ 与 $-\sigma$ 平面，场在两个值之间交替，但每个双层的平均场不为零，因而

$$
\Delta V_{\mathrm{bilayer}}
=-\frac{\sigma d}{\epsilon}
$$

并随层数累积。若在界面加入 $-\sigma/2$ 补偿，两个层间场变为 $-\sigma/(2\epsilon)$ 和 $+\sigma/(2\epsilon)$，双层平均斜率消失。这就是理想平面模型中“半电子/胞”极限的静电来源。

有限厚度、离子位移、非线性介电、多个界面、缺陷和实际带填充都会改变自洽分布。形式极化还可把同一问题写成两种材料极化 branch 的界面束缚电荷差；branch choice 与极化量子在 Chapter 24 中处理。

Alternating charged sheets produce a nonzero bilayer-averaged field and a thickness-growing potential. A compensating sheet of minus one-half the formal layer charge makes the two interval fields symmetric and removes the bilayer-averaged slope. This classical limit is not a unique prediction of the real carrier density or mechanism.

</DerivationBlock>

<PolarDiscontinuityExplorer />

<BilingualSection titleZh="金属化不识别补偿机制" titleEn="Metallization does not identify the compensation mechanism" eyebrow="Evidence boundary">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

界面费米能附近出现占据态，只说明该模型具有低能载流子。它不能区分理想电子重构、氧空位、阳离子互扩散、表面供体或电极电荷。DFT 超胞中加入一个氧空位并得到金属态，只证明该缺陷模型可以供电子；未比较形成能、化学势、浓度、位置和竞争缺陷时，不能证明真实样品由它主导。反过来，理想无缺陷超胞出现接近 $0.5e$ 的补偿，也不能证明实验面密度达到形式极限。

机制判定需要结合结构与缺陷表征、厚度和氧分压依赖、载流子面密度与迁移率、空间分辨价态、core-level 或宏观势弯曲、缺陷形成能、不同补偿模型的总能和谱，以及与磁性、超导或输运主张相匹配的独立证据。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Occupied interface states near the Fermi level establish low-energy carriers in the declared model, not whether they originate from ideal electronic reconstruction, oxygen vacancies, cation intermixing, surface donors, or an electrode. A metallic vacancy supercell shows that the defect can donate electrons; without formation energies, chemical potentials, concentrations, sites, and competitors it does not establish dominance in a real sample. Conversely, an ideal defect-free cell approaching $0.5e$ compensation does not prove that an experiment reaches the formal limit.

Mechanism identification combines structural and defect probes, thickness and oxygen-pressure dependence, sheet density and mobility, spatially resolved valence, core-level or potential bending, defect formation energies, total energies and spectra of competing compensation models, and independent evidence matched to magnetic, superconducting, or transport claims.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="STO 轨道子带、限域与 Rashba" titleEn="STO orbital subbands, confinement, and Rashba coupling" eyebrow="Orbital interpretation">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

STO 导带底主要来自 Ti $t_{2g}$ 轨道。$d_{xy}$ 在界面平面两个方向都有较强跃迁，通常形成较宽的二维带；$d_{xz}$ 与 $d_{yz}$ 分别在一个方向宽、另一个方向窄。法向量子限域、晶场、八面体转动和应变可移动这些子带。SOC 与界面反演破缺在交叉附近产生 avoided crossings 和 Rashba 型自旋纹理。

层分带图中的轨道颜色只是投影权重，强混合区并非严格量子数。要得到二维电子气的面密度、迁移率或输运通道，还需要费米面、占据积分、散射和多带输运，而不是只数费米附近的彩色能带。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The STO conduction edge is mainly Ti $t_{2g}$. The $d_{xy}$ orbital has strong hopping in both interface directions and commonly forms a broad two-dimensional band, whereas $d_{xz}$ and $d_{yz}$ are broad in one direction and narrow in the other. Normal confinement, crystal fields, octahedral rotations, and strain shift these subbands. SOC and broken interface inversion create avoided crossings and Rashba-like spin textures.

Orbital colours in a layer-resolved plot are projection weights, not exact quantum numbers in a strongly mixed region. A two-dimensional sheet density, mobility, or transport channel requires Fermi surfaces, occupation integrals, scattering, and multiband transport rather than merely counting coloured bands near the Fermi level.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>
