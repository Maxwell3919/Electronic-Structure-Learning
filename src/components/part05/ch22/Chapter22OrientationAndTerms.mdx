<h2 id="reading-orientation">阅读定位 | Reading Orientation</h2>

<BilingualSection titleZh="本章解决的问题" titleEn="The problem addressed by this chapter" eyebrow="Reading orientation">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

体周期计算把每个晶胞视为完全等价；表面、界面和真正的低维材料破坏了这种等价性。电子仍可在平行方向保持 Bloch 周期，但在法向方向面对真空、另一种材料、终止、重构或曲率。结果是能量参考、边界条件、筛选、态密度和可观测量都发生变化。

本章的任务是从体电子结构出发，明确边界如何进入 Hamiltonian 和静电势，并判断一个 slab/界面计算能够支持哪些结论。核心问题包括：功函数如何从真空平台和费米能定义；何时一个状态真正局域于表面；Tamm 与 Shockley 机制有何区别；界面带阶如何拆成体带边和界面 lineup；极性不连续为何产生电势累积；层间杂化、曲率和维度如何改变能带与态密度。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A bulk-periodic calculation treats every unit cell as equivalent. A surface, an interface, or an intrinsically lower-dimensional material breaks that equivalence. Bloch periodicity may survive parallel to the boundary, while the normal direction encounters vacuum, another material, a termination, reconstruction, or curvature. Energy references, boundary conditions, screening, density of states, and observables therefore change.

The chapter starts from bulk electronic structure and asks how a boundary enters the Hamiltonian and electrostatic potential, and what a slab or interface calculation can actually support. The central questions are how a work function follows from a vacuum plateau and Fermi energy; when a state is genuinely surface localized; how Tamm and Shockley mechanisms differ; how a band offset separates into bulk-edge and interface-lineup terms; why a polar discontinuity creates electrostatic buildup; and how interlayer hybridization, curvature, and dimensionality reshape bands and densities of states.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

Chapter 23 将把 Bloch 子空间变换为 Wannier 局域表示；Chapter 24 将建立周期晶体的 Berry-phase 极化和极化量子；Chapters 25–28 才负责拓扑不变量与受保护边界态。因此本章可以讨论 Shockley 的体—边界先驱结构和 Au(111) 的表面带，但不会把“表面局域”“Rashba 分裂”或“带反转”单独写成拓扑分类。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Prerequisites include Bloch states, bands, the Kohn–Sham potential, total energy, forces, strain, spin–orbit coupling, plane-wave/localized representations, and periodic Coulomb electrostatics. Chapter 20 supplies dielectric screening near a boundary, Appendix F explains how a surface dipole sets the vacuum level, and Appendix O supplies the Rashba coupling.

Chapter 23 transforms Bloch subspaces into Wannier-localized representations; Chapter 24 establishes Berry-phase polarization and the polarization quantum; Chapters 25–28 own topological invariants and protected boundary states. The present chapter may discuss Shockley’s bulk–boundary precursor and Au(111) surface bands, but surface localization, Rashba splitting, or local band inversion alone is not promoted to a topological classification.

<h2 id="objects-and-conventions">物理对象、数学对象与约定 | Objects and Conventions</h2>

<div className="chapter22-terms">
  <dl>
    <div><dt>表面法向 | surface normal</dt><dd>破坏体平移对称的方向；平行方向仍可定义二维晶格与表面 Brillouin 区。</dd></div>
    <div><dt>真空能级 | vacuum level</dt><dd>远离表面的电子静电/有效势平台；只在平台和宏观场得到控制时才是可用参考。</dd></div>
    <div><dt>功函数 Φ | work function</dt><dd>金属中从费米能移走一个电子到指定真空参考所需的能量，常写为 Φ = V_vac − E_F。</dd></div>
    <div><dt>平面平均 | planar average</dt><dd>对平行于表面的晶胞面积积分，保留法向 z 依赖。</dd></div>
    <div><dt>宏观平均 | macroscopic average</dt><dd>进一步在一个或多个晶格周期上平滑微观振荡，用于识别体平台、内建场和 lineup。</dd></div>
    <div><dt>投影体连续谱 | projected bulk continuum</dt><dd>固定表面动量 k_parallel 后，允许所有体法向波矢形成的能量集合。</dd></div>
    <div><dt>表面束缚态 | bound surface state</dt><dd>位于投影体能隙中，概率向体内和真空衰减的离散态。</dd></div>
    <div><dt>表面共振 | surface resonance</dt><dd>与投影体连续谱重叠但在表面具有增强权重和有限寿命/展宽的状态。</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Tamm 机制</dt><dd>边界局域势、终止或化学变化把状态从体带连续谱拉出。</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Shockley 机制</dt><dd>体能带角色交换/闭隙转变与边界连接相关；具体表面终止仍影响能量和可见性。</dd></div>
    <div><dt>界面 lineup ΔV | interface lineup</dt><dd>自洽界面偶极造成的两侧体样平均势平台差。</dd></div>
    <div><dt>带阶 | band offset</dt><dd>匹配应变状态和方法下的体带边相对平均势差，加上界面 lineup。</dd></div>
    <div><dt>极性不连续 | polar discontinuity</dt><dd>界面两侧形式极化/带电平面序列不兼容而产生的宏观电荷与场边界。</dd></div>

<BilingualSection titleZh="能量零点、符号和单位" titleEn="Energy zero, signs, and units" eyebrow="Convention audit">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

Kohn–Sham 势可整体加常数，因此体能带的绝对零点没有物理意义。只有在同一计算或可验证的 reference transfer 中，费米能、真空平台、平均势和带边的差才可比较。本章把电子能量向上定义为更接近真空；金属功函数采用

$$
\Phi=V_{\mathrm{vac}}-E_F>0.
$$

界面带阶采用“材料 B 带边减材料 A 带边”的有向定义，并在公式处逐项给出。电势与电子势能在电荷符号上可能相反；实际代码输出必须确认其打印对象。长度通常用 Å，面能用 eV/Å² 或 J/m²，偶极可用 eÅ/area，电场用 V/Å。教学模型使用标注的无量纲或 eV/Å 变量，不冒充代码 convention。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A Kohn–Sham potential may be shifted by a constant, so a bulk band structure has no physical absolute zero. Only differences among the Fermi energy, vacuum plateau, averaged potential, and band edges in the same calculation or a verified reference transfer can be compared. Electron energies increase toward vacuum, and the metallic work function is

$$
\Phi=V_{\mathrm{vac}}-E_F>0.
$$

A band offset is directed as the band edge of material B minus that of material A, with every term stated explicitly where used. Electrostatic potential and electron potential energy may have opposite charge signs; a code output must be identified before interpretation. Lengths are usually in Å, surface energies in eV/Å² or J/m², dipoles in eÅ per area, and fields in V/Å. Teaching models use declared dimensionless or eV/Å variables and do not impersonate a code convention.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>
