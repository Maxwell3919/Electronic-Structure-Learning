## 6.6 精确密度泛函理论的细节 | Intricacies of Exact Density Functional Theory

<BilingualSection titleZh="存在性、定义域与可微性是不同问题" titleEn="Existence, domain, and differentiability are separate questions">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

精确 DFT 的严谨性不只取决于 HK 反证法。要把定理变成变分理论，还需指定密度和外势所在的函数空间，使动能、Coulomb 能与外势能有定义，并确认极小值或下确界存在。Levy–Lieb 泛函通过约束搜索处理较宽的 $N$-representable 密度域；凸分析进一步把泛函与势之间的关系写成 Legendre–Fenchel 对偶。

即使 $F[n]$ 在某个密度上有限，也不保证存在普通泛函导数。简并、整数粒子数变化、能量面的折点以及可表示域边界都可能使 $F$ 只有次梯度。若某个势 $v$ 支持密度 $n$ 为极小点，则驻点条件更稳妥地写成

$$
-v(\mathbf r)+\mu\in\partial F[n],
$$

其中 $\partial F[n]$ 是固定粒子数约束下的次微分集合。普通 Euler 方程是该关系在单值可微情形下的特殊形式。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Rigorous exact DFT requires more than the Hohenberg–Kohn contradiction proof. A variational theory must specify function spaces for densities and potentials so that kinetic, Coulomb, and external energies are defined and the relevant minima or infima exist. Levy–Lieb constrained search extends the domain to suitable $N$-representable densities; convex analysis then expresses the relation between universal functionals and potentials through Legendre–Fenchel duality.

Finiteness of $F[n]$ does not guarantee an ordinary functional derivative. Degeneracy, changes across integer particle number, kinks in the energy surface, and boundaries of the representable domain can leave only a subgradient. If a potential $v$ supports $n$ as a minimizer, a robust stationarity statement is

$$
-v(\mathbf r)+\mu\in\partial F[n].
$$

The familiar Euler equation is the single-valued differentiable special case.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="能量与普适泛函的对偶结构 | Dual structure of energy and the universal functional"
  assumptions={[
    '势和密度属于可使配对 ∫vn 有定义的对偶函数空间。',
    '采用系综/凸的普适泛函版本。',
    '势的加法常数由固定 N 或化学势约定处理。',
  ]}
>

基态能量作为外势的泛函可写为

$$
E[v]=\inf_{n\in\mathcal D_N}\left\{F[n]+\int v n\right\}.
$$

对每个固定密度 $n$，右侧关于 $v$ 是仿射函数；对这些仿射函数取下确界，使 $E[v]$ 成为关于 $v$ 的凹泛函。反向对偶关系形式上为

$$
F[n]=\sup_v\left\{E[v]-\int v n\right\},
$$

其精确成立需要选择适当的闭凸泛函版本与函数空间。该式说明，普适泛函可以理解为从所有外势基态能量中消去外势贡献后的上确界。它仍不是计算处方：若 $E[v]$ 只在少量势上已知，不能据此恢复一般 $F[n]$。

The ground-state energy is the infimum of affine functions of $v$, and is therefore concave in the external potential. With an appropriate closed convex formulation, the universal functional is recovered by the conjugate relation

$$
F[n]=\sup_v\{E[v]-\int vn\}.
$$

This mathematical duality is not a practical reconstruction algorithm unless sufficiently complete information about $E[v]$ is available.

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="密度携带完整基态信息的边界" titleEn="Boundary of the density's formal completeness">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

对标量外势的基态问题，精确密度形式上决定 Hamiltonian 与基态可观测量，但不同性质所需的泛函可能极其非局域。偶极矩等一体量可直接由密度积分得到；成对关联、动量分布、激发谱、输运系数和拓扑响应通常不能从一个简单的局域密度表达式读取。它们若能写成基态密度泛函，也可能依赖隐含的波函数重构、响应导数、边界条件或额外基本变量。

周期体系中的宏观极化说明了边界条件的重要性。体密度在晶胞内部的局域形状不足以单独替代现代极化理论中的 Berry 相位和绝热路径。类似地，含磁场时可能需要流密度；激发问题通常需要时间依赖或 Green 函数框架。HK 定理的证据上限是其假设下的基态映射，不是所有电子性质的直接读出公式。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For a scalar-potential ground-state problem, the exact density formally determines the Hamiltonian and ground-state observables, but the corresponding observable functionals may be highly nonlocal. One-body moments can be obtained directly from density integrals; pair correlations, momentum distributions, excitation spectra, transport coefficients, and topological responses generally cannot be read from a simple local density expression. Even when they are formally density functionals, their construction may hide wavefunction recovery, response derivatives, boundary conditions, or additional basic variables.

Macroscopic polarization in a periodic solid illustrates the role of boundary conditions. The local density within a unit cell does not replace the Berry phase and adiabatic path of modern polarization theory. Likewise, magnetic fields can require current density, and excitation problems usually require time-dependent or Green-function frameworks. The evidence ceiling of the Hohenberg–Kohn theorem is a ground-state mapping under its assumptions, not a direct formula for every electronic property.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="精确 DFT 的四个独立问题" titleEn="Four independent questions in exact DFT" kind="source">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <ol>
      <li><strong>存在：</strong>所选密度域上是否定义了有限或扩展实数值的泛函；</li>
      <li><strong>变分：</strong>对目标外势的极小值是否存在并给出基态能量；</li>
      <li><strong>可微：</strong>普通泛函导数是否存在，还是只能使用次梯度；</li>
      <li><strong>可计算：</strong>是否有精确或受控近似的表达式与稳定数值实现。</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Existence:</strong> whether a finite or extended-real functional is defined on the selected density domain;</li>
      <li><strong>Variation:</strong> whether the minimum for the target potential exists and yields the ground-state energy;</li>
      <li><strong>Differentiability:</strong> whether an ordinary derivative exists or only a subgradient is available;</li>
      <li><strong>Computability:</strong> whether an exact or controlled approximate expression and stable numerical implementation are available.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 6.7 从密度继续推进的困难 | Difficulties in Proceeding from the Density

<BilingualSection titleZh="定理之后仍缺少的计算结构" titleEn="The computational structure still missing after the theorem">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

密度把变量数从多体波函数的 $3N$ 空间坐标压缩到三维函数，但未知的普适泛函保留了原多体问题的复杂性。特别是精确动能具有强非局域性：Thomas–Fermi 形式只依赖局域 $n^{5/3}$，无法普遍恢复壳层、节点、成键和量子干涉。电子相互作用也不等于经典 Hartree 项；交换与关联需要满足反对称性、关联洞、尺度关系和多种非局域约束。

直接无轨道 DFT 需要同时近似动能与交换关联。若动能泛函导数不够准确，密度优化会在最初一步失真。Kohn–Sham 构造采用另一种分解：引入能产生目标密度的辅助非相互作用轨道，把主要动能部分写为轨道的显式表达式，只将剩余未知量归入交换—关联泛函。这个构造属于 Chapter 7，并不改变 Chapter 6 的定理定义域。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The density reduces the explicit variables from a $3N$-coordinate wavefunction to a three-dimensional function, but the unknown universal functional retains the complexity of the many-body problem. Exact kinetic energy is strongly nonlocal. The local Thomas–Fermi form cannot generally recover shells, nodes, bonding, or quantum interference. Electron interaction is also not exhausted by the classical Hartree term; exchange and correlation must encode antisymmetry, correlation holes, scaling relations, and nonlocal constraints.

A direct orbital-free approach must approximate both kinetic and exchange–correlation energies. An inaccurate kinetic functional derivative distorts the density optimization at its first step. The Kohn–Sham construction uses a different decomposition: auxiliary noninteracting orbitals reproduce the target density, the dominant kinetic contribution becomes explicit in those orbitals, and the remaining unknown terms are assigned to the exchange–correlation functional. That construction belongs to Chapter 7 and does not alter the foundational density domain established here.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="从普适泛函到 Kohn–Sham 分解的预告 | Preview of the Kohn–Sham decomposition"
  assumptions={[
    '这里只定义代数分解，不推导 Kohn–Sham 方程。',
    '假设目标密度可由某个辅助非相互作用体系表示；该可表示性将在 Chapter 7 讨论。',
    'Hartree 能采用经典 Coulomb 自相互作用形式。',
  ]}
>

可在形式上加减辅助非相互作用动能 $T_s[n]$ 与 Hartree 能

$$
E_H[n]=\frac12\iint\frac{n(\mathbf r)n(\mathbf r')}{|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|}\,d\mathbf r\,d\mathbf r',
$$

定义

$$
E_{\mathrm{xc}}[n]
=F[n]-T_s[n]-E_H[n].
$$

于是

$$
E_v[n]=T_s[n]+E_H[n]+E_{\mathrm{xc}}[n]+\int vn.
$$

这个等式可以作为定义而严格成立，但没有使 $E_{\mathrm{xc}}$ 自动已知。它收集真实相互作用动能与 $T_s$ 的差、交换效应以及 Hartree 之外的关联。Chapter 7 将从轨道约束变分得到辅助方程；Chapters 8–9 才讨论 $E_{\mathrm{xc}}$ 的近似。

Adding and subtracting $T_s[n]$ and $E_H[n]$ defines $E_{\mathrm{xc}}[n]=F[n]-T_s[n]-E_H[n]$. The decomposition can be formally exact as a definition, but it does not make the exchange–correlation functional known. Orbital variation belongs to Chapter 7, and practical approximations belong to Chapters 8–9.

</DerivationBlock>

## 数值实现连接 | Connection to Numerical Implementation

<BilingualSection titleZh="从定理到程序之间的层级" titleEn="Layers between the theorem and a program">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

一个实际 DFT 结果需要经过下列不同层级：

$$
\text{HK/约束搜索}
\rightarrow
\text{能量泛函分解}
\rightarrow
\text{Euler 或 Kohn–Sham 条件}
\rightarrow
\text{基组/网格离散}
\rightarrow
\text{自洽或直接极小化算法}
\rightarrow
\text{数值参数收敛}
\rightarrow
\text{目标 observable 收敛}
\rightarrow
\text{方法适用性与科学解释}.
$$

定理只支持第一层的基态密度变分结构。程序正常退出不能证明 SCF 达到固定点；SCF 达到阈值不能证明基组、$k$ 网格、超胞、赝势或展宽已收敛；这些数值检查通过也不能证明交换—关联近似适合目标性质。验证必须针对当前 observable 和当前结论分别设计。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A practical DFT result passes through distinct layers:

$$
\text{HK/constrained search}
\rightarrow
\text{functional decomposition}
\rightarrow
\text{Euler or Kohn–Sham condition}
\rightarrow
\text{basis/grid discretization}
\rightarrow
\text{self-consistency or direct minimization}
\rightarrow
\text{numerical convergence}
\rightarrow
\text{target-observable convergence}
\rightarrow
\text{method applicability and scientific interpretation}.
$$

The theorem supports only the foundational ground-state density variational structure. Normal program termination does not establish self-consistency; an SCF threshold does not establish convergence with respect to basis, $k$ mesh, cell, pseudopotential, or smearing; and numerical convergence does not validate the functional for the target property. Each claimed observable and conclusion requires its own checks.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## 常见误判、原创练习与来源 | Misconceptions, Original Exercises, and Sources

<BilingualSection titleZh="常见误判" titleEn="Common misinterpretations">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <dl>
      <dt>“HK 定理给出了可用泛函。”</dt>
      <dd>定理与约束搜索定义精确对象和变分结构，不给出一般闭式表达式。</dd>
      <dt>“密度唯一决定波函数，所以波函数容易恢复。”</dt>
      <dd>非简并条件下存在形式映射；构造、连续性和数值稳定性未由定理保证。</dd>
      <dt>“任何非负且积分为 N 的函数都可直接试探。”</dt>
      <dd>还需声明函数空间、有限能量与纯态或系综 $N$-representability。</dd>
      <dt>“HK 第一证明对简并基态原样成立。”</dt>
      <dd>原始双重严格不等式依赖试探态不在目标基态子空间；简并需使用更一般表述。</dd>
      <dt>“LDA 由 HK 定理推出。”</dt>
      <dd>HK 定理不指定近似。TFD 和后续局域泛函引入均匀气体与局域替换假设。</dd>
      <dt>“SCF 收敛验证了 DFT。”</dt>
      <dd>SCF 是 Chapter 7 的数值固定点问题；它不验证交换关联近似或目标 observable。</dd>
    </dl>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <dl>
      <dt>“The Hohenberg–Kohn theorem supplies a usable functional.”</dt>
      <dd>The theorem and constrained search define exact objects and a variational structure, not a general closed expression.</dd>
      <dt>“The density determines the wavefunction, so reconstruction is easy.”</dt>
      <dd>A formal mapping exists under stated conditions; constructivity, continuity, and numerical stability are not guaranteed.</dd>
      <dt>“Every nonnegative function integrating to N is an admissible trial density.”</dt>
      <dd>The function space, finite-energy conditions, and pure-state or ensemble $N$-representability must also be declared.</dd>
      <dt>“The nondegenerate contradiction proof applies unchanged to degeneracy.”</dt>
      <dd>Its strict inequalities rely on the trial state being outside the target ground-state subspace.</dd>
      <dt>“LDA follows from the Hohenberg–Kohn theorem.”</dt>
      <dd>The theorem does not select an approximation; local functionals add uniform-gas and locality assumptions.</dd>
      <dt>“SCF convergence validates DFT.”</dt>
      <dd>Self-consistency is a numerical fixed-point property and does not validate the functional or target observable.</dd>
    </dl>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="原创练习" titleEn="Original exercises" eyebrow="Practice">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <ol>
      <li><strong>势的加法常数。</strong> 证明 $v\to v+C$ 使固定 $N$ 的所有本征能平移 $CN$ 而本征态不变。说明巨正则系综中为什么还需同时讨论化学势。</li>
      <li><strong>HK 反证法审计。</strong> 在两条变分不等式旁逐项标出：何处使用同一密度、何处使用局域一体外势、何处使用非简并性。将一个条件移除并说明证明在哪一行中断。</li>
      <li><strong>约束搜索重排。</strong> 从 $\min_\Psi\langle\Psi|\hat H_v|\Psi\rangle$ 出发，写出按密度划分波函数集合的完整集合论步骤。解释为何这不是交换两个任意极小化顺序。</li>
      <li><strong>泛函导数。</strong> 对 $G[n]=A\int n^\alpha d\mathbf r$ 求导，并用量纲分析确定 $A$ 的量纲。分别代入 TF 的 $\alpha=5/3$ 与 Dirac 交换的 $\alpha=4/3$。</li>
      <li><strong>Hartree 导数。</strong> 对对称双积分显式变分，说明因子 $1/2$ 如何与两个相同贡献抵消。</li>
      <li><strong>凸性。</strong> 使用两个密度矩阵的概率混合证明系综 $F[n]$ 的凸性。说明该证明为何不能直接推出严格凸性或唯一极小密度。</li>
      <li><strong>模型复核。</strong> 对两站点模型解析求本征值和基态占据，验证 $n_L+n_R=1$ 与共同势平移不变量。指出模型与连续相互作用体系之间缺失的自由度。</li>
      <li><strong>证据要求。</strong> 某计算报告“SCF 收敛，因此能隙由 HK 定理保证正确”。列出至少四个独立检查，并说明每个检查能支持和不能支持的结论。</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Additive constants.</strong> Prove that $v\to v+C$ shifts every fixed-$N$ eigenvalue by $CN$ without changing eigenstates. Explain why the chemical potential must also be considered in a grand-canonical setting.</li>
      <li><strong>Audit the contradiction proof.</strong> Mark where the common density, local one-body potential, and nondegeneracy enter. Remove one assumption and identify the exact failed line.</li>
      <li><strong>Regroup constrained search.</strong> Starting from the wavefunction variational principle, partition the state space by density. Explain why this is not an arbitrary interchange of two minimizations.</li>
      <li><strong>Functional derivative.</strong> Differentiate $G[n]=A\int n^\alpha$ and determine the dimensions of $A$. Apply the result to the Thomas–Fermi and Dirac exponents.</li>
      <li><strong>Hartree derivative.</strong> Vary the symmetric double integral explicitly and show how its factor of one half cancels the two identical contributions.</li>
      <li><strong>Convexity.</strong> Prove ensemble convexity by mixing density matrices. Explain why the argument does not establish strict convexity or a unique minimizing density.</li>
      <li><strong>Model reproduction.</strong> Diagonalize the two-site model, verify normalization and additive-shift invariance, and list degrees of freedom missing from a continuum interacting system.</li>
      <li><strong>Evidence requirements.</strong> A calculation claims that an SCF-converged band gap is guaranteed correct by the Hohenberg–Kohn theorem. List at least four independent checks and state the evidence ceiling of each.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>
