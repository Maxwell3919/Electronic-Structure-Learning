## 6.3 Hohenberg–Kohn 定理 | The Hohenberg–Kohn Theorems

<BilingualSection titleZh="定理的问题设置" titleEn="Problem setting of the theorems">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

固定电子数 $N$、动能算符 $\hat T$ 和电子间相互作用 $\hat W$。对于允许的局域标量外势 $v(\mathbf r)$，定义

$$
\hat H_v=\hat T+\hat W+\int v(\mathbf r)\hat n(\mathbf r)\,d\mathbf r.
$$

两个相差常数的外势 $v'(\mathbf r)=v(\mathbf r)+C$ 给出相同本征态，因为 $\hat H_{v'}=\hat H_v+CN$。因此外势的唯一性只能针对等价类 $[v]=\{v+C\}$ 表述。以下第一证明先采用非简并基态；简并情形随后单独说明。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Fix the electron number $N$, kinetic operator $\hat T$, and electron–electron interaction $\hat W$. For an admissible local scalar potential $v(\mathbf r)$, define

$$
\hat H_v=\hat T+\hat W+\int v(\mathbf r)\hat n(\mathbf r)\,d\mathbf r.
$$

Potentials differing by a constant, $v'=v+C$, produce the same eigenstates because $\hat H_{v'}=\hat H_v+CN$. Uniqueness can therefore apply only to the equivalence class $[v]=\{v+C\}$. The first proof below assumes a nondegenerate ground state; degeneracy is treated separately.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="第一定理：基态密度确定外势等价类 | First theorem: the ground-state density determines the potential class"
  assumptions={[
    '固定 N、固定相互作用 W，并限制在允许的局域外势类。',
    '两个 Hamiltonian 都存在非简并归一化基态。',
    '假设两外势不只相差常数，却产生同一个基态密度 n(r)。',
    '使用 Rayleigh–Ritz 严格不等式；若试探态恰为基态，不等式必须改写。',
  ]}
>

设 $v$ 与 $v'$ 的非简并基态分别为 $\Psi$ 与 $\Psi'$，基态能量为 $E_0$ 与 $E_0'$。反设 $v-v'$ 不是常数，但两态产生相同密度 $n$。

因为 $\Psi'$ 不是 $\hat H_v$ 的基态，变分原理给出严格不等式

$$
E_0
<\langle\Psi'|\hat H_v|\Psi'\rangle.
$$

加减 $\hat H_{v'}$：

$$
\begin{aligned}
E_0
&<\langle\Psi'|\hat H_{v'}|\Psi'\rangle
+\langle\Psi'|\hat V_v-\hat V_{v'}|\Psi'\rangle\\
&=E_0'+\int [v(\mathbf r)-v'(\mathbf r)]n(\mathbf r)\,d\mathbf r.
\end{aligned}
$$

交换两个体系，同理有

$$
E_0'
<E_0+\int [v'(\mathbf r)-v(\mathbf r)]n(\mathbf r)\,d\mathbf r.
$$

将两式相加，势能积分逐点抵消，得到

$$
E_0+E_0'<E_0'+E_0,
$$

形成矛盾。因此两个不等价外势不能拥有同一个非简并基态密度；$n_0$ 确定 $v$ 到一个加法常数。

The proof assumes two inequivalent potentials have the same density. Rayleigh–Ritz applied in both directions gives

$$
E_0<E_0'+\int(v-v')n,
\qquad
E_0'<E_0+\int(v'-v)n.
$$

Adding the inequalities yields $E_0+E_0'<E_0+E_0'$, a contradiction. Hence a nondegenerate ground-state density determines the external potential up to an additive constant.

**等号依据与断点。** 期望值 $\langle\Psi'|\hat V_v-\hat V_{v'}|\Psi'\rangle$ 能写成 $\int(v-v')n$，因为外势算符是一体局域算符。严格小于号依赖非简并性以及 $\Psi'$ 不是 $\hat H_v$ 的基态。若两 Hamiltonian 共享某个基态，或存在简并子空间，不能原样使用这两个严格不等式。

**What the proof does not establish.** It does not provide a constructive inversion algorithm $n\mapsto v$, a stable numerical procedure, or a formula for the universal functional. It is an injectivity statement on the specified ground-state domain.

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="由密度到所有基态量的逻辑连接" titleEn="Logical connection from the density to ground-state quantities">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

在第一定理的条件下，$n_0$ 确定 $[v]$，进而确定 $\hat H_v$。非简并基态随后由 Hamiltonian 确定到整体相位，因此任意基态可观测量都可以形式上写成 $n_0$ 的泛函。这里的“可以写成”是存在性陈述。它没有说明从密度恢复波函数或可观测量的映射是否具有闭式表达、连续性或数值稳定性。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Under the assumptions of the first theorem, $n_0$ determines $[v]$, which determines $\hat H_v$. A nondegenerate ground state is then fixed up to a global phase, so every ground-state observable can formally be regarded as a functional of $n_0$. This is an existence statement. It does not imply a closed, continuous, or numerically stable reconstruction of the wavefunction or observable from the density.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="第二定理：密度变分给出基态能量上界 | Second theorem: density variation gives an upper bound"
  assumptions={[
    '先在能由允许反对称波函数产生的试探密度上定义能量。',
    '固定粒子数 N，并使用与目标体系相同的 T、W 和外势 v。',
    '试探态归一化；基态存在。',
  ]}
>

令 $\widetilde n$ 由某个允许试探波函数 $\widetilde\Psi$ 产生。定义该态的内部能量与外势能：

$$
\widetilde E_v
=\langle\widetilde\Psi|\hat T+\hat W|\widetilde\Psi\rangle
+\int v(\mathbf r)\widetilde n(\mathbf r)\,d\mathbf r.
$$

由多体变分原理，

$$
\widetilde E_v
=\langle\widetilde\Psi|\hat H_v|\widetilde\Psi\rangle
\ge E_0.
$$

当试探态属于基态子空间时取等号。若已知一个对允许密度定义的内部泛函 $F[n]$，则

$$
E_v[n]=F[n]+\int v n
$$

在基态密度处达到最小值，最小值为 $E_0$。HK 原始论证对由外势基态产生的密度进行表述；Levy 约束搜索在 §6.4 中给出更清楚的允许域定义，并把波函数变分严格压缩成密度变分。

For any admissible trial wavefunction producing $\widetilde n$,

$$
\langle\widetilde\Psi|\hat H_v|\widetilde\Psi\rangle\ge E_0.
$$

Once the internal functional is defined on an admissible density domain, the density functional $E_v[n]=F[n]+\int vn$ is minimized by a ground-state density. Equality holds for states in the ground-state subspace. Levy's formulation makes the density domain and the reduction from wavefunction variation explicit.

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="简并情形的边界" titleEn="Boundary introduced by degeneracy" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

简并不会使基态密度变分原理失效，但会改变“密度唯一决定波函数”的表述。一个外势可有多个正交基态及多个纯态密度；它们的系综组合也是基态密度。不同势若共享某个基态，原始双重严格不等式不能直接使用。更一般的表述以基态子空间、系综密度与约束搜索为中心：基态密度仍与允许外势和基态集合之间存在受约束的对应关系，但不应把它缩写为任意情形下的单值 $n\leftrightarrow\Psi$。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Degeneracy does not invalidate the ground-state density variational principle, but it changes the claim that a density uniquely determines a wavefunction. One potential may possess several orthogonal ground states and several pure-state densities; ensemble mixtures of them are also ground-state densities. If two potentials share a ground state, the original pair of strict inequalities cannot be used unchanged. General formulations therefore use ground-state subspaces, ensemble densities, and constrained search rather than an unrestricted one-to-one correspondence $n\leftrightarrow\Psi$.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="第一、第二定理分别没有解决什么" titleEn="What the two theorems leave unresolved" kind="source">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

第一定理解决基态密度对外势的唯一性，第二定理解决基态能量的变分排序。它们不提供 $F[n]$ 的显式公式，不保证普通泛函导数处处存在，不说明所有归一化非负密度都可由物理态产生，也不构造实际电子结构算法。这些断点分别引向约束搜索、凸分析与 Chapter 7 的 Kohn–Sham 辅助体系。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The first theorem establishes uniqueness of the external-potential class from a ground-state density; the second establishes the variational ordering of ground-state energy. They do not give an explicit $F[n]$, guarantee ordinary differentiability everywhere, make every normalized nonnegative density physical, or construct an electronic-structure algorithm. Those gaps motivate constrained search, convex analysis, and the Kohn–Sham auxiliary system of Chapter 7.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>
