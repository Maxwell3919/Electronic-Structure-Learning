## 阅读定位 | Reading Orientation

<BilingualSection titleZh="本章解决的问题" titleEn="The problem solved in this chapter" eyebrow="Orientation">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

对给定粒子数 $N$ 的相互作用电子体系，通常的基本变量是含有 $3N$ 个空间坐标并满足反对称性的多体波函数 $\Psi$。Chapter 6 改变基本变量：它研究基态一体密度 $n(\mathbf r)$ 是否足以确定外势、基态和基态可观测量，以及能量是否可以在密度空间中直接极小化。

本章建立的是精确 DFT 的逻辑基础。Hohenberg–Kohn 定理说明，在固定粒子数和相互作用形式下，基态密度确定外势到一个加法常数；Levy 约束搜索则把“只在某个外势的基态密度上成立”的叙述改写为对允许密度集合的普适极小化。两者都不提供可直接计算的闭式泛函，也不产生 Kohn–Sham 轨道。辅助非相互作用体系与自洽方程属于 Chapter 7。

前置知识包括多体 Hamiltonian、变分原理、约化密度和泛函导数的基本概念。读完后，应能检查 HK 反证法的每个假设，区分 $N$-representability 与 $v$-representability，并判断一个所谓“密度泛函推导”究竟使用了严格关系、历史近似，还是未说明的可微性假设。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For an interacting electronic system with fixed particle number $N$, the conventional basic variable is an antisymmetric many-body wavefunction $\Psi$ depending on $3N$ spatial coordinates. Chapter 6 changes the basic variable. It asks whether the ground-state one-body density $n(\mathbf r)$ determines the external potential, the ground state, and ground-state observables, and whether the energy can be minimized directly in density space.

The chapter establishes the logic of exact DFT. The Hohenberg–Kohn theorem states that, for fixed particle number and interaction, the ground-state density determines the external potential up to an additive constant. Levy's constrained search replaces a statement restricted to densities that already arise as ground states of some potential with a universal minimization over an admissible density set. Neither result supplies a closed, readily computable functional, and neither introduces Kohn–Sham orbitals. The auxiliary noninteracting system and its self-consistent equations belong to Chapter 7.

The prerequisites are the many-body Hamiltonian, the variational principle, reduced densities, and a basic understanding of functional derivatives. After completing the chapter, the reader should be able to audit every assumption in the Hohenberg–Kohn contradiction proof, distinguish $N$-representability from $v$-representability, and identify whether a proposed density-functional derivation uses an exact relation, a historical approximation, or an unstated differentiability assumption.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## 物理对象、数学对象与符号 | Physical and Mathematical Objects

<BilingualSection titleZh="对象层级" titleEn="Hierarchy of objects" eyebrow="Notation">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

本章采用 Hartree 原子单位，除非另有说明：$\hbar=m_e=e=4\pi\epsilon_0=1$。体系含 $N$ 个电子，电子—电子相互作用固定为 Coulomb 形式。外势记为 $v(\mathbf r)$，通常由固定原子核产生，但定理只要求它属于使 Hamiltonian 有定义并存在基态的允许势类。

- $\Psi(\mathbf x_1,\ldots,\mathbf x_N)$ 是归一化反对称多体波函数，$\mathbf x=(\mathbf r,\sigma)$ 同时包含空间和自旋坐标。
- $n(\mathbf r)$ 是一体粒子数密度，单位为长度$^{-3}$，满足 $n\ge 0$ 与 $\int n=N$。
- $v(\mathbf r)$ 是单粒子外势，能量泛函中的外势项为 $\int v n$。
- $F[n]$ 是固定相互作用下的普适泛函；方括号表示其输入是一个函数，而非有限维变量。
- $\delta F[n]/\delta n(\mathbf r)$ 是泛函导数；在精确泛函不可微处，更合适的对象可能是次梯度而不是普通导数。
- $E_v[n]$ 是针对特定外势 $v$ 的总能量泛函；下标 $v$ 表明它并不普适。
- $\mu$ 是固定粒子数约束的 Lagrange 乘子；在适当条件下可解释为化学势。

密度不是轨道、波函数或量子态。它是由量子态映射得到的一体可观测量。泛函也不是“密度的一个数值表”；它是把整个允许密度函数映射到一个数的规则。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Hartree atomic units are used unless stated otherwise: $\hbar=m_e=e=4\pi\epsilon_0=1$. The system contains $N$ electrons, and the electron–electron interaction is fixed to the Coulomb form. The external potential is denoted by $v(\mathbf r)$. It is commonly generated by clamped nuclei, but the theorem only requires an admissible class for which the Hamiltonian is defined and a ground state exists.

- $\Psi(\mathbf x_1,\ldots,\mathbf x_N)$ is a normalized antisymmetric many-body wavefunction; $\mathbf x=(\mathbf r,\sigma)$ contains spatial and spin coordinates.
- $n(\mathbf r)$ is the one-body particle-number density, with dimensions of inverse volume, satisfying $n\ge 0$ and $\int n=N$.
- $v(\mathbf r)$ is a one-particle external potential; its energy contribution is $\int v n$.
- $F[n]$ is the universal functional at fixed interaction. Square brackets indicate that the argument is a function rather than a finite-dimensional coordinate.
- $\delta F[n]/\delta n(\mathbf r)$ is a functional derivative. At nondifferentiable densities, a subgradient can be the appropriate mathematical object.
- $E_v[n]$ is the total-energy functional for a specified external potential $v$ and is therefore not universal.
- $\mu$ is the Lagrange multiplier enforcing fixed particle number; under suitable conditions it has the interpretation of a chemical potential.

A density is not an orbital, wavefunction, or quantum state. It is a one-body observable obtained from a state. A functional is not merely a table of density values; it is a rule that maps an entire admissible density function to a scalar.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="从多体波函数得到一体密度 | From the many-body wavefunction to the one-body density"
  assumptions={[
    '固定粒子数 N；多体波函数归一化并满足费米反对称性。',
    '自旋变量离散求和；空间积分覆盖整个定义域。',
    '密度采用粒子数归一化，不采用单位归一化。',
  ]}
>

对归一化波函数，密度定义为

$$
n_\Psi(\mathbf r)
=
N\sum_{\sigma_1\cdots\sigma_N}
\int d\mathbf r_2\cdots d\mathbf r_N\,
\left|\Psi(\mathbf r\sigma_1,\mathbf r_2\sigma_2,\ldots,\mathbf r_N\sigma_N)\right|^2.
$$

将它对 $\mathbf r$ 积分，并利用所有电子坐标在 $|\Psi|^2$ 中的等价性，得到

$$
\int n_\Psi(\mathbf r)\,d\mathbf r
=
N\langle\Psi|\Psi\rangle=N.
$$

因此 $n$ 的量纲为 $L^{-3}$，$v n\,d\mathbf r$ 的量纲为能量。反过来，一个非负且积分为 $N$ 的函数并不自动保证存在反对称有限动能波函数产生它；允许集合还需要可表示性条件。

For a normalized wavefunction, the density is

$$
n_\Psi(\mathbf r)
=
N\sum_{\sigma_1\cdots\sigma_N}
\int d\mathbf r_2\cdots d\mathbf r_N\,
\left|\Psi(\mathbf r\sigma_1,\mathbf r_2\sigma_2,\ldots,\mathbf r_N\sigma_N)\right|^2.
$$

Integrating over $\mathbf r$ and using the equivalence of electron coordinates in $|\Psi|^2$ gives $\int n_\Psi=N$. Thus $n$ has dimensions $L^{-3}$ and $v n\,d\mathbf r$ has dimensions of energy. Conversely, nonnegativity and normalization alone do not guarantee that an antisymmetric finite-kinetic-energy wavefunction generates the density; representability conditions remain necessary.

</DerivationBlock>

## 6.1 概览 | Overview

<BilingualSection titleZh="固定外势问题与密度变分" titleEn="The fixed-potential problem and density variation">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

在 Born–Oppenheimer 电子问题中，原子核位置固定，电子 Hamiltonian 可写成

$$
\hat H_v=\hat T+\hat W+\hat V_v,
\qquad
\hat V_v=\sum_{i=1}^{N}v(\mathbf r_i)
=\int v(\mathbf r)\hat n(\mathbf r)\,d\mathbf r.
$$

$\hat T$ 与 $\hat W$ 对所有具有相同粒子数和相互作用形式的体系相同，材料身份进入 $v(\mathbf r)$。若存在一个普适规则 $F[n]$，可把内部动能与相互作用能写成密度的泛函，则特定外势的能量具有形式

$$
E_v[n]=F[n]+\int v(\mathbf r)n(\mathbf r)\,d\mathbf r.
$$

关键问题不是这个式子能否形式化写出，而是：允许哪些密度进入变分、$F[n]$ 如何定义、极小值是否确实等于多体基态能量，以及极小密度是否为真实基态密度。HK 定理与约束搜索分别解决这些逻辑环节。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

In the Born–Oppenheimer electronic problem, the nuclei are clamped and the electronic Hamiltonian is

$$
\hat H_v=\hat T+\hat W+\hat V_v,
\qquad
\hat V_v=\sum_{i=1}^{N}v(\mathbf r_i)
=\int v(\mathbf r)\hat n(\mathbf r)\,d\mathbf r.
$$

The operators $\hat T$ and $\hat W$ are common to all systems with the same particle number and interaction; material identity enters through $v(\mathbf r)$. If a universal rule $F[n]$ represents internal kinetic and interaction energy, the energy in a specified potential has the form

$$
E_v[n]=F[n]+\int v(\mathbf r)n(\mathbf r)\,d\mathbf r.
$$

The essential questions are not answered by merely writing this expression. One must define the admissible density domain, define $F[n]$, show that the minimum equals the many-body ground-state energy, and show that the minimizing density is a physical ground-state density. The Hohenberg–Kohn theorems and constrained search address different parts of that logical chain.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="带粒子数约束的 Euler 条件 | Euler condition at fixed particle number"
  assumptions={[
    '试探密度属于声明的允许集合，并满足 ∫n=N。',
    '在所考察密度处，F 存在普通泛函导数；不可微情形需要次梯度表述。',
    '允许变分 δn 保持必要的边界、正则性与可表示性条件。',
  ]}
>

引入 Lagrange 泛函

$$
\mathcal L[n]=F[n]+\int v(\mathbf r)n(\mathbf r)\,d\mathbf r
-\mu\left(\int n(\mathbf r)\,d\mathbf r-N\right).
$$

对任意允许的一阶变分 $\delta n$，

$$
\delta\mathcal L
=
\int d\mathbf r\,
\left[
\frac{\delta F[n]}{\delta n(\mathbf r)}+v(\mathbf r)-\mu
\right]\delta n(\mathbf r).
$$

若极小点位于允许集合的光滑内部，括号中的函数必须为零：

$$
\frac{\delta F[n]}{\delta n(\mathbf r)}+v(\mathbf r)=\mu.
$$

这个 Euler 方程是驻点必要条件，不是可计算算法。它没有告诉我们 $F[n]$ 的显式形式，也没有保证该驻点唯一、是全局极小值或位于可表示密度集合的内部。密度在边界上、整数粒子数处或精确泛函不可微时，需要不等式或次梯度条件。

Introducing

$$
\mathcal L[n]=F[n]+\int v n-\mu\left(\int n-N\right)
$$

gives

$$
\delta\mathcal L=\int\left[\frac{\delta F}{\delta n}+v-\mu\right]\delta n.
$$

At a smooth interior minimizer, stationarity yields $\delta F/\delta n+v=\mu$. This is a necessary stationary condition, not a computational procedure. It does not reveal the explicit functional, establish uniqueness, prove a global minimum, or ensure that the density lies in the interior of the representable set. Boundary densities and nondifferentiable exact functionals require inequality or subgradient formulations.

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="严格存在不等于实际可用" titleEn="Formal existence is not practical availability" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

“基态能量是密度泛函”不等于“已知该泛函”。即使精确泛函在适当定义域上存在，仍可能没有闭式表达、普通泛函导数或稳定的离散算法。实际 DFT 的误差来源还包括交换—关联近似、基组或网格、赝势、边界条件、占据处理和未收敛的目标 observable。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Saying that the ground-state energy is a functional of the density does not mean that the functional is known. Even when an exact functional exists on an appropriate domain, it may lack a closed expression, an ordinary functional derivative, or a stable discretization. Practical DFT also introduces errors from exchange–correlation approximation, basis or grid, pseudopotential, boundary conditions, occupation treatment, and unconverged target observables.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 6.2 Thomas–Fermi–Dirac 近似 | Thomas–Fermi–Dirac Approximation

<BilingualSection titleZh="历史密度泛函" titleEn="A historical density functional">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Thomas–Fermi 理论把每个空间点附近的电子看成局域均匀、零温、无相互作用 Fermi 气体。对于自旋简并度为二的三维体系，局域 Fermi 波矢满足 $n=k_F^3/(3\pi^2)$。将均匀气体每体积动能密度代入非均匀密度，得到局域动能泛函

$$
T_{\mathrm{TF}}[n]=C_F\int n(\mathbf r)^{5/3}\,d\mathbf r,
\qquad
C_F=\frac{3}{10}(3\pi^2)^{2/3}.
$$

Dirac 交换采用同样的局域替换：

$$
E_x^{\mathrm D}[n]=-C_x\int n(\mathbf r)^{4/3}\,d\mathbf r,
\qquad
C_x=\frac34\left(\frac3\pi\right)^{1/3}.
$$

加上经典 Hartree 能和外势项，Thomas–Fermi–Dirac 能量为

$$
E_{\mathrm{TFD}}[n]
=
C_F\int n^{5/3}
+\frac12\iint\frac{n(\mathbf r)n(\mathbf r')}{|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|}\,d\mathbf r\,d\mathbf r'
+\int vn
-C_x\int n^{4/3}.
$$

这里的每一项都是显式密度泛函，但 $T_{\mathrm{TF}}$ 与 $E_x^{\mathrm D}$ 是局域近似。它们并非 HK 定理推出的唯一表达式，也没有轨道、壳层和精细化学键结构。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Thomas–Fermi theory treats the neighborhood of each point as a locally uniform, zero-temperature, noninteracting Fermi gas. For a three-dimensional spin-degenerate system, $n=k_F^3/(3\pi^2)$. Substitution of the uniform-gas kinetic-energy density gives

$$
T_{\mathrm{TF}}[n]=C_F\int n(\mathbf r)^{5/3}\,d\mathbf r,
\qquad
C_F=\frac{3}{10}(3\pi^2)^{2/3}.
$$

Dirac exchange applies an analogous local substitution,

$$
E_x^{\mathrm D}[n]=-C_x\int n(\mathbf r)^{4/3}\,d\mathbf r,
\qquad
C_x=\frac34\left(\frac3\pi\right)^{1/3}.
$$

Together with the classical Hartree and external-potential terms, this produces the explicit Thomas–Fermi–Dirac functional shown above. The kinetic and exchange terms are local approximations rather than unique consequences of the Hohenberg–Kohn theorem. The formulation contains no orbitals, shell structure, or detailed chemical bonding.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="Thomas–Fermi 动能系数 | Coefficient of the Thomas–Fermi kinetic term"
  assumptions={[
    '三维、零温、无相互作用、两重自旋简并的均匀电子气。',
    '原子单位；每个 k 态在大体积 Ω 中按 Ω/(2π)^3 计数。',
    '局域密度近似把均匀气体关系逐点应用于缓变密度。',
  ]}
>

每体积的粒子数为

$$
n=2\int_{|\mathbf k|\le k_F}\frac{d^3k}{(2\pi)^3}
=\frac{k_F^3}{3\pi^2}.
$$

每体积动能为

$$
t_s
=2\int_{|\mathbf k|\le k_F}\frac{k^2}{2}\frac{d^3k}{(2\pi)^3}
=\frac{k_F^5}{10\pi^2}.
$$

由 $k_F=(3\pi^2n)^{1/3}$ 消去 $k_F$：

$$
t_s(n)=\frac{3}{10}(3\pi^2)^{2/3}n^{5/3}.
$$

再把 $t_s(n)$ 对空间积分，得到 $T_{\mathrm{TF}}[n]$。量纲检查：$n^{5/3}d^3r$ 的量纲为 $L^{-2}$，在原子单位中与动能量纲一致。这个推导严格适用于均匀无相互作用气体；把它逐点用于非均匀体系是近似步骤。

The state count gives $n=k_F^3/(3\pi^2)$, while the kinetic-energy density is $t_s=k_F^5/(10\pi^2)$. Eliminating $k_F$ yields $t_s=(3/10)(3\pi^2)^{2/3}n^{5/3}$. Spatial integration gives $T_{\mathrm{TF}}[n]$. The uniform-gas integral is exact for the stated reference system; applying it pointwise to an inhomogeneous density is the approximation.

</DerivationBlock>

<DerivationBlock
  title="TFD Euler 方程 | Euler equation of the TFD functional"
  assumptions={[
    '密度足够规则且在考察区域为正；粒子数固定。',
    'Hartree 积分有限；外势与密度乘积可积。',
    '使用普通泛函导数，不处理核尖点或密度支撑边界上的分布项。',
  ]}
>

各项的泛函导数为

$$
\frac{\delta T_{\mathrm{TF}}}{\delta n}
=\frac53C_F n^{2/3},
\qquad
\frac{\delta E_x^{\mathrm D}}{\delta n}
=-\frac43C_x n^{1/3},
$$

以及

$$
\frac{\delta E_H}{\delta n(\mathbf r)}
=\int\frac{n(\mathbf r')}{|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|}\,d\mathbf r'
\equiv v_H(\mathbf r).
$$

因此固定 $N$ 的驻点满足

$$
\frac53C_F n(\mathbf r)^{2/3}
+v(\mathbf r)+v_H(\mathbf r)
-\frac43C_x n(\mathbf r)^{1/3}
=\mu.
$$

这是非线性积分方程。它展示了“密度直接变分”的含义，也暴露其困难：局域动能无法重现原子壳层，密度尾部与核附近行为不受轨道节点结构约束，化学键的方向性也没有可靠编码。加入 Dirac 交换不会消除这些缺陷。

The functional derivatives give the nonlinear integral equation above. It is a direct density-only stationarity condition. Its limitations are structural: a local kinetic term does not reproduce atomic shells, orbital nodal structure, or directional bonding, and adding local Dirac exchange does not repair those missing degrees of freedom.

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="Thomas–Fermi–Dirac 的证据上限" titleEn="Evidence ceiling of Thomas–Fermi–Dirac" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

TFD 说明“显式密度泛函并进行变分”在数学上可以实施，也提供大粒子数与缓变密度极限的物理参照。它不能证明一般材料的定量精度，不能恢复 Kohn–Sham 轨道或能带，也不能由一次数值收敛升级为化学键、壳层或实验谱的可靠描述。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

TFD demonstrates that an explicit density functional can be varied and provides a useful large-particle-number or slowly varying reference. It does not establish quantitative accuracy for general materials, recover Kohn–Sham orbitals or bands, or become a reliable description of shells, bonding, or spectra merely because its numerical equation converges.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>
