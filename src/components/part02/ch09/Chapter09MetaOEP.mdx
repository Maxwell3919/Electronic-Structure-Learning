## 9.4 动能密度泛函：Meta-GGA | Functionals of the Kinetic-Energy Density: Meta-GGAs

<BilingualSection titleZh="在 density 和 gradient 之外加入 orbital environment" titleEn="Adding orbital-environment information beyond density and gradient">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

Meta-GGA 通常在 $n_\sigma$ 和 $\nabla n_\sigma$ 之外使用 positive kinetic-energy density

$$
\tau_\sigma(\mathbf r)
=\frac12\sum_i f_{i\sigma}|\nabla\phi_{i\sigma}(\mathbf r)|^2,
$$

或等价/相关的 Laplacian information。$\tau$ 能区分具有相同局域 density 和 gradient、但由不同 orbital overlap 产生的环境，例如 one-orbital region、slowly varying gas 和 weak-bond/overlap region。

一般能量写成

$$
E_{\mathrm{xc}}^{\mathrm{meta}}
=\int d\mathbf r\,
 f(n_\uparrow,n_\downarrow,\nabla n_\uparrow,\nabla n_\downarrow,	au_\uparrow,	au_\downarrow).
$$

虽然仍写成单点积分，$\tau$ 由轨道决定，因此它不是显式 density-only semilocal functional。对轨道直接变分产生 differential GKS operator，而不是普通 GGA 的单纯局域乘法势。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A meta-GGA commonly uses the positive kinetic-energy density

$$
\tau_\sigma(\mathbf r)=\frac12\sum_i f_{i\sigma}|\nabla\phi_{i\sigma}(\mathbf r)|^2
$$

in addition to spin densities and gradients, or employs related Laplacian information. Kinetic-energy density distinguishes orbital environments that share the same local density and gradient, including one-orbital, slowly varying, and weak-overlap regions.

The energy remains a one-point integral, but $\tau$ is orbital dependent. Direct orbital variation therefore produces a differential GKS operator rather than only the local multiplicative potential of an ordinary GGA.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="meta-GGA 的 differential GKS operator | Differential GKS operator of a meta-GGA"
  assumptions={[
    '先写单自旋通道并固定占据；f 对 τ 可微。',
    '轨道变分保持边界，使分部积分 surface term 消失。',
  ]}
>

取

$$
E[\{\phi\}]=\int d\mathbf r\,f(n,\nabla n,\tau),
\qquad
\tau=\frac12\sum_i f_i\nabla\phi_i^*\cdot\nabla\phi_i.
$$

对 $\phi_i^*$ 的 $\tau$ 部分变化为

$$
\delta\tau
=\frac12 f_i\nabla\delta\phi_i^*\cdot\nabla\phi_i.
$$

于是

$$
\delta E_\tau
=\frac12 f_i\int d\mathbf r\,
\frac{\partial f}{\partial\tau}
\nabla\delta\phi_i^*\cdot\nabla\phi_i.
$$

分部积分得到

$$
\frac{\delta E_\tau}{\delta\phi_i^*(\mathbf r)}
=-\frac12 f_i\nabla\cdot
\left[
\frac{\partial f}{\partial\tau}(\mathbf r)
\nabla\phi_i(\mathbf r)
\right].
$$

因此 meta-GGA orbital equation 含

$$
-\frac12\nabla\cdot
\left[1+\frac{\partial f}{\partial\tau}\right]\nabla,
$$

加上来自 $n$ 和 $\nabla n$ 的局域项。该 differential operator 作用于每条轨道，不等于一个共同乘法函数。不同代码若采用普通 KS OEP-like approximation 或 true GKS meta-GGA，eigenvalues 和 forces 可能不同，必须报告实现方式。

The kinetic-energy-density derivative modifies the differential kinetic operator. This is a GKS operator rather than a common multiplicative potential, so implementation choices affect eigenvalues and derivatives.

</DerivationBlock>

<DerivationBlock
  title="one-orbital、uniform 与 overlap indicators | One-orbital, uniform, and overlap indicators"
  assumptions={[
    'n>0，τ 满足 von Weizsäcker bound τ≥τW。',
    '采用 positive kinetic-energy-density convention。',
  ]}
>

von Weizsäcker kinetic-energy density 为

$$
\tau_W=\frac{|\nabla n|^2}{8n}.
$$

若单个实空间轨道产生 density，$n=f|\phi|^2$，则

$$
\nabla n=2f\phi\nabla\phi,
\qquad
\tau=\frac12 f|\nabla\phi|^2,
$$

从而 $\tau=\tau_W$。均匀气参考为

$$
\tau_{\mathrm{unif}}
=\frac3{10}(3\pi^2)^{2/3}n^{5/3}.
$$

常用指标

$$
z=\frac{\tau_W}{\tau},
\qquad
\alpha=\frac{\tau-\tau_W}{\tau_{\mathrm{unif}}}
$$

满足 one-orbital limit $z=1,\alpha=0$；slowly varying uniform-like region 常有 $\alpha\approx1$；大量 excess kinetic density 可给较大 $\alpha$。这些只是 local environment indicators，必须由完整 functional 把它们映射到能量。

For a one-orbital real density, the positive kinetic-energy density equals the von Weizsäcker form. The dimensionless indicators compare the actual orbital kinetic density with one-orbital and uniform-gas references but do not themselves define the functional.

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="SCAN 的约束思想与 regularization" titleEn="The constraint strategy and regularization of SCAN">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

SCAN 以 $n,\nabla n,\tau$ 为输入，在 normal-density regime 中满足一组可同时满足的 exact constraints，并用 $\alpha$ 等指标在 covalent、metallic 和 weak-bond-like environments 之间插值。其“非经验”含义是参数由 constraints 和 appropriate norms 决定，而不是没有任何设计选择。

原始 interpolation 在某些区域导数变化较快，可能增加 dense grids、SCF 和 force sensitivity。rSCAN、r²SCAN 等 regularized variants 改善 numerical smoothness，同时改变部分 constraint realization。报告必须写明具体版本；“SCAN family” 不是单一公式。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

SCAN uses $n$, $\nabla n$, and $\tau$ to satisfy a large set of compatible exact constraints in the normal-density regime and interpolates among covalent, metallic, and weak-bond-like environments through indicators such as $\alpha$. “Nonempirical” means that parameters are selected from constraints and appropriate norms, not that the construction contains no design choices.

Rapid derivative changes in the original interpolation can increase grid, SCF, and force sensitivity. Regularized variants such as rSCAN and r²SCAN improve numerical smoothness while modifying how some constraints are realized. The exact variant must be reported.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="Meta-GGA 不自动解决所有非局域问题" titleEn="Meta-GGA does not automatically solve every nonlocal problem" kind="boundary">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

$\tau$ 提供 orbital-overlap 信息，但仍是单点量。它可以识别一电子/均匀/overlap environments，却不自动产生远程 $C_6/R^6$ correlation、正确 derivative discontinuity、Mott localization 或 long-range charge transfer。需要哪一种非局域信息取决于目标现象。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Kinetic-energy density carries orbital-overlap information but remains a one-point quantity. It does not automatically generate long-range dispersion, the exact derivative discontinuity, Mott localization, or long-range charge transfer. The required nonlocal information depends on the target phenomenon.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 9.5 优化有效势 | Optimized Effective Potential

<BilingualSection titleZh="同一个 orbital energy 的两种辅助表示" titleEn="Two auxiliary representations of the same orbital energy">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

给定 orbital-dependent energy $E_{\mathrm{orb}}[\{\phi_i\}]$，直接 variation 产生 orbital-specific/nonlocal operator。OEP 施加额外约束：所有轨道必须来自同一个局域乘法势 $v_s(\mathbf r)$，再在这一 restricted manifold 上使总能驻定。OEP 与 GKS 因此优化相同或相关 energy 时仍可给出不同 orbitals 和 eigenvalues。

OEP 的目标不是把 Fock operator 逐点“局域化”。它利用轨道对局域势的 response，通过 chain rule 找到能量对所有允许 $\delta v_s$ 变化为零的势。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For an orbital-dependent energy, direct variation produces an orbital-specific or nonlocal operator. OEP imposes an additional restriction: every orbital must arise from one local multiplicative potential, and the total energy is made stationary within that restricted manifold. OEP and GKS can therefore yield different orbitals and eigenvalues even when they optimize the same or closely related energy.

OEP does not localize the Fock operator point by point. It uses the orbital response to a local potential and applies a chain rule so that the energy is stationary under every admissible variation of that potential.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="OEP chain rule 与 response equation | OEP chain rule and response equation"
  assumptions={[
    '占据轨道是局域势 vs 的非简并或适当处理简并的本征函数。',
    '固定规范，例如令势在无穷远或晶胞平均满足给定条件。',
    '轨道 response sum 和 unoccupied subspace 收敛。',
  ]}
>

驻定条件为

$$
0=\frac{\delta E}{\delta v_s(\mathbf r)}
=\sum_i\int d\mathbf r'\left[
\frac{\delta E}{\delta\phi_i(\mathbf r')}
\frac{\delta\phi_i(\mathbf r')}{\delta v_s(\mathbf r)}
+\mathrm{c.c.}
\right].
$$

一阶轨道 response 为

$$
\frac{\delta\phi_i(\mathbf r')}{\delta v_s(\mathbf r)}
=\sum_{a\ne i}
\frac{\phi_a(\mathbf r')\phi_a^*(\mathbf r)\phi_i(\mathbf r)}
{\epsilon_i-\epsilon_a},
$$

其中 sum 包含允许的 unoccupied states。定义 orbital-specific derivative

$$
u_i(\mathbf r)
=\frac1{\phi_i^*(\mathbf r)}
\frac{\delta E_{\mathrm{orb}}}{\delta\phi_i(\mathbf r)},
$$

可把 OEP equation 组织成 density response kernel $\chi_s$ 作用于未知局域势，或等价的 orbital-shift equation。常数势位于固定粒子数 response 的 null space，因此 OEP 只确定到 additive constant；有限 basis 还可产生额外 near-null modes。

The OEP equation is a chain-rule stationarity condition involving orbital response to a common local potential. The ordinary constant-potential gauge remains a response null mode, and finite bases can introduce additional ill-conditioned directions.

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="Slater 与 KLI 近似" titleEn="Slater and KLI approximations">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

Slater exchange potential 用 exchange hole 或 occupied-orbital average 构造局域 exchange，保留部分 Fock physics，但不满足完整 OEP response equation。Krieger–Li–Iafrate（KLI）近似省略 orbital-shift 中部分能量分母结构，得到 Slater-like term 加 orbital-average constants。它通常比 full OEP 便宜，但近似误差对 shell structure、gap 和 response 可能重要。

比较 EXX-OEP、Hartree–Fock、exact-exchange GKS 与 KLI 时，需要说明是否使用同一个 total-energy functional、是否允许 nonlocal operator、是否 self-consistent，以及 basis/unoccupied response 是否收敛。相同的“exact exchange”标签不保证相同 auxiliary spectrum。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The Slater potential forms a local exchange from an exchange-hole or occupied-orbital average and retains part of Fock physics without satisfying the full OEP response equation. The Krieger–Li–Iafrate approximation neglects part of the orbital-shift denominator structure, producing a Slater-like term plus orbital-average constants. It is cheaper than full OEP but can differ for shell structure, gaps, and response.

Comparisons among EXX-OEP, Hartree–Fock, exact-exchange GKS, and KLI must state the energy functional, whether a nonlocal operator is allowed, self-consistency, and basis or unoccupied-response convergence. The shared label “exact exchange” does not imply the same auxiliary spectrum.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="OEP 的数值病态与证据上限" titleEn="OEP conditioning and evidence ceiling" kind="boundary">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

OEP 需要反演或求解 $\chi_s$，而 occupied–unoccupied products 在有限 auxiliary basis 中可能不完整或线性相关。势出现 oscillations 可能来自真实 shell structure，也可能来自 basis imbalance 和 response null modes。通过能量收敛不足以验证势；还应检查 basis balance、regularization、gauge、density、asymptote、eigenvalues 和 target response。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

OEP requires solving or inverting $\chi_s$, and occupied–unoccupied products can be incomplete or linearly dependent in a finite auxiliary basis. Oscillations in the potential can reflect physical shell structure or basis imbalance and response null modes. Energy convergence alone does not validate the potential; basis balance, regularization, gauge, density, asymptote, eigenvalues, and target response require checks.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>
