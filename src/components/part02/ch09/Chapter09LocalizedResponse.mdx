## 9.6 局域轨道方法：SIC 与 DFT+U | Localized-Orbital Approaches: SIC and DFT+U

<BilingualSection titleZh="为什么需要选择 localized subspace" titleEn="Why a localized subspace must be chosen">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

LDA/GGA 对总密度作局域或半局域处理。对空间上局域的 d/f orbitals，它们常产生过强 fractional occupation、过度离域、过小 splitting 和错误的 integer-energy curvature。SIC 与 DFT+U 都引入 orbital/subspace information，但其对象不同。

Perdew–Zunger 型 SIC 对每条 occupied localized orbital density $n_{i\sigma}$ 减去其 Hartree self-energy 和近似 exchange–correlation energy：

$$
E_{\mathrm{SIC}}
=E_{\mathrm{approx}}[n_\uparrow,n_\downarrow]
-\sum_{i\sigma}^{\mathrm{occ}}
\left[E_H[n_{i\sigma}]+E_{\mathrm{xc}}^{\mathrm{approx}}[n_{i\sigma},0]\right].
$$

对真正一电子 density，精确 functional 应使括号被完全抵消。近似 functional 不满足该条件，SIC 试图恢复 one-electron limit。由于修正依赖 individual orbital densities，占据子空间内一般 unitary rotation 会改变能量；必须选择 localized orbitals 或同时优化 orbital gauge。

DFT+U 则选择一个 correlated subspace $I$，用 projectors 定义 occupation matrix $n^{I\sigma}_{mm'}$，并添加 Hubbard-like interaction 与 double-counting correction。它不是把整个体系替换为 Hubbard model，而是在 DFT energy 中修改所选 subspace 的 occupation energetics。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

LDA and GGA treat the total density locally or semilocally. For spatially localized d or f orbitals, they can favor fractional occupation, excessive delocalization, insufficient splitting, and incorrect curvature between integer electron numbers. SIC and DFT+U both add orbital or subspace information, but they modify different objects.

A Perdew–Zunger-type SIC subtracts the Hartree self-energy and approximate exchange–correlation energy of every occupied localized orbital density. The exact functional cancels these terms for a one-electron density. Because the correction depends on individual orbital densities, occupied-space unitary rotations generally change the energy, so a localized orbital gauge must be selected or optimized.

DFT+U instead chooses a correlated subspace, defines an occupation matrix through projectors, and adds a Hubbard-like interaction minus double counting. It does not replace the entire system by a Hubbard model; it modifies occupation energetics in a selected subspace.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="Dudarev 型 rotationally invariant DFT+U | Dudarev-type rotationally invariant DFT+U"
  assumptions={[
    '对每个 site I 和 spin σ，occupation matrix nIσ 为 Hermitian，eigenvalues 位于 [0,1]。',
    '采用 simplified fully-localized-limit double counting，并把 U−J 合并为 Ueff。',
    'projector/subspace 定义固定；若随原子移动，力需包含 projector derivative。',
  ]}
>

Dudarev correction 写成

$$
E_U
=\frac{U_{\mathrm{eff}}}{2}
\sum_{I\sigma}\operatorname{Tr}
\left[\mathbf n^{I\sigma}-\left(\mathbf n^{I\sigma}\right)^2\right].
$$

对 occupation matrix 变分：

$$
\delta E_U
=\frac{U_{\mathrm{eff}}}{2}
\sum_{I\sigma}\operatorname{Tr}
\left[(\mathbf 1-2\mathbf n^{I\sigma})\delta\mathbf n^{I\sigma}\right],
$$

所以 subspace potential matrix 为

$$
\mathbf V_U^{I\sigma}
=U_{\mathrm{eff}}
\left(\frac12\mathbf1-\mathbf n^{I\sigma}\right).
$$

在 occupation-matrix eigenbasis 中，每个 fractional eigenvalue $f$ 的修正为

$$
E_U(f)=\frac{U_{\mathrm{eff}}}{2}f(1-f),
\qquad
V_U(f)=U_{\mathrm{eff}}\left(\frac12-f\right).
$$

整数 $f=0,1$ 的 correction energy 为零，fractional occupation 被提高。已占据态得到负 shift，未占据态得到正 shift，因此 gap 和 localization 常增加。但总能差还包含 self-consistent density、结构、hybridization 和 double counting 的变化，不能只按 $U_{\mathrm{eff}}$ 线性解释。

The Dudarev correction penalizes fractional eigenvalues of the chosen occupation matrix. Its potential shifts occupied and unoccupied subspace components in opposite directions, but the final energy and spectrum also depend on self-consistency, hybridization, structure, projector choice, and double counting.

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="$U$ 的确定与 projector dependence" titleEn="Determining $U$ and projector dependence">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

$U$ 是特定 subspace 中 screened interaction 的 effective parameter。Constrained DFT、linear response、constrained RPA 或经验拟合可给不同定义；其数值依赖 oxidation state、structure、screening、projector radius、atomic-like/Wannier basis、spin 和 underlying functional。把文献中的 $U$ 直接转移到不同 pseudopotential 或 projector 不是同一方法。

Linear-response 路线可把 bare 与 screened occupation response 写为 $\chi_0$ 和 $\chi$，并用

$$
U=\chi_0^{-1}-\chi^{-1}
$$

在声明的 site/subspace matrix sense 下定义。该式需要明确 perturbation、charge constraint、supercell、screening channels 和 matrix inversion。得到的 $U$ 仍服务于对应 subspace/functionals，不是独立实验常数。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

$U$ is an effective screened interaction in a specified subspace. Constrained DFT, linear response, constrained RPA, and empirical fitting define it differently. Its value depends on oxidation state, structure, screening, projector radius, atomic or Wannier basis, spin, and the underlying functional. Transferring a literature value to a different pseudopotential or projector changes the method.

A linear-response construction can define $U=\chi_0^{-1}-\chi^{-1}$ in a declared site/subspace matrix sense. The perturbation, charge constraint, supercell, screening channels, and matrix inversion must be specified. The result remains tied to the chosen subspace and functional.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="piecewise linearity、curvature 与 generalized Koopmans condition | Piecewise linearity, curvature, and generalized Koopmans condition"
  assumptions={[
    '考虑固定 external potential 下相邻整数粒子数之间的 ensemble density。',
    '允许选定 orbital/subspace 的 fractional occupation，并在每一点重新 self-consistent。',
  ]}
>

精确总能满足

$$
E(N+\eta)=(1-\eta)E(N)+\eta E(N+1),
\qquad 0\le\eta\le1.
$$

因此区间内二阶导数为零，energy derivative 为常数。近似 semilocal functional 常呈 convex behavior，偏好分数电荷和 delocalization。SIC、DFT+U 或 Koopmans-compliant correction 可以针对 orbital occupation curvature 加项，使

$$
\frac{d^2E}{df_i^2}\approx0.
$$

该条件改善 fractional-charge energetics，不自动保证 integer total energies、relaxed geometry、multiplet splitting 或 quasiparticle spectrum。若 correction 针对选定 orbitals，结果还依赖 orbital gauge 和 screening parameter。

Exact ensemble energies are piecewise linear between integers. Orbital corrections can reduce curvature and improve fractional-charge energetics, but linearity in one chosen occupation does not validate all integer energies, geometries, multiplets, or spectra.

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="SIC/DFT+U 的最低报告边界" titleEn="Minimum reporting boundary for SIC and DFT+U" kind="boundary">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

必须报告 corrected orbitals/subspace、projector、$U/J$ 或 SIC localization scheme、double-counting、spin/SOC、initial occupation matrices、metastable-state search、结构是否重弛豫，以及目标量对参数和 projector 的敏感性。SCF 收敛到一个 occupation pattern 不证明它是全局基态。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The corrected orbitals or subspace, projectors, $U/J$ or SIC localization scheme, double counting, spin and SOC treatment, initial occupation matrices, metastable-state search, structural relaxation, and sensitivity to parameters and projectors must be reported. Convergence to one occupation pattern does not establish the global ground state.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 9.7 从响应函数导出的泛函 | Functionals Derived from Response Functions

<BilingualSection titleZh="从 density response 重建 correlation energy" titleEn="Reconstructing correlation energy from density response">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

Exchange–correlation hole 与 density fluctuation 通过 fluctuation–dissipation theorem 相连。沿 density-preserving coupling constant $\lambda$，可以把 correlation energy 写成 interacting response $\chi_\lambda$ 与 independent-particle response $\chi_0$ 的积分。该路线显式使用 occupied/unoccupied states、imaginary frequency 和 Coulomb matrix，而非仅使用局域 density descriptors。

对 imaginary frequency $iu$，独立粒子 KS response 的 spectral form 为

$$
\chi_0(\mathbf r,\mathbf r';iu)
=\sum_{ia}(f_i-f_a)
\frac{\phi_i^*(\mathbf r)\phi_a(\mathbf r)
\phi_a^*(\mathbf r')\phi_i(\mathbf r')}
{iu+\epsilon_i-\epsilon_a},
$$

配合共轭项或等价对称形式。求和对 occupied–unoccupied transitions、bands、$k$ points 和 frequency grid 收敛。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The fluctuation–dissipation theorem connects the exchange–correlation hole with density fluctuations. Along a density-preserving coupling-constant path, the correlation energy can be expressed as an integral over the interacting response $\chi_\lambda$ relative to the independent-particle response $\chi_0$. This route uses occupied and unoccupied states, imaginary frequency, and Coulomb matrices rather than only local density descriptors.

The independent-particle response has a spectral representation in terms of occupied-to-unoccupied transitions and requires convergence with respect to bands, $k$ points, and frequency integration.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="ACFD correlation energy | Adiabatic-connection fluctuation–dissipation correlation energy"
  assumptions={[
    '沿 λ∈[0,1] 保持 ground-state density 固定。',
    '采用因果 response，并将 frequency contour 转到 imaginary axis。',
    'trace 包含空间、spin 和 Coulomb-kernel contractions；周期体系需处理 G=0/小 q。',
  ]}
>

ACFD correlation energy 可写为

$$
E_c
=-\frac1{2\pi}
\int_0^1d\lambda\int_0^\infty du\,
\operatorname{Tr}
\left\{v_C\left[\chi_\lambda(iu)-\chi_0(iu)\right]\right\}.
$$

相互作用 response 满足 Dyson-like equation

$$
\chi_\lambda
=\chi_0+\chi_0
\left(\lambda v_C+f_{\mathrm{xc}}^\lambda\right)
\chi_\lambda.
$$

RPA 令 $f_{\mathrm{xc}}^\lambda=0$，得到

$$
\chi_\lambda^{\mathrm{RPA}}
=\left(1-\lambda\chi_0v_C\right)^{-1}\chi_0.
$$

完成 $\lambda$ 积分后，常写为 trace-log 形式

$$
E_c^{\mathrm{RPA}}
=\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^\infty du\,
\operatorname{Tr}
\left[\ln(1-v_C\chi_0)+v_C\chi_0\right],
$$

具体符号与 matrix ordering 取决于 response convention。展开 $\ln(1-X)$ 时一阶 term 被 $+X$ 抵消，correlation 从二阶 ring diagrams 开始。

The ACFD formula integrates density fluctuations over coupling strength and imaginary frequency. RPA neglects the exchange–correlation kernel in the response and sums Coulomb ring diagrams through a trace logarithm.

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="RPA 的能力、成本和 reference dependence" titleEn="RPA capability, cost, and reference dependence">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

RPA 包含长程 dynamical screening 和 dispersion，可改善 layered systems、adsorption 和部分 reaction/solid energies。它也可能低估短程 correlation、产生 systematic underbinding 或对 starting orbitals/eigenvalues 敏感。常见 total energy 组合为 exact exchange 加 RPA correlation，evaluated non-self-consistently on PBE、hybrid 或 OEP orbitals；这些是不同方法。

数值上需收敛 unoccupied bands、response basis/cutoff、$k/q$ grids、imaginary-frequency quadrature、Coulomb singularity、cell/vacuum 和 extrapolation。相似总能差可能来自大绝对 correlation energies 的 cancellation，因此单点 energy convergence 不足。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

RPA includes long-range dynamical screening and dispersion and can improve layered systems, adsorption, and some reaction or solid-state energies. It can also miss short-range correlation, underbind systematically, and depend on the starting orbitals and eigenvalues. Exact exchange plus RPA correlation evaluated on PBE, hybrid, or OEP references are distinct methods.

Convergence requires unoccupied states, response basis or cutoff, $k/q$ grids, imaginary-frequency quadrature, Coulomb singularities, cell or vacuum, and extrapolation. A small energy difference can arise from cancellation between large absolute correlation energies.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="超越 RPA 需要定义 kernel 或 diagrams" titleEn="Beyond RPA requires a defined kernel or diagram class" kind="boundary">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

加入 exchange kernel、SOSEX、renormalized singles、ladder diagrams 或其他 vertex correction 会修正不同物理缺失。它们不能统一写成“RPA+”而省略定义。每种方案的 self-consistency、double counting、reference dependence 和 cost 不同，benchmark 结论不能跨实现静默转移。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Exchange kernels, SOSEX, renormalized singles, ladder diagrams, and other vertex corrections repair different omissions. They cannot be reported generically as “RPA+” without definition. Self-consistency, double counting, reference dependence, and cost differ among implementations.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>
