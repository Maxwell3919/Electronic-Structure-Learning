## 9.8 van der Waals 色散的非局域泛函 | Nonlocal Functionals for van der Waals Dispersion

<BilingualSection titleZh="局域密度信息为何缺少远程相关" titleEn="Why local density information misses long-range correlation">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

两个彼此不重叠、无永久多极矩的中性片段仍会因瞬时 dipole fluctuations 产生吸引。一个片段的 fluctuation 通过 Coulomb field 极化另一个片段，相关 fluctuation 降低能量。在大距离 $R$，各向同性片段的 leading term 为

$$
E_{\mathrm{disp}}(R)=-\frac{C_6}{R^6}+O(R^{-8}).
$$

单纯 LDA/GGA 在每一点只看局域或 semilocal density，无法把两个远隔 density regions 的 correlated fluctuations 连接起来。某些体系中 LDA 的 spurious exchange/correlation overlap 可产生偶然结合，但不具有正确 asymptote，不能当作色散描述。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Two separated neutral fragments without permanent multipoles still attract through correlated instantaneous dipole fluctuations. At large separation the leading isotropic term is

$$
E_{\mathrm{disp}}(R)=-C_6/R^6+O(R^{-8}).
$$

A purely local or semilocal functional cannot connect correlated fluctuations in distant density regions. Accidental binding from LDA overlap error does not provide the correct asymptote and is not a controlled dispersion description.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="Casimir–Polder 系数与 single-pole 模型 | Casimir–Polder coefficient and a single-pole model"
  assumptions={[
    '两个片段相距足够远，可使用非重叠 dipole approximation。',
    '各向同性 dynamic polarizabilities，在 imaginary frequency 上解析且衰减。',
    '忽略 retardation；讨论 nonretarded London limit。',
  ]}
>

Casimir–Polder relation 为

$$
C_6^{AB}
=\frac3\pi\int_0^\infty du\,
\alpha_A(iu)\alpha_B(iu).
$$

取 single-pole polarizability

$$
\alpha_X(iu)
=\alpha_X(0)\frac{\omega_X^2}{\omega_X^2+u^2}.
$$

代入：

$$
C_6^{AB}
=\frac3\pi\alpha_A(0)\alpha_B(0)
\omega_A^2\omega_B^2
\int_0^\infty
\frac{du}{(u^2+\omega_A^2)(u^2+\omega_B^2)}.
$$

利用 partial fractions，

$$
\int_0^\infty
\frac{du}{(u^2+a^2)(u^2+b^2)}
=\frac\pi{2ab(a+b)},
$$

得到

$$
C_6^{AB}
=\frac32\alpha_A(0)\alpha_B(0)
\frac{\omega_A\omega_B}{\omega_A+\omega_B}.
$$

该模型明确了 $C_6$ 同时依赖 static polarizability 和 characteristic excitation scale。只比较体积或静态极化率不足以确定色散。

The Casimir–Polder integral combines the full dynamic polarizability of both fragments. A single-pole model reduces it to an analytic coefficient and shows that static polarizability alone does not determine dispersion.

</DerivationBlock>

<DispersionExplorer />

<BilingualSection titleZh="pairwise correction、many-body dispersion 与 nonlocal correlation" titleEn="Pairwise corrections, many-body dispersion, and nonlocal correlation">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

常见路线可分为三类。

1. **Pairwise correction。** 在 semilocal DFT energy 上加
   $$
   E_{\mathrm{disp}}=-\sum_{A<B}f_{\mathrm{damp}}(R_{AB})\frac{C_6^{AB}}{R_{AB}^6},
   $$
   其中 $C_6$ 可固定、环境依赖或从 density/atomic volumes 得到。Damping 避免短程与 base functional double counting。
2. **Many-body dispersion。** 把环境中的原子/片段 polarizabilities 耦合为 interacting oscillators，包含非加和 screening 和 collective modes。结果依赖 partition、screening 和 oscillator model。
3. **Nonlocal density functional。** 写成
   $$
   E_c^{\mathrm{nl}}[n]
   =\frac12\iint d\mathbf r\,d\mathbf r'\,
   n(\mathbf r)\Phi(\mathbf r,\mathbf r';[n])n(\mathbf r'),
   $$
   kernel 由局域 density/gradient 设置的 response model 构造。vdW-DF 家族中的 exchange partner、kernel version 和 self-consistency 都是方法定义的一部分。

RPA/ACFD 是另一条 response-level 路线，不等同于 pairwise 或固定 kernel。三类方法都可产生长程 attraction，但 short-range correlation、many-body screening、成本和 forces 的定义不同。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Three broad routes are common: a damped pairwise $-C_6/R^6$ correction added to a semilocal energy; many-body dispersion models that couple environmental polarizabilities and include nonadditive screening; and nonlocal density functionals with a two-point correlation kernel. RPA/ACFD is a separate response-level route. These approaches can share the same asymptotic attraction while differing in short-range correlation, many-body screening, cost, and force definitions.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="正确长程极限不保证平衡区准确" titleEn="The correct long-range limit does not guarantee equilibrium accuracy" kind="boundary">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

平衡 binding 发生在 density overlap、Pauli repulsion、electrostatics、induction 和 dispersion 同时重要的区域。Damping 或 nonlocal kernel 决定如何连接短程和长程。一个方法可以给出正确 $C_6/R^6$ 却预测错误的 equilibrium distance、adsorption site 或 relative polymorph stability。必须分别收敛 cell/vacuum、$k$ mesh、basis、many-body cutoff 和 geometry，并与目标性质参考比较。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Equilibrium binding occurs where density overlap, Pauli repulsion, electrostatics, induction, and dispersion all matter. Damping or a nonlocal kernel controls the short-to-long-range connection. A correct $C_6/R^6$ tail does not guarantee an accurate equilibrium distance, adsorption site, or polymorph ordering.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 9.9 Modified Becke–Johnson 交换—关联势 | Modified Becke–Johnson Potential for $V_{\mathrm{xc}}$

<BilingualSection titleZh="mBJ 是势模型，不是已知总能泛函" titleEn="mBJ is a potential model, not a known total-energy functional">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

Tran–Blaha modified Becke–Johnson（TB-mBJ）构造一个依赖 density、gradient/inhomogeneity measure 和 kinetic-energy density 的 exchange potential。常见写法为

$$
v_{x\sigma}^{\mathrm{mBJ}}(\mathbf r)
=c\,v_{x\sigma}^{\mathrm{BR}}(\mathbf r)
+(3c-2)\frac1\pi\sqrt{\frac5{12}}
\sqrt{\frac{2\tau_\sigma(\mathbf r)}{n_\sigma(\mathbf r)}},
$$

其中 $v_x^{\mathrm{BR}}$ 是 Becke–Roussel model potential，$c$ 由 cell-averaged density inhomogeneity 等量决定；实际实现和参数版本必须核对。correlation 通常另配 LDA correlation。

mBJ 的目标是改善 semiconductor/insulator eigenvalue gaps，而没有已知 $E_{\mathrm{xc}}[n]$ 使其成为一致 functional derivative。因此不能用 mBJ total energy 做普通 variational structure optimization，也不能从同一个 mBJ energy 求一致 forces/stress。常见流程是用 LDA/GGA/meta-GGA/hybrid 弛豫结构，再在固定结构上计算 mBJ bands；这是一条明确的 mixed-method workflow。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The Tran–Blaha modified Becke–Johnson construction is an exchange potential depending on density, an inhomogeneity measure, and kinetic-energy density. A common form combines a Becke–Roussel potential with a kinetic-energy-density term controlled by a cell-dependent parameter $c$; the exact implementation and parameter version must be checked.

mBJ targets semiconductor and insulator eigenvalue gaps and is not known as the functional derivative of a consistent total-energy functional. It therefore does not supply an ordinary variational energy for structure optimization or consistent forces and stress. A common workflow relaxes the structure with another functional and evaluates mBJ bands at the fixed geometry, which is an explicit mixed-method approximation.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="量纲与均匀标度检查 | Dimensional and uniform-scaling check"
  assumptions={[
    'positive kinetic-energy-density convention τσ=1/2Σ|∇φ|²。',
    'nσ>0；低密度数值 regularization 需由实现声明。',
  ]}
>

$n$ 量纲为 $L^{-3}$，$\tau$ 为 $L^{-5}$，因此

$$
\sqrt{\frac{\tau}{n}}\sim L^{-1},
$$

与 Coulomb potential 的原子单位量纲一致。对 uniform coordinate scaling

$$
n_\gamma(\mathbf r)=\gamma^3 n(\gamma\mathbf r),
\qquad
\tau_\gamma(\mathbf r)=\gamma^5\tau(\gamma\mathbf r),
$$

有

$$
\sqrt{\tau_\gamma/n_\gamma}
=\gamma\sqrt{\tau/n}(\gamma\mathbf r),
$$

满足 exchange-potential 的线性 scaling。量纲和 scaling 正确是必要条件，却不证明存在对应 energy functional 或对 gap 普遍准确。

The kinetic-energy-density term has the correct potential dimension and exchange-like uniform scaling. These are necessary structural checks, not proof of an underlying energy functional or universal gap accuracy.

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="mBJ gap 的证据上限" titleEn="Evidence ceiling of an mBJ gap" kind="boundary">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

mBJ orbital gap 仍是 auxiliary eigenvalue difference。与实验 fundamental gap 比较时需考虑 temperature、zero-point electron–phonon renormalization、SOC、defects 和 measurement definition；与 optical gap 比较还需 exciton binding。mBJ 改善一个 gap 不验证 band ordering、effective masses、dielectric response 或 total-energy differences。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

An mBJ orbital gap remains an auxiliary eigenvalue difference. Comparison with a fundamental experimental gap requires temperature, zero-point electron–phonon renormalization, SOC, defects, and measurement definitions; an optical comparison additionally requires exciton binding. Improving one gap does not validate band ordering, effective masses, dielectric response, or total-energy differences.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 9.10 泛函比较 | Comparison of Functionals

<BilingualSection titleZh="比较必须绑定目标量" titleEn="A comparison must be tied to a target quantity">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

“最佳泛函”只有在体系集合、observable、reference、成本和可接受误差明确后才有含义。Martin 以一电子原子、多电子原子和硅等体系比较 self-interaction、总能、eigenvalues、结构和 gap，展示同一个方法在不同目标上的排序可以变化。

- **一电子极限：** exact exchange/SIC 应消除 Hartree self-interaction；LDA/GGA 和部分 meta-GGA 只能近似。
- **原子总能与 ionization：** total-energy error、highest occupied eigenvalue 和整个 occupied spectrum 是不同测试。
- **固体结构：** LDA 常偏小 lattice、部分 GGA 偏大；meta-GGA/hybrid/dispersion 可改变趋势，但依赖 bonding class。
- **Bandgaps：** semilocal KS gaps 常偏小；GKS hybrid、DFT+U 和 mBJ 可改善特定 gap，但物理定义和成本不同。
- **Weak interactions：** pairwise/nonlocal/RPA methods 引入长程 correlation；正确 asymptote 与 equilibrium accuracy 分开。
- **Localized states：** SIC/DFT+U/hybrid 可抑制 delocalization，但结果依赖 orbitals/subspace 和 parameters。

一种方法对某表中的平均误差低，不证明它对当前体系的相顺序、磁性、phonon、surface energy 或 response 可靠。还必须检查是否存在训练/benchmark overlap 和 reference uncertainty。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A “best functional” is meaningful only after the system class, observable, reference, cost, and acceptable error are defined. Comparisons among one-electron atoms, many-electron atoms, and solids show that the ranking changes across self-interaction, total energies, eigenvalues, structures, and gaps.

The one-electron limit tests self-interaction; atomic total energies and ionization test different quantities; lattice constants show bonding-class-dependent LDA/GGA trends; gaps distinguish ordinary KS, GKS, DFT+U, and potential-only approaches; weak interactions require long-range correlation; and localized states depend on orbital or subspace definitions. A low average benchmark error does not establish reliability for the current phase ordering, magnetism, phonon, surface energy, or response.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="方法—性质矩阵" titleEn="Method–property matrix">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

| 方法 | 主要新增信息 | 自洽对象 | 可直接给出的量 | 关键限制 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GGA | $n,\nabla n$ | 局域 KS 势 | energy、forces、ordinary KS eigenvalues | derivative discontinuity、long-range correlation |
| Meta-GGA | $n,\nabla n,\tau$ | differential GKS 或特定 KS 实现 | energy/forces；GKS eigenvalues | grid/operator implementation、仍有限非局域性 |
| Global/range hybrid | occupied density matrix | nonlocal GKS operator | energy/forces、GKS eigenvalues | cost、metals、parameter/range choice |
| OEP/EXX | orbital energy + local-potential response | local OEP | energy、local potential/eigenvalues | response inversion、basis balance |
| SIC/DFT+U | localized orbitals/subspace occupations | orbital/subspace-dependent operator | corrected energy、forces if implemented consistently | gauge/projector、$U/J$、metastability |
| RPA/ACFD | occupied/unoccupied response | often post-KS energy | response-derived correlation energy | cost、reference dependence、unoccupied convergence |
| vdW correction | polarizability/kernel/atomic pairs | additive or self-consistent energy | dispersion energy/forces by scheme | damping、many-body screening、short range |
| mBJ | density/gradient/$\tau$ potential model | model local potential | eigenvalues/gaps | no consistent total energy/forces |

该表说明“可计算输出”，不是准确性评级。即使方法形式上提供 forces，也需核验 analytic derivative、projector/kernel response 和数值收敛。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

| Method | Added information | Self-consistent object | Direct outputs | Main limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GGA | $n,\nabla n$ | local KS potential | energy, forces, ordinary KS eigenvalues | derivative discontinuity and long-range correlation |
| Meta-GGA | $n,\nabla n,\tau$ | differential GKS or a specified KS implementation | energy, forces, GKS eigenvalues | grid/operator implementation and limited nonlocality |
| Global/range hybrid | occupied density matrix | nonlocal GKS operator | energy, forces, GKS eigenvalues | cost, metals, and parameter choice |
| OEP/EXX | orbital energy plus local-potential response | local OEP | energy, local potential and eigenvalues | response inversion and basis balance |
| SIC/DFT+U | localized orbitals or subspace occupations | orbital/subspace-dependent operator | corrected energy and consistent forces when implemented | gauge/projector, $U/J$, metastability |
| RPA/ACFD | occupied/unoccupied response | commonly a post-KS energy | response-derived correlation energy | cost, reference dependence, unoccupied convergence |
| vdW correction | polarizability, kernel, or atomic pairs | additive or self-consistent energy | scheme-dependent dispersion energy and forces | damping, many-body screening, short range |
| mBJ | density, gradient, and $\tau$ potential model | model local potential | eigenvalues and gaps | no consistent total energy or forces |

The table identifies available outputs rather than accuracy. Analytic derivatives, projector or kernel response, and numerical convergence remain necessary.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## 数值实现连接 | Connection to Numerical Implementation

<BilingualSection titleZh="从方法名称到可复现计算" titleEn="From a method name to a reproducible calculation">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

Chapter 9 的最小链条为

$$
\text{target observable}
\rightarrow
\text{method class and variational object}
\rightarrow
\text{specific formula/parameters/subspace}
\rightarrow
\text{consistent energy, potential and derivatives}
\rightarrow
\text{basis/grid/k/q/frequency convergence}
\rightarrow
\text{candidate-state search}
\rightarrow
\text{method sensitivity and reference comparison}.
$$

实际记录至少包括：代码和版本；functional library/version；pseudopotential/PAW 与 functional/relativistic compatibility；exact-exchange fraction/range and singularity treatment；meta-GGA GKS/KS implementation and grid；OEP auxiliary basis/regularization；DFT+U projectors、$U/J$、double counting、initial occupations；RPA reference、empty bands、response cutoff/frequency grid；dispersion scheme、damping 和 many-body setting；mBJ parameter/version 和结构来源。

每种方法的目标 observable 需独立收敛。Hybrid total-energy differences、DFT+U forces、RPA adsorption energies、vdW interlayer distances 和 mBJ gaps 使用不同门槛。程序完成、SCF 收敛和同一参数下复现只支持实现层状态，不自动支持方法适用性和科学结论。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The minimum chain is

$$
\text{target observable}
\to\text{method class and variational object}
\to\text{specific formula, parameters, and subspace}
\to\text{consistent energy, potential, and derivatives}
\to\text{basis/grid/k/q/frequency convergence}
\to\text{candidate-state search}
\to\text{method sensitivity and reference comparison}.
$$

A reproducible record identifies code and version, functional implementation, pseudopotential or PAW compatibility, exact-exchange and range parameters, meta-GGA operator and grid, OEP basis and regularization, DFT+U projectors and double counting, RPA reference and response settings, dispersion and damping scheme, and mBJ version and structural source. Each target observable requires its own convergence gate.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="最低成本的选择策略" titleEn="Minimum-cost selection strategy" kind="practice">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

先用目标主张倒推需要的物理：结构/相稳定性需要一致 total energies 和 forces；bandgap 需要明确 fundamental/optical/orbital definition；localized redox 需要 subspace 与 fractional-charge tests；dispersion-bound geometry 需要 long-range correlation；response 需要 kernel/unoccupied convergence。然后选择少量机制不同的方法，收敛会改变判断的量，并把 method spread 作为不确定性，而不是枚举所有泛函后挑选最有利结果。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Work backward from the claim. Structures and phase stability require consistent energies and forces; a gap requires a fundamental, optical, or orbital definition; localized redox requires a defined subspace and fractional-charge tests; dispersion-bound geometries require long-range correlation; and response requires kernel and unoccupied-state convergence. Compare a small number of mechanistically distinct methods and treat decision-changing method spread as uncertainty rather than selecting the most favorable result.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 常见误判、原创练习与来源 | Misconceptions, Original Exercises, and Sources

<BilingualSection titleZh="常见误判" titleEn="Common misinterpretations">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">
    <dl>
      <dt>“Jacob’s ladder 越高一定越准确。”</dt><dd>它主要组织输入信息和成本；具体约束、参数、体系和 observable 决定误差。</dd>
      <dt>“GKS eigenvalues 都是 quasiparticle energies。”</dt><dd>GKS 把部分非局域 physics 放入辅助算符，但不存在普适的全部本征值定理。</dd>
      <dt>“25% exact exchange 是普适常数。”</dt><dd>PBE0 的比例来自特定构造；screening 和体系类别可支持不同 fraction。</dd>
      <dt>“Hybrid 一定优于 semilocal functional。”</dt><dd>金属、强 screening、结构能量和大体系成本可使 global hybrid 不适用或不经济。</dd>
      <dt>“Meta-GGA 的 $\alpha$ 就是键级或相关强度。”</dt><dd>它是 kinetic-density environment indicator，需要完整 functional 才映射到能量。</dd>
      <dt>“OEP 只是把 Fock operator 取局域平均。”</dt><dd>OEP 是受限于共同局域势的 response-chain stationarity equation。</dd>
      <dt>“DFT+U 的 U 是材料固定常数。”</dt><dd>$U$ 依赖 subspace、projector、screening、结构和 underlying functional。</dd>
      <dt>“DFT+U 打开 gap 就证明 Mott physics。”</dt><dd>gap 可来自 imposed subspace splitting；仍需 magnetic/orbital states、parameter sensitivity 和实验/多体证据。</dd>
      <dt>“RPA 不含经验参数，所以结果已验证。”</dt><dd>RPA 仍有 response approximation、reference dependence 和重数值收敛。</dd>
      <dt>“加上 $-C_6/R^6$ 就完整描述 vdW。”</dt><dd>平衡区需要 damping、short-range exchange/correlation、environment 和 many-body screening。</dd>
      <dt>“mBJ gap 改善说明结构和总能也改善。”</dt><dd>mBJ 是 potential-only method，没有一致总能和 forces。</dd>
      <dt>“一个 benchmark 表可给出万能泛函。”</dt><dd>训练/测试域、property weights 和 reference uncertainty 限制迁移。</dd>
    </dl>
  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <dl>
      <dt>“A higher rung is necessarily more accurate.”</dt><dd>The ladder organizes information and cost; constraints, parameters, systems, and observables determine errors.</dd>
      <dt>“All GKS eigenvalues are quasiparticle energies.”</dt><dd>GKS moves some nonlocal physics into an auxiliary operator without a universal theorem for every eigenvalue.</dd>
      <dt>“Twenty-five percent exact exchange is universal.”</dt><dd>The PBE0 fraction follows one construction; screening and system class can motivate other values.</dd>
      <dt>“A hybrid always outperforms a semilocal functional.”</dt><dd>Metals, screening, structural energetics, and cost can make global hybrids inappropriate.</dd>
      <dt>“The meta-GGA $\alpha$ is a bond order or correlation strength.”</dt><dd>It is a kinetic-density environment indicator that requires a full functional mapping.</dd>
      <dt>“OEP is a local average of the Fock operator.”</dt><dd>OEP is a response-chain stationarity equation restricted to a common local potential.</dd>
      <dt>“$U$ is a fixed material constant.”</dt><dd>It depends on subspace, projector, screening, structure, and the underlying functional.</dd>
      <dt>“A DFT+U gap proves Mott physics.”</dt><dd>A gap can arise from imposed subspace splitting and still requires state, parameter, and experimental or many-body evidence.</dd>
      <dt>“Parameter-free RPA is already validated.”</dt><dd>RPA retains a response approximation, reference dependence, and demanding numerical convergence.</dd>
      <dt>“Adding $-C_6/R^6$ fully describes van der Waals binding.”</dt><dd>Equilibrium requires damping, short-range exchange and correlation, environment, and many-body screening.</dd>
      <dt>“An improved mBJ gap implies improved structures and energies.”</dt><dd>mBJ is a potential-only method without consistent total energies and forces.</dd>
      <dt>“One benchmark table identifies a universal functional.”</dt><dd>Training and test domains, property weights, and reference uncertainty limit transfer.</dd>
    </dl>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="原创练习" titleEn="Original exercises" eyebrow="Practice">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">
    <ol>
      <li><strong>GKS variation。</strong> 从 exact-exchange energy 对轨道变分，写出 Fock operator，并说明为何不能用共同局域函数表示。</li>
      <li><strong>Gap definitions。</strong> 对一个周期绝缘体列出 orbital、fundamental、quasiparticle 和 optical gap 的独立计算与有限尺寸/温度修正。</li>
      <li><strong>Range separation。</strong> 展开 erf/erfc 的小 $r$ 极限，证明 long-range kernel 在原点有限，并分析 $\omega$ 改变 crossover length。</li>
      <li><strong>Screened hybrid。</strong> 对网页模型证明 exact weight 单调下降；比较 global、HSE-like 和 long-range-corrected assignments。</li>
      <li><strong>Meta-GGA operator。</strong> 对 $E=\int f(n,\tau)dr$ 推导 differential GKS term，写明 boundary term 和 Hermiticity 条件。</li>
      <li><strong>Indicators。</strong> 证明单个实轨道给 $\tau=\tau_W$；构造同 $n,\nabla n$ 但不同 $\tau$ 的两组轨道环境。</li>
      <li><strong>OEP gauge。</strong> 证明固定粒子数 response 对常数势为零；讨论有限 auxiliary basis 的额外 null modes。</li>
      <li><strong>SIC gauge。</strong> 对两个占据轨道作 unitary rotation，说明 orbital-density-dependent SIC 为何一般改变。</li>
      <li><strong>DFT+U。</strong> 从 trace formula 推导 potential matrix，证明 integer occupation correction 为零，并检查 projector movement 对 forces 的影响。</li>
      <li><strong>Piecewise linearity。</strong> 用网页模型求 $U_{\rm eff}$ 使曲率消失，并说明 overcorrection 的能量形状。</li>
      <li><strong>ACFD/RPA。</strong> 从 Dyson equation 令 $f_{xc}=0$，展开 trace log 到三阶并识别一阶抵消。</li>
