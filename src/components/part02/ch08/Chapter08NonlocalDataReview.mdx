## 8.7 平均与加权密度表述：ADA 和 WDA | Average and Weighted Density Formulations: ADA and WDA

<BilingualSection titleZh="从局域密度走向邻域密度" titleEn="From a local density to a neighborhood density">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

LDA 把参考电子周围的洞替换为均匀气在局域密度 $n(\mathbf r)$ 下的洞。ADA 和 WDA 保留“引用均匀气信息”的思想，但允许控制洞尺度的密度由邻域决定。一般可引入

$$
\bar n(\mathbf r)
=\int d\mathbf r'\,w(\mathbf r,\mathbf r';[n])n(\mathbf r'),
$$

其中 $w$ 是归一化或通过其他约束确定的权重。能量仍可写成空间积分，但 $\epsilon_{\mathrm{xc}}$ 通过 $\bar n(\mathbf r)$ 依赖远处密度，因此泛函是非局域的。

**ADA** 通常选择与响应或平均密度有关的 kernel，使模型在均匀极限和弱非均匀极限恢复所需行为。**WDA** 更直接地以加权密度构造 model exchange–correlation hole，并通过 hole charge sum rule 决定该加权密度。两者都试图修复“局域密度无法给出正确洞尺度和形状”的问题。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

LDA replaces the hole around a reference electron by that of a uniform gas at the local density $n(\mathbf r)$. ADA and WDA retain the use of uniform-gas information but let a neighborhood determine the density scale of the hole. A general weighted density can be written as

$$
\bar n(\mathbf r)
=\int d\mathbf r'\,w(\mathbf r,\mathbf r';[n])n(\mathbf r').
$$

The energy can still appear as a spatial integral, yet its energy per particle depends on remote density through $\bar n$, making the functional nonlocal. ADA commonly selects a kernel from response or average-density considerations. WDA constructs a model exchange–correlation hole and determines the weighted density through the hole charge sum rule. Both attempt to repair the inability of a strictly local density to set the correct hole scale and shape.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="用 hole sum rule 确定 weighted density | Determining a weighted density from the hole sum rule"
  assumptions={[
    '选择一个均匀气型 model hole h(u;n̄)，其中 u=|r−r′|。',
    '模型对给定 n̄ 足够平滑，并且关于 n̄ 的归一化方程存在可接受解。',
    '权重和边界条件使积分有限。',
  ]}
>

令模型洞为

$$
n_{\mathrm{xc}}^{\mathrm{model}}(\mathbf r,\mathbf r')
=n(\mathbf r')h\!\left(|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|;\bar n(\mathbf r)\right).
$$

WDA 在每个参考点要求

$$
\int d\mathbf r'\,
 n(\mathbf r')h\!\left(|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|;\bar n(\mathbf r)\right)
=-1.
$$

该积分方程隐式决定 $\bar n(\mathbf r)$。再把模型洞代入 Coulomb 表达式：

$$
E_{\mathrm{xc}}^{\mathrm{WDA}}
=\frac12\int d\mathbf r\,n(\mathbf r)
\int d\mathbf r'\,
\frac{n(\mathbf r')h(|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|;\bar n(\mathbf r))}
{|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|}.
$$

即使 $h$ 来自均匀气，$\bar n(\mathbf r)$ 由整个邻域共同决定，因此该能量不是 LDA。若模型洞或权重对交换 $\mathbf r\leftrightarrow\mathbf r'$ 不对称，还需检查 pair symmetry、functional derivative 和力的一致性。

The weighted density is fixed implicitly so that the model hole carries one missing electron at each reference point. Substitution into the Coulomb expression produces a genuinely nonlocal functional. Hole normalization alone does not guarantee pair symmetry, a stable potential, or accurate forces.

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="相对 LDA/GGA 的代价与边界" titleEn="Cost and boundaries relative to LDA and GGA">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

非局域邻域信息可以改善一电子 self-interaction、表面和低密度区域的洞描述，但会增加积分、kernel、边界和 functional derivative 的复杂度。WDA 的势需要对隐式 $\bar n[n]$ 求导；若只更新能量而使用不一致的势，SCF、力和响应将失去变分一致性。

Martin 还指出 core 与 valence 密度混合可能导致 weighted-density 结果恶化。紧致 core 密度会把邻域平均推向与价电子物理不相称的高密度尺度，因此需要明确 all-electron/pseudopotential 表述、core–valence partition 和模型 kernel 的适用范围。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Nonlocal neighborhood information can improve the treatment of one-electron self-interaction, surfaces, and low-density regions, but it adds integral, kernel, boundary, and functional-derivative complexity. A WDA potential must include the implicit derivative of $\bar n[n]$; using an energy and an inconsistent potential breaks variational consistency for SCF, forces, and response.

Martin also notes that mixing compact core density with valence density can degrade a weighted-density description. The all-electron or pseudopotential representation, core–valence partition, and model-kernel domain must therefore be stated.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="非局域不自动等于色散泛函" titleEn="Nonlocal does not automatically mean a dispersion functional" kind="boundary">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

ADA/WDA 的非局域性来自邻域平均或 model hole。它们不自动产生正确的远距离 $-C_6/R^6$ van der Waals asymptote，也不等同于 Chapter 9 的 response-derived 或 nonlocal-correlation dispersion functionals。必须检查所用 kernel 的长程极限，而不能只凭“nonlocal”标签推断色散能力。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

ADA and WDA are nonlocal through neighborhood averaging or a model hole. This does not automatically generate the correct long-range $-C_6/R^6$ asymptote and does not make them equivalent to the response-derived or nonlocal-correlation dispersion functionals of Chapter 9. The long-range kernel limit must be checked explicitly.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 8.8 拟合到数据库的泛函 | Functionals Fitted to Databases

<BilingualSection titleZh="拟合对象成为泛函定义的一部分" titleEn="The fitting target becomes part of the functional definition">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

经验或半经验泛函选择一个参数化形式 $E_{\mathrm{xc}}^{\boldsymbol\theta}$，并用数据库中的能量、几何、势垒或其他 observable 优化参数 $\boldsymbol\theta$。抽象地，训练可写成

$$
\boldsymbol\theta^*
=\arg\min_{\boldsymbol\theta}
\sum_{a,p}w_{ap}
\left[
O_{ap}^{\mathrm{DFT}}(\boldsymbol\theta)-O_{ap}^{\mathrm{ref}}
\right]^2
+\lambda R(\boldsymbol\theta),
$$

其中 $a$ 标记体系，$p$ 标记性质，$w_{ap}$ 决定任务权重，$R$ 是 regularization 或 constraint penalty。训练集合、参考方法、权重、损失和约束共同决定最终泛函；仅报告参数名称不足以解释其偏好。

数据库拟合可以纠正 constraint-only 泛函在特定化学空间的系统偏差，也可能通过误差抵消获得很低的平均训练误差。代价是 transferability 取决于新问题与训练域的关系。对固体训练的参数不自动适用于过渡态、离子、表面、强静态相关或长程电荷转移；反之亦然。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

An empirical or semiempirical functional selects a parameterized form $E_{\mathrm{xc}}^{\boldsymbol\theta}$ and optimizes its parameters against a database of energies, geometries, barriers, or other observables. Abstractly,

$$
\boldsymbol\theta^*
=\arg\min_{\boldsymbol\theta}
\sum_{a,p}w_{ap}
[O_{ap}^{\mathrm{DFT}}(\boldsymbol\theta)-O_{ap}^{\mathrm{ref}}]^2
+\lambda R(\boldsymbol\theta).
$$

The systems, properties, references, weights, loss, and constraints all form part of the functional's effective definition. Database fitting can remove systematic bias in a chosen chemical domain and can also exploit error cancellation. Transferability then depends on the relation between the new problem and the training domain.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="训练误差、验证误差与 domain shift | Training error, validation error, and domain shift"
  assumptions={[
    '训练集、验证集和最终测试问题独立到足以评估泛化。',
    '参考数据的不确定度和方法层级已知。',
  ]}
>

将参考误差分解为

$$
O^{\mathrm{DFT}}-O^{\mathrm{true}}
=\bigl(O^{\mathrm{DFT}}-O^{\mathrm{ref}}\bigr)
+\bigl(O^{\mathrm{ref}}-O^{\mathrm{true}}\bigr).
$$

即使训练残差第一项很小，参考数据本身仍有实验、basis、relativistic、zero-point、finite-temperature 或 higher-level-method uncertainty。验证集误差估计同分布泛化，而目标体系若来自不同元素、价态、键型、spin state 或密度尺度，就发生 domain shift；训练/验证平均误差不再是充分的不确定度指标。

A small residual relative to the fitting reference does not remove uncertainty in the reference itself. Validation error estimates in-domain generalization; a target with different elements, oxidation states, bonding, spin, or density scales is a domain-shift problem and requires separate evidence.

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="机器学习泛函仍受 DFT 结构约束" titleEn="Machine-learned functionals remain constrained by the DFT structure">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

机器学习可以把局域环境、density matrix、orbital features 或多尺度 descriptors 映射到能量密度、势或 kernel。模型容量提高后，训练数据覆盖、对称性、size consistency、spin scaling、坐标标度、one-electron limit、functional derivative、SCF stability 和不确定度估计变得更重要。

只训练能量而不约束其导数，可能得到能量误差小但势、力或 response 不稳定的模型；只在自洽密度上训练也可能无法控制 SCF 迭代中遇到的 off-manifold densities。机器学习没有取消“能量—势—SCF—observable”的链条，只是把近似表达式和参数空间换成数据驱动形式。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Machine learning can map local environments, density matrices, orbital features, or multiscale descriptors to an energy density, potential, or kernel. As model capacity grows, training coverage, symmetry, size consistency, spin and coordinate scaling, the one-electron limit, functional derivatives, SCF stability, and uncertainty estimation become more important.

Training only energies can produce small energy errors but unstable potentials, forces, or response. Training only on self-consistent densities may leave the off-manifold densities encountered during SCF uncontrolled. Machine learning changes the approximation form and parameter space; it does not remove the energy–potential–SCF–observable chain.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="平均 benchmark 排名不能替代当前任务验证" titleEn="An average benchmark rank does not replace task-specific validation" kind="boundary">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

选择泛函时需要匹配体系、observable、参考状态和可接受误差。一个泛函在综合 benchmark 上平均误差较低，不证明它对当前材料的晶格、相对相稳定性、磁矩、带隙、力、声子或响应都更可靠。最低成本验证应优先比较会改变当前结论的性质，并包含数值收敛、候选泛函敏感性和可获得的独立参考。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Functional selection must match the system, observable, reference state, and acceptable error. A low average benchmark error does not establish reliability for every lattice parameter, phase ordering, magnetic moment, gap, force, phonon, or response of the current material. Validation should target the properties capable of changing the present conclusion and include numerical convergence, functional sensitivity, and available independent references.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 数值实现连接 | Connection to Numerical Implementation

<BilingualSection titleZh="从近似定义到可接受结果" titleEn="From an approximation definition to an acceptable result">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

Chapter 8 的理论—软件链条为

$$
\text{exact hole/constraint}
\rightarrow
\text{approximation family}
\rightarrow
\text{specific parameterization}
\rightarrow
\text{energy expression}
\rightarrow
\text{consistent potential/kernel}
\rightarrow
\text{basis/grid implementation}
\rightarrow
\text{SCF and derivative convergence}
\rightarrow
\text{target observable}
\rightarrow
\text{benchmark and applicability}.
$$

软件输入中的 `LDA`、`PBE` 或其他标签还不足以定义计算。需要记录 spin treatment、correlation parameterization、pseudopotential generation functional、nonlinear core correction、relativistic level、dispersion/U/hybrid 等额外项、代码版本及是否采用 libxc 等外部实现。赝势与交换—关联泛函不一致会引入另一层近似，不能用更密 $k$ 网格消除。

能量、势、力、应力和 response 的数值要求不同。GGA potential 比 energy density 包含更高 density derivatives；声子、力常数和 response kernel 通常比总能需要更严格的网格、SCF 和实现一致性。程序正常结束与 `converged` 只覆盖其内部停止条件。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The theory-to-software chain is

$$
\text{exact hole/constraint}
\to\text{approximation family}
\to\text{specific parameterization}
\to\text{energy}
\to\text{consistent potential/kernel}
\to\text{basis/grid implementation}
\to\text{SCF and derivative convergence}
\to\text{target observable}
\to\text{benchmark and applicability}.
$$

An input label such as `LDA` or `PBE` does not fully define a calculation. Spin treatment, correlation parameterization, pseudopotential generation functional, nonlinear core correction, relativistic level, additional dispersion, $U$, or hybrid terms, code version, and external libraries must be recorded. A functional–pseudopotential inconsistency is another approximation and is not removed by a denser $k$ mesh.

Energy, potential, forces, stress, and response have different numerical requirements. A GGA potential contains higher density derivatives than the energy density, and phonons or response kernels can require tighter grids, SCF, and implementation consistency than total energies. Program termination and an internal `converged` message cover only the stated stopping condition.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="最低充分的泛函比较" titleEn="Minimum sufficient functional comparison" kind="practice">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">

对当前材料或分子，最低充分比较不是运行尽可能多的泛函，而是：固定可复现输入；分别收敛目标 observable；选择少量具有不同已知偏差的候选泛函；比较会改变结论的能量差、结构、磁性或谱量；对照实验或更高层级计算时保持温度、零点、相态和 observable 定义一致。若候选泛函跨越结论边界，应报告方法不确定性，而不是静默挑选支持预期的结果。

  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A minimum sufficient comparison does not run as many functionals as possible. It fixes reproducible inputs, converges the target observable for each candidate, selects a small set with distinct known biases, compares the quantities capable of changing the conclusion, and aligns temperature, zero-point, phase, and observable definitions with experiment or higher-level theory. If the candidates cross the decision boundary, the method dependence should be reported rather than selecting the result that supports an expectation.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 常见误判、原创练习与来源 | Misconceptions, Original Exercises, and Sources

<BilingualSection titleZh="常见误判" titleEn="Common misinterpretations">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">
    <dl>
      <dt>“交换—关联洞是一个实际带负电的粒子云。”</dt>
      <dd>它是条件 pair probability 相对独立分布的差，描述一个参考电子排除了多少其他电子概率。</dd>
      <dt>“correlation hole 也必须积分为 −1。”</dt>
      <dd>exchange hole 已承担 −1；定义为剩余部分的 correlation hole 积分为零。</dd>
      <dt>“full-coupling hole 直接给出全部 E_xc。”</dt>
      <dd>完整 $E_{\mathrm{xc}}$ 使用 coupling-constant-averaged hole，包含 kinetic correlation。</dd>
      <dt>“LDA 只适用于常数密度。”</dt>
      <dd>LDA 可用于任意输入密度；缓变或其他条件只影响其合理性，不限制程序是否能求值。</dd>
      <dt>“GGA 是严格 gradient expansion 多保留一项。”</dt>
      <dd>现代 GGA 通常对梯度信息作非线性重组，并在不同极限中满足选定约束。</dd>
      <dt>“Fx 大于 1 说明 GGA 一定改善结合。”</dt>
      <dd>结合能是多个体系和 exchange/correlation 项的差，方向依赖不均匀性分布与误差抵消。</dd>
      <dt>“能量表达式相同，势的离散方式不重要。”</dt>
      <dd>能量和势必须使用一致的 gradient operator 与 adjoint；否则驻定性、力和响应可能错误。</dd>
      <dt>“非局域 weighted density 自动包含 van der Waals。”</dt>
      <dd>是否给出正确远程色散取决于 kernel 的渐近结构，而非 nonlocal 标签。</dd>
      <dt>“数据库平均误差最低的泛函对当前性质最好。”</dt>
      <dd>训练域、property weighting、参考误差和 domain shift 会改变泛化；当前 observable 仍需验证。</dd>
      <dt>“SCF 收敛后 functional error 已经消失。”</dt>
      <dd>SCF 只求解所选近似泛函和离散下的固定点，不验证该近似对真实体系准确。</dd>
    </dl>
  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <dl>
      <dt>“The exchange–correlation hole is a physical negatively charged cloud.”</dt>
      <dd>It is a deficit in conditional pair probability relative to an independent distribution.</dd>
      <dt>“The correlation hole must also integrate to −1.”</dt>
      <dd>The exchange hole already carries −1; the remaining correlation hole integrates to zero.</dd>
      <dt>“The full-coupling hole directly yields the complete E_xc.”</dt>
      <dd>The full energy uses the coupling-constant-averaged hole and contains kinetic correlation.</dd>
      <dt>“LDA applies only to constant densities.”</dt>
      <dd>LDA can be evaluated for any density. Smoothness affects justification, not evaluability.</dd>
      <dt>“GGA is simply one more exact term of a gradient expansion.”</dt>
      <dd>Modern GGAs reorganize gradient information nonlinearly and impose selected constraints across regimes.</dd>
      <dt>“Fx above one guarantees improved binding.”</dt>
      <dd>Binding is a difference among systems and exchange/correlation contributions; its direction depends on inhomogeneity and cancellation.</dd>
      <dt>“The discretization of the potential is irrelevant once the energy is defined.”</dt>
      <dd>Energy and potential require a consistent gradient operator and adjoint for stationarity, forces, and response.</dd>
      <dt>“A nonlocal weighted density automatically includes van der Waals dispersion.”</dt>
      <dd>The long-range kernel asymptote, not the label, determines dispersion behavior.</dd>
      <dt>“The lowest average database error identifies the best functional for the current property.”</dt>
      <dd>Training domain, weights, reference uncertainty, and domain shift control transferability.</dd>
      <dt>“SCF convergence removes functional error.”</dt>
      <dd>SCF solves the fixed point of the chosen approximate functional and discretization; it does not validate that approximation against the physical system.</dd>
    </dl>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="原创练习" titleEn="Original exercises" eyebrow="Practice">
  <div className="bilingual-section__zh">
    <ol>
      <li><strong>pair-density 归一化。</strong> 从归一化 $N$ 电子波函数证明 $\int n^{(2)}(x,x')dx'=(N-1)n(x)$，并指出有序/无序 pair convention 的系数差异。</li>
      <li><strong>hole sum rule。</strong> 由条件密度推导 $\int n_{xc}=-1$；再证明 $\int n_c=0$ 需要哪些 exchange-hole 定义。</li>
      <li><strong>Gaussian 教学洞。</strong> 解析积分网页中的 Gaussian hole，并估算截断在 $6\sigma$ 时遗漏的 charge。</li>
      <li><strong>coupling constant。</strong> 解释为什么保持密度需要随 $\lambda$ 改变外势，并指出若固定外势会破坏推导的哪一步。</li>
      <li><strong>uniform-gas scaling。</strong> 由三维状态计数推导 $k_F$，再由 Coulomb scaling 判断 $\epsilon_x\propto n^{1/3}$。</li>
      <li><strong>spin scaling。</strong> 计算 $\zeta=0,1/2,1$ 的 exchange spin factor，并证明它关于 $\zeta$ 为偶函数。</li>
      <li><strong>LDA 与 convexity。</strong> 对固定平均密度的 cosine modulation，用 Jensen inequality 判断 $\int n^{4/3}$ 如何随振幅变化，并与网页模型核对。</li>
      <li><strong>reduced gradient。</strong> 检查 $s$ 的量纲，并对指数尾部 $n\propto e^{-\alpha r}$ 判断 $s$ 的渐近变化。</li>
      <li><strong>PBE 型增强因子。</strong> 展开 $F_x(s)$ 到 $s^4$，证明单调性和上界，并区分这些代数性质与材料精度。</li>
      <li><strong>GGA functional derivative。</strong> 对 $E=\int[A n^{4/3}+B|\nabla n|^2/2]dr$ 完整求势，写明 surface term 和允许边界。</li>
      <li><strong>离散 adjoint。</strong> 对周期中心差分矩阵证明 $C^T=-C$，用有限差分检查离散能量梯度。</li>
      <li><strong>WDA 方程。</strong> 选择一个简单 model hole，写出确定 $\bar n(r)$ 的积分方程，并讨论多解/无解时的处理。</li>
      <li><strong>数据库拟合。</strong> 设计训练/验证划分，避免同一分子不同构型泄漏；列出参考数据中至少四类不确定度。</li>
      <li><strong>选择证据。</strong> 对“PBE 比 LDA 更适合某二维材料”的主张，列出会改变结论的目标 observable、数值收敛和独立参考。</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
  <div className="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Pair-density normalization.</strong> Derive $\int n^{(2)}(x,x')dx'=(N-1)n(x)$ and track the ordered versus unordered pair convention.</li>
      <li><strong>Hole sum rule.</strong> Derive $\int n_{xc}=-1$ and identify the exchange-hole definition required for $\int n_c=0$.</li>
      <li><strong>Gaussian teaching hole.</strong> Integrate the Gaussian hole analytically and estimate the charge omitted by a six-$\sigma$ cutoff.</li>
      <li><strong>Coupling constant.</strong> Explain why the external potential changes with $\lambda$ to preserve the density and locate the failure if the potential is held fixed.</li>
      <li><strong>Uniform-gas scaling.</strong> Derive $k_F$ by three-dimensional state counting and obtain $\epsilon_x\propto n^{1/3}$ from Coulomb scaling.</li>
      <li><strong>Spin scaling.</strong> Evaluate the exchange factor at $\zeta=0,1/2,1$ and prove evenness in $\zeta$.</li>
