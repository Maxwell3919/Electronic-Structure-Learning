<h2 id="section-4-2">4.2 倒格子与 Brillouin 区 | Reciprocal Lattice and Brillouin Zone</h2>

<BilingualSection titleZh="周期函数只含 reciprocal-lattice Fourier components" titleEn="A periodic function contains only reciprocal-lattice Fourier components" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】 §4.2 · pp. 90–94">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

令 ground-state density、periodic potential 或任意 cell-periodic quantity 满足

$$
f(\mathbf r+\mathbf T)=f(\mathbf r)
$$

对所有 Bravais translations 成立。先在含 $N_i$ cells 的 Born–von Karman supercell 中 Fourier 展开，允许 wavevectors 满足

$$
e^{i\mathbf q\cdot N_i\mathbf a_i}=1,
\qquad
\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf a_i=2\pi\frac{m_i}{N_i}.
$$

Fourier coefficient 可按 cells 分组：

$$
\begin{aligned}
f(\mathbf q)
&=\frac1{N_{\mathrm{cell}}\Omega}
\sum_{\mathbf T}\int_{\Omega}
 f(\mathbf r+\mathbf T)
 e^{-i\mathbf q\cdot(\mathbf r+\mathbf T)}d^dr\\
&=\left[\frac1{N_{\mathrm{cell}}}
\sum_{\mathbf T}e^{-i\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf T}\right]
\frac1\Omega\int_{\Omega}f(\mathbf r)e^{-i\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf r}d^dr.
\end{aligned}
$$

lattice sum 只有在

$$
e^{-i\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf T}=1
$$

对所有 $\mathbf T$ 成立时非零。这定义 reciprocal lattice：

$$
\mathbf G=\sum_i m_i\mathbf b_i,
\qquad m_i\in\mathbb Z,
$$

其中

$$
\boxed{\mathbf a_i\cdot\mathbf b_j=2\pi\delta_{ij}}.
$$

因此

$$
f(\mathbf r)=\sum_{\mathbf G}f(\mathbf G)e^{i\mathbf G\cdot\mathbf r},
\qquad
f(\mathbf G)=\frac1\Omega\int_{\Omega}f(\mathbf r)e^{-i\mathbf G\cdot\mathbf r}d^dr.
$$

这不是 numerical truncation，而是 translation symmetry 的 exact selection rule。plane-wave cutoff 或 FFT grid 只是在无限 reciprocal lattice 中保留有限 subset，是额外数值近似。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Let a ground-state density, periodic potential, or any cell-periodic quantity satisfy

$$
f(\mathbf r+\mathbf T)=f(\mathbf r)
$$

for every Bravais translation. First impose Born–von Karman periodicity over $N_i$ cells. Allowed Fourier vectors obey

$$
e^{i\mathbf q\cdot N_i\mathbf a_i}=1,
\qquad
\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf a_i=2\pi\frac{m_i}{N_i}.
$$

Grouping the Fourier integral by primitive cells produces a lattice sum. It vanishes unless

$$
e^{-i\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf T}=1
$$

for every translation. The surviving vectors form the reciprocal lattice,

$$
\mathbf G=\sum_i m_i\mathbf b_i,
\qquad
\mathbf a_i\cdot\mathbf b_j=2\pi\delta_{ij}.
$$

Hence

$$
f(\mathbf r)=\sum_{\mathbf G}f(\mathbf G)e^{i\mathbf G\cdot\mathbf r},
\qquad
f(\mathbf G)=\frac1\Omega\int_{\Omega}f(\mathbf r)e^{-i\mathbf G\cdot\mathbf r}d^dr.
$$

This is an exact translation-symmetry selection rule rather than a numerical truncation. A plane-wave cutoff or FFT grid retains only a finite subset of the infinite reciprocal lattice and is an additional approximation.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="reciprocal basis 的矩阵与几何构造 | Matrix and geometric construction of the reciprocal basis"
  assumptions={[
    'real-space primitive matrix A is nonsingular;',
    'vectors are stored consistently as columns;',
    'the reciprocal phase convention includes 2π.']}
>

令 $\mathbf A$ 的 columns 为 real-space primitive vectors，$\mathbf B$ 的 columns 为 reciprocal primitive vectors。对偶条件写成

$$
\mathbf A^T\mathbf B=2\pi\mathbf I,
$$

所以

$$
\boxed{
\mathbf B=2\pi(\mathbf A^{-1})^T}.
$$

三维中，

$$
\mathbf b_1
=2\pi\frac{\mathbf a_2\times\mathbf a_3}
{\mathbf a_1\cdot(\mathbf a_2\times\mathbf a_3)},
$$

并作 cyclic permutations。分母若保留有向 volume，$\{\mathbf b_i\}$ 与 $\{\mathbf a_i\}$ 具有一致 handedness；若只取绝对值，需要单独处理 orientation。

取 determinant：

$$
|\det\mathbf B|
=\frac{(2\pi)^d}{|\det\mathbf A|}.
$$

因此 reciprocal primitive-cell measure 与 real primitive-cell measure 满足

$$
\boxed{\Omega_{\mathrm{BZ}}\Omega_{\mathrm{cell}}=(2\pi)^d}.
$$

该关系决定 k-point density 与 real-space supercell 的对偶尺度。real-space cell 扩大 $m$ 倍时，reciprocal primitive cell 缩小 $m$ 倍，primitive-cell bands 折叠成 supercell 中更多 bands。

<p lang="en">The reciprocal basis is the transpose inverse of the real basis multiplied by $2\pi$. The determinant relation gives reciprocal volume $(2\pi)^d/\Omega$. Supercell enlargement and reciprocal-zone shrinkage are therefore exact dual operations, underlying band folding.</p>

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="典型 reciprocal pairs" titleEn="Representative reciprocal pairs">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

1D lattice spacing $a$ 的 reciprocal spacing 是 $2\pi/a$。2D square lattice 的 reciprocal lattice 仍为 square。triangular lattice 的 reciprocal lattice 仍 triangular，但相对于 real-space primitive vectors 旋转并缩放。

3D 中 simple cubic reciprocal 仍 simple cubic，lattice constant 为 $2\pi/a$。fcc 与 bcc reciprocal 互为对偶。使用 Chapter 4.1 的 fcc primitive vectors，可得到 reciprocal primitive vectors proportional to

$$
(1,1,-1),\quad(1,-1,1),\quad(-1,1,1),
$$

它们生成 bcc reciprocal lattice；反向同理。这里“fcc reciprocal is bcc”描述 reciprocal Bravais lattice，不表示 real-space atom arrangement 变成 bcc。

对 hexagonal lattice，若 real-space basal constant 为 $a$、height 为 $c$，reciprocal basal spacing scales as $1/a$，vertical spacing scales as $1/c$。改变 vacuum thickness 的 2D supercell 会压缩 reciprocal $b_z$ spacing，因此三维 reciprocal volume 和 volumetric k density 不能直接当作二维物理量；in-plane sampling 应按面积尺度定义。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A one-dimensional lattice of spacing $a$ has reciprocal spacing $2\pi/a$. A square lattice is reciprocal to a square lattice. A triangular lattice is reciprocal to another triangular lattice, rotated and rescaled relative to the chosen real-space primitive vectors.

In three dimensions, simple cubic is reciprocal to simple cubic. Fcc and bcc are reciprocal Bravais lattices. With standard fcc primitive vectors, reciprocal primitive vectors proportional to

$$
(1,1,-1),\quad(1,-1,1),\quad(-1,1,1)
$$

generate a bcc reciprocal lattice. This statement concerns the reciprocal translation lattice and does not turn the real-space atomic arrangement into bcc.

For a hexagonal lattice, reciprocal basal scale varies as $1/a$ and reciprocal vertical scale as $1/c$. Increasing vacuum in a two-dimensional supercell compresses the artificial reciprocal spacing along $z$. Three-dimensional reciprocal volume and volumetric k density are therefore not direct two-dimensional physical measures; in-plane sampling should be specified by area scale.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="Brillouin zone 是 reciprocal Wigner–Seitz cell" titleEn="The Brillouin zone is the reciprocal Wigner–Seitz cell">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

任意 reciprocal primitive parallelepiped 都可完整表示 inequivalent $\mathbf k$ classes modulo $\mathbf G$。通常选择 reciprocal lattice 的 Wigner–Seitz cell，称 first Brillouin zone。对每个非零 $\mathbf G$，BZ 内点满足

$$
|\mathbf k|\le|\mathbf k-\mathbf G|.
$$

边界平面由

$$
|\mathbf k|^2=|\mathbf k-\mathbf G|^2
$$

给出，即

$$
\boxed{2\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf G=G^2}.
$$

这正是 elastic Bragg condition：$\mathbf k$ 与 $\mathbf k-\mathbf G$ 具有相同 magnitude。BZ interior 不跨越这些 nearest reciprocal Bragg planes；在 nearly-free-electron picture 中周期 potential 在相应 degeneracy planes 打开 gaps。

BZ boundary 的 geometric construction 只使用 reciprocal lattice；high-symmetry labels 还依赖 conventional axes 和 crystallographic convention。不同软件可对同一 physical point 使用不同 labels 或 path order；应保留 Cartesian/fractional coordinates 和 source convention，而不是只记录字母。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Any reciprocal primitive parallelepiped represents all inequivalent $\mathbf k$ classes modulo $\mathbf G$. The conventional choice is the reciprocal-lattice Wigner–Seitz cell, the first Brillouin zone. For each nonzero $\mathbf G$, points inside satisfy

$$
|\mathbf k|\le|\mathbf k-\mathbf G|.
$$

A boundary plane obeys

$$
|\mathbf k|^2=|\mathbf k-\mathbf G|^2,
$$

or

$$
2\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf G=G^2.
$$

This is the elastic Bragg condition: $\mathbf k$ and $\mathbf k-\mathbf G$ have equal magnitude. In a nearly-free-electron picture, a periodic potential opens gaps at such degeneracy planes.

The BZ construction depends only on the reciprocal lattice. High-symmetry labels also depend on conventional axes and crystallographic convention. Different codes may assign different labels or path orders to the same physical point, so Cartesian or fractional coordinates and the convention source must be retained rather than only the letter.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="lattice coordinates、Cartesian coordinates 与 metric" titleEn="Lattice coordinates, Cartesian coordinates, and the metric">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

real-space vector 与 reciprocal-space vector 可分别写成

$$
\boldsymbol\tau=\mathbf A\boldsymbol\tau^L,
\qquad
\mathbf k=\mathbf B\mathbf k^L.
$$

fractional components 不是 Cartesian components。定义 real-space metric

$$
\mathbf g=\mathbf A^T\mathbf A,
$$

则

$$
|\boldsymbol\tau|^2
=(\boldsymbol\tau^L)^T\mathbf g\boldsymbol\tau^L.
$$

reciprocal metric 为

$$
\mathbf g^*=\mathbf B^T\mathbf B
=(2\pi)^2\mathbf g^{-1},
$$

所以

$$
|\mathbf k|^2
=(\mathbf k^L)^T\mathbf g^*\mathbf k^L.
$$

real/reciprocal mixed scalar product 简化为

$$
\mathbf k\cdot\boldsymbol\tau
=2\pi(\mathbf k^L)^T\boldsymbol\tau^L.
$$

只有在 orthonormal cubic axes 中，fractional length 可通过简单 component squares 得到。对 triclinic、hexagonal 或 skew cell，忽略 metric 会产生错误距离、cutoff sphere、neighbor list 和 k-path length。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Write real- and reciprocal-space vectors as

$$
\boldsymbol\tau=\mathbf A\boldsymbol\tau^L,
\qquad
\mathbf k=\mathbf B\mathbf k^L.
$$

Fractional components are not Cartesian components. The real-space metric

$$
\mathbf g=\mathbf A^T\mathbf A
$$

gives

$$
|\boldsymbol\tau|^2
=(\boldsymbol\tau^L)^T\mathbf g\boldsymbol\tau^L.
$$

The reciprocal metric is

$$
\mathbf g^*=\mathbf B^T\mathbf B
=(2\pi)^2\mathbf g^{-1},
$$

and

$$
|\mathbf k|^2
=(\mathbf k^L)^T\mathbf g^*\mathbf k^L.
$$

A mixed scalar product simplifies to

$$
\mathbf k\cdot\boldsymbol\tau
=2\pi(\mathbf k^L)^T\boldsymbol\tau^L.
$$

Only in orthonormal cubic axes can fractional lengths be obtained by naïve component squares. Ignoring the metric in skew, triclinic, or hexagonal cells corrupts distances, cutoff spheres, neighbour lists, and k-path lengths.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="Born–von Karman k grid 与 one-k-per-cell counting | Born–von Karman k grid and one-k-per-cell counting"
  assumptions={[
    'supercell contains N_i primitive translations along a_i;',
    'periodic boundary conditions;',
    'one representative k chosen in a reciprocal primitive cell.']}
>

Bloch phase 在 supercell translation 上必须为一：

$$
e^{i\mathbf k\cdot N_i\mathbf a_i}=1.
$$

令 $\mathbf k=\sum_j\kappa_j\mathbf b_j$，则

$$
\mathbf k\cdot N_i\mathbf a_i
=2\pi N_i\kappa_i=2\pi m_i,
$$

所以

$$
\kappa_i=\frac{m_i}{N_i}.
$$

每个方向有 $N_i$ 个 inequivalent integers modulo $N_i$，因此 reciprocal primitive cell 中 k points 总数为

$$
N_k=\prod_iN_i=N_{\mathrm{cell}}.
$$

每个 k point 占 reciprocal volume

$$
\Delta\Omega_k=\frac{\Omega_{\mathrm{BZ}}}{N_k}.
$$

thermodynamic limit 中

$$
\frac1{N_k}\sum_{\mathbf k}f(\mathbf k)
\longrightarrow
\frac1{\Omega_{\mathrm{BZ}}}
\int_{\mathrm{BZ}}f(\mathbf k)d^dk
=\frac{\Omega_{\mathrm{cell}}}{(2\pi)^d}
\int_{\mathrm{BZ}}f(\mathbf k)d^dk.
$$

该 normalization 是每 primitive cell 的 average。若使用 conventional cell 或 supercell，band count、BZ volume 和 k weights 同时改变；physical per-formula or per-volume observable 必须在一致 normalization 下不变。

<p lang="en">Born–von Karman phases quantize fractional reciprocal coordinates as $m_i/N_i$. The number of inequivalent k points equals the number of primitive cells, giving the standard sum-to-integral conversion. Changing the chosen cell folds bands and changes BZ volume and weights together, but consistently normalized physical observables remain invariant.</p>

</DerivationBlock>
