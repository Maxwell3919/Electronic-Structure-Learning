<h2 id="review">常见误判、原创练习与来源边界 | Misconceptions, Original Exercises, and Source Boundaries</h2>

<BilingualSection titleZh="常见误判" titleEn="Common misconceptions">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <ol>
      <li><strong>“primitive cell 就是最对称的 cell。”</strong> primitive 只要求包含一个 lattice point；Wigner–Seitz cell 才是以距离构造的对称 primitive cell。</li>
      <li><strong>“conventional cell 里的原子数就是 primitive-cell 原子数。”</strong> conventional cell 可能包含多个 primitive cells；必须区分 lattice points 与 basis atoms。</li>
      <li><strong>“honeycomb 是 Bravais lattice。”</strong> honeycomb 通常是 triangular Bravais lattice 加 two-site basis。</li>
      <li><strong>“reciprocal vectors 与 direct vectors 平行。”</strong> 一般 oblique lattice 中不成立；定义是 duality relation。</li>
      <li><strong>“第一 BZ 是任意 primitive reciprocal cell。”</strong> 第一 BZ 是 reciprocal lattice 的 Wigner–Seitz cell。</li>
      <li><strong>“k 与 k+G 是相同普通 momentum。”</strong> 它们是相同 crystal-momentum class，但 plane-wave components 与 matrix elements 仍需正确重标记。</li>
      <li><strong>“Bloch theorem 只适用于 electrons。”</strong> 它适用于任意 translation-invariant linear eigenproblem，包括 phonons 和其他 periodic excitations。</li>
      <li><strong>“band crossing 总能通过沿 path 的线相交判断。”</strong> avoided crossing、degenerate subspace 与 off-path behavior 需要 symmetry 和 full-k analysis。</li>
      <li><strong>“time reversal 意味着每个 k 点 spin degeneracy。”</strong> spinful Kramers partner 一般位于 −k；同一 k degeneracy 只在 TRIM，或有额外 PT symmetry 时成立。</li>
      <li><strong>“高对称路径可以代替 BZ integration。”</strong> 对 2D/3D BZ，路径是 measure-zero subset。</li>
      <li><strong>“DOS peak 直接证明 flat band、instability 或 superconductivity。”</strong> DOS 是 full-BZ energy measure；peak 需要结合 dispersion、matrix elements、interactions 和 response。</li>
      <li><strong>“PDOS 是唯一 orbital population。”</strong> PDOS 依赖 projector、sphere、basis 和 normalization convention。</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <ol>
      <li>A primitive cell need not be the most symmetric cell; the Wigner–Seitz construction provides a symmetric primitive cell.</li>
      <li>A conventional cell can contain several primitive cells, so lattice points and basis atoms must be counted separately.</li>
      <li>The honeycomb structure is usually a triangular Bravais lattice with a two-site basis.</li>
      <li>Direct and reciprocal primitive vectors are not generally parallel; they are dual.</li>
      <li>The first BZ is the reciprocal Wigner–Seitz cell, not an arbitrary primitive reciprocal parallelogram or parallelepiped.</li>
      <li>Wavevectors differing by a reciprocal vector belong to one crystal-momentum class, but basis components still require consistent relabelling.</li>
      <li>Bloch classification applies to any translation-invariant linear eigenproblem, not electrons alone.</li>
      <li>A visual line crossing is not sufficient to classify a degeneracy or its off-path continuation.</li>
      <li>Spinful time reversal pairs k and minus k; same-k Kramers degeneracy is guaranteed at TRIM, or more generally with additional PT symmetry.</li>
      <li>A high-symmetry path has zero volume measure in a two- or three-dimensional BZ and cannot replace integration.</li>
      <li>A DOS peak alone does not establish a flat band, an instability, or superconductivity.</li>
      <li>Projected DOS depends on the chosen projectors, basis, sphere, and normalization.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="原创练习" titleEn="Original exercises">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <ol>
      <li>给定二维 primitive vectors $\mathbf a_1=(a,0)$、$\mathbf a_2=(a\cos\theta,a\sin\theta)$，推导 $\mathbf b_1,\mathbf b_2$，并验证 area product。</li>
      <li>证明一个 integer matrix $M$ 变换后的 vectors $\mathbf a'_i=\sum_j\mathbf a_jM_{ji}$ 在 $|\det M|=1$ 时生成同一 Bravais lattice。</li>
      <li>比较 square lattice 与 honeycomb structure：分别写出 Bravais lattice、basis 和 primitive-cell atom count。</li>
      <li>为 bcc conventional cube 写出一组 primitive vectors，计算 primitive-cell volume，并解释 conventional cell 的 lattice-point count。</li>
      <li>从 reciprocal Wigner–Seitz construction 推导一维第一 BZ 为 $[-\pi/a,\pi/a]$。</li>
      <li>对有限一维 ring 的 $N$ 个 cells，推导 allowed k 与每 band 的 state count。</li>
      <li>从 translation operator eigenvalue 推导 Bloch condition，并说明为什么 k 只定义 modulo G。</li>
      <li>用 free-electron zone-folding model 找出第一 BZ 中最低三条 branches 在 $k=0$ 和 zone edge 的简并关系。</li>
      <li>构造一个同时具有 time reversal 与 inversion 的 spinful two-band spectrum，说明为什么 PT 使每个 k twofold degenerate。</li>
      <li>列出二维 primitive reciprocal coordinates 中四个 TRIM；再说明 labels 如何随 lattice convention 改变而 coordinates class 不变。</li>
      <li>对一个拥有四重 rotation 的 square lattice，写出 generic k 的 star，并讨论 axis/diagonal 上 little group 如何增大。</li>
      <li>对 Fourier integrand $f(k)=1+c\cos(ak)+d\cos(2ak)$，比较一个、两个和四个均匀 k points 的平均误差。</li>
      <li>解释为什么同一 metallic system 的 total energy 可能在较粗 k mesh 上看似收敛，而 DOS at $E_F$ 尚未收敛。</li>
      <li>从 $\varepsilon=\hbar^2k^2/2m^*$ 推导 1D、2D、3D integrated DOS 和 DOS exponent。</li>
      <li>设计一个证据链，区分“high-symmetry path 上没有 imaginary phonon”“full-BZ harmonic stability”和“finite-temperature structural stability”。</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <ol>
      <li>For a two-dimensional oblique lattice, derive the reciprocal basis and verify the cell-area product.</li>
      <li>Show that an integer basis change with determinant plus or minus one generates the same Bravais lattice.</li>
      <li>Separate the Bravais lattice, basis, and primitive-cell atom count for square and honeycomb structures.</li>
      <li>Choose primitive vectors for bcc, calculate their volume, and reconcile it with the conventional-cell lattice-point count.</li>
      <li>Derive the one-dimensional first BZ from the reciprocal Wigner–Seitz construction.</li>
      <li>Derive allowed k values and state counting for a ring of N primitive cells.</li>
      <li>Derive Bloch's condition from translation-operator eigenvalues and explain why k is defined modulo G.</li>
      <li>Use the free-electron folding model to classify degeneracies of the lowest branches at the zone centre and edge.</li>
      <li>Construct a spinful spectrum with time reversal and inversion and explain the same-k PT degeneracy.</li>
      <li>List the four two-dimensional TRIM in primitive reciprocal coordinates and separate coordinate classes from conventional labels.</li>
      <li>Write the k-star of a generic point in a square lattice and discuss how the little group grows on axes and diagonals.</li>
      <li>Compare one-, two-, and four-point quadratures for a short reciprocal Fourier series.</li>
      <li>Explain how a metallic total energy can appear converged while the DOS at the Fermi level remains unconverged.</li>
      <li>Derive the integrated DOS and DOS exponents for parabolic bands in one, two, and three dimensions.</li>
      <li>Design an evidence chain separating path phonons, full-BZ harmonic stability, and finite-temperature structural stability.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>
