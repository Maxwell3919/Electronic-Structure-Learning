<h2 id="reading-orientation">阅读定位 | Reading Orientation</h2>

<BilingualSection titleZh="本章把无限晶体压缩为有限信息" titleEn="This chapter compresses an infinite crystal into finite information" eyebrow="Martin Chapter 4 · printed pp. 81–108">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Chapter 3 从相互作用电子—核 Hamiltonian 出发，说明哪些对象必须在多体理论中定义；Chapter 4 加入晶体的离散平移对称性。无限多个原子位置不再逐一列举，而由有限的 primitive vectors、primitive-cell basis 和空间群操作生成。相同的平移群也对电子、声子、自旋波和其他激发进行严格分类，产生 crystal momentum、Bloch phase、bands 和 Brillouin-zone integrals。

本章的核心不是背诵 sc、fcc、bcc 或高对称点名称，而是掌握三组互相对偶的结构：

$$
\text{real-space lattice}
\longleftrightarrow
\text{reciprocal lattice},
$$

$$
\text{periodic ground-state quantity}
\longleftrightarrow
\mathbf G\text{-space Fourier coefficients},
$$

$$
\text{translation eigenvalue}
\longleftrightarrow
\mathbf k\text{ in one reciprocal primitive cell}.
$$

Brillouin zone、band index、special k points 和 density of states 都从这些关系导出。Chapter 4 只建立普适几何和对称性框架；具体 plane-wave matrix、tight-binding bands、pseudopotential 和 self-consistent implementation 属于后续 Chapters 12–17。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Chapter 3 started from the interacting electron–nuclear Hamiltonian and identified the objects required in a many-body theory. Chapter 4 adds discrete translational symmetry. An infinite set of nuclear positions is generated from finite primitive vectors, a primitive-cell basis, and space-group operations. The same translation group rigorously classifies electrons, phonons, spin waves, and other excitations, producing crystal momentum, Bloch phases, energy bands, and Brillouin-zone integrals.

The purpose is not to memorize names such as sc, fcc, bcc, or lists of high-symmetry points. The chapter develops three dual structures:

$$
\text{real-space lattice}
\longleftrightarrow
\text{reciprocal lattice},
$$

$$
\text{periodic ground-state quantity}
\longleftrightarrow
\mathbf G\text{-space Fourier coefficients},
$$

$$
\text{translation eigenvalue}
\longleftrightarrow
\mathbf k\text{ in one reciprocal primitive cell}.
$$

Brillouin zones, band indices, special points, and density of states follow from these relations. Chapter 4 establishes the universal geometry and symmetry framework; explicit plane-wave matrices, tight-binding bands, pseudopotentials, and self-consistent implementations belong to Chapters 12–17.

<BilingualSection titleZh="前置知识与后续连接" titleEn="Prerequisites and later connections">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

前置知识包括线性代数中的矩阵、行列式和基变换，Fourier series/transform，群的闭合性，以及 Chapter 3 的 independent-particle equation 和 general symmetry classification。Appendix B 提供 lattice/symmetry notation，Appendix F 处理周期 Coulomb sums，Appendix M 连接 FFT 和离散 reciprocal grids，Appendix P–R 则补充 gauge、Berry 和拓扑数学。

Chapter 5 将把本章的 k-space counting 应用于 uniform electron gas；Chapter 14 将把 Bloch theorem 变成 plane-wave 与 tight-binding band equations；Part V 使用 Brillouin-zone integrals 计算 forces、responses 和 spectra；Part VI 将关注 $u_{n\mathbf k}$ 随 $\mathbf k$ 的 global topology。因此本章是后续大多数电子结构计算的共同语言。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Prerequisites include matrices, determinants, and basis transformations; Fourier series and transforms; group closure; and the independent-particle equations and symmetry language of Chapter 3. Appendix B supplies lattice and symmetry notation, Appendix F treats periodic Coulomb sums, Appendix M connects FFTs with discrete reciprocal grids, and Appendices P–R provide gauge, Berry, and topological mathematics.

Chapter 5 applies the present k-space counting to the uniform electron gas. Chapter 14 turns the Bloch theorem into explicit plane-wave and tight-binding band equations. Part V uses Brillouin-zone integrals for forces, response, and spectra. Part VI studies the global topology of $u_{n\mathbf k}$ over the Brillouin zone. This chapter is therefore common language for most later electronic-structure calculations.
