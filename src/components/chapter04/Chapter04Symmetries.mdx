<h2 id="section-4-4">4.4 时间反演与反演对称性 | Time-Reversal and Inversion Symmetries</h2>

<BilingualSection titleZh="spinless time reversal 把 k 映到 −k" titleEn="Spinless time reversal maps k to −k" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】 §4.4 · pp. 98–100">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

对没有磁场、没有磁有序且可选 real scalar potential 的 spinless Schrödinger Hamiltonian，complex conjugation $\mathcal K$ 是 time-reversal operator：

$$
\mathcal T=\mathcal K,
\qquad
\mathcal T^2=+1.
$$

若

$$
\hat H\psi_{n\mathbf k}
=\varepsilon_n(\mathbf k)\psi_{n\mathbf k},
$$

取 complex conjugate 得

$$
\hat H\psi_{n\mathbf k}^*
=\varepsilon_n(\mathbf k)\psi_{n\mathbf k}^*.
$$

Bloch phase 变为

$$
\psi_{n\mathbf k}^*(\mathbf r+\mathbf R)
=e^{-i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf R}\psi_{n\mathbf k}^*(\mathbf r),
$$

所以它属于 $-\mathbf k$ representation。由此 spectrum 满足

$$
\boxed{\varepsilon_n(\mathbf k)=\varepsilon_n(-\mathbf k)}
$$

作为能量集合的关系。若 band 非简并且可连续跟踪，可逐 band 采用同一 relation；在 crossings/degenerate subspaces 中应比较整个 subspace。

时间反演约束不是“任意 band plot 必须左右对称”的视觉规则。需要先明确 reciprocal coordinates、BZ origin、folding convention 和 magnetic state。非零 vector potential、magnetic flux、orbital magnetic order 或 exchange field 可破坏 $\mathcal T$。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For a spinless Schrödinger Hamiltonian with no magnetic field or magnetic order and a real scalar potential, complex conjugation is the time-reversal operator:

$$
\mathcal T=\mathcal K,
\qquad
\mathcal T^2=+1.
$$

Complex conjugation maps a Bloch phase at $\mathbf k$ to one at $-\mathbf k$, so the spectrum obeys

$$
\varepsilon_n(\mathbf k)=\varepsilon_n(-\mathbf k).
$$

For an isolated nondegenerate band the equality may be followed band by band. At crossings or within degenerate subspaces, the invariant statement concerns the whole subspace or energy multiset.

Time-reversal symmetry is not a purely visual rule that any plotted path must look mirror symmetric. Reciprocal coordinates, BZ origin, folding convention, and magnetic state must be specified. Vector potentials, magnetic flux, orbital magnetic order, and exchange fields can break $\mathcal T$.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="spin-1/2 time reversal 与 Kramers theorem" titleEn="Spin-one-half time reversal and Kramers theorem">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

对 spin-$1/2$ particle，time reversal 必须翻转 spin：

$$
\mathcal T=i\sigma_y\mathcal K,
\qquad
\mathcal T^2=-1.
$$

若 Hamiltonian 包含 spin–orbit coupling 但保持 time reversal，$\mathcal T|u_{n\mathbf k}\rangle$ 是 $-\mathbf k$ 上同能量 state。对 time-reversal-invariant momentum（TRIM）

$$
-\mathbf k_*=\mathbf k_*+\mathbf G,
$$

state 与其 time-reversed partner 属于同一 k block。Kramers theorem 说明二者正交。证明从

$$
\langle\psi|\mathcal T\psi\rangle
=\langle\mathcal T^2\psi|\mathcal T\psi\rangle
=-\langle\psi|\mathcal T\psi\rangle
$$

得到 overlap 为零。因此每个 TRIM 上至少 twofold degeneracy。

离开 TRIM 时，partner 位于 $-\mathbf k$，不要求同一 k 上 degeneracy。若 inversion symmetry 同时存在，$\mathcal P$ 把 $\mathbf k$ 映到 $-\mathbf k$，组合 $\mathcal P\mathcal T$ 可在每个 k 生成同能量 orthogonal partner，从而使整个 band structure twofold degenerate。若 inversion broken，Rashba/Dresselhaus-type spin splitting 可出现，但 TRIM Kramers degeneracy 仍保持。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For spin one half, time reversal flips spin:

$$
\mathcal T=i\sigma_y\mathcal K,
\qquad
\mathcal T^2=-1.
$$

A time-reversal-symmetric Hamiltonian, including spin–orbit coupling, maps a state at $\mathbf k$ to an equal-energy state at $-\mathbf k$. At a time-reversal-invariant momentum,

$$
-\mathbf k_*=\mathbf k_*+\mathbf G,
$$

the partner lies in the same k block. Kramers theorem gives orthogonality because

$$
\langle\psi|\mathcal T\psi\rangle
=-\langle\psi|\mathcal T\psi\rangle.
$$

Every TRIM therefore has at least twofold degeneracy.

Away from a TRIM, the partner lies at $-\mathbf k$ and need not be degenerate at the same k. If inversion is also present, the combined $\mathcal P\mathcal T$ operation can generate an orthogonal equal-energy partner at every k, producing twofold degeneracy throughout the BZ. Breaking inversion permits Rashba- or Dresselhaus-type splitting while retaining Kramers degeneracy at TRIM.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="TRIM counting in d dimensions | TRIM counting in d dimensions"
  assumptions={[
    'k is represented modulo reciprocal lattice vectors;',
    'the reciprocal primitive basis is b_i;',
    'time reversal is a symmetry.']}
>

TRIM condition 为

$$
2\mathbf k_*=\mathbf G.
$$

在 reciprocal fractional coordinates 中

$$
\mathbf k_*=\frac12\sum_{i=1}^{d}m_i\mathbf b_i,
\qquad m_i\in\mathbb Z.
$$

由于 adding $\mathbf G$ 不改变 k class，每个 $m_i$ 只需取 $0$ 或 $1$。因此 d-dimensional primitive BZ 有

$$
\boxed{2^d}
$$

个 TRIM：1D 有 2，2D 有 4，3D 有 8。实际 high-symmetry labels 依赖 lattice convention；TRIM 身份应由 fractional coordinates 判断，而不是字母名称。

<p lang="en">The condition $-\mathbf k=\mathbf k+\mathbf G$ reduces to $2\mathbf k=\mathbf G$. Each reciprocal fractional component is zero or one half modulo integers, giving $2^d$ TRIM in dimension $d$.</p>

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="空间反演与 parity" titleEn="Spatial inversion and parity">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

inversion operator $\mathcal P$ 作用为

$$
(\mathcal P\psi)(\mathbf r)=\psi(-\mathbf r),
\qquad
\mathcal P^2=1.
$$

若 crystal 有 inversion center 且 Hamiltonian 与 $\mathcal P$ 对易，则

$$
\varepsilon_n(\mathbf k)=\varepsilon_n(-\mathbf k).
$$

在 inversion-invariant momenta，也就是 TRIM classes，Bloch states 可选为 parity eigenstates：

$$
\mathcal P|u_{n\mathbf k_*}\rangle
=\xi_n(\mathbf k_*)|u_{n\mathbf k_*}\rangle,
\qquad
\xi_n=\pm1.
$$

parity eigenvalues 可用于 selection rules 和某些 time-reversal topological indices，但只能在 inversion symmetry、global occupied subspace 和适当 gap 都成立时使用。观察 band inversion 的 orbital labels 或单个 parity swap 不自动证明 topology；需要对所有 occupied Kramers pairs 和所有 TRIM 使用一致 gauge/convention。

origin choice 与 inversion center 有关。若改变 origin，parity representation phase 也可改变；物理 invariant 必须按同一 crystallographic convention 计算。magnetic order 可保留 inversion 但破坏 time reversal，或反之；对 magnetic space groups 必须使用实际 symmetry 而非非磁 parent group。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The inversion operator acts as

$$
(\mathcal P\psi)(\mathbf r)=\psi(-\mathbf r),
\qquad
\mathcal P^2=1.
$$

If the crystal has an inversion centre and $[H,\mathcal P]=0$, then

$$
\varepsilon_n(\mathbf k)=\varepsilon_n(-\mathbf k).
$$

At inversion-invariant momentum classes, Bloch states may be chosen as parity eigenstates,

$$
\mathcal P|u_{n\mathbf k_*}\rangle
=\xi_n(\mathbf k_*)|u_{n\mathbf k_*}\rangle,
\qquad
\xi_n=\pm1.
$$

Parity eigenvalues can enter selection rules and some time-reversal topological indices, but only when inversion symmetry, the global occupied subspace, and the appropriate gap are established. A visually inferred band inversion or one parity exchange does not itself prove topology.

The representation depends on the chosen inversion centre and origin convention. Magnetic order may preserve inversion while breaking time reversal, or vice versa, so the actual magnetic space group must replace the nonmagnetic parent symmetry.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<h2 id="section-4-5">4.5 点群对称性 | Point Symmetries</h2>

<BilingualSection titleZh="space group = translations + point/nonsymmorphic operations" titleEn="A space group combines translations with point and nonsymmorphic operations" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】 §4.5 · pp. 100–101">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

除 translations 外，crystal 可在 rotations、reflections、inversion 或 rotoinversions 下保持不变。point operation $g$ 作用于位置为

$$
\mathbf r\mapsto \mathbf R_g\mathbf r,
$$

并必须把 Bravais lattice 映射到自身：

$$
\mathbf R_g\mathbf T\in\mathcal L.
$$

所有保持一个固定点的操作构成 crystal point group。完整 space group 还包含 translations，以及 screw rotations 和 glide reflections 这类 point operation 加 fractional translation 的 nonsymmorphic elements。

点群同时作用于 reciprocal space：

$$
\mathbf k\mapsto \mathbf R_g\mathbf k.
$$

若 $g$ 是 Hamiltonian symmetry，energy spectrum 满足

$$
\{\varepsilon_n(\mathbf R_g\mathbf k)\}
=\{\varepsilon_n(\mathbf k)\}.
$$

因此可把 BZ 积分缩减到 irreducible wedge，并用 star multiplicities/weights 恢复 full BZ。结构稍有 distortion、magnetic order、external field 或 numerical symmetry breaking 都可能缩小有效 group；不可用 parent crystal symmetry 强制实际 distorted state。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Beyond translations, a crystal may be invariant under rotations, reflections, inversion, or rotoinversions. A point operation acts as

$$
\mathbf r\mapsto \mathbf R_g\mathbf r
$$

and must map the Bravais lattice onto itself. Operations preserving a fixed point form the crystallographic point group. The full space group also contains translations and nonsymmorphic screw and glide operations that combine a point transformation with a fractional translation.

Point operations act on reciprocal vectors as

$$
\mathbf k\mapsto \mathbf R_g\mathbf k.
$$

For a Hamiltonian symmetry, the energy spectrum is identical over a k-star. Brillouin-zone integration can therefore be restricted to an irreducible wedge with weights reconstructing the full zone. Distortion, magnetic order, external fields, and numerical symmetry breaking can reduce the effective group; the symmetry of a parent structure must not be imposed on a lower-symmetry state without verification.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="little group 与 symmetry-enforced degeneracy" titleEn="Little groups and symmetry-enforced degeneracy">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

给定 $\mathbf k$，little group $G_{\mathbf k}$ 包含满足

$$
\mathbf R_g\mathbf k=\mathbf k+\mathbf G
$$

的 space-group operations。它们在同一 k block 内作用，Bloch eigenstates 可按 little-group irreducible representations（irreps）分类。

若一个 irrep 维数为 $d_\alpha$，对应 states 至少 $d_\alpha$-fold degenerate（再乘可能的 Kramers/other degeneracy）。不同 irreps 的 bands 可在保持 symmetry 的 path/point 上 crossing，因为 matrix element 被 symmetry 禁止；相同 irrep 通常 hybridize 并 avoided-cross，除非存在额外 parameter 或 topology。

离开 high-symmetry point，little group 通常变小，degenerate irrep 可 split 成较低维 irreps。沿 path 的 compatibility relations 描述 point irreps 如何分支到 line irreps，是追踪 band connectivity、nonsymmorphic enforced crossings 和 topological quantum chemistry 的基础。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For a given $\mathbf k$, the little group contains operations satisfying

$$
\mathbf R_g\mathbf k=\mathbf k+\mathbf G.
$$

They act within the same k block, so Bloch eigenstates can be classified by little-group irreducible representations.

A $d_\alpha$-dimensional irrep enforces at least $d_\alpha$-fold degeneracy, before additional Kramers or other factors. Bands belonging to different irreps may cross on a symmetry-preserving line or point because hybridization matrix elements vanish by symmetry. Bands of the same irrep generally repel unless another parameter or topology protects a crossing.

Moving away from a high-symmetry point usually reduces the little group, and a degenerate irrep can split into lower-dimensional irreps. Compatibility relations describe how point irreps branch along lines and underpin band connectivity, nonsymmorphic crossings, and symmetry-based topological analysis.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="projection operator for an irrep | Projection operator for an irrep"
  assumptions={[
    'finite point/little group or a finite co-group representation;',
    'unitary representation U(g);',
    'character table of irrep α is known.']}
>

对 group order $|G|$ 和 irrep $\alpha$ dimension $d_\alpha$，character projection operator 为

$$
\boxed{
\hat P^{(\alpha)}
=\frac{d_\alpha}{|G|}
\sum_{g\in G}
\chi^{(\alpha)}(g)^*\hat U(g)}.
$$

character orthogonality 使

$$
\hat P^{(\alpha)}\hat P^{(\beta)}
=\delta_{\alpha\beta}\hat P^{(\alpha)}.
$$

对任意 trial state 应用 $\hat P^{(\alpha)}$ 可提取属于 irrep $\alpha$ 的 component。若结果为零，trial state 中没有该 irrep；若非零，可据此构造 symmetry-adapted basis。

对 Bloch states，$\hat U(g)$ 还包含 fractional translation phase、spin rotation 和 possible antiunitary operations；简单 point-group character table 只适用于对应 assumptions。double groups 用于 spinor/SOC，magnetic groups 与 corepresentations 用于 antiunitary symmetries。

<p lang="en">Character-weighted group averaging projects a trial state into an irreducible representation. For Bloch spinors, nonsymmorphic phases, spin rotations, and antiunitary operations require the appropriate little-group, double-group, or magnetic-corepresentation formalism rather than an ordinary point-group table.</p>

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="selection rules 是 matrix-element statements" titleEn="Selection rules are matrix-element statements">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

一个 transition matrix element

$$
M_{fi}=\langle f|\hat O|i\rangle
$$

只有当 direct product

$$
\Gamma_f^*\otimes\Gamma_O\otimes\Gamma_i
$$

包含 totally symmetric irrep 时可非零。selection rule 依赖 initial/final irreps、operator symmetry、photon polarization、wavevector 和 broken symmetries；不是由 DOS 或 orbital labels 单独决定。

例如 inversion-symmetric crystal 中 electric-dipole operator 是 odd parity，dipole transition 需要 initial/final parity 相反（在 applicable high-symmetry point 和 local parity picture 下）。若 inversion broken、phonon-assisted、surface-sensitive 或 finite-q process，原 selection rule 可放宽。一个 symmetry-allowed transition 仍可能因 matrix element 很小而不可见；allowed 不等于 strong。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A transition matrix element

$$
M_{fi}=\langle f|\hat O|i\rangle
$$

can be nonzero only if

$$
\Gamma_f^*\otimes\Gamma_O\otimes\Gamma_i
$$

contains the totally symmetric representation. A selection rule depends on initial and final irreps, operator symmetry, polarization, wavevector, and broken symmetries; it cannot be inferred from DOS or orbital labels alone.

For an inversion-symmetric system, the electric-dipole operator has odd parity, so applicable dipole transitions connect opposite parities. Broken inversion, phonon assistance, surfaces, and finite-q probes relax the simple rule. A symmetry-allowed transition may still have a tiny matrix element; allowed does not mean strong.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>
