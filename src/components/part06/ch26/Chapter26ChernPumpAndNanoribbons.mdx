## 26.5 二维参数空间中的 Berry 相位绕行：Chern 数与拓扑转变 | Winding of the Berry Phase in Two Dimensions: Chern Numbers and Topological Transitions

<BilingualSection titleZh="用第三个 Pauli 分量绕开一维闭隙" titleEn="Using the third Pauli component to avoid a one-dimensional gap closing" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

一维反演对称模型把 $\mathbf d(k)$ 限制在一个平面。winding 与 nonwinding 曲线若在该平面内连续连接，必定经过原点并闭隙。Martin 接着允许一个破坏反演的第三分量，使曲线可以离开原平面。对单个固定参数 $\lambda$，Berry phase 不再被量子化为 $0$ 或 $\pi$；它可以连续变化。

若 $\lambda$ 本身完成闭合周期，$(k,\lambda)$ 共同形成二维 torus。每个点映射到非零 $\mathbf d$，归一化后得到

$$
\hat{\mathbf d}:T^2\longrightarrow S^2.
$$

这个二维闭曲面在 d 空间中可以包围原点，也可以不包围。原点是两带简并点；只要整个 torus 上 $|\mathbf d|>0$，映射的整数 degree 不能连续改变。真实二维晶体中的 $(k_x,k_y)$ 与 pump 中的 $(k,\lambda)$ 在数学上都给出二维参数空间，但 $\lambda$ 是外部循环参数，不应与第二个晶体动量混为一谈。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

An inversion-symmetric one-dimensional model confines $\mathbf d(k)$ to a plane. A winding and a nonwinding curve cannot be connected within that plane without passing through the origin and closing the gap. Martin next allows a third component that breaks inversion and lets the curve move out of the original plane. At any fixed $\lambda$, the Berry phase is no longer quantized to $0$ or $\pi$ and may vary continuously.

If $\lambda$ completes a closed cycle, $(k,\lambda)$ forms a two-dimensional torus. Every point maps to a nonzero $\mathbf d$, and normalization gives

$$
\hat{\mathbf d}:T^2\longrightarrow S^2.
$$

The resulting closed surface in d space may enclose the origin or may avoid it. The origin is the two-band degeneracy. As long as $|\mathbf d|>0$ everywhere on the torus, the integer degree of the map cannot change continuously. The coordinates $(k_x,k_y)$ of a physical two-dimensional crystal and $(k,\lambda)$ of a pump are mathematically similar two-dimensional parameter spaces, but $\lambda$ is an external cyclic parameter rather than a second crystal momentum.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="两带 projector 的 Berry 曲率与 Chern 数 | Berry curvature and Chern number from the two-band projector"
  assumptions={[
    '低能带在完整二维参数空间上与高能带分离；',
    '参数取向固定为 (κ1,κ2)；',
    'Hamiltonian 写为 H=d0I+d·σ，低能 projector 为 P−。',
  ]}
>

低能带 projector 为

$$
P_-=\frac12\left(I-\hat{\mathbf d}\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma\right).
$$

单带 Berry 曲率可用 projector 写成

$$
F_{12}=i\,\operatorname{Tr}\!\left(P_-[\partial_1P_-,\partial_2P_-]\right).
$$

代入 $P_-$ 并使用 Pauli 代数，得到

$$
\boxed{F_{12}=-\frac12\hat{\mathbf d}\cdot
\left(\partial_1\hat{\mathbf d}\times\partial_2\hat{\mathbf d}\right)}.
$$

因此低能带 Chern 数为

$$
\boxed{C_-=-\frac{1}{4\pi}\int d\kappa_1d\kappa_2\,
\hat{\mathbf d}\cdot
\left(\partial_1\hat{\mathbf d}\times\partial_2\hat{\mathbf d}\right)}.
$$

积分计算 $\hat{\mathbf d}$ 覆盖单位球面的有向次数。若交换参数顺序、交换能带标签或改变 Pauli convention，符号可以改变；整数绝对值及相变两侧的差必须和约定一起报告。

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="拓扑整数只能在有隙参数空间上定义" titleEn="The integer is defined only on a gapped parameter space" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

局部 Berry curvature 可以在绝大多数点有限，但只要 torus 上存在一个 $\mathbf d=0$ 点，占据带就无法全局分离，Chern 数不再属于该单带流形。数值计算中必须先检查直接能隙，再检查网格收敛、规范不变性和整数稳定性。单个 curvature hotspot 或局部 band inversion 不能替代完整积分。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The local Berry curvature may remain finite almost everywhere, yet a single point with $\mathbf d=0$ prevents the occupied band from being globally separated and removes the single-band Chern classification. A numerical calculation must establish the direct gap before checking mesh convergence, gauge invariance, and integer stability. One curvature hotspot or a local band inversion does not replace the full integral.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 26.6 Thouless 量子化粒子泵 | The Thouless Quantized Particle Pump

<BilingualSection titleZh="闭合绝热循环把一维问题提升为二维拓扑问题" titleEn="A closed adiabatic cycle promotes a one-dimensional problem to a two-dimensional topological problem" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Martin 采用 two-site/Rice–Mele 型模型，使键交替参数与子格能差沿一个闭合环路变化。环路包围 $t_1=t_2$、$\Delta=0$ 的闭隙点，但在实际路径上始终保持 $|\mathbf d|>0$。在周期体系中，低能带 Wannier center 从一个键中心连续移动到站点、另一键中心、另一站点，并在一个周期后平移一个晶格常数；Hamiltonian 返回初态，但电子中心属于相邻的 polarization branch。

在有限链中，同一过程表现为左右端局域态跨越体隙并分别并入相反的体连续谱。一端失去一个占据态，另一端得到一个占据态。改变终止或表面势可以移动端态的具体能量和出现位置，却不能消除净谱流，只要 bulk cycle 保持有隙并具有非零 Chern 数。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Martin uses a two-site/Rice–Mele-type model in which the bond alternation and sublattice offset vary along a closed loop. The loop surrounds the gap-closing point $t_1=t_2$, $\Delta=0$, while the actual path maintains $|\mathbf d|>0$. In a periodic system, the lower-band Wannier centre moves continuously from a bond centre to a site, to the other bond centre, to the other site, and after one cycle is translated by one lattice constant. The Hamiltonian has returned to itself, but the electronic centre lies on the neighbouring polarization branch.

In a finite chain, the same process appears as left- and right-localized states traversing the bulk gap and merging into opposite bulk continua. One end loses an occupied state while the other gains one. A different termination or surface potential may shift the detailed end-state energies and the point in the cycle where they occur, but it cannot remove the net spectral flow as long as the bulk cycle remains gapped and has nonzero Chern number.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="Wannier-center 位移与泵浦整数 | Wannier-centre displacement and the pumped integer"
  assumptions={[
    '目标带在完整 (k,λ) torus 上孤立并完全占据；',
    'λ 从 0 到 2π 构成闭合循环；',
    '演化相对于最小直接能隙足够缓慢；',
    '忽略非绝热激发、耗散和粒子泄漏。',
  ]}
>

对每个固定 $\lambda$，定义一维 Berry phase

$$
\gamma(\lambda)=\oint_{\rm BZ}A_k(k,\lambda)\,dk.
$$

Wannier center 的电子部分满足

$$
\frac{\bar x(\lambda)}{a}=\frac{\gamma(\lambda)}{2\pi}\pmod1.
$$

沿 $\lambda$ 连续展开 Berry phase，完整周期的变化为

$$
\Delta\gamma=\int_0^{2\pi}d\lambda\int_{\rm BZ}dk\,F_{k\lambda}=2\pi C_-.
$$

于是

$$
\boxed{\frac{\Delta\bar x}{a}=C_-}.
$$

对每晶胞一条完全占据的低能带，泵浦粒子数为

$$
\boxed{N_{\rm pump}=C_-}.
$$

若载流子为电子，按电子电荷 $-e$ 计的电荷输运为 $Q=-eC_-$；方向符号取决于 $(k,\lambda)$ 取向和循环方向。反向走过同一环路会反转泵浦方向。

</DerivationBlock>

<ThoulessPumpExplorer />

<BilingualCallout titleZh="量子化不是任意参数扫描的属性" titleEn="Quantization is not a property of an arbitrary parameter scan" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

非闭合路径只定义极化差，不定义整数 pump；穿过闭隙点时 occupied manifold 失去分离；有限速率可产生 Landau–Zener 激发；部分填充时积分不再等同于一条完整 occupied band 的 Chern 数；开放体系中的耗散和电极耦合还需要额外输运理论。页面中的整数结论只适用于声明的绝热、闭合、有隙、完全填充条件。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

An open path defines a polarization difference rather than an integer pump. Crossing a gap-closing point destroys the separated occupied manifold. A finite drive rate can create Landau–Zener excitations. Partial filling no longer integrates one complete occupied band, and dissipation or electrode coupling in an open system requires additional transport theory. The integer statement on this page applies only under the declared adiabatic, closed, gapped, and completely filled conditions.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 26.7 Graphene 纳米带与两站点模型 | Graphene Nanoribbons and the Two-Site Model

<BilingualSection titleZh="界面态形成的有效二聚链" titleEn="An effective dimerized chain formed from interface states" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Martin 以宽度交替的 armchair graphene nanoribbon 为实际连接。窄带段本身是有隙的一维体系；不同宽度段可以具有不同的 occupied-band winding 分类。两段的结处出现局域 midgap interface state。周期性排列这些界面后，相邻界面态之间的重叠交替形成 $t_1$ 与 $t_2$，从而得到一个表面上类似 SSH 的有效两站点带。

关键限制是：这个有效带位于原纳米带所有价带与导带之间的 gap 中。决定端态的 bulk invariant 不是只对两个 midgap 基底态计数，而要对目标 gap 以下的完整 occupied manifold 计数。Martin 强调，在所讨论的 7/9 宽度结构中，只看有效两站点模型会给出与完整多带 winding 相反的“强键/弱键终止”结论。

因此低能有效模型可以准确描述界面态之间的耦合，却不必自动继承原多带体系的绝对拓扑标签。需要把有效模型嵌入其母体带结构，并核验目标 gap、occupied-subspace invariant 和真实终止。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Martin connects the discussion to armchair graphene nanoribbons with alternating widths. Each narrow ribbon segment is itself a gapped one-dimensional system, and segments of different width can carry different occupied-band winding classifications. A localized midgap interface state appears at their junction. Repeating the interfaces periodically produces alternating overlaps $t_1$ and $t_2$ between neighbouring interface states, yielding an effective two-site band that resembles an SSH chain.

The essential limitation is that this effective band lies inside the gap formed by all valence and conduction bands of the parent nanoribbons. The bulk invariant relevant to the end state is not obtained by counting only the two midgap basis states; it must include the full occupied manifold below the target gap. Martin emphasizes that for the discussed 7/9-width structure, the naive two-site model predicts the opposite strong-bond/weak-bond termination rule from the complete multiband winding.

The low-energy effective model may accurately describe couplings among interface states without automatically inheriting the absolute topological label of the parent multiband system. It must be embedded in the parent band structure, with the target gap, occupied-subspace invariant, and physical termination checked explicitly.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="从低能模型回到真实纳米带的证据链" titleEn="Evidence chain from a low-energy model back to the real nanoribbon" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <ol>
      <li>确认具体 ribbon 宽度、边缘化学和结构周期；</li>
      <li>在完整一维 BZ 上确认目标 gap，而不是只看高对称点；</li>
      <li>对该 gap 以下的全部 occupied bands 计算适用的 winding、Berry phase 或 symmetry indicator；</li>
      <li>在 junction/finite ribbon 中检查 midgap 态的空间局域性与母体体带投影；</li>
      <li>对长度、终止、边缘钝化、基底和 Wannier 窗口做收敛与稳定性检查；</li>
      <li>最后才把有效 $t_1$–$t_2$ 参数与端态位置联系起来。</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <ol>
      <li>fix the ribbon widths, edge chemistry, and structural period;</li>
      <li>establish the target gap over the full one-dimensional Brillouin zone rather than only at high-symmetry points;</li>
      <li>evaluate the applicable winding, Berry phase, or symmetry indicator for every occupied band below that gap;</li>
      <li>test the spatial localization of the midgap state and its relation to projected parent bulk bands in a junction or finite ribbon;</li>
      <li>check length, termination, edge passivation, basis, and Wannier-window stability;</li>
      <li>only then relate the effective $t_1$–$t_2$ parameters to the end-state location.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 复核、原创练习与来源 | Review, Original Practice, and Sources

### 常见误判 | Common failure modes

| 误判 | 缺失条件 | 本章允许的结论 |
|---|---|---|
| $d_0$ 改变后拓扑也改变 | $d_0$ 不改变本征矢或 gap | 只要占据定义不变，projector 几何不变 |
| 任意一维两带模型都有整数 winding | 需要 chiral、inversion 或等价平面约束 | 一般三分量曲线可离开平面并连续解绕 |
| $|t_2|>|t_1|$ 永远保证端态 | 还需固定 unit cell、真实终止与对称性 | 对所示 SSH 终止存在可归一化解析左端解 |
| band inversion 足以证明材料拓扑 | 缺少 full-BZ gap、occupied invariant 与边界证据 | 在受控两带反演模型中标记质量符号改变 |
| pump 环路围着图上某点就量子化 | 需要完整 torus 有隙、闭合、绝热、完全填充 | 满足条件时 $N_{\rm pump}=C_-$ |
| 纳米带 midgap 有效模型可单独定绝对拓扑 | 忽略母体全部 occupied bands | 有效模型描述耦合，绝对 invariant 必须回到母体流形 |

### 本站原创练习 | Original exercises

1. 给定 $H=\begin{pmatrix}2&1-2i\\1+2i&-4\end{pmatrix}$，用迹公式求 $d_0$ 与 $\mathbf d$，再验证 $E_\pm=d_0\pm|\mathbf d|$。
2. 对 $t_1=0.7,t_2=1.1,\Delta=0.2$，先解析求最小直接 gap，再用均匀 k 网格验证并量化离散误差。
3. 固定一个物理有限链，把 Bloch unit cell 平移一个站点。记录 $q(k)$、winding/Zak 代表值和终止标签如何同时变化，说明为何不能只改其中一项。
4. 从零能递推式求归一化常数，并估算长度为 $N$ 的两端态能级分裂对 $N$ 的指数依赖。
5. 对 pump 模型分别选择包围一个、零个和两个闭隙点的参数环路。先预测低能带 Chern 数，再用离散固角算法验证。
6. 构造一个有明显隙内局域态但 pump Chern 数为零的表面势例子，说明“有端态”与“存在净谱流”的区别。
7. 设计一份 graphene nanoribbon 计算审计表，要求逐项区分结构来源、full-BZ gap、occupied-band invariant、有效模型拟合和端态局域证据。
