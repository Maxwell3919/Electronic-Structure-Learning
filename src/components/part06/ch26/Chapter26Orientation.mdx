## 阅读定位 | Reading Orientation

<BilingualSection titleZh="本章的受控问题" titleEn="The controlled problem of this chapter" eyebrow="Orientation">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Chapter 25 建立了 Bloch 本征矢、occupied subspace、Berry 相位和 Chern 数的共同语言。本章把这些对象压缩到二维 Hilbert 空间，在那里每个 Hermitian Hamiltonian 都能写成单位矩阵与三支 Pauli 矩阵的线性组合。这个压缩使能隙闭合、规范补丁、winding、边界终止和绝热泵可以逐步计算，而不是只依赖几何比喻。

这里的“两带”指每个参数点上使用二维基底表示的模型空间。真实材料通常需要更多轨道、spin、SOC 和远离费米能的带；一个有效两带模型只有在目标流形、对称性、能隙和参数映射被明确验证时，才能承载材料解释。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Chapter 25 established the common language of Bloch eigenvectors, occupied subspaces, Berry phases, and Chern numbers. This chapter compresses those objects into a two-dimensional Hilbert space, where every Hermitian Hamiltonian is a linear combination of the identity and three Pauli matrices. The reduction makes gap closing, gauge patches, winding, boundary termination, and adiabatic pumping directly calculable rather than merely pictorial.

“Two band” means a two-dimensional basis at each point of parameter space. Real materials normally require additional orbitals, spin, spin–orbit coupling, and remote bands. An effective two-band model can support a material interpretation only after its target manifold, symmetries, gap, and parameter mapping have been validated.

## 对象与约定 | Objects and Conventions

<BilingualSection titleZh="本章固定约定" titleEn="Conventions fixed for this chapter" eyebrow="Conventions">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

本章使用

$$
H(\boldsymbol\kappa)=d_0(\boldsymbol\kappa)I+\mathbf d(\boldsymbol\kappa)\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma,
$$

其中 $\boldsymbol\kappa$ 可以是晶体动量 $k$，也可以是 $(k,\lambda)$ 等联合参数。除非另行说明，二维 Brillouin zone 的正向为 $(k_x,k_y)$，低能带记为 $-$，occupied projector 记为 $P_-$。Bloch sphere 上的单位向量为 $\hat{\mathbf d}=\mathbf d/|\mathbf d|$，只有在 $|\mathbf d|>0$ 时定义。

“winding number”只用于映射确实被限制在允许整数绕行的子空间时；一般三分量 $\mathbf d(k)$ 的一维闭合曲线没有自动量子化的 winding。Zak phase 还依赖 Bloch/unit-cell convention，而端态进一步依赖物理终止。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

We use

$$
H(\boldsymbol\kappa)=d_0(\boldsymbol\kappa)I+\mathbf d(\boldsymbol\kappa)\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma,
$$

where $\boldsymbol\kappa$ may denote crystal momentum $k$ or a joint parameter such as $(k,\lambda)$. Unless stated otherwise, the positive orientation of a two-dimensional Brillouin zone is $(k_x,k_y)$, the lower band is labelled $-$, and its occupied projector is $P_-$. The Bloch-sphere vector $\hat{\mathbf d}=\mathbf d/|\mathbf d|$ exists only where $|\mathbf d|>0$.

A “winding number” is used only when the map is constrained to a subspace that supports an integer winding. A generic three-component closed curve $\mathbf d(k)$ in one dimension is not automatically assigned such an integer. The Zak phase also depends on Bloch and unit-cell conventions, while an end state additionally depends on the physical termination.
