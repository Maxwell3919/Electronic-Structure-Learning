## 阅读定位 | Reading orientation

<BilingualSection titleZh="从二维 Z₂ obstruction 进入三维体相与表面" titleEn="From the two-dimensional Z₂ obstruction to three-dimensional bulk and surfaces" eyebrow="【本章定位 | Chapter role】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Chapter 27 建立了二维 spinful time-reversal insulator 的核心判断：目标 gap 必须在完整 Brillouin zone 上开放，occupied Kramers manifold 必须完整，边缘证据关注 odd/even partner connectivity。Chapter 28 把这些对象放入三维 BZ。三维晶体有八个 time-reversal-invariant momenta（TRIM），由它们或等价的 Wilson-loop/sewing-matrix 数据构造一个 strong index 与三个 weak indices：

$$
(\nu_0;\nu_1\nu_2\nu_3).
$$

$\nu_0=1$ 表示 strong topological insulator。它不能在保持 bulk gap 与 time reversal 的条件下连续拆成彼此独立的二维层，并在任意理想表面具有 odd surface-Dirac parity。$\nu_0=0$ 且至少一个 weak index 非零时，对应 weak phase；它可理解为二维拓扑层的堆叠，因此表面态对晶格平移、表面取向与无序更敏感。

本章后半从 gapped phases 转向 bulk gap 必须在离散点闭合的 semimetals。表面 Dirac cone、bulk Dirac node、bulk Weyl node 和 Fermi arc 是四种不同对象，不能用“看起来像线性交叉”互相替代。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Chapter 27 established the central test for a two-dimensional spinful time-reversal insulator: the target gap must be open throughout the Brillouin zone, the occupied Kramers manifold must be complete, and edge evidence concerns odd/even partner connectivity. Chapter 28 places these objects in a three-dimensional BZ. A three-dimensional crystal has eight time-reversal-invariant momenta (TRIM), from which—or from equivalent Wilson-loop or sewing-matrix data—one constructs one strong and three weak indices:

$$
(\nu_0;\nu_1\nu_2\nu_3).
$$

$\nu_0=1$ denotes a strong topological insulator. It cannot be continuously separated into independent two-dimensional layers while preserving the bulk gap and time reversal, and every ideal surface has odd surface-Dirac parity. When $\nu_0=0$ but at least one weak index is nonzero, the phase is weak. It can be interpreted as a stack of two-dimensional topological layers, so its surface states are more sensitive to lattice translation, surface orientation, and disorder.

The second half of the chapter moves from gapped phases to semimetals whose bulk gap closes at isolated points. A surface Dirac cone, a bulk Dirac node, a bulk Weyl node, and a Fermi arc are four distinct objects; a visually linear crossing cannot substitute for the symmetry and topology that define them.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Read the chapter through the following object chain:

$$
\text{occupied 3D manifold}
\rightarrow (\nu_0;\nu_1\nu_2\nu_3)
\rightarrow \text{surface connectivity}
\rightarrow \text{bulk node charge}
\rightarrow \text{surface Fermi arc}.
$$

The insulating classification in the first half presupposes a three-dimensional bulk gap. The Weyl/Dirac classification in the second half instead treats a controlled bulk gap closing. One then cannot assign a single insulating Z₂ index to the entire occupied manifold; the relevant evidence is a closed two-dimensional surface surrounding a node, its chirality, the protecting or breaking symmetries, and the pairing of nodes.

<BilingualCallout titleZh="本章固定约定" titleEn="Fixed conventions for this chapter" kind="source">

- 八个 TRIM 用二进制坐标 $(n_1n_2n_3)$ 表示，其中 $n_i\in\{0,1\}$，对应 $\mathbf K_{n_1n_2n_3}=\tfrac12(n_1\mathbf b_1+n_2\mathbf b_2+n_3\mathbf b_3)$。
- inversion shortcut 中，$\delta_{n_1n_2n_3}$ 是该 TRIM 下所有 occupied Kramers pairs 的 parity product，不是单条“反转带”的 parity。
- $\nu_0$ 是 strong index；$(\nu_1\nu_2\nu_3)$ 指定 weak reciprocal vector 的模 2 分量，不直接给出某个表面的具体 Dirac-cone 位置。
- Weyl chirality 的符号依赖 momentum orientation 与 Hamiltonian convention；节点的非零 integer charge、相反 charge 配对和 surface spectral flow 是更稳健的陈述。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

- The eight TRIM are labelled by binary coordinates $(n_1n_2n_3)$, with $n_i\in\{0,1\}$ and $\mathbf K_{n_1n_2n_3}=\tfrac12(n_1\mathbf b_1+n_2\mathbf b_2+n_3\mathbf b_3)$.
- In the inversion shortcut, $\delta_{n_1n_2n_3}$ is the parity product of every occupied Kramers pair at that TRIM, not the parity of one selected “inverted band.”
- $\nu_0$ is the strong index. $(\nu_1\nu_2\nu_3)$ gives the mod-two components of a weak reciprocal vector; it does not by itself locate a particular surface Dirac cone.
- The sign of Weyl chirality depends on momentum orientation and Hamiltonian convention. The nonzero integer charge of a node, pairing with an opposite charge, and the associated surface spectral flow are more robust statements.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="证据门槛" titleEn="Evidence gates" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

局部 band inversion、一个高对称点的 parity change、SOC 打开的局部 gap、单条高对称线上的线性交叉或一张 surface-like band 图都不足以完成分类。三维绝缘体至少需要 full-BZ bulk gap、完整 occupied manifold 与适用的 Z₂ 算法；Weyl/Dirac semimetal 至少需要 full-BZ node search、对称性分析、节点附近线性色散、闭合曲面 Berry flux 或等价 charge，以及声明的 surface termination。材料结论还需要方法、结构、SOC、k 网格、能量窗口与数值收敛证据。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A local band inversion, one parity change at one high-symmetry point, a locally SOC-opened gap, a linear crossing along one high-symmetry line, or one surface-like band plot is insufficient for classification. A three-dimensional insulator requires at least a full-BZ bulk gap, the complete occupied manifold, and an applicable Z₂ algorithm. A Weyl or Dirac semimetal requires at least a full-BZ node search, symmetry analysis, linear dispersion near the node, Berry flux through a closed enclosing surface or an equivalent charge, and a declared surface termination. A material claim additionally requires method, structure, SOC, k-mesh, energy-window, and numerical-convergence evidence.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>
