## 28.1 三维 weak/strong topological insulators：四个拓扑指标 | Weak and Strong Topological Insulators in Three Dimensions: Four Topological Invariants

<BilingualSection titleZh="二维切片如何组成三维 Z₂ 数据" titleEn="How two-dimensional slices assemble the three-dimensional Z₂ data" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

三维 reciprocal primitive vectors 记为 $\mathbf b_1,\mathbf b_2,\mathbf b_3$。spinful time reversal 将 $\mathbf k$ 映到 $-\mathbf k$；满足 $\mathbf K\equiv-\mathbf K$ modulo reciprocal vector 的点共有八个：

$$
\mathbf K_{n_1n_2n_3}
=\frac12\left(n_1\mathbf b_1+n_2\mathbf b_2+n_3\mathbf b_3\right),
\qquad n_i\in\{0,1\}.
$$

每个固定 $k_i=0$ 或 $k_i=\pi$ 的 time-reversal-invariant plane 都可视为一个二维 BZ，并在该二维切片保持绝缘时赋予二维 $\mathbb Z_2$ 指数。六个 plane indices 不是独立的：相对的两个平面之间若指数不同，连续改变 $k_i$ 时必然经历拓扑切片变化。三维分类最终只需四个 bit，记为

$$
(\nu_0;\nu_1\nu_2\nu_3),
\qquad \nu_i\in\{0,1\}.
$$

$\nu_0$ 衡量三维体相是否具有不能由简单层堆叠消除的 strong obstruction；后三个 bit 记录 weak stacking vector 的模 2 分量。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Let the reciprocal primitive vectors be $\mathbf b_1,\mathbf b_2,\mathbf b_3$. Spinful time reversal maps $\mathbf k$ to $-\mathbf k$. There are eight momenta satisfying $\mathbf K\equiv-\mathbf K$ modulo a reciprocal lattice vector:

$$
\mathbf K_{n_1n_2n_3}
=\frac12\left(n_1\mathbf b_1+n_2\mathbf b_2+n_3\mathbf b_3\right),
\qquad n_i\in\{0,1\}.
$$

Each time-reversal-invariant plane with fixed $k_i=0$ or $k_i=\pi$ can be regarded as a two-dimensional BZ and assigned a two-dimensional $\mathbb Z_2$ index when that slice is insulating. The six plane indices are not independent. If two opposite planes carry different indices, a topological change of the slices must occur as $k_i$ is varied between them. The three-dimensional classification therefore requires four bits,

$$
(\nu_0;\nu_1\nu_2\nu_3),
\qquad \nu_i\in\{0,1\}.
$$

$\nu_0$ measures the strong three-dimensional obstruction that cannot be removed by interpreting the crystal as a simple stack of layers. The remaining three bits record the mod-two components of a weak stacking vector.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<span id="eight-trim" class="chapter-anchor" aria-hidden="true"></span>

<DerivationBlock
  title="inversion-symmetric 体系的八点 parity formula | Eight-TRIM parity formula for an inversion-symmetric system"
  assumptions={[
    'spinful time reversal 与 inversion 同时保持；',
    '目标 Fermi-level gap 在完整三维 BZ 上开放；',
    '每个 δ(n1n2n3) 包含目标 gap 以下全部 occupied Kramers pairs。',
  ]}
>

在每个 TRIM，occupied Kramers pair 的两个成员具有相同 inversion eigenvalue。定义

$$
\delta_{n_1n_2n_3}
=\prod_{m=1}^{N_{\rm occ}/2}
\xi_m\!\left(\mathbf K_{n_1n_2n_3}\right),
\qquad \xi_m=\pm1.
$$

八点总乘积给出 strong index：

$$
\boxed{(-1)^{\nu_0}
=\prod_{n_1,n_2,n_3=0,1}
\delta_{n_1n_2n_3}}.
$$

三个 weak indices 分别由 $n_i=1$ 的四点平面给出：

$$
\boxed{(-1)^{\nu_i}
=\prod_{n_i=1\atop n_{j\neq i}=0,1}
\delta_{n_1n_2n_3}},
\qquad i=1,2,3.
$$

例如只有 $\delta_{000}$ 为负时，八点总乘积为负而三个 $n_i=1$ 平面乘积均为正，因此

$$
(\nu_0;\nu_1\nu_2\nu_3)=(1;000).
$$

若两个负 parity products 位于同一个切片安排中，总乘积可恢复为正而一个或多个 weak-plane products 为负，得到 weak phase。任何 index 的改变都要求目标 bulk gap 在参数路径某处闭合，或相关保护对称性失效。

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="parity formula 的输入不是几条 frontier bands" titleEn="The parity formula does not take only a few frontier bands" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

$\delta$ 必须对目标 gap 以下全部 occupied Kramers pairs 求积。只追踪靠近 Fermi level 的两条或四条“反转带”可用于解释相变机制，但不能替代完整 invariant。尤其当深能级 bands 在不同 TRIM 具有非平凡 parity pattern 时，截断乘积会给出错误 bit。若体系没有 inversion，parity eigenvalues 不存在，应改用三维 Wilson-loop/hybrid-Wannier flow、sewing matrix 或等价 gauge-consistent 算法。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Each $\delta$ must multiply every occupied Kramers pair below the target gap. Tracking two or four “inverted bands” near the Fermi level may explain a transition mechanism, but it cannot replace the complete invariant. Deep occupied bands can carry a nontrivial parity pattern across the TRIM, so truncating the product can produce the wrong bits. Without inversion, parity eigenvalues do not exist; use a three-dimensional Wilson-loop or hybrid-Wannier flow, a sewing-matrix construction, or an equivalent gauge-consistent algorithm.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

<span id="weak-stacking" class="chapter-anchor" aria-hidden="true"></span>

<BilingualSection titleZh="weak indices 与层堆叠解释" titleEn="Weak indices and the stacked-layer interpretation" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

当 $\nu_0=0$ 而 $(\nu_1\nu_2\nu_3)\neq(000)$，体相属于 weak topological insulator。定义 weak reciprocal vector

$$
\mathbf G_\nu
=\frac12\left(\nu_1\mathbf b_1+\nu_2\mathbf b_2+\nu_3\mathbf b_3\right).
$$

它给出二维 $\nu=1$ layers 的模 2 stacking orientation。若理想晶体沿与堆叠方向兼容的表面截断，每层的 helical edge channels 可耦合成表面 bands，通常出现 even number of surface Dirac cones。因为数目为 even，允许不同 cones 在打破适当 lattice translation 的无序或表面重构下相互散射并开隙。

weak index 因而不是“较小的 strong index”。它记录依赖 crystalline translation 的层状拓扑结构。某些与堆叠垂直的表面可以没有受保护的 gapless cone；另一些侧表面在理想平移对称条件下具有表面谱。具体位置与数目还依赖 termination 和 surface BZ projection。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

When $\nu_0=0$ and $(\nu_1\nu_2\nu_3)\neq(000)$, the bulk is a weak topological insulator. Define the weak reciprocal vector

$$
\mathbf G_\nu
=\frac12\left(\nu_1\mathbf b_1+\nu_2\mathbf b_2+\nu_3\mathbf b_3\right).
$$

It gives the mod-two stacking orientation of two-dimensional $\nu=1$ layers. On an ideal surface compatible with the stacking direction, the helical edge channels of the layers can couple into surface bands, typically producing an even number of surface Dirac cones. Because the number is even, distinct cones can scatter into one another and gap when suitable lattice translation is broken by disorder or surface reconstruction.

A weak index is therefore not a “smaller strong index.” It records a layered topological structure that relies on crystalline translation. Surfaces perpendicular to the stacking direction can lack protected gapless cones, whereas ideal side surfaces can carry surface spectra. Their detailed locations and multiplicities additionally depend on the termination and surface-BZ projection.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="为什么 weak layers 的表面 crossing 通常成偶数 | Why weak-layer surface crossings usually occur in even number"
  assumptions={[
    '每个 isolated 2D layer 具有一对 helical edge branches；',
    '层间耦合保持 time reversal 与沿表面的 translation；',
    '表面 unit cell 与 bulk stacking period 一致。',
  ]}
>

将层编号记为 $j$，每层边缘低能模型写成

$$
H_0=\sum_j\int dk_\parallel\,
\psi_j^\dagger(k_\parallel)
\,v k_\parallel s_z\,
\psi_j(k_\parallel).
$$

沿堆叠方向 Fourier transform，$\psi_j\sim e^{ik_\perp j}$，允许的相邻层耦合产生周期函数 $t(k_\perp)$。表面谱成为

$$
E_\pm(k_\parallel,k_\perp)
=\pm\sqrt{v^2k_\parallel^2+|t(k_\perp)|^2}.
$$

time reversal 将 $(k_\parallel,k_\perp)$ 映到 $(-k_\parallel,-k_\perp)$。在表面 TRIM，Kramers pairing 强制 crossing 或配对重连；一个 periodic $t(k_\perp)$ 在完整 surface BZ 中的零点按 lattice doubling 约束成偶数类。若表面 translation 被加倍或破坏，原本分离的 cones 可折叠到同一动量并由 time-reversal-even intercone mass 开隙。

该推导是层模型的机制解释，不决定任意材料表面的 cone 数目。

</DerivationBlock>

<span id="strong-surface" class="chapter-anchor" aria-hidden="true"></span>

<BilingualSection titleZh="strong index 与 odd surface-Dirac parity" titleEn="The strong index and odd surface-Dirac parity" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

$\nu_0=1$ 时，三维 occupied manifold 不能在保持 time reversal 和 bulk gap 的条件下连续变成相互独立的二维层。对 noninteracting、time-reversal-preserving、未发生对称性破缺的理想表面，surface bands 在 bulk projected gap 内连接 valence 与 conduction manifolds 的次数为 odd modulo 2。常见最简情形是一个 surface Dirac cone。

单个表面 Dirac Hamiltonian 可写成

$$
H_{\rm surf}(\mathbf k_\parallel)
=v\left(k_xs_y-k_ys_x\right),
$$

其能谱为 $E_\pm=\pm v|\mathbf k_\parallel|$，并具有 momentum-locked spin expectation。time reversal 禁止单个 cone 的常数 mass $m s_z$，因为 $s_z\mapsto-s_z$。表面若显式破坏 time reversal，可允许该 mass 并开隙；强拓扑 bulk 本身没有因此消失，但 boundary response 改变。

三维晶体的有限 slab 有两个表面。一个表面上的 odd cone 与另一表面的 partner 共同满足完整二维 lattice spectrum 的 doubling 要求。薄 slab 中相反表面还可通过有限厚度 hybridization 开小 gap，因此“计算到一个小 gap”必须与 slab-thickness convergence 分开判断。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For $\nu_0=1$, the three-dimensional occupied manifold cannot be continuously deformed into independent two-dimensional layers while preserving time reversal and the bulk gap. On an ideal noninteracting surface that preserves time reversal and does not spontaneously break the symmetry, the number of surface-band connections between projected valence and conduction manifolds is odd modulo two. The simplest common realization is one surface Dirac cone.

A single surface Dirac Hamiltonian can be written

$$
H_{\rm surf}(\mathbf k_\parallel)
=v\left(k_xs_y-k_ys_x\right),
$$

with spectrum $E_\pm=\pm v|\mathbf k_\parallel|$ and momentum-locked spin expectation. Time reversal forbids a constant single-cone mass $m s_z$, because $s_z\mapsto-s_z$. Explicitly breaking time reversal at the surface permits this mass and opens a gap. The strong topological bulk need not disappear, but the boundary response changes.

A finite slab has two surfaces. The odd cone on one surface is accompanied by its partner on the opposite surface, satisfying the doubling requirement of the complete two-dimensional lattice spectrum. Opposite surfaces can also hybridize in a thin slab and open a small finite-thickness gap, so observing a small calculated gap must be separated from slab-thickness convergence.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="surface Dirac cone 与 bulk Dirac node 必须分开" titleEn="Keep a surface Dirac cone distinct from a bulk Dirac node" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

strong topological insulator 的 Dirac cone 是二维 boundary spectrum，位于三维 bulk projected gap 中；bulk 仍是绝缘的。Dirac semimetal 的 Dirac node 则是三维 bulk bands 的四重交叉，意味着 bulk gap 闭合，并依赖额外 crystal symmetry 或 fine tuning。两者都可呈线性色散，但维度、简并、保护机制、可定义的 invariant 与实验投影完全不同。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The Dirac cone of a strong topological insulator is a two-dimensional boundary spectrum inside a projected three-dimensional bulk gap; the bulk remains insulating. A Dirac-semimetal node is instead a fourfold crossing of three-dimensional bulk bands, so the bulk gap is closed and additional crystal symmetry or fine tuning is required. Both can look linear, but their dimensionality, degeneracy, protecting mechanism, applicable invariant, and experimental projection are different.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>
