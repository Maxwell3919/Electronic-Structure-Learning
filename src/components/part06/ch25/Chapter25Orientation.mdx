## 阅读定位 | Reading Orientation

<BilingualSection titleZh="本章处理的问题" titleEn="The problem addressed in this chapter" eyebrow="Orientation">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

电子能带的能量 $E_n(\mathbf k)$ 只给出谱。拓扑分类还需要 Bloch 本征矢 $|u_{n\mathbf k}\rangle$ 或由它们张成的占据子空间随 $\mathbf k$ 的连续变化。本章建立这套语言，并说明何时可以把一个整数或 $\mathbb Z_2$ 标签与体能带联系起来，何时必须转向边界谱、Wannier 表示、对称性表示或 Bogoliubov–de Gennes 描述。

前置内容是 Chapter 23 的 Bloch–Wannier 变换和 Chapter 24 的 Berry-phase 极化。Appendix P 提供 Berry 相位与 Chern 数的通用数学，Appendix Q 给出量子 Hall 效应的体—边先例。Chapter 26 将把这里的概念落实到显式 $2\times2$ Hamiltonian；Chapter 27 和 28 分别处理二维与三维拓扑绝缘体。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The band energies $E_n(\mathbf k)$ specify only the spectrum. A topological classification also depends on how the Bloch eigenvectors $|u_{n\mathbf k}\rangle$, or the occupied subspace that they span, vary continuously with $\mathbf k$. This chapter builds that language and states when an integer or $\mathbb Z_2$ label can be attached to the bulk bands, and when the analysis must instead involve a boundary spectrum, a Wannier representation, symmetry representations, or a Bogoliubov–de Gennes description.

The prerequisites are the Bloch–Wannier transformation of Chapter 23 and the Berry-phase theory of polarization in Chapter 24. Appendix P develops the general mathematics of Berry phases and Chern numbers, while Appendix Q supplies the quantum Hall example of a bulk–edge relation. Chapter 26 will implement the present concepts in explicit $2\times2$ Hamiltonians; Chapters 27 and 28 treat two- and three-dimensional topological insulators.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## 数学对象与统一约定 | Mathematical Objects and Conventions

<BilingualSection titleZh="从 Hamiltonian 到占据投影算符" titleEn="From the Hamiltonian to the occupied projector" eyebrow="Conventions">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

周期体系写成 $H(\mathbf k)|u_{n\mathbf k}\rangle=E_n(\mathbf k)|u_{n\mathbf k}\rangle$。完整 Bloch 波函数为 $|\psi_{n\mathbf k}\rangle=e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf r}|u_{n\mathbf k}\rangle$；$|u_{n\mathbf k}\rangle$ 在实空间具有晶格周期性。本站默认在一个原胞内归一化，并把 $\mathbf k$ 与 $\mathbf k+\mathbf G$ 识别为同一晶体动量。

对于单条非简并带，$|u_{n\mathbf k}\rangle$ 可乘任意平滑相位。对于由多条占据带组成的流形，更一般的自由度是占据子空间内部的 $\mathbf k$ 依赖幺正旋转。单个本征矢会变化，而投影算符

$$
P(\mathbf k)=\sum_{n\in\mathrm{occ}}|u_{n\mathbf k}\rangle\langle u_{n\mathbf k}|
$$

不随这种基变换改变。存在简并或带交叉时，分类对象通常应写成 $P(\mathbf k)$ 或非 Abelian connection，而不是强行追踪某一条编号带。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A periodic system is written as $H(\mathbf k)|u_{n\mathbf k}\rangle=E_n(\mathbf k)|u_{n\mathbf k}\rangle$. The full Bloch state is $|\psi_{n\mathbf k}\rangle=e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf r}|u_{n\mathbf k}\rangle$, with $|u_{n\mathbf k}\rangle$ periodic in real space. We use unit-cell normalization and identify $\mathbf k$ and $\mathbf k+\mathbf G$ as the same crystal momentum.

For one nondegenerate band, $|u_{n\mathbf k}\rangle$ may be multiplied by an arbitrary smooth phase. For a manifold of several occupied bands, the more general freedom is a $\mathbf k$-dependent unitary rotation within the occupied subspace. Individual eigenvectors change, whereas the projector

$$
P(\mathbf k)=\sum_{n\in\mathrm{occ}}|u_{n\mathbf k}\rangle\langle u_{n\mathbf k}|
$$

is unchanged by this basis transformation. In the presence of degeneracies or band crossings, the appropriate object is usually $P(\mathbf k)$ or a non-Abelian connection rather than a forced tracking of one numbered band.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

| Object | Definition or role | Gauge status | Minimum condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| $E_n(\mathbf k)$ | energy eigenvalue | invariant | Hamiltonian specified |
| $\lvert u_{n\mathbf k}\rangle$ | cell-periodic Bloch eigenvector | phase/basis dependent | isolated state or declared basis in a degenerate subspace |
| $P(\mathbf k)$ | occupied-subspace projector | invariant under occupied-unitary rotations | occupied manifold separated from the target complement |
| $\mathbf A_n(\mathbf k)$ | Berry connection | gauge dependent | differentiable local gauge |
| $\mathbf\Omega_n(\mathbf k)$ | Berry curvature | single-band gauge invariant | isolated nondegenerate band locally |
| $\gamma[C]$ | Berry phase around a directed loop | invariant modulo $2\pi$ | closed loop and compatible endpoint gauge |
| $C$ | Chern number | integer topological invariant | gapped manifold over a closed 2D parameter space |
| boundary spectrum | eigenvalues or spectral function for a stated boundary | termination and finite-size dependent | boundary geometry and projection specified |

<BilingualCallout titleZh="能隙条件的精确含义" titleEn="Precise meaning of the gap condition" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

绝缘体的间接带隙为负并不总能阻止某个目标流形在每个 $\mathbf k$ 上与其余能带直接分离；反之，费米能附近出现全局载流子也会使“所有占据态”随 $\mathbf k$ 的维数变化。每个不变量必须声明它使用的是全占据流形、一个直接分离的复合带流形，还是包围节点的闭合曲面。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A negative indirect gap does not necessarily prevent a chosen band manifold from being directly separated from all other bands at every $\mathbf k$. Conversely, a Fermi surface can make the dimension of the globally occupied space vary with $\mathbf k$. Every invariant must therefore state whether it uses the full occupied manifold, a directly isolated composite band manifold, or a closed surface enclosing a node.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>
