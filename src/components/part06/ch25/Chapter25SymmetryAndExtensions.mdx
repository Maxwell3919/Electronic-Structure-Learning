## 25.6 时间反演对称性与电子体系拓扑 | Time-Reversal Symmetry and Electronic Topology

<BilingualSection titleZh="反幺正约束与 Kramers 配对" titleEn="Antiunitary constraint and Kramers pairing" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

时间反演算符 $\Theta$ 是反幺正的，并满足

$$
\Theta H(\mathbf k)\Theta^{-1}=H(-\mathbf k).
$$

无自旋或整数自旋有效模型通常有 $\Theta^2=+1$；自旋 $1/2$ 电子有 $\Theta^2=-1$。在时间反演不变动量（TRIM）$\boldsymbol\Lambda=-\boldsymbol\Lambda+\mathbf G$，后者保证 $|u\rangle$ 与 $\Theta|u\rangle$ 正交且等能，形成 Kramers 对。

单条非简并带的 Berry 曲率在时间反演下满足 $\Omega_n(\mathbf k)=-\Omega_{\bar n}(-\mathbf k)$。对完整 Kramers 占据流形积分，普通总 Chern 数为零。然而“总 Chern 数为零”只排除净手性；它不能把所有时间反演对称绝缘体压缩成一个平庸类别。Kramers 对在半个 BZ 上如何交换产生模 2 的分类，Chapter 27 将给出具体构造。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The time-reversal operator $\Theta$ is antiunitary and satisfies

$$
\Theta H(\mathbf k)\Theta^{-1}=H(-\mathbf k).
$$

Spinless or integer-spin effective models usually have $\Theta^2=+1$, whereas spin-$1/2$ electrons have $\Theta^2=-1$. At a time-reversal-invariant momentum (TRIM), $\boldsymbol\Lambda=-\boldsymbol\Lambda+\mathbf G$, the latter condition makes $|u\rangle$ and $\Theta|u\rangle$ orthogonal and degenerate: a Kramers pair.

The Berry curvature of a nondegenerate band obeys $\Omega_n(\mathbf k)=-\Omega_{\bar n}(-\mathbf k)$. Integrating a complete occupied Kramers manifold gives zero ordinary total Chern number. Zero total Chern number, however, only excludes net chirality; it does not collapse all time-reversal-symmetric insulators into one trivial class. A mod-two classification records how Kramers partners switch across half of the Brillouin zone, as Chapter 27 will construct explicitly.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="Kramers 正交性 | Kramers orthogonality"
  assumptions={[
    'Θ 为反幺正算符；',
    'Θ²=-1；',
    'k 为 TRIM，使 Θ|u_k⟩ 与 |u_k⟩ 属于同一 Hamiltonian。',
  ]}
>

反幺正性给出 $\langle\Theta a|\Theta b\rangle=\langle b|a\rangle$。令 $a=u$、$b=\Theta u$：

$$
\langle\Theta u|\Theta^2 u\rangle
=\langle\Theta u|u\rangle.
$$

使用 $\Theta^2=-1$，左侧为 $-\langle\Theta u|u\rangle$，因此

$$
\langle\Theta u|u\rangle=0.
$$

两态正交。又因 $[H(\boldsymbol\Lambda),\Theta]=0$，它们具有同一能量。该结论要求 spinful 时间反演和 TRIM；一般 $\mathbf k$ 点的伙伴位于 $-\mathbf k$，不必在同一 $\mathbf k$ 简并。

The degeneracy is enforced at a TRIM for spinful time reversal. Away from a TRIM, the partner state lies at $-\mathbf k$ and need not be degenerate at the same momentum.

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="空间反演判据的适用边界" titleEn="Scope of inversion-based criteria" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

若同时具有空间反演，Fu–Kane parity 方法可用 TRIM 处占据 Kramers 对的反演本征值计算 $\mathbb Z_2$ 指标。该方法依赖反演对称性、正确占据流形和自旋时间反演；无反演、磁性、非共线序或数值对称性破坏时不能直接套用。它是 Chapter 27–28 的外部计算补充，不替代本章的一般 Berry/投影算符语言。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

With inversion symmetry, the Fu–Kane parity construction evaluates $\mathbb Z_2$ indices from inversion eigenvalues of occupied Kramers pairs at the TRIM. It requires inversion symmetry, the correct occupied manifold, and spinful time reversal. It cannot be applied unchanged without inversion, in a magnetic or noncollinear state, or after numerical symmetry breaking. It is a computational supplement for Chapters 27–28, not a replacement for the general Berry/projector language developed here.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 25.7 表面态及其与量子 Hall 效应的关系 | Surface States and the Relation to the Quantum Hall Effect

<BilingualSection titleZh="Chern 边界与时间反演边界的不同" titleEn="Chern and time-reversal boundaries are distinct" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

量子 Hall 或 Chern 绝缘体破坏时间反演，非零 Chern 数给出净手性边缘通道。填满的二维占据流形在理想条件下满足 $\sigma_{xy}=Ce^2/h$。边缘态的净传播方向不能被保持体隙的弱扰动消除，因为没有同边界上的反向通道可与全部净手性配对。

二维时间反演拓扑绝缘体的总 Chern 数为零，边缘由 Kramers 相关的反向传播伙伴组成。其保护依赖时间反演和费米能处的奇数 Kramers 对结构；自旋不守恒时，不能把它严格写成两个彼此独立的整数 Chern 体系。三维情形将在 Chapter 28 以表面 Dirac 谱讨论。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A quantum Hall or Chern insulator breaks time reversal, and a nonzero Chern number produces net chiral edge channels. For a filled two-dimensional occupied manifold under the appropriate conditions, $\sigma_{xy}=Ce^2/h$. The net propagation direction cannot be removed by a weak perturbation that preserves the bulk gap, because no complete set of counterpropagating channels exists on the same edge to cancel the net chirality.

A two-dimensional time-reversal topological insulator has zero total Chern number and an edge made of counterpropagating Kramers-related partners. Its protection depends on time reversal and an odd Kramers-pair structure at the Fermi energy. When spin is not conserved, the system cannot be treated exactly as two independent integer-Chern copies. Chapter 28 will discuss the three-dimensional analogue in terms of a surface Dirac spectrum.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="“无耗散”需要输运条件" titleEn="“Dissipationless” requires transport conditions" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

拓扑边缘谱不自动给出任意器件中的零纵向电阻。接触、非弹性散射、体载流子、额外边缘通道、磁性扰动和有限温度都进入实验输运。拓扑论证约束可允许的弹性回散射和通道奇偶性；具体电阻仍是边界条件与非平衡输运问题。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A topological edge spectrum does not automatically imply zero longitudinal resistance in an arbitrary device. Contacts, inelastic scattering, bulk carriers, extra edge channels, magnetic perturbations, and finite temperature all enter transport. Topology constrains allowed elastic backscattering and channel parity; the measured resistance remains a boundary-condition and nonequilibrium-transport problem.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 25.8 Wannier 函数与拓扑 | Wannier Functions and Topology

<BilingualSection titleZh="局域化与平滑 Bloch 规范" titleEn="Localization and a smooth Bloch gauge" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Wannier 函数由 Bloch 态在 BZ 上 Fourier 变换得到。指数局域要求能够为目标复合带流形选择足够平滑、周期的 Bloch frame。非零 Chern 数阻止这种全局 frame，因此 Chern 带不能同时具有保持该流形的指数局域正交 Wannier 表示。

时间反演 $\mathbb Z_2$ 拓扑绝缘体的总 Chern 数为零，可以构造指数局域 Wannier 函数；障碍在于不能同时让所选 Wannier frame 按时间反演组成全局平滑的 Kramers 配对。必须区分“存在局域 Wannier 函数”和“存在保持全部目标对称性的原子极限表示”。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Wannier functions are Fourier transforms of Bloch states over the Brillouin zone. Exponential localization requires a sufficiently smooth periodic Bloch frame for the chosen composite band manifold. A nonzero Chern number obstructs such a global frame, so a Chern band cannot have an exponentially localized orthonormal Wannier representation that remains confined to that manifold.

A time-reversal $\mathbb Z_2$ topological insulator has zero total Chern number and can admit exponentially localized Wannier functions. The obstruction is instead to choosing a globally smooth Wannier frame that forms time-reversal-symmetric Kramers pairs. One must distinguish the existence of localized Wannier functions from the existence of an atomic-limit representation preserving all target symmetries.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="Wannierization 的证据边界" titleEn="Evidence boundary of Wannierization" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

软件成功最小化 spread 或重现高对称路径能带，只证明算法得到一个插值模型。拓扑用途还需检查目标能窗、disentanglement、投影选择、整个 BZ 的插值误差、对称性表示、节点或能隙，以及直接计算与 Wannier 模型的不变量一致性。改变能窗可能改变目标流形，不能把一次成功运行当作模型权威。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Successful spread minimization or agreement along a high-symmetry band path only establishes that an interpolation model was produced. Topological use additionally requires checks of the target windows, disentanglement, projections, full-zone interpolation error, symmetry representations, nodes or gaps, and agreement of invariants between direct states and the Wannier model. Changing the windows may change the target manifold, so one successful run is not model authority.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 25.9 拓扑量子化学 | Topological Quantum Chemistry

<BilingualSection titleZh="从原子极限到能带表示" titleEn="From atomic limits to band representations" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 + 外部权威来源 | Martin source + external primary source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

拓扑量子化学从实空间局域轨道的 site-symmetry 表示出发，将其诱导为空间群的能带表示。若一组隔离占据带能够写成满足目标对称性的原子轨道诱导表示，它与某个原子极限相容；若对称性本征值、连接关系或表示分解与任何允许原子极限不相容，则得到拓扑或受阻原子极限的诊断。

这种方法把化学轨道、Wyckoff 位置和动量空间表示连接起来，适合大规模筛选。但 symmetry indicator 通常是商群信息：非零指标能够诊断某些拓扑，零指标不保证平庸；同一指标也可能对应不同拓扑机制。磁性、无序、强关联、非对称终止和超出输入空间群的物理都需要额外处理。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Topological quantum chemistry starts from site-symmetry representations of localized real-space orbitals and induces them to band representations of the space group. If an isolated occupied set can be expressed as an atomic-orbital-induced representation while preserving the target symmetries, it is compatible with an atomic limit. If its symmetry eigenvalues, connectivity, or representation decomposition is incompatible with every allowed atomic limit, the mismatch diagnoses topology or an obstructed atomic limit.

This framework connects chemical orbitals, Wyckoff positions, and momentum-space representations and is well suited to large-scale screening. A symmetry indicator is generally quotient information: a nonzero indicator diagnoses certain topology, but a zero indicator does not prove triviality, and one indicator value may correspond to different mechanisms. Magnetism, disorder, strong correlation, nonsymmetric terminations, and physics outside the input space group require additional treatment.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## 25.10 Majorana 模式 | Majorana Modes

<BilingualSection titleZh="普通绝缘体能带之外的 Nambu 空间" titleEn="Nambu space beyond ordinary insulating bands" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 + 外部权威来源 | Martin source + external primary source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Majorana 准粒子属于超导 Bogoliubov–de Gennes（BdG）问题。引入 Nambu spinor $\Psi=(c,c^\dagger)^T$ 后，Hamiltonian 同时包含正常态块和配对势：

$$
H_{\mathrm{BdG}}(\mathbf k)=
\begin{pmatrix}
h(\mathbf k)-\mu & \Delta(\mathbf k)\\
\Delta^\dagger(\mathbf k) & -h^T(-\mathbf k)+\mu
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

BdG 描述具有由 Nambu 冗余产生的粒子—空穴约束 $\Xi H_{\mathrm{BdG}}(\mathbf k)\Xi^{-1}=-H_{\mathrm{BdG}}(-\mathbf k)$。零能准粒子若满足 $\gamma^\dagger=\gamma$，可称为 Majorana 模式。它需要超导能隙、适用的拓扑分类和边界或缺陷；普通拓扑绝缘体的能带反转或表面 Dirac cone 本身不能推出 Majorana。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Majorana quasiparticles belong to a superconducting Bogoliubov–de Gennes (BdG) problem. In a Nambu spinor $\Psi=(c,c^\dagger)^T$, the Hamiltonian contains both the normal-state block and the pairing potential:

$$
H_{\mathrm{BdG}}(\mathbf k)=
\begin{pmatrix}
h(\mathbf k)-\mu & \Delta(\mathbf k)\\
\Delta^\dagger(\mathbf k) & -h^T(-\mathbf k)+\mu
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The BdG description has a particle–hole constraint from Nambu redundancy, $\Xi H_{\mathrm{BdG}}(\mathbf k)\Xi^{-1}=-H_{\mathrm{BdG}}(-\mathbf k)$. A zero-energy quasiparticle satisfying $\gamma^\dagger=\gamma$ is a Majorana mode. It requires a superconducting gap, an applicable topological class, and a boundary or defect. Band inversion or a surface Dirac cone of an ordinary topological insulator does not by itself imply a Majorana mode.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="零能 BdG 模式的自共轭条件 | Self-conjugacy of a zero-energy BdG mode"
  assumptions={[
    'BdG Hamiltonian has particle–hole symmetry Xi;',
    'the zero-energy subspace is isolated from finite-energy states;',
    'a basis can be chosen within the zero-mode subspace. ',
  ]}
>

若 $H_{\mathrm{BdG}}|\Phi_E\rangle=E|\Phi_E\rangle$，则

$$
H_{\mathrm{BdG}}\Xi|\Phi_E\rangle=-E\,\Xi|\Phi_E\rangle.
$$

粒子—空穴对称把 $E$ 与 $-E$ 配对。仅在 $E=0$ 时，伙伴仍位于同一能量子空间；可在满足相应平方关系的情形选择 $\Xi|\Phi_0\rangle=|\Phi_0\rangle$。对应准粒子算符

$$
\gamma=\sum_j\left(u_jc_j+u_j^*c_j^\dagger\right)
$$

满足 $\gamma^\dagger=\gamma$。粒子—空穴对称只保证谱成对；是否存在受保护零模仍需体拓扑、边界/缺陷和有限尺寸分裂检查。

Particle–hole symmetry pairs the spectrum at $E$ and $-E$. Self-conjugacy can be imposed only within a zero-energy subspace. The symmetry alone does not guarantee a protected zero mode; the bulk class, defect or boundary, and finite-size splitting must still be established.

</DerivationBlock>
