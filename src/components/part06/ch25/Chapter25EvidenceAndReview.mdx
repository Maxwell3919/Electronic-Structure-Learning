<BilingualSection titleZh="连接实际电子结构计算" titleEn="Connection to practical electronic-structure calculations" eyebrow="Calculation boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

真实材料的最低流程是：核验晶体结构与磁序；对 SOC、泛函、$U$ 和基组进行受控计算；确认目标直接能隙或节点流形；提取占据 Bloch 态、对称性表示或经验证的 Wannier 模型；在全 BZ、TR-invariant planes 或包围曲面上计算不变量；最后以收敛 slab 或半无限 Green function 检查边界谱。

各门槛必须分开记录。程序正常结束不等于 SCF 收敛；SCF 收敛不等于带隙或不变量对 k 网格收敛；Wannier 插值吻合不等于保持对称性和拓扑；边界谱出现一条线不等于 bulk invariant 已得到支持。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A minimal material workflow is: verify the crystal structure and magnetic order; perform controlled calculations over SOC, functional, $U$, and basis choices; establish the target direct gap or nodal manifold; extract occupied Bloch states, symmetry representations, or a validated Wannier model; evaluate the invariant over the full Brillouin zone, time-reversal-invariant planes, or an enclosing surface; and finally test the boundary spectrum with a converged slab or semi-infinite Green function.

Each gate must be recorded separately. Program completion is not SCF convergence; SCF convergence is not convergence of a gap or invariant with respect to the k mesh; band interpolation is not preservation of symmetry and topology; and one line in a boundary spectrum is not evidence that the bulk invariant has been established.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## 常见误判、原创练习与来源边界 | Misjudgments, Original Exercises, and Source Boundaries

<BilingualCallout titleZh="高影响误判" titleEn="High-impact misjudgments" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <ul>
      <li><strong>能带反转：</strong>是候选机制，不是 invariant。</li>
      <li><strong>SOC 开隙：</strong>说明相对论项改变谱，不自动给出 $\mathbb Z_2$。</li>
      <li><strong>局部 Berry curvature：</strong>是积分密度，不是全 BZ Chern 数。</li>
      <li><strong>隙内边界态：</strong>可能是 Tamm、Shockley、共振或有限尺寸态。</li>
      <li><strong>parity product：</strong>只在满足空间反演和时间反演等前提时适用。</li>
      <li><strong>高对称路径交叉：</strong>不能代替 3D 节点搜索与拓扑电荷。</li>
      <li><strong>成功 Wannierization：</strong>不能代替流形、对称性与 invariant 核验。</li>
      <li><strong>普通 TI 能带：</strong>不能直接推出 Majorana；必须进入 BdG 超导问题。</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Band inversion:</strong> a candidate mechanism, not an invariant.</li>
      <li><strong>An SOC gap:</strong> evidence that relativistic terms modify the spectrum, not an automatic $\mathbb Z_2$ result.</li>
      <li><strong>Local Berry curvature:</strong> an integrand, not the full-zone Chern number.</li>
      <li><strong>An in-gap boundary state:</strong> possibly Tamm, Shockley, resonant, or finite-size in origin.</li>
      <li><strong>A parity product:</strong> applicable only with the required inversion and time-reversal assumptions.</li>
      <li><strong>A high-symmetry-path crossing:</strong> not a substitute for a three-dimensional node search and topological charge.</li>
      <li><strong>Successful Wannierization:</strong> not a substitute for manifold, symmetry, and invariant validation.</li>
      <li><strong>An ordinary TI band structure:</strong> not a Majorana result; the superconducting BdG problem is essential.</li>
    </ul>

### 原创练习 | Original Exercises

1. **规范变换。** 从归一化条件推导 $\langle u|\partial_k u\rangle$ 为纯虚数，再完整推导 $A\to A-\partial_k\phi$。说明开放路径与闭合路径的差别。
2. **离散 Berry 相位。** 为五个归一化复向量构造闭合重叠乘积。分别给每个向量乘随机相位，验证乘积相位不变；再令一个相邻重叠趋近零，解释数值失效机制。
3. **闭隙判定。** 对可视化 A 的 Hamiltonian 求 $E_\pm(k)$，解析确定所有闭隙点，并说明为什么只绘制 $k=0$ 会漏掉其中一个转变。
4. **Chern 网格。** 在 $m=-1$ 下计算 $N=11,21,41,61$；记录 raw flux、整数和最小 link overlap。给每个网格点施加确定性相位，比较结果。
5. **证据分级。** 给定“SOC 打开 40 meV 能隙、$\Gamma$ 点发生轨道交换、slab 出现一条隙内线”，列出仍缺少的 bulk、symmetry、invariant 和 boundary 证据。
6. **时间反演。** 证明 spinful TRIM 处的 Kramers 正交性；指出该证明在哪一步使用 $\Theta^2=-1$ 和 $\mathbf k=-\mathbf k+\mathbf G$。
7. **Wannier 障碍。** 比较 Chern 带与时间反演 $\mathbb Z_2$ 带的 Wannier 陈述：分别判断“局域 Wannier 存在”和“对称 Kramers frame 存在”。
8. **Majorana 边界。** 对一个普通零能 bound state 与 BdG Majorana 候选，列出区分它们所需的粒子—空穴关系、空间局域、有限尺寸分裂和扰动响应。
