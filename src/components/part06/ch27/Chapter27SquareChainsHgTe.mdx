## 27.5 方格上的 topological-insulator 实例 | Example of a Topological Insulator on a Square Lattice

<BilingualSection titleZh="从 diagonal block picture 回到完整原子 SOC" titleEn="Returning from the diagonal block picture to full atomic SOC" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Martin 在方格上使用每格点一个 $s$ 与三个 $p$ 轨道，并保留完整三维 $\zeta\mathbf L\cdot\mathbf S$。$p_z$ 在位能被设得较高但保持有限，因此低能上近似为 $sp^2$，同时通过虚跃迁保留 diagonal approximation 中缺失的 spin mixing。参数被选择为 $\Gamma$ 点附近 $s/p$ 次序反转，而 zone-boundary TRIM 保持正常次序。

当 $\zeta=0$ 时，$p$ 态在高对称点存在简并，最低两组带之间没有完整 gap。任意非零 SOC 都能打开最低 gap；对 Martin 选用的参数，从小到大的非零 $\zeta$ 中，最低 occupied Kramers pair 与更高带之间保持 $\mathbb Z_2$ odd。strip 中每条边出现一对在 projected TRIM 线性交叉的 counterpropagating states，并把 projected valence 与 conduction manifolds 连接。

上方 gap 的行为不同：中间与最高带在某个 SOC 强度处重新接触，随后按正常次序分离。因而同一 Hamiltonian、同一参数点可以对不同 chemical potential/occupied manifold 给出不同拓扑分类。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Martin uses one $s$ and three $p$ orbitals per site on a square lattice and retains the full three-dimensional interaction $\zeta\mathbf L\cdot\mathbf S$. The $p_z$ on-site level is placed high but kept finite. The low-energy space is therefore approximately $sp^2$, while virtual processes retain the spin mixing absent from the diagonal approximation. Parameters are chosen so that the $s/p$ ordering is inverted near $\Gamma$ but normal at the zone-boundary TRIM.

At $\zeta=0$, the $p$ states remain degenerate at high-symmetry points and there is no complete gap between the two lowest groups. Any nonzero SOC opens the lowest gap. For Martin's parameters, the gap between the lowest occupied Kramers pair and the higher bands remains $\mathbb Z_2$ odd over the displayed nonzero SOC strengths. In a strip, each edge supports one counterpropagating pair crossing linearly at a projected TRIM and connecting the projected valence and conduction manifolds.

The upper gap behaves differently: the middle and highest bands touch again at a particular SOC strength and then separate in normal order. The same Hamiltonian at one parameter point can therefore have different topological classifications for different chemical potentials or occupied manifolds.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="拓扑分类必须绑定目标 gap" titleEn="A topological classification must be tied to a target gap" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

“这个材料/模型是 topological”不是充分精确的陈述。需要指定：目标 Fermi level 位于哪一个 bulk gap；该 gap 以下包含多少 occupied Kramers pairs；这些 bands 是否在完整 BZ 与上方流形分离；不变量针对哪一个 composite occupied subspace。Martin 的方格实例中，最低 gap 可为 odd，而把最低与中间 bands 一起视为 occupied 后，上方 gap 在较大 $\zeta$ 下为 trivial。低能 bands 之间仍可能存在可见 edge states，但它们不跨越当前目标 occupied–unoccupied gap。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

“The material or model is topological” is not sufficiently precise. One must state which bulk gap contains the target Fermi level, how many occupied Kramers pairs lie below it, whether that composite manifold is separated from higher bands over the full Brillouin zone, and which occupied subspace carries the invariant. In Martin's square-lattice example, the lowest gap can be odd, while treating both the lowest and middle bands as occupied makes the upper gap trivial at larger $\zeta$. Visible edge states may remain between lower bands without crossing the selected occupied–unoccupied gap.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

<DerivationBlock
  title="本站补充：BHZ block form 如何保留 full-SOC Z₂ 信息 | Site supplement: how the BHZ block form retains the full-SOC Z₂ information"
  assumptions={[
    '只保留一个 s-like Kramers pair 与一个 p-like Kramers pair；',
    '模型在 Γ 附近展开并用晶格正则化补足 BZ；',
    'time reversal 与 bulk gap 保持。',
  ]}
>

一个常用的四带低能表示写成

$$
H_{\rm BHZ}(\mathbf k)=
\begin{pmatrix}
h(\mathbf k)&0\\
0&h^*(-\mathbf k)
\end{pmatrix},
$$

$$
h(\mathbf k)=\epsilon(\mathbf k)I+A k_x\tau_x-Ak_y\tau_y+M(\mathbf k)\tau_z,
\qquad
M(\mathbf k)=M-Bk^2.
$$

每个 block 的质量 $M(\mathbf k)$ 在 $\Gamma$ 与 large-$k$/zone-boundary regularization 之间是否变号，决定其 Chern parity。两个 blocks 的 signed Chern numbers 相反，总 charge Chern number 为零；若单 block 的 $|C|$ 为奇数，则完整四带模型为 $\nu=1$。加入 time-reversal-preserving block mixing 后，单独 block Chern numbers 不再是精确不变量，但只要 gap 不闭合，$\nu$ 不变。

该 continuum form 用于解释 HgTe/CdTe 等低能流形；它的“square-lattice regularization”不是实际 zinc-blende/diamond crystal lattice 的结构声明。

</DerivationBlock>

### edge spectrum：从 Shockley bands 到 helical connectivity | Edge spectrum: from Shockley bands to helical connectivity

<BilingualSection titleZh="局部 edge dispersion 可变，odd connectivity 不可消除" titleEn="Local edge dispersion can move, but odd connectivity cannot disappear" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

无 SOC 时，strip 的 projected bulk gap 只在部分 edge BZ 存在，终止可产生 quadratic Shockley bands；它们在 projected bulk gap 消失处并入 bulk continuum。非零 SOC 打开完整最低 gap 后，projected TRIM 处出现 Kramers degeneracy，并由相反群速度的两支离开。edge potential、宽度与 termination 可以改变穿越位置和弯曲程度，但 odd pair 必须在半个 edge BZ 内把 lower projected manifold 与 upper projected manifold 连接。

对有限宽 strip，两条相反边缘各有一组 helical bands。若宽度不足，两侧 wavefunctions 可重叠并产生指数小 finite-size gap；这不是 bulk $\mathbb Z_2$ invariant 消失，而是有限几何中两个边界的耦合。需要同时给出 edge localization、width convergence 与 bulk projection。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Without SOC, the projected bulk gap exists only over part of the edge Brillouin zone, and a termination can produce quadratic Shockley bands that merge into the bulk continuum where the projected gap closes. Once nonzero SOC opens a complete lowest gap, Kramers degeneracy appears at a projected TRIM and two branches leave with opposite group velocities. Edge potential, strip width, and termination may change the crossing energy and curvature, but an odd pair must connect the lower and upper projected manifolds over half of the edge Brillouin zone.

A finite strip has one set of helical bands on each opposite edge. If the strip is too narrow, wavefunctions from the two edges can overlap and open an exponentially small finite-size gap. This is coupling between two boundaries in a finite geometry, not the disappearance of the bulk $\mathbb Z_2$ invariant. Edge localization, width convergence, and the projected bulk continuum must be reported together.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## 27.6 从链到平面：拓扑转变实例 | From Chains to Planes: Example of a Topological Transition

<BilingualSection titleZh="interchain hopping 把一维 Shockley state 变成二维 helical edge band" titleEn="Interchain hopping turns a one-dimensional Shockley state into a two-dimensional helical edge band" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Martin 固定较强 full SOC，并把 $y$ 方向、涉及 $s$ 与 $p_y$ 的 hopping 乘以比例 $r$。$r=0$ 是互不耦合的一维 chains；$r=1$ 是各向同性方格。孤立 chain 的端部有 Chapter 26 型 Shockley state。小 $r$ 时，这些端态沿 edge 方向展宽为 bands，除 projected TRIM 外因 SOC 发生 splitting，而在 $k_\parallel=0,\pi/a$ 保持 Kramers degeneracy。

随着 $r$ 增大，interchain dispersion 与 on-site SOC 竞争，bulk gap 缩小。Martin 的特定参数在 $r\approx0.6$–$0.7$ 之间发生 zone-boundary gap closing；此后 gap 重新打开，bulk bands 的连接方式改变，系统进入二维 $\mathbb Z_2$ odd phase。数值比值属于该参数集，不是普适常数。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Martin fixes a relatively strong full SOC and multiplies the $y$-direction hoppings involving $s$ and $p_y$ by a ratio $r$. The limit $r=0$ is a set of uncoupled one-dimensional chains, while $r=1$ is the isotropic square lattice. An isolated chain has a Chapter-26-type Shockley end state. At small $r$, those end states broaden into bands along the edge. SOC splits them away from the projected TRIM, while Kramers degeneracy remains at $k_\parallel=0,\pi/a$.

As $r$ increases, interchain dispersion competes with on-site SOC and reduces the bulk gap. For Martin's specific parameter set, a zone-boundary gap closing occurs between approximately $r=0.6$ and $0.7$. The gap then reopens with different bulk connectivity and the system enters a two-dimensional $\mathbb Z_2$-odd phase. That numerical ratio belongs to the chosen model and is not a universal constant.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="为什么拓扑改变必须经过 bulk gap closing | Why the topological change must pass through a bulk gap closing"
  assumptions={[
    'r 连续改变且始终保持 time reversal；',
    '目标 occupied manifold 在相变两侧均与上方 bands 分离；',
    '没有额外 symmetry-breaking phase intervening。',
  ]}
>

$\nu$ 是离散量。若在整个插值 $r\in[r_1,r_2]$ 上存在连续、time-reversal-symmetric、gapped occupied projector $P(\mathbf k,r)$，则可以把一侧的 occupied bundle 连续变形到另一侧，$\nu$ 不可能变化。因此

$$
\nu(r_1)\neq\nu(r_2)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\exists(\mathbf k_c,r_c):
E_{N_{\rm occ}}(\mathbf k_c,r_c)=E_{N_{\rm occ}+1}(\mathbf k_c,r_c).
$$

strip 图中的 edge bands 在 $r_c$ 处与 projected bulk states 接触；相变后它们以 odd connectivity 重新出现。单个固定 $k_\parallel$ 的 edge-state 能量不受拓扑固定，受保护的是完整半 edge BZ 上的连接 parity。

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="stacked 1D end states 与 intrinsic 2D Z₂ phase 的区别" titleEn="Distinguishing stacked one-dimensional end states from an intrinsic two-dimensional Z₂ phase" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

小 $r$ 时 edge band 可追溯到每条 chain 的终止态，但只要 bulk 仍属于与孤立 chains 连续相连的相，它不自动具有二维 odd helical connectivity。必须检查 interchain-coupled bulk 的 full-BZ gap 与二维 $\nu$。反过来，进入 $\nu=1$ 后，即使局部 edge wavefunction 仍像原 Shockley state，odd connectivity 已由二维 bulk obstruction 控制，而非某个固定终止下单独一条 chain 的 winding。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

At small $r$, an edge band can be traced to the termination state of each chain, but while the bulk remains continuously connected to the isolated-chain regime it does not automatically have intrinsic two-dimensional odd helical connectivity. The full-Brillouin-zone gap and two-dimensional $\nu$ of the coupled bulk must be evaluated. Conversely, after entering the $\nu=1$ phase, the local edge wavefunction may still resemble the original Shockley state, yet its odd connectivity is controlled by the two-dimensional bulk obstruction rather than by the winding of one chain at one termination.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 27.7 Hg/CdTe quantum-well structures | Hg/CdTe Quantum Well Structures

<BilingualSection titleZh="confinement 与 SOC 竞争决定 E1/H1 次序" titleEn="Confinement and SOC compete to determine E1/H1 ordering" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Martin 将 HgTe/CdTe quantum well 作为二维 topological insulator 的原型实验体系。bulk CdTe 在 $\Gamma$ 附近具有 normal order 和较大 gap；bulk HgTe 的 $s$-like $\Gamma_6$ 与 $p$-like $\Gamma_8$ 次序反转，并处于 zero-gap 状态。CdTe barriers 把 HgTe states 沿法向量子化，形成二维 subbands。

quantum well 中最相关的低能 Kramers pairs 记为 E1 与 H1。E1 含 $\Gamma_6$ 与 $\Gamma_8,m_J=\pm1/2$ 成分，H1 主要来自 $\Gamma_8,m_J=\pm3/2$。薄 well 的 confinement 较强，E1/H1 为 normal order；增加厚度时二者在临界 $d_c$ 于 $\Gamma$ 相交并闭隙；更厚的 well 进入 inverted order。time-reversal-symmetric SOC coupling 在 $\Gamma$ 外打开 gap，得到 quantum spin Hall phase。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Martin presents the HgTe/CdTe quantum well as the prototypical experimental two-dimensional topological insulator. Bulk CdTe has normal ordering and a relatively large gap near $\Gamma$. Bulk HgTe has inverted ordering of the $s$-like $\Gamma_6$ and $p$-like $\Gamma_8$ states and is a zero-gap system. CdTe barriers quantize the HgTe states along the growth direction and produce two-dimensional subbands.

The most relevant low-energy Kramers pairs in the quantum well are labelled E1 and H1. E1 contains $\Gamma_6$ and $\Gamma_8,m_J=\pm1/2$ components, while H1 is mainly derived from $\Gamma_8,m_J=\pm3/2$. A thin well has strong confinement and normal E1/H1 ordering. As the thickness grows, the two levels meet at a critical $d_c$ at $\Gamma$ and close the gap. A thicker well enters inverted order. Time-reversal-symmetric SOC coupling opens the gap away from $\Gamma$, producing the quantum spin Hall phase.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="Martin 对 functional 依赖的警告" titleEn="Martin's warning about functional dependence" eyebrow="【来源主张 | Source claim】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Martin 比较 PBE 与 HSE 结果，用它说明 topology prediction 依赖基础 band ordering 的准确性。书中结果中，PBE 对 CdTe/HgTe 的 gap 偏低；对 already inverted HgTe，“偏低”意味着负 gap 绝对值过大，并可给出错误轨道 character。HSE 在该比较中更好地再现 CdTe normal gap，以及 HgTe 中 $\Gamma_6$ 位于 spin–orbit-split $\Gamma_8$ 与 $\Gamma_7$ 之间的次序。

这是一项针对书中计算与体系的来源结论，不应扩展成“hybrid functional 自动给出所有拓扑材料正确答案”。对实际 heterostructure 还需核验 lattice constant、strain、band offsets、well/barrier composition、interface symmetry、thickness 与 many-body correction。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Martin compares PBE and HSE to show that a topology prediction depends on the accuracy of the underlying band ordering. In the calculations discussed in the book, PBE underestimates the CdTe/HgTe gaps. For already inverted HgTe, “too small” means a negative gap with excessive magnitude and may give the wrong orbital character. In that comparison, HSE better reproduces the normal CdTe gap and the HgTe ordering in which $\Gamma_6$ lies between the spin–orbit-split $\Gamma_8$ and $\Gamma_7$ states.

This is a source-specific conclusion for the calculations and systems discussed in the book, not a rule that hybrid functionals automatically solve every topological-material problem. A real heterostructure also requires checks of lattice constant, strain, band offsets, well and barrier composition, interface symmetry, thickness, and many-body corrections.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="E1/H1 continuum mass 与 quantum-well transition | E1/H1 continuum mass and the quantum-well transition"
  assumptions={[
    '低能流形由 E1 与 H1 Kramers pairs 主导；',
    'well 保持 time reversal；',
    '模型 regularization 保证远离 Γ 的 band order 正常。',

>

在 BHZ-type basis 中，可把 $\Gamma$ 点的相对次序写成质量参数

$$
M(d)=\frac{E_{E1}(d)-E_{H1}(d)}{2}.
$$

临界厚度满足

$$
\boxed{M(d_c)=0}.
$$

若 $M/B$ 的符号对应 $\Gamma$ 与 large-$k$/zone-boundary 质量相反，则每个 spin-conserving block 具有 odd Chern parity，完整 time-reversal pair 给出 $\nu=1$。符号约定依赖 basis 与 $B$ 的定义；物理内容是 E1/H1 order 与 BZ 其他区域之间存在一次奇数 parity exchange。仅测得或计算 $M(d)<0$ 还不充分，仍需确认目标 gap 在完整二维 BZ 开放。

</DerivationBlock>
