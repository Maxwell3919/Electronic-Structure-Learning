## 阅读定位 | Reading Orientation

<BilingualSection titleZh="本章控制的核心问题" titleEn="The controlled problem of this chapter" eyebrow="Orientation">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Chapter 26 说明一个两带 Hamiltonian 如何通过 $\mathbf d$ 向量、Berry phase、Chern 数和边界终止产生可计算的拓扑结构。本章增加电子自旋与 spin–orbit interaction（SOC），并区分两类二维绝缘体：破坏 time-reversal symmetry 的 Chern insulator，以及保持 spinful time reversal、总 Chern 数为零但具有非平凡 $\mathbb Z_2$ 拓扑的 quantum spin Hall/topological insulator。

Martin 的叙事先从 $sp^2$ 轨道模型得到一个两带 Chern precursor，再说明“两个相反 Chern block”只在 spin-conserving diagonal approximation 中成立。完整 SOC 允许不同自旋分量混合，可靠分类必须回到 Kramers pairs、time-reversal-invariant momenta（TRIM）、occupied-subspace obstruction、Wannier-center flow 或等价的 $\mathbb Z_2$ 诊断。

本章最后连接方格教学模型、链到平面的相变、Hg/CdTe quantum well、graphene zigzag edge 与大 SOC honeycomb 体系。所有材料连接都保留为证据链：局部 band inversion、SOC 开隙、full-BZ insulating gap、occupied-manifold invariant 和 edge spectral flow 分别验收。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Chapter 26 showed how a two-band Hamiltonian acquires calculable topology through its d vector, Berry phase, Chern number, and boundary termination. This chapter adds electron spin and spin–orbit interaction (SOC), then separates two kinds of two-dimensional insulator: a Chern insulator that breaks time-reversal symmetry, and a quantum spin Hall or topological insulator that preserves spinful time reversal, has zero total Chern number, yet carries a nontrivial $\mathbb Z_2$ topology.

Martin first derives a two-band Chern precursor from an $sp^2$ orbital model. He then shows that the language of “two opposite Chern blocks” is exact only in a spin-conserving diagonal approximation. Full SOC can mix spin components, so a reliable classification must return to Kramers pairs, time-reversal-invariant momenta (TRIM), the occupied-subspace obstruction, Wannier-centre flow, or an equivalent $\mathbb Z_2$ diagnostic.

The chapter finally connects square-lattice teaching models, a chains-to-planes transition, Hg/CdTe quantum wells, graphene zigzag edges, and large-SOC honeycomb systems. Each material connection is treated as an evidence chain: local band inversion, an SOC-opened gap, a full-Brillouin-zone insulating gap, an occupied-manifold invariant, and edge spectral flow are separate acceptance gates.

## 对象、对称性与证据层级 | Objects, Symmetries, and Evidence Levels

<BilingualSection titleZh="本章固定约定" titleEn="Conventions fixed for this chapter" eyebrow="Conventions">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

二维 bulk momentum 记为 $\mathbf k=(k_x,k_y)$，正向取 $(k_x,k_y)$。Pauli 矩阵 $\tau_i$ 作用于轨道/两带子空间，$s_i$ 作用于真实自旋；若某个有效模型改用 $\sigma_i$，页面会明确其作用空间。spinful time-reversal operator 记为

$$
\Theta=is_yK,
\qquad \Theta^2=-1,
$$

其中 $K$ 为复共轭。TRIM 满足 $\mathbf K_a\equiv-\mathbf K_a$ modulo reciprocal lattice vector。二维方形表示中有四个 bulk TRIM；一维 edge BZ 有两个 projected TRIM。

Chern number 的符号随 $(k_x,k_y)$ 取向、Pauli 轴和 occupied-band convention 改变，因此页面同时报告约定与 $|C|$。二维 topological-insulator 指数记为 $\nu\in\{0,1\}$。在 spin-conserving block limit 中可写 $C_\uparrow=-C_\downarrow$ 且 $\nu=C_\uparrow\bmod2$；离开该 limit 后，单独的 $C_\uparrow,C_\downarrow$ 通常不再定义，但 $\nu$ 仍可保持量子化。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The two-dimensional bulk momentum is $\mathbf k=(k_x,k_y)$ with positive orientation $(k_x,k_y)$. Pauli matrices $\tau_i$ act in an orbital or two-band subspace, while $s_i$ act on physical spin; any effective model using $\sigma_i$ will state its Hilbert-space role explicitly. The spinful time-reversal operator is

$$
\Theta=is_yK,
\qquad \Theta^2=-1,
$$

where $K$ denotes complex conjugation. A TRIM obeys $\mathbf K_a\equiv-\mathbf K_a$ modulo a reciprocal lattice vector. A square representation of a two-dimensional Brillouin zone has four bulk TRIM, while a one-dimensional edge Brillouin zone has two projected TRIM.

The sign of a Chern number depends on the orientation of $(k_x,k_y)$, the Pauli-axis convention, and the occupied-band convention, so the page reports both the convention and $|C|$. The two-dimensional topological-insulator index is $\nu\in\{0,1\}$. In a spin-conserving block limit, one may write $C_\uparrow=-C_\downarrow$ and $\nu=C_\uparrow\bmod2$. Away from that limit, separate $C_\uparrow$ and $C_\downarrow$ generally cease to be defined, while $\nu$ can remain quantized.

  </div>

  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

      <li><strong>局部模型：</strong>给定有限维 Hamiltonian 的 mass sign、Chern 或 $\mathbb Z_2$ 结果；</li>
      <li><strong>bulk 数值：</strong>完整 BZ 上有隙的 occupied subspace 与收敛不变量；</li>
      <li><strong>边界数值：</strong>指定 orientation、termination 和尺寸下的局域谱及其 partner connectivity；</li>
      <li><strong>材料归属：</strong>结构、化学、SOC、磁性、functional/Wannier window 和能隙均已验证；</li>
      <li><strong>实验归属：</strong>输运或谱学信号排除 ordinary edge/surface states、bulk leakage 和接触效应。</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Local model:</strong> a mass sign, Chern number, or $\mathbb Z_2$ result for a declared finite-dimensional Hamiltonian;</li>
      <li><strong>Bulk numerics:</strong> a gapped occupied subspace and converged invariant over the full Brillouin zone;</li>
      <li><strong>Boundary numerics:</strong> localized spectra and partner connectivity for a stated orientation, termination, and size;</li>
      <li><strong>Material assignment:</strong> validated structure, chemistry, SOC, magnetism, functional or Wannier window, and insulating gap;</li>
      <li><strong>Experimental assignment:</strong> transport or spectroscopy that excludes ordinary edge or surface states, bulk leakage, and contact artefacts.</li>
    </ol>
