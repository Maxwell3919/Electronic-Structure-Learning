## 27.8 Graphene 与 two-site model | Graphene and the Two-Site Model

<BilingualSection titleZh="Haldane precursor 与 Kane–Mele time-reversal double" titleEn="The Haldane precursor and the Kane–Mele time-reversal double" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

honeycomb lattice 的两个 sublattices 形成最基本 two-site model。最近邻、等价 sublattice、无 SOC 时，$\pi$ bands 在 inequivalent valleys $K,K'$ 形成 Dirac points。Haldane 模型通过有向 complex second-neighbour hopping 破坏 time reversal，在零净磁通下给两个 valleys 质量并实现 Chern insulator。Kane–Mele 构造把两个 time-reversed Haldane blocks 分配给 Kramers-related spin sectors，使总 Chern 数抵消而保留 odd $\mathbb Z_2$ parity。

真实 graphene 的最小低能 basis 是两个 sublattice 上的 $p_z$ 与 spin，共四条 bands；完整原子模型还含 $s,p_x,p_y$，总计十六条 spinful bands。若严格投影到 nearest-neighbour $p_z$ manifold，原子 $\mathbf L\cdot\mathbf S$ 在该子空间内没有一阶有效矩阵元。intrinsic SOC gap 来自 $p_z$ 通过其他 $p$ 轨道的虚混合，因此是高阶且很小。Martin 的教学图人为放大 SOC，以便显示 gap 与 edge connectivity。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The two sublattices of the honeycomb lattice form the basic two-site model. With nearest-neighbour hopping, equivalent sublattices, and no SOC, the $\pi$ bands form Dirac points at the inequivalent valleys $K$ and $K'$. The Haldane model breaks time reversal through oriented complex second-neighbour hopping, gives masses to the two valleys at zero net magnetic flux, and realizes a Chern insulator. The Kane–Mele construction assigns two time-reversed Haldane blocks to Kramers-related spin sectors, cancelling the total Chern number while retaining odd $\mathbb Z_2$ parity.

The minimal low-energy basis of real graphene is $p_z$ on two sublattices with spin, giving four bands. A complete atomic model also contains $s,p_x,p_y$, for sixteen spinful bands. If one projects strictly onto the nearest-neighbour $p_z$ manifold, atomic $\mathbf L\cdot\mathbf S$ has no first-order effective matrix element within that subspace. The intrinsic SOC gap arises from virtual mixing of $p_z$ with the other $p$ orbitals, so it is higher order and very small. Martin deliberately enlarges SOC in the teaching figures so that the gap and edge connectivity are visible.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="为什么 π-only graphene 的 intrinsic SOC 是高阶效应 | Why intrinsic SOC is higher order in π-only graphene"
  assumptions={[
    '低能 subspace P 只含 pz orbitals；',
    'Q subspace 含 s, px, py 等 σ bands，并与 P 有有限能量分离；',
    '无显式 time-reversal breaking。',
  ]}
>

在单原子 $p$ manifold 中，$L_z|p_z\rangle=0$，而 $L_x,L_y$ 把 $p_z$ 送入 $p_x,p_y$。因此

$$
P H_{\rm SO}P=0
$$

在理想 $p_z$-only projection 中。最低非零项来自 Löwdin/Schrieffer–Wolff downfolding：

$$
H_{\rm eff}^{(2)}
=P H_{\rm SO}Q\frac{1}{E-QH_0Q}QH_{\rm hop/SO}P+\mathrm{h.c.}
$$

它可以生成 spin-dependent second-neighbour hopping，低能上等价于 valley-dependent SOC mass。其尺度受 atomic SOC、$\pi$–$\sigma$ energy separation、几何和 hopping pathways 的乘积/比值控制，而不是直接等于裸原子 $\zeta$。这解释 Martin 所强调的 graphene intrinsic gap 极小，同时说明 buckling、substrate hybridization 或 heavier atoms 可显著改变有效 SOC。

</DerivationBlock>

### 无 SOC 的 zigzag Shockley band | Zigzag Shockley band without SOC

<BilingualSection titleZh="固定 edge momentum 后变成 Chapter 26 two-site chain" titleEn="At fixed edge momentum the ribbon becomes a Chapter-26 two-site chain" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

对沿 zigzag 方向周期、横向有限的 ribbon，固定 $k_\parallel$ 后，可把原子行配成沿横向的 double lines。相邻 double lines 间 hopping 保持为 $t_2=t$，而同一 double line 内两个 phase-related neighbors 给

$$
t_1(k_\parallel)=2t\cos\frac{k_\parallel}{2}
$$

（晶格常数与 edge-BZ convention 已吸收进 $k_\parallel$）。这就是 Chapter 26 的 chiral two-site chain。$k_\parallel=0$ 时 $|t_1|=2|t|>|t_2|$，abrupt edge 切在 normal/strong-bond termination，没有零能 edge solution；zone boundary 时 $t_1=0$，最外层成为 decoupled dangling double line，edge state 完全局域。

projected Dirac points 满足 $|t_1|=|t_2|$，即 $|k_\parallel|=2\pi/3$。edge state 只存在于其外侧直到 edge-BZ boundary；它在 Dirac projection 处与 gapless projected bulk continuum 合并。这个无 SOC flat/weakly dispersive band 是 termination-dependent Shockley state，不是尚未打开 bulk gap 的 $\mathbb Z_2$ edge spectral flow。minimal nearest-neighbour armchair ribbon 没有同样的 zigzag edge window。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For a ribbon periodic along the zigzag direction and finite transversely, fixing $k_\parallel$ allows the atomic rows to be grouped into transverse double lines. Hopping between neighbouring double lines remains $t_2=t$, while two phase-related neighbours within one double line give

$$
t_1(k_\parallel)=2t\cos\frac{k_\parallel}{2},
$$

with lattice constants and edge-Brillouin-zone conventions absorbed into $k_\parallel$. This is the chiral two-site chain of Chapter 26. At $k_\parallel=0$, $|t_1|=2|t|>|t_2|$, so the abrupt edge cuts the normal or strong-bond termination and has no zero-energy edge solution. At the zone boundary $t_1=0$, the outer double line decouples and the edge state is completely localized.

The projected Dirac points obey $|t_1|=|t_2|$, hence $|k_\parallel|=2\pi/3$. The edge state exists only outside those points up to the edge-zone boundary and merges into the gapless projected bulk continuum at the Dirac projections. This no-SOC flat or weakly dispersive band is a termination-dependent Shockley state, not yet $\mathbb Z_2$ edge spectral flow because no bulk gap has opened. The minimal nearest-neighbour armchair ribbon has no analogous zigzag edge window.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

### SOC 打开 Dirac gap 后的 helical connectivity | Helical connectivity after SOC opens the Dirac gap

<BilingualSection titleZh="ordinary Shockley band 被嵌入 QSH bulk–boundary structure" titleEn="The ordinary Shockley band becomes embedded in QSH bulk–boundary structure" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

intrinsic SOC 在 $K,K'$ 打开 time-reversed masses。diagonal approximation 中，两个 spin blocks 在同一 valley 的 mass 符号相反，并在 time-reversed valley 交换；每个 block 具有相反 Chern number。原先终止于 gapless projected Dirac points 的 zigzag Shockley band 现在必须分别接入 SOC-split valence/conduction states，形成一对 counterpropagating helical branches。

这并不意味着 ribbon 图中的每一条 ordinary edge band 都受保护。realistic multi-orbital edge 可同时存在 dangling-bond、reconstruction 或 electrostatic edge bands。topological evidence 是目标 bulk gap 内 odd partner connectivity、Kramers degeneracy 与 edge localization；额外 even pairs 可被 time-reversal-symmetric perturbation 移动或 gapped in pairs。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Intrinsic SOC opens time-reversed masses at $K$ and $K'$. In the diagonal approximation, the two spin blocks have opposite mass signs at the same valley and exchange them at the time-reversed valley, giving opposite Chern numbers. The zigzag Shockley band that previously terminated at gapless projected Dirac points must now connect to the SOC-split valence and conduction states, forming a counterpropagating helical pair.

This does not make every ordinary edge band in a ribbon plot protected. A realistic multi-orbital edge may also contain dangling-bond, reconstruction, or electrostatic edge bands. The topological evidence is odd partner connectivity within the target bulk gap, Kramers degeneracy, and edge localization. Additional even pairs can move or gap in pairs under time-reversal-symmetric perturbations.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="graphene 材料结论的边界" titleEn="Boundary of a graphene material conclusion" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

从 nearest-neighbour ribbon model 得到 zigzag edge state，只证明指定 termination 的 Shockley mechanism；从 Kane–Mele SOC term 得到 $\nu=1$，只证明该有效 Hamiltonian。对实际 graphene 还要确认 SOC gap 是否大于 disorder/temperature/broadening、Fermi level 是否位于 bulk gap、edge 是否重构或磁化、substrate 是否破坏关键对称性，以及 intervalley scattering 与 interactions 是否改变 edge transport。源书明确指出 realistic intrinsic gap 很小。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A zigzag edge state in a nearest-neighbour ribbon proves the Shockley mechanism for that termination. A $\nu=1$ result for the Kane–Mele SOC term proves that effective Hamiltonian. Real graphene additionally requires evidence that the SOC gap exceeds disorder, temperature, and broadening; that the Fermi level lies in the bulk gap; that the edge is not reconstructed or magnetized in a destructive way; that the substrate preserves the required structure; and that intervalley scattering and interactions do not invalidate the transport interpretation. Martin explicitly emphasizes that the realistic intrinsic gap is very small.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 27.9 大 spin–orbit interaction 的 honeycomb lattice model | Honeycomb Lattice Model with Large Spin–Orbit Interaction

<BilingualSection titleZh="把 pz 推离 Fermi level，使 px/py SOC 成为一阶效应" titleEn="Removing pz from the Fermi level makes px/py SOC first order" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

graphene 中 $p_z$ 构成低能 $\pi$ bands，而 $p_x,p_y$ 参与远离 Fermi level 的强 $\sigma$ bonding；因此 intrinsic SOC 依赖虚混合，是高阶小效应。Martin 指出相反设计：substrate 与 $p_z$ 形成强 bonding/antibonding bands，把它们推到低能窗口之外；较弱约束的 $p_x,p_y$ 留在 Fermi level 附近。此时

$$
L_zS_z|p_\pm,s_z\rangle
\propto (\pm1)s_z|p_\pm,s_z\rangle
$$

在目标低能 subspace 内已有直接矩阵元，SOC 是一阶效应。$p_x,p_y$ 与 spin 组合成 $J=3/2,m_J=\pm3/2$ 及 $J=1/2,m_J=\mp1/2$ 等 states，SOC split 可直接参与 opening the topological gap。

Martin 以 substrate-stabilized honeycomb bismuth layer 作为这一路线的例子，并引用实验生长与大-gap topological state 的工作。本站把它作为来源中的材料连接，而不是从 tight-binding argument 独立验证具体 substrate、gap 值或实验归属。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

In graphene, $p_z$ forms the low-energy $\pi$ bands while $p_x,p_y$ participate in strong $\sigma$ bonding far from the Fermi level, so intrinsic SOC relies on virtual mixing and is a small higher-order effect. Martin describes the opposite design. A substrate forms strong bonding and antibonding states with $p_z$, removing those orbitals from the low-energy window, while more weakly constrained $p_x,p_y$ states remain near the Fermi level. Then

$$
L_zS_z|p_\pm,s_z\rangle
\propto (\pm1)s_z|p_\pm,s_z\rangle
$$

has a direct matrix element inside the target subspace, so SOC is first order. The $p_x,p_y$ orbitals and spin combine into states such as $J=3/2,m_J=\pm3/2$ and $J=1/2,m_J=\mp1/2$, and the SOC splitting can directly participate in opening the topological gap.

Martin uses a substrate-stabilized honeycomb bismuth layer as an example and cites experimental growth and a large-gap topological-state report. This site treats it as a material connection reported by the source, not as an independent validation of a particular substrate, gap value, or experimental assignment from the tight-binding argument alone.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="first-order SOC 不自动保证 nontrivial Z₂" titleEn="First-order SOC does not automatically guarantee a nontrivial Z₂ index" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

first-order 只描述 SOC matrix element 在目标 orbital manifold 中出现的 perturbative order。拓扑结果还取决于 filling、sublattice potential、crystal field、bandwidth、hybridization、inversion/time-reversal symmetry 和 full-BZ gap。substrate 既可把 $p_z$ 推离 Fermi level，也可引入 Rashba splitting、charge transfer、moiré potential、strain 或额外 bands。必须对实际结构计算 occupied-subspace $\mathbb Z_2$/Wilson loop，并验证 edge/surface spectral evidence。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

“First order” describes only the perturbative order at which an SOC matrix element appears inside the target orbital manifold. The topological result also depends on filling, sublattice potential, crystal field, bandwidth, hybridization, inversion and time-reversal symmetry, and the full-Brillouin-zone gap. A substrate can both remove $p_z$ from the Fermi level and introduce Rashba splitting, charge transfer, moiré potentials, strain, or extra bands. The actual structure still requires an occupied-subspace $\mathbb Z_2$ or Wilson-loop calculation and boundary spectral evidence.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 复核、原创练习与来源 | Review, Original Practice, and Sources

### 常见误判 | Common failure modes

| 误判 | 缺失条件 | 本章允许的结论 |
|---|---|---|
| SOC 打开 gap 就必然是 QSH | 未检查 full-BZ gap 与 $\mathbb Z_2$ | SOC 只提供允许的质量项 |
| 两个 opposite spin Chern numbers 永远可定义 | 需要严格 $S_z$ conservation | 一般 full SOC 只保证总 $\nu$ |
| TRIM 处 Kramers degeneracy 表示所有 k 都 spin-degenerate | generic k 只与 −k 配对 | 无 inversion 可发生 Rashba splitting |
| 一个 TRIM 的 parity inversion 足以给 $\nu$ | 需要四个 TRIM 与全部 occupied pairs | 完整 Fu–Kane product 才是 invariant |
| edge 图有 crossing 就证明 topology | 需要 projected bulk gap、localization 与 odd connectivity | even crossings 可成对移除 |
| zigzag graphene flat band 本身是 QSH edge | 无 SOC 时 bulk Dirac gap 为零 | 它先是 termination-dependent Shockley band |
| first-order heavy-atom SOC 自动产生大 nontrivial gap | 还受 filling、crystal field、substrate 和 band competition 控制 | 只说明 SOC 不需高能 virtual path |
| HgTe E1/H1 inversion 自动证明器件 transport | 缺少 full-BZ invariant、edge geometry 与实验排他 | inversion 是材料证据链的一环 |

### 本站原创练习 | Original exercises

1. 对 $p_\pm$ 与 spin basis 显式写出 $\Theta=is_yK$ 的作用，并把四个 states 分成 Kramers pairs。
2. 对 §27.2 模型推导四个 TRIM Jacobian signs，验证 $C_-=\tfrac12[\operatorname{sgn}m_\Gamma+\operatorname{sgn}m_M-\operatorname{sgn}m_X-\operatorname{sgn}m_Y]$。
3. 固定 $V=-0.75$，分别取 $\xi=-2,-1,0.5,2$，预测 $C_-$；再用 spherical-solid-angle grid 对 15、31、61 meshes 检查整数稳定性与 minimum gap。
4. 从 $\Theta^2=-1$ 证明单 helical pair 的 $s_x,s_y$ constant masses 都破坏 time reversal；再验证 two-pair mass $s_x\tau_y$ 保持 time reversal。
5. 给定四个 TRIM 上三对 occupied Kramers parities，手算 $\nu$；随后翻转一个与两个 TRIM 的 parity products，说明相变次数的 odd/even 差异。
6. 画两组 Wilson-loop flow：一组 partners 在两个 TRIM 间保持原配对，另一组交换 partners。用任意 horizontal reference line 说明 crossing parity 为何 gauge-stable。
7. 为 Martin 方格模型构造两个 chemical potentials，分别位于最低 gap 和上方 gap，列出各自 occupied manifold、parity product 与 edge connectivity 验收项。
8. 用 $t_1(k)=2t\cos(k/2)$ 推导 zigzag edge-state window、normalization constant 和 localization length，并解释 projected Dirac points 处发散。
9. 对 graphene π-only 模型做二阶 downfolding 的量纲估计，列出会增强或抑制 effective intrinsic SOC 的 orbital energy denominator 与 hopping pathways。
10. 为 HgTe/CdTe quantum well 或 substrate-supported bismuth honeycomb 设计一份计算审计表，严格区分结构、band ordering、full-BZ gap、$\mathbb Z_2$、edge localization 和实验归属。
