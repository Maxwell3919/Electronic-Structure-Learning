## D.4 动态响应与 Kramers–Kronig 关系 | Dynamic Response and Kramers–Kronig Relations

<BilingualSection titleZh="阻尼谐振子作为因果响应原型" titleEn="The damped oscillator as a prototype of causal response" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Martin 从受驱阻尼谐振子开始，因为它同时展示 resonance、耗散、复频极点和因果性。采用 $e^{-i\omega t}$，正阻尼使所有 poles 落在复频下半平面，因此 retarded response 在上半平面解析。对正频率，Martin 所用 force-to-displacement convention 给出正的虚部，对应外场向体系输入能量。

这个 sign 结论依赖 source、observable 和 Fourier convention。换成 density 对 scalar potential、conductivity 对 electric field 或不同 Fourier 符号时，耗散部分的符号需要从 power absorption 重新推导，不能仅凭记忆迁移。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Martin begins with a driven damped oscillator because it displays resonance, dissipation, complex-frequency poles, and causality in one model. With the $e^{-i\omega t}$ convention, positive damping places every pole in the lower half-plane, so the retarded response is analytic in the upper half-plane. For positive frequency, Martin's force-to-displacement convention gives a positive imaginary part corresponding to energy transferred from the drive to the system.

This sign statement depends on the source, observable, and Fourier convention. For density response to a scalar potential, conductivity response to an electric field, or a different Fourier sign, the dissipative sign must be re-derived from absorbed power rather than copied from memory.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

$$
M\ddot x(t)+\Gamma\dot x(t)+Kx(t)=F(t),
\qquad
\chi(\omega)=\frac{x(\omega)}{F(\omega)}
=\frac{1}{M\bigl(\omega_0^2-\omega^2-i\gamma\omega\bigr)},
$$

$$
\omega_0^2=K/M,
\qquad
\gamma=\Gamma/M>0.
$$

<BilingualSection titleZh="频率依赖阻尼也是响应函数" titleEn="Frequency-dependent damping is itself a response function" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 + 补充边界 | Martin source + boundary clarification】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

常数阻尼在有限频率窗口内可作为 line-shape 模型，但 Martin 明确指出它不满足一般高频 moments，并且真实 loss mechanism 随频率变化。若将 memory/damping 写成 $\Gamma(\omega)$，它本身必须来自 causal kernel，因而也具有解析性和 Kramers–Kronig 约束。任意指定一个只含虚部的 frequency-dependent damping 会破坏这些条件。

数值计算常用常数 $\eta$ 将 poles 展宽。它可以稳定积分和绘图，但除非与 self-energy、实验 resolution 或 scattering model 建立了独立联系，不能解释成真实寿命。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Constant damping can model a line shape over a limited frequency window, but Martin explicitly notes that it violates general high-frequency moments and that real loss mechanisms depend on frequency. If a memory or damping function $\Gamma(\omega)$ is introduced, it is itself a causal kernel and must obey the associated analyticity and Kramers–Kronig constraints. Prescribing only an arbitrary frequency-dependent imaginary part generally violates those conditions.

A constant numerical $\eta$ is often used to broaden poles and stabilize integration or plotting. It cannot be interpreted as a physical lifetime unless an independent self-energy, experimental resolution, or scattering model establishes that connection.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="因果性如何导出 Kramers–Kronig" titleEn="How causality leads to Kramers–Kronig" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 + 补充推导 | Martin source + supplemental derivation】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

retarded response 在 $t<0$ 为零。按本附录 Fourier convention，它在 $\operatorname{Im}z>0$ 解析，并在足够高频率下衰减。对函数 $\chi(z)/(z-\omega)$ 沿上半平面闭合 contour，闭合路径中没有 response poles，只有 real-axis indentation 产生的 Cauchy residue。将实轴积分拆成 principal value 与 residue，便得到实部和虚部互相决定的 Hilbert transform。

高频衰减不够快时需要 subtractive KK relation；有限频率数据也不能直接用无限区间公式而不处理 extrapolation。数值 KK 一致只是必要条件，不证明谱峰的微观归属。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A retarded response vanishes for $t<0$. Under the present Fourier convention it is analytic for $\operatorname{Im}z>0$ and decays sufficiently at high frequency. Closing the contour of $\chi(z)/(z-\omega)$ in the upper half-plane encloses no response poles; only the indentation at the real-axis evaluation point produces the Cauchy residue. Splitting the real-axis integral into its principal-value and residue pieces gives mutually determining Hilbert transforms for the real and imaginary parts.

If the response does not decay fast enough, subtracted KK relations are required. Finite-window numerical data also need an explicit extrapolation rather than direct use of an infinite-range formula. Numerical KK consistency is necessary for causality but does not identify the microscopic origin of a peak.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

$$
\operatorname{Re}\chi(\omega)
=\frac{1}{\pi}\,\mathcal P\!\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}
\frac{\operatorname{Im}\chi(\omega')}{\omega'-\omega}\,d\omega',
$$

$$
\operatorname{Im}\chi(\omega)
=-\frac{1}{\pi}\,\mathcal P\!\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}
\frac{\operatorname{Re}\chi(\omega')}{\omega'-\omega}\,d\omega'.
$$

<BilingualCallout titleZh="与 Martin Eq. D.18 的符号对照" titleEn="Sign comparison with Martin Eq. D.18" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Martin 将分母写成 $\omega-\omega'$，因此第一式前出现负号、第二式前出现正号。这里改写成 $\omega'-\omega$，物理内容相同。审核公式时必须同时比较分母次序和 prefactor，不能只看积分前的正负号。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Martin writes the denominator as $\omega-\omega'$, producing a minus sign in the first relation and a plus sign in the second. Reversing the denominator to $\omega'-\omega$ gives the equivalent form above. A sign audit must compare both denominator order and prefactor, not just the symbol in front of the integral.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

<BilingualSection titleZh="量子体系的 Kubo–Greenwood 结构" titleEn="Kubo–Greenwood structure for a quantum system" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

对 periodic perturbation，非相互作用响应可写成 transition matrix elements 与 retarded denominator 的求和。Martin Eq. D.19 采用占据—空态表示；更一般的有限温度写法以 occupation difference $f_i-f_j$ 统一 absorption、emission 和 intraband limit。$i\eta$ 规定从上半平面接近实轴，并把 spectral delta function 表示成 Lorentzian limit。

虚部是 joint density of states 乘以矩阵元权重，而不是裸 JDOS。selection rule、velocity/dipole gauge、nonlocal pseudopotential correction 和 normalization 都能改变权重。看到能量差匹配并不足以预测 optical peak 强度。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For a periodic perturbation, the noninteracting response is a sum of transition matrix elements divided by retarded denominators. Martin Eq. D.19 uses an occupied-empty representation; a more general finite-temperature form uses the occupation difference $f_i-f_j$ to combine absorption, emission, and the intraband limit. The $i\eta$ prescription approaches the real axis from the upper half-plane and represents the spectral delta function as a Lorentzian limit.

The imaginary part is a joint density of states weighted by matrix elements, not a bare JDOS. Selection rules, velocity versus dipole gauge, nonlocal-pseudopotential corrections, and normalization all affect the weights. Matching an energy difference is insufficient to predict optical peak intensity.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

$$
\chi_{AB}^{0}(\omega)
=\sum_{ij}(f_i-f_j)
\frac{\langle i|\hat A|j\rangle\langle j|\hat B|i\rangle}
{\omega-(\varepsilon_j-\varepsilon_i)+i\eta}.
$$

$$
-\frac{1}{\pi}\operatorname{Im}\chi_{AB}^{0}(\omega)
\xrightarrow{\eta\to0^+}
\sum_{ij}(f_i-f_j)
\langle i|\hat A|j\rangle\langle j|\hat B|i\rangle
\delta\!\bigl(\omega-\varepsilon_j+\varepsilon_i\bigr),
$$

<BilingualSection titleZh="动态自洽 kernel" titleEn="Dynamical self-consistent kernel" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

自洽反馈推广到频域后，$\chi^0$ 和 $K$ 都依赖频率。instantaneous Coulomb term 不含 memory，而 $f_{\mathrm{xc}}(\omega)$ 一般可能含有时间非局域性。只采用 adiabatic functional derivative 等价于忽略 xc memory；这是一个明确近似，而不是 TDDFT 的定义。

kernel 本身也必须 causal，并在高频满足相应极限。把一个 static kernel 与任意 broadened $\chi^0(\omega)$ 组合可能生成数值谱，但其 pole、sum rule 和高频行为仍需单独检查。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

After self-consistent feedback is generalized to frequency, both $\chi^0$ and $K$ can depend on frequency. The instantaneous Coulomb term has no memory, whereas $f_{\mathrm{xc}}(\omega)$ may be temporally nonlocal. Using an adiabatic functional derivative neglects xc memory; this is a declared approximation, not the definition of TDDFT.

The kernel is itself causal and must obey appropriate high-frequency limits. Combining a static kernel with an arbitrarily broadened $\chi^0(\omega)$ may produce a numerical spectrum, but its poles, sum rules, and high-frequency behavior still require separate checks.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

$$
\chi(\omega)
=\bigl[1-\chi^0(\omega)K(\omega)\bigr]^{-1}\chi^0(\omega),
\qquad
K(\omega)=v_C+f_{\mathrm{xc}}(\omega).
$$

## D.5 Green 函数 | Green’s Functions

<BilingualSection titleZh="resolvent 与谱表示" titleEn="Resolvent and spectral representation" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

对 time-independent independent-particle Hamiltonian，resolvent 定义为 $G(z)=(z-\hat H)^{-1}$。在完整本征基中插入单位算符便得到 Martin Eq. D.24 的谱表示。每个 eigenvalue 是一个 pole，residue 是对应 projector；因此 Green 函数同时保存能量和态的空间权重。

在非正交 basis 中应写成 matrix pencil $G(z)=(zS-H)^{-1}$，并在 trace、density matrix 和 normalization 中保留 metric。直接套用正交基公式会产生电子数和投影权重错误。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For a time-independent independent-particle Hamiltonian, the resolvent is $G(z)=(z-\hat H)^{-1}$. Inserting the identity in a complete eigenbasis gives the spectral form of Martin Eq. D.24. Each eigenvalue is a pole and its residue is the corresponding projector, so the Green function retains both energy and spatial state weight.

In a nonorthogonal basis the correct matrix pencil is $G(z)=(zS-H)^{-1}$, with the metric retained in traces, density matrices, and normalization. Applying orthogonal-basis formulas directly gives incorrect electron counts and projected weights.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

$$
G(z;\mathbf r,\mathbf r')
=\langle\mathbf r|(z-\hat H)^{-1}|\mathbf r'\rangle
=\sum_i\frac{\psi_i(\mathbf r)\psi_i^*(\mathbf r')}{z-\varepsilon_i}.
$$

<BilingualSection titleZh="谱密度、DOS 与展宽" titleEn="Spectral density, DOS, and broadening" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 + 补充数值边界 | Martin source + numerical boundary】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

从上半平面取 $z=E+i0^+$，Sokhotski–Plemelj 关系把 pole 分解成 principal-value 实部和 delta-function 虚部。因此 local/projected DOS 可由 diagonal Green function 的负虚部得到，total DOS 是 trace。有限 $\eta$ 把 delta peak 变为 Lorentzian，并保持无限能量区间上的积分权重。

有限能量窗口、energy-dependent $\eta$、非均匀 sampling 和 matrix truncation 都会破坏简单归一化。谱密度非负要求 Hermitian Hamiltonian 与非负 projector weight；出现负谱权重可能是 convention、metric、analytic continuation 或 numerical instability 问题。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Approaching the real axis from the upper half-plane as $z=E+i0^+$, the Sokhotski-Plemelj relation splits a pole into a principal-value real part and a delta-function imaginary part. A local or projected DOS is therefore the negative imaginary part of a diagonal Green function, and the total DOS is its trace. Finite $\eta$ converts each delta peak to a Lorentzian while preserving integrated weight over the infinite energy axis.

A finite energy window, energy-dependent $\eta$, nonuniform sampling, or matrix truncation can violate the simple normalization. Non-negative spectral density assumes a Hermitian Hamiltonian and non-negative projector weights; negative spectral weight can indicate a convention, metric, analytic-continuation, or numerical-stability problem.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

$$
A(E)=-\frac{1}{\pi}\operatorname{Im}G(E+i0^+),
\qquad
n(E)=-\frac{1}{\pi}\operatorname{Im}\operatorname{Tr}G(E+i0^+).
$$

<BilingualSection titleZh="contour integration 与占据 projector" titleEn="Contour integration and the occupied projector" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

围绕 occupied poles 逆时针积分，residue theorem 给出 occupied projector，即零温 density matrix。electron number、band-energy sum 和其他 integrated one-body quantities 可写成相应 contour trace。contour 不需要穿过每个 pole；在 insulator 中可穿过 gap，而 metal 的 Fermi level 附近存在任意接近 contour 的 poles，数值条件更差。

有限温度时 occupation function 引入 Matsubara/Fermi poles 或额外 contour deformation。选择 contour 点数和 quadrature 仍需要收敛；“不对角化”不等于计算成本消失。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A counterclockwise contour around occupied poles yields the occupied projector by the residue theorem, giving the zero-temperature density matrix. Electron number, the band-energy sum, and other integrated one-body quantities can be written as contour traces with the appropriate factors. The contour need not pass through each pole. In an insulator it may cross the gap, whereas a metal has poles arbitrarily close to the Fermi-level contour and is numerically less well conditioned.

At finite temperature, the occupation function introduces Matsubara or Fermi poles, or requires an additional contour deformation. The number and placement of contour quadrature points still require convergence; avoiding explicit diagonalization does not eliminate computational cost.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

$$
\hat\rho
=\frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint_C dz\,G(z),
\qquad
N=\frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint_C dz\,\operatorname{Tr}G(z),
$$

$$
\sum_{i\in\mathrm{occ}}\varepsilon_i
=\frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint_C dz\,z\operatorname{Tr}G(z).
$$
