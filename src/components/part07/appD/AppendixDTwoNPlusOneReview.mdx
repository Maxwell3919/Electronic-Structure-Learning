## D.6 “2n+1 定理” | The “2n+1 Theorem”

<BilingualSection titleZh="定理陈述及其前提" titleEn="Statement and assumptions" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

`2n+1` 定理说明：对满足变分驻值原理的能量问题，若波函数或变分变量已知到 perturbation order $n$，则能量可确定到 order $2n+1$。最常用的情形是 $n=1$：一阶波函数响应足以构造二阶和三阶能量导数。

它依赖三个结构：目标量来自受约束驻值的能量泛函；各阶态满足相应 projected Euler–Lagrange/eigenvalue equation；归一化或其他约束按相同阶次一致展开。若目标 observable 不是变分能量，或使用的近似破坏驻值关系，阶次消去不能直接套用。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The `2n+1` theorem states that, for an energy problem obeying a variational stationarity principle, knowledge of the wavefunction or variational variables through perturbation order $n$ determines the energy through order $2n+1$. The most common case is $n=1$: a first-order wavefunction response is sufficient for second- and third-order energy derivatives.

The result relies on three structures: the target quantity is a constrained stationary energy functional; each state order satisfies the corresponding projected Euler–Lagrange or eigenvalue equation; and normalization or other constraints are expanded consistently to the same order. If the target observable is not a variational energy, or if the approximation breaks stationarity, the order elimination cannot be applied directly.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="阶次展开与归一化约束" titleEn="Order expansion and normalization constraint" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 + 补充整理 | Martin source + supplemental organization】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

按照 Martin 的记号，将 Hamiltonian、态和本征值分别展开。收集总 order $m$ 的 Schrödinger 方程，得到所有 Hamiltonian order 与 state order 的卷积和。归一化条件同样在每个 order 给出约束；零阶通常应读作 $\langle\psi^{(0)}|\psi^{(0)}\rangle=1$，而 $m>0$ 的系数和为零。

这些约束决定高阶 state 中沿零阶态方向的分量。若不同软件或推导采用 intermediate normalization、full normalization 或平行输运 gauge，具体 $|\psi^{(m)}\rangle$ 会不同，但正确的能量导数应一致。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Following Martin, expand the Hamiltonian, state, and eigenvalue separately. Collecting total order $m$ in the Schrödinger equation gives a convolution over Hamiltonian and state orders. The normalization condition also produces an order-by-order constraint: the zeroth-order term is normally $\langle\psi^{(0)}|\psi^{(0)}\rangle=1$, while the coefficient sum vanishes for $m>0$.

These constraints determine the component of a higher-order state parallel to the reference state. Intermediate normalization, full normalization, and parallel-transport gauges give different explicit $|\psi^{(m)}\rangle$, but a correctly evaluated energy derivative must be invariant.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

$$
\hat H(\lambda)=\sum_{p\ge0}\lambda^p\hat H^{(p)},
\qquad
|\psi(\lambda)\rangle=\sum_{p\ge0}\lambda^p|\psi^{(p)}\rangle,
\qquad
\varepsilon(\lambda)=\sum_{p\ge0}\lambda^p\varepsilon^{(p)}.
$$

$$
\sum_{k=0}^{m}
\bigl(\hat H-\varepsilon\bigr)^{(m-k)}|\psi^{(k)}\rangle=0,
$$

$$
\sum_{j=0}^{m}
\langle\psi^{(j)}|\psi^{(m-j)}\rangle
=\begin{cases}
1,&m=0,\\
0,&m>0.
\end{cases}
$$

<BilingualSection titleZh="三阶能量为何不需要 ψ⁽²⁾ 和 ψ⁽³⁾" titleEn="Why third-order energy does not require ψ⁽²⁾ or ψ⁽³⁾" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 + 补充推导 | Martin source + supplemental derivation】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

对 $m=3$，将所有 bra order、Hamiltonian-minus-energy order 和 ket order 组合成三角形阶次表。高阶态 $\psi^{(2)}$、$\psi^{(3)}$ 的系数正是低阶 stationary equations 和 normalization constraints。将这些条件代回后，它们从三阶能量中消去，留下只含 $\psi^{(0)}$、$\psi^{(1)}$ 和 Hamiltonian derivatives 的表达式，即 Martin Eq. D.36。

“消去”不表示高阶态为零，而表示能量在 stationary point 对未求出的高阶方向一阶不敏感。若一阶态只近似满足响应方程，三阶能量误差由 residual 控制；因此 variational robustness 仍需要明确的 residual 与 basis convergence。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For $m=3$, arrange every combination of bra order, Hamiltonian-minus-energy order, and ket order in a triangular bookkeeping table. The coefficients multiplying $\psi^{(2)}$ and $\psi^{(3)}$ are precisely lower-order stationary equations and normalization constraints. Substitution eliminates those higher-order states from the third-order energy, leaving only $\psi^{(0)}$, $\psi^{(1)}$, and Hamiltonian derivatives, as in Martin Eq. D.36.

“Elimination” does not mean that higher-order states vanish. It means that the stationary energy is first-order insensitive to the unsolved higher-order directions. If the first-order response only approximately satisfies its equation, the third-order energy error is controlled by the residual, so variational robustness still requires explicit residual and basis convergence.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

$$
\boxed{
\varepsilon^{(3)}
=\langle\psi^{(0)}|\hat H^{(3)}|\psi^{(0)}\rangle
+2\operatorname{Re}\langle\psi^{(1)}|\hat H^{(2)}|\psi^{(0)}\rangle
+\langle\psi^{(1)}|
\bigl(\hat H^{(1)}-\varepsilon^{(1)}\bigr)
|\psi^{(1)}\rangle
}.
$$

<BilingualSection titleZh="一个有限维变分检查" titleEn="A finite-dimensional variational check" eyebrow="【原创教学模型 | Original teaching model】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

考虑标量变分能量

$$
\mathcal E(x,\lambda)
=\frac{a}{2}x^2+\frac{c}{3}x^3
+\lambda\left(bx+\frac{d}{2}x^2\right),
\qquad a>0.
$$

stationary coordinate 写成 $x(\lambda)=\lambda x_1+\lambda^2x_2+\cdots$。一阶 stationary equation 给出 $x_1=-b/a$。将展开代入能量，三阶系数中所有 $x_2$ 项组合成 $(ax_1+b)x_2$，因一阶方程严格为零。于是三阶能量只需要 $x_1$。这复现 `n=1→3` 的消去机制，而不涉及具体电子 Hamiltonian。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Consider the scalar variational energy

$$
\mathcal E(x,\lambda)
=\frac{a}{2}x^2+\frac{c}{3}x^3
+\lambda\left(bx+\frac{d}{2}x^2\right),
\qquad a>0.
$$

Write the stationary coordinate as $x(\lambda)=\lambda x_1+\lambda^2x_2+\cdots$. The first-order stationarity equation gives $x_1=-b/a$. After substitution into the energy, every third-order term containing $x_2$ combines into $(ax_1+b)x_2$, which vanishes by the first-order equation. The third-order energy therefore requires only $x_1$. This reproduces the `n=1→3` cancellation mechanism without invoking a specific electronic Hamiltonian.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

$$
E^{(2)}=\frac{a}{2}x_1^2+bx_1,
\qquad
E^{(3)}=\frac{c}{3}x_1^3+\frac{d}{2}x_1^2,
\qquad
x_1=-\frac{b}{a}.
$$

<BilingualCallout titleZh="定理的适用边界" titleEn="Scope boundary of the theorem" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <ul>
      <li>perturbation series 可能只有渐近意义，<code>2n+1</code> 不保证收敛半径。</li>
      <li>简并、metallic occupations、moving basis 和 nonorthogonal constraints 需要相应推广。</li>
      <li>force、response、lifetime 或 spectrum 若不是所用 stationary energy 的导数，不能自动继承相同阶次。</li>
      <li>first-order response residual、SCF residual、basis error 和 numerical quadrature error 是不同误差源。</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <ul>
      <li>A perturbation series may be only asymptotic; <code>2n+1</code> does not establish a radius of convergence.</li>
      <li>Degeneracy, metallic occupations, moving bases, and nonorthogonal constraints require corresponding generalizations.</li>
      <li>Forces, responses, lifetimes, or spectra that are not derivatives of the stationary energy in use do not automatically inherit the same order result.</li>
      <li>First-order response residual, SCF residual, basis error, and numerical quadrature error are distinct error sources.</li>
    </ul>

  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <ol>
      <li>Replacing a small state-sum denominator by an arbitrary $\eta$ and claiming that degeneracy has been treated.</li>
      <li>Identifying $\chi^0=\delta n/\delta V_{\mathrm{eff}}$ with the complete response $\chi$ to an external field.</li>
      <li>Calling RPA fully noninteracting and ignoring Coulomb feedback.</li>
      <li>Reordering $(1-\chi^0K)^{-1}$ and $\chi^0$ in a matrix kernel without checking index conventions.</li>
      <li>Interpreting numerical broadening as a physical lifetime.</li>
      <li>Applying a KK transform to a finite frequency window without tail or subtraction treatment.</li>
      <li>Treating Im$\chi^0$ as a bare JDOS and ignoring matrix elements and occupations.</li>
      <li>Treating a Green-function pole, DOS peak, optical peak, and quasiparticle as the same evidence object.</li>
      <li>Assuming contour integration needs no convergence study or is equally conditioned in metals and insulators.</li>
      <li>Reading <code>2n+1</code> as a statement that higher-order wavefunction corrections are zero.</li>
      <li>Using one total-energy-change threshold to declare response, spectrum, sum-rule, and target-observable convergence simultaneously.</li>

<BilingualSection titleZh="原创练习" titleEn="Original exercises" eyebrow="【原创教学内容 | Original teaching content】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <ol>
      <li><strong>两能级微扰。</strong> 对 $H_0=\operatorname{diag}(0,\Delta)$ 和 off-diagonal perturbation $v$，求基态一阶态修正和二阶能量，并与精确本征值的小 $v/\Delta$ 展开比较。</li>
      <li><strong>occupied–occupied 相消。</strong> 对两个占据正交轨道证明 Hermitian one-body observable 中 $(i,j)$ 与 $(j,i)$ 的一阶项相消，并列出有限温度下为何需要 occupation difference。</li>
      <li><strong>Sternheimer 等价性。</strong> 构造一个四维 Hermitian matrix，分别用 empty-state sum 与 projected linear solve 求一阶态，比较 residual 和 gauge component。</li>
      <li><strong>标量 Dyson。</strong> 推导 $\chi=\chi^0/(1-\chi^0K)$，分析 denominator 接近零时对 input uncertainty 的 amplification。</li>
      <li><strong>matrix ordering。</strong> 给出两个不对易 $2\times2$ matrix $\chi^0,K$，验证 $(1-\chi^0K)^{-1}\chi^0=\chi^0(1-K\chi^0)^{-1}$，但错误的任意换序一般不成立。</li>
      <li><strong>KK 数值实验。</strong> 对 damped oscillator 在逐渐扩大的频率窗口上执行 principal-value quadrature，记录截断误差与 resonance 附近的 grid sensitivity。</li>
      <li><strong>spectral sum。</strong> 验证三个 Lorentzian poles 的谱权重积分，并量化有限窗口和展宽对误差的影响。</li>
      <li><strong>contour conditioning。</strong> 对一个有 gap 和一个跨 Fermi level 的 finite spectrum 设计 contour，比较最近 pole 距离与 quadrature point 数。</li>
      <li><strong>2n+1 标量模型。</strong> 从 stationary condition 推导 $x_1,x_2$，显式证明三阶能量中的 $x_2$ 项相消；再加入一个非驻值 trial $x_1+\delta$，测量误差阶次。</li>
      <li><strong>证据路由。</strong> 给出“DOS 峰、Im$\epsilon$ 峰、loss function 峰和 spectral-function pole”四个输出，为每个输出列出额外需要的 operator、kernel、boundary 和 convergence checks。</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Two-level perturbation.</strong> For $H_0=\operatorname{diag}(0,\Delta)$ and an off-diagonal perturbation $v$, derive the ground-state first-order state and second-order energy and compare with the exact eigenvalue expansion in $v/\Delta$.</li>
      <li><strong>Occupied-occupied cancellation.</strong> For two occupied orthonormal orbitals, prove cancellation of the $(i,j)$ and $(j,i)$ first-order terms for a Hermitian one-body observable, and explain why finite temperature requires occupation differences.</li>
      <li><strong>Sternheimer equivalence.</strong> Construct a four-dimensional Hermitian matrix and compute the first-order state by both an empty-state sum and a projected linear solve, comparing residual and gauge component.</li>
      <li><strong>Scalar Dyson equation.</strong> Derive $\chi=\chi^0/(1-\chi^0K)$ and analyze amplification of input uncertainty near a zero denominator.</li>
      <li><strong>Matrix ordering.</strong> Choose noncommuting $2\times2$ matrices $\chi^0,K$ and verify $(1-\chi^0K)^{-1}\chi^0=\chi^0(1-K\chi^0)^{-1}$ while arbitrary reordering fails.</li>
      <li><strong>Numerical KK experiment.</strong> Apply principal-value quadrature to a damped oscillator over increasing frequency windows and record truncation error and grid sensitivity near resonance.</li>
      <li><strong>Spectral sum.</strong> Verify the integrated weight of three Lorentzian poles and quantify errors from a finite window and broadening.</li>
      <li><strong>Contour conditioning.</strong> Design contours for a finite gapped spectrum and a spectrum crossing the Fermi level; compare nearest-pole distance and required quadrature points.</li>
      <li><strong>Scalar 2n+1 model.</strong> Derive $x_1,x_2$ from stationarity and prove explicitly that $x_2$ cancels from the third-order energy; then perturb $x_1$ by $\delta$ and measure the error order.</li>
      <li><strong>Evidence routing.</strong> For a DOS peak, Im$\epsilon$ peak, loss-function peak, and spectral-function pole, list the additional operator, kernel, boundary, and convergence checks needed for interpretation.</li>
    </ol>
