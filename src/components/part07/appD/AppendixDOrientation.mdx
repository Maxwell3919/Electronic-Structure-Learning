## 使用定位 | Reference Orientation

<BilingualSection titleZh="本附录解决什么问题" titleEn="What this Appendix resolves" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 + 补充组织 | Martin source + supplemental organization】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Appendix D 把电子结构中经常分散出现的三种语言连接起来：对本征态作 Rayleigh–Schrödinger 微扰、用 susceptibility 描述 observable 对外场的响应，以及用 resolvent/Green 函数组织谱和投影。三者处理的是同一线性代数结构，但输入、输出和适用条件不同。

D.1 从非简并一阶波函数修正出发，并给出独立粒子体系的占据—空态求和。D.2 将响应对象专门化为密度，定义静态 $\chi^0$。D.3 加入诱导 Hartree–exchange-correlation 势，形成自洽 Dyson 方程。D.4 将时间、因果性和频率加入响应，建立 Kramers–Kronig 与 Kubo–Greenwood 表示。D.5 以 $G(z)=(z-H)^{-1}$ 统一本征态求和、DOS、density matrix 和 contour integration。D.6 说明变分能量为何用到 $n$ 阶波函数即可确定到 $2n+1$ 阶。

本附录直接支持 Chapter 20 的 DFPT、Chapter 21 的 optical/TDDFT response，以及 Appendices E、L、M。它不替代具体介电张量、声子、光谱或迭代算法的完整叙事。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Appendix D connects three languages that often appear separately in electronic structure: Rayleigh-Schrödinger perturbation theory for eigenstates, susceptibilities for the response of observables to external fields, and resolvent/Green-function methods for spectra and projections. They organize the same underlying linear-algebra structure, but their inputs, outputs, and domains of validity differ.

Section D.1 begins from the nondegenerate first-order state correction and the occupied-empty sum of an independent-particle system. D.2 specializes the observable to density and defines the static $\chi^0$. D.3 adds the induced Hartree-exchange-correlation potential and obtains a self-consistent Dyson equation. D.4 introduces time, causality, and frequency, leading to Kramers-Kronig and Kubo-Greenwood forms. D.5 uses $G(z)=(z-H)^{-1}$ to unify state sums, the DOS, density matrix, and contour integration. D.6 explains why an $n$th-order wavefunction determines a variational energy through order $2n+1$.

This Appendix directly supports DFPT in Chapter 20, optical/TDDFT response in Chapter 21, and Appendices E, L, and M. It does not replace the complete narratives for dielectric tensors, phonons, spectra, or iterative algorithms.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## 数学对象、单位与响应约定 | Mathematical Objects, Units, and Response Conventions

<BilingualSection titleZh="微扰参数、态和 projector" titleEn="Perturbation parameter, states, and projectors" eyebrow="【定义 | Definitions】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

令

$$
\hat H(\lambda)=\hat H^{(0)}+\lambda\hat H^{(1)}+\lambda^2\hat H^{(2)}+\cdots,
$$

并将归一化本征态写为 $|\Psi_n(\lambda)\rangle$。非简并参考态满足

$$
\hat H^{(0)}|\Psi_n^{(0)}\rangle
=E_n^{(0)}|\Psi_n^{(0)}\rangle.
$$

定义参考态 projector $P_n=|\Psi_n^{(0)}\rangle\langle\Psi_n^{(0)}|$ 和补空间 projector $Q_n=1-P_n$。常用中间归一化为

$$
\langle\Psi_n^{(0)}|\Psi_n(\lambda)\rangle=1,
$$

从而 $\langle\Psi_n^{(0)}|\Psi_n^{(p)}\rangle=0$ 对 $p>0$ 成立。其他 normalization/gauge 会重排平行于参考态的分量，但不能改变正确计算的 observable。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Let

$$
\hat H(\lambda)=\hat H^{(0)}+\lambda\hat H^{(1)}+\lambda^2\hat H^{(2)}+\cdots
$$

and denote a normalized eigenstate by $|\Psi_n(\lambda)\rangle$. A nondegenerate reference state satisfies

$$
\hat H^{(0)}|\Psi_n^{(0)}\rangle
=E_n^{(0)}|\Psi_n^{(0)}\rangle.
$$

Define the reference projector $P_n=|\Psi_n^{(0)}\rangle\langle\Psi_n^{(0)}|$ and complementary projector $Q_n=1-P_n$. A common intermediate normalization is

$$
\langle\Psi_n^{(0)}|\Psi_n(\lambda)\rangle=1,
$$

so $\langle\Psi_n^{(0)}|\Psi_n^{(p)}\rangle=0$ for $p>0$. Other normalization or gauge choices redistribute the component parallel to the reference state but cannot change a correctly evaluated observable.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="响应函数的变量与方向" titleEn="Variables and direction of a response function" eyebrow="【定义 | Definitions】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

线性响应写作

$$
\delta A(1)=\int d2\,\chi_{AB}(1,2)\,\delta B(2),
$$

其中 $1,2$ 可以包含位置、时间、自旋、Cartesian 分量和晶胞指标。$\chi_{AB}$ 的第一个下标是被测 observable，第二个下标是 source。交换两个下标一般得到不同对象；只有在额外对称性和静态 thermodynamic 条件下才可能出现 reciprocity。

对密度响应，source 通常是外部标量势，

$$
\delta n(\mathbf r,t)
=\int d\mathbf r' dt'\,
\chi(\mathbf r t,\mathbf r't')\delta V_{\mathrm{ext}}(\mathbf r',t').
$$

$\chi$ 的单位为 density 除以 potential，再除以时间积分采用的单位因子。频域定义后时间积分被吸收，单位随 Fourier convention 改变。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Linear response is written as

$$
\delta A(1)=\int d2\,\chi_{AB}(1,2)\,\delta B(2),
$$

where $1,2$ may include position, time, spin, Cartesian components, and cell indices. The first subscript labels the measured observable and the second the source. Interchanging them generally gives a different object; reciprocity requires additional symmetry and static thermodynamic conditions.

For density response, the source is usually an external scalar potential,

$$
\delta n(\mathbf r,t)
=\int d\mathbf r' dt'\,
\chi(\mathbf r t,\mathbf r't')\delta V_{\mathrm{ext}}(\mathbf r',t').
$$

The units of $\chi$ are density divided by potential and by the units carried by the time integral. After transforming to frequency, the time integration is absorbed and units depend on the Fourier convention.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="时间与 Fourier 约定" titleEn="Time and Fourier convention" eyebrow="【统一约定 | Common convention】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

全 Part VII 采用物理场时间依赖 $e^{-i\omega t}$。对应的 forward transform 写为

$$
X(\omega)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}dt\,e^{i\omega t}X(t),
\qquad
X(t)=\int\frac{d\omega}{2\pi}e^{-i\omega t}X(\omega).
$$

retarded response 满足 $X^R(t<0)=0$，因此 $X^R(z)$ 在 $\operatorname{Im}z>0$ 解析。实频极限从上半平面取 $z=\omega+i0^+$。在该约定下，passive density/oscillator response 的耗散部分符号还取决于 source 与 observable 的定义；不能脱离公式只记忆“虚部必须正/负”。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Throughout Part VII, physical fields carry time dependence $e^{-i\omega t}$. The corresponding forward transform is

$$
X(\omega)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}dt\,e^{i\omega t}X(t),
\qquad
X(t)=\int\frac{d\omega}{2\pi}e^{-i\omega t}X(\omega).
$$

A retarded response obeys $X^R(t<0)=0$, so $X^R(z)$ is analytic for $\operatorname{Im}z>0$. The real-frequency limit is approached from the upper half-plane as $z=\omega+i0^+$. With this convention, the sign of the dissipative part of a passive density or oscillator response also depends on the definitions of source and observable; it should not be memorized independently of the formula.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="四种层级不能混用" titleEn="Four response levels that must remain distinct" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

- $\chi^0$：固定独立粒子 Hamiltonian 对其有效势的响应。
- $K=\delta V_{\mathrm{eff}}/\delta n$：把诱导密度反馈成诱导势的 kernel。
- $\chi=[1-\chi^0K]^{-1}\chi^0$：对外部 source 的可约自洽响应。
- 实验 observable：还可能包含电磁边界、local-field inversion、matrix elements、温度、disorder、lifetime 和测量几何。

前一层计算完成不能自动推出后一层正确。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

- $\chi^0$: response of a fixed independent-particle Hamiltonian to its effective potential.
- $K=\delta V_{\mathrm{eff}}/\delta n$: kernel that turns an induced density into an induced potential.
- $\chi=[1-\chi^0K]^{-1}\chi^0$: reducible self-consistent response to an external source.
- Experimental observable: may additionally require electromagnetic boundaries, local-field inversion, matrix elements, temperature, disorder, lifetime, and measurement geometry.

Completion of one layer does not automatically validate the next.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>
