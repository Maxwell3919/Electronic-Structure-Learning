## 使用定位 | Reference Orientation

<BilingualSection titleZh="本附录解决什么问题" titleEn="What this Appendix resolves" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

电子结构理论反复要求“对一个函数求导”：对密度变化求能量变化、对轨道变化求驻值条件、对密度梯度变化求交换—关联势。普通函数的导数处理数值变量；这里的输入是完整函数，因此需要先指定允许函数的集合、变化方向和边界条件，再谈泛函导数。

Martin Appendix A 的原书范围很短：A.1 建立函数、泛函、变化和泛函导数的基本记号；A.2 处理同时依赖密度与密度梯度的积分泛函。本网站在这一结构上补足约束变分、Euler–Lagrange 方程、边界项和二阶变分，以便正文可以引用完整的数学连接。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Electronic-structure theory repeatedly asks for a derivative “with respect to a function”: the change of an energy under a density variation, orbital stationarity, or the exchange-correlation potential generated by a density-gradient functional. An ordinary derivative acts on numerical variables. Here the input is an entire function, so the admissible set, variation directions, and boundary conditions must be fixed before a functional derivative is meaningful.

Martin's Appendix A is concise. Section A.1 introduces functions, functionals, variations, and functional derivatives; A.2 treats integral functionals that depend on both a density and its gradient. The website supplies constrained variation, the Euler–Lagrange equation, boundary terms, and second variation so that the numbered chapters can refer to a complete mathematical connection.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## 数学对象、单位与约定 | Mathematical Objects, Units, and Conventions

<BilingualSection titleZh="定义域、函数空间与变化空间" titleEn="Domain, function space, and variation space" eyebrow="【补充推导 | Supplemental derivation】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

令 $\Omega\subset\mathbb R^d$ 为积分区域，边界记为 $\partial\Omega$，外法向量为 $\boldsymbol\nu$。泛函写作

$$
F:X\longrightarrow\mathbb R,
\qquad
n\longmapsto F[n],
$$

其中 $X$ 是允许函数的集合。若 $F$ 含 $\nabla n$，一个常用最低选择是 $X\subset H^1(\Omega)$；若推导中直接出现 $\nabla^2n$，则需要更强正则性，或将方程理解为弱形式。

允许的变化写成 $\eta\in V$，路径为 $n_\varepsilon=n+\varepsilon\eta$。$V$ 由问题的约束决定：固定边界值时 $\eta|_{\partial\Omega}=0$；周期体系要求 $\eta$ 周期；保持粒子数时要求 $\int_\Omega\eta\,d\mathbf r=0$。因此“任意变化”只表示在已经指定的 $V$ 内任意。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Let $\Omega\subset\mathbb R^d$ be the integration domain, with boundary $\partial\Omega$ and outward normal $\boldsymbol\nu$. A functional is written as

$$
F:X\longrightarrow\mathbb R,
\qquad
n\longmapsto F[n],
$$

where $X$ is the admissible set of functions. If $F$ contains $\nabla n$, a common minimal choice is $X\subset H^1(\Omega)$. If a derivation uses $\nabla^2n$ pointwise, stronger regularity is required, or the equation must be interpreted in a weak sense.

An admissible variation is denoted by $\eta\in V$, and the path is $n_\varepsilon=n+\varepsilon\eta$. The problem determines $V$: fixed boundary values impose $\eta|_{\partial\Omega}=0$; a periodic problem requires periodic $\eta$; fixed particle number imposes $\int_\Omega\eta\,d\mathbf r=0$. Thus “arbitrary variation” means arbitrary only within the already specified space $V$.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="一阶变化的定义" titleEn="Definition of the first variation" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 + 补充澄清 | Martin source + clarification】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

沿方向 $\eta$ 的一阶变化定义为单变量路径 $\varepsilon\mapsto F[n+\varepsilon\eta]$ 在零点的导数：

$$
\delta F[n;\eta]
=
\left.\frac{d}{d\varepsilon}F[n+\varepsilon\eta]\right|_{\varepsilon=0}.
$$

若存在函数或广义函数 $g(\mathbf r)$，使所有允许 $\eta$ 都满足

$$
\delta F[n;\eta]
=
\int_\Omega g(\mathbf r)\eta(\mathbf r)\,d\mathbf r,
$$

则在所选内积与边界约定下写作 $g=\delta F/\delta n$。这个表示依赖配对方式；在离散非正交基中，梯度还会含重叠矩阵或质量矩阵。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The first variation in direction $\eta$ is the derivative at zero of the scalar path $\varepsilon\mapsto F[n+\varepsilon\eta]$:

$$
\delta F[n;\eta]
=
\left.\frac{d}{d\varepsilon}F[n+\varepsilon\eta]\right|_{\varepsilon=0}.
$$

If a function or distribution $g(\mathbf r)$ exists such that every admissible $\eta$ satisfies

$$
\delta F[n;\eta]
=
\int_\Omega g(\mathbf r)\eta(\mathbf r)\,d\mathbf r,
$$

then, for the chosen pairing and boundary convention, one writes $g=\delta F/\delta n$. This representation depends on the pairing. In a discrete nonorthogonal basis, the corresponding gradient also involves the overlap or mass matrix.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="Gâteaux 与 Fréchet 的最低必要区别" titleEn="Minimal distinction between Gâteaux and Fréchet derivatives" kind="derivation">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

方向导数对每个固定 $\eta$ 存在，只给出 Gâteaux 层面的候选导数。若还存在有界线性映射 $DF[n]$，使

$$
F[n+h]-F[n]-DF[n](h)=o(\lVert h\rVert_X)
$$

对所有足够小的 $h$ 一致成立，才得到 Fréchet 可微性。电子结构教材中的形式变分通常目标是推导驻值方程；它本身不自动证明上述一致余项、解的存在性或唯一性。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The existence of a directional derivative for each fixed $\eta$ supplies a Gâteaux-level candidate. Fréchet differentiability additionally requires a bounded linear map $DF[n]$ such that

$$
F[n+h]-F[n]-DF[n](h)=o(\lVert h\rVert_X)
$$

uniformly over sufficiently small $h$. Formal variations in electronic-structure texts are usually used to derive stationarity equations; by themselves they do not establish this uniform remainder, existence of a solution, or uniqueness.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

<BilingualSection titleZh="量纲检查" titleEn="Dimensional check" eyebrow="【补充推导 | Supplemental derivation】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

若 $n(\mathbf r)$ 是三维数密度，量纲为 $L^{-3}$，而 $F[n]$ 是能量，则

$$
\delta F
=
\int d^3r\,\frac{\delta F}{\delta n(\mathbf r)}\,\delta n(\mathbf r)
$$

要求 $\delta F/\delta n$ 具有能量量纲，因为 $d^3r\,\delta n$ 无量纲。若变分变量换成无量纲轨道系数、面密度或自旋磁化密度，泛函导数的单位随之改变。单位检查必须针对实际变分变量，不能只看符号 $\delta F/\delta n$。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

If $n(\mathbf r)$ is a three-dimensional number density with dimension $L^{-3}$ and $F[n]$ is an energy, then

$$
\delta F
=
\int d^3r\,\frac{\delta F}{\delta n(\mathbf r)}\,\delta n(\mathbf r)
$$

requires $\delta F/\delta n$ to have units of energy, because $d^3r\,\delta n$ is dimensionless. If the variational variable is instead a dimensionless orbital coefficient, an areal density, or a spin-magnetization density, the derivative has different units. Dimensional analysis must follow the actual variable rather than the notation alone.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>
