## 常见误判、原创练习与来源边界 | Misjudgments, Original Exercises, and Source Boundaries

<BilingualSection titleZh="会改变推导或实现的误判" titleEn="Misjudgments that change a derivation or implementation" eyebrow="【补充说明 | Supplemental clarification】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

1. **把 $F[n+\eta]-F[n]$ 直接称为 $\delta F$。** 前者含全部阶数；后者是路径参数的一阶系数。
2. **看到 $\delta F/\delta n=0$ 就删除约束。** 固定粒子数时正确方程一般是 $\delta F/\delta n=\mu$；轨道正交约束需要乘子矩阵。
3. **分部积分后不写边界项。** 固定、周期、无穷远衰减和自由边界给出不同理由及不同条件。
4. **把方向导数存在当作完整可微性证明。** 各方向的导数可能存在，却不形成连续有界的 Fréchet 导数。
5. **把驻值当作极小值。** 一阶方程也允许极大值和鞍点；约束问题的二阶检验还必须限制在可行切空间。
6. **把连续泛函导数逐点采样成离散梯度。** 离散梯度由实际求和、矩阵和边界闭合求导，通常含离散梯度算子的伴随。
7. **在 $|\nabla n|=0$ 处直接除以梯度模。** 应检查组合极限或选择平方梯度等更适合实现的变量。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

1. **Calling $F[n+\eta]-F[n]$ the variation without an order qualifier.** The finite difference contains all orders; $\delta F$ is the first-order coefficient of a parameterized path.
2. **Dropping constraints after writing $\delta F/\delta n=0$.** Fixed particle number generally gives $\delta F/\delta n=\mu$, and orbital orthogonality requires a multiplier matrix.
3. **Omitting the boundary term after integration by parts.** Fixed, periodic, decaying-at-infinity, and free boundaries remove or retain it for different reasons.
4. **Treating directional differentiability as a proof of full differentiability.** Directional derivatives may exist without forming a continuous bounded Fréchet derivative.
5. **Equating stationarity with a minimum.** First-order equations also admit maxima and saddles; in a constrained problem the second-order test must be restricted to feasible tangent directions.
6. **Sampling a continuous functional derivative pointwise to obtain a discrete gradient.** The discrete gradient follows from differentiating the actual sum, matrices, and boundary closure and normally contains the adjoint of the discrete gradient operator.
7. **Dividing directly by $|\nabla n|$ at zero gradient.** The combined limiting expression or a better implementation variable such as the squared gradient must be examined.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="原创练习" titleEn="Original exercises" eyebrow="【原创教学内容 | Original teaching content】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

1. **局域幂泛函。** 对 $F[n]=\int_\Omega a(\mathbf r)n(\mathbf r)^3d\mathbf r$，从路径定义推导一阶和二阶变分，并写出各项单位。说明需要对 $n$ 和 $a$ 作何可积性假设。
2. **固定粒子数。** 最小化 $F[n]=\int_0^1 n(x)^2dx$，约束 $\int_0^1n(x)dx=N$。求驻值函数，并用 Cauchy–Schwarz 不等式判断它是否为全局极小。
3. **边界条件。** 对 $J[n]=\int_0^1[(n')^2/2+V(n)]dx$ 分别推导固定端点与自由端点的 Euler–Lagrange 问题。明确写出自由端点条件。
4. **周期抵消。** 在一维周期区间证明 $[f_{n'}\eta]_0^L=0$，并列出需要周期一致的对象。只要求 $n(0)=n(L)$ 是否足够？
5. **离散方向导数。** 选均匀网格和一个明确的差分矩阵 $D$，构造 $F_h(\mathbf n)=\sum_iw_i(n_i^2+\alpha(D\mathbf n)_i^2)$。解析求 $\nabla F_h$，再用随机方向的中心差分验证相对误差随步长先下降、后受舍入误差限制。
6. **零模判断。** 构造一个因常数平移对称性而具有二阶零方向的泛函。说明零二阶变化为何不能单独判为不稳定。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

1. **Local power functional.** For $F[n]=\int_\Omega a(\mathbf r)n(\mathbf r)^3d\mathbf r$, derive the first and second variations from the path definition and state the units of every term. Identify sufficient integrability assumptions on $n$ and $a$.
2. **Fixed particle number.** Minimize $F[n]=\int_0^1 n(x)^2dx$ subject to $\int_0^1n(x)dx=N$. Find the stationary function and use the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality to decide whether it is a global minimum.
3. **Boundary conditions.** For $J[n]=\int_0^1[(n')^2/2+V(n)]dx$, derive the Euler–Lagrange problem for fixed endpoints and for free endpoints. State the free-endpoint conditions explicitly.
4. **Periodic cancellation.** On a one-dimensional periodic interval, prove $[f_{n'}\eta]_0^L=0$ and list the quantities that must match periodically. Is $n(0)=n(L)$ alone sufficient?
5. **Discrete directional derivative.** Choose a uniform grid and an explicit difference matrix $D$, then construct $F_h(\mathbf n)=\sum_iw_i(n_i^2+\alpha(D\mathbf n)_i^2)$. Derive $\nabla F_h$ and verify it with central differences in random directions, observing truncation-error reduction followed by roundoff limitation.
6. **Zero mode.** Construct a functional with a second-variation zero direction caused by constant-shift symmetry. Explain why the zero quadratic variation alone does not establish instability.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>
