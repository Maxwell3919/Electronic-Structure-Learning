## A.2 密度泛函与梯度依赖 | Functionals in Density Functional Theory Including Gradients

<BilingualSection titleZh="一般一阶梯度泛函" titleEn="General first-gradient functional" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 + 补充推导 | Martin source + supplemental derivation】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

考虑

$$
F[n]
=
\int_\Omega
f\!\left(n(\mathbf r),\nabla n(\mathbf r),\mathbf r\right)
\,d\mathbf r.
$$

令 $\mathbf p=\nabla n$，假设 $f(n,\mathbf p,\mathbf r)$ 对 $n$ 和 $\mathbf p$ 连续可微；$n$ 与允许变化 $\eta$ 至少具有使下述积分与分部积分成立的正则性。路径

$$
n_\varepsilon=n+\varepsilon\eta
$$

满足

$$
\nabla n_\varepsilon
=
\nabla n+\varepsilon\nabla\eta,
$$

因为梯度是线性算子。将路径代入：

$$
F[n_\varepsilon]
=
\int_\Omega
f(n+\varepsilon\eta,
\nabla n+\varepsilon\nabla\eta,
\mathbf r)
\,d\mathbf r.
$$

在允许交换求导与积分的条件下，对 $\varepsilon$ 使用多变量链式法则：

$$
\frac{dF[n_\varepsilon]}{d\varepsilon}
=
\int_\Omega
\left[
\frac{\partial f}{\partial n}\eta
+
\frac{\partial f}{\partial(\nabla n)}\cdot\nabla\eta
\right]
\,d\mathbf r.
$$

这里 $\partial f/\partial(\nabla n)$ 是与 $\nabla n$ 同维的向量，其第 $i$ 个分量为 $\partial f/\partial(\partial_i n)$。令 $\varepsilon=0$ 得到尚未分部积分的一阶变化：

$$
\delta F[n;\eta]
=
\int_\Omega
\left[
f_n\eta
+
f_{\nabla n}\cdot\nabla\eta
\right]d\mathbf r.
$$

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Consider

$$
F[n]
=
\int_\Omega
f\!\left(n(\mathbf r),\nabla n(\mathbf r),\mathbf r\right)
\,d\mathbf r.
$$

Write $\mathbf p=\nabla n$ and assume that $f(n,\mathbf p,\mathbf r)$ is continuously differentiable in $n$ and $\mathbf p$. The function $n$ and admissible variation $\eta$ have at least the regularity needed for the integrals and integration by parts below. Along

$$
n_\varepsilon=n+\varepsilon\eta,
$$

linearity of the gradient gives

$$
\nabla n_\varepsilon
=
\nabla n+\varepsilon\nabla\eta.
$$

Substitution yields

$$
F[n_\varepsilon]
=
\int_\Omega
f(n+\varepsilon\eta,
\nabla n+\varepsilon\nabla\eta,
\mathbf r)
\,d\mathbf r.
$$

When differentiation may be interchanged with integration, the multivariable chain rule gives

$$
\frac{dF[n_\varepsilon]}{d\varepsilon}
=
\int_\Omega
\left[
\frac{\partial f}{\partial n}\eta
+
\frac{\partial f}{\partial(\nabla n)}\cdot\nabla\eta
\right]
\,d\mathbf r.
$$

The object $\partial f/\partial(\nabla n)$ is a vector with the same dimension as $\nabla n$; its $i$th component is $\partial f/\partial(\partial_i n)$. At $\varepsilon=0$, the first variation before integration by parts is

$$
\delta F[n;\eta]
=
\int_\Omega
\left[
f_n\eta
+
f_{\nabla n}\cdot\nabla\eta
\right]d\mathbf r.
$$

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="分部积分与 Euler–Lagrange 导数" titleEn="Integration by parts and the Euler–Lagrange derivative" eyebrow="【补充推导 | Supplemental derivation】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

目标是把 $\nabla\eta$ 转换成乘以 $\eta$ 的体项。使用乘积散度恒等式

$$
\nabla\cdot(\eta\mathbf a)
=
\mathbf a\cdot\nabla\eta
+
\eta\,\nabla\cdot\mathbf a,
$$

取 $\mathbf a=f_{\nabla n}$，重排为

$$
f_{\nabla n}\cdot\nabla\eta
=
\nabla\cdot(\eta f_{\nabla n})
-
\eta\,\nabla\cdot f_{\nabla n}.
$$

代回一阶变化：

$$
\delta F
=
\int_\Omega
\left[
f_n-\nabla\cdot f_{\nabla n}
\right]\eta\,d\mathbf r
+
\int_\Omega
\nabla\cdot(\eta f_{\nabla n})\,d\mathbf r.
$$

对最后一项使用散度定理：

$$
\int_\Omega\nabla\cdot(\eta f_{\nabla n})\,d\mathbf r
=
\oint_{\partial\Omega}
\eta\,f_{\nabla n}\cdot\boldsymbol\nu\,dS.
$$

因此完整结果是

$$
\boxed{
\delta F[n;\eta]
=
\int_\Omega
\left[
\frac{\partial f}{\partial n}
-
\nabla\cdot
\frac{\partial f}{\partial(\nabla n)}
\right]
\eta\,d\mathbf r
+
\oint_{\partial\Omega}
\eta
\frac{\partial f}{\partial(\nabla n)}
\cdot\boldsymbol\nu\,dS
}
$$

只有在边界项依据明确条件消失后，才可把体项系数写成

$$
\boxed{
\frac{\delta F}{\delta n}
=
\frac{\partial f}{\partial n}
-
\nabla\cdot
\frac{\partial f}{\partial(\nabla n)}
}.
$$

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The aim is to replace $\nabla\eta$ by a bulk term multiplying $\eta$. Use the product-divergence identity

$$
\nabla\cdot(\eta\mathbf a)
=
\mathbf a\cdot\nabla\eta
+
\eta\,\nabla\cdot\mathbf a.
$$

With $\mathbf a=f_{\nabla n}$,

$$
f_{\nabla n}\cdot\nabla\eta
=
\nabla\cdot(\eta f_{\nabla n})
-
\eta\,\nabla\cdot f_{\nabla n}.
$$

Substitution into the first variation gives

$$
\delta F
=
\int_\Omega
\left[
f_n-\nabla\cdot f_{\nabla n}
\right]\eta\,d\mathbf r
+
\int_\Omega
\nabla\cdot(\eta f_{\nabla n})\,d\mathbf r.
$$

The divergence theorem converts the final term to

$$
\int_\Omega\nabla\cdot(\eta f_{\nabla n})\,d\mathbf r
=
\oint_{\partial\Omega}
\eta\,f_{\nabla n}\cdot\boldsymbol\nu\,dS.
$$

Hence the complete expression is

$$
\boxed{
\delta F[n;\eta]
=
\int_\Omega
\left[
\frac{\partial f}{\partial n}
-
\nabla\cdot
\frac{\partial f}{\partial(\nabla n)}
\right]
\eta\,d\mathbf r
+
\oint_{\partial\Omega}
\eta
\frac{\partial f}{\partial(\nabla n)}
\cdot\boldsymbol\nu\,dS
}.
$$

Only after an explicit condition makes the boundary term vanish may the bulk coefficient be identified as

$$
\boxed{
\frac{\delta F}{\delta n}
=
\frac{\partial f}{\partial n}
-
\nabla\cdot
\frac{\partial f}{\partial(\nabla n)}
}.
$$

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## 边界项与允许变化 | Boundary Terms and Admissible Variations

<BoundaryTermDiagram />

<BilingualSection titleZh="四类常见边界处理" titleEn="Four common boundary treatments" eyebrow="【补充推导 | Supplemental derivation】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

1. **固定值（Dirichlet）边界。** $n$ 在 $\partial\Omega$ 上预先指定，因此可行路径必须满足 $\eta|_{\partial\Omega}=0$，边界项消失。
2. **周期边界。** 相对的晶胞面上 $n$、$\eta$ 和相应通量周期一致，而外法向相反；成对表面积分抵消。
3. **无穷远衰减。** 对孤立体系取扩展到无穷远的区域时，需要 $\eta f_{\nabla n}$ 衰减得足够快，使无穷远表面积分为零。仅说明 $n\to0$ 并不足够，还要控制乘积的衰减率。
4. **自由边界。** 若 $\eta$ 在边界上任意，驻值要求自然边界条件

$$
\frac{\partial f}{\partial(\nabla n)}\cdot\boldsymbol\nu=0
\quad\text{on }\partial\Omega
$$

或由额外表面能修改后的相应条件。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

1. **Fixed-value (Dirichlet) boundary.** The value of $n$ is prescribed on $\partial\Omega$, so feasible paths obey $\eta|_{\partial\Omega}=0$ and the boundary term vanishes.
2. **Periodic boundary.** On opposite cell faces, $n$, $\eta$, and the relevant flux are periodic while the outward normals are opposite; paired surface integrals cancel.
3. **Decay at infinity.** For an isolated system on an expanding domain, $\eta f_{\nabla n}$ must decay fast enough for the surface integral at infinity to vanish. The statement $n\to0$ alone is insufficient; the product's decay rate matters.
4. **Free boundary.** If $\eta$ is arbitrary on the boundary, stationarity requires the natural condition

$$
\frac{\partial f}{\partial(\nabla n)}\cdot\boldsymbol\nu=0
\quad\text{on }\partial\Omega,
$$

or the corresponding condition modified by any explicit surface energy.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="不能静默删除边界项" titleEn="The boundary term cannot be silently discarded" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

同一个体积分表达式在不同边界条件下可能产生不同驻值问题。直接从未分部积分的式子跳到 Euler–Lagrange 方程，等价于暗中选择了一类允许变化。数值实现若采用有限区间、真空层、截断库仑势或非周期边界，也必须重新检查离散边界处理是否对应连续推导。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The same bulk integral can define different stationary problems under different boundary conditions. Jumping from the pre-integration-by-parts expression to the Euler–Lagrange equation silently selects a class of admissible variations. A numerical implementation using a finite box, vacuum region, truncated Coulomb interaction, or nonperiodic boundary must also check whether its discrete boundary treatment matches the continuous derivation.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

<BilingualSection titleZh="只依赖梯度模长的情形" titleEn="Dependence on the gradient magnitude" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 + 补充澄清 | Martin source + clarification】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

若被积函数写成 $g(n,s,\mathbf r)$，其中

$$
s=|\nabla n|,
$$

则在 $s\ne0$ 处

$$
\frac{\partial s}{\partial(\nabla n)}
=
\frac{\nabla n}{|\nabla n|},
$$

由链式法则

$$
\frac{\partial g}{\partial(\nabla n)}
=
\frac{\partial g}{\partial s}
\frac{\nabla n}{|\nabla n|}.
$$

因此

$$
\frac{\delta F}{\delta n}
=
\frac{\partial g}{\partial n}
-
\nabla\cdot\left(
\frac{\partial g}{\partial s}
\frac{\nabla n}{|\nabla n|}
\right),
$$

并保留前述边界条件。$|\nabla n|=0$ 时，单位向量表达式表面上奇异。具体泛函若在均匀极限可微，其组合可能有有限极限；实现仍应通过解析重写、极限展开或以 $q=|\nabla n|^2$ 为变量来避免直接除零，而不是把 NaN 当作物理不连续。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

If the integrand is written as $g(n,s,\mathbf r)$ with

$$
s=|\nabla n|,
$$

then, where $s\ne0$,

$$
\frac{\partial s}{\partial(\nabla n)}
=
\frac{\nabla n}{|\nabla n|},
$$

and the chain rule gives

$$
\frac{\partial g}{\partial(\nabla n)}
=
\frac{\partial g}{\partial s}
\frac{\nabla n}{|\nabla n|}.
$$

Thus

$$
\frac{\delta F}{\delta n}
=
\frac{\partial g}{\partial n}
-
\nabla\cdot\left(
\frac{\partial g}{\partial s}
\frac{\nabla n}{|\nabla n|}
\right),
$$

subject to the boundary conditions already stated. At $|\nabla n|=0$ the unit-vector form appears singular. A concrete functional that is differentiable in the uniform limit may have a finite combined limit; an implementation should still use analytic rearrangement, a limiting expansion, or $q=|\nabla n|^2$ as the variable to avoid direct division by zero. A NaN is not evidence of a physical discontinuity.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="交换—关联能的 DFT 连接" titleEn="Connection to a DFT exchange-correlation energy" eyebrow="【推断连接 | DFT connection】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

一个梯度依赖的交换—关联能可概括写成

$$
E_{\mathrm{xc}}[n]
=
\int_\Omega
n(\mathbf r)\,
\varepsilon_{\mathrm{xc}}
\bigl(n(\mathbf r),|\nabla n(\mathbf r)|\bigr)
\,d\mathbf r.
$$

令 $f=n\varepsilon_{\mathrm{xc}}$，交换—关联势是

$$
v_{\mathrm{xc}}(\mathbf r)
=
\frac{\delta E_{\mathrm{xc}}}{\delta n(\mathbf r)}
=
f_n-\nabla\cdot f_{\nabla n},
$$

前提是定义域、可微性和边界项处理满足上述要求。这个公式解释为什么 GGA 势不仅是每个网格点上的能量密度偏导，还含一个散度项。它不说明某个具体 GGA 的参数、精确约束或材料精度；这些属于 Appendix B 与 Chapters 8–9。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A gradient-dependent exchange-correlation energy may be written schematically as

$$
E_{\mathrm{xc}}[n]
=
\int_\Omega
n(\mathbf r)\,
\varepsilon_{\mathrm{xc}}
\bigl(n(\mathbf r),|\nabla n(\mathbf r)|\bigr)
\,d\mathbf r.
$$

With $f=n\varepsilon_{\mathrm{xc}}$, the exchange-correlation potential is

$$
v_{\mathrm{xc}}(\mathbf r)
=
\frac{\delta E_{\mathrm{xc}}}{\delta n(\mathbf r)}
=
f_n-\nabla\cdot f_{\nabla n},
$$

provided the domain, differentiability, and boundary treatment meet the preceding requirements. The formula explains why a GGA potential contains a divergence term rather than only a pointwise derivative of an energy density. It does not specify the parameters, exact constraints, or material accuracy of a particular GGA; those belong to Appendix B and Chapters 8–9.

<BilingualSection titleZh="连续关系与离散算法必须分开" titleEn="Continuous identity versus discrete algorithm" eyebrow="【原创教学模型 | Original implementation note】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

在网格上令 $\mathbf n$ 为节点值、$D$ 为离散梯度、$W$ 为积分权重矩阵，并定义

$$
F_h(\mathbf n)
=
\sum_i w_i f(n_i,(D\mathbf n)_i).
$$

对离散向量直接求导得到

$$
\nabla_{\mathbf n}F_h
=
W\mathbf f_n
+
D^{\mathsf T}W\mathbf f_{Dn},
$$

符号取决于 $D$ 的定义和边界闭合。连续式中的 $-\nabla\cdot$ 在离散系统中对应“离散梯度的加权负伴随”，而不是逐点把连续散度公式再采样一次。周期 Fourier 网格、有限差分、有限元和非正交基会给出不同矩阵表示，但都应满足相应的离散分部积分或 summation-by-parts 关系。

验证至少包括：常数密度极限、周期边界总散度积分、解析可导测试函数、网格加密、有限差分方向导数与解析离散梯度的一致性，以及零梯度处无 NaN/Infinity。通过一次 SCF 并不能替代这些导数检查。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

On a grid, let $\mathbf n$ contain nodal values, $D$ be a discrete gradient, and $W$ be the quadrature-weight matrix. Define

$$
F_h(\mathbf n)
=
\sum_i w_i f(n_i,(D\mathbf n)_i).
$$

Direct differentiation of this finite-dimensional function gives

$$
\nabla_{\mathbf n}F_h
=
W\mathbf f_n
+
D^{\mathsf T}W\mathbf f_{Dn},
$$

with the sign determined by the definition of $D$ and its boundary closure. The continuous $-\nabla\cdot$ becomes the weighted negative adjoint of the discrete gradient; it is not obtained reliably by merely sampling the continuous divergence formula point by point. Periodic Fourier grids, finite differences, finite elements, and nonorthogonal bases have different matrix representations, but each should satisfy its corresponding discrete integration-by-parts or summation-by-parts relation.

Minimum checks include the constant-density limit, the integral of a periodic divergence, analytic test functions, grid refinement, agreement between finite-difference directional derivatives and the analytic discrete gradient, and protection against NaN/Infinity at zero gradient. One successful SCF calculation does not replace these derivative checks.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>
