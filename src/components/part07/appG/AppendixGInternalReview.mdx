## G.4 内应变 | Internal Strain

<BilingualSection titleZh="homogeneous cell strain 不决定所有原子位置" titleEn="A homogeneous cell strain does not determine every atomic position" eyebrow="【Martin G.4 | Martin G.4】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

前几节的 homogeneous scaling 同时变换电子坐标、核位置和 cell vectors。它给出 clamped fractional coordinates 下的 stress derivative，但实际测量或 fully relaxed calculation 还要求每个原子在 strained cell 中满足

$$
\mathbf F_I(\boldsymbol\epsilon,\{\mathbf R_J\})=0.
$$

只有 one-atom primitive cell 或某些高对称 strain 下，内部坐标才由 symmetry 完全固定。一般多原子 cell 会沿允许的 optical/internal modes 位移。若只缩放 cell 而不重新 relax ions，得到的是 clamped-ion stress 或 elastic response，不是 relaxed-ion response。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The homogeneous scaling of the preceding sections transforms electronic coordinates, nuclear positions, and cell vectors together. It gives a stress derivative at clamped fractional coordinates, whereas a measured or fully relaxed calculation additionally requires every atom in the strained cell to satisfy

$$
\mathbf F_I(\boldsymbol\epsilon,\{\mathbf R_J\})=0.
$$

Only a one-atom primitive cell or selected high-symmetry strains fix all internal coordinates by symmetry. A general multi-atom cell can move along symmetry-allowed optical or internal modes. Scaling the cell without re-relaxing the ions gives a clamped-ion stress or elastic response rather than a relaxed-ion response.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="总位移分成 affine 与 internal 两部分" titleEn="The total displacement splits into affine and internal parts" eyebrow="【Martin Eq. G.13 | Martin Eq. G.13】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

设 reference basis position 为 $\boldsymbol\tau_s$。应变后的原子位移可写成

$$
u_{s,\alpha}
=\epsilon_{\alpha\beta}\tau_{s,\beta}
+u^{\mathrm{int}}_{s,\alpha}.
$$

第一项是 cell 的 affine scaling；第二项是相对于固定 fractional coordinate 的额外 internal displacement。整体平移是 gauge freedom，通常通过固定 center of mass、固定一个原子或使用 acoustic sum rule 去除。只有相对 internal displacements 影响能量。

internal coordinate 的定义依赖 reference cell 和 atom mapping。跨越 phase transition、原子交换或 cell reorientation 时，简单地相减 fractional coordinates 可能产生 branch jump，需要先建立连续映射。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Let the reference basis position be $\boldsymbol\tau_s$. The displacement of atom $s$ under strain can be written as

$$
u_{s,\alpha}
=\epsilon_{\alpha\beta}\tau_{s,\beta}
+u^{\mathrm{int}}_{s,\alpha}.
$$

The first term is the affine scaling of the cell; the second is the additional internal displacement relative to fixed fractional coordinates. A uniform translation is a gauge freedom and is commonly removed by fixing the centre of mass, fixing one atom, or enforcing an acoustic sum rule. Only relative internal displacements affect the energy.

The definition of an internal coordinate depends on the reference cell and atom mapping. Across a phase transition, atomic exchange, or cell reorientation, direct subtraction of fractional coordinates can produce branch jumps and must be preceded by a continuous mapping.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="internal-strain tensor 是 strain–force coupling 的结果" titleEn="The internal-strain tensor follows from strain-force coupling" eyebrow="【Martin Eq. G.14 + supplemental Hessian form | Martin Eq. G.14 + supplemental Hessian form】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

在线性区间，Martin 定义

$$
u^{\mathrm{int}}_{s,\gamma}
=\Gamma_{s,\gamma\alpha\beta}\epsilon_{\alpha\beta}.
$$

更一般地，在 equilibrium 附近将 energy 展开为 macroscopic strain $\eta$ 与 internal coordinates $\mathbf q$：

$$
E=E_0+rac{1}{2}\Omega C^{\mathrm{cl}}\eta^2
+\eta\,\boldsymbol\Lambda^T\mathbf q
+\frac{1}{2}\mathbf q^T\mathbf K\mathbf q.
$$

零力条件给出

$$
\mathbf q_{\mathrm{rel}}=-\mathbf K^{-1}\boldsymbol\Lambda\,\eta,
$$

所以 $\Gamma$ 本质上由 force-constant matrix 的 inverse 与 strain–internal-coordinate coupling 决定。若 $\mathbf K$ 接近 singular soft mode，internal response 可很大，linear approximation 也更容易失效。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

In the linear regime, Martin defines

$$
u^{\mathrm{int}}_{s,\gamma}
=\Gamma_{s,\gamma\alpha\beta}\epsilon_{\alpha\beta}.
$$

More generally, expand the energy near equilibrium in a macroscopic strain $\eta$ and internal coordinates $\mathbf q$:

$$
E=E_0+rac{1}{2}\Omega C^{\mathrm{cl}}\eta^2
+\eta\,\boldsymbol\Lambda^T\mathbf q
+\frac{1}{2}\mathbf q^T\mathbf K\mathbf q.
$$

The zero-force condition gives

$$
\mathbf q_{\mathrm{rel}}=-\mathbf K^{-1}\boldsymbol\Lambda\,\eta.
$$

Thus the internal-strain tensor is controlled by the inverse force-constant matrix and the strain-internal-coordinate coupling. If $\mathbf K$ approaches a singular soft mode, the internal response can become large and the linear approximation becomes fragile.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="relaxation 总会降低 harmonic elastic curvature" titleEn="Relaxation lowers the harmonic elastic curvature" eyebrow="【补充推导 | Supplemental derivation】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

把 relaxed coordinate 代回 energy：

$$
C^{\mathrm{rel}}
=C^{\mathrm{cl}}
-\frac{1}{\Omega}
\boldsymbol\Lambda^T\mathbf K^{-1}\boldsymbol\Lambda.
$$

若 stable subspace 上 $\mathbf K$ positive definite，第二项 nonnegative，因此 relaxed-ion elastic tensor 在相应 strain channel 上不比 clamped-ion 更硬。多维 tensor 情况中这是 Schur complement；必须先去除 translational zero modes 或使用 pseudoinverse constrained to the optical/internal subspace。

这条关系也解释了为什么 DFPT 中 elastic constants、internal-strain tensors、Born charges 和 piezoelectric response 经常一起出现：它们都涉及 strain、atomic displacement 与 electric boundary 之间的 coupled derivatives。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Substituting the relaxed coordinate back into the energy gives

$$
C^{\mathrm{rel}}
=C^{\mathrm{cl}}
-\frac{1}{\Omega}
\boldsymbol\Lambda^T\mathbf K^{-1}\boldsymbol\Lambda.
$$

If $\mathbf K$ is positive definite on the stable internal subspace, the second term is nonnegative, so the relaxed-ion response cannot be stiffer than the clamped-ion response in the corresponding strain channel. In the multidimensional tensor problem this is a Schur complement; translational zero modes must first be removed or a pseudoinverse must be restricted to the optical or internal subspace.

This relation also explains why DFPT treatments often place elastic constants, internal-strain tensors, Born charges, and piezoelectric response together: all involve coupled derivatives among strain, atomic displacement, and electrical boundary conditions.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="diatomic chain 给出可解析 benchmark" titleEn="A diatomic chain provides an analytic benchmark" eyebrow="【原创模型，受 Martin G.4 启发 | Original model inspired by Martin G.4】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

考虑 cell 内两段 harmonic bonds，reference lengths 为 $R_1,R_2$，spring constants 为 $K_1,K_2$。总长度变化 $\Delta L$ 固定，令两段变化为 $\Delta R_1+\Delta R_2=\Delta L$。零 internal force 给出

$$
K_1\Delta R_1=K_2\Delta R_2,
$$

因此

$$
\Delta R_1=\frac{K_2}{K_1+K_2}\Delta L,
\qquad
\Delta R_2=\frac{K_1}{K_1+K_2}\Delta L,
$$

以及 series effective spring

$$
K_{\mathrm{eff}}=\frac{K_1K_2}{K_1+K_2}.
$$

相对于 affine scaling 的 internal displacement parameter 为

$$
\Gamma=L_0\frac{K_2}{K_1+K_2}-R_1,
\qquad L_0=R_1+R_2.
$$

对称条件 $R_1=R_2$、$K_1=K_2$ 给出 $\Gamma=0$；$K_1\gg K_2$ 时第一段接近不可压缩。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Consider two harmonic bonds in one cell, with reference lengths $R_1,R_2$ and spring constants $K_1,K_2$. The total change $\Delta L$ is fixed and $\Delta R_1+\Delta R_2=\Delta L$. Zero internal force gives

$$
K_1\Delta R_1=K_2\Delta R_2,
$$

so

$$
\Delta R_1=\frac{K_2}{K_1+K_2}\Delta L,
\qquad
\Delta R_2=\frac{K_1}{K_1+K_2}\Delta L,
$$

and the series effective spring is

$$
K_{\mathrm{eff}}=\frac{K_1K_2}{K_1+K_2}.
$$

The internal-displacement parameter relative to affine scaling is

$$
\Gamma=L_0\frac{K_2}{K_1+K_2}-R_1,
\qquad L_0=R_1+R_2.
$$

The symmetric condition $R_1=R_2$ and $K_1=K_2$ gives $\Gamma=0$, while $K_1\gg K_2$ makes the first segment nearly incompressible.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="对称性可禁止或约束 internal strain" titleEn="Symmetry can forbid or constrain internal strain" eyebrow="【Martin G.4 boundary | Martin G.4 boundary】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

one-atom primitive cell 没有非平移 internal coordinate，因此 homogeneous strain 下 internal strain 为零。多原子 cell 也可能因 inversion、mirror、rotation 或 sublattice equivalence 使某些 $\Gamma_{s,\gamma\alpha\beta}$ 分量严格为零。判断必须使用 strained configuration 所保留的 little group，而不是只看 unstrained structure 的完整 space group。

数值上，symmetry-constrained relaxation 若错误地保留了本应被 strain 打破的 operation，会人为压制 internal displacement；反之，关闭所有 symmetry 会放大数值噪声并允许不相关 modes。应记录所施加的 strain、保留的 symmetry subgroup 和自由 internal coordinates。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A one-atom primitive cell has no nontranslational internal coordinate, so its internal strain vanishes under homogeneous strain. A multi-atom cell can also have selected components of $\Gamma_{s,\gamma\alpha\beta}$ forced to zero by inversion, mirror, rotation, or sublattice equivalence. The relevant symmetry is the little group preserved by the strained configuration, not merely the full space group of the unstrained crystal.

In a numerical relaxation, retaining a symmetry operation that the applied strain should break can suppress a genuine internal displacement. Disabling all symmetry can instead amplify numerical noise and allow unrelated modes. The applied strain, preserved symmetry subgroup, and free internal coordinates should therefore be recorded.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="relaxed-ion 计算的最小工作流" titleEn="Minimal relaxed-ion workflow" kind="practice">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

从已完成 force/stress convergence 的 reference cell 出发；对每个正负 strain amplitude 生成 cell；先做 clamped-ion single-point stress，再在固定 strained cell 下弛豫所有允许内部坐标到统一 force threshold；重新计算 final stress 与 energy；分别拟合 clamped/relaxed curves；检查 acoustic translations、symmetry、force-constant conditioning、strain window、$k$ mesh、cutoff 和 SCF。若 cell 也在内部 relaxation 阶段继续变化，就不再是在固定 strain 上测量同一个 elastic derivative。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Start from a reference cell with converged forces and stress. Generate cells at paired positive and negative strain amplitudes; evaluate a clamped-ion single-point stress; relax every allowed internal coordinate at fixed strained cell to one common force threshold; recompute the final stress and energy; fit clamped and relaxed curves separately; and check acoustic translations, symmetry, conditioning of the force-constant matrix, strain window, $k$ mesh, cutoff, and SCF. If the cell continues to change during the internal relaxation, the calculation no longer measures the same fixed-strain elastic derivative.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

<BilingualSection titleZh="十二个高频误判" titleEn="Twelve recurrent misjudgments" eyebrow="【诊断清单 | Diagnostic checklist】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <ol>
      <li>把 Martin 的 internal-stress sign 与 tensile-positive engineering sign 直接比较。</li>
      <li>把 antisymmetric displacement gradient 当成 shear strain。</li>
      <li>认为所有原子力为零就必然 stress 为零。</li>
      <li>用 equilibrium atom 的 total force 替代 pair virial 中的 pair-force contribution。</li>
      <li>只对显式 ionic distance 求导，遗漏 reciprocal vectors、volume 或 electronic terms。</li>
      <li>认为单个 kinetic、Hartree 或 Ewald contribution 的符号决定 total pressure。</li>
      <li>比较不同代码任意分组的 stress components，而不比较 total stress。</li>
      <li>把固定 fractional coordinates 的 elastic constants 当成 relaxed-ion constants。</li>
      <li>在 strained cell 中未重新收敛原子力就报告 internal-strain parameter。</li>
      <li>用单一较大 strain amplitude 拟合 harmonic elastic constant。</li>
      <li>忽略 fixed cutoff 下 plane-wave basis 数目随 strain 改变产生的 Pulay-like noise。</li>
      <li>把 homogeneous elastic stability 当成 full phonon、finite-temperature 或 phase stability。</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <ol>
      <li>Comparing Martin's internal-stress sign directly with a tensile-positive engineering sign.</li>
      <li>Treating an antisymmetric displacement gradient as shear strain.</li>
      <li>Assuming that zero atomic forces imply zero stress.</li>
      <li>Replacing pair-force contributions in a virial by the total force on an equilibrium atom.</li>
      <li>Differentiating only explicit ionic distances and omitting reciprocal vectors, volume, or electronic terms.</li>
      <li>Inferring total pressure from the sign of one kinetic, Hartree, or Ewald contribution.</li>
      <li>Comparing arbitrarily regrouped stress components across codes instead of the total stress.</li>
      <li>Identifying fixed-fractional-coordinate elastic constants with relaxed-ion constants.</li>
      <li>Reporting an internal-strain parameter without re-converging forces in the strained cell.</li>
      <li>Fitting a harmonic elastic constant from one large strain amplitude.</li>
      <li>Ignoring Pulay-like noise when a fixed plane-wave cutoff changes the basis size under strain.</li>
      <li>Equating homogeneous elastic stability with full phonon, finite-temperature, or phase stability.</li>
    </ol>
