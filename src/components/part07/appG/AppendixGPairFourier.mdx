## G.2 两体成对力的应力 | Stress from Two-Body Pair-Wise Forces

<BilingualSection titleZh="中心两体势的 strain derivative" titleEn="Strain derivative of a central pair potential" eyebrow="【Martin Eqs. G.6–G.7 | Martin Eqs. G.6–G.7】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

对相对向量 $\mathbf r_{kk'}=\mathbf r_k-\mathbf r_{k'}$ 施加 homogeneous scaling：

$$
r_{kk',\mu}'=(\delta_{\mu\nu}+\epsilon_{\mu\nu})r_{kk',\nu}.
$$

在零 strain 处

$$
\frac{\partial r_{kk'}}{\partial\epsilon_{\alpha\beta}}
=\frac{r_{kk',\alpha}r_{kk',\beta}}{r_{kk'}}.
$$

若 pair energy 为 $V(r_{kk'})$，则唯一 pair 对内部应力的贡献可写为

$$
\sigma_{\alpha\beta}^{(kk')}
=\frac{1}{\Omega}
\frac{r_{kk',\alpha}r_{kk',\beta}}{r_{kk'}}
\frac{dV}{dr_{kk'}}.
$$

若使用对所有有序 $k\ne k'$ 的双重求和，则前面出现 $1/2$ 以消除 double counting。这就是 Martin G.6–G.7 的 generalized virial。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Apply a homogeneous scaling to the relative vector $\mathbf r_{kk'}=\mathbf r_k-\mathbf r_{k'}$:

$$
r_{kk',\mu}'=(\delta_{\mu\nu}+\epsilon_{\mu\nu})r_{kk',\nu}.
$$

At zero strain,

$$
\frac{\partial r_{kk'}}{\partial\epsilon_{\alpha\beta}}
=\frac{r_{kk',\alpha}r_{kk',\beta}}{r_{kk'}}.
$$

For a pair energy $V(r_{kk'})$, the contribution of one unique pair to the internal stress is

$$
\sigma_{\alpha\beta}^{(kk')}
=\frac{1}{\Omega}
\frac{r_{kk',\alpha}r_{kk',\beta}}{r_{kk'}}
\frac{dV}{dr_{kk'}}.
$$

If all ordered pairs $k\ne k'$ are summed, a factor $1/2$ removes double counting. This is the generalized virial of Martin G.6–G.7.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="pair force contribution 不是平衡原子的 total force" titleEn="A pair-force contribution is not the total force on an equilibrium atom" eyebrow="【Martin G.2 boundary | Martin G.2 boundary】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

定义 $\mathbf F_{kk'}$ 为粒子 $k'$ 对 $k$ 的单个贡献。central force 满足

$$
\mathbf F_{kk'}
=-\frac{dV}{dr_{kk'}}\frac{\mathbf r_{kk'}}{r_{kk'}},
\qquad
\mathbf F_{kk'}=-\mathbf F_{k'k}.
$$

平衡时

$$
\mathbf F_k=\sum_{k'\ne k}\mathbf F_{kk'}=0,
$$

但每个 $\mathbf F_{kk'}$ 通常非零，且 $\sum\mathbf r_{kk'}\otimes\mathbf F_{kk'}$ 也不为零。因此把 virial 中的 pair force 换成 total force 会错误地得到“平衡体系应力恒为零”。原子力平衡与宏观应力平衡是两组独立条件。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Let $\mathbf F_{kk'}$ denote the contribution from particle $k'$ to the force on particle $k$. A central force obeys

$$
\mathbf F_{kk'}
=-\frac{dV}{dr_{kk'}}\frac{\mathbf r_{kk'}}{r_{kk'}},
\qquad
\mathbf F_{kk'}=-\mathbf F_{k'k}.
$$

At equilibrium,

$$
\mathbf F_k=\sum_{k'\ne k}\mathbf F_{kk'}=0,
$$

but the individual $\mathbf F_{kk'}$ are generally nonzero, and so is the sum of dyadics $\sum\mathbf r_{kk'}\otimes\mathbf F_{kk'}$. Replacing pair forces by total forces would therefore produce the false conclusion that every equilibrium system has zero stress. Atomic-force equilibrium and macroscopic-stress equilibrium are independent conditions.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="central pair stress 自动对称" titleEn="Central-pair stress is automatically symmetric" eyebrow="【Martin Eq. G.7 | Martin Eq. G.7】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

G.7 的 tensor 由 $\mathbf r\otimes\mathbf r$ 构成，因此

$$
\sigma_{\alpha\beta}^{\mathrm{pair}}
=\sigma_{\beta\alpha}^{\mathrm{pair}}.
$$

单个 pair contribution 为 rank-one tensor，principal direction 沿 bond，principal value 为 $r(dV/dr)/\Omega$。其 trace 给出 scalar virial contribution，但不同 bond orientations 的求和决定最终 anisotropy。

若 interaction 含 angle、torsion、many-body embedding、nonlocal projector、spin–orbit 或外磁场，不能先假设每一项都可写成 central-pair form。应从完整 energy 对 strain 求导，并检查总 stress 的 symmetry 是否与 angular-momentum balance 和外部场条件一致。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The tensor in G.7 is built from $\mathbf r\otimes\mathbf r$, so

$$
\sigma_{\alpha\beta}^{\mathrm{pair}}
=\sigma_{\beta\alpha}^{\mathrm{pair}}.
$$

One pair gives a rank-one tensor whose principal direction is the bond and whose principal value is $r(dV/dr)/\Omega$. Its trace gives a scalar virial contribution, while the distribution of bond orientations controls the final anisotropy.

For angular, torsional, many-body embedding, nonlocal-projector, spin-orbit, or external-field interactions, one cannot assume that every contribution has a central-pair form. The complete energy must be differentiated with respect to strain, and the symmetry of the total stress must be checked against angular-momentum balance and the stated external-field conditions.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="real-space virial 适用于距离依赖项" titleEn="The real-space virial applies to distance-dependent terms" eyebrow="【Martin G.2 cross-reference | Martin G.2 cross-reference】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

任何显式依赖原子间距的 total-energy term 都可沿 G.7 处理，例如 empirical pair potential、Ewald real-space term，以及 tight-binding/local-orbital 中被参数化为 bond length 的 matrix elements 或 repulsive energy。关键是 derivative 必须作用于该表示中的全部 distance dependence，包括 cutoff function、environment-dependent parameter 和 cell-image selection。

对周期体系，最小镜像或邻居表在 strain 下可能改变。若 cutoff 处不光滑，stress 会出现不连续。production implementation 应使用平滑 cutoff、固定拓扑区间或显式处理 neighbour-list changes，并用 finite-difference energy derivative 检查。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Any total-energy term that depends explicitly on interatomic distance can be treated in the form of G.7, including empirical pair potentials, the real-space part of an Ewald sum, and tight-binding or local-orbital matrix elements and repulsive energies parameterized by bond length. The derivative must act on every distance dependence in that representation, including cutoff functions, environment-dependent parameters, and the selection of periodic images.

In a periodic system, minimum-image assignments or neighbour lists can change under strain. A nonsmooth cutoff then produces a discontinuous stress. A production implementation should use smooth cutoffs, remain within a fixed-topology strain interval, or treat neighbour-list changes explicitly, and it should be checked against finite differences of the energy.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## G.3 Fourier 分量表达式 | Expressions in Fourier Components

<BilingualSection titleZh="实空间伸长对应 reciprocal-space 收缩" titleEn="Real-space stretching implies reciprocal-space contraction" eyebrow="【Martin G.3 | Martin G.3】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

若 direct-lattice matrix 变为 $\mathbf h'=(\mathbf I+\boldsymbol\epsilon)\mathbf h$，reciprocal matrix 满足

$$
\mathbf b'=2\pi(\mathbf h')^{-T}
\simeq(\mathbf I-\boldsymbol\epsilon^T)\mathbf b.
$$

按 Martin 的 index convention 可写

$$
q_\alpha'=(\delta_{\alpha\beta}-\epsilon_{\alpha\beta})q_\beta.
$$

在 direct coordinates 与 reciprocal integer indices 同时随 cell 变换时，相位 $\mathbf G\cdot\mathbf r$ 保持不变，因此 structure factors 与按 cell-normalized convention 定义的 Fourier coefficients 可视为 invariant。strain derivative 主要作用于 $\Omega$、$G^2$、kinetic momentum 和显式 cell-dependent kernels。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

If the direct-lattice matrix becomes $\mathbf h'=(\mathbf I+\boldsymbol\epsilon)\mathbf h$, the reciprocal matrix obeys

$$
\mathbf b'=2\pi(\mathbf h')^{-T}
\simeq(\mathbf I-\boldsymbol\epsilon^T)\mathbf b.
$$

In Martin's index convention this is written

$$
q_\alpha'=(\delta_{\alpha\beta}-\epsilon_{\alpha\beta})q_\beta.
$$

When direct coordinates and reciprocal integer indices co-deform with the cell, the phase $\mathbf G\cdot\mathbf r$ is invariant. Structure factors and Fourier coefficients in the declared cell-normalized convention can therefore be held fixed while the strain derivative acts on $\Omega$, $G^2$, kinetic momenta, and explicitly cell-dependent kernels.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="Hartree stress 的 longitudinal–transverse 结构" titleEn="Longitudinal-transverse structure of the Hartree stress" eyebrow="【Martin Eq. G.8 | Martin Eq. G.8】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

对 neutral periodic density 的 nonzero reciprocal components，Hartree energy 可写为

$$
E_H=\frac{\Omega}{2}\sum_{\mathbf G\ne0}
\frac{4\pi e^2}{G^2}|n(\mathbf G)|^2,
$$

具体 prefactor 随 Fourier normalization 改变。对 strain 求导时既有 $\Omega$ 的导数，也有

$$
\frac{\partial G^2}{\partial\epsilon_{\alpha\beta}}
=-2G_\alpha G_\beta.
$$

组合后得到 Martin G.8 的 tensor factor

$$
\left(2\frac{G_\alpha G_\beta}{G^2}-\delta_{\alpha\beta}\right).
$$

单个 $\mathbf G$ mode 沿 $\mathbf G$ 的 eigenvalue 与两个 transverse eigenvalues 符号相反。完整 sum 的 crystal symmetry 才决定总 Hartree stress。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For the nonzero reciprocal components of a neutral periodic density, the Hartree energy can be written as

$$
E_H=\frac{\Omega}{2}\sum_{\mathbf G\ne0}
\frac{4\pi e^2}{G^2}|n(\mathbf G)|^2,
$$

with prefactors depending on the Fourier normalization. The strain derivative acts on both $\Omega$ and

$$
\frac{\partial G^2}{\partial\epsilon_{\alpha\beta}}
=-2G_\alpha G_\beta.
$$

Their combination produces the tensor factor in Martin G.8,

$$
\left(2\frac{G_\alpha G_\beta}{G^2}-\delta_{\alpha\beta}\right).
$$

For one $\mathbf G$ mode, the eigenvalue along $\mathbf G$ has the opposite sign to the two transverse eigenvalues. The symmetry of the complete reciprocal sum determines the total Hartree stress.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="plane-wave kinetic stress 是 momentum dyadic" titleEn="Plane-wave kinetic stress is a momentum dyadic" eyebrow="【Martin Eqs. G.9–G.10 | Martin Eqs. G.9–G.10】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

plane-wave orbital 的 kinetic energy 含 $|\mathbf k+\mathbf G_m|^2$。由于 derivative operator 与 reciprocal vectors 在 real-space strain 下反向缩放，energy derivative 产生

$$
\sigma_{\alpha\beta}^{\mathrm{kin}}
\propto
\sum_{n\mathbf k m}
|c_{n m}(\mathbf k)|^2
(k+G_m)_\alpha(k+G_m)_\beta.
$$

这是 occupation-weighted momentum dyadic sum，天然 symmetric 且 positive semidefinite before Martin's overall bookkeeping is combined with other terms。仅凭 kinetic contribution 的正负或 trace 不能判断总体系处于 tension 还是 compression，因为 Hartree、ionic、XC、nonlocal 与 basis contributions 可能抵消。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The kinetic energy of a plane-wave orbital contains $|\mathbf k+\mathbf G_m|^2$. Because derivatives and reciprocal vectors contract under a real-space strain, the energy derivative generates

$$
\sigma_{\alpha\beta}^{\mathrm{kin}}
\propto
\sum_{n\mathbf k m}
|c_{n m}(\mathbf k)|^2
(k+G_m)_\alpha(k+G_m)_\beta.
$$

This is an occupation-weighted sum of momentum dyadics, naturally symmetric and positive semidefinite before all terms are assembled under Martin's bookkeeping. The sign or trace of the kinetic term alone does not determine whether the complete system is under tension or compression because Hartree, ionic, XC, nonlocal, and basis contributions can cancel it.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="Ewald stress 必须保持 splitting-parameter cancellation" titleEn="Ewald stress must preserve cancellation of the splitting parameter" eyebrow="【Martin Eqs. G.11–G.12 + Appendix F | Martin Eqs. G.11–G.12 + Appendix F】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Appendix F 把 point-charge Coulomb energy 分成 real-space、reciprocal-space、self 与 background terms。对 strain 求导后，各部分分别产生 tensor contributions；Martin G.11 展示了与 plane-wave total-energy formula 配套的一种 Ewald stress 表达式。式中的收敛参数与 strain tensor 使用相似符号，但它只是数值 splitting parameter。

正确实现应满足：提高 real/reciprocal cutoffs 后，改变 splitting parameter 不改变 total Ewald stress；charged cell 的 background convention 与 $G=0$ treatment 必须和 energy 完全一致；real-space image vectors、reciprocal vectors、volume、self/background terms 都要同时求导。只对 Ewald energy 的一部分求导会留下人工参数依赖。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Appendix F divides the point-charge Coulomb energy into real-space, reciprocal-space, self, and background terms. Differentiating with respect to strain produces a tensor contribution from each part. Martin G.11 gives one Ewald-stress expression matched to a plane-wave total-energy formula. Its convergence parameter resembles the strain symbol but is only a numerical splitting parameter.

A correct implementation must satisfy three checks: after real- and reciprocal-space cutoffs converge, changing the splitting parameter must not change the total Ewald stress; the compensating-background and $G=0$ conventions for a charged cell must match the energy exactly; and real-space image vectors, reciprocal vectors, volume, self terms, and background terms must all be differentiated. Differentiating only part of the Ewald energy leaves an artificial parameter dependence.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="DFT stress 是所选 total-energy representation 的完整导数" titleEn="DFT stress is the complete derivative of the chosen total-energy representation" eyebrow="【Martin G.3 + implementation boundary | Martin G.3 + implementation boundary】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Kohn–Sham potential 不是由可辨认的“另一粒子位置”直接生成，因此不能把全部 DFT stress 都压缩成 classical pair virial。实际方法是逐项 differentiate total-energy functional：kinetic、Hartree、exchange–correlation、local ionic、nonlocal projector、ion–ion/Ewald、augmentation、entropy/occupation，以及 basis/grid dependence。

不同代码可通过积分分部、Poisson equation、stationarity 或 neutral-atom rearrangement 把 contributions 重新分组，所以 term-by-term 数字通常不具唯一可比性。可比对象是同一 Hamiltonian、同一 boundary、同一 pseudopotential/basis convention 下的 total stress，以及它与 total-energy finite difference 的一致性。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A Kohn-Sham potential is not generated directly by identifiable positions of other particles, so the complete DFT stress cannot be reduced to a classical pair virial. In practice, every term in the selected total-energy functional is differentiated: kinetic, Hartree, exchange-correlation, local ionic, nonlocal projector, ion-ion or Ewald, augmentation, entropy or occupation, and basis or grid dependence.

Different codes can regroup these contributions through integration by parts, the Poisson equation, stationarity, or neutral-atom rearrangements. Term-by-term numbers are therefore not uniquely comparable. The comparable object is the total stress for the same Hamiltonian, boundary, and pseudopotential or basis convention, together with agreement against a finite difference of the total energy.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="Fourier-space stress 的常见遗漏" titleEn="Common omissions in Fourier-space stress" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

不要遗漏 reciprocal-vector scaling、volume derivative、$k$-point/occupation convention、nonlocal projector metric、augmentation compensation charge、charged-cell background、finite electronic-temperature entropy、basis cutoff 随 cell 的变化或 Pulay correction。对 plane-wave fixed-energy cutoff，strain 会改变纳入 basis 的 $\mathbf G$ 集合，产生离散 Pulay-like noise；需要更高 cutoff 或 constant-basis strategy 评估。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Do not omit reciprocal-vector scaling, the volume derivative, $k$-point and occupation conventions, nonlocal-projector metrics, augmentation compensation charges, charged-cell backgrounds, finite-electronic-temperature entropy, changes of a basis cutoff with the cell, or Pulay corrections. With a fixed plane-wave energy cutoff, strain can change the discrete set of included $\mathbf G$ vectors and generate Pulay-like noise; higher cutoffs or a constant-basis strategy are required to assess it.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>
