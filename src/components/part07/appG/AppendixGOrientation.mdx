<BilingualSection titleZh="本附录解决什么问题" titleEn="What this Appendix resolves" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 + 课程组织 | Martin source + course organization】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Appendix G 把“晶胞形状改变时总能如何变化”组织成可计算的宏观应力。它连接四个层级：几何上的 displacement gradient 与 symmetric strain；热力学上的 energy derivative 与 stress；微观相互作用中的 pair virial、Hartree、kinetic 和 Ewald contributions；以及应变下原子内部坐标重新弛豫产生的 internal strain。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Appendix G organizes the change of total energy under a change of cell shape into a computable macroscopic stress. It connects four levels: the displacement gradient and symmetric strain in geometry; energy derivatives and stress in thermodynamics; pair-virial, Hartree, kinetic, and Ewald contributions at the microscopic level; and internal strain generated when atomic coordinates relax under an imposed macroscopic strain.

The Appendix is also the common reference layer for plane-wave and local-orbital stresses in Chapters 13–15, variable-cell dynamics in Chapter 19, elasticity and long-wavelength phonons in Chapter 20, Ewald electrostatics in Appendix F, local stress densities in Appendix H, and alternative force expressions in Appendix I.

## 对象、符号与统一约定 | Objects, Symbols, and Conventions

<BilingualSection titleZh="内部应力的符号固定为 Martin G.4" titleEn="The internal-stress sign is fixed to Martin G.4" eyebrow="【Martin Eq. G.4 | Martin Eq. G.4】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

本附录采用

$$
\sigma_{\alpha\beta}^{\mathrm{int}}
=-\frac{1}{\Omega}\frac{\partial E_{\mathrm{total}}}{\partial u_{\alpha\beta}},
$$

其中 $u_{\alpha\beta}$ 是 symmetric small strain，$\Omega$ 是 reference/current volume，具体选择必须随 finite-strain convention 声明。正的 expansive strain 若提高能量，则内部 restoring stress 为负。平衡条件是

$$
\sigma^{\mathrm{int}}=\sigma^{\mathrm{ext}},
$$

但有些工程与代码文档把 tensile stress 定为正并直接写 $+\Omega^{-1}\partial E/\partial u$。比较数值前必须先转换符号，而不是把差一个负号判成物理冲突。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

This Appendix uses

$$
\sigma_{\alpha\beta}^{\mathrm{int}}
=-\frac{1}{\Omega}\frac{\partial E_{\mathrm{total}}}{\partial u_{\alpha\beta}},
$$

where $u_{\alpha\beta}$ is the symmetric small strain and $\Omega$ is a reference or current volume that must be declared with the finite-strain convention. If positive expansive strain raises the energy, the restoring internal stress is negative. Mechanical equilibrium requires

$$
\sigma^{\mathrm{int}}=\sigma^{\mathrm{ext}}.
$$

Some engineering and code conventions instead define tensile stress as positive and write $+\Omega^{-1}\partial E/\partial u$. Numerical comparisons must convert the sign before interpreting a minus sign as a physical disagreement.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="压力是应力 trace 的特例" titleEn="Pressure is the trace special case of stress" eyebrow="【Martin G.1 | Martin G.1】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

在本符号下 hydrostatic pressure 为

$$
P=-\frac{1}{3}\operatorname{Tr}\boldsymbol\sigma^{\mathrm{int}}.
$$

各向同性压缩状态对应 $\sigma_{xx}=\sigma_{yy}=\sigma_{zz}<0$，因此 $P>0$。一个标量 pressure 只保留 trace；它丢弃 deviatoric normal stress 和 shear stress。晶体即使原子力全部为零，也可在外加 shear stress 下处于平衡，因此“forces converged”不能替代 cell-stress convergence。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

With this sign convention, hydrostatic pressure is

$$
P=-\frac{1}{3}\operatorname{Tr}\boldsymbol\sigma^{\mathrm{int}}.
$$

An isotropically compressed state has $\sigma_{xx}=\sigma_{yy}=\sigma_{zz}<0$ and therefore $P>0$. A scalar pressure retains only the trace and discards deviatoric normal stress and shear stress. A crystal can have every atomic force equal to zero while remaining in equilibrium under an applied shear stress, so converged forces do not replace convergence of the cell stress.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="位移梯度、对称应变与 metric" titleEn="Displacement gradient, symmetric strain, and metric" eyebrow="【Martin Eqs. G.1–G.3 | Martin Eqs. G.1–G.3】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

令位移场为 $\mathbf u(\mathbf r)$。infinitesimal displacement gradient

$$
\epsilon_{\alpha\beta}=\frac{\partial u_\alpha}{\partial r_\beta}
$$

可分为 symmetric strain 与 antisymmetric rotation：

$$
u_{\alpha\beta}
=\frac{1}{2}(\epsilon_{\alpha\beta}+\epsilon_{\beta\alpha}),
\qquad
\omega_{\alpha\beta}
=\frac{1}{2}(\epsilon_{\alpha\beta}-\epsilon_{\beta\alpha}).
$$

到一阶，距离变化只由 $u_{\alpha\beta}$ 决定，metric 为

$$
g_{\alpha\beta}=\delta_{\alpha\beta}+2u_{\alpha\beta}.
$$

antisymmetric part 代表 rigid rotation，不改变内部相对距离。有限变形时 $F=\partial\mathbf r'/\partial\mathbf r$，可选 Green–Lagrange、Euler–Almansi、logarithmic strain 等；应力还要转换到与该 strain measure 共轭的 stress measure。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Let the displacement field be $\mathbf u(\mathbf r)$. The infinitesimal displacement gradient

$$
\epsilon_{\alpha\beta}=\frac{\partial u_\alpha}{\partial r_\beta}
$$

splits into symmetric strain and antisymmetric rotation:

$$
u_{\alpha\beta}
=\frac{1}{2}(\epsilon_{\alpha\beta}+\epsilon_{\beta\alpha}),
\qquad
\omega_{\alpha\beta}
=\frac{1}{2}(\epsilon_{\alpha\beta}-\epsilon_{\beta\alpha}).
$$

To first order, changes in distance depend only on $u_{\alpha\beta}$ and the metric is

$$
g_{\alpha\beta}=\delta_{\alpha\beta}+2u_{\alpha\beta}.
$$

The antisymmetric part is a rigid rotation and does not change internal relative distances. At finite deformation, one introduces $F=\partial\mathbf r'/\partial\mathbf r$ and must choose a Green–Lagrange, Euler–Almansi, logarithmic, or other strain measure together with its conjugate stress measure.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="平衡状态需要两组独立条件" titleEn="Equilibrium requires two independent sets of conditions" eyebrow="【Martin summary + G.1 | Martin summary + G.1】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

凝聚态体系的机械状态不能只用原子坐标描述。固定外界温度和组成后，至少需要同时检查：

$$
\mathbf F_I=-\frac{\partial E}{\partial\mathbf R_I}=0
\quad\text{for every atom }I,
$$

以及

$$
\boldsymbol\sigma^{\mathrm{int}}-\boldsymbol\sigma^{\mathrm{ext}}=0.
$$

第一组决定 cell 内的 internal coordinates，第二组决定 cell vectors 或 macroscopic strain。固定晶胞的 ionic relaxation 只能完成第一组；variable-cell relaxation 若 force 或 stress threshold 过松，也可能只近似完成两组条件。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The mechanical state of condensed matter cannot be described by atomic coordinates alone. At fixed external temperature and composition, at least two independent sets of conditions must be checked:

$$
\mathbf F_I=-\frac{\partial E}{\partial\mathbf R_I}=0
\quad\text{for every atom }I,
$$

and

$$
\boldsymbol\sigma^{\mathrm{int}}-\boldsymbol\sigma^{\mathrm{ext}}=0.
$$

The first set determines the internal coordinates within the cell; the second determines the cell vectors or macroscopic strain. A fixed-cell ionic relaxation completes only the first set. A variable-cell relaxation can also satisfy the two sets only approximately if either the force or stress threshold is loose.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="stress theorem 依赖变分驻定性与表示一致性" titleEn="The stress theorem depends on variational stationarity and representation consistency" eyebrow="【Martin G.1 + generalized force theorem | Martin G.1 + generalized force theorem】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

若电子态在给定 strain 下满足变分驻定性，总能导数可按 generalized force theorem 组织为显式 strain derivative；这与 Hellmann–Feynman force 的逻辑相同。但“波函数自洽”并不自动消除所有附加项。若 basis、integration grid、augmentation region、pseudopotential projector、occupation、$k$ mesh 或 cutoff 随 cell 改变，导数中必须保留相应的 Pulay、metric、grid 或 discretization contributions。

因此 stress implementation 的基本验收不是某一项看起来像 virial，而是完整解析 stress 与 finite-difference total-energy derivative 在同一 strain path、同一数值设置和同一 sign/volume convention 下收敛一致。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

If the electronic state is variationally stationary at a given strain, the total-energy derivative can be organized as an explicit strain derivative through the generalized force theorem, following the same logic as a Hellmann–Feynman force. Electronic self-consistency, however, does not automatically eliminate every additional term. If the basis, integration grid, augmentation regions, pseudopotential projectors, occupations, $k$ mesh, or cutoff change with the cell, the corresponding Pulay, metric, grid, or discretization contributions must remain in the derivative.

The primary acceptance test for a stress implementation is therefore not whether one term resembles a virial. It is whether the complete analytic stress converges to the finite-difference derivative of the total energy along the same strain path, with the same numerical settings and the same sign and volume convention.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>
