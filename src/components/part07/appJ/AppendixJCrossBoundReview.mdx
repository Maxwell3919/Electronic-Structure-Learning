## 相移与散射截面 | Phase Shifts and Scattering Cross Sections

<BilingualSection titleZh="远场相位的物理含义" titleEn="Physical meaning of the far-field phase" eyebrow="【Martin Eq. J.7 | Martin Eq. J.7】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

对 $kr\gg l$，球 Bessel functions 的渐近式给出

$$
j_l(kr)\sim\frac{\sin(kr-l\pi/2)}{kr},\qquad
n_l(kr)\sim-\frac{\cos(kr-l\pi/2)}{kr}.
$$

代入外解并吸收 normalization 后，

$$
\psi_l^{>}(r)\sim
\frac{A_l}{kr}\sin\left(kr-\frac{l\pi}{2}+\delta_l\right).
$$

自由通道的相位是 $kr-l\pi/2$；局域势带来的附加位移就是 $\delta_l(E)$。它编码该通道在势区内积累的延迟或提前，但只有相对于同一自由参考和同一分支的相位差才可比较。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For $kr\gg l$, the spherical Bessel functions have the asymptotic forms

$$
j_l(kr)\sim\frac{\sin(kr-l\pi/2)}{kr},\qquad
n_l(kr)\sim-\frac{\cos(kr-l\pi/2)}{kr}.
$$

Substitution into the exterior solution, with normalization absorbed, gives

$$
\psi_l^{>}(r)\sim
\frac{A_l}{kr}\sin\left(kr-\frac{l\pi}{2}+\delta_l\right).
$$

The free-channel phase is $kr-l\pi/2$, and the additional displacement generated by the localized potential is $\delta_l(E)$. It encodes delay or advance accumulated inside the potential region, but only phases referenced to the same free solution and branch can be compared.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="从 S 矩阵到散射振幅" titleEn="From the S matrix to the scattering amplitude" eyebrow="【Martin Eq. J.8 | Martin Eq. J.8】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

弹性单通道的部分波 S 矩阵为

$$
S_l=e^{2i\delta_l},\qquad |S_l|=1.
$$

利用

$$
\frac{S_l-1}{2i}=e^{i\delta_l}\sin\delta_l,
$$

总波函数的远场可写成入射平面波加 outgoing spherical wave：

$$
\Psi(\mathbf r)\sim e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf r}
+\frac{e^{ikr}}{r}f(\theta),
$$

其中

$$
f(\theta)=\frac1k\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}
(2l+1)e^{i\delta_l}\sin\delta_l\,P_l(\cos\theta).
$$

若存在吸收或非弹性通道，$|S_l|<1$，单一实相移不足，需要 inelasticity parameter 或完整多通道 S 矩阵。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For one elastic channel, the partial-wave S matrix is

$$
S_l=e^{2i\delta_l},\qquad |S_l|=1.
$$

Using

$$
\frac{S_l-1}{2i}=e^{i\delta_l}\sin\delta_l,
$$

the far field is an incident plane wave plus an outgoing spherical wave,

$$
\Psi(\mathbf r)\sim e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf r}
+\frac{e^{ikr}}{r}f(\theta),
$$

with

$$
f(\theta)=\frac1k\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}
(2l+1)e^{i\delta_l}\sin\delta_l\,P_l(\cos\theta).
$$

If absorption or inelastic channels are present, $|S_l|<1$. A single real phase shift is then insufficient, and an inelasticity parameter or full multichannel S matrix is required.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="微分截面与总截面" titleEn="Differential and total cross sections" eyebrow="【Martin Eqs. J.9–J.10 | Martin Eqs. J.9–J.10】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

outgoing flux 与入射 flux 的比值给出

$$
\frac{d\sigma}{d\Omega}=|f(\theta)|^2.
$$

对角度积分时使用 Legendre orthogonality：

$$
\int d\Omega\,P_l(\cos\theta)P_{l'}(\cos\theta)
=\frac{4\pi}{2l+1}\delta_{ll'}.
$$

不同 $l$ 的干涉项在总角积分中消失，得到

$$
\sigma_{\mathrm{tot}}
=\frac{4\pi}{k^2}\sum_l(2l+1)\sin^2\delta_l.
$$

每个通道贡献非负，并由 unitarity 限制为

$$
\sigma_l\le \frac{4\pi}{k^2}(2l+1),
$$

当 $\delta_l=\pi/2$ 模 $\pi$ 时达到该通道的弹性 unitarity limit。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The ratio of outgoing to incident flux gives

$$
\frac{d\sigma}{d\Omega}=|f(\theta)|^2.
$$

Angular integration uses Legendre orthogonality,

$$
\int d\Omega\,P_l(\cos\theta)P_{l'}(\cos\theta)
=\frac{4\pi}{2l+1}\delta_{ll'}.
$$

Interference between different $l$ channels vanishes in the full angular integral, yielding

$$
\sigma_{\mathrm{tot}}
=\frac{4\pi}{k^2}\sum_l(2l+1)\sin^2\delta_l.
$$

Each channel contribution is nonnegative and bounded by elastic unitarity,

$$
\sigma_l\le \frac{4\pi}{k^2}(2l+1),
$$

with equality when $\delta_l=\pi/2$ modulo $\pi$.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="光学定理是同一部分波代数的前向检查" titleEn="The optical theorem is a forward-scattering check of the same algebra" eyebrow="【补充验收 | Supplemental acceptance】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

在 $\theta=0$ 时 $P_l(1)=1$，因此

$$
\operatorname{Im}f(0)
=\frac1k\sum_l(2l+1)\sin^2\delta_l,
$$

从而

$$
\sigma_{\mathrm{tot}}=\frac{4\pi}{k}\operatorname{Im}f(0).
$$

这是 J.9–J.10 实现的高杠杆检查：相移、$e^{i\delta}$ 因子、$1/k$ normalization 或通道权重任一错误都会破坏一致性。有限 $l_{\max}$ 下也应使用同一截断比较两侧。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

At $\theta=0$, $P_l(1)=1$, so

$$
\operatorname{Im}f(0)
=\frac1k\sum_l(2l+1)\sin^2\delta_l,
$$

and therefore

$$
\sigma_{\mathrm{tot}}=\frac{4\pi}{k}\operatorname{Im}f(0).
$$

This is a high-leverage implementation check for J.9–J.10. An error in the phase, the $e^{i\delta}$ factor, the $1/k$ normalization, or the channel weight breaks the identity. With a finite $l_{\max}$, both sides must be compared using the same truncation.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="总截面不直接给出电阻率" titleEn="The total cross section does not directly give resistivity" eyebrow="【Summary connection + boundary | Summary connection + boundary】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

电输运抑制 forward scattering，常见球对称弹性输运截面为

$$
\sigma_{\mathrm{tr}}
=\int d\Omega\,(1-\cos\theta)\frac{d\sigma}{d\Omega}
=\frac{4\pi}{k^2}\sum_l(l+1)
\sin^2(\delta_{l+1}-\delta_l).
$$

即使得到 $\sigma_{\mathrm{tr}}$，电阻率还需要散射体密度、载流子速度、费米面平均、multiple scattering、screening 和可能的 inelastic channels。Appendix J 的单站点相移是输入层，不是最终输运 observable。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Electrical transport suppresses forward scattering. A common elastic spherical transport cross section is

$$
\sigma_{\mathrm{tr}}
=\int d\Omega\,(1-\cos\theta)\frac{d\sigma}{d\Omega}
=\frac{4\pi}{k^2}\sum_l(l+1)
\sin^2(\delta_{l+1}-\delta_l).
$$

Even after $\sigma_{\mathrm{tr}}$ is known, resistivity requires the scatterer density, carrier velocity, Fermi-surface averaging, multiple scattering, screening, and possible inelastic channels. The single-site phase shifts of Appendix J are an input layer rather than a final transport observable.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## 负能与束缚态极限 | Negative Energy and the Bound-State Limit

<BilingualSection titleZh="负能时外部解必须指数衰减" titleEn="At negative energy the exterior solution must decay exponentially" eyebrow="【Martin J.1 negative-energy paragraph | Negative-energy paragraph in Martin J.1】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

令 $E=-B<0$ 且外部势为零，定义

$$
\kappa=\sqrt{2B}>0.
$$

外部 radial equation 的可接受解必须在无穷远衰减。可用 modified spherical Bessel function $k_l(\kappa r)$ 表示，也可通过 outgoing Hankel function 的 imaginary-wave-number analytic continuation 获得。增长的独立解必须舍弃，否则 wavefunction 不可归一化。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Let $E=-B<0$ with zero exterior potential and define

$$
\kappa=\sqrt{2B}>0.
$$

The acceptable exterior radial solution must decay at infinity. It can be written with the modified spherical Bessel function $k_l(\kappa r)$ or obtained by analytic continuation of the outgoing Hankel function to imaginary wave number. The independent growing solution must be discarded because it is not normalizable.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="束缚态是内解与纯衰减外解的匹配" titleEn="A bound state matches the inner solution to a purely decaying exterior solution" eyebrow="【J.4 analytic continuation | Analytic continuation of J.4】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

正能外解同时含 regular 与 irregular oscillatory components。束缚态要求 analytic continuation 后只保留衰减 Hankel/modified-Bessel combination。等价地，matching determinant 必须为零；在常见相移语言中，对应 scattering amplitude 或 S-matrix 在复能量/复波数平面的 pole。

对 $l=0$ 有限球方阱，$E=-B$ 时

$$
q=\sqrt{2(V_0-B)},\qquad \kappa=\sqrt{2B},
$$

reduced radial functions 为 $u_{<}=\sin(qr)$ 与 $u_{>}=Ae^{-\kappa r}$。在 $r=a$ 匹配值和斜率得到

$$
q\cot(qa)=-\kappa.
$$

第一个 s 波束缚态在 $B\to0^+$ 时满足 $qa=\pi/2$，因而阈值

$$
V_{0,c}=\frac{\pi^2}{8a^2}.
$$

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A positive-energy exterior solution contains both regular and irregular oscillatory components. A bound state requires the analytically continued exterior solution to contain only the decaying Hankel or modified-Bessel combination. Equivalently, the matching determinant vanishes, corresponding in scattering language to a pole of the amplitude or S matrix in the complex energy or wave-number plane.

For an $l=0$ finite spherical well at $E=-B$,

$$
q=\sqrt{2(V_0-B)},\qquad \kappa=\sqrt{2B},
$$

with reduced radial functions $u_{<}=\sin(qr)$ and $u_{>}=Ae^{-\kappa r}$. Matching value and slope at $r=a$ gives

$$
q\cot(qa)=-\kappa.
$$

At the threshold of the first s-wave state, $B\to0^+$ and $qa=\pi/2$, so

$$
V_{0,c}=\frac{\pi^2}{8a^2}.
$$

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <p>正能实轴上 $\delta_l$ 穿过 $\pi/2$、$\sin^2\delta_l$ 接近 1 或截面出现峰，可能表示 resonance，但不自动表示 normalizable bound state。束缚态需要 $E<0$、纯衰减外解和匹配 determinant 的零点。virtual state、shape resonance 和 true bound state 对应不同的复平面 pole 位置。</p>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <p>A phase crossing of $\pi/2$, $\sin^2\delta_l$ near one, or a peak in a positive-energy cross section can indicate a resonance but does not automatically establish a normalizable bound state. A bound state requires $E<0$, a purely decaying exterior solution, and a zero of the matching determinant. Virtual states, shape resonances, and true bound states occupy different pole locations in the complex plane.</p>
  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

<BilingualSection titleZh="十二个高频误判" titleEn="Twelve recurrent misjudgments" eyebrow="【诊断清单 | Diagnostic checklist】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <ol>
      <li>混用 $E=k^2/2$ 与 $E=k^2$ 的单位约定。</li>
      <li>混用 $\psi_l$ 与 $u_l=r\psi_l$ 的对数导数。</li>
      <li>在势仍变化的位置选择匹配半径。</li>
      <li>把 $n_l$ 在原点的奇异性误认为外部解不可用。</li>
      <li>只匹配函数值，不匹配斜率或对数导数。</li>
      <li>把相移主值的 $\pi$ 跳跃解释为物理不连续。</li>
      <li>把 $D_l$ 的节点极点与 scattering resonance 混同。</li>
      <li>忽略 $l_{\max}$ 收敛，直接积分角分布。</li>
      <li>把总截面当作输运截面、寿命或电阻率。</li>
      <li>把 $\delta_l\approx\pi/2$ 或截面峰单独当作束缚态。</li>
      <li>负能时保留指数增长外解。</li>
      <li>把单中心球对称结果直接推广到 nonspherical 或 multichannel scattering。</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <ol>
      <li>Mixing the unit conventions $E=k^2/2$ and $E=k^2$.</li>
      <li>Mixing logarithmic derivatives of $\psi_l$ and $u_l=r\psi_l$.</li>
      <li>Choosing a matching radius where the potential is still varying.</li>
      <li>Treating the singularity of $n_l$ at the origin as a prohibition on its exterior use.</li>
      <li>Matching value without matching slope or logarithmic derivative.</li>
      <li>Interpreting a principal-phase jump of $\pi$ as a physical discontinuity.</li>
      <li>Confusing a nodal pole of $D_l$ with a scattering resonance.</li>
      <li>Integrating an angular distribution without converging $l_{\max}$.</li>
      <li>Treating total cross section as transport cross section, lifetime, or resistivity.</li>
      <li>Using $\delta_l\approx\pi/2$ or a cross-section peak alone as a bound-state criterion.</li>
      <li>Retaining the exponentially growing exterior solution at negative energy.</li>
      <li>Applying a single-centre spherical result directly to nonspherical or multichannel scattering.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>
