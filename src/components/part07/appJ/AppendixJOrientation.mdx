## 使用定位 | Reference Orientation

<BilingualSection titleZh="本附录解决什么问题" titleEn="What this Appendix resolves" eyebrow="【Martin Appendix J | Martin Appendix J】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Appendix J 把一个局域球对称势对电子波的作用压缩为每个角动量通道的相移 $\delta_l(E)$。相移连接三类对象：球内径向 Schrödinger 解及其对数导数；球外 regular/irregular 自由解的混合比例；远场散射振幅与截面。Chapter 11 用这一连接比较全电子势与赝势的散射性质，Chapters 16–17 用它构造 augmented 和 multiple-scattering 方法。

本附录只处理单中心、球对称、弹性、非相对论问题。它不把单站点截面直接等同于金属电阻率，也不把正能共振峰直接等同于负能束缚态。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Appendix J compresses the action of a localized spherical potential on an electronic wave into one phase shift $\delta_l(E)$ for each angular-momentum channel. The phase shift connects three kinds of object: the inner radial Schrödinger solution and its logarithmic derivative; the mixing ratio of regular and irregular free solutions outside the sphere; and the far-field scattering amplitude and cross section. Chapter 11 uses this connection to compare all-electron and pseudopotential scattering, while Chapters 16–17 use it in augmented and multiple-scattering methods.

The Appendix is restricted to a single-centre, spherical, elastic, nonrelativistic problem. It does not identify a single-site cross section with metallic resistivity, nor a positive-energy resonance peak with a negative-energy bound state.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## 对象、单位与相位约定 | Objects, Units, and Phase Conventions

<BilingualSection titleZh="能量—波数约定必须先固定" titleEn="The energy-wave-number convention must be fixed first" eyebrow="【来源审计 | Source audit】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

网站数值模型固定 Hartree 原子单位

$$
\hat H=-\frac12\nabla^2+V,\qquad E=\frac{k^2}{2}
$$

用于外部常势区。Martin 在 J.4 后的文字印刷为 $\kappa^2=\varepsilon$，而 J.7 前又写正能 $\varepsilon=k^2/2$。这两处不能在同一个 Schrödinger normalization 下同时作为同一波数定义。网站保留这一来源差异，并统一用 $E=k^2/2$ 推导数值图；若使用 Rydberg units，则 kinetic operator 与波数关系必须整体重标度。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The numerical models fix Hartree atomic units,

$$
\hat H=-\frac12\nabla^2+V,\qquad E=\frac{k^2}{2},
$$

in the constant exterior region. The text following J.4 prints $\kappa^2=\varepsilon$, whereas the text preceding J.7 gives the positive-energy relation $\varepsilon=k^2/2$. These cannot both define the same wave number under one Schrödinger normalization. The website records the source difference and uses $E=k^2/2$ consistently in its numerical derivations. A Rydberg-unit treatment must rescale the kinetic operator and wave-number relation together.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="三个径向对象不能混用" titleEn="Three radial objects must not be mixed" eyebrow="【Martin J.3 + Chapter 10 | Martin J.3 + Chapter 10】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

球对称势下

$$
\Psi_{lm}(\mathbf r)=i^l\psi_l(r)Y_{lm}(\hat{\mathbf r})
=i^l\frac{\phi_l(r)}{r}Y_{lm}(\hat{\mathbf r}),
$$

其中 $\psi_l$ 是三维 radial factor，$\phi_l=r\psi_l$ 是 reduced radial function。网站定义

$$
D_l(E,r)=r\frac{\psi_l'(r)}{\psi_l(r)}.
$$

若改用 $\phi_l$，则

$$
r\frac{\phi_l'}{\phi_l}=D_l+1.
$$

因此匹配公式中的对数导数、Bessel function 和导数必须属于同一径向约定。把 $D_l[\psi]$ 与 $\phi_l$ 的外解组合会产生一个单位偏移并改变相移。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For a spherical potential,

$$
\Psi_{lm}(\mathbf r)=i^l\psi_l(r)Y_{lm}(\hat{\mathbf r})
=i^l\frac{\phi_l(r)}{r}Y_{lm}(\hat{\mathbf r}),
$$

where $\psi_l$ is the three-dimensional radial factor and $\phi_l=r\psi_l$ is the reduced radial function. The website defines

$$
D_l(E,r)=r\frac{\psi_l'(r)}{\psi_l(r)}.
$$

If the reduced function is used instead,

$$
r\frac{\phi_l'}{\phi_l}=D_l+1.
$$

The logarithmic derivative, Bessel functions, and derivatives in a matching formula must therefore use one radial convention. Combining $D_l[\psi]$ with an exterior expression for $\phi_l$ introduces a unit offset and changes the phase shift.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="相移是模 π 的量" titleEn="A phase shift is defined modulo pi" eyebrow="【J.4–J.10 interpretation | Interpretation of J.4–J.10】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

球外解写成

$$
\psi_l^{>}(r)=C_l\left[j_l(kr)-\tan\delta_l\,n_l(kr)\right].
$$

由于 $\tan(\delta_l+N\pi)=\tan\delta_l$，单个主值分支不是独立 observable。弹性散射中更稳健的对象是

$$
S_l=e^{2i\delta_l},\qquad
\sin^2\delta_l,
$$

以及随能量连续展开后的相移差。绘图程序若直接使用 `atan`，会在分母过零时产生 $\pi$ 跳跃；这需要 phase unwrapping，而不是把跳跃解释为物理不连续。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The exterior solution is written

$$
\psi_l^{>}(r)=C_l\left[j_l(kr)-\tan\delta_l\,n_l(kr)\right].
$$

Because $\tan(\delta_l+N\pi)=\tan\delta_l$, a single principal branch is not an independent observable. More robust elastic-scattering quantities are

$$
S_l=e^{2i\delta_l},\qquad
\sin^2\delta_l,
$$

and phase differences after continuous energy unwrapping. A program that applies `atan` directly produces jumps of $\pi$ when the denominator changes sign. Those jumps require phase unwrapping rather than interpretation as physical discontinuities.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## 平面波的部分波展开 | Partial-Wave Expansion of a Plane Wave

<BilingualSection titleZh="球对称势把角变量分解为独立 l 通道" titleEn="A spherical potential separates the angular variable into independent l channels" eyebrow="【Martin Eqs. J.1–J.3 | Martin Eqs. J.1–J.3】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

平面波在 spherical harmonics 中展开为

$$
e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf r}
=4\pi\sum_{lm}i^l j_l(kr)Y_{lm}^{*}(\hat{\mathbf k})Y_{lm}(\hat{\mathbf r}).
$$

把入射方向选为极轴并使用 addition theorem，可化成

$$
e^{ikr\cos\theta}
=\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}(2l+1)i^l j_l(kr)P_l(\cos\theta).
$$

球对称 Hamiltonian 与 $L^2,L_z$ 对易，因此不同 $l,m$ 通道不耦合；势只改变每个 $l$ 的径向函数和相移。若势不是球对称，单一 $\delta_l$ 不再足够，通常需要耦合通道或完整 scattering matrix。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A plane wave expands in spherical harmonics as

$$
e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf r}
=4\pi\sum_{lm}i^l j_l(kr)Y_{lm}^{*}(\hat{\mathbf k})Y_{lm}(\hat{\mathbf r}).
$$

Choosing the incident direction as the polar axis and applying the addition theorem gives

$$
e^{ikr\cos\theta}
=\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}(2l+1)i^l j_l(kr)P_l(\cos\theta).
$$

A spherical Hamiltonian commutes with $L^2$ and $L_z$, so different $l,m$ channels do not couple. The potential changes only the radial function and phase shift of each $l$. For a nonspherical potential, one phase shift per $l$ is insufficient and a coupled-channel or full scattering matrix is generally required.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="有限 l 截断的收敛尺度" titleEn="Convergence scale of a finite l truncation" eyebrow="【补充数值解释 | Supplemental numerical interpretation】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

在固定 $kr$ 下，$j_l(kr)$ 在 $l$ 明显大于 $kr$ 后快速变小，因此常见经验是至少保留到 $l_{\max}\gtrsim kr$，再以目标误差收敛。这个尺度不是严格通用的散射截断规则：势半径、能量、角分布尖锐程度和所需 observable 都会改变所需通道数。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

At fixed $kr$, $j_l(kr)$ decreases rapidly once $l$ is appreciably larger than $kr$. A common starting estimate is therefore $l_{\max}\gtrsim kr$, followed by convergence to the target error. This is not a universal scattering cutoff rule: the potential radius, energy, angular sharpness, and chosen observable all affect the required number of channels.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>
