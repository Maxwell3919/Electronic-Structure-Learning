## 径向匹配与对数导数 | Radial Matching and the Logarithmic Derivative

<BilingualSection titleZh="球内径向方程与球外自由解" titleEn="Inner radial equation and exterior free solutions" eyebrow="【Martin Eqs. J.3–J.4 | Martin Eqs. J.3–J.4】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

对局域球对称势 $V(r)$，reduced radial function $u_l(r)=r\psi_l(r)$ 在 Hartree 原子单位满足

$$
\left[-\frac12\frac{d^2}{dr^2}
+\frac{l(l+1)}{2r^2}+V(r)\right]u_l(r)=E u_l(r).
$$

原点 regularity 要求 $u_l\propto r^{l+1}$，等价于 $\psi_l\propto r^l$。若 $r\ge a$ 时势已成为常数，并把该常数选为能量零点，则正能外解可写成

$$
\psi_l^{>}(r)
=C_l\left[j_l(kr)-\tan\delta_l(E)\,n_l(kr)\right].
$$

$j_l$ 在原点 regular，$n_l$ irregular；后者在外部区域合法，因为外解不需要延伸到 $r=0$。$C_l$ 只固定 normalization，相移由内外解在 $a$ 的相对混合决定。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For a localized spherical potential $V(r)$, the reduced radial function $u_l(r)=r\psi_l(r)$ obeys, in Hartree atomic units,

$$
\left[-\frac12\frac{d^2}{dr^2}
+\frac{l(l+1)}{2r^2}+V(r)\right]u_l(r)=E u_l(r).
$$

Regularity at the origin requires $u_l\propto r^{l+1}$, or equivalently $\psi_l\propto r^l$. If the potential is constant for $r\ge a$, and that constant defines the energy zero, the positive-energy exterior solution can be written

$$
\psi_l^{>}(r)
=C_l\left[j_l(kr)-\tan\delta_l(E)\,n_l(kr)\right].
$$

The function $j_l$ is regular at the origin and $n_l$ is irregular. The latter is allowed in the exterior region because the exterior solution need not extend to $r=0$. The coefficient $C_l$ fixes normalization, whereas the phase shift is fixed by the relative mixture required to match the inner solution at $a$.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="为什么对数导数消去 normalization" titleEn="Why the logarithmic derivative removes normalization" eyebrow="【Martin Eq. J.5 | Martin Eq. J.5】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

定义

$$
D_l(E,a)=a\frac{\psi_l'(E,a)}{\psi_l(E,a)}
=a\frac{d}{dr}\ln\psi_l(E,r)\bigg|_{r=a}.
$$

若 $\psi_l\to A\psi_l$，$D_l$ 不变，因此它只记录 boundary shape 而不记录幅度。Chapter 11 的赝势 transferability 正是比较 reference energies 附近的 $D_l(E,a)$ 或相移，而不是比较任意归一化的径向函数值。

$D_l$ 在 $\psi_l(a)=0$ 时有极点。跨越节点时主值会从正无穷跳到负无穷；数值插值必须按极点区间处理，不能在极点两侧直接线性拟合。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Define

$$
D_l(E,a)=a\frac{\psi_l'(E,a)}{\psi_l(E,a)}
=a\frac{d}{dr}\ln\psi_l(E,r)\bigg|_{r=a}.
$$

The quantity is unchanged by $\psi_l\to A\psi_l$, so it records the boundary shape without retaining the amplitude. Pseudopotential transferability in Chapter 11 therefore compares $D_l(E,a)$ or phase shifts around reference energies rather than arbitrary normalized radial values.

The logarithmic derivative has a pole when $\psi_l(a)=0$. As a node crosses the boundary, the principal value jumps from positive to negative infinity. Numerical interpolation must respect pole intervals rather than fitting linearly across a pole.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="由边界匹配推导 tan δl" titleEn="Deriving tan delta_l from boundary matching" eyebrow="【Martin Eq. J.6 | Martin Eq. J.6】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

令 $x=ka$，在 $r=a$ 对外解取对数导数：

$$
D_l
=x\frac{j_l'(x)-t_l n_l'(x)}{j_l(x)-t_l n_l(x)},
\qquad t_l\equiv\tan\delta_l.
$$

移项得到

$$
D_l[j_l-t_l n_l]=x[j_l'-t_l n_l'],
$$

因此

$$
\tan\delta_l(E)
=\frac{xj_l'(x)-D_l(E,a)j_l(x)}
{x n_l'(x)-D_l(E,a)n_l(x)}.
$$

这就是 J.6 的统一无量纲形式。若代码使用 reduced radial function $u_l$ 的对数导数，必须先应用 $D_l[u]=D_l[\psi]+1$，否则分子分母都会发生系统偏移。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Let $x=ka$ and take the logarithmic derivative of the exterior solution at $r=a$:

$$
D_l
=x\frac{j_l'(x)-t_l n_l'(x)}{j_l(x)-t_l n_l(x)},
\qquad t_l\equiv\tan\delta_l.
$$

Rearrangement gives

$$
D_l[j_l-t_l n_l]=x[j_l'-t_l n_l'],
$$

and therefore

$$
\tan\delta_l(E)
=\frac{xj_l'(x)-D_l(E,a)j_l(x)}
{x n_l'(x)-D_l(E,a)n_l(x)}.
$$

This is the dimensionless form of J.6. If a code uses the logarithmic derivative of the reduced radial function $u_l$, it must first apply $D_l[u]=D_l[\psi]+1$. Otherwise both numerator and denominator acquire a systematic offset.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="Wronskian 保证匹配公式非平凡" titleEn="The Wronskian keeps the matching relation nontrivial" eyebrow="【补充推导 | Supplemental derivation】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

球 Bessel 与 Neumann functions 满足

$$
j_l(x)n_l'(x)-j_l'(x)n_l(x)=\frac{1}{x^2}.
$$

因此它们在 $x>0$ 线性独立，外解的 regular/irregular mixing 可唯一固定到模 $\pi$ 的相位分支。若 J.6 的分子和分母同时数值接近零，通常表示输入的 $D_l$、径向约定、能量零点或 Bessel 导数不一致，而不是一个普通相移值。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The spherical Bessel and Neumann functions satisfy

$$
j_l(x)n_l'(x)-j_l'(x)n_l(x)=\frac{1}{x^2}.
$$

They are therefore linearly independent for $x>0$, so the regular-irregular mixture fixes the phase shift up to its branch modulo $\pi$. If the numerator and denominator of J.6 are both numerically close to zero, the likely cause is an inconsistency in $D_l$, the radial convention, the energy zero, or the Bessel derivatives rather than an ordinary phase-shift value.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="共振、节点与相移分支" titleEn="Resonances, nodes, and phase branches" eyebrow="【解释边界 | Interpretation boundary】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

当 J.6 的分母穿过零而分子有限时，$\tan\delta_l$ 发散，主值相移穿过 $\pm\pi/2$。这常对应该通道的强散射或 shape resonance，但 resonance identity 还需要能量连续的 unwrapped phase、截面线形和宽度。节点导致的 $D_l$ 极点与散射共振并非同一件事；二者可能在公式中都表现为某个比值发散，必须通过连续能量扫描和外部波条件区分。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

When the denominator of J.6 crosses zero while the numerator remains finite, $\tan\delta_l$ diverges and the principal phase crosses $\pm\pi/2$. This often signals strong scattering or a shape resonance in that channel, but a resonance assignment also requires a continuously unwrapped phase, the cross-section line shape, and a width. A pole of $D_l$ caused by an inner node is not the same as a scattering resonance. Both can make a ratio diverge, so they must be distinguished through an energy scan and the exterior-wave condition.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="匹配计算的最低验收" titleEn="Minimum acceptance for a matching calculation" kind="practice">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <ol>
      <li>声明 Hamiltonian 和 $E$–$k$ 关系。</li>
      <li>声明使用 $\psi_l$ 还是 $u_l=r\psi_l$。</li>
      <li>确认匹配半径外势已为同一个常数。</li>
      <li>分别检查 value 和 slope continuity，而非只检查 $D_l$。</li>
      <li>使用 `atan2` 和能量连续性处理相移分支。</li>
      <li>对半径、积分步长、Bessel evaluator 和 $l_{\max}$ 做收敛检查。</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <ol>
      <li>State the Hamiltonian and the $E$-$k$ relation.</li>
      <li>State whether the radial object is $\psi_l$ or $u_l=r\psi_l$.</li>
      <li>Verify that the potential is the same constant outside the matching radius.</li>
      <li>Check value and slope continuity separately rather than checking only $D_l$.</li>
      <li>Use `atan2` and continuity in energy to manage the phase branch.</li>
      <li>Converge the radius, integration step, Bessel evaluator, and $l_{\max}$.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
