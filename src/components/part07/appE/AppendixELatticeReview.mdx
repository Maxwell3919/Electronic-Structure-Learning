## E.6 晶格对介电响应的贡献 | Lattice Contributions to Dielectric Response

<BilingualSection titleZh="低频介电响应的电子与离子部分" titleEn="Electronic and ionic parts of low-frequency dielectric response" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

在 ionic insulator 中，频率低于电子 excitation、接近 infrared-active phonon 时，ions 的运动对介电响应有显著贡献。此时 electronic dielectric tensor 可近似取低频常数 $\boldsymbol\epsilon_\infty$，而 lattice contribution 由 force constants、masses、mode eigenvectors 和 Born effective charges 决定。

Martin 强调所有 intrinsic quantities 均在固定 macroscopic electric field $\mathbf E_{\mathrm{mac}}$ 下定义。具体实验或 supercell calculation 的宏观场由电极、开路/短路边界、sample shape、surface charge 和 depolarization field 决定，不能从 intrinsic tensor 自动推断。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

In an ionic insulator, ionic motion contributes strongly to the dielectric response at frequencies below electronic excitations and near infrared-active phonons. The electronic dielectric tensor can then be approximated by a low-frequency constant $\boldsymbol\epsilon_\infty$, while the lattice contribution is determined by force constants, masses, mode eigenvectors, and Born effective charges.

Martin emphasizes that all intrinsic quantities are defined at fixed macroscopic electric field $\mathbf E_{\mathrm{mac}}$. The macroscopic field of a specific experiment or supercell calculation is set by electrodes, open- or short-circuit boundaries, sample shape, surface charge, and depolarization fields; it cannot be inferred from an intrinsic tensor alone.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="Born effective charge 是混合响应张量" titleEn="The Born effective charge is a mixed-response tensor" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 + normalization audit | Martin source + normalization audit】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Martin Eq. E.20 定义 ion $I$ 的 Born effective charge tensor：

$$
Z^*_{I,\alpha\beta}|e|
=\left.
\frac{\partial P_\alpha}{\partial R_{I\beta}}
\right|_{\mathbf E_{\mathrm{mac}}}.
$$

它描述“沿 $\beta$ 方向移动 ion $I$ 时，$\alpha$ 方向宏观极化如何变化”，是 mixed polarization-displacement derivative。它不是 Bader、Mulliken、Hirshfeld 或 formal oxidation state，也不要求等于 nominal ionic charge。

若 $P$ 是 polarization density，常见现代 convention 为

$$
Z^*_{I,\alpha\beta}|e|
=\Omega\left.
\frac{\partial P_\alpha}{\partial R_{I\beta}}
\right|_{\mathbf E_{\mathrm{mac}}}.
$$

Martin 的印刷式未显式写出 $\Omega$。因此任何数值比较都必须核对 $P$ 是 cell dipole、polarization density，还是已吸收体积 normalization。acoustic sum rule 还要求 unit-cell 中 Born tensors 对所有 ions 的和在 translationally invariant insulator 中为零；偏离可能来自数值未收敛、charge leakage 或 convention mismatch。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Martin Eq. E.20 defines the Born effective-charge tensor of ion $I$:

$$
Z^*_{I,\alpha\beta}|e|
=\left.
\frac{\partial P_\alpha}{\partial R_{I\beta}}
\right|_{\mathbf E_{\mathrm{mac}}}.
$$

It describes how the macroscopic polarization component $\alpha$ changes when ion $I$ moves along $\beta$. It is a mixed polarization-displacement derivative, not a Bader, Mulliken, Hirshfeld, formal-oxidation, or nominal ionic charge.

If $P$ is a polarization density, a common modern convention is

$$
Z^*_{I,\alpha\beta}|e|
=\Omega\left.
\frac{\partial P_\alpha}{\partial R_{I\beta}}
\right|_{\mathbf E_{\mathrm{mac}}}.
$$

Martin's printed equation does not display $\Omega$ explicitly. Any numerical comparison must therefore identify whether $P$ is a cell dipole, a polarization density, or a quantity with volume normalization already absorbed. The acoustic sum rule also requires the sum of Born tensors over all ions in a translationally invariant insulating cell to vanish; deviations can arise from incomplete convergence, charge leakage, or convention mismatch.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="nonanalytic force constants 与方向依赖" titleEn="Nonanalytic force constants and directional dependence" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

长波 polar displacement 产生宏观 electric field。对 $\mathbf q\to0$、方向 $\hat{\mathbf q}$，force-constant matrix 包含 analytic short-range part 与 nonanalytic Coulomb part：

$$
C_{I\alpha,J\beta}(\mathbf q\to0)
=C^{N}_{I\alpha,J\beta}
+\frac{4\pi e^2}{\Omega}
\frac{
\bigl(\hat{\mathbf q}\cdot\mathbf Z_I^*\bigr)_\alpha^*
\bigl(\hat{\mathbf q}\cdot\mathbf Z_J^*\bigr)_\beta
}{
\hat{\mathbf q}\cdot\boldsymbol\epsilon_\infty\cdot\hat{\mathbf q}
}.
$$

这就是 Martin Eq. E.21 的 tensor structure。分子投影 mode-induced charge，分母是该方向的 electronic screening。$\mathbf q=0$ 点本身没有方向，因此 polar crystal 的 dynamical matrix limit 依赖 approach direction；只报告“Gamma frequency”而不说明 nonanalytic correction 与 direction 不足以确定 LO–TO splitting。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A long-wavelength polar displacement creates a macroscopic electric field. For $\mathbf q\to0$ along $\hat{\mathbf q}$, the force-constant matrix contains an analytic short-range part and a nonanalytic Coulomb part:

$$
C_{I\alpha,J\beta}(\mathbf q\to0)
=C^{N}_{I\alpha,J\beta}
+\frac{4\pi e^2}{\Omega}
\frac{
\bigl(\hat{\mathbf q}\cdot\mathbf Z_I^*\bigr)_\alpha^*
\bigl(\hat{\mathbf q}\cdot\mathbf Z_J^*\bigr)_\beta
}{
\hat{\mathbf q}\cdot\boldsymbol\epsilon_\infty\cdot\hat{\mathbf q}
}.
$$

This is the tensor structure of Martin Eq. E.21. The numerator projects the mode-induced charge and the denominator gives electronic screening along the chosen direction. The point $\mathbf q=0$ itself has no direction, so the dynamical-matrix limit in a polar crystal depends on the approach direction. Reporting a “Gamma frequency” without the nonanalytic correction and direction is insufficient to establish LO–TO splitting.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="单模式 LO–TO 与 Lyddane–Sachs–Teller 关系" titleEn="Single-mode LO–TO splitting and the Lyddane–Sachs–Teller relation" eyebrow="【补充推导 | Supplemental derivation】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

对各向同性、单个 infrared-active mode，忽略 damping 时可写

$$
\epsilon(\omega)
=\epsilon_\infty+
\frac{S}{\omega_{\mathrm{TO}}^2-\omega^2},
\qquad S>0.
$$

transverse resonance 是 denominator 的 pole：$\omega=\omega_{\mathrm{TO}}$。longitudinal collective mode 满足无 external charge 时 $D=\epsilon E=0$ 且 $E\ne0$，因此 $\epsilon(\omega_{\mathrm{LO}})=0$：

$$
\omega_{\mathrm{LO}}^2
=\omega_{\mathrm{TO}}^2+rac{S}{\epsilon_\infty}.
$$

静态介电常数为

$$
\epsilon(0)=\epsilon_\infty+rac{S}{\omega_{\mathrm{TO}}^2}.
$$

消去 $S$ 得到单模式 Lyddane–Sachs–Teller relation：

$$
\boxed{
\frac{\epsilon(0)}{\epsilon_\infty}
=\frac{\omega_{\mathrm{LO}}^2}{\omega_{\mathrm{TO}}^2}
}.
$$

多模式、各向异性、mode mixing 或 damping 情形需要 determinant/eigenvalue generalized relation，不能把每个 mode 独立套入 scalar formula。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For an isotropic system with one infrared-active mode and no damping,

$$
\epsilon(\omega)
=\epsilon_\infty+
\frac{S}{\omega_{\mathrm{TO}}^2-\omega^2},
\qquad S>0.
$$

The transverse resonance is the pole at $\omega=\omega_{\mathrm{TO}}$. A longitudinal collective mode has $D=\epsilon E=0$ with $E\ne0$ in the absence of external charge, so $\epsilon(\omega_{\mathrm{LO}})=0$:

$$
\omega_{\mathrm{LO}}^2
=\omega_{\mathrm{TO}}^2+rac{S}{\epsilon_\infty}.
$$

The static dielectric constant is

$$
\epsilon(0)=\epsilon_\infty+rac{S}{\omega_{\mathrm{TO}}^2}.
$$

Eliminating $S$ gives the single-mode Lyddane–Sachs–Teller relation:

$$
\boxed{
\frac{\epsilon(0)}{\epsilon_\infty}
=\frac{\omega_{\mathrm{LO}}^2}{\omega_{\mathrm{TO}}^2}
}.
$$

Multiple modes, anisotropy, mode mixing, or damping require determinant or eigenvalue generalizations; the scalar relation cannot be applied independently to every mode.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="proper piezoelectric tensor 与 internal strain" titleEn="Proper piezoelectric tensor and internal strain" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

在没有 macroscopic field 的 intrinsic definition 中，Martin Eq. E.22 将 piezoelectric tensor 写为 polarization 对 strain 的导数：

$$
e_{\alpha,\beta\gamma}
=\left.
\frac{dP_\alpha}{du_{\beta\gamma}}
\right|_{\mathbf E_{\mathrm{mac}}}.
$$

应变同时直接改变 electronic polarization，并驱动内部原子 relaxations。Martin Eq. E.23 将 response 分为 pure-strain 与 internal-displacement 两部分：

$$
\boxed{
e_{\alpha,\beta\gamma}
=e^0_{\alpha,\beta\gamma}
+|e|\sum_{s\delta}
Z^*_{s,\alpha\delta}
\Gamma_{s\delta,\beta\gamma}
},
$$

其中 $\Gamma_{s\delta,\beta\gamma}=\partial R_{s\delta}/\partial u_{\beta\gamma}$ 是 internal-strain response，具体 normalization 与 Chapter G 的定义保持一致。

具有 permanent polarization 的晶体还存在 improper contribution：刚体 rotation、cell-volume 或 polarization quantum 的改变可能产生表面上非零的 $dP/du$，却不对应可测 current。proper Berry-phase polarization expression 移除这些纯几何项。比较 piezoelectric tensors 时必须核对 proper/improper、clamped-ion/relaxed-ion、fixed-E/fixed-D 和 strain convention。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For the intrinsic definition without a macroscopic field, Martin Eq. E.22 writes the piezoelectric tensor as the derivative of polarization with respect to strain:

$$
e_{\alpha,\beta\gamma}
=\left.
\frac{dP_\alpha}{du_{\beta\gamma}}
\right|_{\mathbf E_{\mathrm{mac}}}.
$$

Strain changes the electronic polarization directly and also drives internal atomic relaxation. Martin Eq. E.23 separates the response into pure-strain and internal-displacement parts:

$$
\boxed{
e_{\alpha,\beta\gamma}
=e^0_{\alpha,\beta\gamma}
+|e|\sum_{s\delta}
Z^*_{s,\alpha\delta}
\Gamma_{s\delta,\beta\gamma}
},
$$

where $\Gamma_{s\delta,\beta\gamma}=\partial R_{s\delta}/\partial u_{\beta\gamma}$ is the internal-strain response, with normalization consistent with Appendix G.

Crystals with permanent polarization also have improper contributions: rigid rotation, cell-volume changes, or shifts of the polarization quantum can produce a nonzero-looking $dP/du$ without a measurable current. Proper Berry-phase polarization expressions remove these purely geometric terms. A comparison of piezoelectric tensors must identify proper versus improper, clamped-ion versus relaxed-ion, fixed-E versus fixed-D, and the strain convention.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>
