## 使用定位 | Reference Orientation

<BilingualSection titleZh="本附录解决什么问题" titleEn="What this Appendix resolves" eyebrow="【Martin Appendix I | Martin Appendix I】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

通常的 Hellmann–Feynman 推导沿“改变核坐标，同时把电子态暂时固定”的路径求总能导数。Appendix I 指出：若参考电子态对其变分自由度已经驻定，则 first-order generalized force 不依赖于电子变量随参数变化的具体一阶路径。于是可以选择更适合局域 basis、core region、球面边界或 surface flux 的路径，把难处理的体积分改写为 eigenvalue difference、pressure 或 boundary traction。

这种自由只属于一阶导数。不同路径的二阶曲率、有限位移误差、occupation change 和 basis-response terms 可以不同。Appendix I 的公式不是“少算一些项仍然精确”的许可，而是“在完整驻定与边界条件下重新组织同一导数”的方法。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The usual Hellmann–Feynman derivation differentiates the total energy along a path that changes the nuclear coordinate while temporarily holding the electronic state fixed. Appendix I observes that if the reference electronic state is stationary with respect to its variational degrees of freedom, the first-order generalized force is independent of the particular first-order path chosen for the electronic variables. One may therefore select a path adapted to a localized basis, core region, spherical boundary, or surface flux, rewriting difficult volume terms as eigenvalue differences, pressure, or boundary traction.

This freedom applies only to the first derivative. Different paths can have different second-order curvature, finite-displacement error, occupation changes, and basis-response terms. Appendix I is not permission to omit terms while retaining exactness; it is a way to reorganize the same derivative under complete stationarity and boundary conditions.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## 对象、符号与适用边界 | Objects, Symbols, and Boundaries

<BilingualSection titleZh="Generalized force 与符号" titleEn="Generalized force and sign" eyebrow="【符号审计 | Sign audit】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

令外部参数为 $\lambda$。本附录统一定义

$$
\mathcal F_\lambda=-\frac{dE}{d\lambda}.
$$

若 $\lambda=R_{I\alpha}$，则 $\mathcal F_\lambda$ 是原子力分量；若 $\lambda=V$，则 $P=-dE/dV$；若 $\lambda=u_{\alpha\beta}$，则需结合 Appendix G 的 internal-stress sign convention。不能把 force、pressure 和 stress 的负号从一个公式机械复制到另一个公式。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Let the external parameter be $\lambda$. This Appendix defines

$$
\mathcal F_\lambda=-\frac{dE}{d\lambda}.
$$

For $\lambda=R_{I\alpha}$, $\mathcal F_\lambda$ is an atomic-force component. For $\lambda=V$, it gives $P=-dE/dV$. For $\lambda=u_{\alpha\beta}$, it must be combined with the internal-stress sign convention of Appendix G. The minus sign cannot be transferred mechanically among force, pressure, and stress formulas.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="Chain rule 揭示路径自由" titleEn="The chain rule exposes path freedom" eyebrow="【补充推导 | Supplemental derivation】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

把所有电子自由度记为 $x$，总能写成 $E[x(\lambda),\lambda]$。则

$$
\frac{dE}{d\lambda}
=\left.\frac{\partial E}{\partial\lambda}\right|_x
+\left\langle\frac{\delta E}{\delta x},\frac{dx}{d\lambda}\right\rangle.
$$

在参考点 $(x_0,\lambda_0)$，若约束后的 Euler–Lagrange residual 为零，

$$
\left.\frac{\delta E}{\delta x}\right|_{x_0,\lambda_0}=0,
$$

第二项不论选取何种有限 $dx/d\lambda$ 都在一阶消失。若 SCF residual、orthonormality residual、occupation discontinuity 或 moving-basis residual 不为零，则路径项重新进入一阶导数。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Collect all electronic degrees of freedom in $x$ and write the total energy as $E[x(\lambda),\lambda]$. Then

$$
\frac{dE}{d\lambda}
=\left.\frac{\partial E}{\partial\lambda}\right|_x
+\left\langle\frac{\delta E}{\delta x},\frac{dx}{d\lambda}\right\rangle.
$$

At the reference point $(x_0,\lambda_0)$, if the constrained Euler–Lagrange residual vanishes,

$$
\left.\frac{\delta E}{\delta x}\right|_{x_0,\lambda_0}=0,
$$

the second term vanishes to first order for any finite choice of $dx/d\lambda$. If the SCF, orthonormality, occupation, or moving-basis residual is nonzero, path dependence returns at first order.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="四条常见路径不能混名" titleEn="Four common paths must not be conflated" eyebrow="【路径地图 | Path map】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Fixed-electron path：</strong>固定 wavefunction/density，只改变 external potential 或 nuclear coordinate。</li>
      <li><strong>Relaxed path：</strong>每个 $\lambda$ 都重新求驻定电子态，给出真正的 Born–Oppenheimer energy curve。</li>
      <li><strong>Frozen-reference path：</strong>在同一个参考势、密度或 Hamiltonian 上比较两个参数状态，只保证小扰动的一阶准确性。</li>
      <li><strong>Rigid-region path：</strong>让一个包含 core 和附近 charge 的区域随核刚性移动，同时重新分配跨边界项；它是 coordinate/path choice，不是忽略内部能量。</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Fixed-electron path:</strong> hold the wavefunction or density fixed while changing the external potential or nuclear coordinate.</li>
      <li><strong>Relaxed path:</strong> solve for a stationary electronic state at every $\lambda$, producing the actual Born–Oppenheimer energy curve.</li>
      <li><strong>Frozen-reference path:</strong> compare parameter states in the same reference potential, density, or Hamiltonian; this is only first-order accurate for a small perturbation.</li>
      <li><strong>Rigid-region path:</strong> translate a region containing the core and nearby charge rigidly with the nucleus while reallocating terms crossing its boundary. It is a coordinate or path choice, not omission of the internal energy.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>

  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Appendix G 给出 homogeneous strain 的 macroscopic stress；Appendix H 给出 local energy/stress gauge 与 closed-surface invariants；Appendix I 利用同一 stationarity principle 选择 alternative force paths。I 不重新定义 stress gauge，也不把任意 open-surface local stress map升级为原子力。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Appendix G gives macroscopic stress under homogeneous strain. Appendix H establishes local energy and stress gauges and closed-surface invariants. Appendix I uses the same stationarity principle to select alternative force paths. It neither redefines the stress gauge nor promotes an arbitrary open-surface local-stress map to an atomic force.

  </div>
