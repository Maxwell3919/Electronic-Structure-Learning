## B.1 局域自旋密度近似 | Local Spin-Density Approximation

<BilingualSection titleZh="均匀电子气的局域取样" titleEn="Local sampling of the uniform electron gas" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 + 补充推导 | Martin source + supplemental derivation】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

LSDA 在每个位置使用具有相同 $n_\uparrow$ 和 $n_\downarrow$ 的均匀电子气作为参考：

$$
E_{\mathrm{xc}}^{\mathrm{LSDA}}[n_\uparrow,n_\downarrow]
=
\int d^3r\,n(\mathbf r)
\left[
\varepsilon_x^{\mathrm{hom}}(r_s,\zeta)
+
\varepsilon_c^{\mathrm{hom}}(r_s,\zeta)
\right].
$$

交换部分对均匀气是解析的。未极化情况下

$$
\varepsilon_x^{\mathrm{hom}}(n,0)
=-\frac{3}{4}\left(\frac{3}{\pi}\right)^{1/3}n^{1/3}
$$

（Hartree 原子单位）。共线自旋的精确交换自旋缩放可写成

$$
\varepsilon_x(n,\zeta)
=
\varepsilon_x(n,0)
\,f_x(\zeta),
\qquad
f_x(\zeta)
=
\frac{(1+\zeta)^{4/3}+(1-\zeta)^{4/3}}{2}.
$$

$f_x(0)=1$，而完全极化时 $f_x(\pm1)=2^{1/3}$。该关系来自两个自旋通道各自的 $n_\sigma^{4/3}$ 交换能，不是对相关能的通用定理。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

At each position, LSDA uses a uniform electron gas with the same $n_\uparrow$ and $n_\downarrow$ as its reference:

$$
E_{\mathrm{xc}}^{\mathrm{LSDA}}[n_\uparrow,n_\downarrow]
=
\int d^3r\,n(\mathbf r)
\left[
\varepsilon_x^{\mathrm{hom}}(r_s,\zeta)
+
\varepsilon_c^{\mathrm{hom}}(r_s,\zeta)
\right].
$$

Exchange is analytic for the uniform gas. In the unpolarized case,

$$
\varepsilon_x^{\mathrm{hom}}(n,0)
=-\frac{3}{4}\left(\frac{3}{\pi}\right)^{1/3}n^{1/3}
$$

in Hartree atomic units. Exact collinear spin scaling for exchange is

$$
\varepsilon_x(n,\zeta)
=
\varepsilon_x(n,0)
\,f_x(\zeta),
\qquad
f_x(\zeta)
=
\frac{(1+\zeta)^{4/3}+(1-\zeta)^{4/3}}{2}.
$$

The factor satisfies $f_x(0)=1$ and $f_x(\pm1)=2^{1/3}$. It follows from adding the $n_\sigma^{4/3}$ exchange contributions of the two spin channels; it is not a universal theorem for correlation.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## 从相关能到相关势 | From Correlation Energy to Correlation Potential

<BilingualSection titleZh="式 B.1 的逐步推导" titleEn="Step-by-step derivation of Eq. B.1" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 + 补充推导 | Martin source + supplemental derivation】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

对未极化均匀电子气，局域相关能量密度为

$$
e_c(n)=n\varepsilon_c(r_s(n)).
$$

相关势是对密度的普通导数：

$$
V_c(n)=\frac{d}{dn}\left[n\varepsilon_c(r_s(n))\right]
=\varepsilon_c+n\frac{d\varepsilon_c}{dr_s}\frac{dr_s}{dn}.
$$

由 $r_s\propto n^{-1/3}$，

$$
\frac{dr_s}{dn}=-\frac{r_s}{3n}.
$$

代回得到 Martin 式 B.1：

$$
\boxed{
V_c(r_s)
=
\varepsilon_c(r_s)
-
\frac{r_s}{3}
\frac{d\varepsilon_c(r_s)}{dr_s}
}.
$$

第一项来自显式的密度因子 $n$，第二项来自 $r_s$ 随密度变化。只对 $\varepsilon_c$ 求导会漏掉第一项；把 $d\varepsilon_c/dr_s$ 直接当作势也会漏掉链式法则中的 $dr_s/dn$。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For an unpolarized uniform electron gas, the local correlation energy density is

$$
e_c(n)=n\varepsilon_c(r_s(n)).
$$

The correlation potential is its derivative with respect to density:

$$
V_c(n)=\frac{d}{dn}\left[n\varepsilon_c(r_s(n))\right]
=\varepsilon_c+n\frac{d\varepsilon_c}{dr_s}\frac{dr_s}{dn}.
$$

Since $r_s\propto n^{-1/3}$,

$$
\frac{dr_s}{dn}=-\frac{r_s}{3n}.
$$

Substitution gives Martin's Eq. B.1,

$$
\boxed{
V_c(r_s)
=
\varepsilon_c(r_s)
-
\frac{r_s}{3}
\frac{d\varepsilon_c(r_s)}{dr_s}
}.
$$

The first term comes from the explicit factor $n$, while the second arises because $r_s$ changes with density. Differentiating $\varepsilon_c$ alone loses the first term; identifying $d\varepsilon_c/dr_s$ directly with a potential also omits the chain rule through $dr_s/dn$.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="单位检查" titleEn="Dimensional check" kind="derivation">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

$r_s$ 无量纲，因此 $d\varepsilon_c/dr_s$ 与 $\varepsilon_c$ 都具有能量单位，式 B.1 的两项可以相加。若相关参数化以 Rydberg 而代码以 Hartree 工作，能量与势必须同时转换；只转换能量而保留旧势会破坏变分一致性。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Because $r_s$ is dimensionless, both $d\varepsilon_c/dr_s$ and $\varepsilon_c$ have units of energy, so the two terms in Eq. B.1 are commensurate. If a correlation parametrization is tabulated in Rydberg while a code uses Hartree, both energy and potential must be converted. Converting the energy alone breaks variational consistency.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## 代表性相关参数化 | Representative Correlation Parametrizations

<BilingualSection titleZh="Hedin–Lundqvist 形式" titleEn="Hedin-Lundqvist form" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

对未极化情况，Martin 记录 Hedin–Lundqvist 形式

$$
\varepsilon_c^{\mathrm{HL}}(r_s)
=-\frac{C}{2}
\left[
(1+x^3)\ln\left(1+\frac{1}{x}\right)
+\frac{x}{2}-x^2-\frac{1}{3}
\right],
$$

其中 $A=21$、$C=0.045$、$x=r_s/A$，并采用原子单位能量尺度。对该表达求导并代入式 B.1，可化简为

$$
V_c^{\mathrm{HL}}(r_s)
=-\frac{C}{2}\ln\left(1+\frac{1}{x}\right).
$$

这里的简洁势依赖于能量式中各项的精确组合；若更改常数、单位或舍入方式，应重新从能量表达求导，而不是独立复制势公式。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For the unpolarized gas, Martin lists the Hedin-Lundqvist form

$$
\varepsilon_c^{\mathrm{HL}}(r_s)
=-\frac{C}{2}
\left[
(1+x^3)\ln\left(1+\frac{1}{x}\right)
+\frac{x}{2}-x^2-\frac{1}{3}
\right],
$$

where $A=21$, $C=0.045$, and $x=r_s/A$, with the energy scale expressed in atomic units. Differentiation and substitution into Eq. B.1 reduce the potential to

$$
V_c^{\mathrm{HL}}(r_s)
=-\frac{C}{2}\ln\left(1+\frac{1}{x}\right).
$$

The compact potential relies on the exact combination of terms in the energy. If constants, units, or rounding are changed, the potential should be re-derived from the energy rather than copied independently.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="Perdew–Zunger 分段形式" titleEn="Perdew-Zunger piecewise form" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 + 补充导数 | Martin source + supplemental derivative】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Martin 给出的未极化 PZ 相关能为

$$
\varepsilon_c^{\mathrm{PZ}}(r_s)
=
\begin{cases}
-0.0480+0.0311\ln r_s-0.0116r_s+0.0020r_s\ln r_s,
& r_s<1,\\[4pt]
-\dfrac{0.1423}{1+1.0529\sqrt{r_s}+0.3334r_s},
& r_s>1.
\end{cases}
$$

高密度支的导数为

$$
\frac{d\varepsilon_c}{dr_s}
=
\frac{0.0311}{r_s}-0.0116+0.0020(\ln r_s+1),
$$

低密度支则令

$$
D(r_s)=1+1.0529\sqrt{r_s}+0.3334r_s,
$$

得到

$$
\frac{d\varepsilon_c}{dr_s}
=
\frac{0.1423}{D(r_s)^2}
\left[
\frac{1.0529}{2\sqrt{r_s}}+0.3334
\right].
$$

两支分别代入式 B.1 即得势。印刷系数只有有限位数，因此在 $r_s=1$ 处能量值约相差 $3.2\times10^{-5}$ Hartree；这属于舍入层面的实现边界。生产代码应说明采用的原始参数表、分界点处理以及能量和一阶导数是否连续。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Martin gives the unpolarized PZ correlation energy as

$$
\varepsilon_c^{\mathrm{PZ}}(r_s)
=
\begin{cases}
-0.0480+0.0311\ln r_s-0.0116r_s+0.0020r_s\ln r_s,
& r_s<1,\\[4pt]
-\dfrac{0.1423}{1+1.0529\sqrt{r_s}+0.3334r_s},
& r_s>1.
\end{cases}
$$

The derivative of the high-density branch is

$$
\frac{d\varepsilon_c}{dr_s}
=
\frac{0.0311}{r_s}-0.0116+0.0020(\ln r_s+1),
$$

while the low-density branch can be written with

$$
D(r_s)=1+1.0529\sqrt{r_s}+0.3334r_s
$$

to give

$$
\frac{d\varepsilon_c}{dr_s}
=
\frac{0.1423}{D(r_s)^2}
\left[
\frac{1.0529}{2\sqrt{r_s}}+0.3334
\right].
$$

Each branch is inserted into Eq. B.1 to obtain the potential. The printed constants carry limited precision, so the two energy branches differ by about $3.2\times10^{-5}$ Hartree at $r_s=1$. This is an implementation-level rounding boundary. Production code should identify the parameter source, branch convention, and whether energy and first derivative are continuous.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="Vosko–Wilk–Nusair 形式" titleEn="Vosko-Wilk-Nusair form" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Martin 还记录 VWN 的未极化参数化。定义

$$
y=\sqrt{r_s},\qquad
Y(y)=y^2+by+c,\qquad
Q=\sqrt{4c-b^2},
$$

以及 $y_0=-0.10498$、$b=3.72744$、$c=12.93532$、$A=0.0621814$。相关能由对数与反正切项组合：

$$
\begin{aligned}
\varepsilon_c^{\mathrm{VWN}}(r_s)
=\frac{Ae^2}{2}\Bigg[
&\ln\frac{y^2}{Y(y)}
+\frac{2b}{Q}\tan^{-1}\frac{Q}{2y+b}\\
&-\frac{by_0}{Y(y_0)}
\left(
\ln\frac{(y-y_0)^2}{Y(y)}
+\frac{2(b+2y_0)}{Q}\tan^{-1}\frac{Q}{2y+b}
\right)
\Bigg].
\end{aligned}
$$

Martin 给出用于式 B.1 的导数组合

$$
r_s\frac{d\varepsilon_c^{\mathrm{VWN}}}{dr_s}
=
A\frac{e^2}{2}
\frac{c(y-y_0)-by_0y}{(y-y_0)(y^2+by+c)}.
$$

VWN 与 PZ 都是对均匀电子气数据的解析拟合。它们数值接近并不表示公式、导数或自旋插值完全相同；软件标签应保留具体参数化身份。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Martin also records the unpolarized VWN parametrization. Define

$$
y=\sqrt{r_s},\qquad
Y(y)=y^2+by+c,\qquad
Q=\sqrt{4c-b^2},
$$

with $y_0=-0.10498$, $b=3.72744$, $c=12.93532$, and $A=0.0621814$. The correlation energy combines logarithmic and inverse-tangent terms,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\varepsilon_c^{\mathrm{VWN}}(r_s)
=\frac{Ae^2}{2}\Bigg[
&\ln\frac{y^2}{Y(y)}
+\frac{2b}{Q}\tan^{-1}\frac{Q}{2y+b}\\
&-\frac{by_0}{Y(y_0)}
\left(
\ln\frac{(y-y_0)^2}{Y(y)}
+\frac{2(b+2y_0)}{Q}\tan^{-1}\frac{Q}{2y+b}
\right)
\Bigg].
\end{aligned}
$$

For use in Eq. B.1, Martin gives

$$
r_s\frac{d\varepsilon_c^{\mathrm{VWN}}}{dr_s}
=
A\frac{e^2}{2}
\frac{c(y-y_0)-by_0y}{(y-y_0)(y^2+by+c)}.
$$

Both VWN and PZ are analytic fits to uniform-electron-gas data. Their numerical similarity does not make their formulas, derivatives, or spin interpolation identical; software labels should preserve the specific parametrization identity.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="相关自旋插值的边界" titleEn="Boundary of correlation spin interpolation" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

原书指出 PZ 与 VWN 都需要在未极化和完全极化数据之间构造分数自旋极化插值。交换的 $f_x(\zeta)$ 可以作为插值形状的一部分，但中间极化相关能仍依赖所选参数化及拟合数据。不能用交换的精确自旋缩放替代相关能的独立定义。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Martin notes that both PZ and VWN require interpolation between unpolarized and fully polarized correlation data. The exchange factor $f_x(\zeta)$ may enter the interpolation shape, but intermediate-polarization correlation still depends on the selected parametrization and fitted data. Exact exchange spin scaling cannot replace an independent definition of correlation.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>
