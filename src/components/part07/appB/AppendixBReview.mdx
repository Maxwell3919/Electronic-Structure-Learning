## 常见误判、原创练习与来源边界 | Misjudgments, Original Exercises, and Source Boundaries

<BilingualSection titleZh="会改变计算或解释的误判" titleEn="Misjudgments that change a calculation or interpretation" eyebrow="【补充说明 | Supplemental clarification】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

1. **把 LDA 与 LSDA 当作不同精度等级。** LSDA 是局域近似的自旋分辨形式；在 $n_\uparrow=n_\downarrow$ 时退化为未极化 LDA。
2. **把 $\varepsilon_c$ 当作 $V_c$。** 势是 $d[n\varepsilon_c]/dn$，包含显式密度因子和 $r_s(n)$ 的链式法则。
3. **把交换自旋缩放直接当作相关的精确关系。** 相关能的分数极化需要独立参数化或插值。
4. **认为 $s$ 和 $t$ 是同一个梯度变量。** PBE 交换用 $k_F$ 缩放的 $s$，相关用 $k_{\mathrm{TF}}$ 和 $\phi$ 缩放的 $t$。
5. **只检查 $F_x(0)=1$ 就宣布 PBE 实现正确。** 还要检查小梯度系数、大梯度上界、相关项、势的导数、低密度处理和离散梯度。
6. **把能量相近解释为势或响应相近。** 两个泛函可能给出接近的总能，却给出不同密度、势、力、声子或响应。
7. **把函数库名称当作完整 provenance。** 参数化版本、常数、spin convention、赝势生成泛函和代码版本仍需记录。
8. **把均匀极限正确当作材料准确性证明。** 精确约束降低不合理行为，但不会替代对目标 observable 的验证。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

1. **Treating LDA and LSDA as different accuracy rungs.** LSDA is the spin-resolved local approximation and reduces to unpolarized LDA when $n_\uparrow=n_\downarrow$.
2. **Identifying $\varepsilon_c$ with $V_c$.** The potential is $d[n\varepsilon_c]/dn$ and contains both the explicit density factor and the chain rule through $r_s(n)$.
3. **Using exchange spin scaling as an exact correlation relation.** Fractional-polarization correlation requires an independent parametrization or interpolation.
4. **Treating $s$ and $t$ as the same gradient variable.** PBE exchange uses $s$ scaled by $k_F$, whereas correlation uses $t$ scaled by $k_{\mathrm{TF}}$ and $\phi$.
5. **Checking only $F_x(0)=1$ and declaring a PBE implementation correct.** The small-gradient coefficient, large-gradient bound, correlation term, potential derivative, low-density handling, and discrete gradient also matter.
6. **Inferring similar potentials or responses from similar energies.** Two functionals may produce close total energies but different densities, potentials, forces, phonons, or response functions.
7. **Treating a library name as complete provenance.** Parametrization version, constants, spin convention, pseudopotential-generation functional, and code version still need to be recorded.
8. **Treating the correct uniform limit as proof of material accuracy.** Exact constraints exclude some unphysical behavior but do not replace validation of a target observable.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="原创练习" titleEn="Original exercises" eyebrow="【原创教学内容 | Original teaching content】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

1. **相关势推导。** 从 $r_s=(3/4\pi n)^{1/3}/a_0$ 出发，完整推导式 B.1，并对 $\varepsilon_c=Ar_s^p$ 检查结果。
2. **PZ 分界点。** 使用印刷系数计算 $r_s=1$ 两侧的 $\varepsilon_c$ 和 $V_c$。区分舍入不连续、真实参数化定义和数值实现选择。
3. **交换自旋缩放。** 从 $E_x\propto-\int(n_\uparrow^{4/3}+n_\downarrow^{4/3})d^3r$ 推导 $f_x(\zeta)$，并证明它是偶函数。
4. **单位检查。** 分别使用 bohr$^{-3}$ 和 Å$^{-3}$ 输入一个密度，计算 $r_s$ 与 $s$。说明未转换单位时错误如何随密度放大。
5. **PBE 极限。** 对式 B.7 求一阶和二阶导数，验证 $s=0$ 的曲率、$s>0$ 的单调性和大 $s$ 饱和。
6. **能量—势有限差分。** 在一维周期网格上构造 $E[n]=\sum_iw_i n_i\varepsilon(n_i)$，解析求离散梯度，并用随机方向中心差分验证。
7. **GGA 离散伴随。** 取明确差分矩阵 $D$，对 $E_h(\mathbf n)=\sum_iw_if(n_i,(D\mathbf n)_i)$ 推导包含 $D^T$ 的梯度，比较周期与固定端点闭合。
8. **低密度尾部。** 令 $n(x)=e^{-2x}$，计算 $r_s(x)$ 与 $s(x)$ 的渐近行为，说明为何密度尾部需要稳定的数值表达。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

1. **Correlation potential.** Starting from $r_s=(3/4\pi n)^{1/3}/a_0$, derive Eq. B.1 and check it for $\varepsilon_c=Ar_s^p$.
2. **PZ branch point.** With the printed coefficients, compute $\varepsilon_c$ and $V_c$ on both sides of $r_s=1$. Distinguish rounding mismatch, the underlying parametrization, and an implementation convention.
3. **Exchange spin scaling.** Starting from $E_x\propto-\int(n_\uparrow^{4/3}+n_\downarrow^{4/3})d^3r$, derive $f_x(\zeta)$ and prove that it is even.
4. **Unit check.** Evaluate $r_s$ and $s$ for the same density expressed in bohr$^{-3}$ and Å$^{-3}$. Explain how an unconverted unit error grows with density.
5. **PBE limits.** Differentiate Eq. B.7 once and twice and verify the curvature at $s=0$, monotonicity for $s>0$, and large-$s$ saturation.
6. **Energy-potential finite difference.** On a one-dimensional periodic grid, construct $E[n]=\sum_iw_i n_i\varepsilon(n_i)$, derive its discrete gradient, and verify it by central differences in random directions.
7. **Discrete GGA adjoint.** For an explicit difference matrix $D$, derive the gradient of $E_h(\mathbf n)=\sum_iw_if(n_i,(D\mathbf n)_i)$ containing $D^T$, comparing periodic and fixed-endpoint closures.
8. **Low-density tail.** For $n(x)=e^{-2x}$, determine the asymptotic behavior of $r_s(x)$ and $s(x)$ and explain why density tails require numerically stable formulas.

  </div>
