## B.2 广义梯度近似 | Generalized-Gradient Approximations

<BilingualSection titleZh="从局域密度到半局域梯度信息" titleEn="From local density to semilocal gradient information" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 + 补充组织 | Martin source + supplemental organization】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Martin 在 B.2 只强调 GGA 形式很多，没有试图列出完整目录。统一结构可写成

$$
E_{\mathrm{xc}}^{\mathrm{GGA}}
=
\int d^3r\,
f\!\left(
 n_\uparrow,n_\downarrow,
 \nabla n_\uparrow,\nabla n_\downarrow
\right).
$$

为了不让梯度变量携带单位，实际构造通常把 $|\nabla n|$ 除以由局域密度形成的同量纲尺度。交换常用

$$
s=\frac{|\nabla n|}{2k_Fn},
\qquad
k_F=(3\pi^2n)^{1/3}.
$$

在均匀密度中 $\nabla n=0$，所以 $s=0$，GGA 若满足正确局域极限，应退化到 LSDA/LDA。缓慢变化只说明 $s\ll1$；它不说明高阶梯度项、边界或自旋结构一定可以忽略。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Martin's B.2 stresses that many GGA forms exist and does not attempt an exhaustive list. Their common structure can be written as

$$
E_{\mathrm{xc}}^{\mathrm{GGA}}
=
\int d^3r\,
f\!\left(
 n_\uparrow,n_\downarrow,
 \nabla n_\uparrow,\nabla n_\downarrow
\right).
$$

To avoid a dimensional gradient variable, practical constructions divide $|\nabla n|$ by a local density scale of the same dimensions. Exchange commonly uses

$$
s=\frac{|\nabla n|}{2k_Fn},
\qquad
k_F=(3\pi^2n)^{1/3}.
$$

A uniform density has $\nabla n=0$ and therefore $s=0$. A GGA with the correct local limit must then reduce to LSDA/LDA. A slowly varying density only establishes $s\ll1$; it does not by itself remove higher-gradient, boundary, or spin effects.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="能量、势与分部积分" titleEn="Energy, potential, and integration by parts" eyebrow="【Appendix A 交叉引用 | Appendix A cross-reference】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

对标量密度形式

$$
E[n]=\int d^3r\,f(n,\nabla n),
$$

连续泛函导数为

$$
\boxed{
v(\mathbf r)
=
\frac{\partial f}{\partial n}
-
\nabla\cdot\frac{\partial f}{\partial(\nabla n)}
}
$$

并依赖 Appendix A 中说明的周期、固定值或衰减边界条件。若 $f$ 通过 $s=|\nabla n|/(2k_Fn)$ 依赖梯度，则

$$
\frac{\partial f}{\partial(\nabla n)}
=
\frac{\partial f}{\partial s}
\frac{\nabla n}{2k_Fn|\nabla n|}
$$

在 $|\nabla n|\ne0$ 处成立。零梯度处必须使用解析极限，使 $\partial f/\partial s$ 的小 $s$ 行为与方向因子共同给出有限结果。直接计算单位向量再用数值截断掩盖除零，可能使势与能量不再互为导数。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For a scalar-density form

$$
E[n]=\int d^3r\,f(n,\nabla n),
$$

the continuous functional derivative is

$$
\boxed{
v(\mathbf r)
=
\frac{\partial f}{\partial n}
-
\nabla\cdot\frac{\partial f}{\partial(\nabla n)}
}
$$

subject to the periodic, fixed-value, or decaying boundary conditions stated in Appendix A. If $f$ depends on the gradient through $s=|\nabla n|/(2k_Fn)$, then

$$
\frac{\partial f}{\partial(\nabla n)}
=
\frac{\partial f}{\partial s}
\frac{\nabla n}{2k_Fn|\nabla n|}
$$

where $|\nabla n|\ne0$. At zero gradient, an analytic limit must combine the small-$s$ behavior of $\partial f/\partial s$ with the direction factor to produce a finite result. Computing the unit vector first and hiding division by zero through numerical clipping can break the derivative relation between energy and potential.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="GGA 标签不能确定实现" titleEn="A GGA label does not identify an implementation" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

同一 GGA 名称仍可能对应不同自旋约定、相关参数化、常数精度、密度截断、梯度离散、势的解析或数值导数以及赝势生成泛函。比较结果前至少需要核对这些身份；输入文件中出现 `PBE` 只是一条来源主张，不构成实现等价的独立验证。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The same GGA label can still hide different spin conventions, correlation parametrizations, constant precision, density floors, gradient discretizations, analytic versus numerical potentials, and pseudopotential-generation functionals. These identities must be checked before comparing results. The appearance of `PBE` in an input file is a source claim, not independent verification of implementation equivalence.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## B.3 GGA 的显式 PBE 形式 | Explicit PBE Form

<BilingualSection titleZh="交换能和增强因子" titleEn="Exchange energy and enhancement factor" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

PBE 交换写成未极化均匀电子气交换能乘以增强因子：

$$
E_x^{\mathrm{PBE}}[n]
=
\int d^3r\,
n\varepsilon_x^{\mathrm{hom}}(n)F_x(s).
$$

Martin 式 B.7 为

$$
\boxed{
F_x(s)
=
1+\kappa
-
\frac{\kappa}{1+\mu s^2/\kappa}
},
$$

其中 $\kappa=0.804$、$\mu=0.21951$。前者控制大梯度上界，后者控制小梯度二次项及交换—相关线性响应的组合。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

PBE exchange is written as the exchange energy of an unpolarized uniform gas multiplied by an enhancement factor,

$$
E_x^{\mathrm{PBE}}[n]
=
\int d^3r\,
n\varepsilon_x^{\mathrm{hom}}(n)F_x(s).
$$

Martin's Eq. B.7 is

$$
\boxed{
F_x(s)
=
1+\kappa
-
\frac{\kappa}{1+\mu s^2/\kappa}
},
$$

with $\kappa=0.804$ and $\mu=0.21951$. The former controls the large-gradient bound, while the latter controls the small-gradient quadratic term and the combined exchange-correlation linear response.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## PBE 交换的极限和导数 | Limits and Derivatives of PBE Exchange

<BilingualSection titleZh="均匀极限、小梯度展开与有界性" titleEn="Uniform limit, small-gradient expansion, and boundedness" eyebrow="【补充推导 | Supplemental derivation】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

令 $u=\mu s^2/\kappa$。在 $s=0$ 时 $u=0$，因此

$$
F_x(0)=1+\kappa-\kappa=1,
$$

恢复局域交换。对 $|u|<1$ 使用

$$
\frac{1}{1+u}=1-u+u^2+O(u^3),
$$

得到

$$
F_x(s)
=
1+\mu s^2-
\frac{\mu^2}{\kappa}s^4+O(s^6).
$$

大梯度时分式趋于零：

$$
\lim_{s\to\infty}F_x(s)=1+\kappa=1.804.
$$

一阶导数为

$$
\frac{dF_x}{ds}
=
\frac{2\mu s}{\left(1+\mu s^2/\kappa\right)^2},
$$

所以 $s>0$ 时单调增加，$s=0$ 处导数为零。二阶展开、单调性和饱和值构成独立于图形外观的低成本回归检查。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Let $u=\mu s^2/\kappa$. At $s=0$, $u=0$ and

$$
F_x(0)=1+\kappa-\kappa=1,
$$

recovering local exchange. For $|u|<1$,

$$
\frac{1}{1+u}=1-u+u^2+O(u^3),
$$

which gives

$$
F_x(s)
=
1+\mu s^2-
\frac{\mu^2}{\kappa}s^4+O(s^6).
$$

At large gradient the fraction vanishes,

$$
\lim_{s\to\infty}F_x(s)=1+\kappa=1.804.
$$

The first derivative is

$$
\frac{dF_x}{ds}
=
\frac{2\mu s}{\left(1+\mu s^2/\kappa\right)^2},
$$

so the factor increases for $s>0$ and has zero slope at $s=0$. The quadratic expansion, monotonicity, and saturation value provide low-cost regression tests independent of the appearance of a plotted curve.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="PBE 的小梯度选择" titleEn="The PBE small-gradient choice" kind="source">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Martin 说明 PBE 的 $\mu$ 选择用于恢复均匀电子气的交换—相关线性响应，并与相关项的梯度贡献抵消；因此它不等同于单独恢复交换的已知低梯度展开系数。这是构造约束之间的取舍，不能解释成某个材料结果的经验拟合参数。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Martin explains that PBE chooses $\mu$ to recover the exchange-correlation linear response of the uniform gas through cancellation with the correlation gradient contribution. It therefore does not coincide with independently restoring the known exchange-only low-gradient coefficient. This is a tradeoff among construction constraints, not an empirical fit to a particular material result.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## PBE 相关修正 | PBE Correlation Correction

<BilingualSection titleZh="局域相关加梯度修正" titleEn="Local correlation plus a gradient correction" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Martin 式 B.8 将 PBE 相关写成均匀电子气相关能和附加项 $H$：

$$
E_c^{\mathrm{PBE}}[n_\uparrow,n_\downarrow]
=
\int d^3r\,n
\left[
\varepsilon_c^{\mathrm{hom}}(r_s,\zeta)
+H(r_s,\zeta,t)
\right].
$$

相关约化梯度为

$$
t=\frac{|\nabla n|}{2\phi k_{\mathrm{TF}}n},
\qquad
\phi(\zeta)=
\frac{(1+\zeta)^{2/3}+(1-\zeta)^{2/3}}{2}.
$$

$t$ 使用 Thomas–Fermi 屏蔽波矢 $k_{\mathrm{TF}}$，与交换的 $s$ 使用 $k_F$ 不同。把两者混用会改变函数的密度缩放和约束。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Martin's Eq. B.8 writes PBE correlation as uniform-gas correlation plus an additive term $H$,

$$
E_c^{\mathrm{PBE}}[n_\uparrow,n_\downarrow]
=
\int d^3r\,n
\left[
\varepsilon_c^{\mathrm{hom}}(r_s,\zeta)
+H(r_s,\zeta,t)
\right].
$$

The correlation reduced gradient is

$$
t=\frac{|\nabla n|}{2\phi k_{\mathrm{TF}}n},
\qquad
\phi(\zeta)=
\frac{(1+\zeta)^{2/3}+(1-\zeta)^{2/3}}{2}.
$$

The variable $t$ uses the Thomas-Fermi screening wave vector $k_{\mathrm{TF}}$, whereas exchange uses $k_F$ in $s$. Interchanging them changes density scaling and the constraints of the functional.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="式 B.9–B.10" titleEn="Equations B.9-B.10" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

PBE 相关修正写为

$$
\boxed{
H
=
\frac{e^2}{a_0}\gamma\phi^3
\ln\left[
1+\frac{\beta}{\gamma}t^2
\frac{1+At^2}{1+At^2+A^2t^4}
\right]
},
$$

其中

$$
\boxed{
A
=
\frac{\beta}{\gamma}
\left[
\exp\left(
-\frac{\varepsilon_c^{\mathrm{hom}}}
{\gamma\phi^3(e^2/a_0)}
\right)-1
\right]^{-1}
}.
$$

在 Hartree 原子单位中 $e^2/a_0=1$。$A$ 依赖局域 $r_s$ 和 $\zeta$，不是全局常数。$t=0$ 时对数参数为 1，因此 $H=0$ 并恢复 LSDA 相关。小 $t$ 展开给出

$$
H
=
\frac{e^2}{a_0}\beta\phi^3t^2+O(t^4),
$$

因为 $\ln(1+x)=x+O(x^2)$ 且有理因子在 $t=0$ 为 1。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The PBE correlation correction is

$$
\boxed{
H
=
\frac{e^2}{a_0}\gamma\phi^3
\ln\left[
1+\frac{\beta}{\gamma}t^2
\frac{1+At^2}{1+At^2+A^2t^4}
\right]
},
$$

with

$$
\boxed{
A
=
\frac{\beta}{\gamma}
\left[
\exp\left(
-\frac{\varepsilon_c^{\mathrm{hom}}}
{\gamma\phi^3(e^2/a_0)}
\right)-1
\right]^{-1}
}.
$$

In Hartree atomic units $e^2/a_0=1$. The quantity $A$ depends on the local $r_s$ and $\zeta$; it is not a global constant. At $t=0$, the logarithm has argument one, so $H=0$ and LSDA correlation is recovered. The small-$t$ expansion is

$$
H
=
\frac{e^2}{a_0}\beta\phi^3t^2+O(t^4),
$$

because $\ln(1+x)=x+O(x^2)$ and the rational factor tends to one at $t=0$.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="公式能支持和不能支持的判断" titleEn="What the formulas do and do not establish" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

式 B.7–B.10 规定一个解析近似及若干极限。它们可以检查维度、局域极限、梯度展开、上界和导数一致性；不能单独证明晶格常数、能隙、磁矩、反应能或声子对任一材料准确。材料级判断仍需要与适当参考比较，并检查赝势、基组、采样、自洽和目标 observable 收敛。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Equations B.7-B.10 define an analytic approximation and selected limits. They support checks of dimensions, the local limit, gradient expansion, boundedness, and derivative consistency. They do not prove that a lattice constant, band gap, magnetic moment, reaction energy, or phonon is accurate for a material. Material-level judgment still requires an appropriate reference and convergence of pseudopotential, basis, sampling, self-consistency, and the target observable.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>
