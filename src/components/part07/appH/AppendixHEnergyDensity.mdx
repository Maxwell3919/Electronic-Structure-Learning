## H.1 能量密度 | Energy Density

<BilingualSection titleZh="总能分解先于局域分配" titleEn="The total-energy decomposition precedes any local partition" eyebrow="【Martin Eqs. H.1–H.5 | Martin Eqs. H.1–H.5】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Martin 先把电子—核体系总能写成 interacting 与 Kohn–Sham 两种等价组织：

$$
E
=\langle\hat T\rangle
+\bigl[\langle\hat V_{\mathrm{int}}\rangle-E_H\bigr]
+E^{\mathrm{CC}}
=T_s+E^{\mathrm{CC}}+E_{xc}.
$$

其中 $E^{\mathrm{CC}}$ 把 classical Coulomb terms 按 extended neutral system 可定义的方式组合。随后才引入

$$
E=\int d\mathbf r\,e(\mathbf r),
\qquad
e=t_{\mathrm{ip}}+e_{\mathrm{CC}}+e_{xc}.
$$

把 ion–ion term $E_{II}$ 单独提出时，可写

$$
E=\int d\mathbf r\,n(\mathbf r)\varepsilon(\mathbf r)+E_{II},
$$

并在 Martin 的 charge/potential convention 下组织

$$
\varepsilon
=\tau_{\mathrm{ip}}+V_{\mathrm{ext}}
+\frac12V_H+\varepsilon_{xc}.
$$

这些式子固定总能 bookkeeping，但不自动固定每一项唯一的 pointwise gauge。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Martin first writes the total energy of the electron-nuclear system in equivalent interacting and Kohn-Sham organizations:

$$
E
=\langle\hat T\rangle
+\bigl[\langle\hat V_{\mathrm{int}}\rangle-E_H\bigr]
+E^{\mathrm{CC}}
=T_s+E^{\mathrm{CC}}+E_{xc}.
$$

Here $E^{\mathrm{CC}}$ groups the classical Coulomb terms in a form that is defined for an extended neutral system. Only then is a local density introduced:

$$
E=\int d\mathbf r\,e(\mathbf r),
\qquad
e=t_{\mathrm{ip}}+e_{\mathrm{CC}}+e_{xc}.
$$

When the ion-ion term $E_{II}$ is separated,

$$
E=\int d\mathbf r\,n(\mathbf r)\varepsilon(\mathbf r)+E_{II},
$$

and Martin's charge and potential convention organizes

$$
\varepsilon
=\tau_{\mathrm{ip}}+V_{\mathrm{ext}}
+\frac12V_H+\varepsilon_{xc}.
$$

These equations fix the total-energy bookkeeping but do not automatically fix a unique pointwise gauge for every term.

</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="classical Coulomb energy 有 field 与 charge 两种局域形式" titleEn="Classical Coulomb energy has field and charge local forms" eyebrow="【Martin Eq. H.6 | Martin Eq. H.6】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

对 signed total charge density $\rho_{\mathrm{tot}}$ 与 Coulomb potential $V^{\mathrm{CC}}$，在 Gaussian electrostatic units 和允许的 boundary 下：

$$
E^{\mathrm{CC}}
=\frac{1}{8\pi}\int d\mathbf r\,|\mathbf E^{\mathrm{CC}}|^2
=\frac12\int d\mathbf r\,
V^{\mathrm{CC}}(\mathbf r)\rho_{\mathrm{tot}}(\mathbf r),
$$

其中 $\mathbf E^{\mathrm{CC}}=-\nabla V^{\mathrm{CC}}$。第一式把能量分配给 field，第二式把能量分配给 charges 所在位置；二者通过 Poisson equation 与 integration by parts 连接，pointwise integrands 不相等。

第二种形式方便拆成 nuclear external potential 与 electronic Hartree potential，从而进入 H.5 的 per-electron expression。第一种 Maxwell field form 在 surface-energy integration 中可更方便。charged cell、self energy、pseudopotential compensation charge、uniform background 或 Coulomb cutoff 必须沿用 Appendix F 的同一 convention；不能只交换 integrand 而遗漏其余 reference terms。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For a signed total charge density $\rho_{\mathrm{tot}}$ and Coulomb potential $V^{\mathrm{CC}}$, under Gaussian electrostatic units and an allowed boundary,

$$
E^{\mathrm{CC}}
=\frac{1}{8\pi}\int d\mathbf r\,|\mathbf E^{\mathrm{CC}}|^2
=\frac12\int d\mathbf r\,
V^{\mathrm{CC}}(\mathbf r)\rho_{\mathrm{tot}}(\mathbf r),
$$

where $\mathbf E^{\mathrm{CC}}=-\nabla V^{\mathrm{CC}}$. The first expression assigns energy to the field, while the second assigns it to the positions of the charges. They are connected through the Poisson equation and integration by parts but are not pointwise equal.

The charge form is convenient for separating a nuclear external potential from an electronic Hartree potential and entering the per-electron expression H.5. The Maxwell field form can be convenient for surface-energy integration. A charged cell, self energy, pseudopotential compensation charge, uniform background, or Coulomb cutoff must use the same convention as Appendix F; one cannot exchange only the integrand while omitting the remaining reference terms.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="exchange–correlation per-electron density 由额外定义固定" titleEn="The exchange-correlation per-electron density is fixed by an additional definition" eyebrow="【Martin H.1 + Chapter 8 | Martin H.1 + Chapter 8】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Martin 采用 coupling-constant integrated exchange–correlation hole 来定义 $\varepsilon_{xc}(\mathbf r)$：它是 reference electron 位于 $\mathbf r$ 时，由 exchange 与 correlation 引起的 additional energy per electron。这个定义比“任何积分得到 $E_{xc}$ 的函数”更强；后者仍允许任意零积分项。

即使如此，实际 LDA/GGA/meta-GGA code 输出的“XC energy density”仍可能采用不同 integration-by-parts 或 per-volume/per-particle convention。比较时要确认是 hole-defined $\varepsilon_{xc}$、functional integrand $n\varepsilon_{xc}$、potential $V_{xc}=\delta E_{xc}/\delta n$，还是 stress-related combination $n(\varepsilon_{xc}-V_{xc})$。这些对象不能只因都带 $xc$ 下标而互换。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Martin uses the coupling-constant-integrated exchange-correlation hole to define $\varepsilon_{xc}(\mathbf r)$ as the additional energy per electron associated with exchange and correlation when the reference electron is at $\mathbf r$. This definition is stronger than choosing any function whose integral gives $E_{xc}$, because the latter still permits arbitrary zero-integral additions.

Even so, an XC energy density reported by an LDA, GGA, or meta-GGA implementation can use a different integration-by-parts or per-volume versus per-particle convention. A comparison must distinguish the hole-defined $\varepsilon_{xc}$, the functional integrand $n\varepsilon_{xc}$, the potential $V_{xc}=\delta E_{xc}/\delta n$, and the stress-related combination $n(\varepsilon_{xc}-V_{xc})$. A shared $xc$ subscript does not make these objects interchangeable.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="independent-particle kinetic energy 的两种 gauge" titleEn="Two gauges of the independent-particle kinetic energy" eyebrow="【Martin Eqs. H.7–H.8 | Martin Eqs. H.7–H.8】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

对每个 spin channel 单独求和，independent-particle kinetic energy 可写成

$$
T_s
=-\frac12\sum_i\int d\mathbf r\,
\psi_i^*\nabla^2\psi_i
=\frac12\sum_i\int d\mathbf r\,|\nabla\psi_i|^2.
$$

相应 local forms 为

$$
t^{(1)}(\mathbf r)
=-\frac12\sum_i\psi_i^*\nabla^2\psi_i,
\qquad
t^{(2)}(\mathbf r)
=\frac12\sum_i|\nabla\psi_i|^2.
$$

对实 orbitals，二者差为 $\frac14\nabla^2 n$；复 orbitals 需按 Hermitian/real energy-density convention 处理。等价积分依赖 boundary term 消失。$t^{(2)}$ nonnegative，而 $t^{(1)}$ 可局域为负，因此不能把正负 patch 直接解释成局域 kinetic-energy observable。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Summing separately within each spin channel, the independent-particle kinetic energy can be written as

$$
T_s
=-\frac12\sum_i\int d\mathbf r\,
\psi_i^*\nabla^2\psi_i
=\frac12\sum_i\int d\mathbf r\,|\nabla\psi_i|^2.
$$

The corresponding local forms are

$$
t^{(1)}(\mathbf r)
=-\frac12\sum_i\psi_i^*\nabla^2\psi_i,
\qquad
t^{(2)}(\mathbf r)
=\frac12\sum_i|\nabla\psi_i|^2.
$$

For real orbitals, the difference is $\frac14\nabla^2 n$; complex orbitals require a declared Hermitian or real energy-density convention. Equality of the integrals relies on a vanishing boundary term. The positive form $t^{(2)}$ is nonnegative, while $t^{(1)}$ can be locally negative, so positive and negative patches are not direct local kinetic-energy observables.

For one spin channel, write

\psi_i(\mathbf r)=u(\mathbf r)\phi_i(\mathbf r),

The normalization identities

t=t_n+t_x,

\tau_x

<BilingualSection titleZh="Weizsäcker reference 是 density-only bosonic part" titleEn="The Weizsäcker reference is the density-only bosonic part" eyebrow="【Martin Eqs. H.14–H.16 | Martin Eqs. H.14–H.16】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

remaining density-only kinetic term 仍有两种形式：

$$
t_n^{(1)}=-\frac12u\nabla^2u,
\qquad
t_n^{(2)}=\frac12|\nabla u|^2.
$$

选择 positive form 并除以 density，得到每个 spin channel 的 Weizsäcker kinetic energy per particle：

$$
\tau_W

$$

$$
\tau_x
=\frac{1}{2n}\sum_i|\nabla\psi_i|^2-\tau_W.
$$

单个 occupied spatial orbital 的 spin channel 满足 positive kinetic density 等于 Weizsäcker term，因此 $	au_x=0$。多 fermion occupancy 的额外 curvature 构成 meta-GGA 与 ELF 使用的 Pauli/excess kinetic information。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The remaining density-only kinetic term still has two forms:

$$
t_n^{(1)}=-\frac12u\nabla^2u,
\qquad
t_n^{(2)}=\frac12|\nabla u|^2.
$$

Choosing the positive form and dividing by the density gives the Weizsäcker kinetic energy per particle for each spin channel:

$$
\tau_W
=\frac12\left|\frac{\nabla u}{u}\right|^2
=\frac18\left|\frac{\nabla n}{n}\right|^2.
$$

Martin H.16 can then be written as

$$
\tau_x
=\frac{1}{2n}\sum_i|\nabla\psi_i|^2-\tau_W.
$$

For a spin channel containing one occupied spatial orbital, the positive kinetic density equals the Weizsäcker term and $\tau_x=0$. The additional curvature from multiple fermionic orbitals supplies the Pauli or excess kinetic information used by meta-GGA functionals and ELF.

<BilingualSection titleZh="H.17 的印刷 normalization 需要显式审计" titleEn="The printed normalization of H.17 requires an explicit audit" eyebrow="【来源记录 + dimensional audit | Source record + dimensional audit】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Martin H.17 印刷为

$$
\varepsilon(\mathbf r)
=\sum_i\varepsilon_i|\psi_i(\mathbf r)|^2
-\frac12V_H(\mathbf r)
+\bigl[\varepsilon_{xc}(\mathbf r)-V_{xc}(\mathbf r)\bigr].
$$

正文把第一项称为 eigenvalue-weighted density。此前 H.2–H.5 又把 $\varepsilon=e/n$ 定义为 energy per particle。按通常 orbital normalization，$\sum_i\varepsilon_i|\psi_i|^2$ 是 energy per volume，而 $V_H$ 与 $\varepsilon_{xc}$ 是 energy per particle；印刷式因此存在 normalization/notation mismatch。

网站不静默补上或删去 $n$。使用该关系时必须先选择一致对象：若求 per-particle quantity，eigenvalue-weighted density 应除以 $n$；若求 per-volume energy density，potential correction terms 应乘以 $n$。无论哪种 consistent normalization，物理内容都是从 Kohn–Sham eigenvalue sum 中减去 Hartree double counting，并加入 $\varepsilon_{xc}-V_{xc}$ correction。该式可用于 local-energy/projection analysis，但 eigenvalue-weighted density 仍受 gauge、basis、energy zero 与 projection convention 约束。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Martin H.17 is printed as

$$
\varepsilon(\mathbf r)
=\sum_i\varepsilon_i|\psi_i(\mathbf r)|^2
-\frac12V_H(\mathbf r)
+\bigl[\varepsilon_{xc}(\mathbf r)-V_{xc}(\mathbf r)\bigr].
$$

The prose calls the first term an eigenvalue-weighted density, while H.2–H.5 previously define $\varepsilon=e/n$ as an energy per particle. With standard orbital normalization, $\sum_i\varepsilon_i|\psi_i|^2$ is an energy per volume, whereas $V_H$ and $\varepsilon_{xc}$ are energies per particle. The printed equation therefore contains a normalization or notation mismatch.

The site does not silently insert or remove a factor of $n$. A consistent object must be chosen before using the relation. For a per-particle quantity, divide the eigenvalue-weighted density by $n$; for a per-volume energy density, multiply the potential correction terms by $n$. In either consistent normalization, the physical content is to remove Hartree double counting from the Kohn-Sham eigenvalue sum and add the $\varepsilon_{xc}-V_{xc}$ correction. The expression can support local-energy or projection analysis, but the eigenvalue-weighted density remains sensitive to gauge, basis, energy zero, and projection convention.

</BilingualSection>
