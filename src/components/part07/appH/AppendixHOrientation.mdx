<BilingualSection titleZh="本附录解决什么问题" titleEn="What this Appendix resolves" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 + 课程组织 | Martin source + course organization】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Appendix H 处理一个容易被图像误导的问题：总能、总力和宏观应力是明确对象，但把它们分配到每个空间点时，局域 integrand 通常并不唯一。能量密度可以加入积分为零的 divergence 或 density-Laplacian 项；stress density 可以加入 divergence-free tensor；这些 gauge transformations 改变局域图形，却不改变满足相应 boundary conditions 的总积分、完整 surface flux 或特定差分。

原书同时指出，并非所有局域结构都只是任意 gauge。exchange–correlation hole 定义的能量 per electron、fermionic excess kinetic energy density，以及由其构造的 ELF 具有更明确的物理组织。学习目标不是寻找“唯一的每点能量”，而是区分 pointwise gauge dependence、可证明 invariant 的 integrated quantity，以及由额外定义固定的 meaningful density。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Appendix H addresses a question for which plots can be misleading. Total energy, total force, and macroscopic stress are well-defined objects, but assigning them to every point in space is generally nonunique. An energy density can be changed by a divergence or density-Laplacian term whose allowed integral vanishes, while a stress density can be changed by a divergence-free tensor. Such gauge transformations alter the local plot without changing a total integral, a complete surface flux, or a selected difference under the corresponding boundary conditions.

The source also explains that not every local structure is merely arbitrary gauge. The exchange-correlation energy per electron defined from the exchange-correlation hole, the fermionic excess kinetic-energy density, and the ELF constructed from it have a more specific physical organization. The goal is not to find one unique energy at every point, but to separate pointwise gauge dependence, provably invariant integrated quantities, and meaningful densities fixed by additional definitions.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## 对象、gauge 与统一约定 | Objects, Gauges, and Conventions

<BilingualSection titleZh="density、per-particle density 与 total quantity" titleEn="Density, per-particle density, and total quantity" eyebrow="【Martin Eqs. H.2–H.5 | Martin Eqs. H.2–H.5】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

网站用 $e(\mathbf r)$ 表示 energy per volume，满足

$$
E=\int d\mathbf r\,e(\mathbf r).
$$

若电子密度 $n(\mathbf r)>0$，可定义 energy per electron

$$
\varepsilon(\mathbf r)=\frac{e(\mathbf r)}{n(\mathbf r)}.
$$

per-particle form 在连接 Kohn–Sham equation 和 eigenvalue-weighted density 时方便，但在 node、vacuum 或极低 density 区域可能数值发散。任何图都应说明展示的是 $e$、$\varepsilon$、cell-integrated energy、planar average，还是 bulk-subtracted excess。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The site uses $e(\mathbf r)$ for energy per volume, satisfying

$$
E=\int d\mathbf r\,e(\mathbf r).
$$

Where the electron density is positive, the energy per electron can be defined as

$$
\varepsilon(\mathbf r)=\frac{e(\mathbf r)}{n(\mathbf r)}.
$$

The per-particle form is convenient for connecting to the Kohn-Sham equation and an eigenvalue-weighted density, but it can become numerically singular at nodes, in vacuum, or in very-low-density regions. Every plot should state whether it shows $e$, $\varepsilon$, a cell-integrated energy, a planar average, or a bulk-subtracted excess.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="gauge transformation 不等于 electromagnetic gauge" titleEn="A density gauge is not automatically an electromagnetic gauge" eyebrow="【定义边界 | Definition boundary】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

本附录中的 gauge 指局域 density 的重分配。例如

$$
e'(\mathbf r)=e(\mathbf r)+\nabla\cdot\mathbf A(\mathbf r)
$$

或

$$
\sigma'_{\alpha\beta}
=\sigma_{\alpha\beta}+G_{\alpha\beta},
\qquad
\nabla_\beta G_{\alpha\beta}=0.
$$

它们与 vector potential 的 electromagnetic gauge、Bloch phase gauge、Berry gauge 或 pseudopotential gauge 不是同一个数学对象。共同点只是不同表示给出相同的声明 observable；允许的 transformation 与 invariant 必须逐问题定义。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Here, a gauge is a redistribution of a local density, for example

$$
e'(\mathbf r)=e(\mathbf r)+\nabla\cdot\mathbf A(\mathbf r)
$$

or

$$
\sigma'_{\alpha\beta}
=\sigma_{\alpha\beta}+G_{\alpha\beta},
\qquad
\nabla_\beta G_{\alpha\beta}=0.
$$

This is not the same mathematical object as an electromagnetic vector-potential gauge, a Bloch-phase or Berry gauge, or a pseudopotential gauge. The common idea is only that different representations give the same declared observable. The allowed transformation and invariant must be defined for each problem.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="invariant 由积分区域和边界决定" titleEn="The invariant is determined by the region and its boundary" eyebrow="【Martin introduction + H.3 | Martin introduction + H.3】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Gauss theorem 给出

$$
\int_V d\mathbf r\,\nabla\cdot\mathbf A
=\oint_{\partial V}\mathbf A\cdot d\mathbf S.
$$

因此 divergence term 只有在 boundary flux 为零时才从积分中消失。常见充分条件包括：周期 cell 的 opposite faces cancellation；bound state 的足够快 decay；surface energy 中 vacuum 与 bulk interior 端点的 matched limit；完整闭合 surface 上的 gauge flux cancellation。任意 finite atomic basin、半个 cell、未收敛真空或 truncated numerical domain 不自动满足这些条件。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Gauss's theorem gives

$$
\int_V d\mathbf r\,\nabla\cdot\mathbf A
=\oint_{\partial V}\mathbf A\cdot d\mathbf S.
$$

A divergence term therefore disappears from an integral only when the boundary flux vanishes. Common sufficient conditions include cancellation of opposite faces in a periodic cell, sufficiently rapid decay for a bound state, matched vacuum and bulk-interior limits in a surface-energy difference, or cancellation of gauge flux over a complete closed surface. An arbitrary atomic basin, half cell, unconverged vacuum, or truncated numerical domain does not automatically satisfy these conditions.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="三层证据：表示、invariant、interpretation" titleEn="Three evidence layers: representation, invariant, interpretation" eyebrow="【课程证据合同 | Course evidence contract】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

对局域 density 的科学主张分三层：第一层说明采用的公式与 gauge；第二层证明目标 integral/difference/flux 在允许 transformations 下不变；第三层才讨论 bonding、surface energy、shell structure 或 local force transmission。跳过第二层，直接把一张彩色图解释为“这里储存了多少能量”或“这里承受多少应力”，通常超过证据。

同样，两个代码的局域图不同不构成矛盾，除非先确认 Hamiltonian、density、boundary、normalization、gauge 和 all-electron/pseudopotential reconstruction 完全一致。更稳健的比较对象是 total、bulk-subtracted integral、complete surface flux 或明确 gauge-fixed descriptor。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Scientific claims from a local density have three layers. First, specify the formula and gauge. Second, prove that the target integral, difference, or flux is invariant under the allowed transformations. Only then interpret bonding, surface energy, shell structure, or local force transmission. Skipping the second layer and treating a colour plot as the amount of energy stored or stress carried at one point usually exceeds the evidence.

Likewise, different local plots from two codes are not contradictory until the Hamiltonian, density, boundary, normalization, gauge, and all-electron or pseudopotential reconstruction are matched. More robust comparison targets are a total, a bulk-subtracted integral, a complete surface flux, or an explicitly gauge-fixed descriptor.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="数值实现的最低元数据" titleEn="Minimal numerical metadata" kind="boundary">

  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Record at least the energy or stress-density formula, kinetic gauge, Coulomb field or charge form, XC per-particle convention, spin summation, core or valence and pseudopotential reconstruction, grid, cell, units, integration region and boundary, bulk reference, and any smoothing or coarse graining. Without these data, a local map is an unreproducible visualization rather than quantitative evidence.

  </div>
