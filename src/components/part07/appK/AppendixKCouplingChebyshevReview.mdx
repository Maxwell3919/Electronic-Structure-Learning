## K.4 Clebsch–Gordan 与 Gaunt 系数 | Clebsch–Gordan and Gaunt Coefficients

<BilingualSection titleZh="Clebsch–Gordan 与 Wigner 3j 的转换" titleEn="Conversion between Clebsch–Gordan and Wigner 3j" eyebrow="【Martin K.12–K.13 | Martin K.12–K.13】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

标准 convention 下

$$
\langle j_1m_1,j_2m_2|JM\rangle
=(-1)^{j_1-j_2+M}\sqrt{2J+1}
\begin{pmatrix}
j_1&j_2&J\\
m_1&m_2&-M
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

3j symbol 的 factorial sum 只对允许的整数或半整数 quantum numbers 有意义；每个 factorial argument 必须非负。网站 deterministic kernel 只实现非负整数 <code>j</code>，对应 orbital harmonics 与 Gaunt coefficients。半整数 spin coupling 仍应使用经过验证的专业库。

Martin 原节标题印作 “Clebsch–Gordon”，正文采用标准拼写 “Clebsch–Gordan”，并保留 source-title audit。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Under the standard convention,

$$
\langle j_1m_1,j_2m_2|JM\rangle
=(-1)^{j_1-j_2+M}\sqrt{2J+1}
\begin{pmatrix}
j_1&j_2&J\\
m_1&m_2&-M
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The factorial sum defining a 3j symbol is meaningful only for allowed integer or half-integer quantum numbers, with every factorial argument nonnegative. The deterministic website kernel implements nonnegative integer <code>j</code> only, matching orbital harmonics and Gaunt coefficients. Half-integer spin coupling should use a separately validated specialist library.

The printed source heading says “Clebsch–Gordon”. The exposition uses the standard spelling “Clebsch–Gordan” and records the source-title audit.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="先应用 selection rules" titleEn="Apply selection rules first" eyebrow="【解析零值 | Analytic zeros】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

3j symbol 非零要求

$$
m_1+m_2+m_3=0,
\qquad
|j_1-j_2|\le j_3\le j_1+j_2,
$$

并要求 <code>j_1+j_2+j_3</code> 为整数。对于 orbital-harmonic Gaunt integral，若三阶总和为奇数，<code>(l_1 l_2 l_3;0 0 0)=0</code>，因此还需要偶 parity。

这些是解析 gate。代码应在 factorial sum 前判定并返回精确零，而不是让非法 factorial、<code>NaN</code> 或浮点残差决定 selection rule。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A nonzero 3j symbol requires

$$
m_1+m_2+m_3=0,
\qquad
|j_1-j_2|\le j_3\le j_1+j_2,
$$

and <code>j_1+j_2+j_3</code> must be an integer. For an orbital-harmonic Gaunt integral, an odd sum <code>l_1+l_2+l_3</code> makes <code>(l_1 l_2 l_3;0 0 0)=0</code>, adding an even-parity gate.

These are analytic gates. An implementation should return an exact zero before entering a factorial sum rather than letting an invalid factorial, <code>NaN</code>, or floating-point residue decide a selection rule.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="标准复球谐 Gaunt 三重积" titleEn="Standard complex-harmonic Gaunt triple integral" eyebrow="【Martin K.14–K.17 + normalization audit | Martin K.14–K.17 + normalization audit】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

对 K.4 归一的复球谐，标准三重积为

$$
G_{123}=\int d\Omega\,
Y_{l_1m_1}Y_{l_2m_2}Y_{l_3m_3}
$$

$$
=\sqrt{\frac{(2l_1+1)(2l_2+1)(2l_3+1)}{4\pi}}
\begin{pmatrix}l_1&l_2&l_3\\0&0&0\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}l_1&l_2&l_3\\m_1&m_2&m_3\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Martin K.14–K.17 使用一个只含 <code>θ</code> 的归一函数 <code>Θ(l,m)</code> 和系数 <code>c</code>，其 normalization/index placement 不是这里的标准复球谐三重积符号。网站分别标记 source-specific <code>c</code> 与 standard <code>G</code>，不把两者无说明互换。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For the complex harmonics normalized in K.4, the standard triple integral is

$$
G_{123}=\int d\Omega\,
Y_{l_1m_1}Y_{l_2m_2}Y_{l_3m_3}
$$

$$
=\sqrt{\frac{(2l_1+1)(2l_2+1)(2l_3+1)}{4\pi}}
\begin{pmatrix}l_1&l_2&l_3\\0&0&0\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}l_1&l_2&l_3\\m_1&m_2&m_3\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Martin K.14–K.17 uses a theta-only normalized function <code>Θ(l,m)</code> and a coefficient <code>c</code> whose normalization and index placement are not identical to this standard complex-harmonic triple-integral notation. The website labels source-specific <code>c</code> and standard <code>G</code> separately rather than interchanging them without explanation.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="乘积展开与 multipole coupling" titleEn="Product expansion and multipole coupling" eyebrow="【电子结构接口 | Electronic-structure interface】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

两个球谐的乘积可在允许的 <code>L,M</code> 通道展开，系数由 Clebsch–Gordan 或 Gaunt integral 给出。该结构控制 density products、Hartree multipoles、XC matrix elements、augmentation charges 和 local-orbital products。

selection rule 只说明某个 angular channel 可否非零，不说明 radial integral、能量分母或材料矩阵元的大小。一个 allowed channel 仍可能因 radial orthogonality、symmetry beyond rotations 或数值抵消而很小。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The product of two spherical harmonics can be expanded over allowed <code>L,M</code> channels, with coefficients determined by Clebsch–Gordan or Gaunt integrals. This structure controls density products, Hartree multipoles, exchange-correlation matrix elements, augmentation charges, and local-orbital products.

A selection rule states whether an angular channel can be nonzero; it does not determine the size of a radial integral, energy denominator, or material matrix element. An allowed channel can still be small because of radial orthogonality, additional crystal symmetry, or numerical cancellation.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## K.5 Chebyshev 多项式 | Chebyshev Polynomials

<BilingualSection titleZh="递推与 cosine 表示" titleEn="Recurrence and cosine representation" eyebrow="【Martin K.18–K.20 | Martin K.18–K.20】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

K.19 定义 type-I Chebyshev polynomials：

$$
T_0(x)=1,\qquad T_1(x)=x,
$$

$$
T_{n+1}(x)=2xT_n(x)-T_{n-1}(x).
$$

若 <code>x=cosθ</code>，递推等价于

$$
T_n(x)=\cos(n\arccos x),\qquad |x|\le1.
$$

因此 <code>|T_n(x)|≤1</code> 在设计区间内成立。区间外令 <code>x=coshη</code>，则 <code>T_n=cosh(nη)</code>，随阶数指数增长；operator spectrum 未映射进 <code>[−1,1]</code> 会直接破坏稳定性。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Equation K.19 defines type-I Chebyshev polynomials:

$$
T_0(x)=1,\qquad T_1(x)=x,
$$

$$
T_{n+1}(x)=2xT_n(x)-T_{n-1}(x).
$$

With <code>x=cosθ</code>, the recurrence is equivalent to

$$
T_n(x)=\cos(n\arccos x),\qquad |x|\le1.
$$

Hence <code>|T_n(x)|≤1</code> on the design interval. Outside it, writing <code>x=coshη</code> gives <code>T_n=cosh(nη)</code>, which grows exponentially with order. An operator spectrum not mapped into <code>[−1,1]</code> therefore destroys the stability premise.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="加权正交与系数" titleEn="Weighted orthogonality and coefficients" eyebrow="【补充归一化 | Supplemental normalization】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

type-I polynomials 在权重 <code>w(x)=1/√(1−x²)</code> 下正交：

$$
\int_{-1}^{1}\frac{T_m(x)T_n(x)}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}dx
=\begin{cases}
\pi,&m=n=0,\\
\pi/2,&m=n&gt;0,\\
0,&m\ne n.
\end{cases}
$$

因此 K.20 的 convention

$$
f(x)\approx\frac{c_0}{2}+\sum_{n=1}^{M}c_nT_n(x)
$$

对应

$$
c_n=\frac{2}{\pi}\int_{-1}^{1}
\frac{f(x)T_n(x)}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}dx.
$$

书中“least-squares sense”必须理解为这一 Chebyshev weight 下的正交投影，而非一般 unweighted uniform-grid least squares。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Type-I polynomials are orthogonal under the weight <code>w(x)=1/√(1−x²)</code>:

$$
\int_{-1}^{1}\frac{T_m(x)T_n(x)}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}dx
=\begin{cases}
\pi,&m=n=0,\\
\pi/2,&m=n&gt;0,\\
0,&m\ne n.
\end{cases}
$$

Thus the K.20 convention

$$
f(x)\approx\frac{c_0}{2}+\sum_{n=1}^{M}c_nT_n(x)
$$

uses

$$
c_n=\frac{2}{\pi}\int_{-1}^{1}
\frac{f(x)T_n(x)}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}dx.
$$

The source phrase “least-squares sense” must be understood as an orthogonal projection under this Chebyshev weight, not as a generic unweighted least-squares fit on a uniform grid.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="Clenshaw evaluation 与 operator scaling" titleEn="Clenshaw evaluation and operator scaling" eyebrow="【算法接口 | Algorithmic interface】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

给定 <code>[E_min,E_max]</code>，先定义

$$
\tilde H=\frac{2H-(E_{\max}+E_{\min})I}{E_{\max}-E_{\min}},
$$

使谱落入 <code>[−1,1]</code>。随后可用三项递推生成 <code>T_n(\tilde H)|ψ\rangle</code>。标量和矩阵 polynomial 都可用 Clenshaw recurrence 减少存储与舍入误差。

若谱界估计过窄，某些 eigenvalue 映射到 <code>|x|&gt;1</code>，递推会爆炸；若界过宽，目标函数在映射区间内变化更尖锐，需要更高阶。spectral bounds 因而是算法输入和接受门，不是绘图参数。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For a spectral interval <code>[E_min,E_max]</code>, define

$$
\tilde H=\frac{2H-(E_{\max}+E_{\min})I}{E_{\max}-E_{\min}},
$$

so that the spectrum lies in <code>[−1,1]</code>. The three-term recurrence can then generate <code>T_n(\tilde H)|ψ\rangle</code>. Scalar and matrix polynomials can be evaluated through a Clenshaw recurrence to reduce storage and rounding error.

If the estimated spectral interval is too narrow, an eigenvalue maps to <code>|x|&gt;1</code> and the recurrence grows explosively. If the interval is excessively broad, the mapped target function varies more sharply and requires a higher order. Spectral bounds are therefore algorithmic inputs and acceptance gates, not plotting parameters.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="截断误差、非光滑函数与 damping" titleEn="Truncation error, nonsmooth functions, and damping" eyebrow="【收敛边界 | Convergence boundary】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

解析函数通常给出快速、接近指数的 coefficient decay；有限光滑性导致代数 decay；discontinuity 产生 Gibbs oscillations。Jackson 或其他 damping kernels 可抑制 oscillation，但会引入受控 broadening。它们改变近似器，不能被描述为“无代价稳定化”。

K.5 的公式本身不选择 truncation order、spectral interval、damping kernel 或 target observable。Chapter 18 的实际 propagation 仍需 unitarity、time-step/interval、norm 和 observable convergence。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Analytic functions usually give rapid, nearly exponential coefficient decay. Finite smoothness gives algebraic decay, while a discontinuity produces Gibbs oscillations. Jackson or related damping kernels can suppress oscillations but introduce controlled broadening. They change the approximant and are not a cost-free stabilization.

The formulas in K.5 do not choose a truncation order, spectral interval, damping kernel, or target observable. A production propagation in Chapter 18 still requires checks of unitarity, time step or interval, norm, and observable convergence.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="十二个高频误判" titleEn="Twelve recurrent misjudgments" eyebrow="【诊断清单 | Diagnostic checklist】">

      <li>在 <code>x=0</code> 直接计算 <code>n_l</code>，把 divergence 当作 overflow bug。</li>

      <li>只检查低阶闭式，不检查高阶渐近和尺度分离。</li>

      <li>按 <code>p_x/d_xy</code> 标签比较实球谐，却不比较 transformation matrix。</li>

      <li>把 source-specific Gaunt <code>c</code> 与标准复球谐三重积 <code>G</code> 无说明互换。</li>
      <li>把 selection-rule allowed 当成矩阵元一定很大。</li>
      <li>把 “Clebsch–Gordon” 来源标题当作标准术语。</li>
      <li>未映射谱区间便把 Hamiltonian 放进 Chebyshev recurrence。</li>
      <li>把低阶 polynomial fit 误当成生产 propagation 或 matrix-function convergence。</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <ol>
      <li>Evaluating <code>n_l</code> at <code>x=0</code> and treating its divergence as an overflow bug.</li>
      <li>Using one upward recurrence for every <code>l,x</code> regime.</li>
      <li>Checking low-order closed forms but not high-order asymptotics or dynamic-range separation.</li>
      <li>Mixing associated-Legendre implementations with and without the Condon–Shortley factor.</li>
      <li>Comparing real harmonics by labels such as <code>p_x</code> or <code>d_xy</code> rather than by transformation matrices.</li>
      <li>Treating a real harmonic as an eigenstate of <code>L_z</code>.</li>
      <li>Entering a factorial sum when the triangle or magnetic-sum rule is already violated.</li>
      <li>Interchanging the source-specific Gaunt <code>c</code> and a standard complex-harmonic triple integral <code>G</code> without a normalization map.</li>
      <li>Assuming that a selection-rule-allowed matrix element must be large.</li>
      <li>Treating the printed “Clebsch–Gordon” heading as the standard terminology.</li>
      <li>Applying a Chebyshev recurrence before mapping the spectrum into <code>[−1,1]</code>.</li>
