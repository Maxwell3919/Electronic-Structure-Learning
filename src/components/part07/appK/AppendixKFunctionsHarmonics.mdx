## K.1 Bessel、Neumann 与 Hankel 函数 | Bessel, Neumann, and Hankel Functions

<BilingualSection titleZh="三维 Helmholtz 方程的径向基" titleEn="Radial bases of the three-dimensional Helmholtz equation" eyebrow="【Martin K.1–K.3 | Martin K.1–K.3】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

对分离形式 <code>R_l(r)Y_lm(r̂)</code>，无源 Helmholtz equation 给出

$$
\left[\frac{1}{r^2}\frac{d}{dr}r^2\frac{d}{dr}
-\frac{l(l+1)}{r^2}+k^2\right]R_l(r)=0.
$$

令 <code>x=kr</code>，两个实线性独立解为球 Bessel <code>j_l(x)</code> 与球 Neumann <code>n_l(x)</code>。K.1–K.2 将它们与半整数阶 ordinary Bessel functions 联系；低阶式 K.3 固定了 sign 和 normalization：

$$
j_0=\frac{\sin x}{x},\qquad
n_0=-\frac{\cos x}{x},
$$

$$
j_1=\frac{\sin x}{x^2}-\frac{\cos x}{x},\qquad
n_1=-\frac{\cos x}{x^2}-\frac{\sin x}{x}.
$$

<code>j_l</code> 在原点 regular，<code>n_l</code> singular。这个差别决定 radial boundary condition，不能仅因二者都满足同一 differential equation 而交换。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For a separated field <code>R_l(r)Y_lm(r̂)</code>, the source-free Helmholtz equation gives

$$
\left[\frac{1}{r^2}\frac{d}{dr}r^2\frac{d}{dr}
-\frac{l(l+1)}{r^2}+k^2\right]R_l(r)=0.
$$

With <code>x=kr</code>, two real linearly independent solutions are the spherical Bessel function <code>j_l(x)</code> and spherical Neumann function <code>n_l(x)</code>. Equations K.1–K.2 connect them to half-integer ordinary Bessel functions, while the low-order formulas in K.3 fix sign and normalization:

$$
j_0=\frac{\sin x}{x},\qquad
n_0=-\frac{\cos x}{x},
$$

$$
j_1=\frac{\sin x}{x^2}-\frac{\cos x}{x},\qquad
n_1=-\frac{\cos x}{x^2}-\frac{\sin x}{x}.
$$

The function <code>j_l</code> is regular at the origin, whereas <code>n_l</code> is singular. They cannot be interchanged merely because both solve the same differential equation; the boundary condition selects the physical combination.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="递推、导数与 Wronskian" titleEn="Recurrences, derivatives, and the Wronskian" eyebrow="【补充推导 | Supplemental derivation】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

由 differential equation 或相邻阶恒等式得到

$$
f_{l+1}(x)=\frac{2l+1}{x}f_l(x)-f_{l-1}(x),
$$

$$
f_l'(x)=f_{l-1}(x)-\frac{l+1}{x}f_l(x),
\qquad f\in\{j,n\}.
$$

同一 normalization 下

$$
j_l n_l'-j_l'n_l=\frac{1}{x^2}.
$$

Wronskian 证明两解线性独立，也为 Appendix J 的 matching denominator 提供诊断。数值上，当 <code>j_l</code> 极小、<code>n_l</code> 极大时，直接相减会有 catastrophic cancellation；验证应在良态区检查 Wronskian，并在 <code>l≫x</code> 区单独检查渐近式。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The differential equation or adjacent-order identities give

$$
f_{l+1}(x)=\frac{2l+1}{x}f_l(x)-f_{l-1}(x),
$$

$$
f_l'(x)=f_{l-1}(x)-\frac{l+1}{x}f_l(x),
\qquad f\in\{j,n\}.
$$

Under one normalization,

$$
j_l n_l'-j_l'n_l=\frac{1}{x^2}.
$$

The Wronskian proves linear independence and diagnoses the matching denominator used in Appendix J. Numerically, direct subtraction suffers catastrophic cancellation when <code>j_l</code> is extremely small and <code>n_l</code> extremely large. A robust validation checks the Wronskian in a conditioned regime and checks the high-<code>l</code> asymptotic separately.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="原点与远场渐近" titleEn="Origin and far-field asymptotics" eyebrow="【渐近结构 | Asymptotic structure】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

当 <code>x→0</code>，

$$
j_l(x)\sim\frac{x^l}{(2l+1)!!},
\qquad
n_l(x)\sim-\frac{(2l-1)!!}{x^{l+1}}.
$$

当实 <code>x≫l</code>，

$$
j_l(x)\sim\frac{\sin(x-l\pi/2)}{x},
\qquad
n_l(x)\sim-\frac{\cos(x-l\pi/2)}{x}.
$$

第一组控制 radial-origin regularity；第二组使 Appendix J 的 phase shift 成为可见的远场相位平移。<code>l</code> 大于 <code>x</code> 很多时，<code>j_l</code> 的简单 upward recurrence 会混入 dominant <code>n_l</code> 解，网站内核改用幂级数。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

As <code>x→0</code>,

$$
j_l(x)\sim\frac{x^l}{(2l+1)!!},
\qquad
n_l(x)\sim-\frac{(2l-1)!!}{x^{l+1}}.
$$

For real <code>x≫l</code>,

$$
j_l(x)\sim\frac{\sin(x-l\pi/2)}{x},
\qquad
n_l(x)\sim-\frac{\cos(x-l\pi/2)}{x}.
$$

The first pair controls regularity at the radial origin. The second exposes the scattering phase shift in the far field. When <code>l</code> greatly exceeds <code>x</code>, simple upward recurrence for <code>j_l</code> can pick up the dominant <code>n_l</code> solution; the website kernel switches to a power series.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="Hankel combinations 与边界方向" titleEn="Hankel combinations and boundary direction" eyebrow="【Martin K.1 | Martin K.1】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

K.1 定义

$$
h_l^{(1)}=j_l+i n_l,
\qquad
h_l^{(2)}=j_l-i n_l.
$$

在常见 <code>e^(-iωt)</code> 时间 convention 下，实正波数的 <code>h_l^(1)</code> 对应 outgoing spherical wave，<code>h_l^(2)</code> 对应 incoming wave。若时间 Fourier sign 改变，标签解释也会改变。对 imaginary wave number，必须选择指数衰减的解析延拓；Appendix J 的负能束缚态正是这个边界条件。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Section K.1 defines

$$
h_l^{(1)}=j_l+i n_l,
\qquad
h_l^{(2)}=j_l-i n_l.
$$

Under the common <code>e^(-iωt)</code> time convention, <code>h_l^(1)</code> at positive real wave number is outgoing and <code>h_l^(2)</code> incoming. Reversing the Fourier-time sign reverses that interpretation. For an imaginary wave number, the physical analytic continuation must decay exponentially; this is the negative-energy boundary condition used in Appendix J.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## K.2 球谐与 Legendre 多项式 | Spherical Harmonics and Legendre Polynomials

<BilingualSection titleZh="复球谐的归一化" titleEn="Normalization of complex spherical harmonics" eyebrow="【Martin K.4–K.5 | Martin K.4–K.5】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

采用 K.4 convention，

$$
Y_{lm}(\theta,\phi)
=\sqrt{\frac{2l+1}{4\pi}\frac{(l-m)!}{(l+m)!}}
P_l^m(\cos\theta)e^{im\phi},
$$

满足

$$
\int d\Omega\,Y_{lm}^*(\Omega)Y_{l'm'}(\Omega)
=\delta_{ll'}\delta_{mm'}.
$$

normalization、<code>P_l^m</code> 中的 phase 与 azimuthal factor 必须作为一组固定。若只复制 normalization 而从另一库导入不含 Condon–Shortley factor 的 <code>P_l^m</code>，奇数 <code>m</code> 通道会翻转符号。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Under the K.4 convention,

$$
Y_{lm}(\theta,\phi)
=\sqrt{\frac{2l+1}{4\pi}\frac{(l-m)!}{(l+m)!}}
P_l^m(\cos\theta)e^{im\phi},
$$

with

$$
\int d\Omega\,Y_{lm}^*(\Omega)Y_{l'm'}(\Omega)
=\delta_{ll'}\delta_{mm'}.
$$

The normalization, the phase inside <code>P_l^m</code>, and the azimuthal factor must be fixed together. Copying the normalization while importing an associated-Legendre function without the Condon–Shortley factor flips every odd-<code>m</code> channel.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="Rodrigues、associated functions 与负 m" titleEn="Rodrigues, associated functions, and negative m" eyebrow="【Martin K.6–K.10 | Martin K.6–K.10】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Rodrigues formula 为

$$
P_l(x)=\frac{1}{2^l l!}\frac{d^l}{dx^l}(x^2-1)^l,
$$

K.6 的 associated form 为

$$
P_l^m(x)=(-1)^m(1-x^2)^{m/2}\frac{d^mP_l}{dx^m},
\qquad m\ge0.
$$

负 <code>m</code> 由

$$
P_l^{-m}(x)=(-1)^m\frac{(l-m)!}{(l+m)!}P_l^m(x)
$$

定义。结合 K.4 可得

$$
Y_{l,-m}=(-1)^mY_{lm}^*.
$$

低阶多项式 K.10 是快速 sign test；数值实现通常使用稳定 recurrence，而不是高阶 symbolic differentiation。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Rodrigues' formula is

$$
P_l(x)=\frac{1}{2^l l!}\frac{d^l}{dx^l}(x^2-1)^l,
$$

and the associated form in K.6 is

$$
P_l^m(x)=(-1)^m(1-x^2)^{m/2}\frac{d^mP_l}{dx^m},
\qquad m\ge0.
$$

Negative <code>m</code> is defined through

$$
P_l^{-m}(x)=(-1)^m\frac{(l-m)!}{(l+m)!}P_l^m(x).
$$

Combined with K.4, this gives

$$
Y_{l,-m}=(-1)^mY_{lm}^*.
$$

The low-order expressions of K.10 are useful sign tests. Numerical implementations normally use stable recurrences rather than high-order symbolic differentiation.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="Addition theorem 与部分波接口" titleEn="Addition theorem and the partial-wave interface" eyebrow="【补充连接 | Supplemental connection】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

由球谐完备性可得 addition theorem

$$
\sum_{m=-l}^{l}Y_{lm}(\hat r)Y_{lm}^*(\hat q)
=\frac{2l+1}{4\pi}P_l(\hat r\cdot\hat q).
$$

它把 Appendix J 的球谐平面波展开 J.1 化为轴对称 Legendre form J.2。这里的 <code>4π</code>、<code>i^l</code> 和 complex conjugate 位置都由同一 normalization 决定。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Completeness gives the addition theorem

$$
\sum_{m=-l}^{l}Y_{lm}(\hat r)Y_{lm}^*(\hat q)
=\frac{2l+1}{4\pi}P_l(\hat r\cdot\hat q).
$$

It converts the spherical-harmonic plane-wave expansion J.1 into the axisymmetric Legendre form J.2. The factors <code>4π</code>, <code>i^l</code>, and the placement of complex conjugation are all fixed by one normalization convention.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## K.3 实球谐 | Real Spherical Harmonics

<BilingualSection titleZh="Martin K.11 的复—实变换" titleEn="The complex-to-real transformation of Martin K.11" eyebrow="【Martin K.11 | Martin K.11】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

对 <code>m&gt;0</code>，K.11 定义

$$
S_{lm}^{+}=\frac{Y_{lm}+Y_{lm}^*}{\sqrt2}=\sqrt2\,\mathrm{Re}\,Y_{lm},
$$

$$
S_{lm}^{-}=\frac{Y_{lm}-Y_{lm}^*}{\sqrt2 i}=\sqrt2\,\mathrm{Im}\,Y_{lm},
$$

并令 <code>S_l^0=Y_l0</code>。在每个 <code>±m</code> 二维子空间，这是一组 unitary basis change，因此保持内积、总 density 与完整子空间 projector。

实球谐便于显示和构造 real-valued localized orbitals，但它们把 <code>+m</code>、<code>−m</code> 混合，通常不是 <code>L_z</code> eigenstates。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For <code>m&gt;0</code>, K.11 defines

$$
S_{lm}^{+}=\frac{Y_{lm}+Y_{lm}^*}{\sqrt2}=\sqrt2\,\mathrm{Re}\,Y_{lm},
$$

$$
S_{lm}^{-}=\frac{Y_{lm}-Y_{lm}^*}{\sqrt2 i}=\sqrt2\,\mathrm{Im}\,Y_{lm},
$$

with <code>S_l^0=Y_l0</code>. In each two-dimensional <code>±m</code> subspace, this is a unitary basis change, so it preserves inner products, total density, and the complete subspace projector.

Real harmonics are convenient for visualization and real-valued localized orbitals, but they mix <code>+m</code> and <code>−m</code> and are generally not eigenstates of <code>L_z</code>.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>
