## 常见误判、原创练习与来源边界 | Misjudgments, Original Exercises, and Source Boundaries

<BilingualSection titleZh="会改变推导或计算结论的误判" titleEn="Misjudgments that change a derivation or calculation" eyebrow="【补充说明 | Supplemental clarification】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

1. **把电子通道展开本身称为 Born–Oppenheimer 近似。** 完备展开仍包含全部通道；近似发生在忽略非对角 $C_{ii'}$ 时。
2. **只凭核质量大就宣布绝热。** $m_e/M_J$ 小会抑制核动能，但电子能隙、导数耦合和核速度可抵消这种抑制。
3. **由归一化直接推出任意 gauge 下 $A_{ii}=0$。** 归一化只保证对角导数耦合纯虚；令其为零还需要相位约定。
4. **把 Martin 的 $B_{ii}$ 与所有文献中的正 DBOC 同号。** 不同来源的定义和 Hamiltonian 排列不同，必须核对符号。
5. **把有限带隙当作非绝热跃迁严格为零。** 有限带隙只提供分母；跃迁还受核速度、numerator、轨迹和相位影响。
6. **在简并点直接使用式 C.8。** 分母为零时逐态公式失效，应处理整个简并子空间。
7. **把导数耦合 $d$ 与 EPC 矩阵元 $g$ 混为同一个量。** 前者含电子能量分母，后者通常使用势导数 numerator 乘声子零点振幅。
8. **从 metallic bands 或单个 $g$ 推断超导。** 还需要完整电子/声子相空间、$\alpha^2F$、$\lambda$、$\omega_{\log}$、数值收敛和理论适用条件。
9. **把静态 frozen-phonon 稳定性等同于动力学过程绝热。** 势能面曲率与真实时间演化中的通道跃迁是不同问题。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

1. **Calling the electronic-channel expansion itself the Born-Oppenheimer approximation.** A complete expansion still contains all channels; the approximation occurs when off-diagonal $C_{ii'}$ terms are neglected.
2. **Declaring adiabaticity from large nuclear mass alone.** A small $m_e/M_J$ suppresses nuclear kinetic energy, but electronic gaps, derivative couplings, and nuclear velocity can offset that suppression.
3. **Using normalization to set $A_{ii}=0$ in every gauge.** Normalization only makes the diagonal derivative coupling purely imaginary; setting it to zero also requires a phase convention.
4. **Assuming Martin's $B_{ii}$ has the same sign as every positive DBOC definition.** Sources use different definitions and Hamiltonian rearrangements; signs must be checked.
5. **Treating a finite gap as proof of exactly zero nonadiabatic transition.** The gap supplies a denominator, while velocity, the numerator, trajectory, and phase also matter.
6. **Applying Eq. C.8 directly at degeneracy.** When the denominator vanishes, a state-by-state formula fails and the degenerate subspace must be treated as a matrix.
7. **Identifying derivative coupling $d$ with EPC matrix element $g$.** The former contains an electronic energy denominator; the latter normally uses the potential-derivative numerator multiplied by the phonon zero-point amplitude.
8. **Inferring superconductivity from metallic bands or one large $g$.** Complete electron/phonon phase space, $\alpha^2F$, $\lambda$, $\omega_{\log}$, numerical convergence, and method applicability are still required.
9. **Equating static frozen-phonon stability with adiabatic real-time dynamics.** Potential-surface curvature and channel transitions during time evolution are distinct questions.

<BilingualSection titleZh="原创练习" titleEn="Original exercises" eyebrow="【原创教学内容 | Original teaching content】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

1. **乘积法则。** 从 $\hat T_N=-\sum_Jm_e\nabla_J^2/(2M_J)$ 和通道展开出发，逐项推导式 C.4–C.6，检查每个负号和质量因子。
2. **gauge 变换。** 对 $|\Psi_i\rangle\to e^{i\theta_i(\mathbf R)}|\Psi_i\rangle$ 推导 $\mathbf d_{ii}$ 的变换，并构造一条开放路径上的平行输运 gauge。
3. **二阶归一化。** 在实 gauge 中证明 $\langle\Psi_i|\nabla_J^2\Psi_i\rangle=-\langle\nabla_J\Psi_i|\nabla_J\Psi_i\rangle$，再说明复 gauge 下严格确定的是实部。
4. **两能级模型。** 对 $H(R)=aR\sigma_z+\Delta\sigma_x$ 求绝热能量和本征态，验证 $|d_{-+}|=a\Delta/[2((aR)^2+\Delta^2)]$ 及式 C.8。
5. **质量缩放。** 对相同力常数比较质量 $M$ 与 $4M$ 的谐振频率、零点能和导数修正尺度，区分 $M^{-1/2}$ 与 $M^{-1}$ 缩放。
6. **简并子空间。** 令 $\Delta\to0$ 分析两能级模型在 $R=0$ 的逐态导数，并解释为什么应选择 diabatic 或子空间形式。
7. **声子归一化。** 从 normal-coordinate Hamiltonian 推导 $\sqrt{\hbar/(2M_J\omega)}$，检查位移、势导数和 $g$ 的单位。
8. **相位跟踪数值实验。** 对相邻参数点的本征向量随机乘相位，展示直接有限差分失败；再用 overlap 最大化修复并检查步长收敛。
9. **金属判据。** 设计一个二维参数表，分别改变声子频率、电子展宽、q 点和 Fermi-surface sampling，说明哪些结果可测试静态绝热极限，哪些需要动态响应。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

1. **Product rule.** Starting from $\hat T_N=-\sum_Jm_e\nabla_J^2/(2M_J)$ and the channel expansion, derive Eqs. C.4-C.6 term by term, checking every sign and mass factor.
2. **Gauge transformation.** For $|\Psi_i\rangle\to e^{i\theta_i(\mathbf R)}|\Psi_i\rangle$, derive the transformation of $\mathbf d_{ii}$ and construct a parallel-transport gauge along an open path.
3. **Second derivative of normalization.** In a real gauge, prove $\langle\Psi_i|\nabla_J^2\Psi_i\rangle=-\langle\nabla_J\Psi_i|\nabla_J\Psi_i\rangle$, then explain why only the real part is fixed in a complex gauge.
4. **Two-level model.** For $H(R)=aR\sigma_z+\Delta\sigma_x$, derive the adiabatic energies and eigenstates and verify $|d_{-+}|=a\Delta/[2((aR)^2+\Delta^2)]$ and Eq. C.8.
5. **Mass scaling.** For the same force constant, compare harmonic frequencies, zero-point energies, and derivative-correction scales for masses $M$ and $4M$, distinguishing $M^{-1/2}$ from $M^{-1}$ behavior.
6. **Degenerate subspace.** Take $\Delta\to0$ in the two-level model at $R=0$ and explain why a diabatic or subspace formulation is required.
7. **Phonon normalization.** Derive $\sqrt{\hbar/(2M_J\omega)}$ from a normal-coordinate Hamiltonian and check the units of displacement, potential derivative, and $g$.
8. **Numerical phase tracking.** Multiply eigenvectors at adjacent parameter points by random phases to show that direct finite differences fail; repair them by overlap maximization and check step-size convergence.
9. **Metallic criterion.** Design a two-dimensional parameter table varying phonon frequency, electronic broadening, q point, and Fermi-surface sampling. State which results test a static adiabatic limit and which require dynamic response.

  </div>
