## 绝热投影与核方程 | Adiabatic Projection and Nuclear Equation

<BilingualSection titleZh="忽略非对角通道耦合" titleEn="Neglecting off-diagonal channel couplings" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

绝热或 Born–Oppenheimer 近似在 Martin 的表述中，是对 $i\ne i'$ 的 $C_{ii'}=A_{ii'}+B_{ii'}$ 置零。电子波函数 $\Psi_i(\mathbf r;\mathbf R)$ 和能量 $E_i(\mathbf R)$ 随核位置连续变化，但总态只保留一个电子通道；核自由度不通过这些非对角项向另一个电子态传递振幅。

对角项保留后，Martin 定义

$$
U_i(\mathbf R)=E_i(\mathbf R)+B_{ii}(\mathbf R),
$$

并得到式 C.7：

$$
\boxed{
\left[
-\sum_J\frac{m_e}{2M_J}\nabla_J^2
+U_i(\mathbf R)-E_{ni}
\right]\chi_{ni}(\mathbf R)=0
}.
$$

$n$ 标记同一电子势能面上的核量子态。若进一步忽略质量抑制的 $B_{ii}$，核就在 $E_i(\mathbf R)$ 上运动。这是 frozen-phonon 势能曲率和 Born–Oppenheimer 分子动力学的共同起点。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

In Martin's formulation, the adiabatic or Born-Oppenheimer approximation sets $C_{ii'}=A_{ii'}+B_{ii'}$ to zero for $i\ne i'$. The electronic wavefunction $\Psi_i(\mathbf r;\mathbf R)$ and energy $E_i(\mathbf R)$ continue to change with nuclear position, but the total state remains in one electronic channel; the nuclear degrees of freedom no longer transfer amplitude to another electronic state through those off-diagonal terms.

After retaining the diagonal contribution, Martin defines

$$
U_i(\mathbf R)=E_i(\mathbf R)+B_{ii}(\mathbf R)
$$

and obtains Eq. C.7,

$$
\boxed{
\left[
-\sum_J\frac{m_e}{2M_J}\nabla_J^2
+U_i(\mathbf R)-E_{ni}
\right]\chi_{ni}(\mathbf R)=0
}.
$$

The index $n$ labels nuclear quantum states on the same electronic surface. If the mass-suppressed $B_{ii}$ term is also neglected, the nuclei move on $E_i(\mathbf R)$. This is the common starting point of frozen-phonon curvature calculations and Born-Oppenheimer molecular dynamics.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="归一化、对角一次导数与 gauge" titleEn="Normalization, diagonal first derivative, and gauge" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 + 补充澄清 | Martin source + clarification】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

对归一化条件求一次导数得到

$$
0=\nabla_J\langle\Psi_i|\Psi_i\rangle
=\langle\nabla_J\Psi_i|\Psi_i\rangle
+\langle\Psi_i|\nabla_J\Psi_i\rangle.
$$

因此

$$
2\operatorname{Re}\mathbf d_{ii}^{J}=0,
$$

严格结论是对角导数耦合纯虚，而不是在任意相位约定下自动为零。Martin 采用可令 $A_{ii}=0$ 的约定；对非简并实 Hamiltonian 可选局域实 gauge，或沿路径选平行输运 gauge，使

$$
\langle\Psi_i|\nabla_J\Psi_i\rangle=0.
$$

若作相位变换 $|\Psi_i\rangle\to e^{i\theta_i(\mathbf R)}|\Psi_i\rangle$，则

$$
\mathbf d_{ii}^{J}\to\mathbf d_{ii}^{J}+i\nabla_J\theta_i.
$$

因此删除对角 $A_{ii}$ 必须与 gauge 选择一起陈述。闭合参数路径、简并子空间或拓扑问题可能保留不可全局消去的几何相位，相关数学见 Appendix P。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Differentiating normalization once gives

$$
0=\nabla_J\langle\Psi_i|\Psi_i\rangle
=\langle\nabla_J\Psi_i|\Psi_i\rangle
+\langle\Psi_i|\nabla_J\Psi_i\rangle.
$$

Hence

$$
2\operatorname{Re}\mathbf d_{ii}^{J}=0.
$$

The exact conclusion is that the diagonal derivative coupling is purely imaginary, not that it vanishes in every phase convention. Martin uses a convention in which $A_{ii}=0$. For a nondegenerate real Hamiltonian one may choose a local real gauge, or a parallel-transport gauge along a path, such that

$$
\langle\Psi_i|\nabla_J\Psi_i\rangle=0.
$$

Under $|\Psi_i\rangle\to e^{i\theta_i(\mathbf R)}|\Psi_i\rangle$,

$$
\mathbf d_{ii}^{J}\to\mathbf d_{ii}^{J}+i\nabla_J\theta_i.
$$

Eliminating diagonal $A_{ii}$ must therefore be stated together with a gauge choice. Closed parameter loops, degenerate subspaces, or topological problems can retain a geometric phase that cannot be removed globally; Appendix P develops the related mathematics.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="二次导数项及其符号约定" titleEn="Second-derivative term and its sign convention" eyebrow="【补充推导 | Supplemental derivation】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

在局域实或平行输运 gauge 中，对归一化条件求二次导数：

$$
0=\nabla_J^2\langle\Psi_i|\Psi_i\rangle
=2\langle\nabla_J\Psi_i|\nabla_J\Psi_i\rangle
+2\operatorname{Re}\langle\Psi_i|\nabla_J^2\Psi_i\rangle.
$$

因此

$$
\operatorname{Re}\tau_{ii}^{J}
=-\langle\nabla_J\Psi_i|\nabla_J\Psi_i\rangle.
$$

按 Martin 式 C.6 的定义，$B_{ii}$ 在这一实 gauge 中为非正量，其绝对值按 $m_e/M_J$ 缩放。其他文献常把正量

$$
\sum_J\frac{m_e}{2M_J}
\langle\nabla_J\Psi_i|\nabla_J\Psi_i\rangle
$$

称为 diagonal Born–Oppenheimer correction，并采用与 Martin 的 $B_{ii}$ 相反的符号或不同的有效 Hamiltonian 排列。复用公式前必须核对定义，不能只比较名称。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

In a local real or parallel-transport gauge, differentiating normalization twice gives

$$
0=\nabla_J^2\langle\Psi_i|\Psi_i\rangle
=2\langle\nabla_J\Psi_i|\nabla_J\Psi_i\rangle
+2\operatorname{Re}\langle\Psi_i|\nabla_J^2\Psi_i\rangle.
$$

Therefore

$$
\operatorname{Re}\tau_{ii}^{J}
=-\langle\nabla_J\Psi_i|\nabla_J\Psi_i\rangle.
$$

With the definition in Martin's Eq. C.6, $B_{ii}$ is non-positive in this real gauge and its magnitude scales with $m_e/M_J$. Other literature often calls the positive quantity

$$
\sum_J\frac{m_e}{2M_J}
\langle\nabla_J\Psi_i|\nabla_J\Psi_i\rangle
$$

the diagonal Born-Oppenheimer correction, using the opposite sign from Martin's $B_{ii}$ or a differently rearranged effective Hamiltonian. Definitions must be compared before formulas are reused; a shared name is insufficient.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

It does not claim to exhaust all gauge-covariant geometric terms or identify an arbitrary software output field with Martin's $B_{ii}$.

## 平衡位置附近的谐振动 | Harmonic Motion Near Equilibrium

<BilingualSection titleZh="从势能面曲率到 normal modes" titleEn="From potential-surface curvature to normal modes" eyebrow="【补充推导 | Supplemental derivation】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

令 $\mathbf R^0$ 为电子态 $i$ 的稳定平衡构型，并写位移 $u_{J\alpha}=R_{J\alpha}-R^0_{J\alpha}$。若一阶力为零且势能面二阶可微，

$$
U_i(\mathbf R)
=U_i(\mathbf R^0)
+\frac12\sum_{J\alpha,K\beta}
\Phi_{J\alpha,K\beta}u_{J\alpha}u_{K\beta}
+O(u^3),
$$

其中

$$
\Phi_{J\alpha,K\beta}
=\left.
\frac{\partial^2U_i}
{\partial R_{J\alpha}\partial R_{K\beta}}
\right|_{\mathbf R^0}.
$$

引入质量加权坐标 $q_{J\alpha}=\sqrt{M_J/m_e}\,u_{J\alpha}$，二次 Hamiltonian 的矩阵为

$$
D_{J\alpha,K\beta}
=\frac{m_e\Phi_{J\alpha,K\beta}}
{\sqrt{M_JM_K}}.
$$

对 $D$ 对角化得到 $\omega_\nu^2$ 和 normal coordinates $Q_\nu$，核方程分解成

$$
\left[-\frac12\frac{d^2}{dQ_\nu^2}
+\frac12\omega_\nu^2Q_\nu^2\right]\chi
=\epsilon_\nu\chi.
$$

因此基态能量包含 $\frac12\sum_\nu\hbar\omega_\nu$。二阶展开只有在平衡点、Hessian 数值收敛且没有需要处理的非绝热通道时才支持谐振动判断。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Let $\mathbf R^0$ be a stable equilibrium configuration on electronic state $i$ and define $u_{J\alpha}=R_{J\alpha}-R^0_{J\alpha}$. If the first derivative vanishes and the surface is twice differentiable,

$$
U_i(\mathbf R)
=U_i(\mathbf R^0)
+\frac12\sum_{J\alpha,K\beta}
\Phi_{J\alpha,K\beta}u_{J\alpha}u_{K\beta}
+O(u^3),
$$

where

$$
\Phi_{J\alpha,K\beta}
=\left.
\frac{\partial^2U_i}
{\partial R_{J\alpha}\partial R_{K\beta}}
\right|_{\mathbf R^0}.
$$

With mass-weighted coordinates $q_{J\alpha}=\sqrt{M_J/m_e}\,u_{J\alpha}$, the quadratic Hamiltonian contains

$$
D_{J\alpha,K\beta}
=\frac{m_e\Phi_{J\alpha,K\beta}}
{\sqrt{M_JM_K}}.
$$

Diagonalizing $D$ gives $\omega_\nu^2$ and normal coordinates $Q_\nu$, reducing the nuclear equation to

$$
\left[-\frac12\frac{d^2}{dQ_\nu^2}
+\frac12\omega_\nu^2Q_\nu^2\right]\chi
=\epsilon_\nu\chi.
$$

The ground-state energy therefore contains $\frac12\sum_\nu\hbar\omega_\nu$. This quadratic expansion supports a harmonic-vibration judgment only at an equilibrium point, after the Hessian is numerically converged, and when relevant nonadiabatic channels have been addressed.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

## 绝热近似的失效条件 | Breakdown of the Adiabatic Approximation

<AvoidedCrossingExplorer />

<BilingualSection titleZh="能隙、核速度和导数耦合是不同条件" titleEn="Gap, nuclear velocity, and derivative coupling are distinct conditions" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 + 补充分析 | Martin source + supplemental analysis】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Martin 指出：若电子激发能隙远大于典型核运动能量，绝热项通常能够良好描述核激发；电子态简并或近简并时必须特别处理。沿经典核路径 $\mathbf R(t)$，瞬时电子基的时间导数为

$$
\frac{d}{dt}|\Psi_i\rangle
=\sum_J\dot{\mathbf R}_J\cdot\nabla_J|\Psi_i\rangle,
$$

通道 $i\to j$ 的运动学耦合尺度为

$$
\left|\sum_J\dot{\mathbf R}_J\cdot\mathbf d_{ji}^{J}\right|.
$$

将它与 $|E_j-E_i|/\hbar$ 比较，可构造局域绝热指标。有限能隙并不自动保证指标小；很快的核运动或很大的导数耦合仍可造成通道混合。反过来，金属没有全局带隙，也不意味着所有静态声子计算都必然无效；需要针对 $\mathbf q$、$\omega$、占据、电子散射和目标 observable 检查动态修正。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Martin states that nuclear excitations are usually well described by adiabatic terms when the electronic excitation gap is much larger than typical nuclear-motion energies, while degeneracies and near degeneracies require special care. Along a classical nuclear path $\mathbf R(t)$,

$$
\frac{d}{dt}|\Psi_i\rangle
=\sum_J\dot{\mathbf R}_J\cdot\nabla_J|\Psi_i\rangle,
$$

so the kinematic coupling scale for channel $i\to j$ is

$$
\left|\sum_J\dot{\mathbf R}_J\cdot\mathbf d_{ji}^{J}\right|.
$$

Comparison with $|E_j-E_i|/\hbar$ produces a local adiabatic indicator. A finite gap does not automatically make that indicator small; rapid nuclear motion or a large derivative coupling can still mix channels. Conversely, the absence of a global gap in a metal does not make every static phonon calculation automatically invalid. Dynamic corrections must be assessed for the relevant $\mathbf q$, $\omega$, occupations, electronic scattering, and target observable.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="失效信号不等于最终物理结论" titleEn="A breakdown signal is not a final physical conclusion" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

小能隙、尖锐 avoided crossing、较大导数耦合或对电子展宽敏感，都是需要升级模型的信号。它们不能单独证明发生了表面跳跃、锥形交叉动力学、Kohn anomaly、结构相变或超导。最终结论还需要匹配的时间依赖方法、采样、收敛和可观测量判据。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A small gap, sharp avoided crossing, large derivative coupling, or sensitivity to electronic broadening signals that the model may need to be extended. None alone proves surface hopping, conical-intersection dynamics, a Kohn anomaly, a structural transition, or superconductivity. A final conclusion requires a matching time-dependent method, sampling, convergence tests, and an observable-specific criterion.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>
