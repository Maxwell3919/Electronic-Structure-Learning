## 使用定位 | Reference Orientation

<BilingualSection titleZh="本附录解决什么问题" titleEn="What this Appendix resolves" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 + 补充组织 | Martin source + supplemental organization】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

电子结构计算通常先固定核坐标，求电子能量 $E_i(\mathbf R)$，再把它当作核运动的势能面。Appendix C 说明这一步从完整电子—核 Hamiltonian 如何得到，以及被丢弃的项是什么。关键小参数来自核动能中的质量比 $m_e/M_J$；但是否可以忽略非绝热通道，还取决于电子能隙、核速度、导数耦合和简并结构。

原书 C.1 先在每个核构型上建立完备电子本征态，再把完整电子—核态展开到这些电子通道中。核动能对该展开作用后产生一次与二次核坐标导数矩阵元。绝热近似忽略不同电子通道之间的非对角耦合，留下每个电子态对应的核方程。C.2 进一步把电子态对核坐标的导数写成势导数矩阵元除以电子能量差，并连接到 Chapter 20 的电子—声子矩阵元。

本附录支持 Chapters 19–21：Chapter 19 的 Born–Oppenheimer 分子动力学沿单一电子势能面传播核；Chapter 20 的 frozen phonon、DFPT 和 EPC 需要知道绝热与非绝热对象的边界；Chapter 21 的“adiabatic kernel”是时间依赖响应中的另一种用法，不能与核—电子绝热分离静默混同。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Electronic-structure calculations commonly fix the nuclei, solve for an electronic energy $E_i(\mathbf R)$, and use it as a potential-energy surface for nuclear motion. Appendix C shows how that construction follows from the full electron-nuclear Hamiltonian and identifies the discarded terms. The mass ratio $m_e/M_J$ enters as a small parameter through the nuclear kinetic energy, but the neglect of nonadiabatic channels also depends on electronic gaps, nuclear velocities, derivative couplings, and degeneracies.

Section C.1 first constructs a complete set of electronic eigenstates at every nuclear configuration and expands the full electron-nuclear state in those electronic channels. When the nuclear kinetic operator acts on this expansion, first- and second-derivative matrix elements appear. The adiabatic approximation neglects off-diagonal couplings between different electronic channels and leaves a nuclear equation for each electronic state. Section C.2 then relates the nuclear-coordinate derivative of an electronic state to a potential-derivative matrix element divided by an electronic energy separation, connecting the result to the electron-phonon matrix elements of Chapter 20.

This Appendix supports Chapters 19-21. Born-Oppenheimer molecular dynamics in Chapter 19 propagates nuclei on one electronic surface. Frozen phonons, DFPT, and EPC in Chapter 20 require a clear boundary between adiabatic and nonadiabatic objects. The “adiabatic kernel” of time-dependent response in Chapter 21 is a different use of the word and must not be silently identified with electron-nuclear adiabatic separation.

</BilingualSection>

## 数学对象、单位与约定 | Mathematical Objects, Units, and Conventions

<BilingualSection titleZh="坐标、态空间与内积" titleEn="Coordinates, state spaces, and inner products" eyebrow="【定义 | Definitions】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

用 $\mathbf r=\{\mathbf r_a,\sigma_a\}$ 表示全部电子空间与自旋变量，用 $\mathbf R=\{\mathbf R_J\}$ 表示全部核坐标。固定 $\mathbf R$ 的电子 Hamiltonian 为

$$
\hat H_e(\mathbf R)=\hat T_e+\hat U(\mathbf r,\mathbf R),
$$

满足

$$
\hat H_e(\mathbf R)\Psi_i(\mathbf r;\mathbf R)
=E_i(\mathbf R)\Psi_i(\mathbf r;\mathbf R).
$$

电子态在每个核构型上正交归一：

$$
\langle\Psi_i(\mathbf R)|\Psi_j(\mathbf R)\rangle_{\mathbf r}=\delta_{ij}.
$$

下标 $\mathbf r$ 表示只积分电子变量；$\nabla_J$ 作用于参数 $\mathbf R_J$。核通道振幅 $\chi_{si}(\mathbf R)$ 属于核坐标函数空间，并服从核边界条件。电子完备性应理解为在所选电子 Hilbert 空间内包含离散与必要的连续谱；实际有限基截断会引入额外误差。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Let $\mathbf r=\{\mathbf r_a,\sigma_a\}$ denote all electronic spatial and spin variables and $\mathbf R=\{\mathbf R_J\}$ all nuclear coordinates. The fixed-$\mathbf R$ electronic Hamiltonian is

$$
\hat H_e(\mathbf R)=\hat T_e+\hat U(\mathbf r,\mathbf R),
$$

with

$$
\hat H_e(\mathbf R)\Psi_i(\mathbf r;\mathbf R)
=E_i(\mathbf R)\Psi_i(\mathbf r;\mathbf R).
$$

The electronic states are orthonormal at each nuclear configuration,

$$
\langle\Psi_i(\mathbf R)|\Psi_j(\mathbf R)\rangle_{\mathbf r}=\delta_{ij}.
$$

The subscript $\mathbf r$ means that only electronic variables are integrated, while $\nabla_J$ differentiates the parameter $\mathbf R_J$. Nuclear channel amplitudes $\chi_{si}(\mathbf R)$ belong to a function space over nuclear coordinates and obey nuclear boundary conditions. Electronic completeness should include the discrete and necessary continuum spectrum in the chosen Hilbert space; a practical finite-basis truncation adds a separate approximation.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="质量与单位约定" titleEn="Mass and unit convention" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

本页使用 Hartree 原子单位，但像 Martin 一样保留电子质量与核质量之比，以显示展开参数：

$$
\hat T_N
=-\sum_J\frac{m_e}{2M_J}\nabla_J^2.
$$

若 $M_J$ 已用电子质量为单位，则 $m_e=1$。$\nabla_J$ 的量纲为长度的负一次方，$m_e\nabla_J^2/M_J$ 具有能量量纲。对 normal coordinate 或质量加权坐标改写时，质量因子会移动到坐标、极化向量或零点振幅中，必须声明具体归一化。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The page uses Hartree atomic units but, following Martin, retains the electron-to-nuclear mass ratio to display the expansion parameter,

$$
\hat T_N
=-\sum_J\frac{m_e}{2M_J}\nabla_J^2.
$$

If $M_J$ is already measured in electron masses, then $m_e=1$. The operator $\nabla_J$ has dimensions of inverse length, so $m_e\nabla_J^2/M_J$ has dimensions of energy. Rewriting the problem in normal or mass-weighted coordinates moves mass factors into the coordinates, polarization vectors, or zero-point amplitude; the normalization must therefore be stated explicitly.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="术语边界" titleEn="Terminology boundary" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Martin 在本附录把“adiabatic approximation”和“Born–Oppenheimer approximation”并用，指忽略不同电子态之间的非对角耦合。更广泛文献有时把 Born–Oppenheimer 称为静态势能面分离，把 adiabatic theorem 称为缓慢参数变化下保持瞬时本征态。网站引用外部来源时必须说明采用哪种定义，不能用同一个词自动合并不同近似。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Martin uses “adiabatic approximation” and “Born-Oppenheimer approximation” together here for the neglect of off-diagonal couplings between electronic states. Wider literature sometimes reserves Born-Oppenheimer for static potential-surface separation and the adiabatic theorem for following an instantaneous eigenstate under slow parameter variation. Any external use must state its definition rather than merging distinct approximations through a shared word.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>
