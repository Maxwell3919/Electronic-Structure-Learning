## F.1 基本问题 | Basic Issues

<BilingualSection titleZh="长程项必须组成中性分组" titleEn="Long-range terms must be organized into neutral groups" eyebrow="【Martin F.1 | Martin F.1】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Kohn–Sham 总能中的经典 Coulomb 部分来自电子、核或离子及外部电荷。单独写电子 Hartree 能、裸核势和核—核能时，每一项都可能在无限周期体系中发散；物理总能之所以有限，是因为这些项的长程部分相互抵消。正确实现必须在解析式和数值离散中保存这一抵消，而不能分别计算三个发散量后期待浮点数自动给出有限差。

交换—相关和非局域量子项通常是短程或按晶胞可积的；Appendix F 的收敛问题集中在经典 $1/r$ 部分。原书强调三种等价重排：均匀背景、涂抹离子和中性原子参考。它们改变的是记账方式，不应改变同一 Hamiltonian、同一边界条件下的总能、力和应力。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The classical Coulomb part of the Kohn-Sham total energy is generated by electrons, nuclei or ions, and external charge. If the electronic Hartree energy, bare nuclear potential, and nucleus-nucleus energy are written separately, each can diverge in an infinite periodic system. The physical total remains finite because their long-range pieces cancel. A correct implementation must preserve that cancellation analytically and numerically rather than subtracting independently evaluated divergent quantities in floating-point arithmetic.

Exchange-correlation and nonlocal quantum terms are normally short ranged or cell integrable. The convergence problem of Appendix F belongs to the classical $1/r$ terms. Martin emphasizes three equivalent rearrangements: a uniform background, smeared ions, and neutral-atom references. They change bookkeeping, not the total energy, forces, or stress for the same Hamiltonian and boundary conditions.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="为什么裸周期 Coulomb 和不够" titleEn="Why the bare periodic Coulomb sum is insufficient" eyebrow="【Martin F.1–F.2 | Martin F.1–F.2】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

形式上的周期点电荷势

$$
V(\mathbf r)=\sum_{\mathbf T}\frac{q}{|\mathbf r-\mathbf T|}
$$

既不绝对收敛，也没有内禀的平均值。若每个晶胞净电荷 $Q\neq0$，半径 $R$ 内包含的总电荷随 $R^3$ 增长，远场能量直接发散。即使 $Q=0$，dipole 排列和求和区域形状仍可能留下条件收敛的宏观项。

因此有限结果的定义至少包含：晶胞电荷约束；是否加入均匀背景；平均电场边界；平均势零点；无穷求和的形状或等价外部介质。代码输出一个数字并不自动证明这些条件已被满足。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The formal periodic point-charge potential

$$
V(\mathbf r)=\sum_{\mathbf T}\frac{q}{|\mathbf r-\mathbf T|}
$$

is not absolutely convergent and has no intrinsic average value. If each cell carries $Q\neq0$, the charge enclosed within radius $R$ grows as $R^3$ and the far-field energy diverges. Even for $Q=0$, a dipole arrangement and the shape of the summation region can leave a conditionally convergent macroscopic term.

A finite result therefore requires at least a cell-charge constraint, a statement about any uniform background, a macroscopic-field boundary, a potential-zero convention, and a summation shape or equivalent exterior dielectric. A numerical value from a code does not by itself establish those conditions.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="外势的命名陷阱" titleEn="The naming trap in the external potential" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

DFT 中的 $V_{\mathrm{ext}}$ 常指核对电子的势，但无限周期核势本身并不是有限对象。实际平面波公式中的“external potential”已经与电子 Hartree 长程部分、离子背景或伪电荷约定配套。把它从该配套中抽出，再单独比较不同代码的 $G=0$ 值，通常没有物理意义。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

In DFT, $V_{\mathrm{ext}}$ often denotes the potential of the nuclei acting on electrons, yet the bare nuclear potential of an infinite periodic array is not finite. The “external potential” used in practical plane-wave formulas is already paired with the long-range electronic Hartree part and with an ionic-background or pseudocharge convention. Extracting it from that grouping and comparing isolated $G=0$ values between codes is generally not physically meaningful.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

## F.2 背景中的点电荷：Ewald 求和 | Point Charges in a Background: Ewald Sums

<BilingualSection titleZh="Gaussian 筛分恒等式" titleEn="Gaussian screening identity" eyebrow="【Martin F.2 | Martin F.2】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Ewald 方法在每个点电荷旁加上并减去同一个 Gaussian。利用

$$
\frac{1}{r}=\frac{\operatorname{erfc}(\alpha r)}{r}
+\frac{\operatorname{erf}(\alpha r)}{r},
$$

第一项在实空间按 $\exp(-\alpha^2r^2)$ 快速衰减；第二项是平滑 Gaussian 电荷的势，可在倒空间快速求和。其非零倒格矢 kernel 为

$$
\frac{4\pi}{G^2}\exp\!\left(-\frac{G^2}{4\alpha^2}\right),
\qquad \mathbf G\neq0.
$$

$\alpha$ 只决定工作在两空间之间的分配。较大 $\alpha$ 使实空间更短程，却要求更多倒格矢；较小 $\alpha$ 则相反。只有两边都达到收敛时，总结果才与 $\alpha$ 无关。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The Ewald construction adds and subtracts the same Gaussian around every point charge. The identity

$$
\frac{1}{r}=\frac{\operatorname{erfc}(\alpha r)}{r}
+\frac{\operatorname{erf}(\alpha r)}{r}
$$

makes the first term decay rapidly in real space as $\exp(-\alpha^2r^2)$. The second is the potential of a smooth Gaussian charge and is rapidly summed in reciprocal space, where its nonzero reciprocal-vector kernel is

$$
\frac{4\pi}{G^2}\exp\!\left(-\frac{G^2}{4\alpha^2}\right),
\qquad \mathbf G\neq0.
$$

The parameter $\alpha$ only partitions work between the two spaces. A larger $\alpha$ shortens the real-space range but requires more reciprocal vectors; a smaller value does the reverse. The total becomes independent of $\alpha$ only after both sums are converged.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="中性背景下的势与零平均 convention" titleEn="Potential with a neutralizing background and zero-average convention" eyebrow="【Martin Eq. F.4 的统一记号 | Unified notation for Martin Eq. F.4】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

对周期单位点电荷格子，加均匀负背景并选择晶胞平均势为零，可写成

$$
\begin{aligned}
V_\alpha(\mathbf r)=&\sum_{\mathbf T}
\frac{\operatorname{erfc}(\alpha|\mathbf r-\mathbf T|)}{|\mathbf r-\mathbf T|}\\
&+\frac{4\pi}{\Omega}\sum_{\mathbf G\neq0}
\frac{e^{-G^2/(4\alpha^2)}}{G^2}\cos(\mathbf G\cdot\mathbf r)
-\frac{\pi}{\Omega\alpha^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

最后常数是 $G\to0$ 的解析极限与零平均势选择的一部分，不是可任意丢弃的“小修正”。该势仍包含点电荷位置的自奇点；计算离子能时必须排除每个离子与自身的 $\mathbf T=0$ 相互作用。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For a periodic lattice of unit point charges, adding a uniform negative background and choosing a zero cell-average potential gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
V_\alpha(\mathbf r)=&\sum_{\mathbf T}
\frac{\operatorname{erfc}(\alpha|\mathbf r-\mathbf T|)}{|\mathbf r-\mathbf T|}\\
&+\frac{4\pi}{\Omega}\sum_{\mathbf G\neq0}
\frac{e^{-G^2/(4\alpha^2)}}{G^2}\cos(\mathbf G\cdot\mathbf r)
-\frac{\pi}{\Omega\alpha^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The final constant belongs to the analytic $G\to0$ limit and the zero-average-potential convention; it is not a dispensable small correction. The potential still contains the point-charge self singularity. Ionic energies must exclude the interaction of each ion with its own $\mathbf T=0$ image.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="通用 Ewald 能量的四个组成" titleEn="The four components of the general Ewald energy" eyebrow="【Martin Eqs. F.5–F.7 的标准形式 | Standard form of Martin Eqs. F.5–F.7】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

令晶胞内点电荷为 $q_i$，位置为 $\mathbf r_i$，总电荷 $Q=\sum_iq_i$，结构因子

$$
S(\mathbf G)=\sum_i q_i e^{i\mathbf G\cdot\mathbf r_i}.
$$

在三维周期、均匀补偿背景和 tin-foil 边界下，Ewald 能量可写为

$$
\begin{aligned}
E_{\mathrm{Ew}}=&\frac12\sum_{ij}\sum_{\mathbf T}'
q_iq_j\frac{\operatorname{erfc}(\alpha R_{ij\mathbf T})}{R_{ij\mathbf T}}\\
&+\frac{2\pi}{\Omega}\sum_{\mathbf G\neq0}
\frac{e^{-G^2/(4\alpha^2)}}{G^2}|S(\mathbf G)|^2
-\frac{\alpha}{\sqrt\pi}\sum_i q_i^2
-\frac{\pi Q^2}{2\Omega\alpha^2},
\end{aligned}
$$

其中 $R_{ij\mathbf T}=|\mathbf r_i-\mathbf r_j+\mathbf T|$，撇号去掉 $i=j,\mathbf T=0$。四项依次是短程点电荷减 Gaussian 相互作用、Gaussian 的倒空间相互作用、倒空间中误含的 Gaussian 自能，以及净点电荷与均匀背景的解析 $G=0$ 项。若点电荷集合本身中性，$Q=0$，最后一项消失；若只求正离子在负背景中的 Madelung 能，它不可省略。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Let point charges $q_i$ occupy positions $\mathbf r_i$ in the cell, with total charge $Q=\sum_iq_i$ and structure factor

$$
S(\mathbf G)=\sum_i q_i e^{i\mathbf G\cdot\mathbf r_i}.
$$

Under three-dimensional periodicity, a uniform compensating background, and tin-foil boundary conditions, the Ewald energy is

$$
\begin{aligned}
E_{\mathrm{Ew}}=&\frac12\sum_{ij}\sum_{\mathbf T}'
q_iq_j\frac{\operatorname{erfc}(\alpha R_{ij\mathbf T})}{R_{ij\mathbf T}}\\
&+\frac{2\pi}{\Omega}\sum_{\mathbf G\neq0}
\frac{e^{-G^2/(4\alpha^2)}}{G^2}|S(\mathbf G)|^2
-\frac{\alpha}{\sqrt\pi}\sum_i q_i^2
-\frac{\pi Q^2}{2\Omega\alpha^2},
\end{aligned}
$$

where $R_{ij\mathbf T}=|\mathbf r_i-\mathbf r_j+\mathbf T|$ and the prime removes $i=j,\mathbf T=0$. The four terms are the short-range point-minus-Gaussian interaction, reciprocal interaction of the Gaussians, subtraction of Gaussian self energy inadvertently included in reciprocal space, and the analytic $G=0$ contribution of the net point charge with its uniform background. The final term vanishes when the point-charge set is neutral, $Q=0$, but is required for the Madelung energy of positive ions embedded in a negative background.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="Ewald 收敛验收" titleEn="Ewald convergence acceptance" kind="practice">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <ol>
      <li>同时增加 real-space 与 reciprocal-space cutoff；只增加一侧不能证明收敛。</li>
      <li>在一段 $\alpha$ 区间内，总能变化应远小于单个分项变化。</li>
      <li>将 cutoff 提高后，$\alpha$ plateau 应扩大，而不是靠选择单个“漂亮”参数点。</li>
      <li>对中性晶胞验证 $S(0)=0$；对带背景组分验证 $Q^2$ 项的符号和体积缩放。</li>
      <li>用有限差分能量核对 analytic force；用均匀缩放核对应力。</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <ol>
      <li>Increase real- and reciprocal-space cutoffs together; convergence of only one side is insufficient.</li>
      <li>Across a range of $\alpha$, the total energy should vary far less than its individual components.</li>
      <li>Raising the cutoffs should widen the $\alpha$ plateau rather than merely producing one favorable parameter value.</li>
      <li>Check $S(0)=0$ for a neutral cell; for a background-neutralized component, verify the sign and volume scaling of the $Q^2$ term.</li>
      <li>Check analytic forces against finite energy differences and stress against homogeneous cell scaling.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

<BilingualSection titleZh="Madelung 常数、力与应力的适用边界" titleEn="Madelung constant, forces, and stress boundaries" eyebrow="【Martin F.2 | Martin F.2】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

对固定晶格形状，Coulomb 能按长度反比缩放，可定义无量纲 Madelung 常数 $\alpha_M$：

$$
E_M=-\alpha_M\frac{(Ze)^2}{2R}.
$$

$R$ 的定义必须与所引用表格一致；ionic crystal 常取半个最近邻距离，元素金属背景模型常取 Wigner–Seitz 半径。不同定义下数值不能直接比较。

Ewald 能对原子位置的解析导数给出离子力，自能和均匀背景因不依赖单个位置而不直接贡献平移力。应力则涉及晶胞体积、倒格矢和所有距离对 strain 的导数，背景项可能贡献各向同性部分。能量公式正确并不保证应力实现正确；cell derivative 必须单独验证。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For a fixed lattice shape, Coulomb energy scales inversely with length, allowing a dimensionless Madelung constant $\alpha_M$ to be defined by

$$
E_M=-\alpha_M\frac{(Ze)^2}{2R}.
$$

The definition of $R$ must match the referenced table. Ionic-crystal conventions commonly use half the nearest-neighbor distance, while elemental-background models often use the Wigner-Seitz radius. Numerical constants obtained with different length definitions are not directly comparable.

Analytic derivatives of the Ewald energy with respect to ionic positions give ionic forces. Self and uniform-background terms do not directly contribute to a translational force because they are independent of an individual position. Stress also differentiates the cell volume, reciprocal vectors, and every distance with respect to strain, so the background can contribute an isotropic term. A correct energy implementation does not by itself certify the stress implementation; the cell derivative requires an independent check.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>
