<BilingualSection titleZh="十二个高频误判" titleEn="Twelve recurrent misjudgments" eyebrow="【诊断清单 | Diagnostic checklist】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <ol>
      <li>“电中性”不等于每一种带电组分分别中性。</li>
      <li>删除 $G=0$ 不会自动把带电周期体系变成 isolated charged object。</li>
      <li>Ewald 的 $\alpha$ 不是材料介电 screening parameter。</li>
      <li>总能对 $\alpha$ 稳定但 cutoff 太低时，可能只是误差抵消。</li>
      <li>potential 可加常数，不表示 surface work function 可任意平移。</li>
      <li>离子 self energy 对固定物种可能是常数，但换伪势或参考态时不能忽略。</li>
      <li>伪电荷不重叠时 pair correction 为零，不表示所有离子—离子力为零。</li>
      <li>中性原子重排不会使不同赝势的绝对总能可比较。</li>
      <li>增加 slab 真空不会自动消除净偶极产生的周期电场。</li>
      <li>cubic Makov–Payne 型项不能直接套到二维 slab 或强各向异性 cell。</li>
      <li>只修正最终能量不能撤销 SCF 中镜像势造成的错误极化。</li>
      <li>一个 cell-size pair 的接近不能建立有限尺寸缩放律。</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <ol>
      <li>“Charge neutral” does not mean that every charged component is separately neutral.</li>
      <li>Removing $G=0$ does not turn a charged periodic system into an isolated charged object.</li>
      <li>The Ewald parameter $\alpha$ is not a material dielectric-screening parameter.</li>
      <li>An apparently stable total across $\alpha$ can still arise from cancellation of two unconverged cutoffs.</li>
      <li>An additive potential constant does not make a surface work function arbitrary.</li>
      <li>An ionic self energy may be constant for fixed species but cannot be ignored when pseudopotentials or references change.</li>
      <li>Vanishing pseudocharge-overlap correction does not imply vanishing ion-ion force.</li>
      <li>A neutral-atom rearrangement does not make absolute energies from different pseudopotentials comparable.</li>
      <li>Adding slab vacuum does not automatically remove the periodic field of a net dipole.</li>
      <li>A cubic Makov-Payne-type term cannot be transplanted directly to a two-dimensional slab or strongly anisotropic cell.</li>
      <li>Correcting only the final energy cannot undo polarization caused by an image potential during SCF.</li>
      <li>Agreement of one pair of cell sizes does not establish a finite-size scaling law.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="十道原创练习" titleEn="Ten original exercises" eyebrow="【原创替代练习；不复制原书习题 | Original replacements; source exercises not reproduced】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Fourier neutrality：</strong>从给定 normalization 推导周期 Hartree 能，并证明中性密度的 $G=0$ 不贡献能量。</li>
      <li><strong>Ewald plateau：</strong>对教学 cell 扫描 $\alpha$ 与两个 cutoff，画出 total-error contour，找出误差抵消区域和真正收敛区域。</li>
      <li><strong>Background sign：</strong>构造两个同号点电荷加均匀背景的 cell，验证 $Q^2/(\Omega\alpha^2)$ 项缺失时总能随 $\alpha$ 漂移。</li>
      <li><strong>Scaling：</strong>保持 fractional coordinates 不变，把 cell 全部缩放 $s$，证明 Ewald 能按 $1/s$ 变化并从导数求静水压力。</li>
      <li><strong>Gaussian limit：</strong>推导 $R\to0$ 时 $\operatorname{erf}(R/2\sigma)/R$ 的有限极限，并与 Gaussian self energy 区分。</li>
      <li><strong>Pseudocharge audit：</strong>给定一个 radial local potential，数值计算 Laplacian、积分模型电荷，并检查远场尾部。</li>
      <li><strong>Neutral-reference invariance：</strong>用两种不同 width 的中性原子参考分解同一总密度，证明完整 Hartree 能不变而三个分项改变。</li>
      <li><strong>Interface step：</strong>为两个不同宽度但相同 dipole moment 的平面电荷分布求 $\bar V(z)$，验证远处势阶跃相同。</li>
      <li><strong>Slab image scaling：</strong>固定面积与偶极矩，扫描 $L_z$，区分 intrinsic potential step 与 $1/L_z$ 周期镜像能。</li>
      <li><strong>Charged-cell extrapolation：</strong>生成含 $L^{-1}$、$L^{-3}$ 与噪声的合成数据，比较两点扣除、三点拟合和错误 $1/L_z$ 模型的偏差。</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Fourier neutrality:</strong> derive the periodic Hartree energy from the stated normalization and prove that a neutral density has no $G=0$ energy contribution.</li>
      <li><strong>Ewald plateau:</strong> scan $\alpha$ and both cutoffs for the teaching cell, plot a total-error contour, and distinguish cancellation from genuine convergence.</li>
      <li><strong>Background sign:</strong> build a cell with two like charges and a uniform background, then show that omission of the $Q^2/(\Omega\alpha^2)$ term causes $\alpha$ drift.</li>
      <li><strong>Scaling:</strong> keep fractional coordinates fixed, scale the complete cell by $s$, prove the $1/s$ Ewald-energy law, and obtain hydrostatic pressure from the derivative.</li>
      <li><strong>Gaussian limit:</strong> derive the finite $R\to0$ limit of $\operatorname{erf}(R/2\sigma)/R$ and distinguish it from the Gaussian self energy.</li>
      <li><strong>Pseudocharge audit:</strong> for a radial local potential, numerically evaluate its Laplacian, integrate the model charge, and inspect the far-field tail.</li>
      <li><strong>Neutral-reference invariance:</strong> decompose one total density using neutral atomic references of two widths and show that the complete Hartree energy is invariant while its three components change.</li>
      <li><strong>Interface step:</strong> solve for $\bar V(z)$ from two planar charge profiles with different widths but identical dipole moments and verify the same far-field step.</li>
      <li><strong>Slab image scaling:</strong> hold area and dipole moment fixed, scan $L_z$, and separate the intrinsic potential step from the $1/L_z$ periodic-image energy.</li>
      <li><strong>Charged-cell extrapolation:</strong> generate synthetic data containing $L^{-1}$, $L^{-3}$, and noise, then compare a two-point subtraction, a three-point fit, and an incorrect $1/L_z$ model.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>
