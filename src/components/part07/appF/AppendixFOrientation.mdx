  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Appendix F 处理周期体系总能中最容易被公式外观掩盖的部分：Coulomb kernel 的长程性、周期求和的条件收敛、中性化方式、平均场边界、势零点和人工镜像。它并不再推导一套新的 Kohn–Sham 理论，而是说明电子 Hartree 项、核或离子项、局域伪势项、自能与参考能必须怎样组合，才能得到有限、可微且可比较的能量。

原书按三条主线组织这些问题：点电荷加补偿背景的 Ewald 形式；把离子电荷涂抹为有限宽分布的形式；相对于中性原子参考态的重排。随后把同一套长程静电原则用于表面/界面偶极和有限体系的周期超胞误差。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Appendix F treats the part of periodic total-energy theory most easily hidden by compact formulas: the long range of the Coulomb kernel, conditional convergence of lattice sums, neutralization conventions, macroscopic-field boundaries, the potential zero, and artificial images. It does not introduce a new Kohn-Sham theory. Instead, it specifies how the electronic Hartree term, nuclear or ionic terms, local pseudopotential terms, self energies, and reference energies must be grouped to produce a finite, differentiable, and comparable energy.

Martin organizes the subject through three equivalent strategies: Ewald summation for point charges with a compensating background; finite-width or smeared ionic charges; and a rearrangement relative to neutral atomic references. The same long-range electrostatic principles are then applied to surface/interface dipoles and periodic-supercell errors for finite systems.

## 对象、单位与边界约定 | Objects, Units, and Boundary Conventions

<BilingualSection titleZh="电荷符号、体积与 Fourier normalization" titleEn="Charge signs, cell volume, and Fourier normalization" eyebrow="【定义 | Definitions】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

本附录采用 Gaussian/atomic-unit 风格的 Coulomb 公式，令 $4\pi\epsilon_0=1$；若保留电子电荷量，则点电荷相互作用写为 $q_iq_j/r$ 或 $e^2 Z_iZ_j/r$。SI 中必须整体恢复 $1/(4\pi\epsilon_0)$，不能只在某一项补因子。

周期晶胞体积记为 $\Omega$，实空间平移为 $\mathbf T$，倒格矢为 $\mathbf G$。采用

$$
\rho(\mathbf G)=\int_\Omega d^3r\,\rho(\mathbf r)e^{-i\mathbf G\cdot\mathbf r},
\qquad
\rho(\mathbf r)=\frac{1}{\Omega}\sum_{\mathbf G}\rho(\mathbf G)e^{i\mathbf G\cdot\mathbf r}.
$$

于是中性条件为 $Q=\rho(\mathbf G=0)=\int_\Omega\rho(\mathbf r)d^3r=0$，而非“体系中正负粒子数相等”。电子数密度与电荷密度的符号必须分开；正文中的 $\rho$ 统一表示带符号的总电荷密度。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

This Appendix uses Gaussian/atomic-unit Coulomb formulas with $4\pi\epsilon_0=1$. If the elementary charge is retained, point-charge interactions are written as $q_iq_j/r$ or $e^2Z_iZ_j/r$. Restoring SI units requires multiplying the complete electrostatic expression by $1/(4\pi\epsilon_0)$ rather than patching one term in isolation.

The cell volume is $\Omega$, real-space translations are $\mathbf T$, and reciprocal vectors are $\mathbf G$. We use

$$
\rho(\mathbf G)=\int_\Omega d^3r\,\rho(\mathbf r)e^{-i\mathbf G\cdot\mathbf r},
\qquad
\rho(\mathbf r)=\frac{1}{\Omega}\sum_{\mathbf G}\rho(\mathbf G)e^{i\mathbf G\cdot\mathbf r}.
$$

The neutrality condition is therefore $Q=\rho(\mathbf G=0)=\int_\Omega\rho(\mathbf r)d^3r=0$, not merely equality of positive and negative particle counts. Number density and signed charge density must not be interchanged; throughout the Appendix, $\rho$ denotes a signed total charge density.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="中性、零宏观场与势零点是三件事" titleEn="Neutrality, zero macroscopic field, and the potential zero are distinct" eyebrow="【Martin F.1 | Martin F.1】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

周期体相参考态需要至少两项条件：晶胞中性，以及没有平均宏观电场。中性保证总能不发生 monopole 发散；零平均场指定可作为热力学参考的 bulk state。即使这两项都满足，平均静电势仍可加任意常数，因为 Poisson 方程只约束势的梯度和非零 Fourier 分量。

因此“去掉 $G=0$”可能表示不同操作：对中性密度，$\rho(0)=0$ 本来就消失；对人为加入均匀背景的带电组分，它表示采用特定中性化约定；对电势，它还选择了平均势零点。三者必须逐项说明。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A periodic bulk reference state requires at least two conditions: a neutral cell and no average macroscopic electric field. Neutrality removes the monopole divergence of the total energy; zero average field specifies a thermodynamic bulk reference state. Even after both conditions are imposed, the average electrostatic potential remains arbitrary up to an additive constant because Poisson's equation fixes gradients and nonzero Fourier components, not the absolute zero.

Consequently, “dropping $G=0$” can denote different operations. For a neutral density, $\rho(0)=0$ vanishes identically. For a charged component paired with a uniform background, it encodes a specific neutralization convention. For the potential, it also chooses the average-potential zero. These meanings must be stated separately.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="宏观边界条件属于结果定义" titleEn="The macroscopic boundary condition belongs to the definition of the result" eyebrow="【Martin F.1–F.2 + 补充边界分层 | Martin F.1–F.2 + supplemental boundary hierarchy】">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Coulomb 晶格和在中性后仍可能条件收敛。求和区域趋于无穷大的形状、外部介质以及是否允许表面电荷，会决定 dipole 或更高矩项留下的宏观贡献。常用三维 Ewald “tin-foil” convention 等价于把外部介质视为理想导体，从而去掉某些表面项；真空边界、球形样品或 slab 几何并不自动给出相同结果。

网站数值核采用三维周期、均匀补偿背景和 tin-foil 边界，只用于演示 Ewald 分解与参数收敛。它不代表二维 Coulomb cutoff、open boundary Poisson solver、真实介电嵌入或任意形状的有限样品。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A Coulomb lattice sum can remain conditionally convergent after neutrality is imposed. The shape by which the summation region approaches infinity, the external dielectric environment, and whether surface charge is allowed determine macroscopic contributions from dipole and higher moments. The common three-dimensional Ewald “tin-foil” convention treats the exterior as an ideal conductor and removes particular surface terms. Vacuum boundaries, a spherical specimen, and a slab geometry do not automatically yield the same result.

The website kernel uses three-dimensional periodicity, a uniform compensating background, and tin-foil boundary conditions solely to demonstrate Ewald decomposition and parameter convergence. It is not a model of a two-dimensional Coulomb cutoff, an open-boundary Poisson solver, a real dielectric embedding, or a finite specimen of arbitrary shape.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="能量、势与本征值的零点" titleEn="Zeros of energy, potential, and eigenvalues" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

对中性体系，给静电势加常数 $C$ 不改变总 Coulomb 能，因为能量变化为 $CQ=0$。但单粒子本征值会整体移动；只有与同一参考对齐的能量差有意义。表面功函数、界面 band offset 和 defect level alignment 需要额外的真空、平面平均势或 bulk reference，不能由无限体相中的绝对平均势直接得到。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For a neutral system, adding a constant $C$ to the electrostatic potential does not change the total Coulomb energy because the change is $CQ=0$. Single-particle eigenvalues nevertheless shift together, so only differences aligned to the same reference are meaningful. Surface work functions, interface band offsets, and defect-level alignment require an additional vacuum, planar-average, or bulk reference and cannot be read from an absolute average potential of an infinite bulk crystal.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>
