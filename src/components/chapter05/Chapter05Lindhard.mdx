<h2 id="section-5-5">5.5 激发与 Lindhard dielectric function | Excitations and the Lindhard Dielectric Function</h2>

<BilingualSection titleZh="两类低能激发" titleEn="Two classes of low-energy excitation" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】 §5.5 · pp. 122–125">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

homogeneous gas 的 excitations 可分为：

1. 改变 particle number 的 electron addition/removal states，对应 interacting Fermi liquid 的 quasiparticle dispersion 与 spectral function；
2. particle number 不变的 neutral density/spin fluctuations，对应 charge response、plasmons 与 spin response。

noninteracting $k^2/2$ dispersion 在 simple metals 中常是 useful starting point，但 quasiparticle bandwidth、mass 与 lifetime 是 many-body quantities。unscreened HF 预测 band broadened 并在 $k_F$ 出现 slope divergence；实验 simple-metal bands 往往比这一预测更接近 screened/noninteracting starting point。

Kohn–Sham eigenvalues、HF eigenvalues 和 quasiparticle poles 是不同对象。它们可数值接近，但只有定义和 approximation chain 清楚时才可比较。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Excitations either add or remove an electron, producing quasiparticles, or preserve particle number and create collective charge or spin fluctuations. A free-electron parabola can be a useful starting point for simple metals, but quasiparticle masses, bandwidths, weights, and lifetimes are many-body observables. Kohn–Sham, Hartree–Fock, and quasiparticle energies are distinct objects even when their numerical values are close.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="density response、proper response 与 dielectric function" titleEn="Density response, proper response, and the dielectric function">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

linear response 定义

$$
\delta n(q,\omega)
=\chi(q,\omega)\delta V_{\mathrm{ext}}(q,\omega).
$$

proper response $\chi^*$ 对 total/internal potential 响应：

$$
\delta n=\chi^*\delta V_{\mathrm{tot}},
\qquad
\delta V_{\mathrm{tot}}
=\delta V_{\mathrm{ext}}+v(q)\delta n.
$$

消去 $\delta n$：

$$
\chi=\frac{\chi^*}{1-v\chi^*}.
$$

dielectric function 为

$$
\boxed{
\epsilon(q,\omega)=1-v(q)\chi^*(q,\omega),
\qquad
v(q)=\frac{4\pi}{q^2}.
}
$$

screened potential 满足

$$
V_{\mathrm{tot}}=\epsilon^{-1}V_{\mathrm{ext}}.
$$

RPA 取 $\chi^*\approx\chi_0$，即 proper polarization 使用 noninteracting particle–hole bubble，但 Coulomb Hartree series 被无限重求和。名称“random phase”不能理解为完全忽略 interaction；collective plasmon 正是 Coulomb resummation 的结果。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The proper response relates density to the total internal potential. Eliminating the induced Hartree field gives

$$
\epsilon(q,\omega)=1-v(q)\chi^*(q,\omega),
\qquad
V_{\mathrm{tot}}=\epsilon^{-1}V_{\mathrm{ext}}.
$$

RPA replaces the proper response by the noninteracting particle–hole response but resums the Hartree Coulomb series to all orders. The interaction is therefore not simply discarded.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="noninteracting Lindhard response | Noninteracting Lindhard response"
  assumptions={[
    'zero-temperature homogeneous gas;',
    'parabolic dispersion ε_k=k²/2;',
    'retarded response with positive infinitesimal η.']}
>

对 spin-degenerate gas，

$$
\boxed{
\chi_0(q,\omega)
=2\int\frac{d^3k}{(2\pi)^3}
\frac{f_{\mathbf k}-f_{\mathbf k+\mathbf q}}
{\omega+\varepsilon_{\mathbf k}-\varepsilon_{\mathbf k+\mathbf q}+i\eta}.
}
$$

numerator 只允许 occupied-to-empty particle–hole processes。imaginary part 来自

$$
\omega=\varepsilon_{\mathbf k+\mathbf q}-\varepsilon_{\mathbf k}
$$

能够在 occupied/unoccupied constraints 下满足的 k-space region；这定义 particle–hole continuum。continuum 外 $\operatorname{Im}\chi_0=0$ in ideal zero-temperature gas。

real 与 imaginary parts 由 causality/Kramers–Kronig relation 连接。numerical implementation 必须检查 frequency grid、broadening $\eta$、sum rules 与 high-frequency tail；单独画一个 broadened curve 不能证明 analytic continuum boundary。

<p lang="en">The Lindhard function is a weighted sum over occupied-to-empty particle–hole transitions. Its imaginary part is nonzero only where energy and occupation constraints define a particle–hole continuum, while causality connects the real and imaginary parts.</p>

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="static 3D Lindhard function 与 2k_F nonanalyticity" titleEn="The static 3D Lindhard function and the 2k_F nonanalyticity">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

令

$$
x=\frac{q}{2k_F}.
$$

zero-temperature static response 可写为

$$
\chi_0(q,0)=-N(E_F)F(x),
$$

其中 $N(E_F)$ 是包括相应 spin convention 的 DOS，dimensionless shape function 为

$$
\boxed{
F(x)=\frac12+rac{1-x^2}{4x}
\ln\left|\frac{1+x}{1-x}\right|.
}
$$

limits：

$$
F(0)=1,
\qquad
F(1)=\frac12,
\qquad
F(x\gg1)\sim\frac{1}{3x^2}.
$$

$F$ 在 $x=1$ continuous，但 derivative logarithmically nonanalytic。该 $q=2k_F$ structure Fourier transforms 成 real-space Friedel oscillations，并可在 electron–phonon screening 中产生 Kohn anomaly。real crystal 中 relevant spanning vectors 来自 anisotropic Fermi surface，不能把 scalar $2k_F$ 直接套用。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For $x=q/(2k_F)$,

$$
\chi_0(q,0)=-N(E_F)F(x),
$$

with

$$
F(x)=\frac12+rac{1-x^2}{4x}\ln\left|\frac{1+x}{1-x}\right|.
$$

The function is continuous at $x=1$ but has a logarithmically nonanalytic derivative. This $2k_F$ structure produces Friedel oscillations and can generate Kohn anomalies. In crystals, anisotropic Fermi-surface spanning vectors replace a single scalar $2k_F$.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="small-q static screening | Small-q static screening"
  assumptions={[
    'RPA;',
    'q is much smaller than k_F;',
    'static limit is taken before the long-wavelength limit.']}
>

当 $x\to0$，$F(x)\to1$，因此

$$
\chi_0(q,0)\to-N(E_F).
$$

RPA dielectric function：

$$
\epsilon(q,0)
=1-v(q)\chi_0(q,0)
\to1+\frac{4\pi N(E_F)}{q^2}.
$$

定义

$$
k_{\mathrm{TF}}^2=4\pi N(E_F),
$$

得到

$$
\boxed{
\epsilon(q,0)\to1+\frac{k_{\mathrm{TF}}^2}{q^2},
\qquad
v_{\mathrm{scr}}(q)	o\frac{4\pi}{q^2+k_{\mathrm{TF}}^2}.
}
$$

static limit 与 dynamic limit 不可任意交换。$q\to0$ at fixed nonzero $\omega$ 描述 uniform time-dependent field，产生 plasma response；$\omega\to0$ first 描述 thermodynamic static screening。

<p lang="en">The long-wavelength static Lindhard limit reproduces Thomas–Fermi screening. Taking the dynamic and static limits in a different order probes different physics and must not be interchanged silently.</p>

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="particle–hole continuum 与 Landau damping" titleEn="Particle–hole continuum and Landau damping">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

对 parabolic band，energy transfer 为

$$
\Delta\varepsilon
=\varepsilon_{\mathbf k+\mathbf q}-\varepsilon_{\mathbf k}
=\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf q+\frac{q^2}{2}.
$$

在 $|\mathbf k|\le k_F$ 且 final state unoccupied 的 constraints 下，allowed $\omega$ 形成 finite region。small $q$ 时上边界 scale 约为

$$
\omega_+(q)=qv_F+\frac{q^2}{2},
$$

另有 lower boundary determined by occupation geometry。collective mode 若进入 continuum，可 decay 成 particle–hole pairs，形成 Landau damping even without disorder。

finite temperature、impurity、phonon 与 electron–electron scattering 会进一步 broaden spectral features。RPA ideal continuum boundary 不是真实 material lifetime model。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The energy transferred to a particle–hole pair is $\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf q+q^2/2$. Occupation constraints define a continuum in the $(q,\omega)$ plane. A collective mode entering that continuum can decay into particle–hole pairs through Landau damping even in a clean ideal gas. Temperature and scattering introduce additional broadening.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="plasmons 与 loss function" titleEn="Plasmons and the loss function">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

collective longitudinal mode 满足 approximately

$$
\operatorname{Re}\epsilon(q,\omega_q)=0
$$

且 damping small。long wavelength limit 为

$$
\boxed{
\omega_q\to\omega_p=\sqrt{4\pi n}.
}
$$

实验 electron-energy-loss spectrum 常与

$$
-\operatorname{Im}\epsilon^{-1}(q,\omega)
$$

相关。plasmon 是 inverse dielectric function 的 pole/peak，不是 $\operatorname{Im}\epsilon$ 的任意峰。mode 进入 particle–hole/interband continuum 时 broaden，并可能不再是 well-defined quasiparticle。

real solids 具有 dielectric tensor、local-field matrix $\epsilon_{\mathbf G\mathbf G'}$、interband transitions 与 multiple plasmon-like modes。homogeneous scalar $\epsilon(q,\omega)$ 只是 reference limit。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A weakly damped longitudinal collective mode occurs near a zero of the real dielectric function. In the long-wavelength gas it approaches $\omega_p=\sqrt{4\pi n}$. Energy-loss spectroscopy probes $-\operatorname{Im}\epsilon^{-1}$, so a plasmon is associated with a pole or peak of the inverse dielectric response rather than an arbitrary peak of $\operatorname{Im}\epsilon$.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="从均匀体系响应到材料计算语境" titleEn="From homogeneous response to material-calculation contexts">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

homogeneous-gas response 提供三个 transferable ideas：

- compressibility controls long-wavelength static screening；
- Fermi-surface geometry controls low-energy particle–hole phase space 与 nonanalyticities；
- Coulomb interaction can create collective modes outside single-particle continuum。

真实材料计算还需要 band indices、matrix elements、local fields、crystal symmetry、SOC、interband screening、electron–hole interaction 和 convergence over full BZ。沿 high-symmetry line 的 bands 或 total DOS 不能重建 $\chi(q,\omega)$。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The homogeneous response teaches that compressibility controls static screening, Fermi-surface geometry controls low-energy phase space and nonanalyticities, and Coulomb interactions create collective modes. A material response additionally requires bands, matrix elements, local fields, symmetry, SOC, interband screening, electron–hole interactions, and full-BZ convergence. A band path or total DOS cannot reconstruct $\chi(q,\omega)$.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>
