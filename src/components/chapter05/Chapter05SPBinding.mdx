<h2 id="section-5-4">5.4 sp 成键金属的结合 | Binding in sp-Bonded Metals</h2>

<BilingualSection titleZh="从 jellium energy scale 到 simple metals" titleEn="From the jellium energy scale to simple metals" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】 §5.4 · pp. 121–122">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

alkali 与其他 simple $sp$ metals 的 valence electrons 在很多 observables 上接近 nearly free-electron picture，但 crystal binding 仍来自 valence gas 与 discrete positive ion cores 的共同能量。一个早期 energy-balance model 写成

$$
\frac{E_{\mathrm{tot}}}{N}
=\frac{1.105}{r_s^2}
-\frac{0.458}{r_s}
+\varepsilon_c(r_s)
-\frac{\alpha}{2r_s}
+\varepsilon_R(r_s,R_c).
$$

前两项是 unpolarized kinetic 与 exchange，第三项是 correlation。$-\alpha/(2r_s)$ 是 ions in background 的 Madelung-like attraction；$\varepsilon_R$ 表示 finite core/pseudopotential repulsion。对于简单 empty-core picture，

$$
\varepsilon_R\sim\frac{3R_c^2}{2r_s^3}.
$$

各项 scaling 解释 equilibrium density：$r_s^{-2}$ kinetic 与 $r_s^{-3}$ core repulsion 防止 collapse，$r_s^{-1}$ attractive terms 促进 binding，correlation 修正 balance。

该表达不是 universal material Hamiltonian。$\alpha$、$R_c$、valence、crystal structure 与 pseudopotential form 都是 material/model inputs；transition metals 与 covalent solids 不能由单一 average $r_s$ 可靠描述。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Simple sp metals often have nearly free valence electrons, but binding still combines electron-gas energies with discrete ionic-core terms. A schematic per-electron balance contains kinetic $r_s^{-2}$, exchange $r_s^{-1}$, correlation, Madelung-like ionic attraction, and a short-range core-repulsion term such as $R_c^2r_s^{-3}$.

The competition explains an equilibrium density, but the coefficients, valence, core radius, crystal structure, and pseudopotential are material-dependent inputs. The expression is a scale model rather than a universal crystal-energy functional.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="equilibrium r_s and stability | Equilibrium radius and stability"
  assumptions={[
    'a differentiable per-electron energy ε(r_s);',
    'fixed particle number;',
    'the model coefficients are held fixed during isotropic volume variation.']}
>

volume per electron 为

$$
v=\frac{4\pi}{3}r_s^3.
$$

zero external pressure equilibrium 满足

$$
P=-\frac{d\varepsilon}{dv}=0.
$$

因

$$
\frac{dv}{dr_s}=4\pi r_s^2,
$$

所以 equilibrium condition 等价于

$$
\boxed{\frac{d\varepsilon}{dr_s}=0.}
$$

local stability 要求

$$
\frac{d^2\varepsilon}{dv^2}>0.
$$

在 stationary point 可写为

$$
\frac{d^2\varepsilon}{dv^2}
=\frac{1}{(4\pi r_s^2)^2}
\frac{d^2\varepsilon}{dr_s^2}.
$$

bulk modulus 是

$$
B=v\frac{d^2E}{dv^2}
=v\frac{d^2\varepsilon}{dv^2}
$$

per electron convention 下的 isotropic curvature。一个 stationary point 若 curvature negative 是 maximum，不是 stable crystal。

<p lang="en">At fixed particle number, zero pressure is equivalent to stationarity with respect to $r_s$. Positive second derivative with respect to volume is a separate stability condition, and its curvature sets the bulk-modulus scale.</p>

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="为什么 core repulsion 改变 equilibrium density" titleEn="Why core repulsion changes the equilibrium density">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

若忽略 $\varepsilon_c$ 与 core term，写

$$
\varepsilon(r_s)=\frac{A}{r_s^2}-\frac{C}{r_s},
$$

stationarity 给出

$$
-\frac{2A}{r_s^3}+\frac{C}{r_s^2}=0,
\qquad
r_s^{(0)}=\frac{2A}{C}.
$$

加入 positive $D/r_s^3$ 后，

$$
\frac{d\varepsilon}{dr_s}
=-\frac{2A}{r_s^3}+rac{C}{r_s^2}-\frac{3D}{r_s^4}=0,
$$

即

$$
Cr_s^2-2Ar_s-3D=0,
$$

positive root 为

$$
\boxed{
r_s=\frac{A+\sqrt{A^2+3CD}}{C}.
}
$$

$D>0$ 将 equilibrium 推向 larger $r_s$，即 lower density。这个结果展示 finite ion core 如何阻止 valence gas 过度压缩；它不确定真实 crystal symmetry 或 phase competition。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Without a core term, balancing $A/r_s^2$ against $-C/r_s$ gives $r_s=2A/C$. Adding a positive $D/r_s^3$ term moves the positive stationary root to larger $r_s$, representing the resistance of finite ion cores to valence-electron compression.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="bulk-modulus scale" titleEn="Bulk-modulus scale">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

单独看 kinetic energy $A/r_s^2$，isotropic curvature 随

$$
B_{\mathrm{kin}}\propto r_s^{-5}.
$$

因此 compressed high-density material 通常有较大的 electronic stiffness scale。真实 bulk modulus 是所有 energy terms、internal relaxation、crystal anisotropy 与 finite-temperature free energy 的二阶导数；只用 kinetic term 得到的是 order-of-magnitude scale。

比较 theory 与 experiment 时需统一：0 K static lattice 还是 finite-T、equilibrium volume、zero-point phonon correction、pressure calibration 与 exchange–correlation functional。total energy 数值收敛不自动保证 second derivative/B 已收敛。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The kinetic contribution scales roughly as $r_s^{-5}$ in the bulk modulus, giving a useful stiffness scale. A real bulk modulus is the curvature of the complete free energy, including all electronic terms, internal relaxation, anisotropy, and temperature. Energy convergence does not automatically establish convergence of a second derivative.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="nearly free electron picture 的适用范围" titleEn="Scope of the nearly-free-electron picture">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

simple metal 的 valence band 可接近 free-electron parabola，是因为 ion pseudopotential 对 relevant states 是 relatively weak scattering，而不是因为 ionic potential 不存在。near Bragg planes，哪怕 weak periodic potential 也会 open gaps 并重塑 Fermi surface。

successful total-energy model 需要 ion–ion、electron–ion、electron kinetic、Hartree、exchange–correlation 与 pseudopotential nonlocality consistency。使用 empirical core radius 拟合 equilibrium volume 不等于预测 transferability、phonons、surfaces 或 high-pressure phases。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A valence band can resemble a free-electron parabola because the ionic pseudopotential is weak for the relevant states, not because the ionic potential is absent. Even a weak periodic potential opens gaps at Bragg planes. A fitted core radius that reproduces one equilibrium volume does not establish transferability to phonons, surfaces, or high-pressure phases.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>
