<h2 id="review">常见误判、原创练习与来源边界 | Misconceptions, Original Exercises, and Source Boundaries</h2>

<BilingualSection titleZh="常见误判" titleEn="Common misconceptions">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <ol>
      <li><strong>“$r_s$ 是最近邻 electron distance。”</strong> 它由 average volume per electron 定义。</li>
      <li><strong>“uniform positive background 可以从 Hamiltonian 删除。”</strong> background terms 是 Coulomb neutrality 和 finite thermodynamic energy 的必要部分。</li>
      <li><strong>“高 density 意味着 interaction energy 绝对更小。”</strong> kinetic 与 Coulomb energies 都增大，但相对 coupling 按 $r_s$ 变弱。</li>
      <li><strong>“Hartree–Fock 对 homogeneous metal 是可靠 quasiparticle theory。”</strong> unscreened HF 在 $k_F$ 产生 slope divergence。</li>
      <li><strong>“exchange hole 是 Coulomb correlation hole。”</strong> exchange hole 来自 antisymmetry，即使 interaction 为零也存在。</li>
      <li><strong>“pair distribution $g$ 的积分应为 −1。”</strong> 归一化对象是 hole density $n(g-1)$。</li>
      <li><strong>“correlation energy 只是 potential-energy lowering。”</strong> 它同时包含 kinetic correlation。</li>
      <li><strong>“full-coupling hole 可以直接给完整 $E_{xc}$。”</strong> energy hole expression 需要 coupling-constant average。</li>
      <li><strong>“Thomas–Fermi screening 等同完整 Lindhard response。”</strong> TF 只给 static long-wavelength limit。</li>
      <li><strong>“RPA 忽略了 Coulomb interaction。”</strong> RPA 使用 noninteracting proper polarization，但 resums Hartree screening。</li>
      <li><strong>“$2k_F$ Kohn anomaly 在任何 crystal 都是一个 scalar wavevector。”</strong> real materials 由 anisotropic Fermi-surface spanning vectors 决定。</li>
      <li><strong>“DOS at $E_F$ 足以预测 superconductivity。”</strong> 还需要 phonon spectrum、electron–phonon matrix elements、full-BZ convergence 与 Coulomb treatment。</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <ol>
      <li>$r_s$ is an average-volume radius, not a nearest-neighbour distance.</li>
      <li>The positive background is required for neutrality and a finite thermodynamic Coulomb energy.</li>
      <li>High density weakens interaction relative to kinetic energy even though absolute energy scales grow.</li>
      <li>Unscreened Hartree–Fock has an intrinsic Fermi-surface slope divergence in a metal.</li>
      <li>The exchange hole is generated by antisymmetry and exists without Coulomb correlation.</li>
      <li>The normalized object is the hole density $n(g-1)$, not the dimensionless pair distribution by itself.</li>
      <li>Correlation energy contains kinetic as well as interaction contributions.</li>
      <li>The coupling-constant-averaged hole, not only the full-coupling hole, yields the complete $E_{xc}$ hole expression.</li>
      <li>Thomas–Fermi theory is only the static long-wavelength limit of the response.</li>
      <li>RPA resums Hartree screening even though it uses a noninteracting proper polarization.</li>
      <li>Crystal Kohn anomalies are controlled by anisotropic Fermi-surface spanning vectors.</li>
      <li>A DOS at the Fermi level does not determine superconductivity.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="原创练习" titleEn="Original exercises">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <ol>
      <li>从 $4\pi r_s^3/3=1/n$ 推导 isotropic pressure 对 $d\varepsilon/dr_s$ 的表达。</li>
      <li>证明 uniform scaling 后 Hamiltonian 可写为 $r_s^{-2}(T+r_sV)$。</li>
      <li>从 k-space state count 推导 $k_{F\sigma}=(6\pi^2n_\sigma)^{1/3}$。</li>
      <li>推导 polarized gas 的 kinetic spin factor 与 fully polarized limit。</li>
      <li>从 Fermi sphere Fourier transform 推导 $F(y)=3(\sin y-y\cos y)/y^3$。</li>
      <li>用 series 证明 $F(y)=1-y^2/10+O(y^4)$。</li>
      <li>从 determinant pair density 推导 $g_{\uparrow\uparrow}=1-F^2$，并解释 contact zero。</li>
      <li>数值验证 same-spin exchange-hole charge 为 $-1$，研究 cutoff 与 quadrature error。</li>
      <li>分析 HF factor $f(x)$ 在 $x\to0$、$x\to1$ 与 $x\to\infty$ 的 limits。</li>
      <li>比较 kinetic 与 exchange spin factors，讨论 high-density polarization tendency。</li>
      <li>从 compressibility 与 Poisson equation 推导 $k_{\mathrm{TF}}^2=4k_F/\pi$。</li>
      <li>说明为什么 high-density correlation expansion 包含 $\ln r_s$ 而不是普通 Taylor series。</li>
      <li>对 energy model $A/r_s^2-C/r_s+D/r_s^3$ 求 equilibrium radius 与 stability condition。</li>
      <li>证明 static Lindhard shape 满足 $F(0)=1$、$F(1)=1/2$ 和 large-$x$ asymptote。</li>
      <li>设计一个从 band structure 到 Kohn anomaly 再到 EPC claim 的证据链，列出每一步不能跳过的 full-BZ quantity。</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <ol>
      <li>Convert an isotropic volume derivative into an $r_s$ derivative.</li>
      <li>Derive the scaled Hamiltonian $r_s^{-2}(T+r_sV)$.</li>
      <li>Derive the spin-resolved Fermi wavevector from state counting.</li>
      <li>Derive the kinetic spin factor and its fully polarized limit.</li>
      <li>Derive the zero-temperature density-matrix kernel from a filled Fermi sphere.</li>
      <li>Obtain the small-$y$ series of the kernel.</li>
      <li>Derive the same-spin pair distribution and Pauli contact.</li>
      <li>Numerically test the exchange-hole normalization and cutoff error.</li>
      <li>Analyze the Hartree–Fock exchange factor in its three limiting regimes.</li>
      <li>Compare kinetic and exchange spin-polarization factors.</li>
      <li>Derive the Thomas–Fermi wavevector from compressibility.</li>
      <li>Explain the logarithm in the high-density correlation expansion.</li>
      <li>Find and classify the stationary point of a three-term sp-metal energy model.</li>
      <li>Verify the static Lindhard limits and asymptote.</li>
      <li>Design a full evidence chain from Fermi-surface geometry to a defensible EPC claim.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
</BilingualSection>
