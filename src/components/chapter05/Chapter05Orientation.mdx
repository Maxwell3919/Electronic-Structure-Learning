<BilingualSection titleZh="本章在 Part I 中的位置" titleEn="The role of this chapter in Part I">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Chapter 3 给出了 interacting-electron problem、density matrix、Hartree–Fock 与 exchange/correlation 的一般定义；Chapter 4 建立 periodic solid、reciprocal space、Bloch band 与 BZ integration。本章把这些抽象对象放进唯一能够大量解析计算的凝聚态多体模型：均匀电子气。

均匀电子气没有离散原子核和晶格势。电子在 uniform positive background 中运动，系统由 density 和 spin polarization 指定。因此本章可以把 density scale、Fermi statistics、exchange、screening、correlation 与 response 的数量级直接写出，同时也清楚暴露 Hartree–Fock 在金属中的失败。

本章并不把 jellium 当作真实材料。真实 $sp$ metal 的离子结构、pseudopotential scattering、interband transitions、phonons、disorder 和 finite lifetime 都超出 homogeneous model。jellium 的作用是建立 scale、limit 与 reference system。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Chapter 3 introduced the general interacting-electron problem, density matrices, Hartree–Fock, exchange, and correlation. Chapter 4 established periodic solids, reciprocal space, Bloch bands, and BZ integration. Chapter 5 places those abstractions in the condensed-matter many-body model for which many quantities can be evaluated analytically: the homogeneous electron gas.

The model removes discrete nuclei and the lattice potential. Electrons move in a uniform compensating positive background, so density and spin polarization specify the state. This makes density scales, Fermi statistics, exchange, screening, correlation, and response magnitudes explicit, while also exposing the failure of unscreened Hartree–Fock in a metal.

Jellium is a reference system rather than a material model. Ionic structure, pseudopotential scattering, interband transitions, phonons, disorder, and finite lifetimes in real sp metals require additional evidence.
