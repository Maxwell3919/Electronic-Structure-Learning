<BilingualSection titleZh="密度与自旋变量" titleEn="Density and spin variables">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

除特别说明外使用 Hartree atomic units：$\hbar=m_e=e=4\pi\epsilon_0=1$。总 density、spin densities 和 polarization 为

$$
n=n_\uparrow+n_\downarrow,
\qquad
\zeta=\frac{n_\uparrow-n_\downarrow}{n},
\qquad
n_{\uparrow,\downarrow}=\frac n2(1\pm\zeta),
$$

其中 $-1\le\zeta\le1$。Wigner–Seitz density radius 定义为

$$
\frac{4\pi}{3}r_s^3=\frac1n,
\qquad
n=\frac{3}{4\pi r_s^3}.
$$

$r_s$ 是由 average volume per electron 定义的 density parameter，不是 nearest-neighbour distance，也不是 correlation length。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Hartree atomic units are used unless stated otherwise. Total density, spin densities, and polarization are

$$
n=n_\uparrow+n_\downarrow,
\qquad
\zeta=\frac{n_\uparrow-n_\downarrow}{n},
\qquad
n_{\uparrow,\downarrow}=\frac n2(1\pm\zeta).
$$

The density radius obeys

$$
\frac{4\pi}{3}r_s^3=\frac1n.
$$

It represents the average volume per electron, not a nearest-neighbour distance or a correlation length.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="Fermi 与 screening scales" titleEn="Fermi and screening scales">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

每个 spin channel 的 Fermi wavevector 为

$$
k_{F\sigma}=(6\pi^2n_\sigma)^{1/3},
$$

unpolarized gas 有

$$
k_F=(3\pi^2n)^{1/3}
=\left(\frac{9\pi}{4}\right)^{1/3}\frac1{r_s}.
$$

常用 scales：

$$
E_F=\frac{k_F^2}{2},
\qquad
v_F=k_F,
\qquad
\omega_p=\sqrt{4\pi n}=\sqrt{\frac3{r_s^3}},
\qquad
k_{\mathrm{TF}}^2=\frac{4k_F}{\pi}.
$$

$\omega_p$ 是 long-wavelength homogeneous-gas plasma scale；真实 crystal 的 plasmon 会受 band structure、interband screening 与 damping 修改。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Each spin channel has

$$
k_{F\sigma}=(6\pi^2n_\sigma)^{1/3}.
$$

For an unpolarized gas,

$$
k_F=(3\pi^2n)^{1/3}
=\left(\frac{9\pi}{4}\right)^{1/3}r_s^{-1}.
$$

Useful reference scales are $E_F=k_F^2/2$, $v_F=k_F$, $\omega_p=\sqrt{4\pi n}$, and $k_{\mathrm{TF}}^2=4k_F/\pi$. The plasma frequency is a homogeneous long-wavelength reference, not an exact crystal plasmon.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="能量与 hole conventions" titleEn="Energy and hole conventions">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

每电子能量用 lowercase $\varepsilon$ 或 explicit $E/N$。noninteracting kinetic、exchange 与 correlation 分别记为 $t_s$、$\varepsilon_x$、$\varepsilon_c$。exchange–correlation energy 是

$$
\varepsilon_{xc}=\varepsilon_x+\varepsilon_c,
$$

但其 potential-energy hole expression 必须使用 coupling-constant-averaged hole，才能包含 kinetic correlation。

pair distribution $g_{\sigma\sigma'}(r)$ 给定参考 electron 周围的 conditional density $n_{\sigma'}g_{\sigma\sigma'}(r)$。hole density 为

$$
n_{h,\sigma\sigma'}(r)
=n_{\sigma'}[g_{\sigma\sigma'}(r)-1].
$$

同 spin exchange hole 积分为 $-1$；correlation hole 在固定 exchange definition 下净积分为 $0$；total xc hole 积分为 $-1$。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Per-electron kinetic, exchange, and correlation energies are denoted $t_s$, $\varepsilon_x$, and $\varepsilon_c$. The full exchange–correlation energy requires a coupling-constant-averaged hole when expressed as a Coulomb hole integral, because kinetic correlation is included.

The conditional pair density is $n_{\sigma'}g_{\sigma\sigma'}(r)$ and the associated hole is

$$
n_{h,\sigma\sigma'}(r)=n_{\sigma'}[g_{\sigma\sigma'}(r)-1].
$$

The same-spin exchange hole integrates to minus one electron, the correlation hole carries zero net charge under the declared decomposition, and the total exchange–correlation hole integrates to minus one.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>
