<h2 id="section-5-3">5.3 关联 hole 与能量 | Correlation Hole and Energy</h2>

<BilingualSection titleZh="screening 是集体重排" titleEn="Screening is collective rearrangement" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】 §5.3 · pp. 117–121">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

bare Coulomb interaction 在 reciprocal space 中为 $4\pi/q^2$，其 $q\to0$ divergence 对应 long range。screening 不是简单“截断 interaction”，而是电子 density 对 test charge 的 collective response。induced density 产生的 field 与 external field 共同决定 effective interaction。

Thomas–Fermi approximation 用 local compressibility response 把

$$
\frac{4\pi}{q^2}
\longrightarrow
\frac{4\pi}{q^2+k_{\mathrm{TF}}^2}.
$$

real space 对应 Yukawa form

$$
v_{\mathrm{TF}}(r)=\frac{e^{-k_{\mathrm{TF}}r}}{r}.
$$

unpolarized atomic-unit relation 为

$$
k_{\mathrm{TF}}^2=\frac{4k_F}{\pi}
\propto r_s^{-1},
\qquad
k_{\mathrm{TF}}\propto r_s^{-1/2}.
$$

screening length $k_{\mathrm{TF}}^{-1}$ 随 density 增大而缩短，但相对于 mean spacing 的 scaling 不同。Thomas–Fermi 只控制 long-wavelength static limit，不能描述 $2k_F$ nonanalyticity、dynamic continuum、interband screening 或 local-field effects。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The bare interaction $4\pi/q^2$ is long ranged. Screening is the collective density rearrangement induced by a test charge. Thomas–Fermi theory replaces the static Coulomb kernel by

$$
\frac{4\pi}{q^2+k_{\mathrm{TF}}^2},
$$

corresponding to a Yukawa interaction in real space. For the unpolarized gas $k_{\mathrm{TF}}^2=4k_F/\pi$. This controls the long-wavelength static limit but not the $2k_F$ nonanalyticity, dynamic particle–hole continuum, interband screening, or crystal local-field effects.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="Thomas–Fermi screening from compressibility | Thomas–Fermi screening from compressibility"
  assumptions={[
    'static and long-wavelength response;',
    'the local chemical potential follows the noninteracting Fermi energy;',
    'atomic units and an unpolarized gas.']}
>

small electrostatic potential $\delta V$ 改变 local chemical potential：

$$
\delta\mu=\frac{d\mu}{dn}\delta n+\delta V=0.
$$

因此

$$
\delta n=-\frac{dn}{d\mu}\delta V.
$$

Poisson equation in Fourier space 为

$$
q^2\delta V_{\mathrm{ind}}=4\pi\delta n.
$$

令 total potential $\delta V=\delta V_{\mathrm{ext}}+\delta V_{\mathrm{ind}}$，得到

$$
\delta V
=\frac{q^2}{q^2+4\pi(dn/d\mu)}\delta V_{\mathrm{ext}}.
$$

所以

$$
k_{\mathrm{TF}}^2=4\pi\frac{dn}{d\mu}.
$$

对 $n=k_F^3/(3\pi^2)$ 与 $\mu=k_F^2/2$，

$$
\frac{dn}{d\mu}=\frac{k_F}{\pi^2},
$$

从而

$$
\boxed{k_{\mathrm{TF}}^2=\frac{4k_F}{\pi}}.
$$

<p lang="en">Linearizing the local chemical potential and combining it with Poisson's equation produces a screened denominator $q^2+k_{\mathrm{TF}}^2$. The screening wavevector is determined by the electronic compressibility.</p>

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="correlation energy 的定义" titleEn="Definition of the correlation energy">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

exact ground-state energy per electron 可分为

$$
\varepsilon(r_s,\zeta)
=t_s(r_s,\zeta)
+\varepsilon_x(r_s,\zeta)
+\varepsilon_c(r_s,\zeta).
$$

correlation energy 定义为 exact energy 与 Hartree–Fock minimum 的差；因此

$$
\varepsilon_c\le0
$$

for the exact ground state under the same Hamiltonian and state constraints。它包含 determinant 无法表示的 wavefunction relaxation，包括 kinetic correlation 与 interaction-energy reduction。

“correlation” 不是简单等同 opposite-spin avoidance。同-spin particles 已有 exchange hole，但 Coulomb interaction 仍会进一步重排 same- and opposite-spin pairs。dynamic correlation 描述大量 near-independent configurations 的短程响应；static correlation 出现在多个 determinants near-degenerate 时。uniform gas 的常规 metallic regime 主要是 screening-driven dynamic correlation reference。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The exact energy is written

$$
\varepsilon=t_s+\varepsilon_x+\varepsilon_c.
$$

Correlation is the energy gained beyond the best Hartree–Fock determinant under the same constraints. It contains both kinetic and interaction contributions and is not synonymous with opposite-spin avoidance alone.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="high-density 与 low-density limits" titleEn="High- and low-density limits">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

high density $r_s\to0$ 是 scaled weak-coupling limit，但 long-range Coulomb diagrams 逐阶 divergence，需要 ring-diagram resummation。unpolarized gas 的 asymptotic structure 为

$$
\varepsilon_c(r_s)
=A\ln r_s+B
+r_s(C\ln r_s+D)+\cdots.
$$

logarithm 表明 correlation 不是普通 Taylor series。leading coefficient 由 high-density many-body theory 决定；不同 energy-unit conventions 会改变数值表示。

low density 时 Coulomb energy 相对 kinetic energy dominant，electrons 倾向 localization 并最终形成 Wigner crystal。energy expansion 具有

$$
\varepsilon(r_s)
\sim -\frac{M}{r_s}
+\frac{C_{\mathrm{zp}}}{r_s^{3/2}}
+\frac{C_2}{r_s^2}+\cdots,
$$

分别对应 classical Madelung、zero-point motion 与更高 corrections。这个 asymptotic phase 不是普通 homogeneous Fermi liquid，不能用同一 analytic continuation 无条件覆盖。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

At high density, long-range Coulomb terms require resummation and produce logarithms,

$$
\varepsilon_c=A\ln r_s+B+r_s(C\ln r_s+D)+\cdots.
$$

At low density, Coulomb energy dominates and the system approaches a Wigner crystal with Madelung and zero-point terms. The low-density crystal is a different phase rather than a smooth continuation of every metallic approximation.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="parameterization 与 reference data" titleEn="Parameterization and reference data">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

analytic interpolation formulas 把 known asymptotic limits 与 finite-$r_s$ reference energies 连接。历史上 simple rational forms 曾用于定性估计，例如

$$
\varepsilon_c^{\mathrm{W}}(r_s)
=-\frac{0.44}{r_s+7.8}
$$

in Hartree units。现代 LDA correlation 通常基于 high-accuracy quantum Monte Carlo data 与 exact constraints 的 parameterization，而不是该简单式。

使用任何 parameterization 必须记录：reference dataset、spin interpolation、energy units、valid $r_s$ range、high-/low-density constraints 和 derivative continuity。energy fit 很准确并不保证 correlation potential、compressibility 或 second derivatives 同样准确。

QMC finite-cell energies 需要 shell/twist/Coulomb finite-size correction 和 thermodynamic extrapolation。parameterization 误差、QMC statistical uncertainty 与 fixed-node bias 是不同来源。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Interpolation formulas connect exact limits to finite-density reference data. A historical teaching form is $-0.44/(r_s+7.8)$ Hartree, but modern LDA correlation uses constrained fits to accurate many-body data. Dataset, spin interpolation, units, valid density range, derivative behavior, and finite-size treatment must be reported. A good energy fit does not automatically guarantee an accurate potential or compressibility.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="pair correlation 与 correlation hole" titleEn="Pair correlation and the correlation hole">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

spin-resolved pair distribution $g_{\sigma\sigma'}(r)$ 描述给定一个 spin-$\sigma$ electron 后在 separation $r$ 找到 spin-$\sigma'$ electron 的相对概率。limits：

$$
g_{\uparrow\uparrow}(0)=0
$$

由 Pauli antisymmetry 强制；opposite-spin contact 不被 Pauli 禁止，但 Coulomb repulsion 使其减少并满足 cusp condition。

exchange–correlation hole density 为

$$
n_{xc,\sigma}(\mathbf r,\mathbf r')
=\sum_{\sigma'}n_{\sigma'}
[g_{\sigma\sigma'}(r)-1].
$$

exact sum rule：

$$
\int d^3r'\,n_{xc,\sigma}(\mathbf r,\mathbf r')=-1.
$$

correlation hole defined relative to exchange only carries zero net charge：它在 short range 进一步 depletion，并在较大 distance 形成 positive compensating tail。screening 的 essential picture 正是 probability redistribution，而不是删除一个 electron。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The spin-resolved pair distribution gives the conditional probability of finding a second electron. Pauli antisymmetry enforces zero same-spin contact, while Coulomb repulsion suppresses opposite-spin contact and creates a cusp. The total exchange–correlation hole integrates to minus one electron. Correlation redistributes this hole while carrying zero net charge relative to the exchange-only decomposition.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="coupling-constant-averaged hole | Coupling-constant-averaged hole"
  assumptions={[
    'the electron density is held fixed as the interaction strength λ varies from zero to one;',
    'the external potential is adjusted to maintain that density;',
    'the ground state remains on the selected adiabatic branch.']}
>

定义 density-fixed Hamiltonian family

$$
\hat H^\lambda
=\hat T+\lambda\hat V_{ee}+\hat V_{\mathrm{ext}}^\lambda.
$$

Hellmann–Feynman theorem 给出

$$
\frac{dE^\lambda}{d\lambda}
=\langle\Psi^\lambda|\hat V_{ee}|\Psi^\lambda\rangle
+\int n(\mathbf r)\frac{\partial v_{\mathrm{ext}}^\lambda}{\partial\lambda}d^3r.
$$

subtract density-fixed noninteracting and Hartree pieces 后，integrate $\lambda$：

$$
E_{xc}
=\frac12\int d^3r\,n(\mathbf r)
\int d^3r'\,
\frac{\bar n_{xc}(\mathbf r,\mathbf r')}{|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|},
$$

其中

$$
\boxed{
\bar n_{xc}=\int_0^1d\lambda\,n_{xc}^\lambda.
}
$$

full-coupling hole $n_{xc}^{\lambda=1}$ 只给 interaction part；coupling-constant average 才把 kinetic correlation 包含进 $E_{xc}$。两者不能在公式和可视化中互换。

<p lang="en">Integrating the density-fixed interaction strength converts the exchange–correlation energy into a Coulomb interaction with the coupling-constant-averaged hole. The full-coupling hole alone does not include kinetic correlation.</p>

</DerivationBlock>
