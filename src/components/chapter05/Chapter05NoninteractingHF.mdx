<h2 id="section-5-2">5.2 非相互作用与 Hartree–Fock 近似 | Noninteracting and Hartree–Fock Approximations</h2>

<BilingualSection titleZh="occupied Fermi spheres 与 state counting" titleEn="Occupied Fermi spheres and state counting" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】 §5.2 · pp. 111–117">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

noninteracting orbitals 是 normalized plane waves

$$
\phi_{\mathbf k\sigma}(\mathbf r)
=\Omega^{-1/2}e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf r}\chi_\sigma,
\qquad
\varepsilon_k^{(0)}=\frac{k^2}{2}.
$$

zero-temperature ground state 对每个 spin 填满 $|\mathbf k|\le k_{F\sigma}$ 的 sphere。每个 k state 占 reciprocal-space volume $(2\pi)^3/\Omega$，因此

$$
N_\sigma
=\frac{\Omega}{(2\pi)^3}\frac{4\pi}{3}k_{F\sigma}^3,
$$

即

$$
\boxed{k_{F\sigma}=(6\pi^2n_\sigma)^{1/3}}.
$$

unpolarized 情形 $n_\sigma=n/2$ 给出 $k_F=(3\pi^2n)^{1/3}$。Fermi sphere volume 固定 particle count；在 normal Fermi liquid 且没有改变 Fermi-volume-counting 的 phase transition 时，interacting quasiparticle Fermi volume 仍受 Luttinger counting 约束。但 Fermi surface shape、quasiparticle residue 和 effective mass 不由这个 count 决定。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Noninteracting orbitals are plane waves with energy $k^2/2$. At zero temperature each spin channel fills a sphere in k space. Since one allowed k state occupies reciprocal volume $(2\pi)^3/\Omega$,

$$
N_\sigma=\frac{\Omega}{(2\pi)^3}\frac{4\pi}{3}k_{F\sigma}^3,
$$

and therefore $k_{F\sigma}=(6\pi^2n_\sigma)^{1/3}$. Fermi-volume counting constrains particle number but does not determine quasiparticle mass, spectral weight, or a material-specific Fermi-surface shape.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="kinetic energy per electron | Kinetic energy per electron"
  assumptions={[
    'zero temperature;',
    'three-dimensional parabolic dispersion k²/2;',
    'each spin channel is integrated over its own Fermi sphere.']}
>

对一个 spin channel，

$$
\frac{T_\sigma}{N_\sigma}
=\frac{\int_0^{k_{F\sigma}}4\pi k^2dk\,(k^2/2)}
{\int_0^{k_{F\sigma}}4\pi k^2dk}
=\frac35\frac{k_{F\sigma}^2}{2}
=\frac35E_{F\sigma}.
$$

总 per-electron kinetic energy 为 spin-weighted average：

$$
t_s(r_s,\zeta)
=\frac{3}{10}k_F^2
\frac{(1+\zeta)^{5/3}+(1-\zeta)^{5/3}}{2},
$$

其中 $k_F$ 是相同 total density 的 unpolarized value。于是

$$
t_s\propto r_s^{-2}.
$$

fully polarized gas 的 kinetic factor 为 $2^{2/3}$，说明 polarization 具有 Pauli kinetic cost。

<p lang="en">Averaging the parabolic dispersion over each filled Fermi sphere gives three fifths of the corresponding Fermi energy. The total kinetic energy scales as $r_s^{-2}$ and increases upon full spin polarization by $2^{2/3}$.</p>

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="one-body density matrix 与 Friedel scale" titleEn="One-body density matrix and the Friedel scale">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

对每个 spin，zero-temperature one-body reduced density matrix 为 occupied Fermi sphere 的 Fourier transform：

$$
\rho_\sigma(\mathbf r,\mathbf r')
=\int_{|\mathbf k|\le k_{F\sigma}}
\frac{d^3k}{(2\pi)^3}e^{i\mathbf k\cdot(\mathbf r-\mathbf r')}.
$$

令 $R=|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|$、$y=k_{F\sigma}R$，angular integration 得

$$
\rho_\sigma(R)
=n_\sigma F(y),
\qquad
F(y)=3\frac{\sin y-y\cos y}{y^3}.
$$

$F(0)=1$，large $y$ 时以 oscillatory $1/y^2$ envelope 衰减。long-range algebraic oscillation 源于 occupation step 在 $k_F$ 的 nonanalyticity。finite temperature 将 step 平滑化，使 real-space coherence 在 thermal length 上 exponential damping。

Friedel oscillation 的 wavevector scale 与 $2k_F$ response nonanalyticity 相关，但具体 impurity density response 还需要 scattering amplitude 与 screened susceptibility；不能把 one-body density matrix 本身当作 impurity-induced charge density。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The spin-resolved one-body density matrix is the Fourier transform of the occupied Fermi sphere,

$$
\rho_\sigma(R)=n_\sigma F(k_{F\sigma}R),
\qquad
F(y)=3\frac{\sin y-y\cos y}{y^3}.
$$

The algebraically decaying oscillations arise from the zero-temperature occupation discontinuity. Finite temperature smooths the Fermi edge and introduces thermal damping. The density-matrix kernel sets the Fermi length scale but is not by itself the density response to an impurity.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="removable y=0 singularity | The removable singularity at y=0"
  assumptions={[
    'Taylor expansions of sine and cosine about zero;',
    'the density-matrix kernel is evaluated continuously at y=0.']}
>

展开

$$
\sin y=y-\frac{y^3}{6}+\frac{y^5}{120}+\cdots,
$$

$$
y\cos y=y-\frac{y^3}{2}+\frac{y^5}{24}+\cdots.
$$

因此

$$
\sin y-y\cos y
=\frac{y^3}{3}-\frac{y^5}{30}+\cdots,
$$

从而

$$
\boxed{F(y)=1-\frac{y^2}{10}+O(y^4)}.
$$

numerical implementation 在小 $|y|$ 下必须使用 series 或 stable spherical-Bessel routine；直接计算两个接近数的差会造成 catastrophic cancellation。

<p lang="en">The apparent $0/0$ form is removable. The normalized kernel tends to one and has leading correction $-y^2/10$, which provides a stable small-argument implementation.</p>

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="Hartree–Fock plane waves 与 exchange self-energy" titleEn="Hartree–Fock plane waves and the exchange self-energy">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

translation invariance 使 HF Fock operator 在 plane waves 上 diagonal。uniform Hartree term 与 positive background cancel，剩下 nonlocal exchange self-energy。对 unpolarized gas，定义 $x=k/k_F$：

$$
\varepsilon_k^{\mathrm{HF}}
=\frac{k^2}{2}+\frac{k_F}{\pi}f(x),
$$

其中

$$
f(x)
=-\left[
1+\frac{1-x^2}{2x}
\ln\left|\frac{1+x}{1-x}\right|
\right].
$$

limits 为

$$
f(0)=-2,
\qquad
f(1)=-1,
\qquad
f(x\to\infty)\to0^-.
$$

exchange 使 occupied bandwidth 增大，但 derivative 在 $x=1$ logarithmically diverges。因 group velocity 是 $d\varepsilon/dk$，unscreened HF predicts an ill-defined Fermi velocity and vanishing DOS at $E_F$, 与 metallic thermodynamics/experiments 冲突。这是 long-range unscreened exchange 与 sharp Fermi surface 的 intrinsic failure，不是 k-grid 数值误差。

在 insulator 中 finite gap regularizes occupied–empty separation，因此 HF 可 qualitatively useful；在 metal 中需要 screened interaction 或 many-body resummation。把 hybrid functional 用于 metal 也必须检查 exact-exchange range、screening 和 k convergence。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Translation invariance keeps plane waves as Hartree–Fock eigenfunctions. After cancellation of the uniform Hartree term by the background, the exchange self-energy gives

$$
\varepsilon_k^{\mathrm{HF}}=rac{k^2}{2}+rac{k_F}{\pi}f(k/k_F),
$$

with

$$
f(x)=-\left[1+\frac{1-x^2}{2x}\ln\left|\frac{1+x}{1-x}\right|\right].
$$

The value at the Fermi surface is finite, but its derivative diverges logarithmically. Unscreened Hartree–Fock therefore produces an unphysical metallic Fermi velocity and DOS. This is an intrinsic long-range-exchange failure rather than a numerical mesh artifact.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="exchange energy and spin scaling | Exchange energy and spin scaling"
  assumptions={[
    'Hartree–Fock determinant of occupied plane waves;',
    'uniform positive background cancels the direct Hartree term;',
    'exchange acts only between equal spins.']}
>

exchange energy per electron in one spin channel is

$$
\varepsilon_x^\sigma
=-\frac{3}{4\pi}k_{F\sigma}.
$$

对总 density fixed，unpolarized exchange 为

$$
\boxed{
\varepsilon_x(r_s,0)
=-\frac{3}{4\pi}
\left(\frac{9\pi}{4}\right)^{1/3}rac1{r_s}
\approx-\frac{0.458165}{r_s}.
}
$$

spin polarization dependence 为

$$
\varepsilon_x(r_s,\zeta)
=\varepsilon_x(r_s,0)
\frac{(1+\zeta)^{4/3}+(1-\zeta)^{4/3}}{2}.
$$

fully polarized factor 为 $2^{1/3}$。与 kinetic factor $2^{2/3}$ 相比，exchange 更负但 kinetic 增长更快 at high density；correlation 会进一步改变 magnetic balance。

<p lang="en">Exchange acts within each spin channel. At fixed total density it scales as $r_s^{-1}$ and becomes more negative under polarization by a factor $2^{1/3}$ at full polarization, while the kinetic cost grows as $2^{2/3}$.</p>

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="exchange hole" titleEn="The exchange hole">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

对同 spin pair，Slater determinant 给出

$$
g_{x}^{\sigma\sigma}(R)
=1-|F(k_{F\sigma}R)|^2,
$$

而 opposite-spin exchange contribution 为零。conditional same-spin density 在 $R=0$ 处严格为零，这是 Pauli exclusion，而不是 Coulomb repulsion。exchange-hole density 为

$$
n_{x,\sigma}(R)
=-n_\sigma F(k_{F\sigma}R)^2.
$$

它满足

$$
\int d^3R\,n_{x,\sigma}(R)=-1.
$$

hole 长程 oscillatory tail 来自 sharp Fermi surface。exchange hole always nonpositive；correlation hole 可有 negative short-range region 与 positive compensating tail。把 pair-distribution $g$ 与 hole density $n(g-1)$ 混用会造成 normalization 错误。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

For equal spins, a plane-wave determinant gives

$$
g_x^{\sigma\sigma}(R)=1-|F(k_{F\sigma}R)|^2,
$$

and exchange vanishes between opposite spins. The same-spin conditional density is exactly zero at contact because of antisymmetry. The exchange-hole density $-n_\sigma F^2$ integrates to minus one electron and has a long oscillatory tail set by the sharp Fermi surface.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>
