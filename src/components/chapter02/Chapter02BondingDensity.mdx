<h2 id="section-2-2">2.2 电子基态：成键与特征结构 | Electronic Ground State: Bonding and Characteristic Structures</h2>

<BilingualSection titleZh="成键类型是电子基态的组织语言" titleEn="Bonding types organize the electronic ground state" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】 §2.2 · pp. 17–19">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

稳定结构可以按电子基态形成的成键方式进行分类。这个分类描述主导机制，不要求真实材料只含一种键。闭壳层体系保留较强的原子或分子特征，凝聚主要依赖弱的色散和其他范德华相互作用；离子晶体具有显著电荷转移，结构在短程排斥与长程 Coulomb 能之间取得平衡；金属存在低能电子激发和可变能带填充，容易形成合金及较致密结构；共价键由定向的成键态产生，常对应开放结构与能隙；氢键则利用质子无电子芯层这一特殊条件，在水、生物分子和高压氢化物中具有重要作用。

“离子”“共价”“金属”或“范德华”不是由单一数值自动决定的标签。异极共价材料同时含有电荷转移与定向成键；分子晶体和层状异质结构在分子内或层内具有强键，在分子间或层间由弱相互作用结合。分类的用途是选择需要保留的物理尺度和验证量，而不是把连续的电子重排切成互斥类别。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Stable structures can be classified by the bonding generated by the electronic ground state. The classification identifies dominant mechanisms; a real material need not contain only one bond type. Closed-shell systems retain strong atomic or molecular character and condense mainly through weak dispersion and other van der Waals interactions. Ionic crystals involve substantial charge transfer and balance short-range repulsion against long-range Coulomb energy. Metals possess low-energy electronic excitations and variable band filling, which facilitates alloying and often favours dense structures. Covalent bonds form directional bonding states and frequently produce open structures with an energy gap. Hydrogen bonding exploits the special absence of an electronic core around the proton and is important in water, biomolecules, and high-pressure hydrides.

“Ionic,” “covalent,” “metallic,” and “van der Waals” are not labels determined automatically by one number. Heteropolar covalent materials combine charge transfer with directional bonding. Molecular crystals and layered heterostructures contain strong intramolecular or intralayer bonds together with weak intermolecular or interlayer binding. The classification helps select the physical scales and validation quantities that matter; it does not partition continuous electronic rearrangement into mutually exclusive categories.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="典型成键与结构倾向" titleEn="Characteristic bonding and structural tendencies">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

闭壳层稀有气体通常形成致密堆积，是因为近球形组分之间缺少强方向性；非球形分子则可能形成复杂分子晶体。离子化合物常可理解为较大阴离子构成近密堆积，小阳离子占据能降低静电能的位置，但配位数和结构仍受离子尺寸、极化和共价混合影响。

简单 $sp$ 金属可从近均匀电子气出发理解，而过渡金属的部分占据 $d$ 态和稀土/锕系的 $f$ 态兼有局域与能带特征，磁性和关联使其更具挑战。共价半导体通过定向杂化形成键合/反键合分裂和开放网络；压力增大时，体积代价会推动部分体系向配位更高、结构更致密的金属相转变。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Closed-shell rare-gas solids commonly form dense packings because nearly spherical constituents provide little directional preference, whereas nonspherical molecules may form complex molecular crystals. Ionic compounds can often be understood as large anions in approximately close-packed arrangements with smaller cations occupying sites that lower electrostatic energy, although coordination and structure also depend on ionic size, polarization, and covalent mixing.

Simple $sp$ metals can be approached from the nearly uniform electron gas, while the partially filled $d$ states of transition metals and $f$ states of rare-earth and actinide materials retain both local and band character; magnetism and correlation make them more difficult. Covalent semiconductors form directional hybridized networks with bonding–antibonding splitting. Under pressure, the cost of volume can drive some systems toward more highly coordinated, denser metallic phases.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="电子密度能告诉我们什么" titleEn="What the electron density can establish">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

电子密度 $n(\mathbf r)$ 是可以通过 X 射线或高能电子散射约束的基态量。除最轻元素外，总密度主要由芯层电子贡献，因此实验密度同时包含近原子型芯层分布、热运动和零点运动造成的 Debye–Waller 展宽，以及成键和电荷转移引起的较小变化。理论与实验比较必须在相容的温度、热运动处理和分辨率下进行。

为了突出成键重排，可以构造晶体密度与叠加中性球形原子密度之间的差分。共价键区域通常出现电子积累，其他区域出现电子亏损。Martin 以 Si 为例说明 LDA 和 GGA 都能再现实验差分密度的主要特征，而芯层高梯度区域对泛函形式更敏感。这里的结论是密度能够被高精度计算和测量，不是任一差分等值面都能直接给出唯一“键级”或电荷转移数。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The electron density $n(\mathbf r)$ is a ground-state quantity constrained by X-ray or high-energy electron scattering. Except for the lightest elements, the total density is dominated by core electrons. An experimental density therefore contains nearly atomic core distributions, Debye–Waller smearing from thermal and zero-point motion, and smaller changes caused by bonding and charge transfer. Theory–experiment comparison requires compatible temperature, nuclear-motion treatment, and spatial resolution.

Bonding rearrangement can be emphasized by subtracting a superposition of neutral spherical atomic densities from the crystal density. Covalent-bond regions commonly show accumulation, accompanied by depletion elsewhere. Martin uses Si to show that LDA and GGA reproduce the principal experimental difference-density features, while high-gradient core regions are more sensitive to functional form. The supported conclusion is that density can be measured and calculated accurately; an arbitrary difference-density isosurface does not uniquely determine a bond order or charge-transfer number.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

$$
\Delta n(\mathbf r)
= n_{\mathrm{crystal}}(\mathbf r)
- \sum_A n_A^{\mathrm{neutral,spherical}}(\mathbf r-\mathbf R_A).
$$

<BilingualSection titleZh="差分密度是定义依赖的对象" titleEn="Difference density depends on its reference">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

上式是定义，不是可观测量的唯一分解。改变参考原子、价态、赝势、芯层处理或几何，会改变 $\Delta n(\mathbf r)$ 的图形。若比较异质结构成键，必须让复合体系和各片层使用相同晶胞、原子位置、基组、赝势、采样与电子数定义；否则差值可能混入基组和几何差异。

电子密度本身也不包含所有基态信息。Martin 指出，体相极化不能仅由周期晶体的体密度唯一确定，而可以借助 Wannier 中心或 Berry 相位描述。类似地，把总密度分到原子、轨道或层上需要额外分区规则；Bader、Mulliken、Löwdin 或投影电荷可能给出不同数值，重复同一名称并不构成独立验证。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The equation above is a definition, not a unique observable decomposition. Changing reference atoms, charge states, pseudopotentials, core treatment, or geometry changes the appearance of $\Delta n(\mathbf r)$. For a heterostructure comparison, the combined system and isolated layers must use the same cell, atomic positions, basis, pseudopotentials, sampling, and electron-number convention; otherwise the difference may include basis or geometry effects.

The density also does not contain every piece of ground-state information. Martin notes that bulk polarization cannot be determined uniquely from the periodic bulk density alone, whereas Wannier centres or a Berry-phase formulation can describe it. Likewise, assigning total density to atoms, orbitals, or layers requires an additional partitioning rule. Bader, Mulliken, Löwdin, and projected charges may give different values, and repeated use of one named partition does not constitute independent validation.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="成键判断的证据上限" titleEn="Evidence ceiling for bonding claims" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

结构、差分密度、投影态密度和电荷分区可以共同支持成键解释，但任何单项都不自动证明因果机制。等值面形状受绘图阈值影响；PDOS 受投影子空间影响；原子电荷受分区方案影响。强结论应同时检查结构趋势、可比较能量、密度重排、轨道或 Wannier 表征，并在结论依赖时比较至少一种具有不同定义的分析。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Structure, difference density, projected density of states, and charge partitioning can jointly support a bonding interpretation, but no single item automatically proves a causal mechanism. Isosurface shapes depend on plotting thresholds, PDOS depends on projection subspaces, and atomic charges depend on partitioning. Strong claims should examine structural trends, comparable energies, density rearrangement, orbital or Wannier representations, and—when the conclusion depends on it—at least one analysis based on a genuinely different definition.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>
