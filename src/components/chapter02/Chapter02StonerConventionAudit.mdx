而 Appendix D 的式 (D.11) 对扰动 $\delta\hat H=V_m^{\mathrm{ext}}m$ 采用 $\chi=\delta m/\delta V_m^{\mathrm{ext}}$。这些式子若把所有 $V_m$ 和 $I$ 都视为完全相同的带符号变量，就不能直接串成一条推导；它们反映了未显式写出的共轭场符号切换，或等价的 kernel 符号重定义。这里不擅自断定作者意图，也不把它宣布为已确认勘误。

本站后续推导改用单独声明的“促进正磁化”的源场 $h$，并把正的 Stoner 参数 $I$ 定义为增强该响应的 kernel：

$$
m=\chi_0 h_{\mathrm{eff}},
\qquad
h_{\mathrm{eff}}=h_{\mathrm{ext}}+I m.
$$

于是

$$
\chi_h\equiv\frac{m}{h_{\mathrm{ext}}}
=\frac{\chi_0}{1-I\chi_0}.
$$

若要与式 (2.2) 的变量对应，可以取 $h=-V_m$；但此时 kernel 也必须同步变换，$I_h=-I_{V_m}$。不能只翻转外场而继续沿用同一个带符号 $I$。采用 Martin 在式 (2.3) 附近的简化归一化 $\chi_0=N(0)$，便得到 Stoner 形式。这里的 $N(0)$、$I$ 与 $\chi$ 已吸收所选自旋计数、磁矩和单位因子；其他资料可能使用单自旋或双自旋 DOS，并显式保留 $\mu_B^2$。只有同时变换 DOS、$I$ 和磁场单位后，$I N(0)=1$ 的判据才可比较。

这一审计不改变 Stoner 平均场的物理结论：正反馈使顺磁响应增强，分母趋近零表示该参考态的平均场失稳。它只禁止混用未声明的场符号、kernel 符号和 DOS 归一化。

Immediately before Eq. (2.2), Martin denotes the spin-dependent potential by $V_m$ and writes

$$
m(\mathbf r)=-\frac{\delta E}{\delta V_m(\mathbf r)},
\qquad
\chi(\mathbf r,\mathbf r')
=-\frac{\delta m(\mathbf r)}{\delta V_m(\mathbf r')}.
$$

The same section then writes $V_m=V_m^{\mathrm{ext}}+I m$ immediately before Eq. (2.3) and gives

$$
\chi=\frac{N(0)}{1-I N(0)}.
$$

Appendix D, Eq. (D.11), instead uses $\chi=\delta m/\delta V_m^{\mathrm{ext}}$ for the perturbation $\delta\hat H=V_m^{\mathrm{ext}}m$. These relations cannot be chained while treating every occurrence of $V_m$ and $I$ as the same signed variables. They expose an unstated change of conjugate-field sign, or an equivalent redefinition of the kernel sign. The site records this as a source-convention ambiguity without claiming knowledge of the intended correction or declaring a confirmed erratum.

The derivation below therefore introduces a separately declared magnetizing source $h$, defined to favour positive magnetization, and a positive Stoner parameter $I$ defined as the kernel that enhances that response:

$$
m=\chi_0 h_{\mathrm{eff}},
\qquad
h_{\mathrm{eff}}=h_{\mathrm{ext}}+I m.
$$

It follows that

$$
\chi_h\equiv\frac{m}{h_{\mathrm{ext}}}
=\frac{\chi_0}{1-I\chi_0}.
$$

To map this notation to Eq. (2.2), one may set $h=-V_m$, but the kernel must then transform as well: $I_h=-I_{V_m}$. Flipping only the source field while retaining the same signed $I$ is inconsistent. Using Martin's simplified normalization near Eq. (2.3), $\chi_0=N(0)$, gives the Stoner form. Here $N(0)$, $I$, and $\chi$ absorb the chosen spin counting, magnetic-moment factors, and units. Other conventions use a per-spin or two-spin DOS and may retain factors such as $\mu_B^2$ explicitly. The criterion $I N(0)=1$ is comparable only after the DOS, $I$, and field units have been transformed consistently.

This audit does not alter the Stoner mean-field conclusion: positive feedback enhances the paramagnetic response, and a vanishing denominator signals a mean-field instability of the reference state. It only prohibits mixing undeclared source-field signs, kernel signs, and DOS normalizations.
