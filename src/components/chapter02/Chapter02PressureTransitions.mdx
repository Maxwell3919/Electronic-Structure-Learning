<h2 id="section-2-5">2.5 压力下的相变 | Phase Transitions under Pressure</h2>

<BilingualSection titleZh="固定压力下比较焓" titleEn="Comparing enthalpy at fixed pressure" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】 §2.5 · pp. 23–26">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

压力缩短原子间距，通常使开放、低配位结构与致密、高配位结构之间发生竞争。对每个候选相，零温电子结构计算给出一条 $E_i(V)$ 曲线；在给定外压 $P$ 下，稳定相不是简单选择最低 $E$，而是最小化焓

$$
H_i(P)=\min_V\left[E_i(V)+PV\right].
$$

$PV$ 项惩罚大体积，因此随着压力升高，较致密相可以克服其零压能量劣势。准确预测转变压力要求同一近似能够描述不同配位、成键和电子状态，这使高压相变成为泛函和数值实现的严格测试。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Pressure reduces interatomic distances and commonly creates competition between open, low-coordination structures and dense, high-coordination structures. For each candidate phase, a zero-temperature electronic-structure calculation produces an $E_i(V)$ curve. At a specified external pressure $P$, the stable phase is not selected simply by the lowest $E$; it minimizes the enthalpy

$$
H_i(P)=\min_V\left[E_i(V)+PV\right].
$$

The $PV$ term penalizes large volume, allowing a denser phase to overcome an energy disadvantage at zero pressure. Accurate transition pressures require one approximation to describe different coordination environments, bonding types, and electronic states, making high-pressure transitions stringent tests of functionals and numerical implementations.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="补充推导 | Supplemental derivation: common-tangent construction"
  assumptions={[
    '零温、静水压力、固定组成；',
    '每个相的内部自由度已在给定体积下优化；',
    '忽略界面能、成核势垒和有限温度熵。',
  ]}
>

设相 $A$ 与 $B$ 在转变压力 $P_t$ 下共存，平衡体积分别为 $V_A$ 与 $V_B$。每个相在固定压力下满足

$$
\left.\frac{d}{dV}(E_i+P_tV)\right|_{V_i}=0,
$$

因此

$$
\left.\frac{dE_A}{dV}\right|_{V_A}
=
\left.\frac{dE_B}{dV}\right|_{V_B}
=-P_t.
$$

共存还要求焓相等：

$$
E_A(V_A)+P_tV_A=E_B(V_B)+P_tV_B.
$$

整理可得

$$
\frac{E_B(V_B)-E_A(V_A)}{V_B-V_A}=-P_t.
$$

这说明连接两条 $E(V)$ 曲线接触点的直线具有共同斜率 $-P_t$，即公共切线。若 $V_B<V_A$，高压相通常是 $B$。体积跃变 $\Delta V=V_B-V_A$ 非零时，对应一阶相变；若两相通过连续软模连接，转变可能表现为连续结构不稳定，不能仅靠两条独立抛物线概括。

<p lang="en">At coexistence, each phase minimizes $E_i(V)+P_tV$, so both $E(V)$ curves have slope $-P_t$ at their equilibrium volumes. Equality of enthalpy then shows that the line connecting the two contact points has the same slope. This is the common-tangent construction. A finite volume jump is characteristic of a first-order transition; a continuous soft-mode transition requires a different description.</p>

维度检查：切线斜率为能量/体积，等于负压力。绘图时若横轴使用 $V/V_0$，斜率不能直接当作压力，必须恢复真实体积尺度。

<p lang="en">Dimensional check: the tangent slope is energy per volume, equal to minus pressure. If the horizontal axis is normalized as $V/V_0$, the graphical slope is not a pressure until the physical volume scale is restored.</p>

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="亚稳相、滞后与实验路径" titleEn="Metastability, hysteresis, and experimental paths">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

焓最低只给出热力学平衡相。实验相变还需要成核和原子重排，因而可能出现压力滞后、亚稳相保留和卸压后生成不同结构。Martin 以 Si 为例：金刚石结构在压力下转变到更致密金属相，卸压路径可以保留 bc8 或 st12 等亚稳结构。理论能够比较尚未制备的候选结构，但若结构搜索不完整，所谓“全局最低焓”仍只是已搜索集合中的最低值。

因此实验转变压力不应与零温平衡压力机械比较。需要说明加压还是卸压、温度、压力介质、应力非静水性、样品缺陷和结构鉴定方法。理论侧需要区分平衡相界、动力学路径与有限温度自由能。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Minimum enthalpy identifies only the thermodynamic equilibrium phase. Experimental transitions also require nucleation and atomic rearrangement, producing pressure hysteresis, retained metastable phases, and decompression products different from the compression path. Martin uses Si as an example: the diamond structure transforms to denser metallic phases under pressure, while decompression can retain metastable bc8 or st12 structures. Theory can compare structures not yet synthesized, but if the structural search is incomplete, a claimed “global minimum” is only the lowest member of the searched set.

An experimental transition pressure should therefore not be compared mechanically with a zero-temperature equilibrium pressure. Compression versus decompression, temperature, pressure medium, nonhydrostatic stress, defects, and structural identification must be stated. Theory must distinguish an equilibrium phase boundary, a kinetic pathway, and a finite-temperature free-energy transition.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="泛函误差如何移动相界" titleEn="How functional error moves a phase boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

若泛函相对偏爱高密度结构，其致密相 $E(V)$ 曲线会相对降低，公共切线在更小压力处出现。Martin 给出的 Si 和 SiO$_2$ 例子显示，LDA 的高密度偏好可以低估转变压力，甚至在 SiO$_2$ 中错误稳定致密相；其他泛函改善定性相序，但数值转变压力仍有方法依赖。

相界对很小的相对能量误差敏感。粗略地说，若两相能量差误差为 $\delta(\Delta E)$，体积差为 $\Delta V$，转变压力误差尺度约为

$$
\delta P_t\sim -\frac{\delta(\Delta E)}{\Delta V}.
$$

当 $\Delta V$ 很小时，即使能量误差不大也会产生较大压力误差；此时还要检查零点能、磁态和有限温度贡献。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

If a functional preferentially stabilizes high-density structures, the dense-phase $E(V)$ curve is shifted downward and the common tangent appears at a lower pressure. Martin's Si and SiO$_2$ examples show that LDA's high-density bias can underestimate a transition pressure and can even stabilize the wrong dense phase in SiO$_2$. Other functionals improve the qualitative phase ordering, while the numerical transition pressure remains method dependent.

A phase boundary is sensitive to small relative-energy errors. If the error in the phase energy difference is $\delta(\Delta E)$ and the volume difference is $\Delta V$, a characteristic transition-pressure error is

$$
\delta P_t\sim -\frac{\delta(\Delta E)}{\Delta V}.
$$

When $\Delta V$ is small, even a modest energy error can produce a large pressure error. Zero-point energy, magnetic state, and finite-temperature terms then require particular attention.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="相变主张的最低证据" titleEn="Minimum evidence for a phase-transition claim" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <ul>
      <li>候选相集合及其来源可追踪，不能只比较两个预选结构；</li>
      <li>所有相在相同理论层级和相容数值精度下计算；</li>
      <li>每条 $E(V)$ 曲线经过内部弛豫、拟合敏感性与残差检查；</li>
      <li>报告焓交叉及不确定性，不用单点总能差代替相界；</li>
      <li>分别检查动力学稳定性、有限温度自由能和可能的分解反应；</li>
      <li>与实验比较时区分平衡压力、观测压力和滞后区间。</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <ul>
      <li>provide a traceable candidate-phase set rather than comparing only two preselected structures;</li>
      <li>calculate every phase at the same theoretical level and compatible numerical precision;</li>
      <li>relax internal degrees of freedom and assess fit sensitivity and residuals for every $E(V)$ curve;</li>
      <li>report enthalpy crossings and uncertainty rather than replacing a phase boundary with a single-point energy difference;</li>
      <li>check dynamical stability, finite-temperature free energy, and possible decomposition separately;</li>
      <li>distinguish equilibrium pressure, observed pressure, and hysteresis range when comparing with experiment.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</BilingualCallout>
