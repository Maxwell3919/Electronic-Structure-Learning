<h2 id="section-2-3">2.3 体积或压力：最基本的控制变量 | Volume or Pressure as the Most Fundamental Variable</h2>

<BilingualSection titleZh="从固定体积总能到状态方程" titleEn="From fixed-volume total energy to an equation of state" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】 §2.3 · pp. 19–21">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

凝聚态的稳定结构由压力和温度决定。对理论计算，固定晶胞体积 $V$ 并求电子基态总能 $E(V)$ 通常比直接固定压力更自然，因此体积成为可控参数。零温时，$E(V)$ 的最低点给出零外压平衡体积；一阶导数给出压力，二阶导数给出体模量。有限温度问题需要加入振动、电子、构型或其他熵贡献，比较对象从总能转为相应热力学势。

这些关系只对固定粒子数和清楚定义的能量归一化成立。晶体计算常使用每原胞能量与每原胞体积；若两个相使用不同原胞，必须换算到相同化学计量单位后才能比较。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The stable structure of condensed matter is determined by pressure and temperature. In theory it is usually more natural to fix the cell volume $V$ and calculate the electronic ground-state total energy $E(V)$ than to impose pressure directly, so volume becomes a convenient control parameter. At zero temperature, the minimum of $E(V)$ gives the zero-external-pressure equilibrium volume; its first derivative gives pressure and its second derivative gives the bulk modulus. At finite temperature, vibrational, electronic, configurational, or other entropy terms must be added, and the relevant comparison changes from total energy to the appropriate thermodynamic potential.

These relations assume fixed particle number and a clearly stated energy normalization. Crystal calculations commonly use energy and volume per cell; phases represented by different cells must be converted to the same chemical formula unit before comparison.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

$$
P(V)=-\left(\frac{\partial E}{\partial V}\right)_{N},
\qquad
B(V)=-V\left(\frac{\partial P}{\partial V}\right)_{N}
=V\frac{\partial^2E}{\partial V^2}.
$$

<DerivationBlock
  title="补充推导 | Supplemental derivation: pressure and bulk modulus from work"
  assumptions={[
    '零温、固定粒子数，并采用静水体积变化；',
    'E(V) 在讨论点附近可微；',
    '正压力定义为压缩体系的外部压力。',
  ]}
>

对准静态体积变化，外界对体系做功为 $\delta W=-P\,dV$。在零温可逆变化中，体系能量变化等于该功：

$$
dE=-P\,dV.
$$

两边除以 $dV$ 得到 $P=-dE/dV$。负号说明当压缩使能量升高时，$dV<0$ 而 $P>0$。体模量定义为体积相对变化所需的压力增量：

$$
B\equiv- V\frac{dP}{dV}.
$$

代入 $P=-dE/dV$ 得 $B=Vd^2E/dV^2$。稳定平衡点 $V_0$ 满足 $dE/dV=0$ 且 $d^2E/dV^2>0$，因此 $B_0>0$。若曲率为负，该点对均匀体积扰动不稳定。

<p lang="en">For a quasistatic hydrostatic volume change, work done on the system is $\delta W=-P\,dV$. At zero temperature in a reversible change, $dE=-P\,dV$, giving $P=-dE/dV$. The bulk modulus is defined by $B=-VdP/dV$, hence $B=Vd^2E/dV^2$. A stable equilibrium volume satisfies $dE/dV=0$ and positive curvature, so $B_0>0$.</p>

维度检查：$dE/dV$ 的单位是能量/体积，即压力；$Vd^2E/dV^2$ 仍为能量/体积。用 eV/Å$^3$ 计算时，换算到 GPa 必须使用明确的单位因子。

<p lang="en">Dimensional check: $dE/dV$ has units of energy per volume, i.e. pressure; $Vd^2E/dV^2$ has the same units. When energies and volumes are in eV and Å$^3$, conversion to GPa requires an explicit unit factor.</p>

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="有限温度下选择哪一个热力学势" titleEn="Choosing a thermodynamic potential at finite temperature">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

固定 $V,T$ 时比较 Helmholtz 自由能

$$
F(V,T)=E(V,T)-T S(V,T).
$$

固定 $P,T$ 时比较 Gibbs 自由能

$$
G(P,T)=H(P,T)-TS(P,T),\qquad H=E+PV.
$$

零温固体计算经常把电子总能作为主项，并在第二步加入零点能和振动自由能。这个近似是否充分取决于相之间的能量差和熵差；当竞争相仅相差数 meV/原子时，忽略零点运动、磁熵或构型熵可能改变相序。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

At fixed $V,T$, phases are compared using the Helmholtz free energy

$$
F(V,T)=E(V,T)-T S(V,T).
$$

At fixed $P,T$, the Gibbs free energy is appropriate:

$$
G(P,T)=H(P,T)-TS(P,T),\qquad H=E+PV.
$$

Zero-temperature solid-state calculations often take the electronic total energy as the dominant term and add zero-point and vibrational free energies in a second step. Whether this is sufficient depends on the energy and entropy differences between phases. If competing phases differ by only a few meV per atom, omitting zero-point motion, magnetic entropy, or configurational entropy may change the ordering.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="状态方程计算的最低验证要求" titleEn="Minimum validation for an equation-of-state calculation" kind="practice">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">
    <ul>
      <li>每个体积点使用一致的结构约束、电子数、赝势、基组和 $k$ 点密度；</li>
      <li>在固定体积下充分优化允许变化的内部坐标和晶胞形状；</li>
      <li>体积采样覆盖最低点两侧，拟合残差不能只在图上“看起来平滑”；</li>
      <li>对拟合形式和采样窗口做敏感性检查，报告 $V_0$、$B_0$ 及其数值稳定性；</li>
      <li>与实验比较时处理温度、零点膨胀、压力标定和实验相身份。</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <ul>
      <li>use consistent structural constraints, electron count, pseudopotentials, basis, and $k$-point density at every volume;</li>
      <li>relax all permitted internal coordinates and cell-shape degrees of freedom at fixed volume;</li>
      <li>sample both sides of the minimum and assess fit residuals rather than relying on a visually smooth curve;</li>
      <li>test sensitivity to fit form and volume window, reporting numerical stability of $V_0$ and $B_0$;</li>
      <li>account for temperature, zero-point expansion, pressure calibration, and experimental phase identity when comparing with measurement.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</BilingualCallout>
