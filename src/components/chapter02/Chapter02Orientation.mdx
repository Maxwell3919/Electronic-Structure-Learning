<h2 id="reading-orientation">阅读定位 | Reading Orientation</h2>

<BilingualSection titleZh="本章承担的任务" titleEn="The role of this chapter" eyebrow="Martin Chapter 2 · printed pp. 15–59">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Chapter 1 建立了电子结构理论的历史与概念入口；Chapter 2 将视角转向物质性质，展示同一套电子理论为何需要同时处理结构、相变、弹性、声子、热力学、表面、低维体系、能带、光谱、拓扑与关联。它是一张物理问题地图：每一节说明目标 observable、控制变量和方法边界，详细理论在后续章节展开。

本章的核心分界是电子基态与电子激发态。基态电子能量面决定平衡结构、压力、力、弹性与绝热核运动；激发谱决定输运、加减电子谱、光学响应和低能准粒子。电子与原子核的耦合跨越这条分界，例如电子—声子相互作用会影响谱线宽化、结构不稳定和超导，但不同现象需要不同计算对象与收敛标准。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Chapter 1 established the historical and conceptual entrance to electronic structure. Chapter 2 turns to properties of matter and shows why the same electronic theory must address structures, phase transitions, elasticity, phonons, thermodynamics, surfaces, low-dimensional systems, bands, spectra, topology, and correlation. It functions as a map of physical problems: each section identifies a target observable, control variables, and methodological boundaries, while later chapters supply the detailed theory.

The central division is between the electronic ground state and electronic excited states. The ground-state energy surface controls equilibrium structure, pressure, forces, elasticity, and adiabatic nuclear motion. Excitation spectra control transport, electron-addition and removal spectra, optical response, and low-energy quasiparticles. Electron–nuclear coupling crosses this division: electron–phonon interaction can broaden spectra, drive structural instabilities, and participate in superconductivity, but these phenomena require distinct computational objects and convergence criteria.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="前置知识与后续连接" titleEn="Prerequisites and later connections">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

前置知识包括 Chapter 1 的独立电子图像、电子密度、Kohn–Sham DFT 与关联边界，以及基本热力学中的能量、熵、压力和自由能。本章不会要求先掌握完整 DFT 推导，但会使用能量导数、状态方程、应力—应变和声子等概念来说明后续计算为什么需要不同的数值门槛。

Chapter 3 将写出相互作用电子与原子核的基本方程；Chapter 4–5 提供周期固体、能带和电子气背景；Part II 建立 DFT；Part IV 给出具体电子结构方法；Part V–VI 回到本章列出的性质与拓扑。因此本章适合作为全书的交叉索引，而不是独立的计算配方。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Prerequisites include Chapter 1's independent-electron picture, electron density, Kohn–Sham DFT, and correlation boundaries, together with the elementary thermodynamic concepts of energy, entropy, pressure, and free energy. A complete DFT derivation is not required here, but energy derivatives, equations of state, stress–strain relations, and phonons are used to explain why later calculations require different numerical gates.

Chapter 3 writes the basic equations for interacting electrons and nuclei; Chapters 4–5 provide the periodic-solid, band, and electron-gas background; Part II develops DFT; Part IV introduces concrete electronic-structure methods; Parts V–VI return to the properties and topology surveyed here. Chapter 2 is therefore a cross-index for the book rather than a standalone computational recipe.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>
