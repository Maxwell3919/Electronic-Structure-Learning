<h2 id="section-2-9">2.9 声子与位移型相变 | Phonons and Displacive Phase Transitions</h2>

<BilingualSection titleZh="核位移响应仍由电子基态能量面决定" titleEn="Nuclear-displacement response still comes from the electronic ground-state surface" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】 §2.9 · pp. 35–38">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

在绝热近似下，电子随核位置 $\{\mathbf R_I\}$ 调整到瞬时基态，总能可以写成 $E(\{\mathbf R_I\})$。力是一阶导数，原子间力常数是二阶导数：

$$
\mathbf F_I=-\frac{\partial E}{\partial\mathbf R_I},
\qquad
C_{I\alpha,J\beta}
=\frac{\partial^2E}
{\partial R_{I\alpha}\partial R_{J\beta}}.
$$

声子频率和本征矢由完整力常数矩阵决定。实验常直接测得频率和对称性，而理论同时给出本征矢、实空间力常数、有效电荷、介电响应和电子—声子矩阵元。频率与实验接近不能自动验证这些附加量；每个 observable 仍需要独立比较。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Within the adiabatic approximation, electrons adjust to the instantaneous ground state for nuclear positions $\{\mathbf R_I\}$, so the total energy is $E(\{\mathbf R_I\})$. Forces are first derivatives and interatomic force constants are second derivatives:

$$
\mathbf F_I=-\frac{\partial E}{\partial\mathbf R_I},
\qquad
C_{I\alpha,J\beta}
=\frac{\partial^2E}
{\partial R_{I\alpha}\partial R_{J\beta}}.
$$

Phonon frequencies and eigenvectors follow from the complete force-constant matrix. Experiments often measure frequencies and symmetries directly, whereas theory also provides eigenvectors, real-space force constants, effective charges, dielectric response, and electron–phonon matrix elements. Agreement in frequencies does not automatically validate these additional quantities; each observable requires its own comparison.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="补充推导 | Supplemental derivation: harmonic modes and the dynamical matrix"
  assumptions={[
    '参考结构满足所有原子力为零；',
    '位移足够小，总能保留到二阶；',
    'Born–Oppenheimer 近似成立，暂不含显式非绝热频率依赖。',
  ]}
>

令 $u_{I\alpha}=R_{I\alpha}-R^{(0)}_{I\alpha}$。在平衡点附近

$$
E=E_0+\frac12\sum_{I\alpha,J\beta}
C_{I\alpha,J\beta}u_{I\alpha}u_{J\beta}+O(u^3).
$$

Newton 方程为

$$
M_I\ddot u_{I\alpha}
=-\sum_{J\beta}C_{I\alpha,J\beta}u_{J\beta}.
$$

取简谐形式 $u_{I\alpha}(t)=M_I^{-1/2}e_{I\alpha}e^{-i\omega t}$，得到质量加权本征问题

$$
\sum_{J\beta}D_{I\alpha,J\beta}e_{J\beta}
=\omega^2e_{I\alpha},
\qquad
D_{I\alpha,J\beta}
=\frac{C_{I\alpha,J\beta}}{\sqrt{M_IM_J}}.
$$

稳定模式要求 $\omega^2>0$。若 $\omega^2<0$，程序常输出“虚频” $\omega=i|\omega|$；其物理含义是参考结构沿该本征矢具有负曲率，不是原子以虚数频率振动。$\omega=0$ 可能是平移声学模、连续对称性 Goldstone 模，或临界软模，必须结合波矢与对称性解释。

<p lang="en">Expanding the Born–Oppenheimer energy to quadratic order and inserting a mass-weighted harmonic displacement gives the dynamical-matrix eigenproblem $D e=\omega^2e$. Negative $\omega^2$ means negative curvature along the eigenvector, commonly printed as an imaginary frequency. A zero eigenvalue may be a translational acoustic mode, a Goldstone mode, or a critical soft mode and must be interpreted using wavevector and symmetry.</p>

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="冻结声子与线性响应" titleEn="Frozen phonons and linear response">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

冻结声子法直接构造正负位移结构，计算总能或力，并以有限差分获得力常数。它复用普通基态计算，适合特定模式、非线性势能面和有限振幅失稳；缺点是非 Γ 波矢需要相容超胞，计算量随超胞和位移数增加。

密度泛函微扰理论或一般线性响应方法直接求能量导数，可以在原胞中处理任意可访问 $\mathbf q$ 点，并系统获得介电、Born 有效电荷、压电和电子—声子响应。两种方法在相同理论与收敛极限下应一致；它们共享交换—关联、赝势、采样和绝热近似误差，因此相互吻合不等于独立验证物理模型。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The frozen-phonon method constructs structures with positive and negative displacements, computes energies or forces, and obtains force constants by finite differences. It reuses ordinary ground-state calculations and is useful for selected modes, nonlinear energy surfaces, and finite-amplitude instabilities. Its cost grows with supercell size and displacement count, and non-$\Gamma$ wavevectors require compatible supercells.

Density-functional perturbation theory or general linear-response methods calculate energy derivatives directly, treating accessible $\mathbf q$ points in the primitive cell and producing dielectric, Born-effective-charge, piezoelectric, and electron–phonon responses systematically. The two approaches should agree at the same theory and convergence limit. They share functional, pseudopotential, sampling, and adiabatic-approximation errors, so agreement between them is not independent validation of the physical model.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="软模与位移型相变" titleEn="Soft modes and displacive phase transitions">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

若高对称结构沿某一模式的二阶曲率为负，能量在原点附近下降，原结构是不稳定驻点。沿本征矢扫描有限位移可以识别双井或多井势能面、最低能方向和非谐项。Martin 以 BaTiO$_3$ 的极性位移和 Zr 的晶格调制为例，说明软模如何连接铁电相变或结构转变。

单个 Γ 点软模只说明该波矢的局域失稳。确认动力学稳定需要足够密的全布里渊区 $\mathbf q$ 网格、声学和约规则、非解析 LO–TO 修正（对极性材料）、超胞尺寸或插值收敛。二维材料还需区分真实柔性 ZA 模与数值破坏平移/旋转不变性造成的小虚频。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

If a high-symmetry structure has negative quadratic curvature along a mode, its energy decreases away from the origin and the structure is an unstable stationary point. Finite-displacement scans along the eigenvector can reveal double- or multi-well energy surfaces, the lowest-energy direction, and anharmonic terms. Martin uses polar displacements in BaTiO$_3$ and a lattice modulation in Zr to illustrate how a soft mode can connect to ferroelectric or structural transitions.

One soft mode at $\Gamma$ establishes only a local instability at that wavevector. Dynamical stability requires a sufficiently dense full-Brillouin-zone $\mathbf q$ mesh, acoustic-sum-rule checks, nonanalytic LO–TO corrections for polar materials, and supercell or interpolation convergence. In two-dimensional materials, genuine flexible ZA modes must be distinguished from small imaginary frequencies caused by numerical violation of translational or rotational invariance.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="声子谱不能单独支持的更强结论" titleEn="Claims that a phonon plot cannot establish alone" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

“所有谐波频率为实数”只支持指定结构在指定理论、体积和数值精度下的局域谐波稳定。它不证明有限温度稳定、全局热力学稳定、无扩散或重构路径、无强非谐效应、可合成性或超导。反之，小虚频也不应在未检查收敛、和规则、二维真空与插值前直接解释为真实相变。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

“All harmonic frequencies are real” supports only local harmonic stability of the specified structure at the stated theory, volume, and numerical precision. It does not establish finite-temperature stability, global thermodynamic stability, absence of diffusion or reconstruction pathways, weak anharmonicity, synthesizability, or superconductivity. Conversely, a small imaginary frequency should not be interpreted as a physical transition before convergence, sum rules, two-dimensional vacuum, and interpolation have been checked.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>
