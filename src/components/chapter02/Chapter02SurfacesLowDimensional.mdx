<h2 id="section-2-11">2.11 表面与界面 | Surfaces and Interfaces</h2>

<BilingualSection titleZh="表面会重构、换化学计量并产生新电子态" titleEn="Surfaces reconstruct, change stoichiometry, and create new electronic states" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】 §2.11 · pp. 44–47">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

真实固体总有表面。截断体相周期性会产生悬挂键、局域电荷和新的结构自由度，表面原子可以弛豫、成键、重构或与环境交换原子。Si/Ge(100) 表面的二聚与翘曲说明电子占据如何驱动几何重构：对称二聚体的半占据表面态进一步分裂为一个主要占据态和一个主要空态，从而降低能量。

表面不是固定化学计量的“真空界面”。气体分压、温度和生长条件改变原子化学势，可能稳定具有不同阴/阳离子数目的终止与重构。只比较相同原子数的 slab 总能无法处理这种竞争；必须使用开放粒子数的热力学势。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Every real solid has a surface. Terminating bulk periodicity creates dangling bonds, local charge, and new structural degrees of freedom, allowing surface atoms to relax, rebond, reconstruct, or exchange atoms with the environment. Dimerization and buckling on Si/Ge(100) illustrate how electronic occupation drives geometry: the half-filled states of a symmetric dimer split into a predominantly occupied state and a predominantly empty state, lowering the energy.

A surface is not merely a fixed-stoichiometry interface with an inert vacuum. Gas partial pressures, temperature, and growth conditions change atomic chemical potentials and can stabilize terminations or reconstructions with different anion and cation counts. Comparing slab total energies at equal atom number cannot treat this competition; an open-particle-number thermodynamic potential is required.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="补充推导 | Supplemental derivation: surface grand potential"
  assumptions={[
    '表面与体相及元素储库达到热力学平衡；',
    'slab 两侧表面积和表面数目已明确；',
    '所有候选使用相同计算定义并对有限尺寸收敛。',
  ]}
>

对含各元素 $N_I$ 个原子的表面模型，开放体系的热力学势为

$$
\Omega=E-TS-\sum_I\mu_I N_I.
$$

若 slab 含两个等价表面、单面面积为 $A$，表面自由能可写为

$$
\gamma(\{\mu_I\},T)
=\frac{1}{2A}
\left[G_{\mathrm{slab}}(T)-\sum_I\mu_I N_I\right].
$$

零温静态近似常用 $G_{\mathrm{slab}}\approx E_{\mathrm{slab}}$。对二元化合物 $AB$ 与体相平衡时，化学势满足

$$
\mu_A+\mu_B=\mu_{AB}^{\mathrm{bulk}}.
$$

同时必须避免元素析出，因而 $\mu_A$ 和 $\mu_B$ 受纯元素及其他竞争相限制。代入约束后，$\gamma$ 对一个独立化学势呈分段线性关系；不同重构的直线交点给出平衡表面相界。

<p lang="en">For a slab exchanging atoms with reservoirs, the grand potential subtracts chemical-potential terms from the slab free energy. Dividing the excess by the number and area of surfaces gives the surface free energy. Bulk equilibrium constrains the chemical potentials, while precipitation and competing compounds restrict their allowed range. Surface reconstructions therefore appear as piecewise-linear free-energy branches versus the remaining independent chemical potential.</p>

“最低表面能”只在声明的化学势区间和平衡假设下成立。非平衡生长可以形成不在平衡相图上的终止，需要动力学模型而非继续调节化学势解释。

<p lang="en">A “lowest-energy surface” is defined only over a stated chemical-potential range and under equilibrium assumptions. Nonequilibrium growth may produce terminations outside the equilibrium diagram and requires a kinetic model rather than further adjustment of equilibrium chemical potentials.</p>

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="界面能带对齐包含体相参考与界面偶极" titleEn="Band alignment contains bulk references and an interface dipole">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

半导体界面的能带偏移不能只用两个孤立材料的本征值直接相减。每个体相计算内部的能量零点任意，必须先把价带或导带边相对于该材料内部平均静电势或其他可比较参考定位；再通过显式界面计算确定两侧参考势的相对位移。界面电荷重排形成的偶极会改变这一步，因此真空能级对齐或电子亲和势规则只在相应边界条件下成立。

显式界面还引入应变、原子重构、缺陷、极化、有限厚度和可能的电场。层分辨 DOS 可以帮助识别态的来源，却不能单独确定电荷转移因果或 Type-I/II/III 对齐；需要清楚的能量参考、足够厚的体相区、势线性区或宏观平均，以及对界面模型的收敛。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Band offsets at a semiconductor interface cannot be obtained by directly subtracting eigenvalues from two isolated bulk calculations. The energy zero inside each bulk calculation is arbitrary. Band edges must first be positioned relative to an internal reference such as the average electrostatic potential, after which an explicit interface calculation determines the relative shift of the two references. Charge rearrangement creates an interface dipole and changes this lineup, so vacuum-level alignment or electron-affinity rules apply only under their stated boundary conditions.

An explicit interface also introduces strain, atomic reconstruction, defects, polarization, finite thickness, and possible electric fields. Layer-resolved DOS can identify the origin of states but cannot by itself determine the cause of charge transfer or establish Type-I/II/III alignment. A clear energy reference, sufficiently bulk-like regions, potential plateaus or macroscopic averaging, and convergence of the interface model are required.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="表面态与拓扑表面态不是同一证据" titleEn="A surface state is not automatically a topological surface state" kind="boundary">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

悬挂键、重构、吸附物和普通 Shockley/Tamm 机制都可在体带隙附近产生表面态。认定拓扑表面态还需确定体相拓扑不变量或等价充分证据、保护对称性、表面态与体带的连接方式，以及对表面终止和扰动的预期稳健性。单张 slab 能带或局域在表面的波函数不够。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Dangling bonds, reconstructions, adsorbates, and ordinary Shockley or Tamm mechanisms can all create surface states near a bulk gap. Identifying a topological surface state additionally requires a bulk topological invariant or equivalent sufficient evidence, the protecting symmetry, the connectivity between surface and bulk bands, and the expected robustness to termination and perturbations. One slab band plot or a surface-localized wavefunction is insufficient.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>

<h2 id="section-2-12">2.12 低维材料与范德华异质结构 | Low-Dimensional Materials and van der Waals Heterostructures</h2>

<BilingualSection titleZh="降维会改变能带、屏蔽、声子和相空间" titleEn="Reduced dimensionality changes bands, screening, phonons, and phase space" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】 §2.12 · pp. 47–48">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

单层材料并非体相的简单薄片。层间耦合消失会改变轨道杂化和能带极值；屏蔽减弱会增强电子—空穴和电子—电子相互作用；二维声子含有柔性模并对环境敏感。Martin 以 MoS$_2$ 为例说明体相间接隙到单层直接隙的变化，以及由此增强的光学吸收。

层状材料可通过弱层间结合堆叠成范德华异质结构。弱结合允许晶格常数不同的层组合，却不表示层间影响可忽略。界面距离、扭角、应变、堆垛 registry、静电势差、轨道杂化和声子失配都会改变电子、光学与热输运。层内强键和层间弱键必须用不同长度与能量尺度描述。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

A monolayer is not merely a thin slice of its bulk parent. Removing interlayer coupling changes orbital hybridization and band extrema; reduced screening strengthens electron–hole and electron–electron interactions; two-dimensional phonons include flexural modes and are sensitive to the environment. Martin uses MoS$_2$ to illustrate the change from an indirect bulk gap to a direct monolayer gap and the associated increase in optical absorption.

Layered materials can be stacked into van der Waals heterostructures through weak interlayer binding. Weak binding permits layers with different lattice constants to be combined, but it does not make interlayer effects negligible. Interface separation, twist angle, strain, stacking registry, electrostatic potential difference, orbital hybridization, and phonon mismatch can modify electronic, optical, and thermal transport. Strong intralayer and weak interlayer bonds require distinct length and energy scales.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="二维计算的边界条件" titleEn="Boundary conditions for two-dimensional calculations">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

三维周期程序通常用含真空的超胞表示二维层。真空厚度只是减少镜像耦合的数值参数，不是材料厚度。带电层、面外偶极、极化和长程 Coulomb 响应可能需要偶极修正、二维 Coulomb 截断或专门边界条件；仅增加真空并不总能消除误差。能量、应力和介电量的归一化也必须说明是每面积、每原胞还是按人为超胞体积。

异质结构建模还需搜索可比较的超胞、扭角和 registry。小失配并不证明结构可形成；强制共格应变改变层内能带和声子。结构优化得到局域最低点后，仍要分别检查相对形成/结合能、滑移势垒、全布里渊区声子、电子态的层分辨身份和目标性质收敛。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Three-dimensional periodic codes usually represent a two-dimensional layer using a supercell containing vacuum. Vacuum thickness is a numerical parameter used to reduce image coupling; it is not the physical material thickness. Charged layers, out-of-plane dipoles, polarization, and long-range Coulomb response may require dipole corrections, two-dimensional Coulomb truncation, or specialized boundary conditions. Increasing vacuum alone does not always remove the error. Energy, stress, and dielectric quantities must be normalized explicitly per area, per cell, or by the artificial supercell volume.

Heterostructure modelling also requires a search over commensurate cells, twist angles, and stacking registries. Small lattice mismatch does not establish synthesizability, and imposed coherent strain changes intralayer bands and phonons. After relaxation reaches a local minimum, relative formation or binding energy, sliding barriers, full-Brillouin-zone phonons, layer-resolved state identity, and convergence of the target property remain separate checks.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualCallout titleZh="二维异质结构超导的证据顺序" titleEn="Evidence order for superconductivity in a two-dimensional heterostructure" kind="practice">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

结构可构建 → 弛豫后界面身份明确 → 全布里渊区目标电子态具有所需层/轨道成分 → 电荷与势对齐由可比分析支持 → 动力学稳定 → 声子和电子—声子矩阵元对 $k/q$ 网格、展宽和能窗收敛 → 适用的 Migdal–Eliashberg 或其他理论给出 $\lambda$、$\omega_{\log}$ 与 $T_c$。能带金属化、DOS 峰、Γ 线口袋或单点软模不能跳过中间门槛。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Buildable structure → preserved interface identity after relaxation → full-Brillouin-zone target states with the required layer/orbital character → comparable charge and potential-lineup evidence → dynamical stability → phonons and electron–phonon matrix elements converged with respect to $k/q$ meshes, broadening, and energy windows → an applicable Migdal–Eliashberg or alternative theory yielding $\lambda$, $\omega_{\log}$, and $T_c$. Metallic bands, a DOS peak, a pocket seen only along a $\Gamma$-centred line, or one soft mode cannot bypass the intermediate gates.

  </div>
</BilingualCallout>
