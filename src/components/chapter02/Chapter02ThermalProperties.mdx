<h2 id="section-2-10">2.10 热性质：固体、液体与相图 | Thermal Properties: Solids, Liquids, and Phase Diagrams</h2>

<BilingualSection titleZh="有限温度把单个最低结构变成统计系综" titleEn="Finite temperature replaces one minimum by a statistical ensemble" eyebrow="【Martin 原书 | Martin source】 §2.10 · pp. 38–44">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

零温电子总能给出重要基线，但有限温度性质取决于可访问微观状态的统计权重。固体中，声子自由能产生热容、热膨胀和温度依赖相稳定性；磁、自旋、电子和构型自由度也可能贡献熵。液体没有单一平衡晶体结构，必须对不断变化的原子构型进行采样。

Martin 以地球深部材料和液态水为例说明电子结构方法如何进入有限温度问题。高压矿物相图要求同时处理压力、温度和多相自由能；从头算分子动力学用电子结构力推进原子轨迹，可直接给出结构分布和时间相关量，但可靠性取决于系综、尺寸、时间步长、总时长和电子结构近似。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Zero-temperature electronic total energy provides an essential baseline, but finite-temperature properties depend on statistical weights over accessible microscopic states. In solids, phonon free energy produces heat capacity, thermal expansion, and temperature-dependent phase stability; magnetic, electronic, and configurational degrees of freedom may also contribute entropy. A liquid has no single equilibrium crystal structure and requires sampling over continuously changing atomic configurations.

Martin uses deep-Earth materials and liquid water to show how electronic-structure methods enter finite-temperature problems. High-pressure mineral phase diagrams require pressure, temperature, and multiphase free energies. Ab initio molecular dynamics propagates atomic trajectories using electronic-structure forces and can yield structural distributions and time-dependent quantities, but its reliability depends on ensemble, size, time step, trajectory length, and electronic approximation.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<DerivationBlock
  title="补充推导 | Supplemental derivation: harmonic vibrational free energy"
  assumptions={[
    '结构在谐波近似下稳定，声子模式彼此独立；',
    '频率不随温度显式改变，暂不含热膨胀和强非谐；',
    '电子保持绝热基态。',
  ]}
>

单个频率 $\omega$ 的量子简谐振子能级为

$$
E_n=\hbar\omega\left(n+\frac12\right),\qquad n=0,1,2,\ldots
$$

其配分函数为几何级数

$$
Z_\omega=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}e^{-\beta E_n}
=\frac{e^{-\beta\hbar\omega/2}}
{1-e^{-\beta\hbar\omega}}.
$$

Helmholtz 自由能 $F=-k_BT\ln Z$，因此

$$
F_\omega(T)=\frac12\hbar\omega
+k_BT\ln\left(1-e^{-\hbar\omega/k_BT}\right).
$$

对所有声子支和 $\mathbf q$ 点求和：

$$
F_{\mathrm{vib}}(V,T)
=\sum_{\mathbf q\nu}
\left[
\frac12\hbar\omega_{\mathbf q\nu}(V)
+k_BT\ln\left(1-e^{-\hbar\omega_{\mathbf q\nu}(V)/k_BT}\right)
\right].
$$

第一项是零点能，第二项是热占据贡献。准谐波近似通过频率对体积的依赖引入热膨胀；若模式强烈非谐、扩散或结构重排发生，谐波或准谐波自由能可能失效。

<p lang="en">Summing the quantum harmonic-oscillator partition function gives a zero-point term plus a thermal logarithmic term for each phonon. Summation over wavevectors and branches yields the harmonic vibrational free energy. The quasiharmonic approximation adds volume-dependent frequencies and thermal expansion, while strong anharmonicity, diffusion, or structural rearrangement lies outside this derivation.</p>

</DerivationBlock>

<BilingualSection titleZh="从头算分子动力学的对象" titleEn="The object sampled by ab initio molecular dynamics">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Born–Oppenheimer 分子动力学在每个时间步求电子基态力，再积分经典核方程；Car–Parrinello 方法引入辅助电子自由度并与核运动共同演化。无论实现如何，轨迹都只是所选系综中的有限样本。液体结构通常用径向分布函数

$$
g_{AB}(r)=\frac{1}{4\pi r^2\rho_B N_A}
\left\langle\sum_{i\in A}\sum_{j\in B}
\delta(r-|\mathbf r_i-\mathbf r_j|)\right\rangle
$$

描述，它统计在 $A$ 原子周围距离 $r$ 处找到 $B$ 原子的相对概率。峰位置和积分配位数是统计量，不对应一张“代表性快照”中的固定键。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Born–Oppenheimer molecular dynamics obtains electronic ground-state forces at each step and integrates classical nuclear equations. Car–Parrinello dynamics introduces auxiliary electronic degrees of freedom and evolves them together with the nuclei. In either implementation, a trajectory is a finite sample from the chosen ensemble. Liquid structure is commonly described by the radial distribution function

$$
g_{AB}(r)=\frac{1}{4\pi r^2\rho_B N_A}
\left\langle\sum_{i\in A}\sum_{j\in B}
\delta(r-|\mathbf r_i-\mathbf r_j|)\right\rangle,
$$

which measures the relative probability of finding a $B$ atom at distance $r$ from an $A$ atom. Peak positions and integrated coordination numbers are statistical quantities, not fixed bonds in one representative snapshot.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="深部地球与相图" titleEn="Deep-Earth conditions and phase diagrams">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

地球内部的压力和温度跨越巨大范围，材料组成和相结构决定密度、弹性、热输运和地震波速度。电子结构计算可预测高压晶体的状态方程和弹性；声子或分子动力学提供热修正；多相 Gibbs 自由能交叉给出理论相界。实验数据仍不可替代，因为真实样品可能含多组分、缺陷、非静水应力和动力学滞后。

相图计算必须保持化学势和组成约束一致。只比较同一化学式的晶体不能排除分解；只计算静态焓不能给出高温熵稳定相；在有限尺寸轨迹中观察到一种局域结构也不能证明宏观相已平衡。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Pressure and temperature span enormous ranges inside Earth, where composition and phase structure control density, elasticity, heat transport, and seismic velocities. Electronic-structure calculations can predict equations of state and elastic properties of high-pressure crystals; phonons or molecular dynamics supply thermal corrections; crossings of multiphase Gibbs free energies provide theoretical phase boundaries. Experiment remains indispensable because real samples may contain multiple components, defects, nonhydrostatic stress, and kinetic hysteresis.

Phase-diagram calculations must maintain consistent chemical-potential and composition constraints. Comparing crystals at one formula does not exclude decomposition; static enthalpy alone cannot identify entropy-stabilized high-temperature phases; and observing a local motif in a finite trajectory does not establish macroscopic equilibrium.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

    <ul>
      <li>声明系综、恒温/恒压算法、时间步长、超胞、电子收敛和初始条件；</li>
      <li>区分平衡化区间与生产区间，检查能量或目标温压分布；</li>

      <li>报告统计不确定性和相关时间，避免把连续帧当作独立样本；</li>
      <li>检查泛函、色散、核量子效应或化学反应是否会改变结论。</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">
    <ul>
      <li>state ensemble, thermostat or barostat, time step, cell, electronic convergence, and initial conditions;</li>
      <li>separate equilibration from production and inspect energy or target temperature/pressure distributions;</li>
      <li>converge the target observable with trajectory length and system size rather than relying on a visually stable trajectory;</li>
      <li>report statistical uncertainty and correlation time instead of treating consecutive frames as independent samples;</li>
      <li>check whether functional choice, dispersion, nuclear quantum effects, or chemical reactions can alter the conclusion.</li>
    </ul>
