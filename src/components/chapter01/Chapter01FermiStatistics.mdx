$$
f_i=\frac{1}{\exp[\beta(\varepsilon_i-\mu)]+1},
\qquad \beta=\frac{1}{k_{\mathrm B}T}.
$$

<DerivationBlock
  title="补充推导：单个费米能级的 grand-canonical 配分函数"
  assumptions={[
    '能级 i 的允许占据只有 n_i=0 或 1；',
    '能级与温度 T、化学势 μ 的热库达到平衡；',
    '推导分配既定能级的占据，不决定 ε_i 本身。',
  ]}
>

单个能级的 grand-canonical 权重是 $\exp[-\beta n_i(\varepsilon_i-\mu)]$。因此

$$
\mathcal Z_i=\sum_{n_i=0}^{1}e^{-\beta n_i(\varepsilon_i-\mu)}
=1+e^{-\beta(\varepsilon_i-\mu)}.
$$

平均占据按定义为

$$
\langle n_i\rangle=
\frac{\sum_{n_i=0}^{1}n_i e^{-\beta n_i(\varepsilon_i-\mu)}}{\mathcal Z_i}
=\frac{e^{-\beta(\varepsilon_i-\mu)}}{1+e^{-\beta(\varepsilon_i-\mu)}}.
$$

分子分母同乘 $e^{\beta(\varepsilon_i-\mu)}$，便得到 Fermi–Dirac 形式。三个直接检查是：$\varepsilon_i=\mu$ 时 $f_i=1/2$；$T\to0^+$ 时分布趋于阶跃；$\varepsilon_i-\mu\gg k_{\mathrm B}T$ 时恢复稀疏占据的 Boltzmann 极限。

<p lang="en">Only occupations zero and one are allowed for a single fermionic level. Summing their grand-canonical weights gives the partition function, and evaluating the occupation average yields the Fermi–Dirac formula. The checks $f(\mu)=1/2$, the zero-temperature step, and the dilute Boltzmann limit follow directly. The derivation assigns occupations to prescribed levels and does not compute those levels.</p>

</DerivationBlock>
