$$
f_i=\frac{1}{\exp[\beta(\varepsilon_i-\mu)]+1},
\qquad \beta=\frac{1}{k_{\mathrm B}T}.
$$

单个能级的 grand-canonical 权重是 $\exp[-\beta n_i(\varepsilon_i-\mu)]$。由于费米能级只允许 $n_i=0$ 或 $1$，配分函数只有两项。

<p lang="en">The grand-canonical weight of one level is $\exp[-\beta n_i(\varepsilon_i-\mu)]$. Because a fermionic level permits only $n_i=0$ or $1$, its partition function contains two terms.</p>

$$
\mathcal Z_i=\sum_{n_i=0}^{1}e^{-\beta n_i(\varepsilon_i-\mu)}
=1+e^{-\beta(\varepsilon_i-\mu)}.
$$

平均占据按概率加权定义。$n_i=0$ 项对分子没有贡献，$n_i=1$ 项留下指数权重。

<p lang="en">The mean occupation is the probability-weighted average. The $n_i=0$ term contributes nothing to the numerator, while the $n_i=1$ term leaves its exponential weight.</p>

$$
\langle n_i\rangle=
\frac{\sum_{n_i=0}^{1}n_i e^{-\beta n_i(\varepsilon_i-\mu)}}{\mathcal Z_i}
=\frac{e^{-\beta(\varepsilon_i-\mu)}}{1+e^{-\beta(\varepsilon_i-\mu)}}.
$$

分子分母同乘 $e^{\beta(\varepsilon_i-\mu)}$，便得到 Fermi–Dirac 形式。三个直接检查是：$\varepsilon_i=\mu$ 时 $f_i=1/2$；$T\to0^+$ 时分布趋于阶跃；$\varepsilon_i-\mu\gg k_{\mathrm B}T$ 时恢复稀疏占据的 Boltzmann 极限。以上推导只分配既定能级的占据，不计算能级本身。

<p lang="en">Multiplying numerator and denominator by $e^{\beta(\varepsilon_i-\mu)}$ gives the Fermi–Dirac form. Three checks follow: $f_i=1/2$ at $\varepsilon_i=\mu$; the distribution approaches a step as $T\to0^+$; and the dilute Boltzmann limit is recovered when $\varepsilon_i-\mu\gg k_{\mathrm B}T$. The derivation assigns occupations to prescribed levels and does not calculate the levels.</p>

</DerivationBlock>
