## 1.4 最大挑战：电子相互作用与关联 | The Greatest Challenge: Electron Interaction and Correlation

<BilingualSection titleZh="交换与关联必须分开" titleEn="Exchange and correlation must be separated" eyebrow="Martin 1.4 · p. 10">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

交换源于反对称性。即使 Hamiltonian 中没有显式自旋—自旋势，交换后的波函数符号仍会使不同自旋排列具有不同能量期望，这为磁性提供了量子力学基础。Hartree–Fock 精确处理单个 Slater determinant 内的交换，却把真实波函数限制在 determinant 流形上。

关联是 exact 多体态与所选独立粒子或单 determinant 描述之间剩余的协同运动。它不能用一个附加势或“电子彼此避开”完整概括。动态关联、近简并导致的静态关联、局域化竞争和长程屏蔽涉及不同机制。一个近似在弱关联 $sp$ 金属中成功，不能据此推断它在窄 $d$ 或 $f$ 带、Mott 转变或断键问题中同样可靠。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Exchange follows from antisymmetry. Even without an explicit spin–spin potential, the sign structure under exchange changes energy expectation values for different spin arrangements, providing a quantum-mechanical basis for magnetism. Hartree–Fock treats exchange exactly within one Slater determinant while restricting the physical wavefunction to the determinant manifold.

Correlation is the remaining coordinated motion between the exact many-body state and the chosen independent-particle or single-determinant description. It cannot be exhausted by one added potential or by saying that electrons avoid one another. Dynamic correlation, near-degeneracy or static correlation, localization competition, and long-range screening involve distinct mechanisms. Success for a weakly correlated $sp$ metal does not establish reliability for narrow $d$ or $f$ bands, a Mott transition, or bond breaking.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

<BilingualSection titleZh="能带宽度与局域相互作用的竞争" titleEn="Competition between bandwidth and local interaction">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

电子在晶格间离域可获得动能并形成宽带；局域 Coulomb 代价则抑制同一局域区域的双占据。当有效相互作用尺度 $U$ 与带宽 $W$ 的比值增大，简单部分填充能带给出的金属图像可能失效。$U/W$ 只是组织变量，真实判定还依赖轨道简并、晶体场、Hund 耦合、杂化、维度和磁序。

因此，部分填充的 Kohn–Sham 或 tight-binding 能带不能单独排除关联绝缘态。反过来，实验绝缘行为也不能仅凭名称确定是 Mott、charge-transfer、Peierls、Slater 还是无序局域化机制。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Delocalization across a lattice lowers kinetic energy and forms broad bands, whereas a local Coulomb cost suppresses double occupation of the same local region. As an effective interaction scale $U$ grows relative to bandwidth $W$, the metallic picture suggested by a partially filled band may fail. The ratio $U/W$ is an organizing variable, not a complete criterion; orbital degeneracy, crystal fields, Hund coupling, hybridization, dimensionality, and magnetic order also matter.

A partially filled Kohn–Sham or tight-binding band therefore cannot by itself exclude a correlated insulator. Conversely, insulating behavior does not identify a Mott, charge-transfer, Peierls, Slater, or disorder-localization mechanism without further evidence.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>
