<BilingualSection titleZh="周期势、Bloch 态与能带填充" titleEn="Periodic potentials, Bloch states, and band filling">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

完美晶体中的单粒子态可以按晶体动量 $\mathbf{k}$ 标记，并写成平面波相位与晶格周期函数的乘积。周期势不要求电子在每个原子间反复停止；Bloch 态本身是理想晶格的定态。实际电阻需要缺陷、无序、声子或其他散射机制。

含 $N_c$ 个原胞的有限晶体在周期边界条件下有 $N_c$ 个互不等价的 $\mathbf{k}$ 点。因此每条能带、每种自旋包含 $N_c$ 个态，即每原胞每自旋一个态。若两种自旋简并，一条完整能带可容纳每原胞两个电子。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

One-particle states in a perfect crystal can be labelled by crystal momentum $\mathbf{k}$ and written as a plane-wave phase times a lattice-periodic function. A periodic potential does not make an electron stop at each atom; Bloch states are stationary states of the ideal lattice. Resistance requires defects, disorder, phonons, or other scattering processes.

A finite crystal with $N_c$ primitive cells has $N_c$ inequivalent $\mathbf{k}$ points under periodic boundary conditions. Each band therefore contains $N_c$ states per spin, or one state per cell per spin. With twofold spin degeneracy, a complete band holds two electrons per cell.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

$$
\psi_{n\mathbf{k}}(\mathbf r+N_j\mathbf a_j)
=e^{i\mathbf{k}\cdot N_j\mathbf a_j}\psi_{n\mathbf{k}}(\mathbf r),
\qquad
e^{i\mathbf{k}\cdot N_j\mathbf a_j}=1.
$$

<BilingualSection titleZh="周期边界下的态计数" titleEn="State counting under periodic boundary conditions" compact={true}>
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

周期条件给出 $\mathbf{k}\cdot\mathbf a_j=2\pi m_j/N_j$。沿第 $j$ 个原始方向，$m_j$ 有 $N_j$ 个互不等价取值；全部方向的组合数 $N_1N_2\cdots N_d$ 恰好等于原胞总数 $N_c$。固定带指标 $n$ 后，每个允许的 $\mathbf{k}$ 对应一个态，所以每条能带每原胞每自旋有一个态。自旋、谷或其他内部简并必须另行计数。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

The periodic condition gives $\mathbf{k}\cdot\mathbf a_j=2\pi m_j/N_j$. Along primitive direction $j$, the integer $m_j$ has $N_j$ inequivalent values; the product $N_1N_2\cdots N_d$ equals the total number of cells $N_c$. For a fixed band index $n$, each allowed $\mathbf{k}$ labels one state, giving one state per cell per spin in each band. Spin, valley, and other internal degeneracies must be counted separately.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>
