<BilingualSection titleZh="相同电子与交换反对称性" titleEn="Identical electrons and exchange antisymmetry">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

对相同电子交换完整坐标 $\mathbf{x}_i$ 与 $\mathbf{x}_j$ 时，物理概率不能依赖人为编号，但电子的多体波函数必须变号：

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Exchanging the complete coordinates $\mathbf{x}_i$ and $\mathbf{x}_j$ of identical electrons cannot change physical probabilities, while the electronic many-body wavefunction changes sign:

  </div>
</BilingualSection>

$$
\Psi(\ldots,\mathbf{x}_i,\ldots,\mathbf{x}_j,\ldots)
=-\Psi(\ldots,\mathbf{x}_j,\ldots,\mathbf{x}_i,\ldots).
$$

<BilingualSection compact={true}>
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

令两套坐标相同，交换前后函数值又必须相等，因此有 $\Psi=-\Psi$，只能得到 $\Psi=0$。这就是同一自旋轨道不能被两个电子同时占据的波函数表达。它来自费米子交换对称性，不是电子间 Coulomb 排斥的近似说法。即使把电子—电子相互作用设为零，反对称性仍然存在。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

If the two complete coordinates are identical, the exchanged value must also equal the original value. Antisymmetry gives $\Psi=-\Psi$, so $\Psi=0$. This is the wavefunction statement that two electrons cannot occupy the same spin orbital. It follows from fermionic exchange symmetry, not from an approximate picture of Coulomb repulsion, and remains when the electron–electron interaction is set to zero.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>
