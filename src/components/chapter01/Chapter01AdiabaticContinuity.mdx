独立电子图像的成功还依赖绝热连续性：从可解的独立粒子体系逐渐打开相互作用，如果过程中没有相变、能隙闭合或基态简并结构突变，一些分类可以保持。普通 Fermi 液体中，Luttinger 体积关系把 Fermi 面包围的体积与粒子数联系起来。

The success of the independent-electron picture also uses adiabatic continuity. Interactions may be turned on gradually from a solvable independent-particle system; selected classifications persist if no phase transition, gap closing, or abrupt change in ground-state degeneracy occurs. In an ordinary Fermi liquid, the Luttinger-volume relation connects particle number to the volume enclosed by the Fermi surface.

The argument has explicit boundaries. Superconductivity, Mott insulation, magnetic order, and other new phases alter low-energy degrees of freedom or symmetry. An independent-particle metallic result is therefore a starting point, not an unconditional conclusion; interaction-driven phases require evidence beyond simple band filling.
