## 常见误判 | Common Misconceptions

1. **“独立电子”表示相互作用不存在。** 有效势、Hartree–Fock 和 Kohn–Sham 方程都可具有独立粒子形式，同时以平均场、交换或泛函形式包含相互作用效应。  
   **“Independent electrons” means interactions are absent.** Independent-particle equations can still include mean fields, exchange, or functional interaction effects.
2. **Pauli 排斥等同于 Coulomb 排斥。** 前者来自费米子反对称性，后者来自 Hamiltonian 的相互作用项。  
   **Pauli exclusion is Coulomb repulsion.** They coexist but have different origins.
3. **一条能带的容量由化学价决定。** 容量来自允许的 $\mathbf k$ 点数和内部简并；化学价决定填入多少电子。  
   **Chemical valence fixes band capacity.** Capacity follows from state counting; valence fixes filling.
4. **能带图出现 gap 就证明实验绝缘行为。** 方法误差、温度、缺陷、掺杂、表面通道和多体激发仍需判断。  
   **A plotted gap proves experimental insulation.** Method error and additional conduction channels remain relevant.
5. **DFT 的对象是独立电子。** 真实对象是相互作用体系；Kohn–Sham 独立粒子体系是辅助构造。  
   **DFT describes independent physical electrons.** Its auxiliary system reproduces the interacting ground-state density.
6. **拓扑能隙闭合必然产生非平庸相。** 闭合使指标改变成为可能，但两端指标仍需计算或由充分对称性信息确定。  
   **Every gap closing creates a nontrivial phase.** A closing is possible transition evidence, not sufficient classification.

## 练习与复盘 | Exercises and Review

以下问题均为本站原创，不对应 Martin 原书习题。  
The following questions are original to this site and do not reproduce Martin’s exercises.

1. 给出 $\Psi(\mathbf{x}_1,\mathbf{x}_2)$、$\psi_i(\mathbf r)$、$n(\mathbf r)$、$g(E)$ 和 $\varepsilon_{n\mathbf k}$ 的定义域与物理角色，并说明从多体波函数得到密度时丢失了哪些信息。  
   State the domains and physical roles of $\Psi(\mathbf{x}_1,\mathbf{x}_2)$, $\psi_i(\mathbf r)$, $n(\mathbf r)$, $g(E)$, and $\varepsilon_{n\mathbf k}$. Explain what information is lost when the density is obtained from a many-body wavefunction.
2. 从两个自旋轨道写出二电子 Slater determinant，交换两套电子坐标验证符号变化，再令两个轨道相同并解释 determinant 为零。  
   Construct a two-electron Slater determinant from two spin orbitals, verify its sign change under exchange, then make the orbitals identical and explain why the determinant vanishes.
3. 从 Fermi–Dirac 公式推导 $T\to0^+$ 时 $\varepsilon<\mu$、$\varepsilon=\mu$、$\varepsilon>\mu$ 的极限，并用交互图检查趋势。  
   Derive the $T\to0^+$ limits for $\varepsilon<\mu$, $\varepsilon=\mu$, and $\varepsilon>\mu$, then check the trend with the interactive model.
4. 对一维含 $N$ 个原胞的周期环列出允许 $k$，再把态计数推广到三维 $N_1N_2N_3$ 个原胞。  
   List the allowed $k$ values for a one-dimensional periodic ring with $N$ cells, then generalize the counting to $N_1N_2N_3$ cells in three dimensions.
5. 日志显示 `convergence has been achieved` 且能带穿过费米能。列出判断“材料是稳定金属”仍缺少的结构、数值、方法和实验层证据。  
   A log reports `convergence has been achieved`, and a band crosses the Fermi level. List the structural, numerical, methodological, and experimental evidence still needed before calling the material a stable metal.
6. 解释 Hartree–Fock 已含交换却仍可能在断键、窄带或近简并问题中失效。  
   Explain why Hartree–Fock can fail for bond breaking, narrow bands, or near-degeneracy even though it includes exchange.
7. 审查“PBE gap 为 0.8 eV，因此实验光学吸收边为 0.8 eV”，区分 Kohn–Sham gap、基本 gap、光学 gap、激子与数值收敛。  
   Critique the claim “the PBE gap is 0.8 eV, so the experimental optical absorption edge is 0.8 eV.” Distinguish the Kohn–Sham, fundamental, and optical gaps, excitons, and numerical convergence.
8. 画出参数驱动的两带模型，分别表示保持 gap 的平庸变形、闭合后指标不变、闭合后指标改变，并说明为何仅观察闭合不够。  
   Sketch parameter-driven two-band models for a gap-preserving trivial deformation, a closing with unchanged invariant, and a closing with changed invariant. Explain why observing a closing alone is insufficient.

### 解题检查框架 | Solution-check framework

- 先写对象与假设，再写方程。  
  State the objects and assumptions before writing equations.
- 每个等号注明定义、代数变形、边界条件或近似。  
  Identify whether each equality follows from a definition, algebraic step, boundary condition, or approximation.
- 检查单位、归一化与特殊极限。  
  Check units, normalization, and limiting cases.
- 把模型内部结论与真实材料结论分开。  
  Separate conclusions internal to a model from claims about real materials.
- 指出最小反例或缺失证据。  
  Identify the minimal counterexample or missing evidence.
