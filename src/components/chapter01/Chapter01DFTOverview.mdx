## 1.5 密度泛函理论 | DFT

<BilingualSection titleZh="密度是基本变量" titleEn="The density is the basic variable" eyebrow="Martin 1.5 · p. 11">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

Hohenberg–Kohn 理论把固定粒子数的基态问题表述为电子密度的泛函问题。在适用条件下，基态密度决定外势至多差一个常数，进而决定 Hamiltonian 与基态性质。这个存在性结论没有直接给出通用、可计算的精确能量泛函。

Kohn–Sham 构造一个辅助非相互作用体系，使其基态密度与真实相互作用体系相同。辅助轨道用于重构密度和总能；它们不等同于完整多体波函数，单个本征值通常也不能直接当作真实加电子或去电子激发能。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Hohenberg–Kohn theory formulates the fixed-particle-number ground-state problem in terms of functionals of the electron density. Under its conditions, the ground-state density determines the external potential up to a constant and therefore determines the Hamiltonian and ground-state properties. This existence statement does not directly provide a universal, computable exact energy functional.

Kohn–Sham theory constructs an auxiliary noninteracting system with the same ground-state density as the interacting system. Its auxiliary orbitals reconstruct the density and total energy; they are not the complete many-body wavefunction, and individual eigenvalues are generally not direct electron-addition or electron-removal energies.

  </div>
