$$
E[n]=T_s[n]+\int v_{\mathrm{ext}}(\mathbf r)n(\mathbf r)\,d\mathbf r
+E_H[n]+E_{\mathrm{xc}}[n].
$$

<BilingualSection titleZh="精确形式与实际近似" titleEn="Exact form and practical approximation">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

$T_s[n]$ 是产生密度 $n$ 的辅助非相互作用体系动能，$E_H[n]$ 是经典 Coulomb 能，$E_{\mathrm{xc}}[n]$ 收集真实动能与 $T_s$ 的差以及 Hartree 项之外的交换—关联贡献。分解形式可以严格成立，实际计算仍必须近似 $E_{\mathrm{xc}}$。LDA、GGA、meta-GGA 和 hybrid 描述不同近似结构，不构成逐级保证更准确的排名。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

$T_s[n]$ is the kinetic energy of the auxiliary noninteracting system that produces $n$; $E_H[n]$ is the classical Coulomb energy. The functional $E_{\mathrm{xc}}[n]$ contains the interacting kinetic correction and exchange–correlation terms beyond Hartree. The decomposition may be formally exact, while practical calculations still approximate $E_{\mathrm{xc}}$. LDA, GGA, meta-GGA, and hybrid denote different approximation structures, not a guaranteed accuracy ranking.

  </div>
</BilingualSection>
