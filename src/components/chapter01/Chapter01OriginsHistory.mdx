## 1.1 量子理论与电子结构的起源 | Quantum Theory and the Origins of Electronic Structure

<BilingualSection titleZh="从带电粒子到稳定原子" titleEn="From charged particles to stable atoms" eyebrow="Martin 1.1 · pp. 2–3">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

电子结构问题在电子被确认为物质的组成粒子之后才具有现代含义。谱线的磁场分裂和气体放电实验给出了电子的电荷符号与很大的荷质比；原子散射实验又表明正电荷集中在小而重的原子核内。经典电动力学不能同时解释稳定原子和离散光谱。

Bohr 模型保留了量子化这一正确方向，却没有给出后来的波函数动力学。1920 年代形成的量子力学把问题改写为 Hamiltonian 在 Hilbert 空间中的本征值问题。离散能谱来自算符、边界条件和可归一化要求；自旋与 Pauli 不相容原理进一步约束多电子态。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

Electronic structure acquired its modern meaning after the electron was established as a constituent of matter. Magnetic splitting of spectral lines and gas-discharge experiments determined the sign of its charge and its large charge-to-mass ratio; atomic scattering experiments then located positive charge in a small, massive nucleus. Classical electrodynamics could not explain both stable atoms and discrete spectra.

Bohr’s model retained the crucial idea of quantization but not the later dynamics of wavefunctions. Quantum mechanics in the 1920s reformulated the problem as an eigenvalue problem for a Hamiltonian acting in Hilbert space. Discrete spectra followed from the operator, boundary conditions, and normalizability; spin and the Pauli exclusion principle further constrained many-electron states.
