<BilingualSection titleZh="本章要建立的主线" titleEn="The line of reasoning established here" eyebrow="Orientation">
  <div class="bilingual-section__zh">

本章回答一个范围明确的问题：现代电子结构理论为什么会同时依赖多电子量子力学与独立粒子方程？电子、原子核、量子化、交换反对称性、能带、自洽场、电子关联和密度泛函理论依次解决了不同层次的困难。读完后，应能判断一个计算结果属于严格多体描述、有效独立粒子模型、密度泛函辅助体系，还是数值实现产生的近似结果。

前置知识只要求定态 Schrödinger 方程、概率解释、基本统计力学和晶体周期性的初步概念。Chapter 2 将这些概念展开为材料性质的全景；Chapter 3 才正式写出电子与原子核的多体 Hamiltonian，并讨论独立电子近似、交换与关联。

  </div>
  <div class="bilingual-section__en" lang="en">

This chapter asks why modern electronic-structure theory relies simultaneously on many-electron quantum mechanics and independent-particle equations. The electron, the nucleus, quantization, exchange antisymmetry, bands, self-consistency, correlation, and density functional theory each resolve a different layer of the problem. By the end, the reader should be able to distinguish an exact many-body description, an effective independent-particle model, a density-functional auxiliary system, and an approximation introduced by numerical implementation.

The prerequisites are the stationary Schrödinger equation, its probabilistic interpretation, basic statistical mechanics, and an initial notion of crystal periodicity. Chapter 2 surveys the material properties built from these ideas. Chapter 3 then writes the full electron–nucleus Hamiltonian and treats independent-electron approximations, exchange, and correlation explicitly.
