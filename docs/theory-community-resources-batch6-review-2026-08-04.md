# Theory community resource expansion — Batch 6

Review date: 2026-08-04

Website baseline: `655a0278d0eff8ad7f171cb480f2039a68fb5496`

Scope: page-local additions for Differential Equations and Many-Body Physics. Methods and Reference remain outside this batch.

## Review method

Community recommendations, Bilibili course searches, and open-course catalogues were used for discovery. Public inclusion required either an institutional primary course or a clearly self-produced creator page, a visible sequence, a distinct role not already filled on the page, and an explicit limitation. Play count, recommendation frequency, institutional reputation, and course length were not treated as scientific validation.

## Accepted additions

| Theory page | Resource | Role and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Differential Equations | MIT OpenCourseWare 18.303, [Linear Partial Differential Equations](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-303-linear-partial-differential-equations-fall-2006/) | A complete open undergraduate PDE route through diffusion, Laplace/Poisson, wave equations, separation of variables, Fourier methods, eigenvalue problems, and Green functions. It closes the previously recorded absence of a fully reviewed open PDE course, but does not provide modern weak-solution or nonlinear-PDE theory. |
| Differential Equations | 热爱数学的卢员外, [基础偏微分方程（数学物理方程）](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1ML41167Wd/) | A self-produced Chinese sequence connecting differential operators, Fourier transforms, distributions, convolution, fundamental solutions, Poisson Green functions, boundary-value problems, minimum principles, and the heat equation. It is a conceptual bridge rather than a full existence/regularity course or an electronic-structure solver manual. |
| Many-Body Physics | Schlichting, [二次量子化与声子系列](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1M9V6z1EbU/) | A self-produced Chinese long-form sequence beginning with harmonic oscillators and variable particle number, then bosons, fermions, quantized electromagnetic modes, and phonon fields. It fills the language gap before Green functions and diagrammatics; it is not a complete many-body course and does not derive self-energy, Dyson equations, response functions, or controlled approximations. |

## Deferred or rejected candidates

- Reuploaded university many-body courses were not added when the platform page did not establish publication permission, even when it named a verifiable original course.
- Exam-cram PDE playlists and solution-only collections were excluded.
- Advanced Evans-based reading groups were deferred because they assume real and functional analysis and do not serve the current first PDE bridge.
- Short second-quantization clips were not used when they did not form a continuous route from variable-particle-number states to operators and field modes.

## Source and access boundaries

The MIT page is the institutional primary source. The two Bilibili routes were checked at their creator pages and are linked rather than reproduced. Course availability, subtitles, playlists, and platform access can change. No external body text, figures, exercises, videos, or downloadable copyrighted materials were copied.

## Validation boundary

This review verifies source identity, visible scope, and the stated learning role of each destination on 2026-08-04. It does not independently validate every derivation, subtitle, example, exercise, future revision, or scientific claim. Completing a course does not establish discretization quality, solver convergence, a many-body approximation, or a material-specific result.
