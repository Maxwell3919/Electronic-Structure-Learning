# Theory batch 8 source-access boundary

The three public pages link only to official public landing pages checked during this batch. Course materials can change, move, or vary by region. The website records the role of each resource and does not promise permanent access to every lecture file, transcript, exercise, or publisher asset.

A working landing page does not independently verify every statement in the resource or make the resource suitable for every learner. Broken or redirected links should be removed or replaced after a fresh check rather than preserved for historical continuity.
