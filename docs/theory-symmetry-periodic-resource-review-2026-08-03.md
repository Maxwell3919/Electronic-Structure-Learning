# Theory symmetry and periodic matter resource review — Batch B

Review date: 2026-08-03

Website baseline: `f3b7fb12899b48bc84d3e1fcdd173ff23300e4d5`

## Scope and review method

This review covers Crystallography, Group Theory and Symmetry, Solid-State
Physics, Brillouin-Zone Sampling, and Berry Phases and Electronic Topology.
Each accepted destination was opened at its named provider or maintainer. The
review checked visible authorship or course ownership, an inspectable outline or
documentation structure, a page-specific learning role, access conditions, and
a limitation. Reputation, enrolment, stars, or search placement were not used as
quality scores.

## Accepted routes

| Page | Primary source | Role and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crystallography | Northwest University, [Crystallography](https://www.icourse163.org/course/0709NWU022-1206313810) | Chinese systematic route through geometric crystallography, macro/micro symmetry, orientation, and crystal chemistry. Its mineralogical emphasis does not replace electronic reciprocal-space theory or structure refinement. |
| Group Theory and Symmetry | Peking University, [Structural Chemistry II](https://www.icourse163.org/course/PKU-1002521004) | Chinese bridge from point-group reasoning to lattices, space groups, and diffraction. It does not replace representation-theory proofs, little groups, double groups, or magnetic symmetry. |
| Solid-State Physics | Xiangtan University, [Solid-State Physics](https://www.icourse163.org/course/0702XTU019-1461179176) | Chinese upper-undergraduate route through periodic solids, phonons, bands, and selected modern topics. It needs prior mathematics and quantum mechanics and cannot validate an electronic-structure calculation. |
| Brillouin-Zone Sampling | DFTK, [Periodic Problems and Plane-Wave Discretisations](https://docs.dftk.org/stable/guide/periodic_problems/) | Maintained open theory-to-code tutorial distinguishing Bloch blocks, k grids, plane-wave cutoffs, and band paths. Simple models and example grids are not convergence defaults. |
| Berry Phases and Electronic Topology | Akhmerov, Sau, van Heck, and collaborators, [Topology in Condensed Matter](https://topocondmat.org/) | Open graduate-level course joining simulations, mathematical concepts, and physical arguments. It does not turn a model result into material-specific topology evidence. |

## Deferred classes

- Unattributed course reuploads, mirrors, and download bundles.
- Narrow software pages that expose a command but no inspectable learning route.
- Topology content that identifies a phase solely from a band inversion, a single
  image, a symmetry label, or a surface crossing.
- Resources whose authorship, licence, access, or intended source could not be
  established at review time.

## Page mapping and remaining gaps

The additions are page-local. The Theory entrance remains a concise navigation
page, and Reference remains intentionally unfilled. Future review should seek a
source-clear Chinese route for band representations and magnetic groups, plus a
carefully bounded introduction to topology that is suitable before graduate-level
prerequisites. It should not duplicate the resources above or merge crystallographic
classification with Brillouin-zone convergence guidance.

## Handoff notes

The new descriptions are public-readable first versions. Later editorial work may
improve the transition from point groups to space-group representations and make
the distinction between a k-path and an integration mesh more intuitive. No
scientific correction or authority change is made by this resource batch.
