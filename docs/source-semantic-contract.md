# Source-semantic contract

## Stable layers

- `textbook-baseline`: catalog identity, original section titles and page locators;
- `original-exposition`: this site's authored explanation;
- `supplemental-derivation`: a derivation added for learning, not a source subsection;
- `numerical-interpretation`: discretization, convergence and evidence limits;
- `practical-mapping`: software or workflow cross-reference;
- `later-literature`: bibliographic and later-development layer;
- `open-discussion`: questions, inference and unresolved issues.

H1 is the catalog Chapter or Appendix title. H2 with a source-section identity represents the catalog layer; authored explanatory themes use H3, and local derivation steps may use H4. A generated source-section map and semantic TOC remain available even where an outline page has no body.

Strong scope words are reviewed in context. Catalog titles are not mechanically rewritten. High-confidence authored headings may be narrowed; body-level uncertainty is recorded as `body-review-needed` for a separate content task.
