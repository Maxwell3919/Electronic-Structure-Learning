## Source structure read directly

- §5.1 defines the homogeneous electron gas through density `n`, Wigner–Seitz radius `r_s`, spin polarization and a uniform compensating positive background; the scaled Hamiltonian exposes kinetic and interaction density scaling.
- §5.2 develops the noninteracting Fermi sphere, state counting, Fermi energy, kinetic energy, the zero-temperature one-body density matrix, Hartree–Fock dispersion, the metallic Fermi-surface derivative divergence, exchange energy and the exchange hole.
- §5.3 introduces Thomas–Fermi screening, correlation-energy high-/low-density limits, parameterization boundaries, spin interpolation, pair-correlation functions and the coupling-constant-averaged exchange–correlation hole.
- §5.4 uses electron-gas kinetic/exchange/correlation terms plus ionic/pseudopotential contributions to explain the energy scale of binding and bulk moduli in simple sp metals.
- §5.5 separates quasiparticle addition/removal from neutral collective excitations and develops the homogeneous-gas Lindhard/RPA dielectric response, particle–hole continuum, screening, plasmons, Friedel oscillations and Kohn-anomaly boundary.
