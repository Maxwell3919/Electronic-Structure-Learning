# Theory batch 8 summary

Batch 8 adds Electromagnetism, Statistical Mechanics, and Solid-State Chemistry. Together with batches 5–7, these pages complete the Tier-2 subject inventory identified by the systematic-review report.

Tier 2 remains a branching map rather than a fixed course sequence:

- periodic representations, reciprocal space, core treatment, sampling, relativity, and magnetism;
- atomic/molecular states, physical chemistry, bonding interpretation, and localized orbitals;
- electrostatic fields, finite-temperature ensembles, and composition/defect chemistry.

Completion here means that each Tier-2 responsibility has an individually reviewed public page and source/acceptance record. It does not mean every resource comparison is exhaustive or that Tier-3 topics and the Learning Map are complete.
