# Theory resource review — Completion route · Quantum Mechanics

**Review date:** 2026-08-04  
**Scope:** `quantum-mechanics` — the one Theory topic not assigned to Batches A–G.

## Existing coverage and gap

The page already had a complementary English sequence: MIT OCW 8.04 for wave mechanics and 8.05 for bra–ket notation, spin, tensor products, angular momentum, and hydrogen, plus a chemistry-oriented publisher text. Its remaining useful gap was a system-level Chinese route that makes the mathematical prerequisites and the bridge to identical electrons explicit without turning this page into a problem-set directory.

The course page was opened at the national platform source. It identifies the host university, teachers, 2026 update and run period, intended audience, visible chapter structure, and its current learning status. The route is included for lectures/concepts only; no exercise, test, or solution recommendation is made.

## Deferred candidates and boundaries

- Courses that only described quantum-information history or lacked a visible standard quantum-mechanics curriculum were not used as substitutes.
- Unattributed uploads, mirrors, and download compilations were excluded.
- No videos, course files, assignments, answers, or platform content are copied. The public link is a study route, not a claim that completing it proves preparation for a particular electronic-structure calculation.

## Remaining gap

A maintained Chinese-language resource that separately teaches rigorous Hilbert-space/functional-analysis foundations for quantum mechanics would complement this introductory-system route. The page intentionally leaves many-electron antisymmetry, correlation, periodicity, and numerical representation to their dedicated Theory pages.
