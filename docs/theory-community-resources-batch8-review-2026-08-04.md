# Theory community resource expansion — Batch 8

Review date: 2026-08-04

Website baseline: `4d6266f9de337945e78ed9e75c078f4e8bfa62c1`

Scope: page-local additions for Hartree and Hartree–Fock Theory, and Many-Body Perturbation Theory and Quasiparticles. Methods, Reference, and the site architecture remain outside this batch.

## Review method

Bilibili course searches, community recommendations, and official advanced-electronic-structure school archives were used for discovery. Public inclusion required a creator-maintained or institutional primary destination, a visible sequence rather than an isolated terminology clip, a distinct role not already filled on the target page, and an explicit limitation. Play count, institution, software adoption, and event prestige were treated as discovery signals rather than scientific validation.

## Deferred or rejected candidates

- Single Hartree–Fock clips without the surrounding determinant, integral, SCF, and basis sequence were not used as the primary route.
- Exam-solution collections and formula-only summaries were excluded.
- Reuploaded university quantum-chemistry lectures without a clear publication chain were excluded.
- GW/BSE promotional talks without inspectable lecture materials or implementation context were deferred.
- Automated workflow packages were not treated as theory courses when they did not explain self-energy, screening, quasiparticle and neutral-excitation distinctions, and convergence boundaries.

## Source and access boundaries

The Bilibili destination was checked at the named uploader's maintained course collection and is linked rather than reproduced. The UT Austin school page is the institutional event archive and exposes schedule entries for slides, recorded lectures, and tutorial files. Platform access, playlists, external cloud files, and software versions can change. No lecture text, figures, videos, exercises, or restricted tutorial files were copied into the repository.

## Validation boundary

This review verifies source identity, visible scope, and the stated learning role of each destination on 2026-08-04. It does not independently validate every derivation, subtitle, lecture statement, code example, tutorial input, software result, or future revision. Completing either route does not establish basis completeness, determinant stability, a controlled many-body approximation, GW/BSE convergence, or a material-specific scientific claim.
