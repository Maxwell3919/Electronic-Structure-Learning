# Theory community resource expansion — Batch 4

Review date: 2026-08-04

Website baseline: `d9f73641a1981ea8b1797d4f062461de9fcbc5ac`

Scope: page-local additions for Classical Mechanics, Statistical Mechanics, Inorganic Chemistry, and Surface and Interface Chemistry. Methods and Reference remain outside this batch.

## Review method

Community recommendations and social-video discovery were used to identify candidate teaching sequences. Public inclusion required a primary provider, an official course archive, or a clearly self-produced original playlist; visible topic coverage; a distinct page-specific role; and an explicit limitation.

Popularity, view counts, repost frequency, course prestige, and repository activity were not treated as scientific validation. Reposted copies were rejected when the original or self-produced destination could be identified.

## Accepted additions

| Classical Mechanics | MIT OpenCourseWare 8.03SC, [Physics III: Vibrations and Waves](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-03sc-physics-iii-vibrations-and-waves-fall-2016/) | Bridges single oscillators to coupled oscillators, normal modes, translation symmetry, wave equations, Fourier decomposition, and dispersion. | Inorganic Chemistry | MIT OpenCourseWare 5.04, [Principles of Inorganic Chemistry II](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/5-04-principles-of-inorganic-chemistry-ii-fall-2008/) | Advanced bridge from group theory and electronic structure to electronic, vibrational, magnetic, and spectroscopic properties of transition-metal complexes. It does not cover f-element chemistry comprehensively or make ligand/crystal-field models unique many-electron observables. |
| Surface and Interface Chemistry | IIT Bombay / NPTEL, [Engineering Chemistry I, Surface Chemistry module](https://archive.nptel.ac.in/content/storage2/courses/122101001/Slide/lect36/36_20.htm) | Open module connecting Gibbs/Langmuir/BET adsorption, surface reactions and catalysis to XPS, AES, LEED, SEM, and STM. It supplies a thermodynamics-to-characterization bridge, but is an introductory engineering-chemistry module rather than a full modern surface-science or first-principles course. |

## Deferred or rejected candidates

- Reuploads of the Huazhong course that explicitly identify themselves as mirrors or copies, despite larger view counts.
- Mechanics playlists that discuss isolated harmonic oscillators but never reach coupled normal modes, translation symmetry, or waves.
- Inorganic resources limited to formal electron counting or crystal-field diagrams without spectroscopy, symmetry, or model boundaries.
- Surface-engineering courses dominated by coating, wear, fabrication, or process operations when they did not supply adsorption thermodynamics and microscopic surface characterization.
- Software tutorials that construct slabs or plot spectra without explaining physical references and applicability limits.

## Validation boundary

The review verifies source identity, visible structure, and the stated learning role of each accepted destination on 2026-08-04. It does not independently validate every lecture statement, subtitle, derivation, example, exercise, future platform revision, or scientific result. None of these routes replaces system- and observable-specific electronic-structure validation.
