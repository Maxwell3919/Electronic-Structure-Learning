# Theory thermal and mechanical foundations resource review — Batch C

Review date: 2026-08-03

Website baseline: `7b78ee2f6a9cc6d13c44ddafeb3323f35ec60862`

## Scope and review method

This review covers Classical Mechanics, Thermodynamics, Statistical Mechanics,
and Electromagnetism. Each accepted source was opened at its publisher or
official course-platform page. Review checked named instructors or author,
visible course or book structure, access and licence information where supplied,
the knowledge gap addressed, prerequisites, and a limit on what the route can
support. Provider reputation, enrolment, and search position were not acceptance
criteria. The requested scope excludes exercise and solution routes.

## Accepted routes

| Page | Resource and source | Learning role and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Classical Mechanics | Harbin Institute of Technology, *Theoretical Mechanics (Physics)*, National Smart Education Platform | Chinese, physics-major video route through constrained and analytical mechanics, small oscillations, Hamiltonian mechanics, Poisson brackets, and canonical transformations. It assumes calculus and introductory physics and does not establish an electronic-structure nuclear or phonon approximation. |
| Thermodynamics | MIT OpenCourseWare 3.020, Rafael Jaramillo, *Thermodynamics of Materials* | Material-facing lectures on potentials, phase coexistence, mixtures, solution models, phase diagrams, and statistical-thermodynamic entry points. It supports interpretation of free-energy and phase language, not a material-specific first-principles stability result. |
| Statistical Mechanics | Konstantin Likharev, *Statistical Mechanics*, Stony Brook / Open Textbook Library | CC BY-NC-SA, text-first graduate route through thermodynamics, physical statistics, gases, phase transitions, and fluctuations. It is not a finite-temperature DFT software guide or a general nonequilibrium prescription. |
| Electromagnetism | Northwestern Polytechnical University, *Electromagnetic Fields and Waves*, National Smart Education Platform | Chinese video route through vector calculus, Maxwell equations, media, boundary conditions, static fields, image methods, and separation of variables. Waveguide and radiation topics are not a substitute for periodic electrostatics or dielectric-response calculations. |

## Deferred sources and limits

- Unattributed video reuploads, download compilations, and pages that could not
  establish the lecturer or original publisher were rejected.
- Engineering-only heat-transfer, circuit, antenna, or software documentation
  was not added when it did not provide a coherent theory route for this batch.
- The accepted Chinese platform courses display a prior or completed session;
  login, regional access, and future session availability must be rechecked.
- No resource was represented as sufficient evidence for phonon stability,
  thermodynamic stability, finite-temperature DFT validity, dielectric response,
  or a material conclusion.

## Page mapping and remaining gaps

The routes are page-local; the Theory index remains concise, Reference remains
unfilled, and Methods is unchanged. The most useful future additions would be a
source-clear Chinese route focused on equilibrium statistical mechanics beyond
ideal gases, and an openly accessible treatment of electrostatics in periodic
or reduced-dimensional cells. Known prose handoff points are the bridge from
classical normal modes to quantum phonons and a gentler explanation of why the
thermodynamic limit matters before an ensemble is introduced.
