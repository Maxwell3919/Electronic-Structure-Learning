# Credits and inspiration

Electronic-Structure-Learning is independent. Acknowledgement means public inspiration only; it does not imply affiliation, collaboration, sponsorship, endorsement, reproduction, or independent replication.

## Science publishing and interaction design

- [Distill](https://distill.pub/): web-native academic reading, figures, notes, and interactive explanation.
- [Bartosz Ciechanowski](https://ciechanow.ski/): direct manipulation and progressive assembly of complex explanations.
- [Mathigon](https://mathigon.org/): learning routes, prerequisites, small steps, tasks, and feedback structure.
- [Quanta Magazine](https://www.quantamagazine.org/): editorial science illustration and explanatory clarity.
- [Stripe Press](https://press.stripe.com/): publication hierarchy, covers, whitespace, and brand-level finish.
- [Observable](https://observablehq.com/): data-driven interaction and reusable visualization ideas.
- [The Pudding](https://pudding.cool/): visual narratives organized around one bounded question.

These references have separate responsibilities. None defines scientific data encoding or acceptance criteria for this project.
