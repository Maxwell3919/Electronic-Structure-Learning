Website baseline: `6ba388bf5f226596db19272725ebcce272252b99`

Scope: page-local additions for Functional Analysis and Variational Methods, and Pseudopotentials, PAW, and Core–Valence Treatments. Methods, Reference, and the site architecture remain outside this batch.

## Review method

Social-platform recommendations and open-course catalogues were used for discovery. Public inclusion required an institutional primary destination or a clearly creator-uploaded page, an inspectable syllabus or sequence, a distinct role not already filled on the target page, and an explicit limitation. Popularity, play count, provider reputation, course recency, and software adoption were treated as discovery signals rather than scientific validation.

## Deferred or rejected candidates

- Short clips that reduce functional analysis to definitions without a continuous route through topology, operators, and spectra were not accepted.
- Exam-oriented functional-analysis playlists and solution collections were excluded.
- Pseudopotential videos centred on one code's input switch, library download, or recommended cutoff without scattering, projector, core-partition, and validation boundaries were excluded.
- Vendor or software-training material dominated by product operation was deferred when it did not supply an independent theory role.
- Reuploaded lectures without a clear publication or permission chain were excluded.

## Source and access boundaries

The NPTEL destination is the institutional primary course page. The Bilibili resource was checked at the named creator's upload and is linked rather than reproduced. Course sessions, page structure, subtitles, platform access, and external links can change. No course text, figures, videos, exercises, or restricted downloads were copied into the repository.

## Validation boundary

This review verifies source identity, visible scope, and the stated learning role of each destination on 2026-08-04. It does not independently validate every theorem proof, lecture statement, subtitle, implementation claim, example, or future revision. Completing either route does not establish the validity of a DFT functional, a pseudopotential/PAW dataset, a finite basis, an SCF solution, or a material-specific result.
