# Theory community resource expansion — Batch 9

Review date: 2026-08-04

Website baseline: `c60bcf6c6ccdb448eca923647885f53b88a2d7e7`

Scope: page-local additions for Localized-Orbital Methods and Kohn–Sham Density Functional Theory. Methods, Reference, the Theory index, and the site architecture remain outside this batch.

## Review method

Community recommendations, school archives, and university teaching pages were used for discovery. Public inclusion required an institutional or project-maintained primary destination, visible course structure, a role not already filled on the target page, and a clear limitation. School prestige, software adoption, number of videos, and platform popularity were treated as discovery signals rather than scientific validation.

## Deferred or rejected candidates

- Isolated Wannier90 command demonstrations without gauge, subspace, disentanglement, or interpolation-validation context were excluded.
- Tutorials tied to obsolete downloads were not used alone when a maintained school index was available.
- Commercial training pages centred on one electronic-structure code were deferred when the scientific role could not be separated from product operation.
- Kohn–Sham videos with restricted institutional login were excluded when a publicly inspectable teaching archive was available.
- Reuploaded university courses without a clear publication chain were excluded.

## Source and access boundaries

Materials Cloud is the primary archive for the listed Wannier90 schools and links recordings and educational material maintained by the project community. The Strasbourg page is the university laboratory's teaching archive and links its own notes and institutional video platform. Event pages, video hosts, software versions, and external files can change. No lecture text, videos, slides, figures, exercises, or downloadable tutorial archives were copied into the repository.

## Validation boundary

This review verifies source identity, visible structure, and the stated learning role of each destination on 2026-08-04. It does not independently validate every lecture statement, subtitle, code example, tutorial input, interpolation, or future revision. Completing either route does not establish basis completeness, Wannier-subspace validity, topological compatibility, Kohn–Sham representability, exchange–correlation accuracy, SCF convergence, or a material-specific result.
