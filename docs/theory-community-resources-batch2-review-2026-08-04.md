Review date: 2026-08-04

Website baseline: `2fc5d36891b0ce3f726a6f22cdf876f447c5bd9d`

Scope: page-local additions for Group Theory and Symmetry, Fourier Analysis, Relativistic Electronic Structure/Spin/Magnetism, and Exchange–Correlation Functionals and Approximations. Methods and Reference remain outside this batch.

## Review method

Social-platform discussions and playlists were used as discovery signals. A resource was accepted only after its named primary destination or clearly self-produced course page exposed identifiable authorship, a sufficiently inspectable sequence, a distinct gap-filling role, and a limitation that prevents it from being presented as a complete replacement.

Popularity, play count, follower count, enrolment, institutional branding, and recommendation frequency were not used as scientific validation.

## Deferred or rejected candidates

- High-play-count course reuploads without a verifiable original publication chain.
- Short “one video explains every functional” material that collapses DFT foundations, Kohn–Sham construction, XC approximation, basis choice, pseudopotentials, and SCF into one undifferentiated introduction.
- SOC or magnetism videos limited to software toggles without a conceptual sequence, compatible relativistic inputs, competing magnetic states, or observable convergence boundaries.
- Fourier resources restricted to signal-processing recipes when they did not explain convergence, convolution, sampling, or the distinction between reciprocal components and Brillouin-zone variables.
- Group-theory explainers that stopped before representations while making claims about band degeneracy or crystalline topology.

## Validation boundary

The review verifies the visible source identity, course outline, and role of each accepted destination on the date above. It does not independently validate every lecture statement, subtitle, exercise, translation, future platform revision, or scientific claim. External availability can change.
